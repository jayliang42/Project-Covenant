## 世界的两面 | Two sides of the world

看看这个世界，会看到两面。一面很美：星空、海洋、母爱、音乐、舍己救人的事。另一面很痛：自私、欺骗、家庭里的伤害、战争和死亡。<br>
Look at the world and you see two sides. One is beautiful: the night sky, the sea, a mother's love, music, people who risk themselves to save others. The other is painful: selfishness, deceit, harm within families, war, and death.

如果世界只是物质偶然碰撞的结果，美和道德尊严很难解释。如果世界没有出过问题，苦难和邪恶又说不通。《创世记》第 1–3 章对这两面都有交代。<br>
If the world is only the result of matter colliding by chance, beauty and moral dignity are hard to explain. If nothing has gone wrong with the world, suffering and evil make no sense. Genesis 1–3 gives an account of both.

## 受造的美善和人的尊严 | The goodness of creation and human dignity

《创世记》第 1 章里，神创造万物，一再说“神看着是好的”。造人的时候，经文说神“照着自己的形像造人……造男造女”。<br>
In Genesis 1, God creates all things and says again and again that it is "good." When he makes humans, the text says God made them "in his own image... male and female."

在古代近东，通常只有帝国的君王才被称为神明的“形像”或代表。《创世记》却说，**每一个普通人，无论男女、贫富、强弱，都有创造主的形像**。人的尊严、平等和良心都以此为根基。<br>
In the ancient Near East, usually only an empire's king was called the "image" or representative of a god. Genesis says that **every ordinary person, male or female, rich or poor, strong or weak, bears the Creator's image**. Human dignity, equality, and conscience rest on this.

## 信任破裂，四种关系受损 | Trust breaks and four relationships are damaged

《创世记》第 3 章讲这一切是怎么坏的：人不再信任神的良善，想自己作主，自己定义善恶。人与神之间的信任一断，四种关系跟着受损：<br>
Genesis 3 tells how things went wrong: humans stopped trusting God's goodness and wanted to rule themselves and define good and evil on their own. Once trust with God broke, four relationships were damaged with it:

1. **人与神**：原来坦然同行，现在害怕，躲在树丛后面。<br>
   **With God**: Open fellowship turned into fear and hiding among the trees.
2. **人与自己**：心里有了羞耻、空虚和防备。<br>
   **Within the self**: Shame, emptiness, and defensiveness set in.
3. **人与人**：亚当马上把责任推给夏娃。到下一章（创 4），该隐杀了亲弟弟亚伯。<br>
   **Between people**: Adam at once blamed Eve. In the next chapter (Genesis 4), Cain killed his own brother Abel.
4. **人与大地**：劳动成了汗流满面的重担，地里长出荆棘。<br>
   **With the earth**: Work became sweat and burden, and the ground grew thorns.

## 神问：“你在哪里？” | God asks, "Where are you?"

人躲起来之后，神来到园中。他说的第一句话是<strong>“你在哪里？”</strong>，是在找人。神还应许，将来有一位“女人的后裔”要伤蛇的头（创 3:15）。圣经后面的故事，讲的就是神怎样一步步把躲藏的人找回来。<br>
After the humans hid, God came into the garden. His first words were **"Where are you?"** He was looking for them. God also promised that one day an offspring of the woman would crush the serpent's head (Genesis 3:15). The rest of the Bible tells how God, step by step, brings back the people who hid.
