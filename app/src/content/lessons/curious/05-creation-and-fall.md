## 我们每天都活在一个矛盾里 | Living inside a daily paradox

只要诚实观察世界，我们就会遇到一个双重的惊讶：一方面，这个世界美得惊人——星空、海洋、母爱、音乐、舍己救人的善举；另一方面，这个世界又痛得刺眼——自私、欺骗、家庭伤害、战争与死亡。<br>
Any honest look at the world confronts us with a double surprise: on one hand, breathtaking beauty — starlight, music, sacrificial kindness; on the other hand, heartbreaking brokenness — selfishness, betrayal, war, and death.

如果世界纯粹是冷漠物质的偶然碰撞，“美与道德尊严”很难解释；如果世界没有任何败坏，“苦难与邪恶”又说不通。《创世记》第 1–3 章恰好同时回答了这两面。<br>
If the universe is nothing but cold matter in motion, human dignity and moral longing are hard to explain; if nothing has gone wrong, our cruelty makes no sense. Genesis 1–3 accounts for both at once.

## 第一面：受造的美善与人的尊严 | Side 1: Original goodness and human dignity

在《创世记》第 1 章，神创造万物，一再宣告“神看着是好的”。到了造人时，经文说神“照着自己的形像造人……造男造女”。<br>
In Genesis 1, God brings order and life into being and repeatedly declares it "good." At the climax of creation, God makes humanity "in his own image... male and female."

在古代近东文化里，通常只有帝国的君王才被称为神明的“形像”或代表；但《创世记》做出石破天惊的宣告：**每一个普通人——无论男女、贫富、强弱——都带有创造主的形像**。这是人类尊严、平等与良心最深的根基。<br>
In the ancient Near East, kings alone were typically called the "image" of a deity; Genesis makes the radical claim that **every human being — male and female, rich or poor — bears the Creator's image**. That is the deepest root of human worth and moral conscience.

## 第二面：信任的破裂与四重失落 | Side 2: Broken trust and a fourfold fracture

《创世记》第 3 章讲述悲剧的源头：人不再信任神的良善，想要自己坐上宝座，自己定义善恶。当人与神的信任一断，四重关系立刻连锁崩塌：<br>
Genesis 3 traces the root of our tragedy: humans stopped trusting God's goodness and reached for autonomy — to define good and evil on their own terms. Once trust in God snapped, four relationships fractured together:

1. **人与神**：从坦然同行变成害怕、躲藏在树丛后。<br>
   **With God**: Fellowship turned into fear and hiding among the trees.
2. **人与自己**：心里产生羞耻、空虚与防备。<br>
   **Within the self**: Peace gave way to shame, anxiety, and self-justification.
3. **人与人**：亚当立刻把责任推给夏娃，到了下一章（创 4），该隐甚至杀害亲弟弟亚伯。<br>
   **Between people**: Adam immediately blamed Eve, and by chapter 4 Cain murdered his brother Abel.
4. **人与大地**：劳动变成汗流满面的重担，大地长出荆棘。<br>
   **With the earth**: Joyful stewardship turned into painful toil among thorns.

## 树林里的呼唤：“你在哪里？” | The voice among the trees: "Where are you?"

然而，最动人的一幕就在人躲藏之后：神走进园中，发出的第一句话不是咒骂，而是寻找——**“你在哪里？”** 并应许将来有一位“女人的后裔”要伤蛇的头（创 3:15）。整本圣经接下来的故事，就是神如何一步步把躲藏的人找回来。<br>
Yet the most moving moment comes right after humanity hides: God walks into the garden asking not "How dare you?" but **"Where are you?"** — and promises that a future offspring will crush the serpent's head (Genesis 3:15). The rest of the Bible is the story of how God comes looking for people in hiding.
