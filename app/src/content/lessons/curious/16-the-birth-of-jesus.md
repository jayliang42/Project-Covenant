## 两卷福音书的记载 | Two Gospel accounts

四卷福音书里，《马太福音》和《路加福音》记载了耶稣的出生。两卷书的角度不同。<br>
Of the four Gospels, Matthew and Luke record the birth of Jesus. They tell it from different angles.

《路加福音》从马利亚讲起。天使告诉她，她要怀孕生子，孩子要称为至高者的儿子。后来罗马皇帝下令登记户口，约瑟带着马利亚回到伯利恒。马利亚在那里生了孩子，“用布包起来，放在马槽里，因为客店里没有地方”（路 2:7）。当夜，天使向野地里的牧羊人报信：“今天在大卫的城里，为你们生了救主，就是主基督”（路 2:11）。<br>
Luke begins with Mary. An angel tells her that she will conceive and bear a son, who will be called the Son of the Most High. Later the Roman emperor orders a registration, and Joseph takes Mary to Bethlehem. There she gives birth, wraps the child in cloth, and lays him “in a feeding trough, because there was no room for them in the inn” (Luke 2:7). That night an angel brings the news to shepherds in the fields: “there is born to you today, in David’s city, a Savior, who is Christ the Lord” (Luke 2:11).

《马太福音》从约瑟讲起。天使在梦里告诉他，马利亚所怀的孕是从圣灵来的，要给孩子起名叫耶稣，“因他要将自己的百姓从罪恶里救出来”（太 1:21）。后来有几个博士从东方来，要拜那生下来作犹太人之王的。希律王听见了很不安，想要除掉这个孩子，约瑟就带着母子逃到埃及。<br>
Matthew begins with Joseph. In a dream an angel tells him that the child Mary carries is from the Holy Spirit and is to be named Jesus, “for it is he who shall save his people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21). Later wise men come from the east to worship the one born King of the Jews. King Herod is troubled when he hears it and tries to destroy the child, and Joseph flees to Egypt with mother and son.

## 哪些是圣经没有说的 | What the Bible does not say

圣诞节的许多画面来自后来的习俗和艺术作品。<br>
Many Christmas pictures come from later custom and art.

- **日期**：圣经没有说耶稣生在哪一天。把 12 月 25 日作为耶稣的生日，现存最早的记录是主后 336 年罗马的一份年历。今天多数教会在 12 月 25 日庆祝，一些使用旧历的东方教会在 1 月 7 日庆祝。<br>
  **The date**: The Bible does not say on what day Jesus was born. The earliest surviving record of December 25 as his birthday is a Roman almanac entry for AD 336. Most churches today celebrate on December 25, and some Eastern churches that keep the older calendar celebrate on January 7.
- **年份**：福音书说耶稣生在希律王的时候。多数学者根据约瑟夫的记载，把希律的死定在主前 4 年，所以耶稣大约生在主前 6 到 4 年之间。也有学者主张希律死得稍晚。公元纪年是 6 世纪的人推算的，算得不准。<br>
  **The year**: The Gospels say Jesus was born in the days of King Herod. Most scholars, following Josephus, date Herod's death to 4 BC, which puts Jesus' birth at about 6 to 4 BC. Some scholars argue for a slightly later death of Herod. The AD system was calculated in the 6th century, and the calculation was off.
- **博士的人数**：圣经只说“有几个博士”，没有说是三个，也没有说他们是王。“三个”的说法来自他们献上的三样礼物：黄金、乳香、没药。<br>
  **The number of wise men**: The Bible says only “wise men,” without giving a number or calling them kings. The number three comes from their three gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

## 这件事的意思 | What the event means

福音书的作者关心的是这个孩子是谁。马太引用先知以赛亚的话说，人要称他的名为“以马内利”，翻出来就是“神与我们同在”（太 1:23）。约翰用一句话概括：“道成了肉身，住在我们中间”（约 1:14）。<br>
What the Gospel writers care about is who this child is. Matthew quotes the prophet Isaiah, saying that he will be called “Immanuel,” which means “God with us” (Matthew 1:23). John puts it in one sentence: “The Word became flesh, and lived among us” (John 1:14).

基督徒相信，创造世界的神亲自成为人，来到人中间。这就是“道成肉身”。出生的地点是马槽，最先来看的是牧羊人，这些细节表明他来的方式很卑微。<br>
Christians believe that the God who made the world became a human being and came among people. This is what “incarnation” means. The birthplace was a feeding trough and the first visitors were shepherds, and these details show how lowly his coming was.

## 关于童女怀孕 | On the virgin birth

马太和路加都说，马利亚怀孕的时候还是童女，这孕是从圣灵来的。天主教、东正教和新教的历代信经都承认这一点，《使徒信经》说耶稣“因圣灵感孕，由童贞女马利亚所生”。<br>
Matthew and Luke both say that Mary was a virgin when she conceived and that the child was from the Holy Spirit. The historic creeds of Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant churches all confess this. The Apostles' Creed says Jesus was “conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary.”

各传统的不同在于马利亚后来的情况。天主教和东正教相信马利亚终身是童贞女，认为福音书里说的耶稣的“弟兄”是他的近亲。大多数新教徒认为，这些“弟兄”是马利亚和约瑟后来生的孩子，不过路德、慈运理等早期改教家也曾接受马利亚终身童贞的说法。<br>
The traditions differ on what followed. Catholic and Orthodox Christians believe that Mary remained a virgin all her life and that the “brothers” of Jesus mentioned in the Gospels were close relatives. Most Protestants think these “brothers” were children later born to Mary and Joseph, though early Reformers such as Luther and Zwingli accepted Mary's perpetual virginity.

不信的人常常觉得童女怀孕难以接受。福音书的作者知道这件事不寻常，他们把它写成神的作为。一个人怎样看这件事，通常取决于他是否相信有一位能在自己所造的世界里行事的神。<br>
People who do not believe often find the virgin birth hard to accept. The Gospel writers knew it was extraordinary, and they present it as an act of God. How a person regards it usually depends on whether they believe there is a God who can act within the world he made.
