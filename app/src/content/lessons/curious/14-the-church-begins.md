## 四十天和一个吩咐 | Forty days and one instruction

耶稣复活以后，“四十天之久向他们显现，讲说神国的事”（徒 1:3）。他升天之前对门徒说：“但圣灵降临在你们身上，你们就必得着能力，并要在耶路撒冷、犹太全地，和撒马利亚，直到地极，作我的见证”（徒 1:8）。<br>
After his resurrection, Jesus appeared to the apostles “over a period of forty days, and speaking about God’s Kingdom” (Acts 1:3). Before he ascended he told them, “You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you. You will be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the uttermost parts of the earth” (Acts 1:8).

《使徒行传》大致就是照这个次序写的：先是耶路撒冷（1–7 章），然后是犹太和撒马利亚（8–12 章），再往外到罗马帝国的各个城市（13–28 章）。<br>
The book of Acts roughly follows this order: first Jerusalem (chapters 1–7), then Judea and Samaria (chapters 8–12), and then the cities of the Roman Empire further out (chapters 13–28).

## 五旬节 | Pentecost

五旬节是逾越节以后第五十天的犹太节期。那一天，门徒聚在一处，“忽然，从天上有响声下来，好像一阵大风吹过”，他们“都被圣灵充满”，用别国的话讲说神的作为（徒 2:2-4）。从各地来过节的犹太人都听见了。<br>
Pentecost is a Jewish feast held fifty days after Passover. That day the disciples were together, and “suddenly there came from the sky a sound like the rushing of a mighty wind.” They “were all filled with the Holy Spirit” and spoke in other languages about what God had done (Acts 2:2–4). Jews who had come from many lands for the feast heard them.

彼得站起来讲道，说被钉十字架的耶稣已经复活，是神立的主和基督。那一天约有三千人受洗（徒 2:41）。这些人“都恒心遵守使徒的教训，彼此交接，擘饼，祈祷”（徒 2:42）。他们一起吃饭，把东西分给有需要的人。<br>
Peter stood up and preached that the crucified Jesus had risen and that God had made him Lord and Christ. About three thousand people were baptized that day (Acts 2:41). They “continued steadfastly in the apostles’ teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and prayer” (Acts 2:42). They ate together and shared what they had with anyone in need.

## 福音传到外邦人中间 | The good news reaches the Gentiles

耶路撒冷的信徒受到逼迫，许多人分散到各地，“那些分散的人往各处去传道”（徒 8:4）。腓利到撒马利亚传道。彼得原来认为犹太人不该和外邦人来往，后来去了罗马军官哥尼流的家，说：“我真看出神是不偏待人”（徒 10:34）。<br>
When the believers in Jerusalem were persecuted, many scattered, and “those who were scattered abroad went around preaching the word” (Acts 8:4). Philip preached in Samaria. Peter had believed that Jews should not mix with Gentiles. Then he went to the house of the Roman officer Cornelius and said, “Truly I perceive that God doesn’t show favoritism” (Acts 10:34).

逼迫过教会的扫罗在去大马士革的路上遇见了复活的耶稣，后来他就是使徒保罗。保罗几次远行，在今天的土耳其和希腊一带建立教会。新约里的许多书信，就是他写给这些教会的。<br>
Saul, who had persecuted the church, met the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus and became the apostle Paul. Paul made several long journeys and planted churches in what are now Turkey and Greece. Many of the letters in the New Testament are ones he wrote to these churches.

《使徒行传》的结尾，保罗在罗马被软禁，住了两年，“放胆传讲神国的道……并没有人禁止”（徒 28:31）。书在这里结束，故事却还在继续。今天世界各地的教会都是从这里来的。<br>
At the end of Acts, Paul is under house arrest in Rome for two years, “preaching God’s Kingdom ... with all boldness, without hindrance” (Acts 28:31). The book ends here, but the story goes on. Churches all over the world today trace their roots to these events.
