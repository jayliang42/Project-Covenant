## 为什么要有节期 | Why there were feasts

神救以色列人出埃及以后，在西奈山给了他们律法。律法里规定了每年的节期（利未记 23 章）。节期的作用是让一代一代的人记住神做过的事，也为每年的收成感谢神。<br>
After God brought Israel out of Egypt, he gave them the law at Mount Sinai. The law set out the yearly feasts (Leviticus 23). The feasts helped each generation remember what God had done, and they gave thanks to God for each year's harvest.

其中有三个节期，以色列的男丁要上耶路撒冷去过：除酵节（和逾越节相连）、七七节、住棚节（申 16:16）。福音书里耶稣多次“上耶路撒冷过节”，就是这个缘故。<br>
For three of them the men of Israel went up to Jerusalem: Unleavened Bread (joined to Passover), Weeks, and Booths (Deuteronomy 16:16). This is why the Gospels several times show Jesus going up to Jerusalem for a feast.

## 主要的节期 | The main feasts

- **安息日（每周）**：第七天停下工作，记念神的创造和拯救。<br>
  **The Sabbath (weekly)**: On the seventh day work stops, in remembrance of God's creation and rescue.
- **逾越节和除酵节（春季）**：纪念出埃及。那一夜，以色列人把羔羊的血涂在门框上，神说：“我一见这血，就越过你们去”（出 12:13）。接着七天吃无酵饼，因为当时走得急，面来不及发酵。<br>
  **Passover and Unleavened Bread (spring)**: These remember the exodus. That night the Israelites put a lamb's blood on their doorframes, and God said, “When I see the blood, I will pass over you” (Exodus 12:13). For seven days afterwards they ate bread without yeast, because they had left in such haste that the dough had no time to rise.
- **七七节，又叫五旬节（初夏）**：在逾越节后第五十天，献上初熟的庄稼。后来犹太人也在这一天纪念神在西奈山赐下律法。<br>
  **Weeks, also called Pentecost (early summer)**: On the fiftieth day after Passover the first of the grain harvest was offered. Later the Jews also remembered on this day the giving of the law at Sinai.
- **吹角节（秋季）**：吹角宣告圣会，后来成为犹太新年。<br>
  **Trumpets (autumn)**: Trumpets announced a holy assembly. It later became the Jewish New Year.
- **赎罪日（秋季）**：一年当中最严肃的一天。大祭司这一天进入至圣所，为全体百姓赎罪。“因在这日要为你们赎罪，使你们洁净”（利 16:30）。<br>
  **The Day of Atonement (autumn)**: This was the most solemn day of the year. On it the high priest entered the Most Holy Place to make atonement for all the people. “On this day shall atonement be made for you, to cleanse you” (Leviticus 16:30).
- **住棚节（秋季）**：收成结束以后，百姓住在树枝搭的棚里七天，记念祖先在旷野住帐棚的日子（利 23:42-43）。<br>
  **Booths (autumn)**: After the harvest the people lived for seven days in shelters made of branches, remembering the years their ancestors lived in tents in the wilderness (Leviticus 23:42-43).

旧约后期还增加了两个节日：普珥节纪念《以斯帖记》里犹太人得救，修殿节纪念主前 2 世纪圣殿重新洁净。修殿节今天叫光明节，约翰福音 10:22 提到过它。<br>
Two more festivals were added later. Purim remembers the rescue of the Jews in the book of Esther, and the Feast of Dedication remembers the cleansing of the temple in the 2nd century BC. The Feast of Dedication is now called Hanukkah, and John 10:22 mentions it.

## 新约怎样把节期和耶稣连起来 | How the New Testament connects the feasts with Jesus

- **逾越节**：耶稣是在逾越节期间被钉十字架的。保罗说：“我们逾越节的羔羊基督已经被杀献祭了”（林前 5:7）。<br>
  **Passover**: Jesus was crucified at Passover. Paul says, “Christ, our Passover, has been sacrificed in our place” (1 Corinthians 5:7).
- **五旬节**：耶稣复活后第五十天，圣灵降在门徒身上，教会从这一天开始（徒 2:1）。<br>
  **Pentecost**: On the fiftieth day after Jesus rose, the Holy Spirit came upon the disciples, and the church began that day (Acts 2:1).
- **赎罪日**：《希伯来书》说，基督是大祭司，他只一次献上自己，就成了永远的赎罪（来 9:11-12）。<br>
  **The Day of Atonement**: Hebrews says that Christ is the high priest who offered himself once and obtained an eternal redemption (Hebrews 9:11-12).
- **住棚节**：在这个节期的最后一天，耶稣站着高声说：“人若渴了，可以到我这里来喝”（约 7:37）。<br>
  **Booths**: On the last day of this feast Jesus stood and cried out, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink!” (John 7:37).

所以保罗说，节期和安息日“原是后事的影儿；那形体却是基督”（西 2:17）。<br>
So Paul says that feast days and Sabbaths “are a shadow of the things to come; but the body is Christ’s” (Colossians 2:17).

## 基督徒今天怎样看待这些节期 | How Christians regard the feasts today

- **大多数教会**不守旧约的节期，认为它们所指向的已经在基督里实现了。教会另有自己的年历：圣诞节纪念耶稣降生，受难日和复活节纪念他的死和复活，五旬节（圣灵降临节）纪念圣灵降临。<br>
  **Most churches** do not keep the Old Testament feasts, holding that what they pointed to has come in Christ. The church has a calendar of its own: Christmas for the birth of Jesus, Good Friday and Easter for his death and resurrection, and Pentecost for the coming of the Spirit.
- **礼仪传统的教会**（天主教、东正教、圣公会、信义宗等）有完整的教会年历，包括将临期、大斋期等预备的季节。有些新教教会只守主日，不守别的节期。<br>
  **Liturgical churches** (Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Lutheran, and others) keep a full church year, including seasons of preparation such as Advent and Lent. Some Protestant churches keep only the Lord's Day and no other feasts.
- **安息日**：多数教会在星期日（主日）聚会，纪念耶稣在七日的第一日复活。基督复临安息日会等教会在星期六守安息日。<br>
  **The Sabbath**: Most churches meet on Sunday, the Lord's Day, remembering that Jesus rose on the first day of the week. Seventh-day Adventists and some other churches keep the Sabbath on Saturday.
- **信耶稣的犹太人**（弥赛亚信徒）很多仍然过这些节期，把它们看作指向耶稣的记号。<br>
  **Jewish believers in Jesus** (Messianic believers) often still keep these feasts and see them as signs pointing to Jesus.

保罗对这类分歧的原则是：“不拘在饮食上，或节期、月朔、安息日都不可让人论断你们”（西 2:16）。<br>
Paul's principle for such differences is, “Let no one therefore judge you in eating, or in drinking, or with respect to a feast day or a new moon or a Sabbath day” (Colossians 2:16).
