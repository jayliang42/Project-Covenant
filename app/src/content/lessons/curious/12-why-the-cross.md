## 最羞辱人的死法 | The most shameful death

罗马人用十字架处死奴隶、叛乱者和被征服地区的人。犯人被钉在路边，慢慢地死，让过路的人都看见。罗马公民一般不受这种刑罚。<br>
The Romans used the cross to execute slaves, rebels, and people from conquered lands. The condemned were nailed up by the roadside and died slowly, in full view of passers-by. Roman citizens were generally exempt from this punishment.

耶稣大约在主后 30 年或 33 年的逾越节期间，在罗马巡抚本丢·彼拉多手下被钉十字架。最早的基督徒没有回避这件事。保罗写道：“我们却是传钉十字架的基督，在犹太人为绊脚石，在外邦人为愚拙”（林前 1:23）。<br>
Jesus was crucified at Passover time, around AD 30 or 33, under the Roman governor Pontius Pilate. The first Christians did not hide this. Paul wrote, “We preach Christ crucified: a stumbling block to Jews, and foolishness to Greeks” (1 Corinthians 1:23).

## 福音书怎样记述 | How the Gospels tell it

四福音书用了很大篇幅写耶稣的最后一周。耶稣事先多次告诉门徒，他必须受许多的苦，被杀，“过三天复活”（可 8:31）。被捕前一晚，他在客西马尼园祷告：“阿爸！父啊！在你凡事都能；求你将这杯撤去。然而，不要从我的意思，只要从你的意思”（可 14:36）。<br>
The four Gospels give a large share of their pages to Jesus' last week. Jesus had told his disciples several times that he must suffer many things, be killed, and “after three days rise again” (Mark 8:31). The night before his arrest he prayed in Gethsemane: “Abba, Father, all things are possible to you. Please remove this cup from me. However, not what I desire, but what you desire” (Mark 14:36).

在十字架上，耶稣用《诗篇》22 篇的话呼喊：“我的神！我的神！为什么离弃我？”（可 15:34）。和他同钉的一个犯人求他：“耶稣啊，你得国降临的时候，求你记念我！”耶稣说：“今日你要同我在乐园里了”（路 23:42-43）。《约翰福音》记下他最后说的是：“成了！”（约 19:30）。<br>
On the cross Jesus cried out in the words of Psalm 22: “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” (Mark 15:34). One of the criminals crucified with him asked, “Lord, remember me when you come into your Kingdom.” Jesus said, “Today you will be with me in Paradise” (Luke 23:42–43). John records that his last words were “It is finished” (John 19:30).

## 新约怎样解释他的死 | How the New Testament explains his death

新约用了几种不同的说法来讲十字架的意义。它们互相补充：<br>
The New Testament uses several ways of speaking about what the cross means. They fill one another out:

- **替我们担罪**：以赛亚书 53 章说，那位受苦的仆人“为我们的过犯受害，为我们的罪孽压伤”。彼得写道，耶稣“被挂在木头上，亲身担当了我们的罪”（彼前 2:24）。<br>
  **Bearing our sin**: Isaiah 53 says the suffering servant “was pierced for our transgressions” and “crushed for our iniquities.” Peter writes that Jesus “bore our sins in his body on the tree” (1 Peter 2:24).
- **赎价**：耶稣说，他来是要“舍命作多人的赎价”（可 10:45）。赎价是把奴隶或俘虏赎出来所付的代价。<br>
  **A ransom**: Jesus said he came “to give his life as a ransom for many” (Mark 10:45). A ransom is the price paid to set a slave or captive free.
- **显明神的爱**：“惟有基督在我们还作罪人的时候为我们死，神的爱就在此向我们显明了”（罗 5:8）。<br>
  **Showing God's love**: “God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us” (Romans 5:8).
- **开了到神面前的路**：耶稣断气时，“殿里的幔子从上到下裂为两半”（可 15:38）。从前只有大祭司每年一次进到幔子里面，现在人可以借着耶稣来到神面前（来 10:19-20）。<br>
  **Opening the way to God**: When Jesus died, “the veil of the temple was torn in two from the top to the bottom” (Mark 15:38). In the past only the high priest went behind the veil, once a year. Now people can come to God through Jesus (Hebrews 10:19–20).

故事没有停在十字架。福音书接着讲的是第三天早晨的事，那是下一课的内容。<br>
The story does not stop at the cross. The Gospels go on to the morning of the third day, which is the subject of the next lesson.
