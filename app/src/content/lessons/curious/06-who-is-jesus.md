## 第一层：历史坐标里的拿撒勒人耶稣 | Layer 1: Jesus of Nazareth on the map of history

在探讨神学之前，先看最基本的历史坐标：耶稣不是神话里虚构的人物。主后第一世纪上半叶，他生活在罗马帝国统治下的加利利与犹太地，在拿撒勒长大，约三十岁左右开始公开传道，最终在罗马巡抚本丢·彼拉多任内（约主后 30 或 33 年逾越节期间）于耶路撒冷城外被钉十字架。<br>
Before we look at theology, let us look at the historical baseline: Jesus is not a mythical figure set in a fairy tale. He lived in first-century Galilee and Judea under the Roman Empire, grew up in Nazareth, began his public ministry around age thirty, and was crucified outside Jerusalem under the Roman prefect Pontius Pilate (around AD 30 or 33 at Passover).

除了新约二十七卷文献，第一世纪犹太历史学家**约瑟夫斯**（*Antiquities* 18, 20）与第二世纪初罗马历史学家**塔西佗**（*Annals* 15.44）也都提到了耶稣及早期基督徒。历史学界普遍承认他的存在、受洗、传道与受死；真正的分水岭在于：**他究竟是谁？**<br>
Alongside the New Testament writings, the first-century Jewish historian **Josephus** and the early second-century Roman historian **Tacitus** also refer to Jesus and the movement he started. Mainstream historians agree that he lived, taught, and was crucified; the real question is: **Who was he?**

## 第二层：超越“一位好老师”的言行 | Layer 2: Words and deeds that go beyond "a good teacher"

很多人尊重耶稣是一位伟大的道德导师。但当你打开四卷《福音书》（马太、马可、路加、约翰），会发现耶稣所说所做的事，远远超出了一位普通拉比或先知的范围：<br>
Many people admire Jesus as a great moral teacher. Yet when you actually read the four Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John), you meet someone who says and does things no ordinary teacher would dare:

- **他直接宣布赦免人的罪**（可 2:5-10）——在场的文士立刻明白，只有神自己才有权柄赦免得罪神的罪。<br>
  **He forgave sins on his own authority** (Mark 2:5–10) — shocking the scribes, who knew that only God can forgive offenses against God.
- **他跨越界限亲近边缘人**——他与被人看不起的税吏吃饭，触摸麻风病人，尊重妇女与外邦人，同时又严厉责备外表敬虔、内心冷酷的宗教领袖。<br>
  **He crossed social walls to welcome the outcast** — eating with despised tax collectors, touching lepers, honoring women and outsiders, while confronting self-righteous religious pride.
- **他把权柄定义为舍己的服事**——当时的民众期待一位用刀剑赶走罗马人的军事弥赛亚；耶稣却骑驴进耶路撒冷，甘愿舍命作赎价（可 10:45），并在第三天从死里复活。<br>
  **He redefined royal power as self-giving service** — where crowds expected a military Messiah to crush Rome by force, Jesus rode into Jerusalem on a donkey, gave his life as a ransom (Mark 10:45), and rose on the third day.

因此，耶稣留给每个读者的问题，依然是他在凯撒利亚·腓立比路上问门徒的那一句：**“你们说我是谁？”**<br>
So the question Jesus leaves with every reader is still the one he asked on the road to Caesarea Philippi: **"But who do you say that I am?"**
