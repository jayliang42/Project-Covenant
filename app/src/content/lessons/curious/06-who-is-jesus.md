## 历史上的拿撒勒人耶稣 | Jesus of Nazareth in history

讲神学之前，先看历史。耶稣是真实的历史人物。第一世纪上半叶，他生活在罗马帝国统治下的加利利和犹太地，在拿撒勒长大，大约三十岁开始公开传道，在罗马巡抚本丢·彼拉多任内被钉十字架，地点在耶路撒冷城外，时间约在主后 30 或 33 年的逾越节期间。<br>
Before theology, look at the history. Jesus was a real historical person. In the first half of the first century he lived in Galilee and Judea under Roman rule. He grew up in Nazareth, began his public ministry at about thirty, and was crucified under the Roman prefect Pontius Pilate, outside Jerusalem, at Passover around AD 30 or 33.

除了新约 27 卷书，第一世纪犹太历史学家**约瑟夫斯**（*Antiquities* 18, 20）和第二世纪初罗马历史学家**塔西佗**（*Annals* 15.44）也提到了耶稣和早期基督徒。历史学界普遍承认他存在过、受过洗、传过道、被处死。有分歧的是另一个问题：**他是谁？**<br>
Besides the 27 books of the New Testament, the first-century Jewish historian **Josephus** (*Antiquities* 18, 20) and the early second-century Roman historian **Tacitus** (*Annals* 15.44) also mention Jesus and the early Christians. Historians generally accept that he lived, was baptized, taught, and was executed. Where they differ is on another question: **who was he?**

## 他说的话和做的事 | What he said and did

很多人敬重耶稣，认为他是一位伟大的道德导师。读四卷《福音书》（马太、马可、路加、约翰）会发现，他说的话、做的事超出了一般拉比或先知的范围：<br>
Many people respect Jesus as a great moral teacher. Reading the four Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John) shows that what he said and did went beyond what an ordinary rabbi or prophet would do:

- **他宣布赦免人的罪**（可 2:5-10）。在场的文士立刻明白这话的分量：得罪神的罪，只有神自己有权赦免。<br>
  **He declared people's sins forgiven** (Mark 2:5–10). The scribes present saw at once what this meant: only God has the right to forgive sins against God.
- **他亲近被排斥的人**。他和被人看不起的税吏吃饭，触摸麻风病人，尊重妇女和外邦人，同时严厉责备外表敬虔、内心冷酷的宗教领袖。<br>
  **He drew near to people others shut out.** He ate with despised tax collectors, touched lepers, and honored women and Gentiles, while sharply rebuking religious leaders who looked devout and were cold inside.
- **他用舍己的服事来行使权柄**。当时的民众期待一位用刀剑赶走罗马人的弥赛亚。耶稣骑驴进耶路撒冷，甘愿舍命作赎价（可 10:45），第三天从死里复活。<br>
  **He used his authority to serve and to give himself.** The crowds expected a Messiah who would drive out Rome by the sword. Jesus rode into Jerusalem on a donkey, gave his life as a ransom (Mark 10:45), and rose from the dead on the third day.

耶稣留给每个读者的问题，还是他在凯撒利亚·腓立比路上问门徒的那一句：**“你们说我是谁？”**<br>
The question Jesus leaves with every reader is still the one he asked his disciples on the road to Caesarea Philippi: **"But who do you say that I am?"**
