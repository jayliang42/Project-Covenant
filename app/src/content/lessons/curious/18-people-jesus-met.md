## 尼哥底母：夜里来的老师 | Nicodemus: the teacher who came by night

尼哥底母是法利赛人，是犹太人的官，有学问，受人尊敬。他夜里来见耶稣，很客气地说：“拉比，我们知道你是由神那里来作师傅的”（约 3:2）。<br>
Nicodemus was a Pharisee and a ruler of the Jews, learned and respected. He came to Jesus at night and said politely, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher come from God” (John 3:2).

耶稣没有顺着客套话说下去。他说：“人若不重生，就不能见神的国”（约 3:3）。尼哥底母不明白：人老了怎么能再生一次？耶稣说的是从圣灵来的新生命。对一个靠学问和品行得到地位的人，耶稣说他需要的是重新开始。<br>
Jesus did not continue the polite exchange. He said, “unless one is born anew, he can’t see God’s Kingdom” (John 3:3). Nicodemus did not understand: how can a person be born when he is old? Jesus was speaking of new life from the Spirit. To a man who had gained his standing through learning and conduct, Jesus said that what he needed was a new beginning.

圣经里最有名的一句话，就出在这次谈话里：“神爱世人，甚至将他的独生子赐给他们，叫一切信他的，不致灭亡，反得永生”（约 3:16）。后来耶稣被钉十字架，尼哥底母带着香料来安葬他（约 19:39）。<br>
The best-known sentence in the Bible comes from this conversation: “God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life” (John 3:16). Later, when Jesus was crucified, Nicodemus brought spices to bury him (John 19:39).

## 井边的撒玛利亚妇人 | The Samaritan woman at the well

耶稣路过撒玛利亚，中午坐在井旁。一个妇人独自来打水。耶稣对她说：“请你给我水喝。”妇人很惊讶：“你既是犹太人，怎么向我一个撒马利亚妇人要水喝呢？”（约 4:9）<br>
Jesus was passing through Samaria and sat down by a well at noon. A woman came alone to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Give me a drink.” She was surprised: “How is it that you, being a Jew, ask for a drink from me, a Samaritan woman?” (John 4:9).

耶稣对她说：“凡喝这水的还要再渴；人若喝我所赐的水就永远不渴”（约 4:13-14）。谈话中，耶稣说出了她的情况：她有过五个丈夫，现在同住的不是她的丈夫。耶稣知道这些，仍然和她谈下去，并且第一次明说自己就是基督：“这和你说话的就是他”（约 4:26）。<br>
Jesus told her, “Everyone who drinks of this water will thirst again, but whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never thirst again” (John 4:13-14). In the conversation Jesus named her situation: she had had five husbands, and the man she now lived with was not her husband. Knowing this, Jesus went on talking with her, and here he says openly for the first time that he is the Christ: “I am he, the one who speaks to you” (John 4:26).

妇人留下水罐子，回城里对众人说：“你们来看！有一个人将我素来所行的一切事都给我说出来了。”那城里有好些撒玛利亚人信了耶稣（约 4:28-39）。<br>
The woman left her water jar, went back to the town, and told the people to come and see a man who had told her everything she ever did. Many Samaritans from that town believed in Jesus (John 4:28-39).

## 树上的撒该 | Zacchaeus in the tree

撒该是耶利哥的税吏长，很有钱。当时的税吏替罗马人收税，常常多收，同胞把他们看作叛徒和骗子。耶稣经过耶利哥，撒该想看看他，因为个子矮，就爬到一棵桑树上。<br>
Zacchaeus was the chief tax collector in Jericho and a rich man. Tax collectors of the time gathered taxes for Rome and often took extra, and their own people regarded them as traitors and cheats. When Jesus passed through Jericho, Zacchaeus wanted to see him. Being short, he climbed a sycamore tree.

耶稣走到树下，抬头说：“撒该，快下来！今天我必住在你家里”（路 19:5）。众人议论纷纷，说他竟到罪人家里去住。撒该却站起来说：“主啊，我把所有的一半给穷人；我若讹诈了谁，就还他四倍”（路 19:8）。<br>
Jesus came to the tree, looked up, and said, “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down, for today I must stay at your house” (Luke 19:5). The crowd grumbled that he had gone to be the guest of a sinner. But Zacchaeus stood and said, “Behold, Lord, half of my goods I give to the poor. If I have wrongfully exacted anything of anyone, I restore four times as much” (Luke 19:8).

耶稣说：“人子来，为要寻找、拯救失丧的人”（路 19:10）。<br>
Jesus said, “the Son of Man came to seek and to save that which was lost” (Luke 19:10).

## 三次相遇有什么共同点 | What the three meetings share

这三个人的地位、名声和处境都不一样。耶稣对有学问的人说话不客套，对被人躲着的人主动开口，对被人讨厌的人主动上门。<br>
These three differed in rank, reputation, and circumstance. With the learned man Jesus skipped the courtesies. With the woman others avoided he spoke first. With the man others hated he invited himself in.

三次相遇里，耶稣都清楚对方的情况，也都没有因此走开。他先接纳人，人的改变随后发生。<br>
In all three meetings Jesus knew the person's situation and did not turn away because of it. He welcomed the person first, and the change in the person followed.
