## 读报纸和读情诗，不会用同一种读法 | You do not read a newspaper the way you read a poem

在日常生活里，我们天然会切换阅读方式：读新闻报道时关注时间、地点和事实；读诗歌时体会意象与情感；读朋友的信件时留意写信的原因。圣经既然是一座 66 卷书的图书馆，里面同样包含多种截然不同的文体。<br>
In everyday life we switch reading modes automatically: we read a news report for facts and dates, a poem for imagery and emotion, and a personal letter for relationship and context. Because the Bible is a 66-book library, it likewise contains distinct literary genres.

按文体本身的方式去读，是读懂圣经最省力的第一步。<br>
Reading each book according to its literary genre is the simplest way to avoid confusion.

## 圣经里的六大常见文体 | Six major genres in the Bible

- **历史叙事（如《创世记》《撒母耳记》《使徒行传》）**：圣经超过四成的篇幅是故事。注意：圣经如实记录某人做过某事（Description），不等于圣经赞成我们照着去做（Prescription）。<br>
  **Historical narrative (Genesis, Samuel, Acts)**: Over forty percent of the Bible is narrative. Remember that when Scripture *describes* what a person did, it is not automatically *prescribing* that behavior as a model.
- **律法与盟约条文（如《出埃及记》后半、《申命记》）**：神在古代近东历史处境中赐给以色列民的盟约规范，用来显明神的圣洁、保护弱者并塑造敬拜群体。<br>
  **Law and covenant stipulations (late Exodus, Deuteronomy)**: Covenant terms given to ancient Israel in its historical setting to reflect God's holiness, protect the vulnerable, and shape a worshiping community.
- **诗歌（如《诗篇》《雅歌》）**：大量使用比喻、拟人与希伯来“平行句法”（上下两句意思呼应或对照）。当诗篇说“群山欢呼”时，是在表达受造界的喜乐，而不是地质学报告。<br>
  **Poetry (Psalms, Song of Songs)**: Rich in metaphor and Hebrew parallelism, where two lines echo or contrast one another. When a Psalm says "the hills sing for joy," it is lyrical praise, not a geological report.
- **智慧文学（如《箴言》《约伯记》《传道书》）**：《箴言》总结日常生活的普遍规律；而《约伯记》和《传道书》则诚实面对“为什么好人也会受苦、人算什么”的深层张力。<br>
  **Wisdom literature (Proverbs, Job, Ecclesiastes)**: Proverbs distills general patterns of wise living, while Job and Ecclesiastes wrestle honestly with innocent suffering and life's enigmas.
- **先知书与启示文学（如《以赛亚书》《阿摩司书》《启示录》）**：“先知”不只是预言未来的人，更是神圣约的代言人，责备社会不义与偶像崇拜，并在审判中宣告盼望。像《但以理书》后半与《启示录》则属于“启示文学”，用鲜明的象征画面向受压迫的信徒显明神掌管历史。<br>
  **Prophecy and apocalyptic writing (Isaiah, Amos, Revelation)**: Prophets were covenant spokespeople calling out injustice and idolatry while holding out hope. Apocalyptic texts (parts of Daniel, Revelation) use vivid symbolic visions to assure persecuted believers that God rules over history.
- **福音书与使徒书信（新约）**：四卷《福音书》聚焦于耶稣的生平、教导、受死与复活；21 封《书信》则是使徒写给各地教会或同工的牧养信件，解答具体的信仰与生活问题。<br>
  **Gospels and Epistles (New Testament)**: The four Gospels center on Jesus' life, teaching, death, and resurrection; the twenty-one Epistles are pastoral letters addressing concrete questions in early churches.
