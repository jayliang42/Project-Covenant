## 文体不同，读法也不同 | Different kinds of writing are read differently

平时我们读不同的东西，读法自然不一样：读新闻看时间、地点和事实；读诗体会意象和情感；读朋友的信会留意他为什么写。圣经有 66 卷书，里面也有好几种文体。<br>
We already read different things in different ways. We read a news report for dates, places, and facts, a poem for imagery and feeling, and a friend's letter with an eye to why it was written. The Bible's 66 books also contain several kinds of writing.

先弄清一卷书是什么文体，再照那种文体去读，会省很多力气。<br>
Working out what kind of writing a book is, and reading it that way, saves a lot of confusion.

## 六种常见文体 | Six common genres

- **历史叙事**（如《创世记》《撒母耳记》《使徒行传》）：圣经超过四成的篇幅是故事。要留意，圣经记下某人做了某事，只是记录（description），不代表赞成我们照着做（prescription）。<br>
  **Historical narrative** (Genesis, Samuel, Acts): Over forty percent of the Bible is narrative. Keep in mind that when Scripture describes what someone did, it is a record, and does not by itself prescribe that behavior for us.
- **律法与盟约条文**（如《出埃及记》后半、《申命记》）：神在古代近东的历史处境中赐给以色列民的盟约规范，用来显明神的圣洁、保护弱者、塑造敬拜的群体。<br>
  **Law and covenant terms** (the second half of Exodus, Deuteronomy): Covenant rules given to ancient Israel in its historical setting, to show God's holiness, protect the vulnerable, and shape a worshiping community.
- **诗歌**（如《诗篇》《雅歌》）：多用比喻、拟人和希伯来“平行句法”（上下两句意思呼应或对照）。诗篇说“群山欢呼”，是用诗的语言表达受造界的喜乐。<br>
  **Poetry** (Psalms, Song of Songs): Full of metaphor, personification, and Hebrew parallelism, where two lines echo or contrast with each other. When a psalm says "the hills sing for joy," it is using the language of poetry to express creation's joy.
- **智慧文学**（如《箴言》《约伯记》《传道书》）：《箴言》总结日常生活的一般规律。《约伯记》和《传道书》讨论更难的问题：好人为什么也受苦，人算什么。<br>
  **Wisdom literature** (Proverbs, Job, Ecclesiastes): Proverbs sums up the usual patterns of daily life. Job and Ecclesiastes take up harder questions: why good people also suffer, and what a human life amounts to.
- **先知书与启示文学**（如《以赛亚书》《阿摩司书》《启示录》）：先知是神圣约的代言人。他们也预言未来，但更多是在责备社会不义和偶像崇拜，并在审判中宣告盼望。《但以理书》后半和《启示录》属于“启示文学”，用象征性的画面告诉受压迫的信徒，神掌管历史。<br>
  **Prophecy and apocalyptic writing** (Isaiah, Amos, Revelation): Prophets spoke for God's covenant. They did foretell the future, but more often they called out injustice and idolatry and announced hope in the middle of judgment. The second half of Daniel and Revelation are apocalyptic writing, which uses symbolic visions to tell persecuted believers that God rules over history.
- **福音书与使徒书信**（新约）：四卷《福音书》记述耶稣的生平、教导、受死与复活。21 封《书信》是使徒写给各地教会或同工的信，回答具体的信仰和生活问题。<br>
  **Gospels and Epistles** (New Testament): The four Gospels tell of Jesus' life, teaching, death, and resurrection. The twenty-one Epistles are letters from the apostles to churches or co-workers, answering concrete questions of faith and life.
