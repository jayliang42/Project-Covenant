## 中心信息：神的国近了 | The central message: the kingdom of God is near

耶稣出来传道，中心信息是一句话：**“日期满了，神的国近了。你们当悔改，信福音！”**（可 1:15）。“神的国”（也叫“天国”）指**神仁爱、公义的掌权进入这个破碎的世界**。它的范围比“人死后去的地方”大。<br>
When Jesus began to preach, his central message was one sentence: **"The time is fulfilled, and God’s Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News"** (Mark 1:15). "The kingdom of God" (also called "the kingdom of heaven") means **God's loving and just rule entering this broken world**. It covers more than a place people go after death.

要了解耶稣的教导，可以先读两处：**《登山宝训》**（马太福音 5–7 章）和他的**比喻**。<br>
Two good places to start with Jesus' teaching are the **Sermon on the Mount** (Matthew 5–7) and his **parables**.

## 登山宝训：谁是有福的人 | The Sermon on the Mount: who is blessed

《马太福音》第 5 章开头是著名的“八福”。耶稣说有福的是这些人：**虚心的人**（直译是“灵里贫穷的人”，就是知道自己两手空空、需要神恩典的人）、哀恸的人、温柔的人、饥渴慕义的人、怜恤人的人、清心的人、使人和睦的人。这和人们通常羡慕的强者、成功者很不一样。<br>
Matthew 5 opens with the well-known Beatitudes. Jesus says these are the blessed: **the poor in spirit** (those who know they come to God with empty hands and need his grace), those who mourn, the meek, those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, and the peacemakers. This is very different from the strong and successful whom people usually envy.

接着，耶稣把旧约律法讲到人的内心。他说，手没有杀人还不够，向弟兄动怒、骂弟兄是蠢货的人也要受审判。他教人在暗中向天父祷告，不要站在街角做给人看。他还教人为逼迫自己的人祷告。<br>
Jesus then takes the Old Testament law down to the heart. He says it is not enough that your hands have not killed. Anyone who is angry with a brother and calls him a fool will also face judgment. He teaches people to pray to the Father in secret and not on street corners to be seen. He also teaches them to pray for those who persecute them.

## 比喻：用日常的事讲道 | Parables: teaching through everyday things

耶稣常用当时的日常事情讲道：农夫撒种、妇人找钱币、牧人找迷羊、父亲和儿子。最著名的是《路加福音》15 章**两个儿子（浪子回头）**的比喻：<br>
Jesus often taught through everyday things of his time: a farmer sowing, a woman searching for a coin, a shepherd looking for a lost sheep, a father and his sons. The best known is the parable of **the two sons (the Prodigal Son)** in Luke 15:

- **小儿子**离家挥霍，代表公然远离神、把人生弄糟的人。他满心羞愧往回走，父亲“相离还远”就跑过去抱住他。<br>
  **The younger son** leaves home and wastes his inheritance. He stands for people who openly turn from God and wreck their lives. As he walks home in shame, his father runs to embrace him "while he was still far off."
- **大儿子**留在家里守规矩，心里却把父亲当雇主、把弟弟当外人，代表靠道德记录自夸、却不明白父亲心意的人。<br>
  **The older son** stays home and keeps the rules, but in his heart he treats his father as an employer and his brother as a stranger. He stands for people who boast in their moral record and do not understand the father's heart.

故事结尾，父亲走到门外，劝大儿子进屋一同欢喜。耶稣借这个故事邀请每一个听的人：走远了的小儿子和在家算账的大儿子，都可以回到天父的筵席上。<br>
At the end, the father goes outside and urges the older son to come in and celebrate. Through this story Jesus invites everyone who hears it: the younger son who went far away and the older son keeping accounts at home may both come to the Father's feast.
