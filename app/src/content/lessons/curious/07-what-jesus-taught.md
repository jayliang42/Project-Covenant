## 中心主题：神的国近了 | The central theme: The Kingdom of God

耶稣出来传道时，最核心的信息浓缩在一句话里：**“神的国近了，你们应当悔改，信福音！”**（可 1:15）。这里的“天国/神的国”不是指死后才去的某个地理空间，而是指**神仁爱、公义的掌权闯进了这个破碎的世界**。<br>
When Jesus began his public ministry, his core message fit into one sentence: **"The Kingdom of God is at hand! Repent, and believe in the good news"** (Mark 1:15). In the Gospels, "the Kingdom of God" is not merely a place you go after death; it is **God's good and rightful reign breaking into this broken world**.

要读懂耶稣的教导，最好的两个窗口就是**《登山宝训》**（马太福音 5–7 章）和他的**比喻**。<br>
Two of the clearest windows into Jesus' teaching are the **Sermon on the Mount** (Matthew 5–7) and his **parables**.

## 登山宝训：颠倒过来的有福清单 | The Sermon on the Mount: An upside-down list of blessing

在《马太福音》第 5 章开头（著名的“八福”），耶稣宣告谁是有福的人：不是趾高气扬、把别人踩在脚下的人，而是**虚心的人**（直译是“灵里贫穷的人”——知道自己两手空空、需要神恩典的人）、哀恸的人、温柔的人、饥渴慕义的人、怜恤人的人、清心的人、使人和睦的人。<br>
At the opening of Matthew 5 (the Beatitudes), Jesus names who is truly blessed: not the ruthless or self-sufficient, but **the poor in spirit** (those who know they come to God empty-handed), those who mourn, the meek, those who hunger for righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, and the peacemakers.

接着，耶稣把旧约律法带到内心深处：真正的问题不只是“手有没有杀人”，还有“心里有没有把弟兄当成蠢货来恨”；真正的敬虔不是站在街角表演给人看，而是在暗中向天父祷告、甚至为逼迫你的人代求。<br>
Jesus then presses beyond outward compliance to the heart: the issue is not only whether your hands have committed murder, but whether your heart nurses contempt; true devotion is not performing on street corners for applause, but praying to the Father in secret and even loving your enemies.

## 耶稣的比喻：用日常故事照亮人心 | The parables: Everyday stories that search the heart

耶稣很喜欢用当时的农夫撒种、妇人找钱币、牧人找迷羊、父子关系等日常故事来讲道。最著名的一则是《路加福音》15 章**“两个儿子（浪子回头）”**的比喻：<br>
Jesus loved teaching through everyday stories of farmers, lost coins, shepherds, and families. Perhaps the best known is the parable of **the two sons (the Prodigal Son)** in Luke 15:

- **小儿子**离家挥霍，代表那些公然远离神、把人生搞砸的人；当他带着羞愧往回走时，父亲“相离还远”就跑过去抱住他。<br>
  **The younger son** squanders his inheritance far from home, picturing those who rebel openly; yet while he is still far off, his father runs to embrace him.
- **大儿子**留在家里守规矩，却在心里把父亲当雇主、把弟弟当外人，代表那些靠道德记录自夸、却不认识父亲心肠的人。<br>
  **The older son** obeys every rule at home yet resents his father's generosity, picturing those who trust their own moral resume while remaining cold at heart.

在故事结尾，父亲走出门外劝大儿子进屋一同欢喜——耶稣借此邀请每一个听众：无论你是走远的小儿子，还是算账的大儿子，都可以回到天父的筵席里。<br>
At the end of the story, the father steps outside to plead with the older brother too — Jesus' open invitation to both kinds of people to come into the Father's joy.
