## 从哪一卷开始 | Which book to start with

圣经不必从创世记第一页一直读到启示录最后一页。第一次读，可以按这个次序：<br>
You do not have to read the Bible from the first page of Genesis to the last page of Revelation. For a first reading, try this order:

1. **一卷福音书**：马可福音最短，16 章；路加福音写得最有次序。福音书讲耶稣的一生，整本圣经都围绕着他。<br>
   **A Gospel**: Mark is the shortest, at 16 chapters; Luke is the most orderly. The Gospels tell the life of Jesus, and the whole Bible centres on him.
2. **使徒行传**：接着福音书，讲教会怎样开始、福音怎样从耶路撒冷传到罗马。<br>
   **Acts**: It follows the Gospels and tells how the church began and how the gospel spread from Jerusalem to Rome.
3. **创世记**：回到开头，看世界、人和以色列的来历。<br>
   **Genesis**: Go back to the beginning and see where the world, humanity, and Israel came from.
4. **诗篇**：每天读一篇，学习怎样向神说话。<br>
   **Psalms**: Read one a day and learn how to speak to God.

## 怎样读 | How to read

- **每次读一段**：一章左右就够了。读完问自己三个问题：这段说了什么？它说明神是怎样的？它和我有什么关系？<br>
  **Read one passage at a time**: About a chapter is enough. Afterwards ask yourself three questions: What does this passage say? What does it show about God? What does it have to do with me?
- **把不懂的记下来**：不必每处都马上弄明白。很多问题读到后面自然就清楚了。<br>
  **Write down what you don't understand**: You need not settle every question at once. Many become clear as you read on.
- **读完整段，不要只读一节**：单独读一节经文，很容易误解它的意思。<br>
  **Read whole passages, not single verses**: A verse read on its own is easily misunderstood.
- **选一本读得懂的译本**：和合本是华人教会最常用的，文字比较古雅。如果觉得难读，可以对照新译本、和合本修订版或英文译本。<br>
  **Choose a translation you can understand**: The Union Version is the most widely used in Chinese churches and its language is somewhat old-fashioned. If you find it hard, compare it with the Chinese New Version, the Revised Chinese Union Version, or an English translation.

## 读不懂的时候 | When you don't understand

圣经里有难懂的地方，这很正常，连彼得也说保罗的信里“有些难明白的”（彼后 3:16）。可以：<br>
The Bible has hard places, and that is normal. Even Peter said there are things in Paul's letters “that are hard to understand” (2 Peter 3:16). You can:

- 看看这段经文前后讲什么。<br>
  Look at what comes before and after the passage.
- 查这个 App 里的“资料库”，那里有关于各卷书、人物和地点的资料。<br>
  Look in the library section of this app, which has material on the books, people, and places of the Bible.
- 找一位读圣经比较久的朋友一起读，或者参加一个读经小组。<br>
  Read with a friend who has read the Bible longer, or join a Bible study group.

《使徒行传》说，庇哩亚的人“甘心领受这道，天天考查圣经，要晓得这道是与不是”（徒 17:11）。愿意听，也自己去查证，这样读圣经是好的。<br>
Acts says the people of Berea “received the word with all readiness of mind, examining the Scriptures daily to see whether these things were so” (Acts 17:11). To be willing to listen and to check for yourself is a good way to read.
