## 四个讲“神做了什么”的词 | Four words about what God has done

**1. 福音**。希腊文 euangelion，意思是“好消息”。在新约里，它指的是一个消息：神借着耶稣的死和复活，为人开了一条回到他面前的路。耶稣开始传道时说：“你们当悔改，信福音”（可 1:15）。<br>
**1. Gospel.** The Greek euangelion means “good news.” In the New Testament it refers to an announcement: through the death and resurrection of Jesus, God has opened the way for people to come back to him. When Jesus began to preach he said, “Repent, and believe in the Good News” (Mark 1:15).

**2. 恩典**。希腊文 charis，意思是“白白给的恩惠”。恩典是人没有资格得、神却白白给的。保罗说：“你们得救是本乎恩，也因着信；这并不是出于自己，乃是神所赐的”（弗 2:8）。<br>
**2. Grace.** The Greek charis means “favour freely given.” Grace is what people have no claim to and God gives anyway. Paul says, “by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God” (Ephesians 2:8).

**3. 救恩，得救**。意思是被救出来。圣经说人需要从罪和罪的后果里被救出来，这件事人自己做不到。<br>
**3. Salvation, being saved.** The word means being rescued. The Bible says people need rescue from sin and its results, and that they cannot do this for themselves.

**4. 基督，弥赛亚。**“基督”来自希腊文 Christos，“弥赛亚”来自希伯来文，两个词的意思都是“受膏者”。古代以色列人立王的时候，用油浇在他头上。所以“耶稣基督”的意思是“耶稣是神所立的那位王”。“基督”是称号，不是姓。<br>
**4. Christ, Messiah.** “Christ” comes from the Greek Christos and “Messiah” from the Hebrew, and both mean “anointed one.” When ancient Israel made someone king, oil was poured on his head. So “Jesus Christ” means “Jesus is the king God has appointed.” “Christ” is a title and not a surname.

## 三个讲“人的情况和回应”的词 | Three words about our condition and response

**5. 罪**。中文的“罪”容易让人想到犯法坐牢。圣经里的罪范围更大。新约最常用的那个希腊词 hamartia，原意是“没有射中目标”。圣经说：“世人都犯了罪，亏缺了神的荣耀”（罗 3:23）。罪指人偏离了神，包括做错的事，也包括心里不要神。<br>
**5. Sin.** The Chinese word for sin easily suggests crime and prison. In the Bible sin is wider. The most common New Testament word for it, the Greek hamartia, originally meant “missing the mark.” Scripture says, “all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God” (Romans 3:23). Sin is a turning away from God. It includes wrong acts, and it includes a heart that does not want God.

**6. 悔改**。希腊文 metanoia，意思是“心思转变”。悔改是转过身来，离开原来的方向，回到神那里。它比后悔多了一步：后悔的人可能停在难过里，悔改的人转了方向。<br>
**6. Repentance.** The Greek metanoia means “a change of mind.” To repent is to turn around, leave the old direction, and come back to God. It goes a step beyond regret. A person who regrets may stay in the sorrow, while a person who repents changes direction.

**7. 信，信心**。希腊文 pistis，意思是“信任”“信靠”。圣经讲的“信”，包括知道一件事、认同它是真的，并且把自己交托给神。它很像你坐上一把椅子，把全身的重量放上去。<br>
**7. Faith, believing.** The Greek pistis means “trust” or “reliance.” Faith in the Bible includes knowing something, agreeing that it is true, and entrusting yourself to God. It is much like sitting on a chair and putting your whole weight on it.

## 三个聚会里常听到的词 | Three words you hear in worship

**8. 阿们**。希伯来文，意思是“确实如此”“诚心所愿”。祷告结束时说“阿们”，表示“我也这样求”。<br>
**8. Amen.** A Hebrew word meaning “so it is” or “let it be so.” Saying “Amen” at the end of a prayer means “I ask this too.”

**9. 哈利路亚**。希伯来文，意思是“你们要赞美耶和华”。《诗篇》里常常用它开头或结尾。<br>
**9. Hallelujah.** A Hebrew phrase meaning “praise Yahweh.” Many psalms begin or end with it.

**10. 和散那**。希伯来文，原来的意思是“求你拯救”，后来成了欢呼称颂的话。耶稣进耶路撒冷的时候，众人就这样喊。<br>
**10. Hosanna.** A Hebrew phrase that first meant “save, we pray,” and later became a shout of praise. The crowd shouted it when Jesus entered Jerusalem.

## 把这些词连起来 | Putting the words together

用这些词可以把基督信仰的内容说成一段话：人都犯了**罪**，离开了神。神出于**恩典**，差**基督**来，为人死而复活，这就是**福音**。人**悔改**、**信**靠他，就**得救**。<br>
With these words the content of the Christian faith can be put in one paragraph. All people have **sinned** and turned from God. Out of **grace** God sent the **Christ**, who died for people and rose again, and this is the **gospel**. Those who **repent** and put their **faith** in him are **saved**.
