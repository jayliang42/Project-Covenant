## 以色列要一个王 | Israel wants a king

以色列人进入迦南以后，很长一段时间没有王，由“士师”带领。《士师记》反复说：“那时，以色列中没有王，各人任意而行”（士 21:25）。后来百姓对先知撒母耳说：“求你为我们立一个王治理我们，像列国一样”（撒上 8:5）。<br>
After Israel entered Canaan, for a long time they had no king and were led by “judges.” The book of Judges keeps saying, “In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes” (Judges 21:25). Later the people said to the prophet Samuel, “Make us a king to judge us like all the nations” (1 Samuel 8:5).

第一个王是扫罗，他后来离弃了神的话。第二个王是大卫。大卫定都耶路撒冷，神借先知拿单应许他：他的后裔中会有一位，神要“坚定他的国位，直到永远”（撒下 7:13）。大卫也犯了大罪，他夺了别人的妻子，又害死了她的丈夫。圣经把这件事和他的悔改都写了下来（撒下 11–12 章，诗 51 篇）。<br>
The first king was Saul, who later turned away from God's word. The second was David. David made Jerusalem his capital, and through the prophet Nathan God promised that one of his descendants would reign and God would “establish the throne of his kingdom forever” (2 Samuel 7:13). David also sinned gravely. He took another man's wife and had her husband killed. The Bible records this and his repentance (2 Samuel 11–12; Psalm 51).

大卫的儿子所罗门在耶路撒冷建了圣殿。可是他晚年拜别的神，他死后国家就分裂了：北方十个支派成为“以色列”，南方成为“犹大”（王上 12 章）。这大约发生在主前 930 年。<br>
David's son Solomon built the temple in Jerusalem. But in his later years he worshipped other gods, and after his death the nation split. The ten northern tribes became “Israel,” and the south became “Judah” (1 Kings 12). This happened around 930 BC.

## 先知在说什么 | What the prophets said

两个国家的王，大多数没有跟随神。神不断差遣先知去对他们说话，例如以利亚、阿摩司、何西阿、以赛亚、耶利米。<br>
Most of the kings of both nations did not follow God. God kept sending prophets to speak to them, such as Elijah, Amos, Hosea, Isaiah, and Jeremiah.

先知的话大多有两部分。一部分是责备：百姓拜偶像，富人欺压穷人，法庭不公正。弥迦说：“他向你所要的是什么呢？只要你行公义，好怜悯，存谦卑的心，与你的神同行”（弥 6:8）。另一部分是应许：神要兴起一位大卫的后裔作王（赛 9:6-7），还要与百姓另立新约，把律法写在他们心上（耶 31:33）。<br>
Most of what the prophets said had two parts. One part was rebuke: the people worshipped idols, the rich oppressed the poor, and the courts were unjust. Micah said, “What does Yahweh require of you, but to act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?” (Micah 6:8). The other part was promise: God would raise up a descendant of David as king (Isaiah 9:6–7), and he would make a new covenant with his people and write his law on their hearts (Jeremiah 31:33).

## 被掳和归回 | Exile and return

主前 722 年，亚述灭了北国以色列。主前 586 年，巴比伦攻陷耶路撒冷，烧毁圣殿，把许多犹大人带到巴比伦。《诗篇》137 篇写的就是这时候的心情：“我们曾在巴比伦的河边坐下，一追想锡安就哭了。”<br>
In 722 BC Assyria destroyed the northern kingdom of Israel. In 586 BC Babylon captured Jerusalem, burned the temple, and took many people of Judah to Babylon. Psalm 137 captures how they felt: “By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down. Yes, we wept, when we remembered Zion.”

波斯灭了巴比伦以后，波斯王塞鲁士（Cyrus）在主前 538 年准许犹太人回乡（拉 1:1-4）。他们重建了圣殿，后来尼希米又带领百姓修好了耶路撒冷的城墙。<br>
After Persia conquered Babylon, the Persian king Cyrus allowed the Jews to go home in 538 BC (Ezra 1:1–4). They rebuilt the temple, and later Nehemiah led the people in rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem.

## 旧约结束时 | Where the Old Testament ends

百姓回到了家乡，可是先知应许的那位君王还没有来，他们仍然在外国的统治之下。旧约最后一卷《玛拉基书》说，主要差遣使者在他前面预备道路（玛 3:1）。新约一开头，就是施洗约翰出来预备道路，然后耶稣登场。<br>
The people were back in their land, but the king the prophets had promised had not yet come, and they were still ruled by foreign powers. Malachi, the last book of the Old Testament, says the Lord will send his messenger to prepare the way before him (Malachi 3:1). The New Testament opens with John the Baptist preparing the way, and then Jesus appears.
