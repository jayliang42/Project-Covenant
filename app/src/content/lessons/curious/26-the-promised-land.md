## 进入迦南 | Entering Canaan

摩西死后，神对约书亚说：“你当刚强壮胆！不要惧怕，也不要惊惶；因为你无论往哪里去，耶和华你的神必与你同在”（书 1:9）。约书亚带领以色列人过约旦河，进入神应许给亚伯拉罕的迦南地。约书亚记把土地分给各支派，并说：“耶和华应许赐福给以色列家的话一句也没有落空，都应验了”（书 21:45）。<br>
After Moses died, God said to Joshua, “Be strong and courageous. Don't be afraid. Don't be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go” (Joshua 1:9). Joshua led Israel across the Jordan into Canaan, the land God had promised to Abraham. The book of Joshua divides the land among the tribes and says, “Nothing failed of any good thing which Yahweh had spoken to the house of Israel. All came to pass” (Joshua 21:45).

约书亚记里有很多战争的记载，今天的读者常常读不下去。“认真思考信仰”路径里“旧约里的战争和审判”一课专门讨论这个问题。<br>
Joshua contains many accounts of war, which readers today often find hard. The lesson on war and judgment in the Old Testament, in the Seeking path, takes up this question.

约书亚晚年召集百姓，请他们选择：“今日就可以选择所要事奉的……至于我和我家，我们必定事奉耶和华”（书 24:15）。<br>
In his old age Joshua gathered the people and asked them to choose: “choose today whom you will serve... but as for me and my house, we will serve Yahweh” (Joshua 24:15).

## 士师时代：一再重复的循环 | The judges: a repeating cycle

约书亚死后，以色列人没有一位统一的领袖。士师记描写了一个一再重复的循环：百姓离开神，去拜迦南的神明；神让外族欺压他们；他们哀求；神兴起一位“士师”（拯救者和领袖）拯救他们；士师一死，他们又离开神，“比他们列祖更甚”（士 2:18-19）。<br>
After Joshua died, Israel had no single leader. Judges describes a cycle repeated again and again: the people turn from God to the gods of Canaan; God lets foreign nations oppress them; they cry out; God raises up a “judge” (a deliverer and leader) to save them; when the judge dies they turn back and deal “more corruptly than their fathers” (Judges 2:18-19).

士师里有底波拉、基甸、参孙等人。他们并不完美，有的问题很大，但神仍然用他们拯救百姓。全书越到后面越黑暗，最后一句是：“那时，以色列中没有王，各人任意而行”（士 21:25）。这句话为后来的君王时代做了铺垫。<br>
The judges include Deborah, Gideon, and Samson. They were far from perfect, and some had serious flaws, yet God used them to rescue his people. The book grows darker toward the end, closing with, “In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes” (Judges 21:25). This sets the stage for the age of the kings.

## 路得：黑暗中的一线光 | Ruth: a light in the darkness

路得记的故事发生在“士师秉政的时候”（得 1:1）。一个以色列家庭因饥荒搬到摩押，丈夫和两个儿子都死了。寡妇拿俄米要回乡，她的摩押儿媳路得坚持跟随她：“你往哪里去，我也往那里去……你的国就是我的国，你的神就是我的神”（得 1:16）。<br>
Ruth's story takes place “in the days when the judges judged” (Ruth 1:1). An Israelite family moved to Moab because of famine, and the husband and both sons died. When the widow Naomi decided to return home, her Moabite daughter-in-law Ruth insisted on going with her: “where you go, I will go... Your people will be my people, and your God my God” (Ruth 1:16).

在伯利恒，路得去田里拾麦穗，遇见了拿俄米的亲属波阿斯。波阿斯按律法作“至近的亲属”，娶了路得，保住了这个家。他们的儿子俄备得，“是耶西的父，耶西是大卫的父”（得 4:17）。<br>
In Bethlehem Ruth gleaned in the fields and met Boaz, a relative of Naomi. Acting as the “near kinsman” under the law, Boaz married Ruth and preserved the family. Their son Obed “is the father of Jesse, the father of David” (Ruth 4:17).

在“各人任意而行”的年代，路得记讲的是忠诚、恩慈和神安静的看顾。一个外邦女子，成了以色列最伟大的君王的曾祖母。马太福音的耶稣家谱里也写着她的名字（太 1:5）。<br>
In an age when “everyone did that which was right in his own eyes,” Ruth tells of loyalty, kindness, and God's quiet care. A foreign woman became the great-grandmother of Israel's greatest king. Her name also appears in Matthew's genealogy of Jesus (Matthew 1:5).
