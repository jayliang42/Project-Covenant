## 圣经怎样结尾 | How the Bible ends

圣经第一章是“起初，神创造天地”。最后两章，约翰看见“一个新天新地；因为先前的天地已经过去了”（启 21:1）。故事从创造开始，到新的创造结束。<br>
The Bible's first chapter begins, “In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.” In the last two chapters John sees “a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth have passed away” (Revelation 21:1). The story begins with creation and ends with a new creation.

很多人想象的天堂，是死后灵魂离开地球，到云上去。《启示录》写的是圣城“由神那里从天而降”，有声音说：“神的帐幕在人间。他要与人同住，他们要作他的子民”（启 21:2-3）。<br>
Many people picture heaven as souls leaving the earth after death and going up into the clouds. In Revelation the holy city comes “down out of heaven from God,” and a voice says, “God’s dwelling is with people, and he will dwell with them, and they will be his people” (Revelation 21:2–3).

## 那时会是什么样子 | What it will be like

圣经讲的细节不多，下面是它讲到的几件事：<br>
The Bible gives few details. Here are some things it does say:

- **没有死亡和眼泪**：“神要擦去他们一切的眼泪；不再有死亡，也不再有悲哀、哭号、疼痛”（启 21:4）。<br>
  **No death and no tears**: “He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more; neither will there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain, any more” (Revelation 21:4).
- **没有战争**：先知以赛亚说，到那日，“他们要将刀打成犁头，把枪打成镰刀”（赛 2:4）。<br>
  **No war**: The prophet Isaiah says that in that day, “They shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruning hooks” (Isaiah 2:4).
- **身体复活**：保罗说，耶稣要把“我们这卑贱的身体改变形状，和他自己荣耀的身体相似”（腓 3:21）。基督徒的盼望包括身体。<br>
  **Bodily resurrection**: Paul says Jesus “will change the body of our humiliation to be conformed to the body of his glory” (Philippians 3:21). Christian hope includes the body.
- **生命树又出现了**：城里有生命水的河，河两边有生命树，“树上的叶子乃为医治万民”（启 22:2）。在《创世记》里，人离开了有生命树的园子。在《启示录》里，生命树又出现了。<br>
  **The tree of life again**: In the city flows the river of the water of life, with the tree of life on both banks, and “the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations” (Revelation 22:2). In Genesis, people left the garden where the tree of life grew. In Revelation, the tree of life appears again.

## 这对今天有什么意义 | What this means for today

保罗说：“我想，现在的苦楚若比起将来要显于我们的荣耀就不足介意了”（罗 8:18）。保罗承认现在有苦楚，他也相信苦楚以后有荣耀。<br>
Paul writes, “I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which will be revealed toward us” (Romans 8:18). Paul admits that suffering is real now, and he believes glory will follow it.

因为神要更新这个世界，基督徒今天照顾穷人、医治病人、做好手里的工作，都有长远的意义。这些事都和神最后要做的事方向一致。<br>
Because God means to renew this world, the things Christians do today, caring for the poor, healing the sick, doing their work well, all have lasting worth. They point in the same direction as what God will finally do.

耶稣什么时候再来，他自己说：“那日子，那时辰，没有人知道……惟独父知道”（太 24:36）。圣经最后一句祷告是：“主耶稣啊，我愿你来！”（启 22:20）。<br>
As for when Jesus will return, he himself said, “No one knows of that day and hour ... but my Father only” (Matthew 24:36). The Bible's last prayer is, “Yes, come, Lord Jesus” (Revelation 22:20).
