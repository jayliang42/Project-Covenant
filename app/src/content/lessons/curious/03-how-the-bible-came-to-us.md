## 第一步：书写与正典汇聚 | Step 1: Writing and gathering the canon

圣经的各卷书最初写在莎草纸（papyrus）或羊皮卷（parchment）上。旧约主要用**希伯来文**（其中《但以理书》和《以斯拉记》有几章用亚兰文）写成；新约则用第一世纪地中海世界通行的**通用希腊文（Koine Greek）**写成，方便在各城之间传阅。<br>
The books of the Bible were first written on papyrus scrolls and parchment sheets. The Old Testament was written mainly in **Hebrew** (with a few chapters of Ezra and Daniel in Aramaic); the New Testament was written in **Koine Greek**, the shared language of the first-century Mediterranean world.

“正典”（Canon，意为“准绳”或“书目清单”）不是某一次会议凭空发明的，而是信仰群体在历史中承认并汇集那些具有先知与使徒权柄、在敬拜中被长期诵读的书卷。<br>
The "canon" (meaning "measuring rod" or "recognized list") was not invented out of thin air by a late committee; rather, worshiping communities recognized and preserved the prophetic and apostolic writings that had been read and handed down from the beginning.

## 第二步：手抄时代的保存与核对 | Step 2: Copying and checking manuscripts

在印刷术出现之前，圣经靠手工抄写流传。犹太传统中的**马所拉文士（Masoretes）**在抄写希伯来旧约时极其严谨，甚至会统计字母、词频与中间字，以防漏抄；而新约则因为早期基督徒在罗马帝国各地广泛传抄，留下了数千份希腊文抄本与更早的古译本。<br>
Before the printing press, Scripture was transmitted by hand. Jewish **Masoretic scribes** copied the Hebrew Bible with extraordinary care, counting words and letters to catch slips; meanwhile, early Christians copied the New Testament across the Mediterranean, leaving thousands of Greek manuscripts and early translations.

1947 年在死海昆兰洞穴发现的**死海古卷（Dead Sea Scrolls）**，把我们能看到的希伯来圣经抄本年代一下子往前推了一千多年（到主前 3 世纪至主后 1 世纪），让学者能够直接比对千年间的文本稳定性与差异。<br>
The discovery of the **Dead Sea Scrolls** at Qumran in 1947 pushed surviving Hebrew biblical manuscripts back by more than a thousand years (to the 3rd century BC – 1st century AD), allowing scholars to compare ancient and medieval copies directly.

## 第三步：从原文到你手中的中英文译本 | Step 3: From ancient texts to your Chinese and English Bibles

今天你打开的中文或英文圣经，不是“二手转译”，而是圣经学者直接根据希伯来文、亚兰文和希腊文校勘底本翻译而成的：<br>
Modern Chinese and English Bibles are not translations of translations; scholars translate them directly from critical editions of the Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts:

- **偏重字面对应（Formal equivalence）**：尽量保留原文的句式和词序，适合细读比对。<br>
  **Word-for-word (formal equivalence)**: Preserves original phrasing and structure as closely as possible for close study.
- **偏重意义通顺（Dynamic equivalence）**：更注重用现代自然的表达传达原意，适合初读与通读。<br>
  **Thought-for-thought (dynamic equivalence)**: Aims for natural phrasing in the target language so first-time readers can follow the flow easily.

这也是为什么本应用支持**中文 / EN / 中英对照**：两种语言对照着读，常常能一眼看清单语容易忽略的细节。<br>
That is why this app offers **中文 / EN / Bilingual** modes: reading two languages side by side often illuminates nuances a single translation cannot capture alone.
