## 书写与正典 | Writing and the canon

圣经各卷最初写在莎草纸（papyrus）或羊皮（parchment）上。旧约主要用**希伯来文**写成，《但以理书》和《以斯拉记》有几章用亚兰文。新约用**通用希腊文（Koine Greek）**写成，这是第一世纪地中海世界通行的语言，方便在各城之间传阅。<br>
The books of the Bible were first written on papyrus and parchment. The Old Testament was written mainly in **Hebrew**, with a few chapters of Daniel and Ezra in Aramaic. The New Testament was written in **Koine Greek**, the shared language of the first-century Mediterranean world, which made it easy to pass from city to city.

“正典”（Canon）原意是“准绳”，也指公认的书目。这份书目是在历史中逐渐确定的：信仰群体承认并汇集了那些带有先知和使徒权柄、在敬拜中长期诵读的书卷。没有哪一次会议凭空定出过这份书目。<br>
"Canon" means "measuring rod," and also the recognized list of books. The list took shape over time, as worshiping communities recognized and gathered the writings that carried prophetic and apostolic authority and had long been read in worship. No single council made it up from nothing.

## 手抄与核对 | Copying and checking

印刷术出现之前，圣经靠手抄流传。犹太传统中的**马所拉文士（Masoretes）**抄写希伯来旧约时非常严谨，会统计字母、词频和中间字，防止漏抄。新约由早期基督徒在罗马帝国各地传抄，留下了数千份希腊文抄本和早期译本。<br>
Before printing, Scripture was passed on by hand. Jewish **Masoretic scribes** copied the Hebrew Bible with great care, counting letters and words and marking the middle word to catch omissions. Early Christians copied the New Testament across the Roman Empire and left thousands of Greek manuscripts and early translations.

1947 年，在死海边的昆兰洞穴发现了**死海古卷（Dead Sea Scrolls）**。它把现存希伯来圣经抄本的年代往前推了一千多年，到主前 3 世纪至主后 1 世纪。学者因此可以直接比较相隔千年的抄本，看文本有多稳定、有哪些差异。<br>
In 1947 the **Dead Sea Scrolls** were found in caves at Qumran, by the Dead Sea. They pushed the age of surviving Hebrew biblical manuscripts back by more than a thousand years, to the 3rd century BC through the 1st century AD. Scholars can now compare copies made a thousand years apart and see how stable the text is and where it differs.

## 翻译成中文和英文 | Translation into Chinese and English

近几十年出版的主要中英文译本，多数是学者根据希伯来文、亚兰文和希腊文的校勘本直接翻译的。最常用的中文《和合本》（1919）情况不同：它以英文《修订本》（English Revised Version）为基础，再用原文核对。翻译大致有两种取向：<br>
Most major Chinese and English translations of recent decades were made by scholars directly from critical editions of the Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts. The widely used Chinese Union Version (1919) is different: it took the English Revised Version as its base and checked it against the original languages. Translations lean one of two ways:

- **偏重字面对应（Formal equivalence）**：尽量保留原文的句式和词序，适合细读和比对。<br>
  **Word-for-word (formal equivalence)**: Keeps the original phrasing and word order as far as possible. Good for close study and comparison.
- **偏重意思通顺（Dynamic equivalence）**：用自然的现代语言表达原意，适合初读和通读。<br>
  **Thought-for-thought (dynamic equivalence)**: Puts the meaning into natural modern language. Good for a first reading or reading straight through.

本应用提供**中文 / EN / 中英对照**三种模式。两种语言对照着读，常能看到只读一种时容易忽略的细节。<br>
This app offers three modes: **中文 / EN / Bilingual**. Reading two languages side by side often shows details that are easy to miss in one.
