## 神迹有哪几类 | The kinds of miracles

四卷福音书一共记载了三十多个耶稣所行的神迹，大致可以分成四类：<br>
The four Gospels record more than thirty miracles of Jesus. They fall roughly into four groups:

- **医治疾病**：瞎眼的、瘸腿的、长大麻风的、瘫痪的、患血漏的。<br>
  **Healing illness**: people who were blind, lame, leprous, paralysed, or suffering from bleeding.
- **赶出污鬼**：使被鬼附的人恢复清醒。<br>
  **Driving out unclean spirits**: restoring people who were possessed to their right mind.
- **掌管自然**：平静风浪，在水面上行走，用五个饼两条鱼喂饱五千人。<br>
  **Power over nature**: stilling a storm, walking on water, and feeding five thousand people with five loaves and two fish.
- **使死人复活**：睚鲁的女儿，拿因城寡妇的儿子，拉撒路。<br>
  **Raising the dead**: Jairus's daughter, the son of the widow of Nain, and Lazarus.

## 神迹是记号 | Miracles are signs

《约翰福音》把神迹叫作“记号”。记号的作用是指向别的东西。约翰在书的结尾说：“记这些事要叫你们信耶稣是基督，是神的儿子，并且叫你们信了他，就可以因他的名得生命”（约 20:31）。<br>
John's Gospel calls the miracles “signs.” A sign points beyond itself. Near the end of the book John says, “these are written, that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name” (John 20:31).

所以福音书记载神迹，重点在于神迹说明了耶稣是谁。旧约的先知说过，神来拯救他百姓的时候，瞎子的眼要睁开，瘸子要跳跃。施洗约翰派人来问耶稣是不是那位要来的，耶稣回答说：“瞎子看见，瘸子行走，长大痲疯的洁净，聋子听见，死人复活，穷人有福音传给他们”（路 7:22）。<br>
So when the Gospels record miracles, the point is what the miracles show about who Jesus is. The Old Testament prophets had said that when God came to save his people, the eyes of the blind would be opened and the lame would leap. When John the Baptist sent to ask whether Jesus was the one to come, Jesus answered that “the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them” (Luke 7:22).

## 神迹也显出他的怜悯 | Miracles also show his compassion

有一个长大麻风的人来求耶稣说：“你若肯，必能叫我洁净了。”福音书说：“耶稣动了慈心，就伸手摸他，说：我肯，你洁净了吧”（可 1:40-41）。按当时的规矩，麻风病人必须和别人隔开，没有人会去碰他们。耶稣本可以只说一句话，他却伸手摸了这个人。<br>
A man with leprosy comes and begs Jesus, “If you want to, you can make me clean.” The Gospel says, “Being moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand, and touched him, and said to him, ‘I want to. Be made clean’” (Mark 1:40-41). By the rules of the time people with leprosy had to live apart, and no one would touch them. Jesus could have spoken a word only. He reached out and touched the man.

福音书里的神迹大多是这样：对象是病人、穷人、外邦人、被人看不起的人。耶稣没有用神迹为自己谋好处，也拒绝了为表演而行神迹的要求（太 12:38-39）。<br>
Most of the miracles in the Gospels are like this. They are done for the sick, the poor, foreigners, and people who were looked down on. Jesus did not use miracles for his own benefit, and he refused requests to perform them as a show (Matthew 12:38-39).

## 人们怎样看这些记载 | How people regard these accounts

- **当时反对耶稣的人**：福音书记载，他们没有否认耶稣行了这些事，只是说他是靠鬼王赶鬼（太 12:24）。<br>
  **Jesus' opponents at the time**: According to the Gospels, they did not deny that he did these things. They said he drove out demons by the ruler of the demons (Matthew 12:24).
- **不相信的人**：很多人认为神迹不可能发生，这些故事是后来加上去的，或者有自然的解释。这种看法的前提是：自然规律不会被打破。<br>
  **Sceptics**: Many people hold that miracles cannot happen, and that these stories were added later or have a natural explanation. This view assumes that the laws of nature are never broken.
- **基督徒**：相信创造世界的神能在世界里行事，所以神迹是可能的。他们也指出，福音书成书的时候，还有见过这些事的人活着。<br>
  **Christians**: They believe that the God who made the world can act within it, so miracles are possible. They also point out that the Gospels were written while people who had seen these events were still alive.

至于今天还有没有这样的神迹，基督徒当中也有不同的看法。五旬节派和灵恩派的教会认为，医病等神迹今天仍然经常发生。另一些教会认为，这类神迹主要是为了在使徒时代证实福音，后来就很少了。两方都相信神垂听祷告，也都为病人祷告。<br>
Christians also differ on whether such miracles still happen today. Pentecostal and charismatic churches hold that healings and other miracles still occur often. Other churches hold that such miracles served mainly to confirm the gospel in the time of the apostles and became rare afterwards. Both sides believe that God hears prayer, and both pray for the sick.

这一课不要求你现在就下结论。你可以先读一读这些记载，看看其中的耶稣是怎样的人。<br>
This lesson does not ask you to decide now. You can begin by reading the accounts and seeing what kind of person Jesus is in them.
