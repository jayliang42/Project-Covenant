## 耶稣讲给谁听 | Whom Jesus was addressing

《路加福音》15 章开头说：“众税吏和罪人都挨近耶稣，要听他讲道。法利赛人和文士私下议论说：这个人接待罪人，又同他们吃饭”（路 15:1-2）。耶稣就讲了三个比喻：失羊、失钱、失去的儿子。所以听故事的有两种人，一种是名声不好的人，一种是自认为正派的人。<br>
Luke 15 opens: “all the tax collectors and sinners were coming close to him to hear him. The Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying, ‘This man welcomes sinners, and eats with them’” (Luke 15:1-2). Jesus then told three parables: the lost sheep, the lost coin, and the lost son. So two kinds of people were listening, those with a bad name and those who considered themselves upright.

## 小儿子 | The younger son

小儿子对父亲说：“父亲，请你把我应得的家业分给我”（路 15:12）。父亲还活着，儿子就要分家产，这等于说“我只要你的钱，不要你这个人”。父亲把产业分给了他。他去了远方，把钱花光，又遇上饥荒，最后去给人放猪，饿得想吃猪食。<br>
The younger son said to his father, “Father, give me my share of your property” (Luke 15:12). To ask for the inheritance while the father was alive was as good as saying, “I want your money and I do not want you.” The father divided the property. The son went to a far country, spent everything, was caught in a famine, and ended up feeding pigs, hungry enough to want their food.

他醒悟过来，准备好一段话：“父亲！我得罪了天，又得罪了你；从今以后，我不配称为你的儿子，把我当作一个雇工吧”（路 15:18-19）。他以为儿子的身份已经没有了，回去只能靠做工换一口饭。<br>
He came to himself and prepared a speech: “Father, I have sinned against heaven, and in your sight. I am no more worthy to be called your son. Make me as one of your hired servants” (Luke 15:18-19). He assumed his place as a son was gone and that he could only work for his food.

## 父亲 | The father

“相离还远，他父亲看见，就动了慈心，跑去抱着他的颈项，连连与他亲嘴”（路 15:20）。父亲一直在望着那条路。儿子的话只说了一半，父亲就吩咐仆人拿上好的袍子、戒指和鞋来，又宰了肥牛犊。袍子、戒指和鞋都是儿子身份的记号。<br>
“While he was still far off, his father saw him, and was moved with compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him” (Luke 15:20). The father had been watching the road. The son got through only half his speech before the father called for the best robe, a ring, and sandals, and had the fattened calf killed. Robe, ring, and sandals were all marks of a son.

父亲说：“我这个儿子是死而复活，失而又得的”（路 15:24）。耶稣用这位父亲来说明神怎样对待回转的人。<br>
The father said, “this, my son, was dead, and is alive again. He was lost, and is found” (Luke 15:24). Jesus uses this father to show how God treats those who turn back.

## 大儿子 | The older son

故事没有在宴席上结束。大儿子从田里回来，听见音乐，知道了原因，“却生气，不肯进去”。他对父亲说：“我服事你这多年，从来没有违背过你的命，你并没有给我一只山羊羔”（路 15:28-29）。<br>
The story does not end at the feast. The older son came in from the field, heard music, learned the reason, “was angry, and would not go in.” He said to his father, “these many years I have served you, and I never disobeyed a commandment of yours, but you never gave me a goat” (Luke 15:28-29).

他一直在家，心却离父亲很远。他把自己和父亲的关系看成做工和报酬，也不肯称弟弟为“我的兄弟”，只说“你这个儿子”。<br>
He had never left home, yet his heart was far from his father. He thought of their relationship as work and wages, and he would not call the younger man “my brother,” saying only “this your son.”

父亲同样出来劝他，说：“儿啊！你常和我同在，我一切所有的都是你的”（路 15:31）。故事到这里就停了，耶稣没有说大儿子最后有没有进去。这个开放的结尾是留给那些法利赛人和文士的。<br>
The father came out to him as well and said, “Son, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours” (Luke 15:31). The story stops there. Jesus does not say whether the older son went in. The open ending was left for the Pharisees and scribes.

## 这个故事说明什么 | What the story shows

两个儿子用不同的方式离开了父亲。一个远走他乡，一个留在家里却把自己当雇工。父亲对两个儿子都主动走出去。<br>
The two sons left their father in different ways. One went abroad, and the other stayed home and thought of himself as a hired hand. The father went out to both.

耶稣借这个故事说，神愿意接纳回转的人，无论那人是明显走错了路的，还是一直自以为没有错的。<br>
Through the story Jesus says that God is willing to receive those who turn back, whether they have plainly gone wrong or have always thought themselves in the right.
