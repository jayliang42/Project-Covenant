## 从牧童到君王 | From shepherd to king

以色列的第一位王扫罗不听从神，神就差先知撒母耳到伯利恒，从耶西的儿子里另选一位。耶西让七个儿子一一从撒母耳面前经过，神都没有拣选。神说：“人是看外貌；耶和华是看内心”（撒上 16:7）。最后，人把在野地放羊的小儿子大卫叫了回来，撒母耳就膏他作王。<br>
Saul, the first king of Israel, did not obey God, so God sent the prophet Samuel to Bethlehem to choose another from among the sons of Jesse. Jesse brought seven sons before Samuel, and God chose none of them. God said, “man looks at the outward appearance, but Yahweh looks at the heart” (1 Samuel 16:7). At last the youngest, David, was called in from keeping the sheep, and Samuel anointed him king.

大卫年轻的时候用机弦和石子打倒了非利士的巨人歌利亚，后来被扫罗追杀多年。扫罗死后，他作了全以色列的王，在位约四十年，时间大约在主前 1000 年前后。他攻取了耶路撒冷，把它定为都城。<br>
As a youth David struck down the Philistine giant Goliath with a sling and a stone, and then spent years as a fugitive from Saul. After Saul's death he became king over all Israel and reigned about forty years, around 1000 BC. He captured Jerusalem and made it his capital.

1993 年，考古学家在以色列北部的但丘发现了一块亚兰文石碑的残片，上面有“大卫家”的字样。多数学者认为这是圣经以外最早提到大卫王朝的文字。<br>
In 1993 archaeologists at Tel Dan in northern Israel found fragments of an Aramaic stone inscription bearing the words “House of David.” Most scholars regard it as the earliest reference to David's dynasty outside the Bible.

## 大卫的罪 | David's sin

圣经没有把大卫写成完人。他作王以后，和部下乌利亚的妻子拔示巴同寝，又设计让乌利亚死在战场上。先知拿单当面责备他，他承认说：“我得罪耶和华了”（撒下 12:13）。<br>
The Bible does not present David as flawless. As king he slept with Bathsheba, the wife of his officer Uriah, and then arranged for Uriah to die in battle. The prophet Nathan confronted him, and he admitted, “I have sinned against Yahweh” (2 Samuel 12:13).

《诗篇》51 篇的标题说，这首诗是大卫在这件事之后写的。他祷告说：“神啊，求你为我造清洁的心”（诗 51:10）。神赦免了他，但这件事的后果在他家里延续了很多年。<br>
The title of Psalm 51 says David wrote it after this event. He prays, “Create in me a clean heart, O God” (Psalm 51:10). God forgave him, yet the consequences of what he had done ran on in his family for many years.

## 神给大卫的应许 | God's promise to David

大卫想为神建一座殿。神借先知拿单回答他：你不用为我建殿，我要为你建立家室。“我必使你的后裔接续你的位……我必坚定他的国位，直到永远”（撒下 7:12-13）。<br>
David wanted to build a house for God. Through the prophet Nathan God answered that David need not build him a house, and that God would establish a house for David. “I will set up your offspring after you... and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever” (2 Samuel 7:12-13).

大卫的儿子所罗门建了圣殿，但王国后来分裂、灭亡。以色列人就盼望将来有一位大卫的后裔来作王，这位王被称为“弥赛亚”，意思是“受膏者”。新约开头的第一句话就称耶稣基督为“大卫的子孙”（太 1:1）。基督徒相信，神给大卫的应许在耶稣身上实现了。<br>
David's son Solomon built the temple, but the kingdom later split and fell. Israel then hoped for a descendant of David who would come as king. This king was called the “Messiah,” which means “anointed one.” The New Testament opens with the words, “The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, the son of David” (Matthew 1:1). Christians believe that God's promise to David was fulfilled in Jesus.

## 诗篇 | The Psalms

《诗篇》是圣经里的诗歌集，共 150 篇，是以色列人敬拜和祷告用的。其中 73 篇的标题写着“大卫的诗”，其他的出自可拉的后裔、亚萨等人，也有很多没有署名。<br>
The Psalms are the Bible's book of songs, 150 in all, used by Israel in worship and prayer. The titles of 73 of them name David, others come from the sons of Korah, Asaph, and others, and many are unsigned.

诗篇里有各种情绪：赞美、感谢、认罪、求救、愤怒、疑惑。数量最多的一类是哀歌，就是人在难处里向神呼求的诗。最为人熟悉的是 23 篇：“耶和华是我的牧者，我必不致缺乏……我虽然行过死荫的幽谷，也不怕遭害，因为你与我同在”（诗 23:1，4）。<br>
The Psalms hold every kind of feeling: praise, thanks, confession, cries for help, anger, and doubt. The largest group is the laments, in which people call out to God from trouble. The best known is Psalm 23: “Yahweh is my shepherd: I shall lack nothing... Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me” (Psalm 23:1, 4).

耶稣和门徒都熟悉诗篇，新约引用最多的旧约书卷就是《诗篇》。两千多年来，犹太人和基督徒一直用这些诗祷告。<br>
Jesus and his disciples knew the Psalms well, and the Psalms are the Old Testament book most often quoted in the New Testament. For more than two thousand years Jews and Christians have prayed with these songs.
