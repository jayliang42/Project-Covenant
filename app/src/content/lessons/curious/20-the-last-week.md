## 为什么这一周这样重要 | Why this week matters so much

福音书讲耶稣三年左右的工作，写得很快。讲到最后一周，速度就慢了下来。《马可福音》16 章里有 6 章讲这一周，《约翰福音》21 章里有将近一半讲这几天。作者这样安排，说明他们认为耶稣的死和复活是整个故事的重点。<br>
The Gospels move quickly through about three years of Jesus' work. When they reach the last week, they slow down. Six of Mark's 16 chapters cover this week, and nearly half of John's 21 chapters cover these days. By this arrangement the writers show that they regard Jesus' death and resurrection as the centre of the whole story.

## 一天一天地看 | Day by day

下面的日子是教会传统的排法。福音书没有给每件事都标明星期几，个别细节学者有不同的看法。<br>
The days below follow the traditional arrangement of the church. The Gospels do not label every event with a day of the week, and scholars differ on some details.

- **周日：进耶路撒冷**。耶稣骑着驴驹进城，众人把衣服和树枝铺在路上，喊着说：“奉主名来的是应当称颂的”（太 21:9）。<br>
  **Sunday: the entry into Jerusalem.** Jesus rides into the city on a colt. The people spread cloaks and branches on the road and shout, “Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!” (Matthew 21:9).
- **周一到周三：在圣殿里**。耶稣赶出殿里做买卖的人，在殿里教训人，回答宗教领袖的质问。领袖们商议要捉拿他，门徒犹大答应把他交给他们。<br>
  **Monday to Wednesday: in the temple.** Jesus drives out those trading in the temple, teaches there, and answers the challenges of the religious leaders. The leaders plan to arrest him, and the disciple Judas agrees to hand him over.
- **周四：最后的晚餐**。耶稣和门徒吃逾越节的筵席。他为门徒洗脚，又拿起饼和杯说：“这是我的身体，为你们舍的”（路 22:19）。饭后，他在客西马尼园祷告，当夜被捕。<br>
  **Thursday: the last supper.** Jesus eats the Passover meal with the disciples. He washes their feet, and he takes bread and a cup, saying, “This is my body which is given for you” (Luke 22:19). After the meal he prays in the garden of Gethsemane, and that night he is arrested.
- **周五：受审和钉十字架**。耶稣先在犹太公会受审，又被带到罗马巡抚彼拉多面前。彼拉多把他交给兵丁钉十字架。耶稣在十字架上说：“父啊！赦免他们；因为他们所做的，他们不晓得”（路 23:34）。下午他断了气，当天被安放在一座石头坟墓里。<br>
  **Friday: the trials and the crucifixion.** Jesus is tried before the Jewish council and then brought to the Roman governor Pilate, who hands him over to soldiers to be crucified. On the cross Jesus says, “Father, forgive them, for they don’t know what they are doing” (Luke 23:34). In the afternoon he dies, and the same day he is laid in a rock tomb.
- **周六：安息日**。门徒都躲了起来。坟墓有兵丁看守。<br>
  **Saturday: the Sabbath.** The disciples are in hiding. The tomb is under guard.
- **周日：空坟墓**。清早，几位妇女来到坟墓，发现石头已经滚开，有声音对她们说：“为什么在死人中找活人呢？他不在这里，已经复活了”（路 24:5-6）。<br>
  **Sunday: the empty tomb.** Early in the morning several women come to the tomb and find the stone rolled away. They are told, “Why do you seek the living among the dead? He isn’t here, but is risen” (Luke 24:5-6).

## 教会怎样纪念这一周 | How churches remember the week

很多教会把这一周叫作“圣周”或“受难周”，每年按日子纪念：<br>
Many churches call this week Holy Week or Passion Week and mark its days each year:

- **棕枝主日**：纪念耶稣进耶路撒冷。<br>
  **Palm Sunday**: remembers the entry into Jerusalem.
- **濯足节（周四）**：纪念最后的晚餐，有的教会举行洗脚礼。<br>
  **Maundy Thursday**: remembers the last supper. Some churches hold a foot-washing.
- **受难日（周五）**：纪念耶稣被钉十字架。<br>
  **Good Friday**: remembers the crucifixion.
- **复活节（周日）**：纪念耶稣复活，是教会一年里最重要的日子。<br>
  **Easter Sunday**: remembers the resurrection and is the chief day of the church's year.

天主教、东正教、圣公会和信义宗等教会有完整的圣周礼仪。很多浸信会和独立教会只特别纪念受难日和复活节。东方教会和西方教会的复活节日期常常不同：两边推算的规则相同，但东方教会按较旧的儒略历计算，西方教会按 1582 年开始使用的格里高利历计算。<br>
Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, and Lutheran churches keep a full Holy Week liturgy. Many Baptist and independent churches mark only Good Friday and Easter. Eastern and Western churches often keep Easter on different dates. They use the same rule, but Eastern churches reckon by the older Julian calendar and Western churches by the Gregorian calendar introduced in 1582.
