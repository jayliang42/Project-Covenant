## 星期天清早 | Early on Sunday

耶稣在星期五下午死去，当天傍晚被葬在一个从磐石凿出来的坟墓里，墓口用大石头挡住。按犹太人的规矩，安息日不能做工，几个跟随耶稣的妇女等到安息日过了，七日的头一日（星期天）清早，带着香料去坟墓。<br>
Jesus died on Friday afternoon and was buried that evening in a tomb cut out of rock, with a large stone rolled across the entrance. Jewish custom did not allow work on the Sabbath, so several women who followed Jesus waited until it was over. Early on the first day of the week, Sunday, they went to the tomb with spices.

她们发现石头已经滚开，坟墓里没有耶稣的身体。《路加福音》说，有两个衣服放光的人对她们说：“为什么在死人中找活人呢？他不在这里，已经复活了”（路 24:5-6）。<br>
They found the stone rolled away and Jesus' body gone. Luke says two men in dazzling clothing told them, “Why do you seek the living among the dead? He isn’t here, but is risen” (Luke 24:5–6).

## 他向许多人显现 | He appeared to many

福音书接着记载了复活的耶稣多次显现：<br>
The Gospels go on to record many appearances of the risen Jesus:

- 抹大拉的马利亚在坟墓外哭，耶稣叫她的名字，她才认出他（约 20:15-16）。<br>
  Mary Magdalene was weeping outside the tomb. She recognized Jesus only when he called her by name (John 20:15–16).
- 两个门徒往以马忤斯去，路上一个陌生人和他们同行。到吃饭的时候，他擘开饼，他们才认出是耶稣（路 24:30-31）。<br>
  Two disciples were walking to Emmaus when a stranger joined them. At the meal he broke the bread, and only then did they recognize Jesus (Luke 24:30–31).
- 耶稣向聚在一起的门徒显现，让他们看他的手和脚，还在他们面前吃了一片烧鱼（路 24:39-43）。<br>
  Jesus appeared to the gathered disciples, showed them his hands and feet, and ate a piece of broiled fish in front of them (Luke 24:39–43).
- 保罗列出一份名单：耶稣显给彼得看，显给十二使徒看，“后来一时显给五百多弟兄看，其中一大半到如今还在”（林前 15:6）。<br>
  Paul gives a list: Jesus appeared to Peter, then to the twelve, then “to over five hundred brothers at once, most of whom remain until now” (1 Corinthians 15:6).

门徒在耶稣被捕时四散逃走，彼得还三次说不认识他。几个星期以后，他们却在耶路撒冷公开宣讲：“这耶稣，神已经叫他复活了，我们都为这事作见证”（徒 2:32）。<br>
When Jesus was arrested, the disciples scattered, and Peter denied knowing him three times. A few weeks later the same men stood up in Jerusalem and declared, “This Jesus God raised up, to which we all are witnesses” (Acts 2:32).

## 复活为什么这么重要 | Why the resurrection matters so much

保罗说得很直接：“若基督没有复活，我们所传的便是枉然，你们所信的也是枉然”（林前 15:14）。在新约里，复活至少说明三件事：<br>
Paul is blunt: “If Christ has not been raised, then our preaching is in vain, and your faith also is in vain” (1 Corinthians 15:14). In the New Testament the resurrection shows at least three things:

1. **神证明了耶稣是谁**：耶稣“因从死里复活，以大能显明是神的儿子”（罗 1:4）。<br>
   **God confirmed who Jesus is**: Jesus “was declared to be the Son of God with power ... by the resurrection from the dead” (Romans 1:4).
2. **死亡被打败了**：基督是“睡了之人初熟的果子”（林前 15:20）。初熟的果子是收割的开始，后面还有整片庄稼。<br>
   **Death has been defeated**: Christ is “the first fruits of those who are asleep” (1 Corinthians 15:20). The first fruits are the start of the harvest, with the whole crop still to come.
3. **跟随他的人也有将来的盼望**：耶稣说：“复活在我，生命也在我。信我的人虽然死了，也必复活”（约 11:25）。<br>
   **His followers have a future too**: Jesus said, “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies” (John 11:25).

历史学家怎样看复活的证据，是“认真思考信仰”路径里的一课。<br>
