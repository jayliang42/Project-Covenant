## 巴别塔之后 | After the tower of Babel

《创世记》1–11 章讲的是全人类：创造、人的悖逆、洪水和巴别塔。从第 12 章开始，故事集中讲一个人。他叫亚伯兰，后来神给他改名叫亚伯拉罕。<br>
Genesis 1–11 is about all of humanity: creation, human rebellion, the flood, and the tower of Babel. From chapter 12 on, the story focuses on one man. His name is Abram, and God later renames him Abraham.

亚伯兰的家族原来住在吾珥，后来迁到哈兰（创 11:31）。神对他说：“你要离开本地、本族、父家，往我所要指示你的地去。”神应许给他三样东西：后裔会成为大国，他们要得到一块土地，**地上的万族都要因他得福**（创 12:1-3）。<br>
Abram's family had lived in Ur and then moved to Haran (Genesis 11:31). God told him, “Leave your country, and your relatives, and your father’s house, and go to the land that I will show you.” God promised him three things: his descendants would become a great nation, they would receive a land, and **all the families of the earth would be blessed through him** (Genesis 12:1–3).

神医治世界的计划，从一个家族开始。<br>
God's plan to heal the world begins with one family.

## 一个很长的等待 | A long wait

亚伯兰出发时七十五岁（创 12:4），妻子撒莱一直没有孩子。神带他到外面看星星，说他的后裔也要这样多。经上说：“亚伯兰信耶和华，耶和华就以此为他的义”（创 15:6）。<br>
Abram was seventy-five when he set out (Genesis 12:4), and his wife Sarai had no children. God took him outside to look at the stars and said his descendants would be as many. The text says, “He believed in Yahweh, who credited it to him for righteousness” (Genesis 15:6).

可是等待很漫长。亚伯兰两次在外地谎称妻子是妹妹（创 12，20 章），又照撒莱的主意和使女夏甲生了以实玛利（创 16 章）。直到亚伯拉罕一百岁，以撒才出生（创 21:5）。<br>
But the wait was long. Twice in foreign lands Abram claimed his wife was his sister (Genesis 12 and 20), and on Sarai's suggestion he had a son, Ishmael, with her servant Hagar (Genesis 16). Isaac was not born until Abraham was a hundred (Genesis 21:5).

## 应许传了下去 | The promise passes on

应许从亚伯拉罕传给以撒，再传给以撒的儿子雅各。雅各骗过哥哥和父亲，自己也被人骗过。神给他改名叫“以色列”（创 32:28），他的十二个儿子成了以色列十二个支派的祖先。<br>
The promise passed from Abraham to Isaac, and then to Isaac's son Jacob. Jacob deceived his brother and his father, and he was deceived in turn. God renamed him Israel (Genesis 32:28), and his twelve sons became the ancestors of the twelve tribes of Israel.

雅各偏爱儿子约瑟，哥哥们嫉妒，把约瑟卖到埃及。后来约瑟在埃及作了宰相，在饥荒中救了全家。他对哥哥们说：“从前你们的意思是要害我，但神的意思原是好的，要保全许多人的性命”（创 50:20）。《创世记》在这里结束，雅各一家住在了埃及。<br>
Jacob favoured his son Joseph, and Joseph's jealous brothers sold him into Egypt. Joseph later rose to second in command over Egypt and saved his whole family in a famine. He told his brothers, “You meant evil against me, but God meant it for good, to save many people alive” (Genesis 50:20). Genesis ends here, with Jacob's family living in Egypt.

## 和后来的故事有什么关系？ | How does this connect to the rest?

新约多次回到亚伯拉罕。保罗说，神预先把好消息传给了亚伯拉罕，就是“万国都必因你得福”（加 3:8）。《马太福音》第一句话就说耶稣是“亚伯拉罕的后裔”（太 1:1）。<br>
The New Testament returns to Abraham again and again. Paul says God announced the good news in advance to Abraham: “In you all the nations will be blessed” (Galatians 3:8). The first sentence of Matthew calls Jesus “the son of Abraham” (Matthew 1:1).

亚伯拉罕生活的年代，传统上放在主前两千年前后，学者对具体年代看法不一。<br>
Abraham's lifetime is traditionally placed around 2000 BC, though scholars disagree on the exact dates.
