## 两部分，66 卷 | Two parts, 66 books

“圣经”（Bible）这个词来自希腊文的“书卷”。打开它，你看到的其实是一座小图书馆：新教圣经分为**旧约**（39 卷）和**新约**（27 卷），一共 66 卷。

The word “Bible” comes from the Greek for “books”. Open it and you find a small library: the Protestant Bible has the **Old Testament** (39 books) and the **New Testament** (27 books), 66 in all.

旧约大多用希伯来文写成，讲耶稣降生以前神与以色列的故事；新约用希腊文写成，讲耶稣的生平，以及最早的教会。

The Old Testament was written mostly in Hebrew and tells the story of God and Israel before Jesus. The New Testament, written in Greek, tells of Jesus' life and the earliest church.

## 不同的文体 | Different kinds of writing

这些书卷的写作跨越一千多年，作者有君王、牧人、渔夫、医生。书里有叙事、律法、诗歌、智慧格言、先知信息、福音书、书信和启示文学。

These books were written across more than a thousand years by kings, shepherds, fishermen and a doctor. They include narrative, law, poetry, wisdom sayings, prophecy, Gospels, letters and apocalyptic writing.

读之前先问一句：“这是哪一类文字？”这样就能避免很多误解。读诗要留意意象；读书信要想想当时的收信人遇到了什么事。

Before reading, ask: “What kind of writing is this?” It prevents many misunderstandings. In poetry, notice the images; in a letter, picture what its first readers were facing.

## 为什么而写 | Why it was written

有些作者直接告诉我们他为什么写。路加说，他“详细考察”过，好让读者知道所学的是确实的（路 1:1-4）。约翰说，他写下这些，是要人信耶稣是基督，并因信得生命（约 20:31）。

Some authors tell us why they wrote. Luke says he traced everything carefully from the beginning so that readers could be certain (Luke 1:1–4). John says he wrote so that people would believe Jesus is the Christ and have life (John 20:31).

你不必先接受这些说法，但知道作者的目的，读起来会清楚很多。

You don't have to accept their claims first, but knowing an author's purpose makes the reading much clearer.
