## 为什么叫“图书馆”？ | Why a "library"?

很多人第一次拿起圣经，会像拿一本小说那样从第一页往下读。读完《创世记》的故事，到了《出埃及记》后半段和《利未记》，突然全是会幕尺寸、献祭条例和家谱，就卡住了。<br>
Many first-time readers open the Bible the way they would open a novel — starting on page one. After the stories of Genesis, they hit tent measurements, sacrifice rules, and genealogies in late Exodus and Leviticus, and stall.

卡住不是因为你读不懂，而是因为**圣经本来就不是单一文体的一本书**。“Bible”来自希腊文 *ta biblia*，字面意思是“那些书卷”。它是一座装在一个封面里的小图书馆。<br>
Stalling is not a personal failure; **the Bible was never a single-genre book**. The word "Bible" comes from the Greek *ta biblia*, literally "the books." It is a small library bound between two covers.

## 两大部分：旧约与新约 | Two halves: Old and New Testaments

这里的“约”（Covenant / Testament）不是“旧合同作废、换新合同”那么简单，而是指神与人建立盟约关系的两个历史阶段：<br>
"Testament" (or covenant) means a solemn relationship bond between God and people across two historical stages:

- **旧约（39 卷）**：主要用希伯来文写成（少量亚兰文），记录耶稣降生之前的以色列历史、律法、诗歌（如《诗篇》）、智慧思考（如《箴言》《传道书》）和先知的信息。<br>
  **Old Testament (39 books)**: Written mostly in Hebrew (with a little Aramaic) before Jesus, containing Israel's history, law, poetry (such as Psalms), wisdom reflection (Proverbs, Ecclesiastes), and the prophets.
- **新约（27 卷）**：用第一世纪通行的希腊文写成，包含四卷《福音书》（从四个角度记述耶稣的生平、死与复活）、《使徒行传》（早期教会如何传开）、21 封书信，以及《启示录》。<br>
  **New Testament (27 books)**: Written in first-century common Greek, containing the four Gospels (Jesus' life, death, and resurrection from four angles), Acts (the early church), twenty-one letters, and Revelation.

（顺便了解：天主教和东正教的旧约还包含“次经/第二正典”书卷，所以总卷数更多；这属于历史传统差异，在“基督教传统”专题里可以细读。）<br>
(Note: Catholic and Eastern Orthodox Bibles include additional Old Testament books known as the Deuterocanon or Apocrypha. That historical difference is explained in the Christian Traditions section of the library.)

## 怎么翻开第一页？ | Where should I open it?

既然是图书馆，你不需要按书架编号从第一本硬啃。对第一次读的人，最自然的两个入口是：<br>
Because it is a library, you do not have to read shelf by shelf from the front. Two natural doors for a first reading are:

1. **想先认识耶稣**：读《马可福音》（最短、节奏快）或《路加福音》（像调查报告一样细致，见上面“今天读的经文”）。<br>
   **To meet Jesus first**: Read the Gospel of Mark (shortest and brisk) or Luke (written like a careful investigation — see today's scripture above).
2. **想先看整条大故事的开头**：读《创世记》第 1–11 章，看看圣经如何回答“世界从哪里来、人为什么会彼此伤害”。<br>
   **To see how the big story begins**: Read Genesis chapters 1–11 to see how the Bible frames where the world came from and why humans hurt one another.
