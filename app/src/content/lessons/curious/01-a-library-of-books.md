## 为什么说它是图书馆 | Why call it a library

很多人第一次读圣经，会像读小说一样从第一页读起。《创世记》的故事读得很顺，到了《出埃及记》后半段和《利未记》，满篇是会幕尺寸、献祭条例和家谱，就读不下去了。<br>
Many first-time readers start the Bible on page one, as they would a novel. The stories in Genesis go well. Then the second half of Exodus and Leviticus bring tent measurements, sacrifice rules, and genealogies, and they stall.

这很正常。圣经里有很多种文体，各卷的读法不一样。“Bible”这个词来自希腊文 *ta biblia*，意思是“那些书卷”。圣经是把 66 卷书装在一个封面里的小图书馆。<br>
This is normal. The Bible holds many kinds of writing, and each kind is read differently. The word "Bible" comes from the Greek *ta biblia*, "the books." It is a small library of 66 books bound in one cover.

## 旧约和新约 | Old and New Testaments

“约”（Covenant / Testament）指神与人立的盟约。旧约和新约是这段盟约关系的两个历史阶段。<br>
"Testament" means covenant, the bond God makes with people. The Old and New Testaments are two historical stages of that relationship.

- **旧约（39 卷）**：主要用希伯来文写成，有少量亚兰文。内容是耶稣降生之前的以色列历史、律法、诗歌（如《诗篇》）、智慧书（如《箴言》《传道书》）和先知的信息。<br>
  **Old Testament (39 books)**: Written mostly in Hebrew, with a little Aramaic. It holds Israel's history before Jesus, the law, poetry (such as Psalms), wisdom books (such as Proverbs and Ecclesiastes), and the prophets.
- **新约（27 卷）**：用第一世纪通行的希腊文写成。包括四卷《福音书》（从四个角度记述耶稣的生平、死与复活）、《使徒行传》（早期教会怎样传开）、21 封书信和《启示录》。<br>
  **New Testament (27 books)**: Written in the common Greek of the first century. It holds the four Gospels (Jesus' life, death, and resurrection from four angles), Acts (how the early church spread), twenty-one letters, and Revelation.

（补充：天主教和东正教的旧约还包含“次经／第二正典”书卷，所以总卷数更多。这是历史传统上的差异，“基督教传统”专题里有说明。）<br>
(Note: Catholic and Eastern Orthodox Bibles include additional Old Testament books known as the Deuterocanon or Apocrypha, so they have more books in total. This is a difference of historical tradition, explained in the Christian Traditions section of the library.)

## 第一次读，从哪里开始 | Where to start

不用从第一页按顺序读到最后。第一次读，可以从下面两处选一处开始：<br>
You don't have to read from the first page straight through. For a first reading, start with either of these:

1. **想先认识耶稣**：读《马可福音》（最短，节奏快）或《路加福音》（写得像一份细致的调查报告，见上面“今天读的经文”）。<br>
   **To meet Jesus first**: Read the Gospel of Mark (the shortest, and fast-paced) or Luke (written like a careful investigation; see today's scripture above).
2. **想先看大故事的开头**：读《创世记》第 1–11 章，看圣经怎样讲世界的来历，以及人为什么彼此伤害。<br>
   **To see how the big story begins**: Read Genesis chapters 1–11 for the Bible's account of where the world came from and why people hurt one another.
