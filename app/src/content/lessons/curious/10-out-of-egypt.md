## 从一家人到一个民族 | From one family to a people

《创世记》结束时，雅各一家七十口人住在埃及。到《出埃及记》开头，他们的后代已经人数众多。新的法老害怕他们，就让他们作苦工，“埃及人严严地使以色列人做工”（出 1:13），还下令把以色列人生的男婴丢进尼罗河。<br>
At the end of Genesis, Jacob's family of seventy lived in Egypt. By the start of Exodus, their descendants had become very numerous. A new Pharaoh feared them and forced them into hard labour. “The Egyptians ruthlessly made the children of Israel serve” (Exodus 1:13), and Pharaoh ordered that Israelite baby boys be thrown into the Nile.

经上说，神“听见他们的哀声，就记念他与亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各所立的约”（出 2:24）。故事从这里转向拯救。<br>
The text says, “God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob” (Exodus 2:24). From here the story turns toward rescue.

## 摩西和逾越节 | Moses and the Passover

摩西是一个被法老的女儿救起、在王宫里长大的以色列人。他长大后杀了一个欺负以色列人的埃及人，逃到旷野，放了多年的羊。神在燃烧的荆棘中向他显现，差他回埃及带百姓出来。摩西问神叫什么名字，神说：“我是自有永有的”（出 3:14）。<br>
Moses was an Israelite rescued by Pharaoh's daughter and raised in the palace. As an adult he killed an Egyptian who was beating an Israelite, fled to the wilderness, and spent many years as a shepherd. God appeared to him in a burning bush and sent him back to Egypt to bring the people out. When Moses asked God's name, God said, “I AM WHO I AM” (Exodus 3:14).

法老不肯放人，埃及遭遇了十次灾殃。最后一灾临到前，神吩咐以色列每家杀一只羊羔，把血涂在门框上，当晚吃羊羔、无酵饼和苦菜。神说：“我一见这血，就越过你们去”（出 12:13）。“逾越节”这个名字就是从这里来的。以色列人到今天仍然每年守这个节，记念那一夜。<br>
Pharaoh refused to let the people go, and ten plagues struck Egypt. Before the last one, God told each Israelite household to kill a lamb, put its blood on the doorframe, and eat the lamb that night with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. God said, “When I see the blood, I will pass over you” (Exodus 12:13). This is where the name Passover comes from. Jewish families still keep the feast every year to remember that night.

以色列人出了埃及，被埃及的军队追到海边。摩西对百姓说：“不要惧怕，只管站住！看耶和华今天向你们所要施行的救恩”（出 14:13）。海水分开，百姓走干地过去了。<br>
After leaving Egypt, the Israelites were chased to the sea by Egypt's army. Moses told the people, “Don’t be afraid. Stand still, and see the salvation of Yahweh, which he will work for you today” (Exodus 14:13). The waters parted, and the people crossed on dry ground.

## 西奈山的约 | The covenant at Sinai

出埃及以后大约三个月，以色列人来到西奈山下（出 19:1）。神在这里与他们立约，赐下十诫和其他律法。十诫的第一句话是：“我是耶和华你的神，曾将你从埃及地为奴之家领出来。”<br>
About three months after leaving Egypt, the Israelites arrived at Mount Sinai (Exodus 19:1). There God made a covenant with them and gave them the Ten Commandments and the rest of the law. The first words of the Ten Commandments are: “I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.”

这个次序很重要。神先救他们，然后才告诉他们怎样作神的子民。他们守律法，是为了回应已经得到的拯救，好在列国面前活出神的样子。<br>
The order matters. God rescued them first and then told them how to live as his people. They were to keep the law in response to a rescue they had already received, and to show the nations what God is like.

## 旷野四十年 | Forty years in the wilderness

以色列人很快就开始抱怨，还造了一只金牛犊来拜（出 32 章）。到了应许之地的边上，他们又因为害怕不肯进去。结果这一代人在旷野飘流了四十年（民 14:33）。神仍然每天供应他们吗哪，在会幕里住在他们中间。<br>
The Israelites soon began to complain, and they even made a golden calf to worship (Exodus 32). At the edge of the promised land, they were afraid and refused to go in. As a result, that generation wandered in the wilderness for forty years (Numbers 14:33). God still gave them manna every day and lived among them in the tabernacle.

新约常用出埃及来讲耶稣。耶稣在逾越节期间被钉十字架，保罗称他是“我们逾越节的羔羊”（林前 5:7）。出埃及的确切年代，学者有不同看法，主要有主前 15 世纪和主前 13 世纪两种。<br>
The New Testament often uses the exodus to speak about Jesus. Jesus was crucified at Passover time, and Paul calls him “Christ, our Passover” (1 Corinthians 5:7). Scholars disagree about the exact date of the exodus. The two main views place it in the 15th or the 13th century BC.
