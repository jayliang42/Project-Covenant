## 不只是格言集 | More than a quote book

如果把圣经当成“名人名言字典”，随便抽一句出来读，很容易读偏。因为每一卷书都处在一个更大的故事里。许多圣经学者喜欢把这个大故事分成**六幕剧**来理解：<br>
If you treat the Bible like a dictionary of inspiring quotes, pulling single lines out of context, it is easy to misread. Every book sits inside a larger narrative, often summarized as a **six-act drama**:

## 第一幕：创造（创世记 1–2） | Act 1: Creation (Genesis 1–2)

世界不是偶然撞出来的孤儿，而是出于一位良善创造主的心意。人被造有神的形像——意思是代表神管理、照看这个世界，彼此相爱。<br>
The world is not a cosmic accident; it comes from a good Creator. Humans are made "in God's image" — called to represent God in caring for creation and loving one another.

## 第二幕：失落（创世记 3–11） | Act 2: Fall (Genesis 3–11)

人选择不信任神，想自己定义善恶。结果四重关系同时裂开：人与神疏远、人自己内心羞耻不安、人与人彼此推诿伤害、人与大地也失去和谐。<br>
Humans choose distrust and try to define good and evil on their own terms. Four relationships fracture at once: with God, within the self, between people, and with the earth.

## 第三幕：应许与以色列（创世记 12 – 旧约结尾） | Act 3: Promise and Israel (Genesis 12 – end of the OT)

神没有放弃世界。他呼召亚伯拉罕，应许借着他的后裔使“地上万族得福”。旧约很大篇幅都在讲这个民族（以色列）的故事：出埃及、领受律法、建立王国、屡次悖逆、流亡异乡，却一直在等候那位真正能带来医治的君王（弥赛亚）。<br>
God does not abandon the world. He calls Abraham and promises that through his family "all families of the earth" will be blessed. Most of the Old Testament follows Israel — exodus, law, kingdom, repeated unfaithfulness, exile — still waiting for the promised King (Messiah) who can heal what is broken.

## 第四幕：耶稣（四福音书） | Act 4: Jesus (The Four Gospels)

整部剧的转折点。新约宣告：以色列等候的弥赛亚就是拿撒勒人耶稣。他传讲神的国，医治软弱的人，在十字架上担当罪恶，并在第三天复活，为世界打开一条和好之路。<br>
The turning point of the drama. The New Testament announces that Israel's long-awaited Messiah has arrived in Jesus of Nazareth — teaching God's kingdom, healing the broken, bearing evil on the cross, and rising on the third day to open a way of reconciliation.

## 第五幕：教会与万国（使徒行传 – 书信） | Act 5: The Church and the Nations (Acts – Epistles)

复活的耶稣差遣门徒把好消息带到万国。这一幕没有在《使徒行传》第 28 章结束——**今天所有跟随耶稣的人，仍然活在第五幕里**。<br>
The risen Jesus sends his followers to carry this good news to all nations. This act did not end in Acts 28 — **everyone following Jesus today is still living inside Act 5**.

## 第六幕：新创造（启示录 21–22） | Act 6: New Creation (Revelation 21–22)

圣经的结局不是“灵魂飘到云端、丢掉地球”，而是天地被更新、神亲自与人同住，“不再有死亡，也不再有悲哀、哭号、疼痛”。<br>
The ending of the Bible is not disembodied souls floating on clouds while the earth is trashed. It is heaven and earth renewed, God dwelling with humanity, and "no more death or mourning or crying or pain."
