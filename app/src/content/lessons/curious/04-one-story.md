## 九个站点 | Nine stops

圣经可以读成一条连贯的故事。下面九站是一张简单的地图，不必一次记住，先看清大方向。

The Bible can be read as one connected story. These nine stops are a simple map. You don't need to memorise them yet; just see the overall direction.

1. **创造**：世界是神赐的礼物，人被托付去照管它。<br>**Creation:** the world is God's gift, and people are entrusted to care for it.
2. **堕落**：不信任和背离带来破碎的关系，罪随之扩散。<br>**Fall:** distrust and turning away break relationships, and wrong spreads.
3. **圣约**：神主动与亚伯拉罕立约，要借着他的后裔祝福万族。<br>**Covenant:** God binds himself to Abraham, promising to bless all nations through his family.
4. **国度**：以色列出埃及、得律法、立君王，学习在神面前生活。<br>**Kingdom:** Israel is rescued from Egypt, receives the law and gains kings, learning to live before God.
5. **被掳**：王国一再背离，最后亡国被掳，但先知仍宣告盼望。<br>**Exile:** the kingdom keeps turning away and falls into exile, yet the prophets still announce hope.
6. **应许**：先知指向一位将要来的君王和一个新的约。<br>**Promise:** the prophets point to a coming king and a new covenant.
7. **基督**：耶稣来了，宣讲神的国，被钉十字架，并且复活。<br>**Christ:** Jesus comes, announces God's kingdom, is crucified, and rises.
8. **教会**：圣灵降临，好消息从耶路撒冷传向万族。<br>**Church:** the Spirit comes, and the good news spreads from Jerusalem to the nations.
9. **新创造**：故事的终点是神更新万物，与人同住。<br>**New creation:** the story ends with God making all things new and dwelling with people.

## 故事的中心 | The centre of the story

基督徒相信，这条故事线的中心是耶稣。约翰福音 3:16 常被称为“小福音”，因为它一句话概括了神在这个故事里要做的事。

Christians believe Jesus stands at the centre of this story. John 3:16 is often called “the gospel in miniature” because it sums up in one sentence what God is doing in the story.
