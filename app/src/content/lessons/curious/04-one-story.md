## 每卷书都在一个大故事里 | Every book sits in one larger story

如果把圣经当成名言集，随手抽一句来读，很容易读偏，因为每一卷书都在一个更大的故事里。有些圣经学者（例如《圣经的戏剧》一书的作者巴塞洛缪和戈欣）把这个故事分成**六幕**：<br>
If you treat the Bible as a book of quotations and pull out single lines, it is easy to misread them, because every book sits inside a larger story. Some Bible scholars (for example Craig Bartholomew and Michael Goheen in *The Drama of Scripture*) divide that story into **six acts**:

## 第一幕：创造（创世记 1–2） | Act 1: Creation (Genesis 1–2)

世界出于一位良善的创造主。人按神的形像被造，意思是人代表神管理和照看这个世界，并且彼此相爱。<br>
The world comes from a good Creator. Humans are made in God's image, which means they represent God in ruling and caring for the world, and are to love one another.

## 第二幕：失落（创世记 3–11） | Act 2: Fall (Genesis 3–11)

人选择不信任神，要自己定义善恶。结果四种关系一起破裂：人与神疏远，人心里羞耻不安，人与人互相推诿伤害，人与大地也不再和谐。<br>
Humans choose not to trust God and try to define good and evil for themselves. Four relationships break together: with God, within the self, between people, and with the earth.

## 第三幕：应许与以色列（创世记 12 – 旧约结尾） | Act 3: Promise and Israel (Genesis 12 – end of the OT)

神没有放弃世界。他呼召亚伯拉罕，应许地上万族要因他的后裔得福。旧约大部分篇幅讲的是以色列这个民族：出埃及，领受律法，建立王国，屡次悖逆，被掳到外邦。他们一直在等候一位能带来医治的君王，就是弥赛亚。<br>
God does not abandon the world. He calls Abraham and promises that through his descendants all the families of the earth will be blessed. Most of the Old Testament follows the people of Israel: the exodus, the law, the kingdom, repeated unfaithfulness, and exile. All along they wait for a king who can bring healing, the Messiah.

## 第四幕：耶稣（四福音书） | Act 4: Jesus (the four Gospels)

这是整个故事的转折点。新约宣告，以色列等候的弥赛亚就是拿撒勒人耶稣。他传讲神的国，医治软弱的人，在十字架上担当罪，第三天复活，为世界开了与神和好的路。<br>
This is the turning point of the story. The New Testament announces that the Messiah Israel waited for is Jesus of Nazareth. He preached God's kingdom, healed the weak, bore sin on the cross, and rose on the third day, opening the way for the world to be reconciled to God.

## 第五幕：教会与万国（使徒行传 – 书信） | Act 5: The church and the nations (Acts – Epistles)

复活的耶稣差遣门徒把好消息带到万国。这一幕到《使徒行传》第 28 章并没有结束，**今天跟随耶稣的人仍然在第五幕里**。<br>
The risen Jesus sends his followers to carry the good news to all nations. This act did not end with Acts 28, and **everyone who follows Jesus today is still in Act 5**.

## 第六幕：新创造（启示录 21–22） | Act 6: New creation (Revelation 21–22)

圣经的结局是天地被更新，神亲自与人同住，“不再有死亡，也不再有悲哀、哭号、疼痛”。这和“灵魂离开地球、飘到云端”的常见想象不一样。<br>
The Bible ends with heaven and earth renewed and God living with people, with "no more death or mourning or crying or pain." This differs from the common picture of souls leaving the earth for the clouds.
