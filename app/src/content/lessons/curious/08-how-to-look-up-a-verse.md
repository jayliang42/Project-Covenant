## 一处经文由三部分组成 | A reference has three parts

你可能见过“约 3:16”或“John 3:16”这样的写法。它由三部分组成：**书卷名**、**章**、**节**。“约”是《约翰福音》的缩写，冒号前的 3 是第 3 章，冒号后的 16 是第 16 节。<br>
You may have seen something like “John 3:16.” A reference has three parts: the **book**, the **chapter**, and the **verse**. “John” is the Gospel of John, the 3 before the colon is chapter 3, and the 16 after it is verse 16.

还有几种常见的写法：<br>
Here are a few other common forms:

- **太 5:3-10**：《马太福音》第 5 章第 3 节到第 10 节。<br>
  **Matt 5:3-10**: Matthew, chapter 5, verses 3 to 10.
- **创 1–2**：《创世记》第 1 章到第 2 章。<br>
  **Gen 1–2**: Genesis, chapters 1 and 2.
- **约壹 4:8**：《约翰一书》第 4 章第 8 节。有几卷书同名，前面加数字区分，中文写成“约壹”“约贰”“约叁”，英文写成 1 John、2 John、3 John。<br>
  **1 John 4:8**: the First Letter of John, chapter 4, verse 8. Several books share a name, so a number is added in front. Chinese writes 约壹, 约贰, 约叁, and English writes 1 John, 2 John, 3 John.

## 章节号是后人加的 | Chapter and verse numbers came later

圣经的作者写书时没有分章分节。现在用的分章方法，一般认为出自 13 世纪的英国学者兰顿（Stephen Langton）。新约的分节出自 16 世纪的法国印刷商斯蒂芬努斯（Robert Estienne），他 1551 年出版的希腊文新约第一次印上了节号。<br>
The biblical authors did not divide their books into chapters and verses. The chapter divisions used today are generally traced to the English scholar Stephen Langton in the 13th century. The New Testament verse numbers come from the 16th-century French printer Robert Estienne, whose Greek New Testament of 1551 was the first to print them.

章节号让人很快找到一句话，可是它也会把一段完整的意思切开。所以读经的时候，最好看看前后几节，再看整段在讲什么。<br>
The numbers make a sentence quick to find, but they can also cut a single thought in two. So when you read, look at a few verses before and after, and then at what the whole passage is about.

## 从哪一卷开始读？ | Which book should you start with?

第一次读圣经，不一定要从《创世记》第一页读起。很多人建议从**《马可福音》**开始。它是四本福音书里最短的一本，只有 16 章，一直在讲耶稣做了什么、说了什么。每天读一章，大约两个多星期就能读完。<br>
You do not have to start on the first page of Genesis. Many people suggest starting with **the Gospel of Mark**. It is the shortest of the four Gospels, with only 16 chapters, and it stays focused on what Jesus did and said. At one chapter a day, you can finish it in a little over two weeks.

读的时候可以准备一个本子，每章记下一件让你印象深的事，和一个你想问的问题。不懂的地方先记下来，不用停下来全部弄明白。<br>
Keep a notebook as you read. For each chapter, write down one thing that stood out to you and one question you want to ask. When something is unclear, note it and keep going. You do not have to work everything out before moving on.
