## 渔夫 | A fisherman

彼得原名西门，是加利利的渔夫。耶稣给他起名叫“矶法”，亚兰文的意思是“磐石”，希腊文就是“彼得”。他第一次见识耶稣的能力时，俯伏在耶稣面前说：“主啊，离开我，我是个罪人！”（路 5:8）耶稣却叫他跟从自己，说要叫他“得人”。<br>
Peter, originally named Simon, was a fisherman from Galilee. Jesus named him Cephas, Aramaic for “rock,” which in Greek is Peter. When he first saw Jesus' power, he fell at Jesus' knees and said, “Depart from me, for I am a sinful man, Lord” (Luke 5:8). Jesus called him to follow and said he would catch people.

在十二门徒的名单里，彼得总是排在第一位。他性子急，常常第一个开口：在海上，他请耶稣叫他从水面上走过去，走了几步，见风甚大就害怕，喊着说：“主啊，救我！”（太 14:30）<br>
Peter is always named first in the lists of the Twelve. He was impulsive and often first to speak. On the lake he asked Jesus to let him walk on the water; after a few steps he saw the wind, grew afraid, and cried, “Lord, save me!” (Matthew 14:30).

## 认出耶稣，又不认他 | Recognising Jesus, then denying him

耶稣问门徒：“你们说我是谁？”彼得回答：“你是基督，是永生神的儿子。”耶稣说：“我还告诉你，你是彼得，我要把我的教会建造在这磐石上”（太 16:16-18）。<br>
Jesus asked the disciples, “Who do you say that I am?” Peter answered, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.” Jesus said, “I also tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my assembly” (Matthew 16:16-18).

最后的晚餐时，彼得说他愿意跟耶稣同死。耶稣对他说：“西门！西门！撒但想要得着你们，好筛你们像筛麦子一样；但我已经为你祈求，叫你不至于失了信心。你回头以后，要坚固你的弟兄”（路 22:31-32）。那天夜里，耶稣被捕受审，彼得在院子里三次说自己不认识耶稣。鸡叫了，“主转过身来看彼得……他就出去痛哭”（路 22:61-62）。<br>
At the Last Supper Peter said he was ready to die with Jesus. Jesus told him, “Simon, Simon, behold, Satan asked to have all of you, that he might sift you as wheat, but I prayed for you, that your faith wouldn't fail. You, when once you have turned again, establish your brothers” (Luke 22:31-32). That night, as Jesus was arrested and tried, Peter three times denied knowing him in the courtyard. The rooster crowed, “The Lord turned and looked at Peter... He went out, and wept bitterly” (Luke 22:61-62).

## 被挽回 | Restored

耶稣复活以后，在提比哩亚海边给门徒预备了早饭。吃完以后，他三次问彼得：“你爱我吗？”彼得三次回答：“主啊，是的，你知道我爱你。”耶稣三次对他说：“你喂养我的羊”（约 21:15-17）。三次不认，三次托付。<br>
After the resurrection Jesus made breakfast for the disciples by the Sea of Tiberias. Afterwards he asked Peter three times, “Do you love me?” Three times Peter answered, “Yes, Lord; you know that I have affection for you.” Three times Jesus told him, “Feed my sheep” (John 21:15-17). Three denials, three commissions.

## 教会的领袖 | A leader of the church

五旬节那天，彼得站起来，高声对耶路撒冷的众人讲道（徒 2:14），那天约有三千人受洗。后来公会的人见彼得和约翰的胆量，“又看出他们原是没有学问的小民，就希奇，认明他们是跟过耶稣的”（徒 4:13）。新约里有两封彼得书信。在第一封里，他称自己是“作长老、作基督受苦的见证”的人，劝长老们“务要牧养在你们中间神的群羊”（彼前 5:1-2）。<br>
On the day of Pentecost Peter stood up and preached to the crowds in Jerusalem (Acts 2:14), and about three thousand were baptised. Later the council members, seeing the boldness of Peter and John, “perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men... They recognized that they had been with Jesus” (Acts 4:13). Two New Testament letters bear Peter's name. In the first he calls himself “a fellow elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ,” and urges the elders, “Shepherd the flock of God which is among you” (1 Peter 5:1-2).

按教会的传统，彼得大约在主后 64 年尼禄在位时在罗马殉道。大英百科说，关于他在罗马的证据和埋葬的地点仍有争论。<br>
According to church tradition, Peter was martyred in Rome around AD 64 under Nero. Britannica notes that the evidence for Rome is debated and the place of his burial is unresolved.

## “这磐石”是什么意思 | What “this rock” means

马太福音 16:18 的理解，是各传统之间的一个分歧。天主教会认为，基督把彼得立为使徒之首，历代罗马主教（教宗）继承彼得的职分。大英百科说，今天多数学者认为“磐石”指的是彼得本人；早期也有解经者认为指基督，或指彼得承认耶稣是基督的信心。东正教和新教一般承认彼得在使徒中的领导地位，但不认为由此可以推出历代罗马主教对全教会的管辖权。<br>
Matthew 16:18 is one point where the traditions differ. The Catholic Church holds that Christ made Peter head of the apostles and that the bishops of Rome (the popes) succeed to Peter's office. Britannica says most scholars today take the “rock” to refer to Peter himself; some earlier interpreters took it to mean Christ, or Peter's faith in confessing Jesus as the Christ. The Orthodox and Protestants generally recognise Peter's leadership among the apostles but do not believe it establishes jurisdiction for later bishops of Rome over the whole church.

各传统都同意：彼得的故事说明，跌倒的人可以被挽回，并且被神重新使用。<br>
All traditions agree that Peter's story shows that someone who falls can be restored and used by God again.
