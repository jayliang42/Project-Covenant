## 为什么需要小组 | Why small groups

使徒行传说，最早的信徒“天天同心合意恒切地在殿里，且在家中擘饼，存着欢喜、诚实的心用饭”（徒 2:46）。他们有在圣殿里的大聚会，也有在家里的小聚会。保罗的书信里也常提到“在他们家中的教会”，例如罗马书 16:5。<br>
Acts says the first believers continued “day by day... with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread at home,” and “took their food with gladness and singleness of heart” (Acts 2:46). They had large gatherings in the temple and small ones in homes. Paul's letters often mention “the assembly that is in their house” (for example Romans 16:5).

在几个人中间，人比较容易说出真实的困难，也比较容易被注意到。传道书说：“两个人总比一个人好……若是跌倒，这人可以扶起他的同伴；若是孤身跌倒，没有别人扶起他来，这人就有祸了”（传 4:9-10）。新约里有很多“彼此”的吩咐：彼此相爱、彼此接纳（罗 15:7）、互相担当重担（加 6:2）、彼此认罪、互相代求（雅 5:16）。这些在小组里最容易做到。<br>
Among a few people it is easier to share real struggles and easier to be noticed. Ecclesiastes says, “Two are better than one... if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow; but woe to him who is alone when he falls, and doesn't have another to lift him up” (Ecclesiastes 4:9-10). The New Testament has many “one another” commands: love one another, accept one another (Romans 15:7), bear one another's burdens (Galatians 6:2), confess to one another and pray for one another (James 5:16). These are easiest to practise in a small group.

## 一次小组聚会通常做什么 | What usually happens at a meeting

各教会的小组形式不同，常见的有这几部分：<br>
Small groups vary from church to church. Common parts include:

- **吃饭或喝茶**，轻松地聊聊这一周。<br>
  **A meal or tea**, chatting about the week.
- **一起读一段经文**，按“观察、解释、应用”的方法讨论。<br>
  **Reading a passage together**, discussing it with observation, interpretation, and application.
- **分享和代祷**：说说近况和需要，彼此祷告。<br>
  **Sharing and prayer**: talking about how things are and what is needed, and praying for one another.
- **实际的关心**：有人生病、搬家、找工作时一起帮忙。<br>
  **Practical care**: helping when someone is ill, moving house, or looking for work.

## 怎样做一个好组员 | How to be a good group member

- **固定参加**。偶尔去一次很难建立关系。<br>
  **Come regularly.** It is hard to build relationships by turning up now and then.
- **预先读经文**。讨论时会有更多收获。<br>
  **Read the passage beforehand.** You will get more out of the discussion.
- **多听，也愿意说**。不要一个人讲太久，也不要一直不开口。<br>
  **Listen, and be willing to speak.** Don't talk too long, and don't stay silent all the time.
- **保密**。组员分享的私事，不要在别处转述。但如果有人可能会受伤害，要请带领者和教会负责的同工一起处理。<br>
  **Keep confidences.** Don't repeat what members share in private. But if someone may be harmed, involve the leader and responsible church leaders.
- **接纳不同的人**。小组里会有不同年龄、背景和看法的人。保罗说：“你们要彼此接纳，如同基督接纳你们一样”（罗 15:7）。<br>
  **Welcome different people.** A group will include people of different ages, backgrounds, and views. Paul says, “accept one another, even as Christ also accepted you” (Romans 15:7).
