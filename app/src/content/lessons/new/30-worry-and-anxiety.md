## 圣经里的忧虑 | Worry in the Bible

耶稣说：“不要为生命忧虑吃什么，喝什么；为身体忧虑穿什么……你们看那天上的飞鸟，也不种，也不收，也不积蓄在仓里，你们的天父尚且养活牠”（太 6:25-26）。他又说：“不要为明天忧虑，因为明天自有明天的忧虑；一天的难处一天当就够了”（太 6:34）。<br>
Jesus said, “don't be anxious for your life: what you will eat, or what you will drink; nor yet for your body, what you will wear... See the birds of the sky, that they don't sow, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns. Your heavenly Father feeds them” (Matthew 6:25-26). He also said, “don't be anxious for tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Each day's own evil is sufficient” (Matthew 6:34).

圣经也没有说信徒不会有沉重的情绪。耶稣在客西马尼园说：“我心里甚是忧伤，几乎要死”（太 26:38）。诗人问自己：“我的心哪，你为何忧闷？为何在我里面烦躁？应当仰望神”（诗 42:5）。先知以利亚在旷野灰心到极点时，神先让他睡觉、吃东西，然后才对他说话（王上 19 章）。<br>
The Bible does not say believers will never feel heavy emotions. In Gethsemane Jesus said, “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death” (Matthew 26:38). The psalmist asks himself, “Why are you in despair, my soul? Why are you disturbed within me? Hope in God!” (Psalm 42:5). When the prophet Elijah was utterly discouraged in the wilderness, God first let him sleep and eat, and only then spoke to him (1 Kings 19).

## 把忧虑带到神面前 | Bringing worry to God

保罗说：“应当一无挂虑，只要凡事借着祷告、祈求，和感谢，将你们所要的告诉神。神所赐、出人意外的平安必在基督耶稣里保守你们的心怀意念”（腓 4:6-7）。彼得说：“你们要将一切的忧虑卸给神，因为他顾念你们”（彼前 5:7）。<br>
Paul says, “In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus” (Philippians 4:6-7). Peter says, “casting all your worries on him, because he cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7).

保罗写腓立比书的时候正在坐监。他没有说问题会马上消失，而是说神的平安会保守人的心。<br>
Paul wrote Philippians from prison. He does not promise that problems will disappear at once; he says God's peace will guard the heart.

## 几个实际的做法 | Some practical steps

- **具体地说出来**。把让你忧虑的事一件一件写下来，告诉神，也可以告诉一位信得过的朋友。<br>
  **Name it specifically.** Write down what worries you, one item at a time, and tell God; you can also tell a friend you trust.
- **分开能做的和不能做的**。今天能做的一步，就去做；做不了的，用祷告交给神。<br>
  **Separate what you can do from what you can't.** Take the one step you can take today; entrust the rest to God in prayer.
- **一天一天过**。“一天的难处一天当就够了。”<br>
  **Take one day at a time.** “Each day's own evil is sufficient.”
- **感谢**。保罗把“感谢”放在祷告里。记下几件值得感谢的事，可以帮助人看见神的看顾。<br>
  **Give thanks.** Paul includes thanksgiving in prayer. Noting a few things to be thankful for can help you see God's care.
- **照顾身体**。睡眠、饮食和运动都会影响情绪。以利亚的故事提醒我们，身体的需要也是神关心的。<br>
  **Look after your body.** Sleep, food, and exercise all affect mood. Elijah's story reminds us that God cares about bodily needs too.

## 什么时候需要专业的帮助 | When to seek professional help

如果焦虑或低落长期影响睡眠、饮食、工作和人际关系，或者常常有恐慌发作，应当去看医生或找专业的心理辅导。寻求这样的帮助并不表示信心不够，就像身体生病要看医生一样。教会的关怀和专业的帮助可以同时进行。<br>
If anxiety or low mood affects your sleep, eating, work, or relationships for a long time, or you have frequent panic attacks, see a doctor or a professional counsellor. Seeking such help is not a sign of weak faith, just as seeing a doctor for a physical illness is not. The church's care and professional help can go on together.

如果你有伤害自己的念头，请马上联系当地的紧急服务或危机热线，并告诉一个信得过的人，不要一个人承担。<br>
If you have thoughts of harming yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away and tell someone you trust. Don't carry it alone.
