## 信经里讲耶稣的一段最长 | The longest part of the Creed is about Jesus

《使徒信经》第二段讲耶稣基督，占了全篇一半以上。这一段用的都是具体的动词，讲的是历史上发生的事：**降生、受难、被钉十字架、受死、埋葬、复活、升天、将来必从那里降临**。<br>
The second part of the Apostles' Creed is about Jesus Christ and takes up more than half of the whole. It is made of concrete verbs describing things that happened in history: **born, suffered, crucified, died, buried, rose again, ascended, and will come again**.

基督教的中心，是神在历史中借着耶稣基督为我们成就的救赎。<br>
The center of Christianity is the redemption God accomplished for us in history through Jesus Christ.

## 降生（道成肉身）：真神，也是真人 | Incarnation: truly God and truly human

《约翰福音》1:14 说：“道成了肉身，住在我们中间。”永恒的圣子借着童女马利亚降生为人。他没有放下神性，取的也是真实的人性，有人的身体和灵魂。<br>
John 1:14 says, "The Word became flesh, and lived among us." The eternal Son was born as a man through the virgin Mary. He did not lay aside his deity, and the humanity he took was real, with a human body and soul.

这为什么重要？早期教父常这样总结：**只有完全的人，才能真实地代表人类顺服神，在十字架上替人受死，并体恤我们的软弱（来 4:15）。只有完全的神，才能胜过死亡，担当世人的罪，把永生赐给人。**<br>
Why does this matter? The early church fathers often summed it up this way: **only one who is fully human can represent humanity in obeying God, die on the cross in our place, and sympathize with our weaknesses (Hebrews 4:15). Only one who is fully God can overcome death, bear the sins of the world, and give eternal life.**

## 十字架与复活 | The cross and the resurrection

新约从几个角度讲耶稣十字架和复活的意义，这几个角度互相补充：<br>
The New Testament describes the meaning of Jesus' cross and resurrection from several angles that complement one another:

- **代赎与称义**（法庭和圣殿的画面）：他是无瑕疵的羔羊，替我们担当罪债。信靠他的人罪得赦免，在神面前被称为义（赛 53:5；罗 3:24-25）。<br>
  **Atonement and justification** (pictures from the law court and the temple): He is the spotless Lamb who bore our debt of sin. Those who trust him are forgiven and counted righteous before God (Isaiah 53:5; Romans 3:24–25).
- **和好与救赎**（家庭和得释放的画面）：他拆毁了人与神之间的隔墙，把我们从罪和死亡的奴役中赎出来，使我们成为神的儿女（可 10:45；林后 5:18-19）。<br>
  **Reconciliation and ransom** (pictures from the family and from release): He broke down the wall between people and God, ransomed us from slavery to sin and death, and made us God's children (Mark 10:45; 2 Corinthians 5:18–19).
- **复活与升天**（得胜和中保的画面）：他第三天复活，表明罪和死亡已经被打败。复活的基督升天，坐在父的右边，作我们的大祭司为我们代求，并差遣圣灵住在信徒心里，直到他在荣耀中再来，更新万有。<br>
  **Resurrection and ascension** (pictures of victory and of a mediator): He rose on the third day, showing that sin and death have been defeated. The risen Christ ascended and sits at the Father's right hand. He intercedes for us as our Great High Priest and has sent the Holy Spirit to live in believers, until he comes again in glory to renew all things.
