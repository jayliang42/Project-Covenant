## 为什么《使徒信经》最长的段落都在讲耶稣？ | Why the longest section of the Creed is about Jesus

读《使徒信经》时，你会注意到一个特点：讲耶稣基督的第二段占了全篇一半以上的篇幅，而且一连串全是具体的历史动词——**降生、受难、被钉十字架、受死、埋葬、复活、升天、将来必从那里降临**。<br>
When you read the Apostles' Creed, its longest section is the second part — about Jesus Christ — and it is packed with concrete historical verbs: **born, suffered, crucified, died, buried, rose again, ascended, and will come again**.

这是因为基督教的核心不是一套抽象哲学，而是神在耶稣基督里为我们成就的救赎历史。<br>
That is because the heart of Christianity is not an abstract philosophy, but what God has done in history through Jesus Christ.

## 降生（道成肉身）：既是真神，又是真人 | Incarnation: Truly God and truly human

《约翰福音》1:14 说：“道成了肉身，住在我们中间。”永恒的圣子借着童女马利亚降生为人，并不是把神性丢掉，也不是披一件假皮囊，而是真实地取了人的身体与灵魂。<br>
John 1:14 says, "The Word became flesh, and lived among us." Born of the virgin Mary, the eternal Son of God did not stop being God or wear a mere human costume; he took on true humanity.

为什么这至关重要？早期教父常这样总结：**因为只有完全的人，才能真实代表人类顺服神、在十字架上替人受死，并体恤我们的软弱（来 4:15）；也只有完全的神，才能胜过死亡、担当世人的罪，把永生赐给人。**<br>
Why does this matter so deeply? **Only one who is truly human can represent humanity, die in our place, and sympathize with our weaknesses (Hebrews 4:15); and only one who is truly God has the power to bear the sins of the world and conquer death.**

## 十字架与复活：赎罪、胜过死亡与新生命 | Cross and Resurrection: Forgiveness, victory, and new life

新约从几个互补的角度来描绘耶稣十字架与复活的丰盛意义：<br>
The New Testament uses several complementary lenses to show the richness of Jesus' cross and resurrection:

- **代赎与称义（法庭与圣殿画面）**：他是无瑕的羔羊，替我们担当罪债，使信靠他的人罪得赦免、在神面前被称为义（赛 53:5；罗 3:24-25）。<br>
  **Atonement and justification**: As the spotless Lamb, he bore our guilt so that all who trust him are forgiven and declared righteous before God (Isaiah 53:5; Romans 3:24–25).
- **和好与救赎（家庭与得释放画面）**：他拆毁人与神之间的隔墙，把我们从罪和死亡的奴役中买赎出来，成为神的儿女（可 10:45；林后 5:18-19）。<br>
  **Reconciliation and ransom**: He broke down the wall of hostility and ransomed us from bondage to sin and death into God's family (Mark 10:45; 2 Corinthians 5:18–19).
- **复活与升天（得胜与中保画面）**：第三天复活显明罪和死亡已经被击败；如今复活的基督升天坐在父的右边，作我们的大祭司为我们代求，并差遣圣灵住在信徒心里，直到他在荣耀中再来更新万有。<br>
  **Resurrection and ascension**: His bodily resurrection on the third day proves that death is defeated; ascended to the Father's right hand, he intercedes as our Great High Priest and pours out the Holy Spirit until he returns to renew all things.
