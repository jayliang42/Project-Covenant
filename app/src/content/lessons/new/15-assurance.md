## 圣经希望人知道 | Scripture wants you to know

约翰说明了他写信的目的：“我将这些话写给你们信奉神儿子之名的人，要叫你们知道自己有永生”（约壹 5:13）。他用的词是“知道”。圣经没有打算让信徒一辈子猜测自己和神的关系。<br>
John states why he wrote his letter: “These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, that you may know that you have eternal life” (1 John 5:13). The word he uses is “know.” Scripture does not intend believers to spend their lives guessing where they stand with God.

## 确据建立在什么上面 | What assurance rests on

**第一，神的应许**。耶稣说：“我又赐给他们永生；他们永不灭亡，谁也不能从我手里把他们夺去”（约 10:28）。这句话的重点在耶稣的手，不在羊的力气。<br>
**First, God's promise.** Jesus says, “I give eternal life to them. They will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand” (John 10:28). The weight of the sentence falls on Jesus' hand, and the sheep's strength is not mentioned.

**第二，圣灵的见证。**“圣灵与我们的心同证我们是神的儿女”（罗 8:16）。信徒能向神喊“父”，愿意回到他面前，这是圣灵在人心里做的事。<br>
**Second, the witness of the Spirit.** “The Spirit himself testifies with our spirit that we are children of God” (Romans 8:16). When believers call God “Father” and want to come back to him, the Spirit is doing this in them.

**第三，生命的改变。**《约翰一书》提到几个记号：遵行神的话，爱弟兄姊妹，不再安心留在罪里。这些改变常常很慢，但方向变了。彼得也说，要“更加殷勤，使你们所蒙的恩召和拣选坚定不移”（彼后 1:10）。<br>
**Third, a changed life.** 1 John mentions several signs: keeping God's word, loving brothers and sisters, and no longer being at ease in sin. These changes are often slow, but the direction is different. Peter also says to “be more diligent to make your calling and election sure” (2 Peter 1:10).

三样当中，感觉最不稳定。同一个人今天觉得很有把握，明天可能觉得很远。确据要建立在神说过的话上面。<br>
Of these, feelings are the least stable. The same person may feel sure today and far away tomorrow. Assurance has to rest on what God has said.

## 各传统的看法 | What the traditions teach

信徒能有多大的把握，真信徒会不会最后离开信仰，教会里一直有不同的看法。<br>
How sure a believer can be, and whether a true believer can finally fall away, are questions on which the church has long disagreed.

- **改革宗**：《威斯敏斯特信条》第 18 章说，真信徒今生可以确实知道自己在恩典里。它也说，这种确据不是信心必有的部分，真信徒可能等候很久才得着，确据还可能因为疏忽或犯罪而动摇。改革宗同时教导，神会保守真信徒到底。<br>
  **Reformed**: Chapter 18 of the Westminster Confession says true believers may in this life be certainly assured that they are in a state of grace. It also says this assurance does not belong to the essence of faith, that a true believer may wait long for it, and that it can be shaken by negligence or sin. Reformed churches also teach that God keeps true believers to the end.
- **浸信会**：美南浸信会的《信仰与信息》第 5 条说，凡真信徒都必坚忍到底，不会从恩典的地位上失落。很多人把这叫作“一次得救，永远得救”。<br>
  **Baptist**: Article V of the Southern Baptist Faith and Message says that all true believers endure to the end and will never fall away from the state of grace. Many people call this “once saved, always saved.”
- **信义宗**：强调确据来自神在福音、洗礼和圣餐里给人的应许，人要看基督，不要看自己的内心。信义宗也教导，已经称义的人可能离弃信仰。<br>
  **Lutheran**: Lutherans stress that assurance comes from the promise God gives in the gospel, baptism, and the Lord's Supper, so a person should look to Christ and away from the inner self. Lutherans also teach that those who have been justified can fall away from faith.
- **卫斯理宗和循道会**：约翰·卫斯理很看重“圣灵的见证”，认为信徒可以确知自己现在蒙神接纳。同时，联合卫理公会说明，他们不接受“一次得救，永远得救”，认为人可能远离神以致失落。<br>
  **Wesleyan and Methodist**: John Wesley gave great weight to the witness of the Spirit and held that believers can know they are accepted by God now. The United Methodist Church also explains that it does not accept “once saved, always saved” and holds that a person can drift so far from God as to be lost.
- **天主教**：特兰托会议（1547 年）说，若没有特别的启示，没有人能以信德的确定性知道自己已经得到神的恩典，或一定能坚持到底。信徒应当把最坚固的盼望放在神的帮助上。<br>
  **Catholic**: The Council of Trent (1547) says that, apart from a special revelation, no one can know with the certainty of faith that he has obtained God's grace or will surely persevere to the end. Believers are to place the firmest hope in God's help.

各方都同意：救恩从头到尾出于神的恩典，信徒要一直信靠基督，不能因为有把握就随便犯罪。<br>
All sides agree that salvation is by God's grace from start to finish, that believers must go on trusting Christ, and that confidence is no excuse for careless sin.

## 怀疑的时候怎么办 | What to do when you doubt

怀疑的时候，先把眼睛从自己身上移开，去看基督和他的应许。保罗说：“我深信那在你们心里动了善工的，必成全这工，直到耶稣基督的日子”（腓 1:6）。<br>
When you doubt, first turn your eyes away from yourself and look at Christ and his promise. Paul says he is “confident of this very thing, that he who began a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ” (Philippians 1:6).

如果是某件罪让你不安，就向神认罪，回到上一课讲的应许。如果怀疑持续很久，去找牧者或成熟的信徒谈一谈。把疑惑说出来，比一个人闷着想要好。<br>
If a particular sin is troubling you, confess it to God and return to the promise in the previous lesson. If the doubt lasts a long time, talk with a pastor or a mature believer. Saying your doubts aloud is better than turning them over alone.
