## 圣灵所结的果子 | The fruit of the Spirit

保罗写道：“圣灵所结的果子，就是仁爱、喜乐、和平、忍耐、恩慈、良善、信实、温柔、节制”（加 5:22-23）。<br>
Paul writes, “the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith, gentleness, and self-control” (Galatians 5:22-23).

这里的“果子”在原文是单数。九样品格是一个整体，都从同一位圣灵来。这和林前 12 章讲的“恩赐”不同。恩赐各人不同，有人会教导，有人会帮助人；果子却是每个信徒都要结的。<br>
The word “fruit” here is singular in the original. The nine qualities belong together and come from the same Spirit. This differs from the “gifts” in 1 Corinthians 12. Gifts differ from person to person: one person teaches, another helps. The fruit is for every believer to bear.

果子是慢慢长出来的。一棵树不会一夜之间结果，信徒的品格也是这样。保罗说，信徒“就变成主的形状，荣上加荣”（林后 3:18），这是一个持续的过程。<br>
Fruit grows slowly. A tree does not bear fruit overnight, and a believer's character is the same. Paul says believers “are transformed into the same image from glory to glory” (2 Corinthians 3:18). It is an ongoing process.

## 神的工作和人的操练 | God's work and our training

成长靠谁？圣经把两样放在一起。保罗说：“就当恐惧战兢做成你们得救的工夫。因为你们立志行事都是神在你们心里运行”（腓 2:12-13）。<br>
Who does the growing? The Bible puts two things together. Paul says, “work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God who works in you both to will and to work” (Philippians 2:12-13).

“做成得救的工夫”，意思不是靠行为得救。这节经文写给已经信主的人，讲的是把已经得着的救恩活出来。<br>
“Work out your own salvation” does not mean being saved by works. The verse is written to people who already believe. It is about living out a salvation they have already received.

所以圣经也要人操练。保罗对提摩太说：“在敬虔上操练自己”（提前 4:7）。彼得列了一串要“加上”的品格：德行、知识、节制、忍耐、虔敬、爱弟兄的心、爱众人的心（彼后 1:5-7）。<br>
So the Bible also calls people to train. Paul tells Timothy, “Exercise yourself toward godliness” (1 Timothy 4:7). Peter lists qualities to “add”: moral excellence, knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, brotherly affection, and love (2 Peter 1:5-7).

## 几个实际的做法 | Some practical steps

- **每次只看一样**。比如这个月特别留意“忍耐”，记下自己在什么时候最不耐烦，为这件事祷告。<br>
  **Focus on one at a time.** For example, this month pay attention to patience. Note when you are most impatient, and pray about it.
- **让读经改变想法**。保罗说要“心意更新而变化”（罗 12:2）。行为的改变常常从想法的改变开始。前面“怎样开始每天读一点圣经”一课讲了怎样开始。<br>
  **Let Bible reading change your thinking.** Paul speaks of being “transformed by the renewing of your mind” (Romans 12:2). Changed behaviour often begins with changed thinking. The earlier lesson on starting to read the Bible each day explains how to start.
- **请一位信得过的弟兄姊妹帮你**。别人常常比我们自己更看得清我们的样子。<br>
  **Ask a fellow believer you trust to help you.** Others often see us more clearly than we see ourselves.
- **失败了就回来**。成长的路上会跌倒。“信主以后又犯错了，怎么办？”一课讲了怎样回到神面前。<br>
  **When you fail, come back.** There will be falls along the way. The lesson on failing after coming to faith explains how to return to God.

## 今生能成长到什么地步 | How far can we grow in this life

各传统都认为信徒应当成长，但对今生能成长到什么地步，看法不同。<br>
Every tradition holds that believers should grow, but they differ on how far that growth can go in this life.

- **改革宗**：《威斯敏斯德信条》说，成圣遍及整个人，“但在今生是不完全的”，信徒里面一直有肉体和圣灵的相争（参加 5:17），直到离世。<br>
  **Reformed**: The Westminster Confession says sanctification reaches the whole person, “yet imperfect in this life.” There remains a struggle between the flesh and the Spirit within believers (compare Galatians 5:17) until death.
- **卫斯理宗和圣洁运动**：例如拿撒勒人会相信“完全成圣”，认为这是神在人重生以后的一次工作，使人全心爱神，有完全的爱。<br>
  **Wesleyan and Holiness**: The Church of the Nazarene, for example, believes in “entire sanctification,” an act of God after regeneration that brings a person into wholehearted love for God and perfect love.
- **天主教和东正教**：把成圣看作一生和神合作的过程。东正教用“神化”来描述人分享神的生命。<br>
  **Catholic and Orthodox**: They see sanctification as a lifelong process of cooperating with God. Orthodox tradition uses “theosis” to describe sharing in God's life.
