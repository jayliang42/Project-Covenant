## 最后的晚餐和客西马尼 | The last supper and Gethsemane

逾越节的晚上，耶稣和门徒一起吃饭。他拿起饼来说：“你们拿着吃，这是我的身体。”又拿起杯来说：“这是我立约的血，为多人流出来的”（可 14:22-24）。逾越节纪念神救以色列人出埃及，耶稣在这一天说明了自己的死是为了什么。<br>
On the night of Passover Jesus eats with the disciples. He takes bread and says, “Take, eat. This is my body.” He takes the cup and says, “This is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many” (Mark 14:22-24). Passover remembers God rescuing Israel from Egypt, and on this day Jesus explains what his own death is for.

饭后，他们到了客西马尼园。耶稣心里非常忧伤，祷告说：“阿爸！父啊！在你凡事都能；求你将这杯撤去。然而，不要从我的意思，只要从你的意思”（可 14:36）。他说出了自己的愿望，然后顺服了父。<br>
After the meal they go to the garden of Gethsemane. Jesus is in great distress and prays, “Abba, Father, all things are possible to you. Please remove this cup from me. However, not what I desire, but what you desire” (Mark 14:36). He states his own wish and then submits to the Father.

犹大带人来捉拿耶稣，门徒都离开他逃走了（14:50）。彼得远远地跟到大祭司的院子里，在鸡叫两遍以前三次说不认得他（14:66-72）。<br>
Judas arrives with men to arrest Jesus, and the disciples all leave him and flee (14:50). Peter follows at a distance into the high priest's courtyard, and before the rooster crows twice he says three times that he does not know him (14:66-72).

## 受审和钉十字架 | The trials and the crucifixion

大祭司问耶稣：“你是那当称颂者的儿子基督不是？”耶稣说：“我是”（14:61-62）。公会就定他该死。罗马巡抚彼拉多查不出他有什么罪，却为了讨众人的喜欢，把他交给人钉十字架（15:15）。<br>
The high priest asks Jesus, “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?” Jesus says, “I am” (14:61-62). The council condemns him as deserving death. The Roman governor Pilate finds no crime in him, yet to please the crowd he hands him over to be crucified (15:15).

从正午到下午三点，遍地都黑暗了。耶稣大声喊着说：“我的神！我的神！为什么离弃我？”（可 15:34）这是《诗篇》22 篇的第一句。然后他大声喊叫，气就断了。<br>
From noon until three in the afternoon darkness covers the whole land. Jesus cries out with a loud voice, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” (Mark 15:34). This is the first line of Psalm 22. Then he cries out loudly and breathes his last.

这时发生了两件事。“殿里的幔子从上到下裂为两半。”站在对面的罗马百夫长说：“这人真是神的儿子！”（可 15:38-39）全书第一句话称耶稣为“神的儿子”，到了这里，第一次有人亲口这样说，说的人是一个外邦军官，地点是在十字架下。<br>
Two things happen at that moment. “The veil of the temple was torn in two from the top to the bottom.” The Roman centurion standing opposite says, “Truly this man was the Son of God!” (Mark 15:38-39). The first sentence of the book calls Jesus “the Son of God.” Here, for the first time, a human being says so aloud. The speaker is a Gentile officer, and the place is the foot of the cross.

## 空坟墓 | The empty tomb

安息日过后的清早，几位妇女带着香膏到坟墓那里，看见石头已经滚开。一位穿白袍的少年人对她们说：“你们寻找那钉十字架的拿撒勒人耶稣，他已经复活了，不在这里”（可 16:6）。<br>
Early on the morning after the Sabbath, several women bring spices to the tomb and find the stone already rolled away. A young man in a white robe says to them, “You seek Jesus, the Nazarene, who has been crucified. He has risen. He is not here” (Mark 16:6).

他又说：“你们可以去告诉他的门徒和彼得”（可 16:7）。彼得刚刚三次不认主，这里特别提到他的名字。<br>
He adds, “go, tell his disciples and Peter” (Mark 16:7). Peter has just denied the Lord three times, and he is mentioned here by name.

## 关于 16:9-20 | About Mark 16:9-20

你的圣经在 16:8 之后可能有括号或注脚。原因是这样的：现存最早的两部希腊文全本抄本，就是 4 世纪的西奈抄本和梵蒂冈抄本，都在 16:8 结束：“她们……什么也不告诉人，因为她们害怕”（可 16:8）。4 世纪的优西比乌也说，他见到的准确抄本大多在这里结束。绝大多数较晚的抄本有 16:9-20。<br>
Your Bible may have brackets or a footnote after 16:8. The reason is this. The two earliest surviving complete Greek manuscripts, Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Vaticanus from the 4th century, both end at 16:8: “They said nothing to anyone; for they were afraid” (Mark 16:8). Eusebius, also in the 4th century, says that the accurate copies he knew mostly ended here. The great majority of later manuscripts include 16:9-20.

学者的看法不一。多数学者认为 9-20 节是后人补上的，内容取自其他福音书和《使徒行传》里的记载。也有学者主张它是原有的。多数现代译本保留这段经文，同时加注说明。<br>
Scholars differ. Most think verses 9-20 were added later, drawing on accounts in the other Gospels and Acts. Some argue that the passage is original. Most modern translations keep the passage and add a note.

这并不影响复活这件事本身。16:1-8 已经宣告耶稣复活了，另外三卷福音书和保罗的书信（例如林前 15:3-8）也都有记载。<br>
None of this affects the resurrection itself. Mark 16:1-8 already announces that Jesus has risen, and the other three Gospels and Paul's letters (for example 1 Corinthians 15:3-8) record it as well.

This week, read Mark 14–16. It takes about 20 minutes. When you finish, you will have read one Gospel from beginning to end.
