## 圣经说了两件事 | Two things the Bible says

圣经清楚地说，神只有一位。以色列人每天背诵：“以色列啊，你要听！耶和华我们神是独一的主”（申 6:4）。<br>
The Bible says clearly that there is only one God. Israel recited every day, “Hear, Israel: Yahweh is our God. Yahweh is one” (Deuteronomy 6:4).

圣经也说，父是神，子是神，圣灵也是神。约翰说：“太初有道，道与神同在，道就是神”（约 1:1）。彼得对欺哄圣灵的亚拿尼亚说，他“不是欺哄人，是欺哄神了”（徒 5:3-4）。<br>
The Bible also says the Father is God, the Son is God, and the Spirit is God. John writes, “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God” (John 1:1). Peter told Ananias, who had lied to the Holy Spirit, “You haven’t lied to men, but to God” (Acts 5:3–4).

而且父、子、圣灵彼此有别。耶稣受洗的时候，子在水里，圣灵像鸽子降下，父从天上说：“这是我的爱子，我所喜悦的”（太 3:17）。耶稣向父祷告，也应许父要差圣灵来。<br>
And the Father, the Son, and the Spirit are distinct from one another. At Jesus' baptism, the Son is in the water, the Spirit descends like a dove, and the Father says from heaven, “This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased” (Matthew 3:17). Jesus prays to the Father and promises that the Father will send the Spirit.

## “三位一体”这个说法 | The word “Trinity”

“三位一体”这个词在圣经里找不到。早期教会为了把圣经的这些话放在一起说清楚，才用了这个词。主后 325 年的尼西亚会议和 381 年的君士坦丁堡会议，把它写进了信经：一位神，有父、子、圣灵三个位格，三者同等、同永恒。<br>
The word “Trinity” does not appear in the Bible. The early church used it to put these biblical statements together clearly. The Council of Nicaea in AD 325 and the Council of Constantinople in AD 381 set it down in the creeds: one God in three persons, Father, Son, and Spirit, equal and eternal.

## 三种常见的误解 | Three common misunderstandings

- **三神论**：以为有三位神。这违背了“神只有一位”。<br>
  **Tritheism**: The idea that there are three gods. This contradicts the truth that God is one.
- **形态论**：以为只有一位神，有时以父的身份出现，有时以子或圣灵的身份出现，就像一个人换三副面具。这和耶稣受洗时三位同时出现、耶稣向父祷告的记载对不上。<br>
  **Modalism**: The idea that there is one God who sometimes appears as the Father and sometimes as the Son or the Spirit, like one person wearing three masks. This does not fit the baptism of Jesus, where all three appear together, or Jesus praying to the Father.
- **从属论**：以为只有父是神，子和圣灵是父造的。4 世纪的亚流（Arius）这样主张，尼西亚会议否定了这个看法。<br>
  **Subordinationism**: The idea that only the Father is God, and the Son and the Spirit were made by him. Arius taught this in the 4th century, and the Council of Nicaea rejected it.

人常用比喻来讲三位一体，例如水、冰和水蒸气。这些比喻都有局限，有的还会把人带到形态论那里去。三位一体是神启示给人的，人可以认识，却没有办法完全想透。<br>
People often use analogies for the Trinity, such as water, ice, and steam. All of them fall short, and some lead toward modalism. The Trinity is something God has revealed. We can know it, but we cannot fully comprehend it.

## 这和信徒有什么关系 | Why it matters to believers

- **神本来就是爱**：耶稣说，父在“创立世界以前”就爱他（约 17:24）。在创造任何东西以前，父、子、圣灵之间已经有爱。所以约翰可以说“神就是爱”（约壹 4:8）。<br>
  **God is love in himself**: Jesus said the Father loved him “before the foundation of the world” (John 17:24). Before anything was made, there was love among the Father, the Son, and the Spirit. That is why John can say “God is love” (1 John 4:8).
- **救恩是三位一起做的**：父差子来，子为我们死而复活，圣灵把这一切带进我们心里。保罗说：“神就差他儿子的灵进入你们的心，呼叫：‘阿爸！父！’”（加 4:6）。<br>
  **Salvation is the work of all three**: The Father sends the Son, the Son dies and rises for us, and the Spirit brings it all into our hearts. Paul writes, “God sent out the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, ‘Abba, Father!’” (Galatians 4:6).
- **祷告**：基督徒通常靠着圣灵，奉子的名，向父祷告。教会的祝福也常用这句话：“愿主耶稣基督的恩惠、神的慈爱、圣灵的感动常与你们众人同在！”（林后 13:14）。<br>
  **Prayer**: Christians usually pray to the Father, in the name of the Son, by the help of the Spirit. Churches often close with this blessing: “The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, God’s love, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all” (2 Corinthians 13:14).
