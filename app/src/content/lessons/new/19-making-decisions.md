## 神已经说明的事 | What God has already stated

圣经讲“神的旨意”，大多数时候讲的是神要人怎样生活。例如：“神的旨意就是要你们成为圣洁，远避淫行”（帖前 4:3）。诚实、爱人、饶恕、不贪心，这些神都已经说清楚了。<br>
When the Bible speaks of “the will of God,” it mostly means how God wants people to live. For example: “this is the will of God: your sanctification, that you abstain from sexual immorality” (1 Thessalonians 4:3). Truthfulness, love, forgiveness, and freedom from greed are all things God has already made clear.

所以做决定的第一步，是看这件事圣经有没有明确的教导。如果一个选择需要你撒谎、欺骗或伤害人，就不必再问它是不是神的带领。<br>
So the first step in a decision is to see whether the Bible gives clear teaching on it. If an option requires you to lie, cheat, or harm someone, there is no need to ask further whether God is leading you to it.

## 圣经没有直接说明的事 | What the Bible leaves open

很多决定圣经没有直接讲：读哪个学校，接受哪份工作，住在哪个城市。圣经在这里给的是做决定的方法。<br>
Many decisions are not addressed directly in the Bible: which school to attend, which job to take, which city to live in. Here the Bible gives a way of deciding.

- **祷告求智慧**：“你们中间若有缺少智慧的，应当求那厚赐与众人、也不斥责人的神”（雅 1:5）。<br>
  **Pray for wisdom**: “if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all liberally and without reproach” (James 1:5).
- **让圣经更新你的想法**：“不要效法这个世界，只要心意更新而变化，叫你们察验何为神的善良、纯全、可喜悦的旨意”（罗 12:2）。<br>
  **Let Scripture renew your thinking**: “Don’t be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may prove what is the good, well-pleasing, and perfect will of God” (Romans 12:2).
- **向人请教**：“不先商议，所谋无效；谋士众多，所谋乃成”（箴 15:22）。<br>
  **Ask for advice**: “Where there is no counsel, plans fail; but in a multitude of counselors they are established” (Proverbs 15:22).
- **了解实际情况**：自己的能力、责任、经济状况，还有这个决定对家人的影响。<br>
  **Know the facts**: your abilities, your responsibilities, your finances, and how the decision affects your family.

## 两种常见的理解 | Two common understandings

信徒对“神的带领”有两种常见的理解。<br>
Believers commonly understand God's leading in one of two ways.

- **寻求神具体的指示**：这种看法认为，神对每个信徒的人生有一个具体的计划，包括和谁结婚、做什么工作。信徒要借着祷告、内心的感动、环境的印证和别人的话，来分辨神在这件事上的心意。五旬节派和灵恩派的教会特别看重圣灵直接的引导，很多福音派信徒也这样理解。天主教有悠久的“分辨”传统，例如耶稣会创始人依纳爵的《神操》。<br>
  **Seeking God's specific direction**: On this view God has a detailed plan for each believer's life, including whom to marry and what work to do. The believer discerns God's mind on the matter through prayer, inner prompting, confirming circumstances, and the words of others. Pentecostal and charismatic churches give special weight to the Spirit's direct guidance, and many evangelicals share this understanding. The Catholic Church has a long tradition of discernment, for example the Spiritual Exercises of Ignatius of Loyola, founder of the Jesuits.
- **凭智慧选择**：这种看法认为，圣经已经足够指导人的生活。圣经有教导的事，信徒要顺服。圣经没有讲的事，信徒可以自由选择，也要为选择负责，只要在神的道德旨意之内，凭智慧做的决定神都接纳。Garry Friesen 的《Decision Making and the Will of God》一书详细讲了这种看法。<br>
  **Choosing with wisdom**: On this view the Bible is sufficient to guide a person's life. Where the Bible teaches, believers obey. Where it is silent, believers are free to choose and are responsible for the choice, and God accepts any wise decision made within his moral will. Garry Friesen sets out this view at length in Decision Making and the Will of God.

两种看法都同意：要祷告，要顺服圣经，要听别人的建议，神掌管信徒的一生。持第一种看法的人需要小心，不要把自己的愿望当成神的声音。持第二种看法的人也需要小心，不要只靠分析而忘记祷告。<br>
Both views agree that a believer should pray, obey Scripture, listen to advice, and trust that God governs the whole of life. Those who hold the first view need to take care that they do not mistake their own wishes for God's voice. Those who hold the second need to take care that they do not rely on analysis and forget to pray.

## 做了决定以后 | After you decide

《箴言》说：“你要专心仰赖耶和华，不可倚靠自己的聪明，在你一切所行的事上都要认定他，他必指引你的路”（箴 3:5-6）。这句话没有保证每个决定的结果都如人所愿，它应许的是神会引导信靠他的人。<br>
Proverbs says, “Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don’t lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight” (Proverbs 3:5-6). The verse does not guarantee that every decision turns out as hoped. It promises that God guides those who trust him.

做了决定以后，如果后来发现选得不好，也不必一直责怪自己。神能在人的错误里继续做工，你可以把下一步再次交给他。<br>
If a decision later proves to be a poor one, there is no need to keep blaming yourself. God can go on working through human mistakes, and you can hand the next step to him again.
