## 恩赐是什么 | What gifts are

保罗说：“恩赐原有分别，圣灵却是一位……圣灵显在各人身上，是叫人得益处”（林前 12:4，7）。恩赐是神给的能力或机会，用来服事别人。每个信徒都有，彼得说：“各人要照所得的恩赐彼此服事”（彼前 4:10）。<br>
Paul writes, “There are various kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit ... to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the profit of all” (1 Corinthians 12:4, 7). A gift is an ability or opportunity God gives for serving others. Every believer has one. Peter says, “As each has received a gift, employ it in serving one another” (1 Peter 4:10).

新约里提到的恩赐很多，大致可以分成几类：<br>
The New Testament mentions many gifts. They can be grouped roughly like this:

- **说话的恩赐**：教导、劝勉、传福音、带领查经。<br>
  **Gifts of speaking**: teaching, encouraging, telling others the gospel, leading Bible studies.
- **服务的恩赐**：执事、施舍、治理、怜悯人（罗 12:6-8），还有各种实际的帮助。<br>
  **Gifts of serving**: serving, giving, leading, and showing mercy (Romans 12:6–8), along with all kinds of practical help.
- **其他恩赐**：新约也提到医病、说方言、翻方言等（林前 12:8-10）。这些恩赐今天怎样运作，不同教会传统的看法不一样。<br>
  **Other gifts**: The New Testament also mentions healing, speaking in tongues, and interpreting tongues (1 Corinthians 12:8–10). Church traditions differ on how these gifts work today.

## 怎样找到自己可以服事的地方 | Finding a place to serve

很多人一开始不知道自己有什么恩赐。比较实际的做法是先去服事，在做的过程中慢慢发现：<br>
Many people do not know their gifts at first. The practical approach is to start serving and discover them along the way:

1. **从有需要的地方开始**：接待新朋友、摆椅子、带孩子、做饭、开车接送。<br>
   **Start where there is a need**: welcoming newcomers, setting up chairs, helping with children, cooking, giving rides.
2. **留意自己做什么比较顺手，也有果效**：哪些事你做得比较好，别人也因此得到帮助？<br>
   **Notice what comes naturally and bears fruit**: Which tasks do you do fairly well, in a way that actually helps others?
3. **问身边的人**：请认识你的弟兄姊妹说说，他们看到你在哪些方面帮助过人。<br>
   **Ask others**: Ask brothers and sisters who know you where they have seen you help people.

保罗说：“眼不能对手说：‘我用不着你。’”（林前 12:21）。不起眼的服事和台前的服事一样重要。<br>
Paul says, “The eye can’t tell the hand, ‘I have no need for you’” (1 Corinthians 12:21). Unseen service matters as much as service up front.

## 服事的态度 | The attitude behind serving

耶稣给门徒洗脚以后说：“我给你们作了榜样，叫你们照着我向你们所做的去做”（约 13:15）。他还说：“你们中间，谁愿为大，就必作你们的用人”（可 10:43）。<br>
After washing his disciples' feet Jesus said, “I have given you an example, that you should also do as I have done to you” (John 13:15). He also said, “Whoever wants to become great among you shall be your servant” (Mark 10:43).

保罗在讲恩赐的两章中间，放了讲爱的一章。他说，一个人就算能说万人的方言，“却没有爱，我就成了鸣的锣，响的钹一般”（林前 13:1）。<br>
Between his two chapters on gifts, Paul put a chapter on love. He says that even if someone speaks with the languages of men and angels, “but don’t have love, I have become sounding brass, or a clanging cymbal” (1 Corinthians 13:1).

服事也要注意休息。不要一下子答应太多，弄得自己筋疲力尽。服事要能做得长久，可以和其他人轮流分担。<br>
Rest matters too. Do not take on so much at once that you burn out. Serving should be something you can keep doing, and the load can be shared and rotated with others.
