## 从福音书开始 | Start with a Gospel

如果不知道从哪里读起，可以先读马可福音。它是四福音书里最短的一卷，节奏快，一直把目光放在耶稣身上。每天读十几节到一章就够了。

If you don't know where to begin, try Mark. It is the shortest Gospel, fast-paced and focused on Jesus. Ten-odd verses to a chapter a day is plenty.

## 三个问题 | Three questions

读完一段，问自己三个问题：

After each passage, ask three questions:

1. **这段经文说了什么？**用自己的话复述一遍。<br>**What does it say?** Retell it in your own words.
2. **它让我看见神或耶稣是怎样的？**<br>**What does it show me about God or Jesus?**
3. **今天我可以怎样回应？**可以是一句祷告、一个改变，或者一个要问别人的问题。<br>**How can I respond today?** A prayer, a change, or a question to ask someone.

## 漏了几天也没关系 | Missing a day is fine

习惯靠的是“回来”，不是“从不中断”。漏了几天，就从今天接着读，不用补，也不必自责。这个 app 只记录你读过什么，不会因为你中断而扣分。

Habits are built by coming back, not by never missing. If you skip a few days, pick up today. No catching up and no guilt. This app records what you've read; it never penalises a break.
