## 圣灵是谁 | Who the Holy Spirit is

很多人觉得父和子比较好理解，圣灵却很模糊，好像是一种能量或气氛。圣经讲圣灵的时候，用的是讲一位“他”的语言：他教导人（约 14:26），他会担忧（弗 4:30），他替人祷告（罗 8:26）。<br>
Many people find the Father and the Son easier to grasp, while the Spirit seems vague, like a kind of energy or atmosphere. When the Bible speaks of the Spirit, it uses the language of a person: he teaches (John 14:26), he can be grieved (Ephesians 4:30), and he prays for people (Romans 8:26).

圣灵就是神。圣经第二节就提到“神的灵运行在水面上”（创 1:2）。耶稣吩咐门徒“奉父、子、圣灵的名”施洗（太 28:19），把圣灵和父、子并列。<br>
The Holy Spirit is God. The Bible's second verse already says “God’s Spirit was hovering over the surface of the waters” (Genesis 1:2). Jesus told his disciples to baptize “in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit” (Matthew 28:19), naming the Spirit alongside the Father and the Son.

耶稣离世前对门徒说：“我要求父，父就另外赐给你们一位保惠师，叫他永远与你们同在，就是真理的圣灵”（约 14:16-17）。“保惠师”的意思是在身边帮助、安慰、为人说话的那一位。<br>
Before he died Jesus told his disciples, “I will pray to the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, that he may be with you forever: the Spirit of truth” (John 14:16–17). The word translated “Counselor” means one who stands beside you to help, comfort, and speak for you.

## 圣灵在信徒身上做什么 | What the Spirit does in believers

- **使人重生**：耶稣说，人要“从水和圣灵生”才能进神的国（约 3:5）。一个人能信耶稣，本身就是圣灵的工作。<br>
  **He gives new birth**: Jesus said a person must be “born of water and spirit” to enter God's Kingdom (John 3:5). A person's coming to faith is itself the Spirit's work.
- **住在信徒里面**：保罗说，信徒的身子“就是圣灵的殿”（林前 6:19）。<br>
  **He lives in believers**: Paul says a believer's body “is a temple of the Holy Spirit” (1 Corinthians 6:19).
- **让人知道自己是神的儿女**：“圣灵与我们的心同证我们是神的儿女”（罗 8:16）。<br>
  **He assures us we are God's children**: “The Spirit himself testifies with our spirit that we are children of God” (Romans 8:16).
- **帮助人祷告和明白真理**：我们不晓得怎样祷告的时候，“圣灵亲自用说不出来的叹息替我们祷告”（罗 8:26）。他也叫门徒想起耶稣说过的话（约 14:26）。<br>
  **He helps us pray and understand**: When we do not know how to pray, “the Spirit himself makes intercession for us with groanings which can’t be uttered” (Romans 8:26). He also reminds disciples of what Jesus said (John 14:26).
- **改变人的品格**：“圣灵所结的果子，就是仁爱、喜乐、和平、忍耐、恩慈、良善、信实、温柔、节制”（加 5:22-23）。果子是慢慢长出来的，需要时间。<br>
  **He changes our character**: “The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith, gentleness, and self-control” (Galatians 5:22–23). Fruit grows slowly and takes time.
- **给人能力作见证**：“圣灵降临在你们身上，你们就必得着能力……作我的见证”（徒 1:8）。<br>
  **He gives power to witness**: “You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you. You will be witnesses to me” (Acts 1:8).

圣灵还赐给信徒不同的恩赐，让他们服事教会。不同的教会传统对说方言、预言这类恩赐看法不一。“在教会中成长”单元有一课专门讲恩赐和服事。<br>
The Spirit also gives believers different gifts for serving the church. Church traditions differ in how they understand gifts such as speaking in tongues and prophecy. A lesson in the “Growing in the church” unit covers gifts and serving.

## 信经最后几句 | The Creed's closing lines

《使徒信经》在“我信圣灵”之后接着说：我信圣而公之教会，我信圣徒相通，我信罪得赦免，我信身体复活，我信永生。<br>
After “I believe in the Holy Spirit,” the Apostles' Creed goes on: I believe in the holy catholic church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting.

这几句讲的都是圣灵在做的事：把信徒聚成一个教会（“公”是普世的意思，指各地所有的信徒），让他们彼此相通，赦免他们的罪，最后叫他们的身体复活，得着永远的生命。<br>
These lines all describe the Spirit's work: gathering believers into one church (“catholic” here means universal, all believers everywhere), joining them to one another, forgiving their sins, and finally raising their bodies to eternal life.
