## “圣”和“公”是什么意思 | What “holy” and “catholic” mean

《使徒信经》说：“我信圣而公之教会。”这里的“公”常常被人误会。它译自希腊文 katholikos，意思是“普世的”，指各地、各时代所有属基督的人。它在这里是一个形容词，说的是教会的范围，不专指今天叫“天主教”的那个教会。<br>
The Apostles' Creed says, “I believe in the holy catholic church.” The word “catholic” is often misunderstood. It comes from the Greek katholikos, meaning “universal,” and refers to all who belong to Christ in every place and age. Here it is an adjective that describes how wide the church is. It does not point only to the church now called “Catholic.”

“圣”的意思是“分别出来归给神”。教会里的人仍然会犯错，教会历史上也有很多过失。教会被称为“圣”，是因为它属于神，神也在其中一步一步地洁净他的子民。<br>
“Holy” means “set apart for God.” People in the church still do wrong, and church history holds many failures. The church is called holy because it belongs to God, and God is at work in it, cleansing his people step by step.

保罗写道：“身体只有一个，圣灵只有一个……一主，一信，一洗，一神”（弗 4:4-6）。信徒分散在不同的国家，用不同的语言敬拜，属于不同的宗派，但他们同有一位主。<br>
Paul writes, “There is one body and one Spirit... one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all” (Ephesians 4:4-6). Believers live in different countries, worship in different languages, and belong to different denominations, yet they have one Lord.

## 教会是一群人 | The church is a people

新约里的“教会”一词，指的是被神召聚的一群人。保罗对哥林多的信徒说：“你们就是基督的身子，并且各自作肢体”（林前 12:27）。身体的各部分彼此需要，信徒也是这样。<br>
In the New Testament the word “church” refers to a people God has called together. Paul tells the believers in Corinth, “Now you are the body of Christ, and members individually” (1 Corinthians 12:27). The parts of a body need one another, and so do believers.

所以圣经里没有“独自一人的基督徒”这种设想。最早的信徒“都恒心遵守使徒的教训，彼此交接，擘饼，祈祷”（徒 2:42）。<br>
So the Bible does not picture a Christian who lives the faith alone. The first believers “continued steadfastly in the apostles’ teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and prayer” (Acts 2:42).

## “圣徒相通”是什么意思 | What “the communion of saints” means

新约里的“圣徒”指所有属基督的人。保罗写信给各地教会，常常称收信人为“圣徒”，这些人当中也有软弱、犯错的。“相通”的意思是“同有一份”：信徒一同领受基督的恩典，也彼此分担、彼此帮助。<br>
In the New Testament, “saints” means all who belong to Christ. Paul often addresses the churches he writes to as “saints,” and these included weak and failing people. “Communion” means “sharing in common”: believers share together in the grace of Christ, and they also carry one another's burdens and help one another.

这种相通也跨过时间。《希伯来书》列举了历代有信心的人，然后说：“我们既有这许多的见证人，如同云彩围着我们”（来 12:1）。今天的信徒和从前的信徒，属于同一个身体。<br>
This communion also reaches across time. Hebrews lists people of faith from past ages and then says that “we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses” (Hebrews 12:1). Believers today and believers of the past belong to one body.

## 各传统怎样理解 | How the traditions understand it

各传统都同意上面这些内容。不同的地方，在于活着的信徒和已经离世的信徒之间有怎样的联系。<br>
All the traditions agree on what is said above. They differ on how living believers are connected with believers who have died.

- **天主教**：《天主教教理》说，教会有三种状态：在世上的信徒，死后正在被炼净的人，和已经在荣耀里的人。在天上的圣徒不断为我们向父代求，所以信徒可以请他们代祷。教会也为离世的人祷告。<br>
  **Catholic**: The Catechism of the Catholic Church says the church exists in three states: believers on earth, those who have died and are being purified, and those already in glory. The saints in heaven do not stop interceding with the Father for us, so believers may ask for their prayers. The church also prays for the dead.
- **东正教**：同样请圣徒和主的母亲马利亚代祷，也为离世的人祷告。东正教的作者强调，圣徒是“代祷者”，中保只有基督一位。<br>
  **Orthodox**: Orthodox Christians likewise ask the saints and Mary the mother of the Lord to pray for them, and they pray for the departed. Orthodox writers stress that the saints are “intercessors,” while Christ alone is the mediator.
- **新教**：纪念历代的圣徒，效法他们的信心和好行为。但祷告只向神，借着基督。《奥斯堡信条》第 21 条说，圣经没有教导人呼求圣徒，并指出基督是唯一的中保。大多数新教教会也不为离世的人祷告。<br>
  **Protestant**: Protestants remember the saints of every age and follow their faith and good works. Prayer, however, is directed to God alone, through Christ. Article XXI of the Augsburg Confession says Scripture does not teach the invocation of saints and points to Christ as the one mediator. Most Protestant churches also do not pray for the dead.

如果你所在的教会属于其中一个传统，可以请牧者讲讲他们为什么这样理解。<br>
If your church belongs to one of these traditions, ask a pastor or priest to explain why they understand it this way.
