## 信主以后还会犯错 | Christians still fail

刚信主的人常常会失望：以为信了耶稣就不会再发脾气、说谎、看不该看的东西，结果过几天又犯了。有人会想：“我是不是没有真信？”<br>
New believers are often disappointed. They expected that after trusting Jesus they would stop losing their temper, lying, or looking at things they should not, and a few days later they fail again. Some wonder, “Did I really believe at all?”

圣经从来没有说基督徒不会再犯罪。约翰写信给信徒说：“我们若说自己无罪，便是自欺，真理不在我们心里了”（约壹 1:8）。保罗也说：“我所愿意的善，我反不做；我所不愿意的恶，我倒去做”（罗 7:19）。<br>
The Bible never says Christians stop sinning. John wrote to believers, “If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us” (1 John 1:8). Paul also wrote, “The good which I desire, I don’t do; but the evil which I don’t desire, that I practice” (Romans 7:19).

## 认罪是什么 | What confession is

认罪就是对神说实话：我做错了什么，这样做是错的。不需要特别的格式，也不需要先让自己难过到一定程度。大卫说：“我向你陈明我的罪，不隐瞒我的恶……你就赦免我的罪恶”（诗 32:5）。<br>
Confession means telling God the truth: what I did, and that it was wrong. It needs no special formula, and you do not have to make yourself feel bad enough first. David said, “I acknowledged my sin to you. I didn’t hide my iniquity ... and you forgave the iniquity of my sin” (Psalm 32:5).

约翰写道：“我们若认自己的罪，神是信实的，是公义的，必要赦免我们的罪，洗净我们一切的不义”（约壹 1:9）。神赦免，是因为耶稣已经为这些罪付上了代价，不是因为我们这次表现得够好。<br>
John writes, “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us the sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” (1 John 1:9). God forgives because Jesus has already paid for these sins. It does not depend on how well we did this time.

如果伤害了别人，认罪还包括向那个人道歉，能弥补的尽量弥补（太 5:23-24）。<br>
If you have hurt someone, confession also means apologizing to that person and making things right where you can (Matthew 5:23–24).

## 彼得的故事 | Peter's story

彼得曾说，就是和耶稣一同死也不会不认他。可是耶稣被捕那一夜，他三次说不认识耶稣。鸡叫的时候，“主转过身来看彼得”，彼得“就出去痛哭”（路 22:61-62）。<br>
Peter once said he would die with Jesus rather than deny him. But on the night Jesus was arrested, he said three times that he did not know him. When the rooster crowed, “the Lord turned and looked at Peter,” and Peter “went out, and wept bitterly” (Luke 22:61–62).

耶稣复活以后，在加利利湖边问彼得三次：“你爱我吗？”彼得每次都说爱，耶稣每次都把工作交给他：“你喂养我的羊”（约 21:15-17）。后来，彼得在五旬节那天站起来讲道。<br>
After his resurrection, by the Sea of Galilee, Jesus asked Peter three times, “Do you love me?” Each time Peter said he did, and each time Jesus gave him work to do: “Feed my sheep” (John 21:15–17). Later, on the day of Pentecost, Peter stood up and preached.

## 几个实际的建议 | Some practical suggestions

- **犯错后尽快回到神面前**，不用等到“状态好一点”再祷告。<br>
  **Come back to God quickly after you fail.** Do not wait until you feel better before you pray.
- **找一位信得过的基督徒朋友**，把反复跌倒的事告诉他，请他为你祷告（雅 5:16）。<br>
  **Find a Christian friend you trust.** Tell them about the things you keep falling into, and ask them to pray for you (James 5:16).
- **分清内疚和定罪**：内疚让你回到神面前，认罪以后就可以放下。保罗说：“如今，那些在基督耶稣里的就不定罪了”（罗 8:1）。<br>
  **Tell guilt and condemnation apart**: Guilt sends you back to God, and once you have confessed, you can let it go. Paul says, “There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus” (Romans 8:1).
