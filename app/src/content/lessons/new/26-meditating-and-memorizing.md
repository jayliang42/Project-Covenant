## 读过就忘 | Read and forgotten

很多人有这样的经验：早上读了一章圣经，到中午已经想不起读了什么。圣经对神的话的期待是：它要留在人心里。诗篇第 1 篇说，有福的人“惟喜爱耶和华的律法，昼夜思想”（诗 1:2）。诗人又说：“我将你的话藏在心里，免得我得罪你”（诗 119:11）。<br>
Many people know this experience: in the morning they read a chapter of the Bible, and by noon they cannot remember what it said. The Bible expects God's word to stay in a person's heart. Psalm 1 says the blessed person's “delight is in Yahweh's law. On his law he meditates day and night” (Psalm 1:2). Another psalmist says, “I have hidden your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you” (Psalm 119:11).

## 什么是默想 | What meditation is

圣经里的“默想”，原文有低声念诵、反复思想的意思。默想要把注意力集中在神的话上，这和把头脑放空的冥想不同。约书亚记说：“这律法书不可离开你的口，总要昼夜思想，好使你谨守遵行”（书 1:8）。默想的目的是遵行。<br>
The Hebrew word for “meditate” carries the sense of murmuring and turning something over in the mind. It means fixing attention on God's word, which differs from meditation that empties the mind. Joshua says, “This book of the law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate on it day and night, that you may observe to do according to all that is written in it” (Joshua 1:8). The aim of meditation is obedience.

教会历史上有一种默想经文的方法，叫“圣言诵读”（lectio divina）。12 世纪的修士圭戈二世（Guigo II）把它分成四步：读、默想、祷告、安息在神的爱里。今天天主教、圣公会和不少新教徒都在用类似的方法。下面是一个简化的做法：<br>
In church history there is a method of meditating on Scripture called lectio divina (“divine reading”). In the twelfth century the monk Guigo II described it in four steps: reading, meditation, prayer, and resting in God's love. Catholics, Anglicans, and many Protestants use similar methods today. Here is a simplified version:

1. **选一小段**：两三节就够了，例如诗篇 23:1-3。<br>
   **Choose a short passage**: Two or three verses are enough, such as Psalm 23:1-3.
2. **慢慢读两三遍**：可以小声读出来。留意哪个词或哪句话让你停下来。<br>
   **Read it slowly two or three times**: You can read it aloud quietly. Notice which word or phrase makes you pause.
3. **想一想**：这句话说了神的什么？对我今天有什么意思？<br>
   **Think it over**: What does this say about God? What does it mean for me today?
4. **用这句话祷告**：把它变成你对神说的话，感谢、认罪或求告。<br>
   **Pray with it**: Turn it into your own words to God, in thanks, confession, or asking.

## 背诵经文 | Memorising Scripture

背经文让神的话在你没有圣经在手的时候也能帮助你，比如受试探、难过，或者别人问你信仰的时候。几个建议：<br>
Memorising Scripture lets God's word help you even when you don't have a Bible in hand, such as when you are tempted, when you are sad, or when someone asks about your faith. A few suggestions:

- **从短的开始**。例如约翰福音 3:16、诗篇 23:1、腓立比书 4:6-7。<br>
  **Start short.** For example, John 3:16, Psalm 23:1, or Philippians 4:6-7.
- **连出处一起背**。这样以后容易找到上下文。<br>
  **Learn the reference too.** That makes it easy to find the context later.
- **写在卡片上或手机里**，空档的时候拿出来复习。<br>
  **Write it on a card or in your phone**, and review it in spare moments.
- **每天复习**。新背的经文头几天每天复习，以后隔几天复习一次。<br>
  **Review every day.** Review a new verse daily for the first few days, then every few days after that.
- **和别人一起背**。家人或小组一起背，可以彼此提醒。保罗说，“当把基督的道理丰丰富富地存在心里”，并且“彼此教导，互相劝戒”（西 3:16）。<br>
  **Memorise with others.** Learning verses with family or a small group lets you remind one another. Paul says, “Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly,” and speaks of “teaching and admonishing one another” (Colossians 3:16).

背经文的时候，也要读它的上下文，明白它原来的意思，免得断章取义。<br>
When you memorise a verse, read its context too and understand its original meaning, so that you do not take it out of context.
