## 转折点：你们说我是谁？ | The turning point: who do you say that I am?

前八章里，人们一直在问耶稣是谁。到了凯撒利亚腓立比附近，耶稣自己问门徒：“你们说我是谁？”彼得回答说：“你是基督”（可 8:29）。“基督”的意思是“受膏者”，也就是旧约应许的那位王。<br>
Through the first eight chapters people keep asking who Jesus is. Near Caesarea Philippi Jesus himself asks the disciples, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answers, “You are the Christ” (Mark 8:29). “Christ” means “anointed one,” the king promised in the Old Testament.

这个回答是对的，但彼得对“基督”的理解还不完整。所以耶稣紧接着开始教导他们：“人子必须受许多的苦，被长老、祭司长，和文士弃绝，并且被杀，过三天复活”（可 8:31）。<br>
The answer is right, but Peter's idea of “the Christ” is still incomplete. So Jesus at once begins to teach them “that the Son of Man must suffer many things, and be rejected by the elders, the chief priests, and the scribes, and be killed, and after three days rise again” (Mark 8:31).

## 三次预告，三次不明白 | Three predictions, three misunderstandings

这一段的结构很清楚。耶稣三次预告自己的受难和复活，每一次门徒的反应都不对，耶稣就借机教导他们。<br>
The structure of this section is clear. Jesus foretells his suffering and resurrection three times. Each time the disciples respond wrongly, and Jesus uses the moment to teach them.

| 预告 Prediction | 门徒的反应 The disciples' response | 耶稣的教导 Jesus' teaching |
|---|---|---|
| 8:31 | 彼得劝耶稣不要这样说<br>Peter rebukes Jesus | 跟从我的人要舍己，背起十字架（8:34）<br>Whoever follows me must deny himself and take up his cross (8:34) |
| 9:31 | 门徒争论谁为大<br>The disciples argue about who is greatest | 愿意为首的，要作众人的用人（9:35）<br>Whoever wants to be first must be servant of all (9:35) |
| 10:33-34 | 雅各和约翰求坐在他左右<br>James and John ask to sit at his right and left | 人子来是要服事人，并且舍命作多人的赎价（10:45）<br>The Son of Man came to serve and to give his life as a ransom for many (10:45) |

## 跟从他意味着什么 | What following him means

耶稣说：“若有人要跟从我，就当舍己，背起他的十字架来跟从我”（可 8:34）。在当时，背十字架的人是走向刑场的人。耶稣的意思是，跟从他的人要把自己的主权交出来。<br>
Jesus says, “Whoever wants to come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me” (Mark 8:34). In that time a person carrying a cross was walking to execution. Jesus means that those who follow him hand over the right to rule their own lives.

他接着说：“凡要救自己生命的，必丧掉生命；凡为我和福音丧掉生命的，必救了生命”（可 8:35）。这句话听起来很重。它同时是一个应许：把生命交给他的人，会得着生命。<br>
He goes on, “whoever wants to save his life will lose it; and whoever will lose his life for my sake and the sake of the Good News will save it” (Mark 8:35). The saying is heavy. It is also a promise: those who give their lives to him will find life.

## 山上的声音和路边的瞎子 | The voice on the mountain and the blind man by the road

第一次预告之后六天，耶稣带三个门徒上了高山，在他们面前变了形像。有声音从云彩里出来说：“这是我的爱子，你们要听他”（可 9:7）。门徒刚听到受苦的预告，神就让他们看见耶稣的荣耀。<br>
Six days after the first prediction, Jesus takes three disciples up a high mountain and is transfigured before them. A voice comes out of the cloud: “This is my beloved Son. Listen to him” (Mark 9:7). The disciples have just heard that he must suffer, and God lets them see his glory.

这一段还有一位父亲的祷告，很多信徒都觉得亲切：“我信！但我信不足，求主帮助”（可 9:24）。<br>
This section also contains a father's prayer that many believers recognise as their own: “I believe. Help my unbelief!” (Mark 9:24).

这一段的开头和结尾各有一个瞎子得医治的故事（8:22-26；10:46-52）。中间的门徒一直“看不见”耶稣要走的路。最后那位瞎子巴底买得了医治，“就在路上跟随耶稣”（可 10:52）。马可用这样的安排告诉读者：人需要耶稣开眼睛，才能看清他是谁、跟他走。<br>
The section opens and closes with the healing of a blind man (8:22-26; 10:46-52). In between, the disciples keep failing to “see” the road Jesus must take. At the end the blind man Bartimaeus is healed and “followed Jesus on the way” (Mark 10:52). By this arrangement Mark tells the reader that people need Jesus to open their eyes before they can see who he is and follow him.
