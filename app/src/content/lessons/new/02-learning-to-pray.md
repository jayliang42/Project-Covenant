## 一个范本 | A pattern

门徒对耶稣说：“求主教导我们祷告。”（路 11:1）耶稣给他们的，就是后来所说的主祷文。它不只是要背的一段话，也是一个可以重复使用的框架。

The disciples asked, “Lord, teach us to pray” (Luke 11:1). Jesus gave them what we call the Lord's Prayer. It is words to say, and also a framework to use again and again.

## 六个部分 | Six movements

1. **称呼**：我们在天上的父。祷告是孩子对父亲说话。<br>**Address:** Our Father in heaven. Prayer is a child speaking to a father.
2. **愿人都尊你的名为圣**：先关心神被尊重。<br>**Hallowed be your name:** care first that God is honoured.
3. **愿你的国降临，愿你的旨意行在地上**：求神的心意在今天的世界成就。<br>**Your kingdom come, your will be done:** ask for God's purposes in today's world.
4. **日用的饮食**：把每天具体的需要告诉神。<br>**Daily bread:** bring today's concrete needs.
5. **饶恕**：求神饶恕我们，也饶恕别人。<br>**Forgiveness:** ask to be forgiven, and to forgive.
6. **保守**：求神不叫我们遇见试探，救我们脱离凶恶。<br>**Protection:** ask to be kept from testing and delivered from evil.

## 今天试一试 | Try it today

每个部分说一两句自己的话，三分钟就能做完。慢慢地，祷告就不再是“不知道说什么”了。

Say one or two sentences of your own for each part. It takes three minutes. Over time, prayer stops being “I don't know what to say”.
