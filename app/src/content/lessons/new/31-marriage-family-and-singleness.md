## 婚姻是盟约 | Marriage is a covenant

创世记说：“人要离开父母，与妻子连合，二人成为一体”（创 2:24）。先知玛拉基称妻子为“盟约的妻”，说耶和华在夫妻中间“作见证”（玛 2:14）。各大传统历来把婚姻理解为一男一女在神面前立的终身盟约。<br>
Genesis says, “a man will leave his father and his mother, and will join with his wife, and they will be one flesh” (Genesis 2:24). The prophet Malachi calls a wife “the wife of your covenant” and says Yahweh “has been witness” between husband and wife (Malachi 2:14). The major traditions have historically understood marriage as a lifelong covenant between a man and a woman before God.

“离开父母”在中国文化里需要特别留意：孝敬父母仍然重要，但夫妻的关系要放在首位，重要的决定由夫妻一同作。<br>
“Leaving father and mother” deserves special attention in Chinese culture: honouring parents remains important, but the marriage comes first, and important decisions are made by husband and wife together.

## 夫妻之间 | Between husband and wife

保罗讲夫妻关系，是从“存敬畏基督的心，彼此顺服”（弗 5:21）开始的。他要丈夫爱妻子，“正如基督爱教会，为教会舍己”（弗 5:25），“不可苦待她们”（西 3:19）。彼得说，丈夫要“按情理和妻子同住”，敬重她，因为她“与你一同承受生命之恩”（彼前 3:7）。<br>
Paul begins his teaching on marriage with “subjecting yourselves to one another in the fear of Christ” (Ephesians 5:21). He tells husbands to love their wives “even as Christ also loved the assembly, and gave himself up for it” (Ephesians 5:25), and “don't be bitter against them” (Colossians 3:19). Peter says husbands should live with their wives “according to knowledge,” honouring them as “joint heirs of the grace of life” (1 Peter 3:7).

妻子要顺服丈夫的经文（弗 5:22-24），各传统的理解不同，“扎根成长”路径里“女性在教会中的服事”和“以弗所书”导读介绍了这些看法。各方都同意：圣经说的顺服和爱，都不包括忍受暴力或虐待。如果家里有暴力或虐待，先顾安全，联系当地的紧急服务和可以帮助的人。<br>
The traditions understand the passages on wives submitting to husbands (Ephesians 5:22-24) differently; the lessons on women in ministry and on Ephesians in the Going Deeper path introduce the views. All agree that the Bible's call to submission and love never includes enduring violence or abuse. If there is violence or abuse at home, put safety first and contact local emergency services and people who can help.

几个实际的做法：<br>
Some practical steps:

- **一起祷告**，哪怕只是几句话。<br>
  **Pray together**, even if only a few sentences.
- **不要带着怒气过夜**：“生气却不要犯罪；不可含怒到日落”（弗 4:26）。<br>
  **Don't go to bed angry**: “Be angry, and don't sin. Don't let the sun go down on your wrath” (Ephesians 4:26).
- **用哥林多前书 13 章检查自己**：“爱是恒久忍耐，又有恩慈……不计算人的恶。”<br>
  **Check yourself against 1 Corinthians 13**: “Love is patient and is kind... takes no account of evil.”
- **需要时寻求帮助**，例如请教会的牧者或专业的婚姻辅导。<br>
  **Seek help when needed**, for example from a pastor or a professional marriage counsellor.

## 配偶还没有信主 | When your spouse does not yet believe

保罗说：“倘若某弟兄有不信的妻子，妻子也情愿和他同住，他就不要离弃妻子”，反过来也是一样（林前 7:12-13）。彼得说，不信的配偶“虽然不听道，也可以因妻子的品行被感化过来”（彼前 3:1）。<br>
Paul says, “if any brother has an unbelieving wife, and she is content to live with him, let him not leave her,” and the same for a wife (1 Corinthians 7:12-13). Peter says an unbelieving spouse may be won “by the behavior” of the believer “without a word” (1 Peter 3:1).

在这样的家庭里，可以尊重配偶的想法，不勉强对方去教会；用爱和忍耐生活；为对方祷告。信仰上的不同，不是不爱对方的理由。<br>
In such a home you can respect your spouse's views and not pressure them to come to church; live with love and patience; and pray for them. A difference in faith is no reason to love them less.

## 父母和儿女 | Parents and children

“要孝敬父母”是“第一条带应许的诫命”（弗 6:2）。保罗也对父母说：“你们作父亲的，不要惹儿女的气，恐怕他们失了志气”（西 3:21）。家里的信仰，更多是通过日常生活传给孩子的：“无论你坐在家里，行在路上，躺下，起来，都要谈论”（申 6:7）。<br>
“Honor your father and mother” is the first commandment with a promise (Ephesians 6:2). Paul also tells parents, “Fathers, don't provoke your children, so that they won't be discouraged” (Colossians 3:21). Faith is passed on in a home mostly through daily life: “talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up” (Deuteronomy 6:7).

## 单身 | Singleness

耶稣和保罗都没有结婚。保罗说：“我愿意众人像我一样；只是各人领受神的恩赐，一个是这样，一个是那样”（林前 7:7）。单身不是“少了什么”，也可以专心服事神（林前 7:32-35）。教会是神的家，单身的弟兄姊妹在其中同样是家人。<br>
Neither Jesus nor Paul married. Paul says, “I wish that all men were like me. However each man has his own gift from God, one of this kind, and another of that kind” (1 Corinthians 7:7). Singleness is not a lack, and it can free a person to serve God with undivided attention (1 Corinthians 7:32-35). The church is God's household, and single brothers and sisters are family there too.

关于离婚、再婚和性伦理，各传统的看法，“扎根成长”路径里有专门的两课介绍。<br>
On divorce, remarriage, and sexual ethics, the Going Deeper path has two lessons introducing the traditions' views.
