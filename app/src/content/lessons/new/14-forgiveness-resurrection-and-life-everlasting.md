## 我信罪得赦免 | The forgiveness of sins

信经说：“我信罪得赦免。”这句话先承认人有罪，然后说罪可以被赦免。圣经的应许很清楚：“我们若认自己的罪，神是信实的，是公义的，必要赦免我们的罪，洗净我们一切的不义”（约壹 1:9）。<br>
The creed says, “I believe in the forgiveness of sins.” The line first admits that people sin, and then says that sin can be forgiven. The promise of Scripture is plain: “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us the sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” (1 John 1:9).

赦免的根据是基督在十字架上所做的事，所以它不取决于我们当天的感觉。《诗篇》这样形容神的赦免：“东离西有多远，他叫我们的过犯离我们也有多远”（诗 103:12）。<br>
Forgiveness rests on what Christ did on the cross, so it does not depend on how we feel that day. The Psalms describe God's forgiveness like this: “As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us” (Psalm 103:12).

## 怎样认罪：各传统的做法 | How to confess: what the traditions do

各传统都相信赦免来自神，也都相信人需要认罪。具体怎样认罪，做法不同。<br>
Every tradition believes that forgiveness comes from God and that people need to confess their sins. They differ in how confession is practised.

- **天主教**：有“和好圣事”，也叫告解。《天主教教理》说，信徒在认真省察以后，要把所意识到的大罪向神父告明。经文根据之一是耶稣对门徒说的话：“你们赦免谁的罪，谁的罪就赦免了”（约 20:23）。<br>
  **Catholic**: There is a sacrament of reconciliation, also called confession. The Catechism of the Catholic Church says that after careful self-examination the faithful must confess to a priest the mortal sins they are aware of. One text behind this is Jesus' word to the disciples: “If you forgive anyone’s sins, they have been forgiven them” (John 20:23).
- **东正教**：同样有向神父认罪的圣事，神父为认罪的人祷告，宣告神的赦免。<br>
  **Orthodox**: The Orthodox also have a sacrament of confession in the presence of a priest, who prays for the penitent and declares God's forgiveness.
- **信义宗**：《奥斯堡信条》第 11 条说，教会应当保留个人的赦罪宣告，但认罪的时候不必把每一样罪都列出来。<br>
  **Lutheran**: Article XI of the Augsburg Confession says that private absolution ought to be retained in the churches, although listing every sin in confession is not necessary.
- **其他新教教会**：大多教导信徒直接向神认罪。很多教会在主日崇拜里有一段全会众一起认罪的祷告。得罪了人，要向那个人认错。圣经也说：“你们要彼此认罪，互相代求”（雅 5:16）。<br>
  **Other Protestant churches**: Most teach believers to confess directly to God. Many churches include a prayer of confession said by the whole congregation in Sunday worship. When you have wronged someone, you go to that person. Scripture also says, “Confess your offenses to one another, and pray for one another” (James 5:16).

## 我信身体复活 | The resurrection of the body

很多人以为基督徒的盼望是“灵魂离开身体，去天堂”。信经说的比这更具体：“我信身体复活。”神造了身体，也要救赎身体。<br>
Many people assume the Christian hope is that the soul leaves the body and goes to heaven. The creed says something more concrete: “I believe in the resurrection of the body.” God made the body, and he will redeem the body.

保罗说，基督已经从死里复活，“成为睡了之人初熟的果子”（林前 15:20）。初熟的果子表明后面还有收成。他又用种子作比方：“所种的是必朽坏的，复活的是不朽坏的”（林前 15:42）。复活的身体和现在的身体有连续，也有改变，就像麦粒和麦穗。<br>
Paul says that Christ has been raised from the dead and “became the first fruits of those who are asleep” (1 Corinthians 15:20). First fruits mean that a harvest follows. He also uses the picture of a seed: “The body is sown perishable; it is raised imperishable” (1 Corinthians 15:42). The resurrection body is connected with the present body and also changed, like a grain of wheat and the ear that grows from it.

## 我信永生 | The life everlasting

耶稣在祷告中说：“认识你独一的真神，并且认识你所差来的耶稣基督，这就是永生”（约 17:3）。按这句话，永生首先讲的是和神的关系，这关系从信的时候就开始了。<br>
Jesus says in prayer, “This is eternal life, that they should know you, the only true God, and him whom you sent, Jesus Christ” (John 17:3). By this definition eternal life is first of all a relationship with God, and it begins when a person believes.

它也指向将来。圣经最后描写新天新地：“神要擦去他们一切的眼泪；不再有死亡，也不再有悲哀、哭号、疼痛”（启 21:4）。信经用“阿们”结束，意思是“确实如此”。<br>
It also points to the future. The Bible ends with a picture of the new heaven and new earth: “He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more; neither will there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain, any more” (Revelation 21:4). The creed ends with “Amen,” which means “so it is.”
