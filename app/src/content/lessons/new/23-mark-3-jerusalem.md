## 王来了 | The king arrives

《马可福音》16 章里，有 6 章讲耶稣在耶路撒冷的最后一周。马可把叙述的速度放慢了，因为这是全书的重点。<br>
Six of Mark's 16 chapters cover Jesus' last week in Jerusalem. Mark slows the story down here, because this is where the weight of the book lies.

耶稣骑着一匹驴驹进城，众人把衣服和树枝铺在路上，喊着说：“奉主名来的是应当称颂的”（可 11:9）。先知撒迦利亚说过，锡安的王要谦谦和和地骑着驴驹来（亚 9:9）。耶稣用这个举动公开表明自己是王，也表明自己是怎样的王。<br>
Jesus rides into the city on a colt. The people spread cloaks and branches on the road and cry out, “Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!” (Mark 11:9). The prophet Zechariah had said that Zion's king would come humble and riding on a colt (Zechariah 9:9). By this act Jesus openly presents himself as king and shows what kind of king he is.

## 洁净圣殿 | Clearing the temple

第二天，耶稣进了圣殿，赶出做买卖的人，推倒兑换银钱之人的桌子。他说：“经上不是记着说：我的殿必称为万国祷告的殿吗？你们倒使它成为贼窝了”（可 11:17）。<br>
The next day Jesus enters the temple, drives out those who are buying and selling, and overturns the tables of the money-changers. He says, “Isn’t it written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations?’ But you have made it a den of robbers!” (Mark 11:17).

马可把这件事夹在咒诅无花果树的故事中间（11:12-14，20-21）。那棵树有叶子，却没有果子。圣殿里有很多活动，却没有神要的果子。这是马可常用的写法：把两件事夹在一起，让它们互相说明。<br>
Mark places this event inside the story of the cursed fig tree (11:12-14, 20-21). The tree has leaves and no fruit. The temple is full of activity and lacks the fruit God looks for. Mark often writes this way, setting one event inside another so that each explains the other.

## 殿里的问答 | Questions in the temple courts

接下来，各方的人轮流来问耶稣难题：他凭什么权柄做这些事，该不该纳税给凯撒，死人复活以后的婚姻怎样算。耶稣一一回答。<br>
Next, one group after another brings Jesus hard questions: by what authority he does these things, whether to pay taxes to Caesar, and how marriage works at the resurrection. Jesus answers each one.

有一个文士问他，诫命中哪一条是第一要紧的。耶稣回答说：“你要尽心、尽性、尽意、尽力爱主你的神。”其次是“要爱人如己”（可 12:30-31）。他把全部律法归结为这两条。<br>
A scribe asks which commandment is first of all. Jesus answers, “you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind, and with all your strength.” The second is, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself” (Mark 12:30-31). He sums up the whole law in these two.

耶稣还讲了一个凶恶园户的比喻（12:1-12）：园主一再打发仆人去收果子，园户把仆人打的打、杀的杀。最后园主打发自己的爱子去，园户把他也杀了。宗教领袖听出这比喻是指着他们说的。<br>
Jesus also tells the parable of the wicked tenants (12:1-12). The owner of a vineyard sends servant after servant to collect its fruit, and the tenants beat some and kill others. At last he sends his beloved son, and they kill him too. The religious leaders realise the parable is aimed at them.

这一段的最后，耶稣坐在银库对面，看见一个穷寡妇投了两个小钱。他对门徒说，她投的比众人都多（12:41-44）。<br>
At the end of this section Jesus sits opposite the treasury and sees a poor widow put in two small coins. He tells the disciples that she has put in more than all the others (12:41-44).

## 橄榄山上的讲论 | The discourse on the Mount of Olives

门徒称赞圣殿的石头，耶稣说，将来没有一块石头留在石头上（13:2）。主后 70 年，罗马军队攻破耶路撒冷，烧毁了圣殿。<br>
The disciples admire the stones of the temple, and Jesus says that not one stone will be left on another (13:2). In AD 70 the Roman army took Jerusalem and burned the temple.

第 13 章是这卷书里最难读的一章。信徒对它有几种读法：有人认为它主要讲主后 70 年的事，有人认为它主要讲世界的末了，也有很多人认为两者都讲到了，近处的事是远处的事的预表。<br>
Chapter 13 is the hardest chapter in the book. Believers read it in several ways. Some think it mainly concerns the events of AD 70, some think it mainly concerns the end of the age, and many hold that it speaks of both, with the nearer event foreshadowing the later one.

无论采用哪种读法，耶稣的结论是清楚的：“天地要废去，我的话却不能废去。”“那日子，那时辰，没有人知道……惟有父知道。你们要谨慎，警醒祈祷”（可 13:31-33）。他要门徒做的是警醒和忠心，没有叫他们推算日期。<br>
Whichever reading is taken, Jesus' conclusion is clear: “Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.” “of that day or that hour no one knows... but only the Father. Watch, keep alert, and pray” (Mark 13:31-33). What he asks of the disciples is watchfulness and faithfulness, and he gives them no dates to calculate.

这一周请读马可福音 11–13 章，大约需要 20 分钟。<br>
This week, read Mark 11–13. It takes about 20 minutes.
