## 耶稣事先说过 | Jesus said it beforehand

耶稣没有应许门徒一路平顺。他说：“在世上，你们有苦难；但你们可以放心，我已经胜了世界”（约 16:33）。所以信徒遇到苦难，并不说明信仰出了问题，也不一定说明自己做错了什么。<br>
Jesus did not promise his disciples a smooth road. He said, “In the world you have trouble; but cheer up! I have overcome the world” (John 16:33). So when believers suffer, it does not show that the faith has failed, and it does not necessarily mean they have done something wrong.

圣经讲到几种不同来源的苦：有的来自这个已经破损的世界，例如疾病和灾害。有的来自别人的罪。有的来自自己的错误。有的是因为跟随基督而受的逼迫。大多数时候，人没法确定某一件事“为什么”发生。约伯的朋友坚持说约伯受苦一定是因为犯了罪，神最后说他们讲得不对（伯 42:7）。<br>
The Bible speaks of suffering from several sources. Some comes from a damaged world, such as illness and disaster. Some comes from other people's sin. Some comes from our own mistakes. Some is persecution for following Christ. Most of the time a person cannot be sure why a particular thing happened. Job's friends insisted that Job must have sinned, and in the end God said they had not spoken rightly (Job 42:7).

## 可以向神说实话 | You can tell God the truth

《诗篇》里约有三分之一是哀歌。作者直接问神：“耶和华啊，你忘记我要到几时呢？要到永远吗？”（诗 13:1）耶稣在十字架上也用《诗篇》22 篇的话祷告。神把这些祷告放在圣经里，说明他允许人这样对他说话。<br>
About a third of the Psalms are laments. The writer asks God directly, “How long, Yahweh? Will you forget me forever?” (Psalm 13:1). Jesus on the cross also prayed in the words of Psalm 22. God put these prayers in the Bible, which shows that he allows people to speak to him this way.

圣经也说：“耶和华靠近伤心的人”（诗 34:18）。耶稣在朋友拉撒路的坟墓前哭了（约 11:35）。神没有站在远处看人受苦。<br>
Scripture also says, “Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart” (Psalm 34:18). At the tomb of his friend Lazarus, Jesus wept (John 11:35). God does not stand at a distance and watch people suffer.

## 神在苦难中做什么 | What God does in suffering

保罗说：“我们晓得万事都互相效力，叫爱神的人得益处”（罗 8:28）。这句话没有说每件事本身是好的。它说的是神能在各样的事里做工，下一节说明目的是让信徒越来越像基督。<br>
Paul says, “We know that all things work together for good for those who love God” (Romans 8:28). The sentence does not say each event is good in itself. It says God is able to work in all kinds of events, and the next verse gives the aim: that believers become more and more like Christ.

保罗又说，患难生忍耐，忍耐生老练，老练生盼望（罗 5:3-4）。很多信徒回头看，会发现自己在最难的日子里学会了倚靠神。但这通常是后来才看到的，受苦的当时未必感觉得到。<br>
Paul also says that suffering produces perseverance, perseverance produces proven character, and proven character produces hope (Romans 5:3-4). Many believers, looking back, find that they learned to rely on God in their hardest days. This is usually seen later and may not be felt at the time.

关于疾病，教会里有不同的教导。各传统都为病人祷告，也都相信神能医治。有些教会教导说，只要信心足够，神一定会医治。大多数教会不这样教导，他们指出保罗求主挪去他身上的“刺”，主没有挪去，只说他的恩典够用（林后 12:7-9）。祷告和看医生并不冲突。<br>
On illness, churches teach differently. Every tradition prays for the sick and believes that God can heal. Some churches teach that God will always heal if there is enough faith. Most churches do not teach this. They point to Paul, who asked the Lord to remove the “thorn” in his flesh and was told that grace was sufficient for him, while the thorn remained (2 Corinthians 12:7-9). Praying and seeing a doctor do not conflict.

## 怎样陪伴受苦的人 | How to stay beside someone who suffers

保罗称神为“赐各样安慰的神”，又说神安慰我们，“叫我们能用神所赐的安慰去安慰那遭各样患难的人”（林后 1:3-4）。<br>
Paul calls God the “God of all comfort” and says God comforts us “that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction” (2 Corinthians 1:3-4).

陪伴的时候，先听，少解释。约伯的朋友起初陪他坐了七天，一句话也不说，那是他们做得最好的时候（伯 2:13）。可以做一些具体的事：送一顿饭，陪着去医院，定期问候。不要急着引用罗马书 8:28，也不要猜测对方受苦的原因。<br>
When you stay with someone, listen first and explain little. Job's friends at first sat with him for seven days without a word, and that was the best thing they did (Job 2:13). Do concrete things: bring a meal, go along to the hospital, check in regularly. Do not hurry to quote Romans 8:28, and do not guess at the reason for the person's suffering.

如果你自己长时间情绪低落，或者有伤害自己的念头，请尽快告诉你信任的人，并寻求医生或心理专业人员的帮助。寻求专业帮助和信靠神并不冲突。<br>
If you yourself have felt low for a long time, or have thoughts of harming yourself, please tell someone you trust soon and seek help from a doctor or a mental health professional. Seeking professional help does not conflict with trusting God.
