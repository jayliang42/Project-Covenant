## 找教会时看什么 | What to look for

每间教会都不完美。找教会的时候，可以先看下面几件事：<br>
No church is perfect. When you look for one, start with these questions:

- **讲不讲圣经**：讲道是不是从圣经出发，讲解经文的意思？耶稣基督的死和复活是不是讲道的中心？<br>
  **Is the Bible taught?** Does the preaching start from Scripture and explain what the passage means? Are Jesus Christ's death and resurrection at the centre?
- **承不承认基本信仰**：教会是否承认《使徒信经》所说的基本内容，例如三位一体、耶稣是神也是人、他的死和复活？<br>
  **Does it hold the basic faith?** Does the church affirm what the Apostles' Creed confesses, such as the Trinity, Jesus being both God and man, and his death and resurrection?
- **有没有彼此关心**：除了主日聚会，有没有小组、查经或其他让人互相认识、互相帮助的机会？<br>
  **Do people care for one another?** Besides Sunday worship, are there small groups, Bible studies, or other ways for people to know and help one another?
- **领袖是否可以问责**：领袖有没有别人监督？财务是否透明？会不会要求你完全顺从某一个人？<br>
  **Are leaders accountable?** Is anyone overseeing the leaders? Are finances open? Is anyone asking you to obey one person without question?

建筑、人数、音乐风格、聚会语言都可以不同，这些可以按你的情况来选。<br>
Buildings, size, music style, and language can all differ. You can choose these according to your situation.

## 第一次去 | Your first visit

很多教会的主日聚会大约一到两个小时，一般有唱诗、祷告、读经、讲道，有时有圣餐和奉献。第一次去，可以早一点到，坐下来观察。奉献是自愿的，不认识的流程也不用担心，可以跟着别人做，或者问身边的人。<br>
Many churches hold a Sunday service of about one to two hours, usually with singing, prayer, Bible reading, and a sermon, and sometimes communion and an offering. On your first visit, arrive a little early and sit and watch. Giving is voluntary. If you do not know what to do, follow others or ask the person next to you.

去过几次以后，可以试着参加一个小组或查经班。在大聚会里不容易认识人，小组里比较容易建立关系。<br>
After a few visits, try joining a small group or Bible study. It is hard to get to know people in a large service, and easier in a small group.

## 教会让你失望的时候 | When church disappoints you

教会是由蒙恩的罪人组成的。时间长了，你可能会被人误解、伤害，或者对一些做法有意见。保罗写给教会的信里，常常处理的就是这些问题。他劝信徒“用爱心互相宽容，用和平彼此联络，竭力保守圣灵所赐合而为一的心”（弗 4:2-3）。<br>
The church is made up of forgiven sinners. Over time you may be misunderstood or hurt, or disagree with how things are done. Many of Paul's letters to churches deal with exactly these problems. He urges believers to bear “with one another in love, being eager to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” (Ephesians 4:2–3).

遇到问题时，可以先直接和对方谈（太 18:15），也可以找你信任的领袖聊。不要只在背后议论。<br>
When problems come, first talk directly with the person involved (Matthew 18:15), or speak with a leader you trust. Avoid talking only behind people's backs.

有些情况确实需要离开，例如教会不再讲纯正的福音，或者有人滥用权力、隐瞒伤害。这时可以请成熟的基督徒朋友帮你判断，再找一间新的教会。<br>
Some situations do call for leaving, for example when a church no longer preaches the true gospel, or when leaders abuse their power or cover up harm. In that case, ask mature Christian friends to help you think it through, and then look for a new church.
