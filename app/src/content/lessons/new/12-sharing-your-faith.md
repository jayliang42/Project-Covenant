## 从自己的经历讲起 | Start with your own story

新约里，很多人谈信仰是从讲自己的经历开始的。安得烈遇见耶稣以后，先去找哥哥西门，说：“我们遇见弥赛亚了”（约 1:41）。撒马利亚的妇人回到城里说：“你们来看！有一个人将我素来所行的一切事都给我说出来了”（约 4:29）。一个生来瞎眼、被耶稣医好的人说：“有一件事我知道，从前我是眼瞎的，如今能看见了”（约 9:25）。<br>
In the New Testament, many people began talking about their faith by telling what had happened to them. After meeting Jesus, Andrew first found his brother Simon and told him, “We have found the Messiah!” (John 1:41). The Samaritan woman went back to her town and said, “Come, see a man who told me everything that I did” (John 4:29). A man born blind, whom Jesus had healed, said, “One thing I do know: that though I was blind, now I see” (John 9:25).

你不需要先成为神学专家。可以想一想这三个问题，准备一个简短的回答：<br>
You do not need to be a theologian first. Think about these three questions and prepare a short answer:

1. 认识耶稣以前，我的生活是什么样子？<br>
   What was my life like before I knew Jesus?
2. 我是怎样认识他的？<br>
   How did I come to know him?
3. 这以后，我的生活有什么改变？<br>
   What has changed since then?

## 几个尊重人的做法 | Some respectful ways to go about it

- **先听**：了解对方关心什么、经历过什么、对信仰有什么看法，再说话。<br>
  **Listen first**: Find out what the other person cares about, what they have been through, and what they think about faith, before you speak.
- **说话带着和气**：保罗说：“你们的言语要常常带着和气，好像用盐调和，就可知道该怎样回答各人”（西 4:6）。<br>
  **Speak graciously**: Paul writes, “Let your speech always be with grace, seasoned with salt, that you may know how you ought to answer each one” (Colossians 4:6).
- **诚实回答问题**：不知道的就说不知道，可以回去查了再说。<br>
  **Answer questions truthfully**: If you do not know, say so, and offer to look it up.
- **尊重对方的选择**：不要逼人表态，也不要因为对方不信就疏远他。<br>
  **Respect their choice**: Do not pressure anyone to decide, and do not pull away from someone because they do not believe.
- **注意场合**：在工作的地方或不合适的场合，要守规矩，也要顾及别人的感受。<br>
  **Mind the setting**: At work or in other settings where it is not appropriate, follow the rules and be considerate of others.
- **为人祷告**：保罗说：“我栽种了，亚波罗浇灌了，惟有神叫他生长”（林前 3:6）。人能不能信，不是靠我们说得多好。<br>
  **Pray for people**: Paul says, “I planted. Apollos watered. But God gave the increase” (1 Corinthians 3:6). Whether someone comes to faith does not depend on how well we speak.

## 怎样生活 | How you live

耶稣说：“你们的光也当这样照在人前，叫他们看见你们的好行为，便将荣耀归给你们在天上的父”（太 5:16）。诚实地工作，善待家人，帮助有需要的人，在困难中仍有盼望，这些都会让别人看见信仰在一个人身上是什么样子。<br>
Jesus said, “Let your light shine before men; that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in heaven” (Matthew 5:16). Working with integrity, treating your family well, helping people in need, and keeping hope in hard times all let others see what faith looks like in a person's life.

彼得也说，有人问你们心中盼望的缘由，“就要常作准备，以温柔、敬畏的心回答各人”（彼前 3:15）。生活和话语可以一起说明你为什么相信。<br>
Peter also says that when anyone asks about the hope in you, you should “always be ready to give an answer ... with humility and fear” (1 Peter 3:15). Your life and your words can work together to show why you believe.
