## 耶稣怎样讲饶恕 | How Jesus spoke about forgiveness

彼得问耶稣：“主啊，我弟兄得罪我，我当饶恕他几次呢？到七次可以吗？”耶稣回答说，要到“七十个七次”（太 18:21-22）。他的意思是不要再数了。<br>
Peter asks Jesus, “Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Until seven times?” Jesus answers, “until seventy times seven” (Matthew 18:21-22). He means: stop counting.

接着耶稣讲了一个比喻。一个仆人欠主人一万他连得银子（和合本译作“一千万银子”），这是一辈子也还不清的数目，主人免了他的债。他出来遇见一个同伴，同伴只欠他一百个银币（和合本译作“十两银子”），他却把同伴下在监里。主人知道后对他说：“你不应当怜恤你的同伴，像我怜恤你吗？”（太 18:33）<br>
Jesus then tells a parable. A servant owes his master ten thousand talents, a sum no one could repay in a lifetime, and the master cancels the debt. The servant goes out and meets a fellow servant who owes him only a hundred silver coins, and has him thrown into prison. When the master hears of it he says, “Shouldn’t you also have had mercy on your fellow servant, even as I had mercy on you?” (Matthew 18:33).

保罗把同样的道理写成一句话：“彼此饶恕，正如神在基督里饶恕了你们一样”（弗 4:32）。基督徒饶恕人，是因为自己先被饶恕了。<br>
Paul puts the same point in one sentence: “forgiving each other, just as God also in Christ forgave you” (Ephesians 4:32). Christians forgive because they were forgiven first.

## 饶恕是什么 | What forgiveness is

饶恕是一个决定：我不再向这个人讨债，也不再自己报复。圣经说：“不要自己伸冤……因为经上记着：主说：伸冤在我，我必报应”（罗 12:19）。饶恕的人把审判交给神，神是公义的。<br>
Forgiveness is a decision: I will no longer collect this debt from the person, and I will not take revenge myself. Scripture says, “Don’t seek revenge yourselves... For it is written, ‘Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay, says the Lord’” (Romans 12:19). The one who forgives hands judgment over to God, who is just.

饶恕也包括为对方祷告，愿意对方得益处。这常常需要时间，也常常需要一再重新做这个决定。<br>
Forgiveness also includes praying for the other person and wishing them good. This often takes time, and the decision often has to be made again and again.

## 饶恕不等于什么 | What forgiveness does not mean

- **不等于说“没关系”。**饶恕的前提是确实有人做错了事。<br>
  **It does not mean saying “it was nothing.”** Forgiveness assumes that a real wrong was done.
- **不等于马上恢复信任。**饶恕可以由一个人决定，和好需要两个人，信任要靠对方的悔改和时间来重建。<br>
  **It does not mean trusting again at once.** One person can decide to forgive. Reconciliation takes two, and trust is rebuilt through the other person's repentance and through time.
- **不等于留在危险里。**如果有人正在伤害你，你可以离开，可以寻求帮助，也可以报警。这样做和饶恕并不冲突。<br>
  **It does not mean staying in danger.** If someone is harming you, you may leave, seek help, and call the police. Doing so does not conflict with forgiveness.
- **不等于不再难过。**伤口愈合需要时间。你可以一边难过，一边选择不报复。<br>
  **It does not mean the pain is gone.** Wounds take time to heal. You can still hurt and still choose to give up revenge.

## 很难饶恕的时候 | When forgiving is hard

先把事情原原本本地告诉神。《诗篇》里有很多这样的祷告，作者把愤怒和委屈都说了出来。<br>
Begin by telling God exactly what happened. The Psalms contain many prayers like this, in which the writer pours out anger and grief.

然后回想神怎样饶恕了你。主祷文里有一句：“免我们的债，如同我们免了人的债。”每次这样祷告，就是在提醒自己这两件事是连在一起的。<br>
Then remember how God has forgiven you. The Lord's Prayer has the line, “Forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.” Each time you pray it, you remind yourself that the two belong together.

如果伤害很深，不要一个人扛。去找牧者或你信任的成熟信徒，请他们陪你走这段路。<br>
If the hurt is serious, do not carry it alone. Go to a pastor or a mature believer you trust and ask them to walk with you through it.
