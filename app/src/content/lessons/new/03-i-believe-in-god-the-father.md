## 初信者需要一份信仰纲要 | New believers need a summary of the faith

刚信主的时候，读经、听道会遇到很多新词：创造、救赎、称义、成圣、教会、复活。怎样把它们连成一个清楚的整体？从早期教会开始，基督徒受洗前都要学习一份简明的信仰告白。其中最著名、各宗派通用的是**《使徒信经》（Apostles' Creed）**。<br>
When you first believe, reading the Bible and hearing sermons bring many new words: creation, redemption, justification, sanctification, the church, resurrection. How do they fit into one clear whole? From the early church on, Christians preparing for baptism learned a short confession of faith. The best known, used across denominations, is the **Apostles' Creed**.

《使徒信经》分三段：**一、我信上帝，全能的父；二、我信我主耶稣基督；三、我信圣灵。**<br>
The Apostles' Creed has three parts: **1. I believe in God, the Father Almighty; 2. I believe in Jesus Christ our Lord; 3. I believe in the Holy Spirit.**

## “我信上帝，全能的父，创造天地的主” | "I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth"

这一句包含圣经关于神的三点教导：<br>
This line holds three things Scripture teaches about God:

1. **他是创造万有的主**。日月星辰、山川草木都是他造的，他自己不属于受造之物。所以基督徒不拜任何受造之物，也不必害怕命运、风水或星象。<br>
   **He is the Creator of all things.** Sun, moon, and stars, mountains, rivers, and plants are his work, and he himself is not part of creation. So Christians do not worship any created thing and need not fear fate, feng shui, or astrology.
2. **他的全能是信实天父的大能**。在圣经里，神的能力总是和他的圣洁、信实、慈爱连在一起。他决不背乎自己，也完全有能力成就他的应许。他不像任性的暴君。<br>
   **His almighty power is the power of a faithful Father.** In Scripture, God's power always goes together with his holiness, faithfulness, and love. He never denies himself, and he is fully able to keep his promises. He is not like a tyrant who acts on whim.
3. **因为耶稣，这位创造主成了我们的天父**。在基督里，我们从孤儿和外人变成蒙收养的儿女（罗 8:15；约壹 3:1）。<br>
   **Because of Jesus, this Creator has become our Father.** In Christ we are changed from orphans and strangers into adopted sons and daughters (Romans 8:15; 1 John 3:1).

## 独一真神：父、子、圣灵（三位一体） | One God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (the Trinity)

基督徒坚信**只有一位真神**（申 6:4），又奉**“父、子、圣灵的名”**施洗（太 28:19，原文的“名”是单数）。这两件事怎样放在一起？圣经启示，这位独一的真神从永远到永远以**父、子、圣灵三个位格**存在，三位彼此相爱。<br>
Christians firmly believe there is **only one true God** (Deuteronomy 6:4), and they baptize in **"the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"** (Matthew 28:19, where "name" is singular in the original). How do these fit together? Scripture reveals that the one true God exists from eternity to eternity as **three persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit**, who love one another.

三位一体的教义是福音的根基：**创造世界以前，父已经爱子（约 17:24）**。所以“神就是爱”（约壹 4:8）说的是神从永远就有的实情。救恩就是天父差遣圣子，又赐下圣灵，把我们带进三一真神里面本来就有的爱和喜乐。<br>
The doctrine of the Trinity is the foundation of the gospel: **before the world was made, the Father loved the Son (John 17:24)**. So "God is love" (1 John 4:8) describes what God has always been. Salvation is the Father sending the Son and giving the Spirit to bring us into the love and joy that have always been in the triune God.
