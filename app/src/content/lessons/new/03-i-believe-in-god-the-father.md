## 为什么初信者需要一张“信仰地图”？ | Why new believers need a clear map

初信主时，读经、听道会接触到大量新词：创造、救赎、称义、成圣、教会、复活……怎样把它们串成一个清楚的整体？从早期教会开始，基督徒在受洗前都会一起学习简明的信仰告白——其中最著名、跨宗派通用的就是**《使徒信经》（Apostles' Creed）**。<br>
When you first come to faith, you encounter a flood of biblical themes — creation, redemption, justification, the church, resurrection. How do they fit together? From the early centuries, Christians preparing for baptism learned a short summary of the faith, most famously the **Apostles' Creed**.

整篇《使徒信经》按三个段落展开：**一、我信上帝，全能的父；二、我信我主耶稣基督；三、我信圣灵。**<br>
The Apostles' Creed unfolds in three movements: **1. God the Father; 2. Jesus Christ his Son; 3. The Holy Spirit.**

## “我信上帝，全能的父，创造天地的主” | "I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth"

这一句浓缩了圣经关于神的三个核心真理：<br>
This opening line distills three foundational truths about who God is:

1. **他是创造万有的主，不是受造物的一部分**：日月星辰、山川草木都是他造的，因此基督徒不拜任何受造之物，也不必害怕命运、风水或星象。<br>
   **He is the Creator, not part of creation**: Sun, moon, stars, and forces of nature are his handiwork; believers do not worship created things or live in fear of fate or superstition.
2. **他的“全能”不是任性的暴君权力，而是信实天父的大能**：在圣经里，神的能力总是与他的圣洁、信实和慈爱连在一起——他决不背乎自己，也完全有能力成就他的应许。<br>
   **His "almighty" power is the power of a faithful Father**: In Scripture, God's power is never arbitrary tyranny; it is always bound to his holiness, faithfulness, and love.
3. **因为耶稣，这位创造主成了我们的“天父”**：在基督里，我们不再是孤儿或外人，而是蒙收养的儿女（罗 8:15；约壹 3:1）。<br>
   **Through Jesus, the Creator welcomes us as his children**: In Christ we are no longer orphans or strangers, but adopted sons and daughters who can cry "Abba! Father!" (Romans 8:15).

## 独一真神：父、子、圣灵（三位一体） | One God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (The Trinity)

为什么基督徒既坚信**只有一位真神**（申 6:4），又奉**“父、子、圣灵的名”**施洗（太 28:19，注意原文“名”是单数）？因为圣经向我们启示：这位独一真神不是孤零零的单子，而是从永远到永远存在于**父、子、圣灵三个位格的相爱之中**。<br>
Why do Christians confess **one God** (Deuteronomy 6:4) and yet baptize in **"the name [singular] of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"** (Matthew 28:19)? Because Scripture reveals that the one true God is not a lonely monad; from eternity God lives in the loving fellowship of **Father, Son, and Spirit**.

这绝对不是数学算术题，而是整个福音最温暖的根基：**在创造世界之前，父已经爱子（约 17:24）**。因此，“神就是爱”（约壹 4:8）不是一句口号——救恩就是天父差遣圣子、又赐下圣灵，把我们带进那份原本就充满在三一真神里面的爱与喜乐中。<br>
Far from being an abstract riddle, this is the warmest truth of the Gospel: **before the world began, the Father loved the Son in the fellowship of the Spirit (John 17:24)**. "God is love" (1 John 4:8) at his very core — and salvation is God drawing us into that eternal life and love through Christ and by the Spirit.
