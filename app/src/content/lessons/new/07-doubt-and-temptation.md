## 有疑惑的时候 | When doubts come

很多基督徒都有过疑惑的时候：神真的在吗？祷告有用吗？我是不是信错了？有人因此很害怕，不敢说出来。<br>
Many Christians go through times of doubt. Is God really there? Does prayer make any difference? Did I believe the wrong thing? Some are frightened by this and do not dare to say it out loud.

圣经里有很多这样的人。《诗篇》73 篇的作者看见恶人顺利，“我的脚几乎失闪”，直到他“进了神的圣所”才想明白（诗 73:2，17）。施洗约翰在监狱里差人去问耶稣：“那将要来的是你吗？”（太 11:3）。耶稣没有责备他，叫人把自己做的事告诉约翰。<br>
The Bible is full of people like this. The writer of Psalm 73 saw the wicked prosper and said, “My feet were almost gone,” until he “entered God’s sanctuary” and understood (Psalm 73:2, 17). From prison John the Baptist sent people to ask Jesus, “Are you he who comes?” (Matthew 11:3). Jesus did not rebuke him. He told them to report to John what he was doing.

有疑惑的时候，可以这样做：<br>
When doubts come, here are some things you can do:

- **把疑惑说出来**：告诉神，也告诉一位成熟的基督徒。一位父亲对耶稣说：“我信！但我信不足，求主帮助”（可 9:24）。<br>
  **Say it out loud**: Tell God, and tell a mature Christian. One father said to Jesus, “I believe. Help my unbelief!” (Mark 9:24).
- **去查**：疑惑常常来自不了解。把问题写下来，去读圣经，读可靠的书，问懂的人。<br>
  **Look into it**: Doubt often comes from not knowing enough. Write the question down, read the Bible, read reliable books, and ask people who know.
- **不要一个人待着**：疑惑的时候更需要和其他基督徒一起聚会、祷告。<br>
  **Do not stay on your own**: Times of doubt are when you most need to meet and pray with other Christians.

## 耶稣也受过试探 | Jesus was tempted too

疑惑多半和想法有关，试探多半和欲望有关：明知不对，却很想去做。耶稣开始传道以前，在旷野受了魔鬼的试探（太 4:1-11）。魔鬼叫他把石头变成食物，耶稣回答：“经上记着说：人活着，不是单靠食物，乃是靠神口里所出的一切话”（太 4:4）。<br>
Doubt mostly has to do with our thinking. Temptation mostly has to do with our desires: we know something is wrong and still want to do it. Before Jesus began his ministry, he was tempted by the devil in the wilderness (Matthew 4:1–11). The devil told him to turn stones into bread, and Jesus answered, “It is written, ‘Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds out of God’s mouth’” (Matthew 4:4).

《希伯来书》说，耶稣“也曾凡事受过试探，与我们一样，只是他没有犯罪”（来 4:15）。所以我们受试探的时候，可以“坦然无惧地来到施恩的宝座前”（来 4:16）求帮助。<br>
Hebrews says Jesus “has been in all points tempted like we are, yet without sin” (Hebrews 4:15). So when we are tempted, we can “draw near with boldness to the throne of grace” (Hebrews 4:16) for help.

## 面对试探的几个办法 | Some ways to face temptation

- **记住出路是有的**：“神是信实的，必不叫你们受试探过于所能受的；在受试探的时候，总要给你们开一条出路”（林前 10:13）。<br>
  **Remember there is a way out**: “God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted above what you are able, but will with the temptation also make the way of escape” (1 Corinthians 10:13).
- **事先避开**：知道自己在哪里容易跌倒，就少去那里。例如晚上容易乱看手机，就把手机放在卧室外面。<br>
  **Avoid it ahead of time**: If you know where you tend to fall, stay away from it. For example, if you tend to scroll on your phone late at night, leave it outside the bedroom.
- **记几句经文**：耶稣用经上的话回答试探。可以把一两节相关的经文背下来。<br>
  **Memorize a few verses**: Jesus answered temptation with Scripture. You can learn one or two relevant verses by heart.
- **用主祷文祷告**：耶稣教我们求：“不叫我们遇见试探；救我们脱离凶恶”（太 6:13）。<br>
  **Pray the Lord's Prayer**: Jesus taught us to ask, “Bring us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one” (Matthew 6:13).
