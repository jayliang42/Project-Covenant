## 工作在堕落以前就有 | Work came before the fall

圣经开头说，神造了人以后，“将那人安置在伊甸园，使他修理，看守”（创 2:15）。那时人还没有犯罪。所以工作本身是好的，是神造人的时候就交给人的事。<br>
At the start of the Bible, after God makes the man, he “put him into the garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it” (Genesis 2:15). Humans had not yet sinned. So work itself is good, and God gave it to people when he made them.

人犯罪以后，神对亚当说，地要长出荆棘和蒺藜，他要汗流满面才得糊口（创 3:18-19）。工作变得辛苦，有时候白费力气。今天人在工作里遇到的挫败、不公平和疲倦，圣经在这里已经讲到了。<br>
After humans sinned, God told Adam that the ground would produce thorns and thistles and that he would eat bread by the sweat of his face (Genesis 3:18-19). Work became hard and sometimes fruitless. The frustration, unfairness, and weariness people meet at work today are already described here.

## 像是给主做的 | As for the Lord

保罗写信给歌罗西教会里作仆人的信徒说：“无论做什么，都要从心里做，像是给主做的，不是给人做的……你们所事奉的乃是主基督”（西 3:23-24）。这些人做的是最普通的劳动，保罗却说他们是在事奉基督。<br>
Paul writes to believers in Colossae who were servants: “whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men... for you serve the Lord Christ” (Colossians 3:23-24). These people did the most ordinary labour, and Paul says they were serving Christ.

16 世纪的改教家很强调这一点。马丁·路德说，农夫、工匠和父母的日常工作都是神给人的呼召，人借着这些工作服事邻舍，这些工作和修士、神父的工作一样圣洁。后人把这叫作“天职”的教导。<br>
The 16th-century Reformers stressed this point. Martin Luther said that the daily work of farmers, craftsmen, and parents is a calling from God, that people serve their neighbours through it, and that it is as holy as the work of a monk or a priest. Later writers call this the teaching on vocation.

对学生来说也是这样。认真上课、自己写作业、考试不作弊，这些都是在神面前忠心。<br>
The same holds for students. Paying attention in class, writing your own assignments, and refusing to cheat in exams are all ways of being faithful before God.

## 做一个可靠的人 | Being someone others can rely on

圣经对工作的人有几样具体的要求：<br>
The Bible asks several concrete things of people who work:

- **诚实**：不偷拿，不虚报，不抄袭。<br>
  **Honesty**: Do not take what is not yours, do not falsify, and do not copy others' work.
- **勤劳**：保罗说，“若有人不肯做工，就不可吃饭”（帖后 3:10）。他责备的是有能力却不肯做工的人。<br>
  **Diligence**: Paul says, “If anyone is not willing to work, don’t let him eat” (2 Thessalonians 3:10). He is rebuking people who are able to work and refuse to.
- **尊重人**：对上司、同事、下属和客户都一样。<br>
  **Respect**: Show it to supervisors, colleagues, those under you, and customers alike.

如果工作要求你做明显违背良心的事，例如造假或欺骗，你需要拒绝。这可能要付代价。遇到这种事，可以先和成熟的信徒商量。<br>
If your job requires something that plainly violates your conscience, such as falsifying records or deceiving people, you need to refuse. This may cost you something. When it happens, talk it over first with a mature believer.

## 工作有界限：安息 | Work has a limit: rest

第四条诫命说：“六日要劳碌做你一切的工，但第七日是向耶和华你神当守的安息日”（出 20:9-10）。神自己在创造以后歇了工，也吩咐人停下来。<br>
The fourth commandment says, “You shall labor six days, and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to Yahweh your God” (Exodus 20:9-10). God himself rested after creation, and he commands people to stop.

基督徒对安息日的具体守法有不同的理解。多数教会在主日聚会敬拜，有的教会强调主日整天不做日常的工作，有的教会认为重点是每周有规律地停下来敬拜和休息。各方都同意：人的价值不在于工作成绩，人需要停下来记得这一点。<br>
Christians understand the keeping of the Sabbath in different ways. Most churches gather for worship on the Lord's Day. Some stress doing no ordinary work that whole day, and others hold that the point is a regular weekly pause for worship and rest. All agree that a person's worth does not lie in achievements at work, and that people need to stop in order to remember this.
