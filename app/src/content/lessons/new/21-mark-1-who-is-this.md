## 这卷书的背景 | About this book

《马可福音》共 16 章，是四福音里最短的一卷。许多学者认为它也是最早写成的一卷，写作时间在主后 70 年前后。书里没有写作者的名字。早期教会一致认为作者是马可，也就是《使徒行传》里的“称呼马可的约翰”。<br>
Mark has 16 chapters and is the shortest of the four Gospels. Many scholars think it was also the first to be written, around AD 70. The book does not name its author. The early church was agreed that the writer was Mark, the “John who was called Mark” of Acts.

2 世纪初的帕皮亚说，马可是彼得的传译，他把彼得所讲的主的言行准确地写下来。这卷书的节奏很快，“立刻”一词在希腊文里出现了 41 次，光是第 1 章就有 10 次。<br>
Papias, in the early 2nd century, says that Mark was Peter's interpreter and wrote down accurately what Peter related of the Lord's words and deeds. The book moves fast. The Greek word for “immediately” occurs 41 times, ten of them in chapter 1 alone.

读的方法很简单：这四课每课读一段，每次读的时候问两个问题。第一，这段经文说耶稣是谁？第二，人们怎样回应他？<br>
The method is simple. Read one section with each of these four lessons, and ask two questions each time. First, who does this passage say Jesus is? Second, how do people respond to him?

## 开头就说明了主题 | The opening states the theme

第一句话是：“神的儿子，耶稣基督福音的起头”（可 1:1）。马可一开始就把结论告诉了读者，然后让读者看书里的人怎样一步一步认出这件事。<br>
The first sentence reads, “The beginning of the Good News of Jesus Christ, the Son of God” (Mark 1:1). Mark gives the reader the conclusion at the outset, and then lets the reader watch people in the story come to see it step by step.

耶稣受洗的时候，有声音从天上来说：“你是我的爱子，我喜悦你”（可 1:11）。然后他开始传道：“日期满了，神的国近了。你们当悔改，信福音！”（可 1:15）<br>
At Jesus' baptism a voice comes from the sky: “You are my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Mark 1:11). Then he begins to preach: “The time is fulfilled, and God’s Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News” (Mark 1:15).

## 他有权柄 | He has authority

1–8 章记了一连串的事，每一件都显出耶稣的权柄：<br>
Chapters 1–8 record a series of events, each showing Jesus' authority:

- **教导**：众人希奇他的教训，因为他教训他们像有权柄的人（1:22）。<br>
  **Teaching**: The people are astonished, because he teaches as one who has authority (1:22).
- **污鬼和疾病**：他赶出污鬼，医好许多病人（1:21-34）。<br>
  **Unclean spirits and illness**: He drives out unclean spirits and heals many who are sick (1:21-34).
- **赦罪**：他对瘫子说“你的罪赦了”，又叫他起来行走，证明“人子在地上有赦罪的权柄”（可 2:10）。<br>
  **Forgiving sins**: He tells a paralysed man that his sins are forgiven and then tells him to get up and walk, to show “that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins” (Mark 2:10).
- **安息日**：他说人子是安息日的主（2:28）。<br>
  **The Sabbath**: He says the Son of Man is lord of the Sabbath (2:28).
- **风和海**：他平静风浪，门徒彼此说：“这到底是谁，连风和海也听从他了”（可 4:41）。<br>
  **Wind and sea**: He stills a storm, and the disciples say to one another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?” (Mark 4:41).
- **死亡**：他叫睚鲁的女儿从死里起来（5:35-43）。<br>
  **Death**: He raises Jairus's daughter from death (5:35-43).
- **饥饿的人群**：他用五个饼两条鱼喂饱五千人（6:30-44）。<br>
  **Hungry crowds**: He feeds five thousand people with five loaves and two fish (6:30-44).

## 人们的回应 | How people respond

回应各不相同。众人希奇，跟着他走。宗教领袖起初质疑，后来商议怎样除掉他（3:6）。他的家人一度以为他癫狂了（3:21）。门徒跟随他，却一再不明白（8:17-21）。<br>
The responses vary. The crowds are amazed and follow him. The religious leaders question him at first and later plot how to destroy him (3:6). His own family at one point thinks he is out of his mind (3:21). The disciples follow him and yet again and again fail to understand (8:17-21).

耶稣讲的撒种的比喻（4:1-20）正好说明这些不同的回应：同样的种子落在不同的土里，结果不同。读的时候可以问自己：我是哪一种土？<br>
Jesus' parable of the sower (4:1-20) fits these different responses: the same seed falls on different soils with different results. As you read, you can ask yourself which soil you are.
