## 圣经里的禁食 | Fasting in the Bible

禁食就是在一段时间里不吃东西（有时也不喝水），专心向神祷告。圣经里的人在几种时候禁食：<br>
Fasting means going without food (sometimes also without water) for a time in order to focus on prayer to God. People in the Bible fasted at several kinds of times:

- **悔改的时候**：“你们应当禁食、哭泣、悲哀，一心归向我。你们要撕裂心肠，不撕裂衣服”（珥 2:12-13）。<br>
  **In repentance**: “turn to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning. Tear your heart, and not your garments” (Joel 2:12-13).
- **遇到危难的时候**：以斯帖在见王以前，请全城的犹太人为她禁食三天（斯 4:16）。<br>
  **In danger**: Before going to the king, Esther asked the Jews of the city to fast for her for three days (Esther 4:16).
- **做重要决定的时候**：安提阿教会“事奉主、禁食的时候”，圣灵差派巴拿巴和扫罗；他们又“禁食祷告”，然后打发他们去（徒 13:2-3）。<br>
  **Before important decisions**: As the church in Antioch “served the Lord and fasted,” the Holy Spirit set apart Barnabas and Saul, and after they “had fasted and prayed” they sent them off (Acts 13:2-3).

耶稣自己在旷野禁食四十天。他教导门徒说：“你们禁食的时候，不可像那假冒为善的人……你禁食的时候，要梳头洗脸，不叫人看出你禁食来，只叫你暗中的父看见”（太 6:16-18）。他又说，新郎离开以后，门徒“就要禁食”（太 9:15）。<br>
Jesus himself fasted forty days in the wilderness. He taught his disciples, “when you fast, don't be like the hypocrites... when you fast, anoint your head, and wash your face; so that you are not seen by men to be fasting, but by your Father who is in secret” (Matthew 6:16-18). He also said that after the bridegroom was taken away, his disciples “will fast” (Matthew 9:15).

禁食的目的不是向神证明自己，也不是赚取什么。以赛亚书 58 章说，神喜悦的禁食是“把你的饼分给饥饿的人，将飘流的穷人接到你家中”（赛 58:7）。禁食和怜悯人应当连在一起。<br>
Fasting is not meant to prove anything to God or to earn anything. Isaiah 58 says the fast God chooses is “to distribute your bread to the hungry, and that you bring the poor who are cast out to your house” (Isaiah 58:7). Fasting and mercy belong together.

## 各传统的做法 | How the traditions practise it

- **早期教会**：约 1 世纪末、2 世纪初写成的《十二使徒遗训》说，不要像假冒为善的人那样在星期一和星期四禁食，而要在星期三和星期五禁食。<br>
  **The early church**: The Didache, written around the end of the first or beginning of the second century, says not to fast with the hypocrites on Mondays and Thursdays, but on Wednesdays and Fridays.
- **东正教**：一年中大部分的星期三和星期五是禁食日，另有四个禁食节期：大斋期、使徒斋、圣母安息斋和圣诞斋。严格的禁食日不吃肉、蛋、奶制品、鱼，也不用酒和油。<br>
  **Orthodox**: Most Wednesdays and Fridays of the year are fast days, and there are four fasting seasons: Great Lent, the Apostles' Fast, the Dormition Fast, and the Nativity Fast. On strict fast days there is no meat, eggs, dairy, fish, wine, or oil.
- **天主教**：在美国，拉丁礼天主教徒在圣灰星期三和耶稣受难日要禁食（18 到 59 岁），这两天只吃一顿正餐，另外两顿小餐加起来不超过一顿正餐；14 岁以上的人在这两天和四旬期的每个星期五不吃肉。<br>
  **Catholic**: In the United States, Latin Catholics aged 18 to 59 fast on Ash Wednesday and Good Friday: one full meal, plus two smaller meals that together do not equal a full meal. Those aged 14 and over abstain from meat on those days and on all Fridays of Lent.
- **新教**：多数新教教会没有规定的禁食日，由信徒自己或教会在特别的时候决定禁食，例如为某件事一同禁食祷告。也有一些新教教会在四旬期鼓励信徒禁食或放下某样东西。<br>
  **Protestant**: Most Protestant churches have no required fast days. Believers or churches decide to fast at particular times, for example fasting and praying together about a specific need. Some Protestant churches encourage fasting or giving something up during Lent.

## 怎样开始，要注意什么 | How to start, and cautions

- **从短的开始**，比如不吃一顿饭，把吃饭的时间用来读经和祷告。<br>
  **Start small**, such as skipping one meal and using that time to read Scripture and pray.
- **定一个祷告的重点**，知道自己为什么禁食。<br>
  **Choose a focus for prayer**, so you know why you are fasting.
- **不要张扬**，也不要用禁食来衡量自己或别人的属灵程度。<br>
  **Don't make a show of it**, and don't use fasting to measure your own or anyone else's spirituality.
- **照顾身体**。怀孕或哺乳期的人、有糖尿病等疾病或在服药的人、曾有饮食方面困难的人，以及青少年，不适合不吃东西的禁食，先问医生。这些人可以选择别的方式，例如一段时间不用手机、不看娱乐节目，把时间用来祷告。<br>
  **Take care of your body.** People who are pregnant or breastfeeding, who have conditions such as diabetes or take medication, who have had difficulties with eating, and teenagers should not fast from food without first asking a doctor. They can choose another form, such as setting aside the phone or entertainment for a time and using that time to pray.
