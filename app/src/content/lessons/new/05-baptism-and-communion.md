## 基督徒需要教会 | Christians need the church

在看重个人的今天，人很容易觉得：“信仰是我和神之间的私事，我自己在家读经就够了。”新约的说法不同：一个人信靠基督，就与元首基督连结，同时也成为**“基督的身体”（林前 12:12-27）**的一部分，成为**“神家里的人”（弗 2:19）**。<br>
In a culture that prizes the individual, it is easy to think: "Faith is a private matter between me and God, and reading the Bible at home is enough." The New Testament says otherwise. A person who trusts Christ is joined to Christ the Head, and at the same time becomes part of **"the body of Christ" (1 Corinthians 12:12–27)** and a member of **"God's household" (Ephesians 2:19)**.

炉子里烧红的炭挤在一起能保持热度，单独夹出来放在地上，很快就凉了。新约里有许多“彼此”的命令，如彼此相爱、彼此担当、彼此劝勉。这些命令只有在实际的教会群体里才能实行。<br>
A glowing coal stays hot among the others in the stove. Taken out alone and set on the ground, it soon cools. The New Testament has many "one another" commands, such as love one another, bear one another's burdens, and encourage one another. They can only be carried out in an actual church community.

## 洗礼：公开归入基督的记号 | Baptism: the public sign of belonging to Christ

主耶稣升天前吩咐门徒：“奉父、子、圣灵的名给他们施洗”（太 28:19）。洗礼用水表明两件事：**罪被洗净**，以及**与基督同死、同埋葬、同复活，进入新生命**（罗 6:3-4）。<br>
Before he ascended, the Lord Jesus told his disciples to baptize "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" (Matthew 28:19). Baptism uses water to show two things: **cleansing from sin**, and **dying, being buried, and rising with Christ into new life** (Romans 6:3–4).

（各基督教传统的洗礼做法有所不同。浸信会和许多福音派教会为信徒施浸礼。改革宗／长老会、路德宗、圣公宗、卫理公会等传统为初信的成人施洗，也按圣约的应许为信徒家庭的婴孩施洗，等孩子长大后再行坚信礼。各传统都承认，奉三一真神的名施洗是严肃的事。）<br>
(Christian traditions differ in how they baptize. Baptist and many evangelical churches baptize believers by immersion. Reformed and Presbyterian, Lutheran, Anglican, Methodist, and other traditions baptize adult converts and also, on the basis of the covenant promise, the infants of believing families, who are confirmed when they are older. All traditions hold that baptism in the name of the triune God is a serious matter.)

## 圣餐（主餐）：记念主，领受喂养 | The Lord's Supper (Communion): remembering the Lord and being fed

耶稣被卖的那一夜，拿起饼和杯，设立了圣餐：“你们应当如此行，为的是记念我”（路 22:19；林前 11:24-26）。教会一同领圣餐的时候，同时在做三件事：<br>
On the night he was betrayed, Jesus took bread and the cup and instituted the Lord's Supper: "Do this in memory of me" (Luke 22:19; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26). When the church takes the Supper together, it does three things at once:

1. **向后看，感恩记念**：记念基督在十字架上为我们舍身流血，重温新约里赦罪的应许。<br>
   **Looking back in thanks**: Remembering that Christ gave his body and shed his blood for us on the cross, and recalling the new covenant's promise of forgiveness.
2. **向内看，也看身边的人**：借着信心，在圣灵里领受基督属灵的喂养。分领同一个饼的时候，也省察自己，与弟兄姊妹和好（林前 10:16-17）。<br>
   **Looking within and around**: By faith, receiving spiritual nourishment from Christ in the Spirit. In sharing the one loaf we also examine ourselves and are reconciled with our brothers and sisters (1 Corinthians 10:16–17).
3. **向前看，盼望等候**：“表明主的死，直等到他来”，预先尝到将来羔羊婚筵的喜乐。<br>
   **Looking ahead in hope**: Proclaiming the Lord's death "until he comes," with a foretaste of the joy of the coming marriage supper of the Lamb.
