## 没有“独行侠式”的基督徒 | Why there are no solo Christians in the New Testament

在个人主义盛行的今天，很容易觉得：“信仰是我和神两个人的私事，我自己在家读经就够了。”但在新约圣经里，当你信靠基督时，你不仅与元首基督连结，也同时成为了**“基督的身体”（林前 12:12-27）和“神家里的人”（弗 2:19）**。<br>
In an individualistic culture, it is easy to think, "Faith is strictly between me and God; I can do it alone at home." Yet in the New Testament, trusting Christ unites you not only to the Head, but also to his **Body** (1 Corinthians 12:12–27) and **God's household** (Ephesians 2:19).

一块烧红的炭留在火炉里会彼此取暖，单独夹出来放在地上很快就会变冷。新约里上百处“彼此相爱、彼此担当、彼此劝勉”的命令，只有在真实的教会群体里才能活出来。<br>
A glowing coal stays hot in the hearth, but cools quickly when pulled out by itself. The New Testament's dozens of "one another" commands — love one another, bear one another's burdens, encourage one another — can only be practiced in real community.

## 洗礼：归入基督的公开印记 | Baptism: The public sign of belonging to Christ

主耶稣在升天前吩咐门徒：“奉父、子、圣灵的名给他们施洗”（太 28:19）。洗礼用“水”显明双重图画：**洗净罪污**，以及**与基督同死、同埋葬、同复活进入新生命**（罗 6:3-4）。<br>
Before ascending, Jesus commanded his disciples to baptize "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" (Matthew 28:19). Baptism uses water to picture two realities: **cleansing from sin** and **dying and rising with Christ into new life** (Romans 6:3–4).

（不同基督教传统在洗礼形式上有所差异：浸信会与许多福音派教会施行信徒受浸；改革宗/长老会、路德宗、圣公宗、卫理公会等传统则除了为初信成人施洗外，也按圣约应许为信徒家庭的婴孩施洗，待其长大后坚信。各传统都共同承认奉三一真神之名施洗的严肃意义。）<br>
(Christian traditions differ in practice: Baptist and many evangelical churches practice believer's baptism by immersion, while Reformed/Presbyterian, Lutheran, Anglican, and Methodist traditions baptize both professing believers and the children of believers within the covenant promise. All unite in honoring baptism in the Triune name.)

## 圣餐（主餐）：在旅途中记念主并受喂养 | The Lord's Supper (Communion): Remembering and being nourished

在被卖的那一夜，耶稣拿起饼和杯设立了圣餐：“你们应当如此行，为的是记念我”（路 22:19；林前 11:24-26）。每逢教会一同领受圣餐，我们同时做三件事：<br>
On the night he was betrayed, Jesus took bread and the cup and instituted the Lord's Supper: "Do this in memory of me" (Luke 22:19; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26). Whenever the church gathers at the table, we look in three directions at once:

1. **向后看（感恩记念）**：记念基督在十字架上为我们舍身流血，重温新约的赦罪应许。<br>
   **Looking back in gratitude**: Remembering Christ's body given and blood poured out for the forgiveness of sins.
2. **向内与向四周看（与主相交、彼此合一）**：借着信心在圣灵里领受基督属灵的喂养，也在分受同一个饼时省察自己、与弟兄姊妹修复关系（林前 10:16-17）。<br>
   **Looking around in fellowship**: Being spiritually nourished by Christ and reconciled with one another as one body sharing one loaf (1 Corinthians 10:16–17).
3. **向前看（盼望等候）**：“表明主的死，直等到他来”——预尝将来天国羔羊婚筵的喜乐。<br>
   **Looking ahead in hope**: Proclaiming the Lord's death "until he comes" and anticipating the feast of the renewed creation.
