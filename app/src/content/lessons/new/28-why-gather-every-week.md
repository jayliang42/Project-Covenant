## 为什么要聚会 | Why gather

有人说：“我在家里也可以读经祷告，为什么一定要去教会？”希伯来书的作者说：“又要彼此相顾，激发爱心，勉励行善。你们不可停止聚会，好像那些停止惯了的人，倒要彼此劝勉”（来 10:24-25）。聚会是为了自己领受，也是为了照顾别人、鼓励别人。<br>
Some say, “I can read the Bible and pray at home. Why do I have to go to church?” The writer of Hebrews says, “Let's consider how to provoke one another to love and good works, not forsaking our own assembling together, as the custom of some is, but exhorting one another” (Hebrews 10:24-25). We gather to receive, and also to care for and encourage others.

五旬节以后，第一批信徒“都恒心遵守使徒的教训，彼此交接，擘饼，祈祷”（徒 2:42）。新约提到他们在“七日的第一日”聚会擘饼（徒 20:7），并在这一天为穷困的信徒抽出钱来（林前 16:2）。启示录称这一天为“主日”（启 1:10）。这一天是耶稣复活的日子。<br>
After Pentecost the first believers “continued steadfastly in the apostles' teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and prayer” (Acts 2:42). The New Testament mentions them gathering to break bread “on the first day of the week” (Acts 20:7) and setting money aside on that day for needy believers (1 Corinthians 16:2). Revelation calls it “the Lord's day” (Revelation 1:10). It is the day Jesus rose.

## 2 世纪的主日聚会 | A Sunday gathering in the second century

大约主后 155 年，殉道者游斯丁（Justin Martyr）写信给罗马皇帝，描述基督徒在“称为太阳日的那一天”怎样聚会：<br>
Around AD 155 Justin Martyr wrote to the Roman emperor and described how Christians met “on the day called Sunday”:

1. 住在城里和乡下的人聚在一处，在时间许可的范围内宣读“使徒的回忆录”或“先知的书”。<br>
   Those living in the cities and the countryside gather in one place, and the “memoirs of the apostles” or the “writings of the prophets” are read as long as time permits.
2. 读完以后，主领的人讲话，劝勉众人效法所读的好榜样。<br>
   After the reading, the presider speaks, urging the people to imitate what has been read.
3. 众人站起来一同祷告。<br>
   All stand and pray together.
4. 拿来饼和掺水的酒，主领的人献上祷告和感谢，众人说“阿们”，每个人领受；执事把饼和酒送给没有来的人。<br>
   Bread and wine mixed with water are brought. The presider offers prayers and thanksgiving, the people say “Amen,” and each receives a share; deacons take portions to those who are absent.
5. 有能力的人随意捐献，用来照顾孤儿、寡妇、病人、坐监的人和外地来的人。<br>
   Those who are able give as they choose, and the gifts are used to care for orphans, widows, the sick, prisoners, and visitors from abroad.

游斯丁说，选在这一天，因为这是神开始创造世界的第一日，也是耶稣基督从死里复活的日子。<br>
Justin says they meet on this day because it is the first day, when God began to make the world, and the day Jesus Christ rose from the dead.

## 今天崇拜里常见的部分 | Common parts of worship today

今天各教会的崇拜形式差别很大：有的有固定的礼仪，有的比较自由。但很多都保留了游斯丁描述的几个部分：<br>
Churches today worship in very different forms. Some follow a set liturgy; others are freer. But many keep the parts Justin described:

- **诗歌和赞美**：保罗说要“用诗章、颂词、灵歌，彼此教导，互相劝戒”（西 3:16）。唱歌是唱给神听的，也是彼此提醒。<br>
  **Songs and praise**: Paul speaks of “teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs” (Colossians 3:16). Singing is addressed to God, and it also reminds one another of the truth.
- **读经和讲道**：一同听神的话，并听人解释和应用。<br>
  **Scripture reading and preaching**: Hearing God's word together, explained and applied.
- **祷告**：为教会、为世界、为有需要的人祷告。很多教会也一同念主祷文或认罪的祷告。<br>
  **Prayer**: For the church, the world, and those in need. Many churches also pray the Lord's Prayer or a prayer of confession together.
- **圣餐**：各教会举行的次数不同，有的每周，有的每月或每季。<br>
  **The Lord's Supper**: Churches celebrate it with different frequency: some weekly, some monthly or quarterly.
- **奉献**：把收入的一部分献给神，用于教会的事工和帮助有需要的人。<br>
  **Giving**: Offering part of one's income to God, for the church's work and for helping those in need.
- **祝福**：聚会结束时，牧者用圣经的话为会众祝福，然后差派大家回到生活中。<br>
  **Blessing**: At the end, the minister blesses the congregation with words from Scripture and sends them back into daily life.

主日要不要像旧约的安息日那样休息，各传统看法不同。“扎根成长”路径里“安息日和主日”一课会介绍。<br>
The traditions differ on whether Sunday should be a day of rest like the Old Testament Sabbath. The lesson on the Sabbath and the Lord's Day in the Going Deeper path introduces the views.
