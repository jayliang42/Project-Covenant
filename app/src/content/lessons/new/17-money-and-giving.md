## 钱和心 | Money and the heart

耶稣说：“你的财宝在哪里，你的心也在那里”（太 6:21）。他又说：“一个人不能事奉两个主……你们不能又事奉神，又事奉玛门”（太 6:24）。“玛门”指钱财。耶稣把钱说成一个可能和神争位置的主人。<br>
Jesus says, “where your treasure is, there your heart will be also” (Matthew 6:21). He also says, “No one can serve two masters... You can’t serve both God and Mammon” (Matthew 6:24). “Mammon” means wealth. Jesus speaks of money as a master that can compete with God for first place.

圣经没有说钱是恶的。保罗说的是：“贪财是万恶之根”（提前 6:10）。有问题的是人爱钱、倚靠钱。钱本身是神给人使用的东西。<br>
The Bible does not say that money is evil. What Paul says is, “the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil” (1 Timothy 6:10). The trouble lies in loving money and relying on it. Money itself is something God gives people to use.

## 知足 | Contentment

保罗在监狱里写信说：“我无论在什么景况都可以知足，这是我已经学会了”（腓 4:11）。他说这是“学会”的，可见知足需要练习。<br>
Paul writes from prison, “I have learned in whatever state I am, to be content in it” (Philippians 4:11). He says he “learned” it, so contentment takes practice.

实际的做法包括：量入为出，尽量不为消费借债，记账，买东西以前等几天再决定。这些做法圣经没有逐条规定，但它们能帮助人不被钱牵着走。<br>
Practical steps include living within your income, avoiding debt for things you consume, keeping a record of spending, and waiting a few days before buying. The Bible gives no list of such steps, yet they help keep money from leading you around.

## 奉献 | Giving

新约讲奉献，讲得最多的是态度。“各人要随本心所酌定的，不要作难，不要勉强，因为捐得乐意的人是神所喜爱的”（林后 9:7）。<br>
When the New Testament speaks about giving, it speaks most about attitude. “Let each man give according as he has determined in his heart, not grudgingly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver” (2 Corinthians 9:7).

耶稣看见一个穷寡妇投了两个小钱，就说她“所投的比众人还多”，因为别人是有余才给，她是在不足的时候给（路 21:3-4）。神看的是人按自己的能力给了多少。<br>
Jesus saw a poor widow put in two small coins and said she “put in more than all of them,” because the others gave from their surplus and she gave out of her poverty (Luke 21:3-4). God looks at how much a person gives in proportion to what they have.

奉献的去处通常有三个：支持你所在的教会，帮助有需要的人，支持传福音的工作。<br>
Giving usually goes in three directions: supporting your own church, helping people in need, and supporting the spread of the gospel.

## 十一奉献：各教会的看法 | The tithe: what churches teach

旧约的律法要求以色列人把出产的十分之一献上。今天的基督徒是否必须照这个比例奉献，各教会看法不同。<br>
The Old Testament law required Israelites to give a tenth of their produce. Churches differ on whether Christians today must give that percentage.

- **把十分之一当作标准**：一些教会和教导理财的事工认为，十分之一是神为他子民定下的原则，今天仍然适用，新约的榜样往往给得更多。美南浸信会长期鼓励信徒至少把收入的十分之一奉献给所在的教会。<br>
  **A tenth as the standard**: Some churches and financial-teaching ministries hold that the tenth is a principle God set for his people which still applies, and that New Testament examples often go beyond it. The Southern Baptist Convention has long encouraged believers to give at least a tenth of their income to their local church.
- **凭恩典奉献**：另一些教会和学者认为，十一奉献属于摩西律法，基督已经成全了律法。新约没有规定比例，只要求信徒甘心、慷慨、按能力、有计划地给。美国浸礼会说，十一奉献是对神恩典的感恩回应，不是律法上的义务。<br>
  **Giving by grace**: Other churches and scholars hold that the tithe belonged to the law of Moses, which Christ has fulfilled. The New Testament sets no percentage and asks believers to give willingly, generously, in proportion to their means, and with a plan. American Baptist Churches USA describes tithing as a grateful response to God's grace and says it is no legal obligation.
- **天主教**：不规定固定的比例。《天主教教理》第 2043 条说，信徒有责任按各自的能力供应教会物质上的需要。<br>
  **Catholic**: The Catholic Church sets no fixed percentage. Paragraph 2043 of the Catechism says the faithful have the duty of providing for the material needs of the church, each according to his own ability.
- **东正教**：美国东正教会说明，教会从来没有正式要求十一奉献。有些堂区鼓励信徒这样做，教会更看重的是信徒出于感恩，把最先的一份献给神。<br>
  **Orthodox**: The Orthodox Church in America explains that it has never officially required tithing. Some parishes encourage it, and the church gives more weight to believers offering the first portion to God out of gratitude.

Barna 在 2021 年做的一项调查发现，美国牧师当中约有三分之一把十分之一作为会众奉献的标准。可见这个问题在教会里并没有一致的答案。<br>
A Barna survey carried out in 2021 found that about a third of U.S. pastors use the tenth as the giving standard for their congregation. The question plainly has no single answer across the churches.

如果你刚开始学习奉献，可以先定一个比例，按时给，过一段时间再重新考虑。也可以问问你的教会是怎样教导的。<br>
If you are just beginning to give, you might settle on a percentage, give it regularly, and review it after a while. You can also ask how your own church teaches on this.
