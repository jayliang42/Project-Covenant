## 两组经文 | Two sets of passages

一组经文强调不以暴力报复：“使人和睦的人有福了”（太 5:9）；“不要与恶人作对。有人打你的右脸，连左脸也转过来由他打”（太 5:39）；“要爱你们的仇敌，为那逼迫你们的祷告”（太 5:44）。彼得拔刀的时候，耶稣说：“收刀入鞘吧！凡动刀的，必死在刀下”（太 26:52）。保罗说：“不要自己伸冤”（罗 12:19）。先知预言将来人们“要将刀打成犁头”（赛 2:4）。<br>
One set of passages stresses not returning violence: “Blessed are the peacemakers” (Matthew 5:9); “don't resist him who is evil; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also” (Matthew 5:39); “love your enemies... and pray for those who mistreat you and persecute you” (Matthew 5:44). When Peter drew his sword, Jesus said, “Put your sword back into its place, for all those who take the sword will die by the sword” (Matthew 26:52). Paul says, “Don't seek revenge yourselves” (Romans 12:19). The prophet foresees a day when people “shall beat their swords into plowshares” (Isaiah 2:4).

另一组经文说到执政者的权柄：执政的“不是空空地佩剑；他是神的用人，是伸冤的，刑罚那作恶的”（罗 13:4）。兵丁问施洗约翰该怎样做，约翰没有叫他们离开军队，只说“不要以强暴待人，也不要讹诈人，自己有钱粮就当知足”（路 3:14）。<br>
Another set speaks of the authority of rulers: the ruler “doesn't bear the sword in vain; for he is a servant of God, an avenger for wrath to him who does evil” (Romans 13:4). When soldiers asked John the Baptist what they should do, he did not tell them to leave the army. He said, “Extort from no one by violence, neither accuse anyone wrongfully. Be content with your wages” (Luke 3:14).

各传统都同意，耶稣的教导要求门徒在个人生活中不报复、爱仇敌。分歧在于：国家能不能用武力保护人，基督徒能不能参与。<br>
All traditions agree that Jesus' teaching calls his disciples not to take revenge and to love their enemies in their personal lives. They differ on whether the state may use force to protect people, and whether Christians may take part.

## 正义战争论 | Just war

正义战争论在早期教会就有讨论，4、5 世纪的奥古斯丁是重要的代表，后来阿奎那等人进一步发展。它的出发点是：战争总是恶的，但在某些严格的条件下，用武力抵抗不义的侵害可以是正当的。常列的条件有：<br>
Just war thinking was discussed in the early church. Augustine, in the fourth and fifth centuries, is a key figure, and later writers such as Aquinas developed it further. Its starting point is that war is always an evil, but under certain strict conditions using force to resist unjust aggression can be justified. Commonly listed conditions include:

- **开战的条件**：有正当的理由（例如抵抗攻击）；由合法的权威决定；是最后的手段；有成功的合理希望；带来的恶不超过要消除的恶。<br>
  **For going to war**: a just cause (such as resisting attack); a decision by legitimate authority; last resort; a reasonable hope of success; and the evil caused must not outweigh the evil to be removed.
- **作战的条件**：区分军人和平民，不可以平民为攻击目标；使用的武力要相称。<br>
  **In the conduct of war**: distinguishing combatants from civilians, who may not be targeted; and using proportionate force.

《天主教教理》第 2309 条用这些条件来衡量用武力进行正当防卫。1530 年的《奥格斯堡信条》第 16 条说，基督徒可以担任公职，“参与正义的战争”“当兵”。改革宗、圣公会和大多数福音派教会也持类似的立场。<br>
Paragraph 2309 of the Catholic Catechism uses these conditions to judge legitimate defence by military force. Article 16 of the Augsburg Confession (1530) says Christians may hold public office, “engage in just wars,” and “serve as soldiers.” Reformed, Anglican, and most evangelical churches hold similar positions.

## 和平主义 | Pacifism

宗教改革时期的重洗派认为，信徒应当完全照耶稣的登山宝训生活。1527 年的《施莱泰姆信条》第 6 条说，刀剑是神在“基督的完全之外”设立的，执政的可以用来惩恶保善；但信徒不用刀剑，也不宜作官。门诺会、弟兄会继承了这个传统。<br>
The Anabaptists of the Reformation era held that believers should live wholly by the Sermon on the Mount. Article 6 of the Schleitheim Confession (1527) says the sword is “ordained of God outside the perfection of Christ,” for rulers to punish evil and protect the good; but believers do not use the sword and should not serve as magistrates. Mennonites and Brethren carry on this tradition.

17 世纪的贵格会（公谊会）在 1660 年给英王查理二世的声明里说：“一切流血的原则和做法，我们都完全否认”，包括一切外在的战争和争斗。门诺会、弟兄会和贵格会常被称为“历史和平教会”。很多和平主义者也积极从事调解、救援和社会服务。<br>
In a declaration to King Charles II in 1660, the Quakers (Society of Friends) said, “All bloody principles and practices we do utterly deny, with all outward wars.” Mennonites, Brethren, and Quakers are often called the “historic peace churches.” Many pacifists are also active in mediation, relief, and social service.

## 彼此的批评 | Criticisms each side makes

和平主义者认为，正义战争论在历史上常被用来为不义的战争辩护，而且很难符合它自己的条件；耶稣的话应当照字面遵行。持正义战争论的人认为，爱邻舍有时意味着保护无辜的人免受攻击；如果所有人都放弃武力，弱者会被欺压；登山宝训讲的是个人的报复，不是政府的职责。<br>
Pacifists argue that just war theory has often been used to justify unjust wars and that its own conditions are hard to meet; Jesus' words should be followed as they stand. Just war proponents argue that loving one's neighbour sometimes means protecting the innocent from attack, that if everyone gave up force the weak would be oppressed, and that the Sermon on the Mount concerns personal revenge, not the duties of government.

两种立场的人都看重和平。保罗说：“若是能行，总要尽力与众人和睦”（罗 12:18）。<br>
People on both sides value peace. Paul says, “If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men” (Romans 12:18).
