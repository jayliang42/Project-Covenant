## 新约里的恩赐 | The gifts in the New Testament

保罗在林前 12–14 章、罗马书 12 章和以弗所书 4 章列了许多恩赐，有教导、劝勉、帮助、治理、施舍，也有说方言、翻方言、预言、医病和行异能。他说：“圣灵显在各人身上，是叫人得益处”（林前 12:7），又说：“你们要追求爱，也要切慕属灵的恩赐”（林前 14:1）。<br>
In 1 Corinthians 12–14, Romans 12, and Ephesians 4, Paul lists many gifts: teaching, encouraging, helping, leading, and giving, as well as tongues, interpretation, prophecy, healing, and miracles. He says, “to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the profit of all” (1 Corinthians 12:7), and “Follow after love and earnestly desire spiritual gifts” (1 Corinthians 14:1).

教导、帮助这类恩赐今天继续，这一点没有争议。争议在于方言、预言、医病这些带神迹性质的恩赐。<br>
No one disputes that gifts such as teaching and helping continue today. The debate concerns the gifts of a miraculous kind: tongues, prophecy, and healing.

## 停止论 | Cessationism

停止论认为，神迹性的恩赐在使徒时代以后已经停止。普林斯顿神学家华腓德（B. B. Warfield）是这个立场的代表人物之一。主要的理由有：<br>
Cessationism holds that the miraculous gifts ceased after the apostolic age. The Princeton theologian B. B. Warfield is one of its best-known representatives. Its main arguments are these:

- 神迹是“使徒的凭据”（林后 12:12）。使徒必须亲眼见过复活的主，由主亲自差派，这样的使徒已经没有了。<br>
  Miracles were “the signs of an apostle” (2 Corinthians 12:12). Apostles had to have seen the risen Lord and been sent by him in person, and there are no such apostles now.
- 这些恩赐的作用是为使徒传的信息作证（来 2:3-4），为教会立根基（弗 2:20）。根基立好了，正典也完成了，作用就结束了。<br>
  These gifts served to confirm the apostolic message (Hebrews 2:3-4) and to lay the church's foundation (Ephesians 2:20). Once the foundation was laid and the canon complete, their purpose was fulfilled.
- 教会历史上，这些恩赐在使徒以后大体上消失了。<br>
  In church history these gifts largely disappeared after the apostles.

停止论者并不否认神今天会医治人、回应祷告。“温和的停止论者”还承认自己可能看错，也看到灵恩运动里有许多好的地方。<br>
Cessationists do not deny that God heals people and answers prayer today. “Soft” cessationists also allow that they could be wrong, and recognise much good in the charismatic movement.

## 延续论 | Continuationism

延续论认为，所有恩赐今天都继续。神学家古德恩（Wayne Grudem）是这个立场的代表人物之一。主要的理由有：<br>
Continuationism holds that all the gifts continue today. The theologian Wayne Grudem is one of its best-known representatives. Its main arguments are these:

- 新约里不是使徒的人也有这些恩赐，例如腓利、司提反和哥林多教会的信徒（徒 6–8 章；林前 12–14 章）。<br>
  People who were not apostles had these gifts in the New Testament, such as Philip, Stephen, and the believers at Corinth (Acts 6–8; 1 Corinthians 12–14).
- 恩赐的目的是造就教会（林前 14:3、26），教会今天仍然需要被造就。<br>
  The gifts were given to build up the church (1 Corinthians 14:3, 26), and the church still needs building up today.
- 林前 13:10 说“等那完全的来到，这有限的必归于无有”。延续论者多认为“那完全的”指基督再来；停止论者中有人认为指正典完成，也有人同意指基督再来，但从别的经文论证恩赐已经停止。<br>
  1 Corinthians 13:10 says, “when that which is complete has come, then that which is partial will be done away with.” Continuationists mostly take “that which is complete” to mean Christ's return. Some cessationists take it to mean the completed canon; others agree it means Christ's return but argue from other passages that the gifts have ceased.

延续论者也重视圣经独特的权威。古德恩说，今天的预言必须服在圣经之下。“第三波”（Third Wave）运动是延续论里的一支，强调在传福音的同时有“神迹奇事”。<br>
Continuationists also value the unique authority of Scripture. Grudem says that prophecy today must be subject to Scripture. The “Third Wave” is one branch of continuationism, which stresses “signs and wonders” alongside the preaching of the gospel.

## 古典五旬节派 | Classical Pentecostalism

神召会的《基本真理宣言》说，所有信徒都可以领受圣灵的洗，这和重生“有分别，并在重生以后”；受圣灵的洗“最初的外在记号”是说方言。这是古典五旬节派的立场。很多灵恩派和延续论者相信恩赐今天继续，但不认为说方言是受圣灵的洗必有的记号。<br>
The Assemblies of God Statement of Fundamental Truths says that all believers are entitled to baptism in the Holy Spirit, which is “distinct from and subsequent to the experience of the new birth,” and that its “initial physical sign” is speaking in other tongues. This is the classical Pentecostal position. Many charismatics and continuationists believe the gifts continue today without holding that tongues is the necessary sign of Spirit baptism.

## 开放但谨慎 | Open but cautious

还有很多福音派信徒处在中间，叫作“开放但谨慎”：不排除这些恩赐今天存在，同时对很多自称的神迹和预言保持警惕。有作者认为美国多数福音派信徒其实是这个立场。<br>
Many evangelicals sit in between, in a position called “open but cautious”: they do not rule out these gifts today, and they are wary of many claimed miracles and prophecies. One writer suggests that most American evangelicals in fact hold this position.

## 各方都同意的地方 | What all sides agree on

- 恩赐是圣灵白白给的，不能证明一个人比别人属灵（林前 12:11）。<br>
  Gifts are given freely by the Spirit and do not prove that one person is more spiritual than another (1 Corinthians 12:11).
- 爱比恩赐更重要（林前 13 章）。<br>
  Love matters more than gifts (1 Corinthians 13).
- 任何“启示”都要用圣经来察验：“不要消灭圣灵的感动；不要藐视先知的讲论。但要凡事察验；善美的要持守”（帖前 5:19-21）。<br>
  Any “revelation” must be tested by Scripture: “Don't quench the Spirit. Don't despise prophecies. Test all things, and hold firmly that which is good” (1 Thessalonians 5:19-21).
- 聚会要“规规矩矩地按着次序行”（林前 14:40）。<br>
  Worship is to be done “decently and in order” (1 Corinthians 14:40).
