## 这是什么样的书 | What kind of book this is

《启示录》开头说明了自己的几种身份：它是“耶稣基督的启示”（启 1:1），是“预言”（启 1:3），也是一封写给“亚细亚的七个教会”的信（启 1:4）。<br>
Revelation describes itself in several ways at the start: it is “the Revelation of Jesus Christ” (Revelation 1:1), it is “prophecy” (Revelation 1:3), and it is a letter to “the seven assemblies that are in Asia” (Revelation 1:4).

“启示文学”是当时犹太人熟悉的一种文体，《但以理书》后半部也有这种写法。它用异象、数字、怪兽和天上的景象来讲历史的真相，特别是在受压迫的时候，告诉读者神仍然掌权。<br>
Apocalyptic writing was a form familiar to Jews of the time. The second half of Daniel is written this way too. It uses visions, numbers, beasts, and heavenly scenes to show what is happening in history, especially in times of oppression, and tells readers that God is still in control.

大多数学者认为这卷书写于主后 1 世纪末，罗马皇帝图密善在位的时候，也有学者认为更早。当时七个教会有的受逼迫，有的被罗马社会同化，有的冷淡（启 2–3 章）。<br>
Most scholars date the book to the end of the 1st century AD, during the reign of the emperor Domitian, though some date it earlier. At the time, some of the seven churches were being persecuted, some were blending into Roman society, and some had grown lukewarm (Revelation 2–3).

## 读象征的方法 | How to read the symbols

《启示录》里的象征大多来自旧约。读的时候可以注意：<br>
Most of Revelation's symbols come from the Old Testament. As you read, notice these:

- **数字**：七常常表示完全，例如七个教会、七印、七号、七碗。<br>
  **Numbers**: Seven often stands for completeness, as in the seven churches, seals, trumpets, and bowls.
- **画面的反差**：约翰听见“犹大支派中的狮子……他已得胜”，转身看见的是“羔羊站立，像是被杀过的”（启 5:5-6）。神得胜的方式是十字架。<br>
  **Contrasting images**: John hears that “the Lion who is of the tribe of Judah ... has overcome,” but when he turns he sees “a Lamb standing, as though it had been slain” (Revelation 5:5–6). God wins by way of the cross.
- **巴比伦**：许多学者认为，对当时的读者来说，“大巴比伦”指的是罗马这样骄傲、压迫人的帝国。<br>
  **Babylon**: Many scholars think that for the first readers, “Babylon the great” pointed to a proud, oppressive empire like Rome.

不要把每个细节都对应到今天的新闻上。有人用“六百六十六”（启 13:18）去推算现代人物，结果一次次落空。<br>
Do not try to match every detail to today's news. People have used the number 666 (Revelation 13:18) to identify modern figures, and their guesses have failed again and again.

## 几种解读方式 | Several ways of interpreting it

教会历史上主要有四种读法：<br>
Church history has produced four main approaches:

- **过去派**：认为书中大部分内容已经在 1 世纪罗马帝国的历史中应验。<br>
  **Preterist**: Most of the book was fulfilled in the history of the 1st-century Roman Empire.
- **历史派**：认为书中的事件对应从使徒时代到基督再来的整个教会历史。<br>
  **Historicist**: The book's events map onto the whole of church history from the apostles to Christ's return.
- **未来派**：认为第 4 章以后大部分内容是基督再来前后才发生的事。<br>
  **Futurist**: Most of chapter 4 onward describes events around the time of Christ's return.
- **理想派**：认为书中的画面讲的是每个时代都有的属灵争战，不对应具体的历史事件。<br>
  **Idealist**: The images portray the spiritual conflict found in every age, without matching specific historical events.

很多读者会把几种读法结合起来用。<br>
Many readers combine elements of several approaches.

## 不同读法都承认的信息 | The message all approaches share

无论采用哪一种读法，有几点是清楚的：神坐在宝座上掌权；被杀的羔羊已经得胜；邪恶会被审判；受苦的信徒要忠心，“你务要至死忠心，我就赐给你那生命的冠冕”（启 2:10）；最后神要使一切都更新（启 21:5）。<br>
Whatever approach you take, some things are clear: God reigns from his throne; the slain Lamb has already won; evil will be judged; suffering believers are called to faithfulness, “Be faithful to death, and I will give you the crown of life” (Revelation 2:10); and in the end God will make all things new (Revelation 21:5).

《启示录》一开头就应许读者蒙福：“念这书上预言的和那些听见又遵守其中所记载的，都是有福的”（启 1:3）。<br>
Revelation opens with a blessing on its readers: “Blessed is he who reads and those who hear the words of the prophecy, and keep the things that are written in it” (Revelation 1:3).
