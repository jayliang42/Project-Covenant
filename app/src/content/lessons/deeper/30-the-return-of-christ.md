## 共同的盼望 | The common hope

耶稣升天的时候，天使对门徒说：“这离开你们被接升天的耶稣，你们见他怎样往天上去，他还要怎样来”（徒 1:11）。《尼西亚信经》说：“他将来必有荣耀再来，审判活人死人，他的国度永无穷尽。”天主教、东正教和新教都承认这些。<br>
When Jesus ascended, angels said to the disciples, “This Jesus, who was received up from you into the sky, will come back in the same way as you saw him going into the sky” (Acts 1:11). The Nicene Creed says, “He will come again in glory to judge the living and the dead, and his kingdom will have no end.” Catholics, Orthodox, and Protestants all confess this.

各传统也都同意耶稣的话：“那日子，那时辰，没有人知道……惟独父知道”（太 24:36）。历史上很多人推算过日期，都落空了。<br>
All traditions also accept Jesus' words: “no one knows of that day and hour... but my Father only” (Matthew 24:36). Many people in history have calculated a date, and every one has failed.

## 启示录 20 章的“一千年” | The “thousand years” of Revelation 20

启示录 20 章说，撒但被捆绑一千年，殉道的人“复活了，与基督一同作王一千年”（启 20:4）。这一千年是什么，什么时候发生，是分歧的中心。<br>
Revelation 20 says that Satan is bound for a thousand years, and that the martyrs “lived and reigned with Christ for a thousand years” (Revelation 20:4). What this thousand years is, and when it happens, is the heart of the disagreement.

- **无千禧年观**：一千年是象征的说法，指基督现在从天上掌权、教会在世上的这段时期，也就是基督两次降临之间的整个时代。基督再来时，死人一同复活，接着是最后审判和新天新地。奥古斯丁在《上帝之城》第 20 卷提出这种理解，以后成为西方教会的主流。天主教、东正教、信义宗和很多改革宗教会持这种看法。《天主教教理》第 676 条说，教会拒绝千禧年主义，包括它的各种修正形式。<br>
  **Amillennialism**: The thousand years is symbolic and refers to the present period in which Christ reigns from heaven and the church lives on earth, that is, the whole age between his two comings. At Christ's return the dead rise together, followed by the final judgment and the new heavens and earth. Augustine set out this understanding in Book 20 of The City of God, and it became the main view in the Western church. Catholics, Orthodox, Lutherans, and many Reformed churches hold it. Paragraph 676 of the Catechism of the Catholic Church says the church has rejected millenarianism, even in modified forms.
- **历史前千禧年观**：基督在千禧年之前再来，在地上作王一千年，然后才是最后的审判。2 世纪的殉道者游斯丁和爱任纽都持某种形式的前千禧年看法。今天不少福音派学者持这种看法，例如 20 世纪的赖德（George Eldon Ladd）。<br>
  **Historic premillennialism**: Christ returns before the millennium and reigns on earth for a thousand years, after which comes the final judgment. In the 2nd century Justin Martyr and Irenaeus held forms of premillennialism. A good number of evangelical scholars hold this view today, for example George Eldon Ladd in the 20th century.
- **时代论前千禧年观**：19 世纪英国弟兄会的达秘提出，把圣经历史分成几个“时代”，严格区分以色列和教会。认为基督在七年大灾难之前先秘密地把教会提到空中（“被提”），大灾难之后再公开降临，在耶路撒冷作王一千年，那时神对以色列民族的应许要在地上实现。1909 年出版的《司可福串珠圣经》使这种看法在美国福音派里广泛流行。它在华人教会里的影响也很大。<br>
  **Dispensational premillennialism**: Proposed in the 19th century by J. N. Darby of the Brethren in Britain, it divides biblical history into several “dispensations” and sharply distinguishes Israel and the church. It holds that before a seven-year tribulation Christ will secretly take the church up into the air (the “rapture”), and after the tribulation he will come publicly and reign for a thousand years from Jerusalem, when God's promises to the nation of Israel will be fulfilled on earth. The Scofield Reference Bible of 1909 spread this view widely among American evangelicals. It has also been very influential in Chinese churches.
- **后千禧年观**：福音会大大兴旺，世界在很长的时期里普遍归向基督，然后基督才再来。17、18 世纪的清教徒和爱德华兹持这种看法，19 世纪很多宣教士也受它鼓舞。两次世界大战以后，持这种看法的人少了很多。<br>
  **Postmillennialism**: The gospel will advance greatly and the world will broadly turn to Christ over a long period, after which Christ returns. The Puritans of the 17th and 18th centuries and Jonathan Edwards held this view, and many 19th-century missionaries were inspired by it. After the two world wars far fewer people held it.

## “被提”是什么时候 | When is the “rapture”?

保罗说，主从天降临的时候，在基督里死了的人先复活，“以后我们这活着还存留的人必和他们一同被提到云里，在空中与主相遇”（帖前 4:17）。各传统都接受这节经文，只是理解不同。<br>
Paul says that when the Lord descends from heaven, the dead in Christ will rise first, “then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air” (1 Thessalonians 4:17). Every tradition accepts this verse. They understand it differently.

- **大灾难前被提**：时代论的看法，教会在大灾难之前被提，不经过大灾难。<br>
  **Pre-tribulation**: The dispensational view: the church is taken up before the tribulation and does not go through it.
- **大灾难后被提**：历史前千禧年观的看法，教会经过大灾难，在基督公开降临时被提迎见他。<br>
  **Post-tribulation**: The historic premillennial view: the church goes through the tribulation and is caught up to meet Christ at his public return.
- **被提就是再来的一部分**：无千禧年观和后千禧年观认为，被提和基督再来是同一件公开的事，信徒“在空中与主相遇”，就像城里的人出城迎接来访的君王，然后一同回来。<br>
  **The rapture as part of the return**: Amillennialists and postmillennialists hold that the rapture and Christ's return are one public event. Believers “meet the Lord in the air,” as a city's people would go out to meet a visiting king and come back with him.

## 这些分歧有多重要 | How much these differences matter

这些看法在教会里长期并存，很多宗派的信仰告白不把某一种千禧年观作为信徒必须接受的。各方都同意：基督必再来；信徒要警醒、忠心地生活；最后的结局是神“要擦去他们一切的眼泪”。圣经最后的祷告是：“主耶稣啊，我愿你来！”（启 22:20）<br>
These views have long coexisted in the church, and many denominations' statements of faith do not require believers to accept any one view of the millennium. All agree that Christ will certainly return, that believers are to live watchful and faithful lives, and that in the end God “will wipe away every tear from their eyes.” The Bible's last prayer is, “Yes, come, Lord Jesus” (Revelation 22:20).
