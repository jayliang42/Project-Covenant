## 圣经里的马利亚 | Mary in the Bible

天使对马利亚说：“蒙大恩的女子，我问你安，主和你同在了！”（路 1:28）她回答：“我是主的使女，情愿照你的话成就在我身上”（路 1:38）。在她的颂歌里，她说：“我灵以神我的救主为乐……从今以后，万代要称我有福”（路 1:47-48）。<br>
The angel said to Mary, “Rejoice, you highly favored one! The Lord is with you” (Luke 1:28). She answered, “Behold, the servant of the Lord; let it be done to me according to your word” (Luke 1:38). In her song she said, “My spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior... from now on, all generations will call me blessed” (Luke 1:47-48).

在迦拿的婚宴上，她对用人说：“他告诉你们什么，你们就做什么”（约 2:5）。耶稣钉十字架的时候，她站在十字架旁边，耶稣把她交给所爱的门徒照顾（约 19:26-27）。耶稣升天以后，她和门徒一同恒切祷告（徒 1:14）。<br>
At the wedding in Cana she told the servants, “Whatever he says to you, do it” (John 2:5). When Jesus was crucified she stood by the cross, and Jesus entrusted her to the disciple he loved (John 19:26-27). After Jesus ascended, she prayed with the disciples (Acts 1:14).

各传统都同意：马利亚以童女的身份怀了耶稣；她是信心和顺服的榜样；她应当被称为有福。<br>
All traditions agree that Mary conceived Jesus as a virgin, that she is an example of faith and obedience, and that she is to be called blessed.

## “神之母” | “Mother of God”

5 世纪初，君士坦丁堡主教聂斯脱利反对称马利亚为 Theotokos（希腊文，“生神者”，中文常译为“天主之母”或“神之母”），认为这个称号损害了基督完全的人性。主后 431 年的以弗所会议定他的看法为异端。<br>
In the early fifth century Nestorius, bishop of Constantinople, objected to calling Mary Theotokos (Greek, “God-bearer,” often rendered “Mother of God”), believing the title undermined Christ's full humanity. The Council of Ephesus in 431 condemned his view as heresy.

这个称号首先是关于耶稣的：马利亚所生的那一位，就是神的儿子。451 年迦克墩会议的定义也用了这个称号，说基督按人性是由童贞女马利亚所生。天主教、东正教都用这个称号。很多新教神学家也接受它的原意，因为它是关于基督的说法；也有很多福音派信徒不常用这个说法，担心被误解为马利亚在神以上。<br>
The title is first of all about Jesus: the one Mary bore is the Son of God. The Definition of the Council of Chalcedon in 451 also uses the title, saying that according to his humanity Christ was born of the Virgin Mary. Catholics and Orthodox use the title. Many Protestant theologians also accept its original meaning, since it is a statement about Christ. Many evangelicals seldom use the phrase, concerned that it may be misunderstood as placing Mary above God.

## 天主教的两条信理 | Two Catholic dogmas

- **无原罪始胎**：1854 年 12 月 8 日，教宗庇护九世宣布，马利亚从受孕的那一刻起就免于原罪。这里说的是马利亚自己的受孕，不是耶稣的童女怀孕。<br>
  **The Immaculate Conception**: On 8 December 1854 Pope Pius IX defined that Mary was conceived free from original sin. This concerns Mary's own conception, not the virginal conception of Jesus.
- **圣母升天**：1950 年 11 月 1 日，教宗庇护十二世发表宗座宪令《荣福童贞玛利亚升天》（Munificentissimus Deus），宣布马利亚在尘世生命结束时，灵魂和肉身一同被提升到天上的荣耀里。<br>
  **The Assumption**: On 1 November 1950 Pope Pius XII issued the apostolic constitution Munificentissimus Deus, defining that at the end of her earthly life Mary was taken up body and soul into heavenly glory.

## 东正教 | Orthodox

东正教称马利亚为 Theotokos，十分尊崇她，在礼仪中常常向她祈求。东正教在 8 月 15 日庆祝“圣母安息”，相信马利亚像所有人一样死了，然后被她的儿子复活，灵魂和身体进入天上。但东正教不把天主教 1950 年的定义当作信理。<br>
The Orthodox Church calls Mary Theotokos, honours her highly, and often asks her intercession in its liturgy. On 15 August it celebrates the Dormition (“falling asleep”) of the Theotokos, holding that Mary died as all people do and was then raised by her Son and taken into heaven in body and soul. But the Orthodox Church does not treat the 1950 Catholic definition as dogma.

东正教多数人不接受无原罪始胎的信理。原因和它对原罪的理解有关：东正教不认为人继承了亚当的罪责（参“人是什么”一课），所以不需要在马利亚受孕时特别除去罪责。东正教传统认为马利亚一生没有犯过个人的罪。<br>
Most Orthodox reject the dogma of the Immaculate Conception. The reason lies in their understanding of original sin: Orthodoxy does not hold that people inherit Adam's guilt (see the lesson on what a human being is), so there is no guilt to be removed at Mary's conception. Orthodox tradition holds that Mary remained free of personal sin.

## 新教 | Protestant

宗教改革家尊敬马利亚。按浸信会学者乔治（Timothy George）的介绍，路德、慈运理和加尔文都看重马利亚，把她看作神恩典的器皿和“因信称义”的榜样；路德保留了几个和马利亚有关的节日。但改教家明确反对向马利亚祈求，也反对把她放在基督的位置上的民间敬拜。<br>
The Reformers honoured Mary. According to the Baptist scholar Timothy George, Luther, Zwingli, and Calvin each valued Mary as a vessel of God's grace and an example of justification by faith, and Luther kept several Marian feast days. But the Reformers clearly rejected prayer to Mary and popular devotion that put her in Christ's place.

今天的新教徒一般不接受无原罪始胎和圣母升天这两条信理，因为圣经没有这样说；也不向马利亚祈求，认为“在神和人中间，只有一位中保，乃是降世为人的基督耶稣”（提前 2:5）。乔治也指出，很多新教徒后来几乎不再谈马利亚，忽略了改教家对她的尊敬。<br>
Protestants today generally do not accept the dogmas of the Immaculate Conception and the Assumption, since Scripture does not teach them, and they do not pray to Mary, holding that “there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus” (1 Timothy 2:5). George also notes that many Protestants later almost stopped speaking of Mary, neglecting the honour the Reformers gave her.

关于马利亚是否终身为童贞女，“对圣经好奇”路径里“耶稣的降生”一课介绍过各传统的看法。<br>
On whether Mary remained a virgin all her life, the lesson on the birth of Jesus in the Curious path introduces the traditions' views.
