## 起初 | In the beginning

创世记第 1 章说，神用六日创造天地万物，然后“神看着一切所造的都甚好”（创 1:31）。人照着神的形象被造，被托付管理这个世界。伊甸园里有河流、生命树，神和人同在。<br>
Genesis 1 says God made heaven and earth and all that is in them, and then “God saw everything that he had made, and, behold, it was very good” (Genesis 1:31). Humans were made in God's image and entrusted with caring for the world. In Eden there was a river and the tree of life, and God was present with humanity.

人犯罪以后，地受了咒诅，人被赶出伊甸园，死亡进入世界。但神没有放弃他所造的世界。挪亚洪水以后，神和“一切活物”立约（创 9:10）。<br>
After humans sinned, the ground was cursed, they were driven out of Eden, and death entered the world. But God did not abandon what he had made. After the flood, God made a covenant with “every living creature” on the earth (Genesis 9:10).

## 先知的盼望 | The prophets' hope

以赛亚说：“看哪！我造新天新地；从前的事不再被记念，也不再追想”（赛 65:17）。他描写一个豺狼和羊羔同食、不再有哭泣的世界（赛 65 章）。<br>
Isaiah says, “behold, I create new heavens and a new earth; and the former things will not be remembered, nor come into mind” (Isaiah 65:17). He pictures a world where the wolf and the lamb feed together and weeping is heard no more (Isaiah 65).

## 在基督里已经开始 | Begun in Christ

新约说，新创造已经在耶稣身上开始。他的复活是新世界的“初熟的果子”。保罗说：“若有人在基督里，他就是新造的人，旧事已过，都变成新的了”（林后 5:17）。神借着基督“叫万有，无论是地上的、天上的，都与自己和好了”（西 1:20）。<br>
The New Testament says the new creation has already begun in Jesus. His resurrection is the “first fruits” of the new world. Paul says, “if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away. Behold, all things have become new” (2 Corinthians 5:17). Through Christ God reconciles “all things to himself... whether things on the earth or things in the heavens” (Colossians 1:20).

受造之物也在等候：“受造之物切望等候神的众子显出来……受造之物仍然指望脱离败坏的辖制，得享神儿女自由的荣耀”（罗 8:19-21）。<br>
Creation itself is waiting: “the creation waits with eager expectation for the children of God to be revealed... the creation itself also will be delivered from the bondage of decay into the liberty of the glory of the children of God” (Romans 8:19-21).

## 新天新地 | A new heaven and a new earth

启示录的最后两章描写新天新地和新耶路撒冷：“看哪，神的帐幕在人间。他要与人同住”（启 21:3）。城里有“一道生命水的河”，河两边有生命树，“树上的叶子乃为医治万民”（启 22:1-2）。伊甸园的河流和生命树又出现了，但这一次是一座城，住满了从各国来的人。<br>
The last two chapters of Revelation describe a new heaven and earth and the New Jerusalem: “Behold, God's dwelling is with people, and he will dwell with them” (Revelation 21:3). In the city is “a river of water of life,” with the tree of life on either side, and “the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations” (Revelation 22:1-2). Eden's river and tree of life return, but now in a city filled with people from every nation.

## 两种理解 | Two understandings

这个世界最后会怎样？基督徒有两种主要的理解：<br>
What will finally become of this world? Christians hold two main understandings:

- **毁灭后重新创造**：神会毁灭现在的天地，另外创造一个全新的。常引用彼得后书 3:10：“地和其上的物都要烧尽了。”<br>
  **Destruction and fresh creation**: God will destroy the present heavens and earth and create entirely new ones. Often cited is 2 Peter 3:10: “the earth and the works that are in it will be burned up.”
- **更新和炼净**：神会审判、炼净现在的世界，使它得到更新，就像人的身体会复活得荣耀一样。常引用罗马书 8 章。<br>
  **Renewal and purification**: God will judge and purify the present world and renew it, as the human body will be raised in glory. Romans 8 is often cited.

彼得后书 3:10 有一处抄本差异和这个讨论有关。和合本和英王钦定本照较晚的抄本，作“都要烧尽了”；较早的西乃抄本和梵蒂冈抄本作“要被显露”（直译“要被发现”），很多现代的经文鉴别本采用这个读法。学者沃尔特斯（Al Wolters）在 1987 年的文章中认为，这里描写的是像炼金属一样的过程，世界经过火的审判以后被炼净。<br>
A textual variant in 2 Peter 3:10 bears on this discussion. The Chinese Union Version and the King James Version follow later manuscripts that read “will be burned up”; the earlier Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Vaticanus read “will be found” (that is, exposed), the reading adopted by many modern critical editions. In a 1987 article, the scholar Al Wolters argued that the passage describes something like the smelting of metal, with the world purified after passing through the fire of judgment.

不管持哪一种理解，各传统都同意：神看他所造的为“甚好”，他要亲自和他的子民同住，并且“将一切都更新了”（启 21:5）。<br>
Whichever view one takes, the traditions agree that God called his creation “very good,” that he will dwell with his people himself, and that he is “making all things new” (Revelation 21:5).
