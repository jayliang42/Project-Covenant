## 只剩下正确的教义吗 | Only correct doctrine?

宗教改革以后，信义宗和改革宗教会写下了详细的信条，神学家花很多精力辩论教义。到 17 世纪后期，德国的一些信徒觉得，很多人只是名义上的基督徒：神职人员腐败，平信徒满足于现状，教会事务被地方政权控制。<br>
After the Reformation, Lutheran and Reformed churches wrote detailed confessions, and theologians spent great energy debating doctrine. By the late seventeenth century some believers in Germany felt that many people were Christians in name only: clergy were corrupt, laypeople complacent, and church affairs controlled by civil rulers.

## 施本尔和敬虔运动 | Spener and Pietism

1675 年，信义宗牧师施本尔（Philipp Jakob Spener）出版《敬虔的愿望》（Pia Desideria），提出六项改革：<br>
In 1675 the Lutheran pastor Philipp Jakob Spener published Pia Desideria (“Pious Desires”), proposing six reforms:

1. 通过公开和私下读经、小组查经和家庭灵修，让人更多认识整本圣经。<br>
   Spread knowledge of the whole Bible through public and private reading, group study, and family devotions.
2. 恢复路德“信徒皆祭司”的教导，信徒要彼此负责。<br>
   Revive Luther's teaching on the priesthood of all believers, with believers responsible for one another.
3. 强调信仰就是遵行神的旨意，特别是爱邻舍。<br>
   Stress that faith means doing God's will, especially loving one's neighbour.
4. 用祷告和爱心处理教义上的争论。<br>
   Handle doctrinal disputes with prayer and love.
5. 改革神学教育，使未来的牧师有属灵的生命。<br>
   Reform theological education so that future pastors are spiritually committed.
6. 讲道要造就人的内在生命。<br>
   Aim preaching at building up the inner life.

早在 1670 年，施本尔就在法兰克福的家中开办查经小组，每周日和周三聚会，参加的人后来被称为“敬虔派”。这些小组有时变得排外，自成“教会中的小教会”。施本尔反对离开教会的敬拜和圣礼。<br>
From 1670 Spener had held Bible study groups in his home in Frankfurt, meeting on Sundays and Wednesdays; those who took part came to be called Pietists. The groups sometimes became exclusive, forming “little churches within the church.” Spener opposed separating from the church's worship and sacraments.

他的同工法兰克（August Hermann Francke）从 1698 年起在哈勒大学任教，在那里建立了孤儿院和学校、出版社、圣经机构，以及为宣教士学语言的学院。法兰克认为信仰的更新和社会的改善是连在一起的。<br>
His colleague August Hermann Francke taught at the University of Halle from 1698. There he built an orphanage with schools, a publishing house, a Bible institute, and a college for training missionaries in languages. Francke held that religious renewal and social reform belong together.

## 摩拉维亚弟兄会 | The Moravians

施本尔的教子亲岑多夫伯爵（Zinzendorf）在 1722 年接纳了一批从摩拉维亚逃来的信徒，在他的庄园上建立了赫恩胡特（Herrnhut）。1732 年 12 月，赫恩胡特差派的杜伯（Leonard Dober）和尼区曼（David Nitschmann）到达西印度群岛的圣托马斯岛，向那里的奴隶传福音。杜伯原是陶匠，他照顾患热病的人，自己也几乎病死。<br>
Count Zinzendorf, Spener's godson, received a group of refugees from Moravia in 1722 and organised the settlement of Herrnhut on his estate. In December 1732 Leonard Dober and David Nitschmann, sent from Herrnhut, arrived on St. Thomas in the West Indies to preach the gospel to enslaved people. Dober, a potter by trade, cared for those sick with fever and nearly died of fever himself.

## 卫斯理、怀特腓和大觉醒 | Wesley, Whitefield, and the Great Awakening

1738 年 5 月 24 日，英国圣公会牧师约翰·卫斯理（John Wesley）在伦敦奥德斯门街的聚会上，听人读路德的罗马书序言，“心里异样地温暖”。他和摩拉维亚弟兄会有密切的来往，还去过赫恩胡特。他和弟弟查理·卫斯理、怀特腓（George Whitefield）一同带出了英国的福音复兴，卫斯理后来被称为循道宗（卫理公会）的创始人。<br>
On 24 May 1738 John Wesley, an Anglican clergyman, heard Luther's preface to Romans read at a meeting in Aldersgate Street, London, and felt his heart “strangely warmed.” He had close ties with the Moravians and visited Herrnhut. With his brother Charles Wesley and George Whitefield he led the evangelical revival in Britain, and he came to be regarded as the founder of Methodism.

在北美的英国殖民地，大约从 1720 年代到 1740 年代发生了“大觉醒”。麻省北安普敦的公理会牧师爱德华兹（Jonathan Edwards）是这场运动最重要的学者和辩护者。1739–1740 年，怀特腓在各殖民地巡回，在露天对大批群众讲道。<br>
In the British North American colonies, the Great Awakening took place roughly from the 1720s to the 1740s. Jonathan Edwards, a Congregational pastor at Northampton, Massachusetts, was its leading scholar and defender. In 1739–1740 George Whitefield toured the colonies, preaching to large crowds in open fields.

大觉醒推动了普林斯顿、布朗、罗格斯等大学和达特茅斯学院的建立。它也引起了分裂：有人支持复兴，有人批评它太重情感，很多宗派因此分成两派。<br>
The Great Awakening spurred the founding of Princeton, Brown, and Rutgers universities and Dartmouth College. It also caused divisions: some supported the revival, others criticised it as too emotional, and many denominations split as a result.

## 留下了什么 | What it left behind

今天福音派教会的很多做法都可以追溯到这段历史：强调“必须重生”（约 3:7）和个人的信心；小组查经和彼此关怀；平信徒参与事奉；向远方宣教；把信仰和社会服务连在一起。<br>
Many practices of evangelical churches today can be traced to this history: stressing that “you must be born anew” (John 3:7) and personal faith; small-group Bible study and mutual care; lay involvement in ministry; mission to distant places; and linking faith with social service.

这段历史也留下了提醒。批评者认为，有时人们太看重个人的感受，轻看了教会的教导和圣礼；有时小组变得排外，造成分裂。施本尔自己就劝信徒不要离开教会。<br>
This history also leaves cautions. Critics argue that at times people valued personal feeling too highly and neglected the church's teaching and sacraments, and that at times small groups became exclusive and caused division. Spener himself urged believers not to leave the church.
