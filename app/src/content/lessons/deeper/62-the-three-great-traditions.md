## 三大传统 | Three great traditions

皮尤研究中心 2011 年的报告说，全世界约 21.8 亿基督徒中，天主教约占一半，广义的新教约占 37%，东正教约占 12%，其他群体约占 1%。此外还有不接受 451 年迦克墩会议的东方正统教会（如科普特、亚美尼亚、埃塞俄比亚教会），以及东方亚述教会。<br>
A 2011 Pew Research Center report says that of about 2.18 billion Christians worldwide, about half are Catholic, about 37% Protestant (broadly defined), about 12% Orthodox, and about 1% other. There are also the Oriental Orthodox churches (such as the Coptic, Armenian, and Ethiopian churches), which did not accept the Council of Chalcedon in 451, and the Assyrian Church of the East.

## 怎样分开的 | How they separated

- **1054 年东西分裂**：拉丁语的西方教会和希腊语的东方教会，因为教宗的权柄、信经里的“和子”句等问题分裂，成为天主教和东正教。“东方和西方”一课讲过这段历史。<br>
  **The East-West schism of 1054**: The Latin-speaking West and the Greek-speaking East divided over the pope's authority, the filioque clause in the creed, and other issues, becoming the Catholic and Orthodox churches. The lesson on East and West covers this history.
- **16 世纪的宗教改革**：路德、慈运理、加尔文等人在西方教会内部呼吁改革，最后形成信义宗、改革宗、圣公会、重洗派等新教传统；后来又有浸信会、循道宗、五旬节派等。“宗教改革”一课讲过这段历史。<br>
  **The Reformation of the sixteenth century**: Luther, Zwingli, Calvin, and others called for reform within the Western church, which led to the Protestant traditions: Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Anabaptist, and later Baptist, Methodist, Pentecostal, and others. The lesson on the Reformation covers this.

## 共同相信的 | What they share

三大传统都接受早期大公会议确定的信仰：一位神，圣父、圣子、圣灵三个位格；耶稣基督是完全的神，也是完全的人；他为我们死，第三天复活，将来要再来审判活人死人。它们都用尼西亚信经（虽然东西方对“和子”句有分歧），都承认圣经是神的话，都施行洗礼和圣餐。保罗说：“一主，一信，一洗，一神，就是众人的父”（弗 4:5-6）。<br>
All three accept the faith defined by the early ecumenical councils: one God in three persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; Jesus Christ fully God and fully human, who died for us, rose on the third day, and will come again to judge the living and the dead. All use the Nicene Creed (though East and West differ over the filioque), all confess Scripture as God's word, and all practise baptism and the Lord's Supper. Paul writes of “one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all” (Ephesians 4:5-6).

## 主要的分别 | Main differences

- **权威**：天主教认为圣经和圣传是同一个神圣启示的来源，由教会的训导权解释；东正教看重圣经和圣传，特别是七次大公会议和教父；新教强调“唯独圣经”是最高的权威，信条和传统在圣经之下。见“圣经的默示、权威和正典”一课。<br>
  **Authority**: Catholics hold Scripture and Tradition as one source of divine revelation, interpreted by the Church's teaching office; the Orthodox honour Scripture and Tradition, especially the seven ecumenical councils and the Fathers; Protestants stress “Scripture alone” as the highest authority, with creeds and tradition under it. See the lesson on inspiration, authority, and canon.
- **教会和教宗**：天主教承认罗马主教（教宗）对全教会有管辖权；东正教由各自治教会组成，承认君士坦丁堡普世牧首有荣誉上的首位，但不是管辖权；新教各宗派的治理方式不同。见“教会怎样治理”一课。<br>
  **Church and pope**: Catholics recognise the bishop of Rome (the pope) as having jurisdiction over the whole church; the Orthodox Church consists of self-governing churches and gives the Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople a primacy of honour, not jurisdiction; Protestant denominations govern themselves in various ways. See the lesson on church government.
- **圣礼**：天主教和东正教有七件圣事（东正教常称“奥秘”）；多数新教只承认洗礼和圣餐两项，对它们的意义理解也不同。见“洗礼和圣餐”一课。<br>
  **Sacraments**: Catholics and Orthodox have seven sacraments (the Orthodox often call them “mysteries”); most Protestants recognise only baptism and the Lord's Supper, and understand them in various ways. See the lesson on baptism and the Lord's Supper.
- **得救**：各传统都说得救是出于神的恩典。新教强调“因信称义”是神的宣告；天主教和东正教更强调恩典在人里面的更新和人的合作。见“称义和成圣”一课。<br>
  **Salvation**: All traditions say salvation is by God's grace. Protestants stress justification by faith as God's declaration; Catholics and Orthodox put more weight on grace renewing a person within and on human cooperation. See the lesson on justification and sanctification.
- **马利亚和圣徒**：天主教和东正教请马利亚和圣徒代求，东正教特别重视圣像；新教一般不这样做。见“马利亚”和“圣徒、圣像和代求”两课。<br>
  **Mary and the saints**: Catholics and Orthodox ask Mary and the saints to intercede, and the Orthodox give icons a central place; Protestants generally do not. See the lessons on Mary and on saints, icons, and intercession.
- **神职人员**：天主教拉丁礼的神父通常从独身的男子中选立；东方的传统（包括东正教和东方礼天主教会）可以按立已婚男子为执事和神父，主教则从独身者中选立；新教的牧师可以结婚。按立女性的问题，见“女性在教会中的服事”一课。<br>
  **Clergy**: Priests of the Latin Catholic Church are normally chosen from celibate men; Eastern traditions (including the Orthodox and the Eastern Catholic churches) may ordain married men as deacons and priests, while bishops are chosen from celibates; Protestant ministers may marry. On ordaining women, see the lesson on women in ministry.

## 怎样读这个单元 | How to read this unit

这个单元的每一课，都尽量按各传统自己的文献来介绍它们的立场。目的是帮助读者了解，不是替读者做决定。耶稣祷告说：“使他们都合而为一……叫世人可以信你差了我来”（约 17:21）。保罗也提醒：“你是谁，竟论断别人的仆人呢？”（罗 14:4）在坚持自己确信的同时，可以尊重和自己看法不同的弟兄姊妹。<br>
Each lesson in this unit tries to present each tradition's position from its own documents. The aim is to help readers understand, leaving the decisions to them. Jesus prayed, “that they may all be one... that the world may believe that you sent me” (John 17:21). Paul also warns, “Who are you who judge another's servant?” (Romans 14:4). One can hold one's own convictions and still respect brothers and sisters who see things differently.
