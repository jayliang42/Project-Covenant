## 洗礼：三个问题 | Baptism: three questions

**给谁施洗**？<br>
**Whom to baptize?**

- **婴孩和信的成人都可以**：天主教、东正教、信义宗、圣公会、改革宗和长老会、卫理公会等。理由包括：彼得说“这应许是给你们和你们的儿女”（徒 2:39）；新约几次记载“全家”受洗（徒 16:33）；旧约里婴孩出生第八天就受割礼，进入神的约。<br>
  **Infants as well as believing adults**: Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Anglican, Reformed and Presbyterian, Methodist, and others. Their reasons include Peter's words, “the promise is to you, and to your children” (Acts 2:39), the New Testament accounts of whole households being baptized (Acts 16:33), and the Old Testament practice of circumcising infants on the eighth day as a sign of the covenant.
- **只给自己认信的人施洗**：浸信会、五旬节派、许多独立教会和华人福音派教会。理由是：新约里受洗的人都是先听道、悔改、相信，然后受洗；洗礼是信的人公开承认基督的行动。这些教会常常为婴孩举行“奉献礼”，为孩子祝福、为父母祷告。<br>
  **Only those who confess faith themselves**: Baptist, Pentecostal, many independent churches, and Chinese evangelical churches. Their reason is that in the New Testament people heard the word, repented, believed, and then were baptized, so baptism is a believer's public confession of Christ. These churches often hold a dedication service for infants, blessing the child and praying for the parents.

**怎样施洗**？<br>
**How to baptize?**

- 东正教把受洗的人三次浸入水中，婴孩也是一样。<br>
  The Orthodox immerse the person three times, infants included.
- 天主教承认浸入和倒水两种方式都有效，常用倒水。<br>
  The Catholic Church recognises both immersion and pouring as valid, and pouring is common.
- 信义宗、圣公会、长老会、卫理公会多用洒水或倒水。<br>
  Lutheran, Anglican, Presbyterian, and Methodist churches mostly sprinkle or pour.
- 浸信会和很多福音派教会坚持全身浸入水中，认为这最能表明“和他一同埋葬……一同复活”（罗 6:4；西 2:12）。<br>
  Baptists and many evangelical churches insist on full immersion, holding that it best shows being “buried with him... raised with him” (Romans 6:4; Colossians 2:12).

**洗礼成就了什么**？<br>
**What does baptism accomplish?**

- **天主教和东正教**：洗礼是圣事，神借着它洗净原罪和本罪，赐下新生命，使人成为教会的一员。<br>
  **Catholic and Orthodox**: Baptism is a sacrament through which God washes away original and actual sin, gives new life, and makes a person a member of the church.
- **信义宗**：神借着洗礼真实地赐下赦罪和信心的应许，所以路德在受试探时常说：“我已经受了洗。”<br>
  **Lutheran**: Through baptism God really gives the promise of forgiveness and faith, which is why Luther, when tempted, would say, “I am baptized.”
- **改革宗和长老会**：洗礼是恩典之约的记号和印证，神借着它坚固信心，但恩典不是自动随着水而来的。<br>
  **Reformed and Presbyterian**: Baptism is a sign and seal of the covenant of grace. God uses it to strengthen faith, though grace does not come automatically with the water.
- **浸信会和多数福音派**：洗礼是顺服和见证，表明人已经因信得救。<br>
  **Baptist and most evangelicals**: Baptism is an act of obedience and testimony, showing that a person has already been saved by faith.

## 圣餐：基督怎样同在 | The Supper: how Christ is present

耶稣拿起饼说“这是我的身体”，拿起杯说“这杯是用我的血所立的新约”（林前 11:24-25）。各传统都认真对待这些话，对它们的理解却很不同。<br>
Jesus took bread and said, “This is my body,” and took the cup and said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood” (1 Corinthians 11:24-25). Every tradition takes these words seriously, and they understand them very differently.

- **天主教：变质说**。祝圣以后，饼和酒的本质变成基督的身体和血，外形和味道不变。《天主教教理》说，弥撒使基督在十字架上一次献上的祭“重现”出来，是同一个祭，并不是另一次献祭。<br>
  **Catholic: transubstantiation.** At the consecration the substance of the bread and wine becomes the body and blood of Christ, while appearance and taste remain. The Catechism teaches that the Mass makes present the one sacrifice Christ offered once on the cross. It is the same sacrifice and not a further one.
- **东正教：真实临在，不加解释**。饼和酒在圣灵的祝圣下成为基督真实的身体和血。美国东正教会说，这是一个奥秘，“无法用纯粹理性和逻辑的方式分析和解释”，所以不像天主教那样用哲学的概念来说明怎样变化。<br>
  **Orthodox: real presence, without explanation.** By the Holy Spirit's consecration the bread and wine become Christ's true body and blood. The Orthodox Church in America says this mystery “defies analysis and explanation in purely rational and logical terms,” so the Orthodox do not use philosophical categories, as Catholics do, to explain how the change happens.
- **信义宗：圣礼的联合**。路德按字面领受“这是我的身体”，认为基督的身体和血真实地“在、同、下”饼和酒同在，但饼和酒的本质并没有改变。<br>
  **Lutheran: sacramental union.** Luther took “This is my body” literally and held that Christ's body and blood are really present “in, with, and under” the bread and wine, while the bread and wine do not change their substance.
- **改革宗：属灵的临在**。加尔文认为，基督的身体在天上，并不在饼和酒里；但信的人领圣餐时，圣灵使他们真实地与基督相交，得着他的喂养。<br>
  **Reformed: spiritual presence.** Calvin held that Christ's body is in heaven and is not contained in the bread and wine. When believers receive the Supper, he taught, the Spirit unites them with Christ in a real way and they are fed by him.
- **慈运理和许多福音派：记念**。饼和杯是记号，圣餐的重点是遵照主的吩咐“为的是记念我”，并且彼此相交、宣告主的死。很多浸信会和独立教会持这种看法，他们也相信基督借着圣灵与聚会的信徒同在。<br>
  **Zwingli and many evangelicals: memorial.** The bread and cup are signs, and the heart of the Supper is obeying the Lord's command, “Do this in memory of me,” sharing fellowship, and proclaiming the Lord's death. Many Baptist and independent churches hold this view, and they also believe Christ is present by the Spirit with the gathered believers.

1529 年，路德和慈运理在德国的马尔堡会面，想要统一新教的立场。他们在十四条上大体一致，唯独在圣餐上没有达成共识，最后没有一起领圣餐。<br>
In 1529 Luther and Zwingli met at Marburg in Germany to unite the Protestant position. They broadly agreed on fourteen articles and could not agree on the Supper, and in the end they did not share it together.

## 谁可以领 | Who may receive

- **天主教和东正教**：一般只让本教会的成员领圣体，因为他们认为一起领圣餐表明已经在信仰上完全合一。<br>
  **Catholic and Orthodox**: As a rule only members of their own church receive, because they hold that sharing communion expresses full unity in the faith.
- **一些信义宗教会**（例如密苏里路德会）：只让和本宗派信仰一致的人领。<br>
  **Some Lutheran churches** (for example the Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod): Only those who share their confession receive.
- **多数新教教会**：凡受过洗、信靠基督的人都可以领。很多教会在领圣餐前提醒信徒先省察自己（林前 11:28）。<br>
  **Most Protestant churches**: All who are baptized and trust in Christ may receive. Many remind believers beforehand to examine themselves (1 Corinthians 11:28).

如果你去另一个传统的教会，可以先问清楚它的做法，尊重它的规矩。<br>
If you visit a church of another tradition, ask first about its practice and respect its rules.
