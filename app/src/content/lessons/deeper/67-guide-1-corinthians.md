## 背景 | Background

哥林多是罗马帝国希腊地区的大商业城市。保罗在第二次旅行时建立了这间教会，信徒多数是外邦人。大英百科说，这封信大约在主后 53 或 54 年从以弗所写成。保罗从“革来氏家里的人”那里听说教会分党纷争（林前 1:11），哥林多人也写信问他一些问题（林前 7:1）。这是新约里少数几封作者没有争议的书信之一。<br>
Corinth was a major commercial city in Roman Greece. Paul founded the church there on his second journey, and most believers were Gentiles. Britannica says the letter was written from Ephesus around AD 53 or 54. Paul had heard from “those who are from Chloe's household” that the church was divided (1 Corinthians 1:11), and the Corinthians had written to ask him questions (1 Corinthians 7:1). Its authorship by Paul is not disputed.

## 结构：一个一个问题 | Structure: one problem at a time

- **1–4 章：分党**。信徒说“我是属保罗的”“我是属亚波罗的”。保罗把他们带回十字架：“十字架的道理，在那灭亡的人为愚拙；在我们得救的人，却为神的大能”（林前 1:18）。<br>
  **Chapters 1–4: factions.** Believers said, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos.” Paul brings them back to the cross: “the word of the cross is foolishness to those who are dying, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God” (1 Corinthians 1:18).
- **5–6 章：淫乱和告状**。教会容忍严重的淫乱，信徒又把彼此的纠纷带到外邦人的法庭。<br>
  **Chapters 5–6: immorality and lawsuits.** The church tolerated serious sexual sin, and believers took their disputes to pagan courts.
- **7 章：婚姻和单身**。回答他们信上的问题。<br>
  **Chapter 7: marriage and singleness.** Answering the questions in their letter.
- **8–10 章：祭偶像之物**。“知识是叫人自高自大，惟有爱心能造就人”（林前 8:1）；“只是你们要谨慎，恐怕你们这自由竟成了那软弱人的绊脚石”（林前 8:9）。<br>
  **Chapters 8–10: food offered to idols.** “Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up” (1 Corinthians 8:1); “be careful that by no means does this liberty of yours become a stumbling block to the weak” (1 Corinthians 8:9).
- **11–14 章：聚会**。蒙头的问题；圣餐时富人和穷人分开吃，保罗重述主设立圣餐的话（林前 11:23-26）；属灵恩赐和身体的比喻；第 13 章爱的诗；聚会要有次序。<br>
  **Chapters 11–14: worship.** Head coverings; at the Lord's Supper rich and poor ate separately, and Paul repeats the words of institution (1 Corinthians 11:23-26); spiritual gifts and the picture of the body; the poem on love in chapter 13; order in worship.
- **15 章：复活**。有人说没有死人复活的事。保罗说：“基督若没有复活，你们的信便是徒然，你们仍在罪里”（林前 15:17）。<br>
  **Chapter 15: resurrection.** Some said there is no resurrection of the dead. Paul answers, “If Christ has not been raised, your faith is vain; you are still in your sins” (1 Corinthians 15:17).
- **16 章：为耶路撒冷的信徒捐款，问安**。<br>
  **Chapter 16: the collection for Jerusalem, and greetings.**

## 最早的福音摘要之一 | One of the earliest summaries of the gospel

保罗说：“我当日所领受又传给你们的：第一，就是基督照圣经所说，为我们的罪死了，而且埋葬了；又照圣经所说，第三天复活了，并且显给矶法看，然后显给十二使徒看”（林前 15:3-5）。“领受”和“传给”是当时传递教导的用语。这封信写于主后 50 年代中期，可见这段内容在更早的时候已经在教会里传开。“耶稣真的复活了吗”一课讨论过这段经文。<br>
Paul writes, “I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures, and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve” (1 Corinthians 15:3-5). “Received” and “delivered” were terms for passing on teaching. Since the letter dates from the mid-50s, this content was already circulating in the church earlier still. The lesson on whether Jesus really rose discusses this passage.

## 几处各方理解不同的经文 | Passages read differently

- **蒙头（11:2-16）**：有的教会认为这是当时的文化习惯，有的认为原则仍然适用。<br>
  **Head coverings (11:2-16)**: Some churches see this as a cultural custom of the time; others think the principle still applies.
- **妇女在会中“闭口不言”（14:34-35）**：见“女性在教会中的服事”一课。<br>
  **Women keeping “quiet” in the assemblies (14:34-35)**: See the lesson on women in ministry.
- **方言和预言（12–14 章）**：见“属灵恩赐今天还有吗”一课。<br>
  **Tongues and prophecy (chapters 12–14)**: See the lesson on spiritual gifts today.

## 给今天的教会 | For the church today

哥林多教会说明，一间有很多恩赐的教会，也可能有很多问题。保罗没有放弃他们。他耐心地回答，并且一再把他们带回到最重要的事上：“我曾定了主意，在你们中间不知道别的，只知道耶稣基督并他钉十字架”（林前 2:2）；“如今常存的有信，有望，有爱这三样，其中最大的是爱”（林前 13:13）。<br>
The Corinthian church shows that a church rich in gifts can also be full of problems. Paul did not give up on them. He answered patiently and kept bringing them back to what matters most: “I determined not to know anything among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified” (1 Corinthians 2:2); “now faith, hope, and love remain—these three. The greatest of these is love” (1 Corinthians 13:13).
