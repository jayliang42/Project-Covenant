## 古代书信的格式 | The form of an ancient letter

新约有 21 卷书信：保罗写的 13 封，还有希伯来书、雅各书、彼得前后书、约翰一二三书和犹大书。它们大多照着当时希腊罗马书信的格式写：<br>
The New Testament has 21 letters: 13 by Paul, plus Hebrews, James, 1 and 2 Peter, 1, 2, and 3 John, and Jude. Most follow the pattern of Greco-Roman letters of the time:

1. **开头**：写信人、收信人、问安。例如“写信给在哥林多神的教会”（林前 1:2）。<br>
   **Opening**: sender, recipients, greeting. For example, “to the assembly of God which is at Corinth” (1 Corinthians 1:2).
2. **感恩或祷告**：保罗常在这里说他为收信人感谢神（腓 1:3-5）。<br>
   **Thanksgiving or prayer**: Paul often thanks God for his readers here (Philippians 1:3–5).
3. **正文**：讲道理，回答问题，劝勉生活。<br>
   **Body**: teaching, answering questions, and urging a way of life.
4. **结尾**：个人问候、代祷请求、祝福。<br>
   **Closing**: personal greetings, prayer requests, and a blessing.

保罗有时请人代笔。《罗马书》16:22 有一句：“我这代笔写信的德提，在主里面问你们安。”<br>
Paul sometimes used a scribe. Romans 16:22 reads, “I, Tertius, who write the letter, greet you in the Lord.”

## 只听到一半的对话 | Hearing half a conversation

书信是写给特定的人、回应特定的情况。《哥林多前书》7:1 说：“论到你们信上所提的事”，后面又说“论到祭偶像之物”（林前 8:1）。保罗在一条一条回答哥林多人的问题。<br>
The letters were written to particular people about particular situations. 1 Corinthians 7:1 says, “Now concerning the things about which you wrote to me,” and later, “Now concerning things sacrificed to idols” (1 Corinthians 8:1). Paul is answering the Corinthians' questions one by one.

所以读书信时，先问这几个问题：<br>
So when you read a letter, start with these questions:

- 收信人是谁？他们住在什么样的城市？<br>
  Who were the readers, and what kind of city did they live in?
- 他们遇到了什么问题？是分裂、逼迫、错误的教导，还是道德上的问题？<br>
  What problems were they facing: division, persecution, false teaching, or moral failure?
- 作者在这一段要说明什么？这一段在整封信里起什么作用？<br>
  What is the author trying to say in this section, and what role does it play in the whole letter?

## 应用到今天 | Applying the letters today

书信里有的话是普遍的原则，有的是针对当时情况的具体做法。例如“你们要彼此接纳，如同基督接纳你们一样”（罗 15:7），适用于所有时代的教会。保罗请提摩太来的时候带上他留在特罗亚的外衣和书（提后 4:13），这是写给提摩太个人的请求。<br>
Some things in the letters are general principles, and some are specific instructions for that situation. For example, “Accept one another, even as Christ also accepted you” (Romans 15:7) applies to the church in every age. When Paul asks Timothy to bring the cloak and books he left at Troas (2 Timothy 4:13), that is a personal request to Timothy.

有些经文怎样区分，基督徒之间看法不一，例如关于蒙头、妇女在教会中的角色等。遇到这样的经文，要先弄清它在原来的处境里是什么意思，再看圣经其他地方怎样说，同时承认真诚的基督徒可能得出不同的结论。<br>
Christians disagree on how to classify some passages, such as those about head coverings or women's roles in the church. With passages like these, first work out what they meant in their original setting, then see what the rest of Scripture says, and accept that sincere Christians may reach different conclusions.

彼得也承认，保罗的信“有些难明白的”（彼后 3:16）。遇到难懂的地方，可以慢慢读，查参考书，和别人一起讨论。<br>
Peter himself admits that Paul's letters contain “some things that are hard to understand” (2 Peter 3:16). When you meet a difficult passage, read slowly, consult reference works, and talk it over with others.
