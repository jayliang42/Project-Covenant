## 修道院 | Monasteries

早在 3、4 世纪，就有基督徒到埃及的旷野独居或群居，专心祷告。在西方，影响最大的是意大利的本笃（Benedict）。他大约在 535–540 年为自己在卡西诺山（Montecassino）的修道院写了一部会规。<br>
As early as the third and fourth centuries, some Christians went into the Egyptian desert to live alone or in community and devote themselves to prayer. In the West the most influential figure was Benedict of Italy, who around 535–540 wrote a rule for his own monastery at Montecassino.

本笃会规把一天分为工作、祷告和默想，把祷告和体力劳动结合在一起。在当时，有地位的人看不起体力劳动，本笃会规却把为神做的劳动看为有尊严的事。和同时代很多会规相比，它比较温和，强调在爱和和睦中共同生活、共同工作。到 9 世纪，它已成为西方教会修道院的标准会规，后来的克吕尼改革和熙笃会改革也以它为基础。<br>
The Rule of Benedict divides the day among work, prayer, and contemplation, joining prayer with manual labour. At a time when the elite looked down on manual work, the Rule treated labour done for God as dignified. Compared with many rules of its time it was moderate, stressing life and work in common, in charity and harmony. By the ninth century it had become the standard monastic rule in the Western church, and it later formed the basis of the Cluniac and Cistercian reforms.

修道院抄写和保存了大量圣经和古代著作的抄本，也开办学校、照顾穷人和病人、接待旅客。<br>
Monasteries copied and preserved many manuscripts of Scripture and ancient works. They also ran schools, cared for the poor and sick, and welcomed travellers.

## 方济各和托钵修会 | Francis and the friars

1209 年，意大利亚西西的方济各（Francis of Assisi）为跟随他的人写了一份简单的会规，目的是“遵行我们主耶稣基督的教导，跟随他的脚踪”。按传统的说法，教宗英诺森三世在 1210 年口头批准了这个修会。方济各和他的弟兄们不拥有财产，在街头讲道。他特别看重甘心贫穷，常被联系到耶稣对少年官说的话：“去变卖你所有的，分给穷人……你还要来跟从我”（太 19:21）。<br>
In 1209 Francis of Assisi wrote a simple rule for his followers, aiming “to follow the teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ and to walk in his footsteps.” According to tradition, Pope Innocent III gave oral approval to the order in 1210. Francis and his brothers owned nothing and preached in the streets. He placed great weight on voluntary poverty, often linked with Jesus' words to the rich young man: “go, sell what you have, and give to the poor... and come, follow me” (Matthew 19:21).

同一时期还有道明会等其他托钵修会。他们不像传统修士那样住在乡间的修道院里，而是走进城市讲道和教学。<br>
In the same period other mendicant orders arose, such as the Dominicans. Unlike traditional monks in rural monasteries, they went into the cities to preach and teach.

## 大学和经院神学 | Universities and scholastic theology

12、13 世纪，欧洲出现了最早的一批大学，神学是其中重要的学科。这时期的神学方法称为“经院神学”，用严谨的逻辑讨论信仰的问题。<br>
In the twelfth and thirteenth centuries the first European universities appeared, and theology was a leading subject. The theological method of this period is called scholasticism, which discussed questions of faith with rigorous logic.

道明会修士托马斯·阿奎那（约 1225–1274）是最有代表性的人物。他在巴黎大学教书，写成《神学大全》（1265–1273）。他把亚里士多德的哲学融入基督教神学，认为理性可以在信仰之内运作：神学家以信仰为起点，哲学家只依靠理性，两者并不冲突。天主教至今非常看重他的神学；新教神学家对他的评价不一，有的大量引用，有的批评他过于依赖哲学。<br>
The Dominican friar Thomas Aquinas (about 1225–1274) is the most representative figure. He taught at the University of Paris and wrote the Summa theologiae (1265–1273). He integrated Aristotle's philosophy into Christian theology and held that reason can work within faith: the theologian starts from faith, while the philosopher relies on reason alone, and the two do not conflict. The Catholic Church still holds his theology in very high regard. Protestant theologians assess him differently: some draw on him heavily, while others criticise him for relying too much on philosophy.

## 问题和改革的呼声 | Problems and calls for reform

中世纪的教会也有严重的问题：圣职买卖、神职人员的腐败、教会和政治权力纠缠不清。十字军东征期间发生了屠杀，宗教裁判所迫害被定为异端的人。“认真思考信仰”路径里“教会做过很多错事”一课讲到了这些事。<br>
The medieval church also had serious problems: the buying and selling of church offices, corruption among the clergy, and the entanglement of church and political power. Massacres took place during the Crusades, and the Inquisition persecuted those judged heretics. The lesson on wrongs done by the church in the Seeking path covers these.

在路德以前，已经有人呼吁改革。14 世纪英格兰的威克里夫（John Wycliffe）批评教会的腐败。波希米亚的扬·胡斯（Jan Hus，约 1370–1415）读了威克里夫的著作，从 1402 年起在布拉格的伯利恒堂用捷克语讲道，让普通人也能听懂。他在康斯坦茨会议被定为异端，1415 年 7 月 6 日被处以火刑。一百年后，路德读到胡斯的著作，发现自己的很多想法和胡斯相同。<br>
Before Luther, some were already calling for reform. In fourteenth-century England John Wycliffe criticised the church's corruption. In Bohemia Jan Hus (about 1370–1415) read Wycliffe's works and, from 1402, preached in Czech at the Bethlehem Chapel in Prague so that ordinary people could understand. He was condemned as a heretic at the Council of Constance and burned on 6 July 1415. A century later Luther read Hus's writings and found that many of his own ideas matched Hus's.

## 怎样看这一千年 | How to see these thousand years

新教徒有时把中世纪看成一片黑暗，天主教徒有时把它看成信仰的黄金时代。实际情况更复杂：这一千年里有忠心的信徒和动人的灵修著作，也有腐败和暴力。希伯来书说：“从前引导你们、传神之道给你们的人，你们要想念他们，效法他们的信心”（来 13:7）。读这段历史，可以学他们的长处，也从他们的错误中得到警戒。<br>
Protestants sometimes see the Middle Ages as a dark age, and Catholics sometimes see them as a golden age of faith. The reality is more complex: these thousand years held faithful believers and moving spiritual writings, and also corruption and violence. Hebrews says, “Remember your leaders, men who spoke to you the word of God, and... imitate their faith” (Hebrews 13:7). Reading this history, we can learn from their strengths and take warning from their mistakes.
