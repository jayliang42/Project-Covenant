## 下卷 | The second volume

使徒行传和路加福音都献给一位叫提阿非罗的人（路 1:3；徒 1:1），是同一位作者的上下两卷。福音书讲耶稣“开头一切所行所教训的”，使徒行传讲他升天以后，借着圣灵在门徒身上继续做的事。<br>
Acts and Luke are both addressed to a man named Theophilus (Luke 1:3; Acts 1:1), two volumes by the same author. The Gospel tells “all that Jesus began both to do and to teach,” and Acts tells what he continued to do through the Spirit in his disciples after the ascension.

## 作者和年代 | Author and date

- **传统看法**：从 2 世纪中期起，教会一致认为作者是“亲爱的医生路加”（西 4:14），保罗的同伴。书中有几段从“他们”改为“我们”（16:10-17；20:5-15；21:1-18；27:1–28:16），常被看作作者亲身同行的记录。有学者认为它写于 60 年代早期，因为全书结束时保罗还在罗马被软禁，没有提到他的死，也没有提到 64 年尼禄对基督徒的逼迫。<br>
  **Traditional view**: From the mid-second century the church held unanimously that the author was “Luke, the beloved physician” (Colossians 4:14), Paul's companion. Several passages shift from “they” to “we” (16:10-17; 20:5-15; 21:1-18; 27:1–28:16), often read as the author's own travel record. Some scholars date it to the early 60s, since the book ends with Paul under house arrest in Rome and mentions neither his death nor Nero's persecution of 64.
- **另一种看法**：大英百科介绍的批判学者认为，使徒行传大约在路加福音（约 85 年）以后不久写成；“我们”段落可能只是古代游记常用的写法，不一定表示作者是保罗的同伴。<br>
  **Another view**: Critical scholars summarised by Britannica date Acts shortly after Luke's Gospel (about 85), and suggest the “we” passages may be a style common in ancient travel accounts rather than proof that the author was Paul's companion.

## 结构：从耶路撒冷到罗马 | Structure: from Jerusalem to Rome

耶稣升天前说：“你们……要在耶路撒冷、犹太全地，和撒马利亚，直到地极，作我的见证”（徒 1:8）。全书大致照这个次序展开：<br>
Before ascending, Jesus said, “You will be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the uttermost parts of the earth” (Acts 1:8). The book unfolds roughly in that order:

- **耶路撒冷（1–7 章）**：五旬节圣灵降临，教会诞生，彼得讲道，司提反殉道。<br>
  **Jerusalem (chapters 1–7)**: The Spirit comes at Pentecost, the church is born, Peter preaches, Stephen is martyred.
- **犹太和撒马利亚（8–12 章）**：门徒分散，腓利到撒马利亚，扫罗归信，彼得到外邦人哥尼流家。<br>
  **Judea and Samaria (chapters 8–12)**: The disciples scatter, Philip goes to Samaria, Saul is converted, and Peter goes to the Gentile Cornelius.
- **直到地极（13–28 章）**：保罗的几次旅行，耶路撒冷会议，保罗被捕，最后到达罗马。<br>
  **To the ends of the earth (chapters 13–28)**: Paul's journeys, the Jerusalem council, Paul's arrest, and his arrival in Rome.

书中常有总结的话，例如“神的道兴旺起来”（徒 6:7），标出一个阶段的结束。<br>
Summary statements such as “The word of God increased” (Acts 6:7) mark the end of each stage.

## 关键的转折：耶路撒冷会议 | A turning point: the Jerusalem council

外邦人信了耶稣，要不要先受割礼、守摩西的律法？使徒和长老在耶路撒冷开会讨论。彼得说：“我们得救乃是因主耶稣的恩，和他们一样”（徒 15:11）。会议决定不把割礼加在外邦信徒身上，只请他们禁戒几样会妨碍和犹太信徒相交的事。<br>
When Gentiles believed in Jesus, must they first be circumcised and keep the law of Moses? The apostles and elders met in Jerusalem to discuss it. Peter said, “we believe that we are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus, just as they are” (Acts 15:11). The council decided not to impose circumcision on Gentile believers, asking them only to abstain from a few things that would hinder fellowship with Jewish believers.

## 描述和规定 | Describing and prescribing

使徒行传是历史叙事。书中记下的事，不一定都是要每个时代照做的模式。门徒用抽签选出马提亚（徒 1:26），早期信徒“凡物公用”（徒 2:44），今天的教会一般不把这些当作必须遵守的规定。<br>
Acts is historical narrative. Not everything it records is a pattern for every age. The disciples chose Matthias by casting lots (Acts 1:26), and the early believers held “all things in common” (Acts 2:44); churches today generally do not treat these as binding rules.

受圣灵的经历也是一个例子：五旬节时信徒说方言；撒马利亚人信了以后，过了一段时间使徒按手才受圣灵（徒 8 章）；哥尼流一家在听道时就受了圣灵，然后才受洗（徒 10 章）。五旬节派常以这些记载说明“圣灵的洗”在重生以后，并以说方言为记号；其他传统则认为这些是福音进入新群体时的特殊情况。“属灵恩赐今天还有吗”一课介绍了这些立场。<br>
Receiving the Spirit is another example. At Pentecost believers spoke in tongues; the Samaritans received the Spirit some time after believing, when the apostles laid hands on them (Acts 8); Cornelius's household received the Spirit while hearing the word, and were baptised afterwards (Acts 10). Pentecostals often take these accounts to show that Spirit baptism follows the new birth, with tongues as its sign; other traditions see them as special cases marking the gospel's entry into new groups. The lesson on spiritual gifts today introduces these positions.

## 没有结束的结尾 | An open ending

全书结束时，保罗在罗马自己租的房子里住了两年，“放胆传讲神国的道……并没有人禁止”（徒 28:31）。书中没有说保罗后来怎样。很多读者认为，这个结尾表示故事还在继续，每一代的教会都在接着写。<br>
The book ends with Paul living two years in his rented house in Rome, “preaching God's Kingdom... with all boldness, without hindrance” (Acts 28:31). It does not say what happened to Paul next. Many readers take this ending to mean the story is still going on, with each generation of the church continuing it.
