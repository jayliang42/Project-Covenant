## 福音书是什么文体 | What kind of writing the Gospels are

“福音”的意思是“好消息”。四卷福音书讲的是耶稣的言行、受死和复活。它们属于什么文体？学者 Richard Burridge 在 1992 年出版的研究里，把福音书和古代希腊罗马的传记（希腊文 bios，“生平”）比较，认为福音书就属于这种文体。<br>
“Gospel” means “good news.” The four Gospels tell of Jesus' words and deeds, his death, and his resurrection. What kind of writing are they? In a study published in 1992, the scholar Richard Burridge compared the Gospels with ancient Graeco-Roman biographies (Greek bios, “life”) and concluded that the Gospels belong to that genre.

古代传记和现代传记不同。它常常不交代人物的童年，也不一定按时间次序写；它把篇幅集中在人物的言行和死亡上，目的是让读者认识这个人，学他的样式。福音书用将近三分之一甚至更多的篇幅写耶稣最后一周，这和古代传记的写法相合。<br>
Ancient biography differs from modern biography. It often says little about childhood and does not always follow chronological order. It gives its space to the person's words, deeds, and death, so that readers may know the person and follow his example. The Gospels devote a large share of their length, a third or more, to Jesus' last week, which fits the way ancient biographies were written.

作者自己也说明了写作的目的。路加说他“从起头都详细考察了，就定意要按着次序写给你，使你知道所学之道都是确实的”（路 1:3-4）。约翰说：“记这些事要叫你们信耶稣是基督，是神的儿子”（约 20:31）。<br>
The writers state their own purposes. Luke says that, “having traced the course of all things accurately from the first,” he wrote “in order... that you might know the certainty concerning the things in which you were instructed” (Luke 1:3-4). John says, “these are written, that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God” (John 20:31).

## 四卷书，四个角度 | Four books, four angles

- **马太福音**：常引旧约，说“这是要应验……的话”。耶稣的教导分成五大段，最有名的是登山宝训。马太似乎特别考虑犹太读者。<br>
  **Matthew**: It often quotes the Old Testament, saying “that it might be fulfilled.” Jesus' teaching is gathered into five large blocks, the best known being the Sermon on the Mount. Matthew seems to have Jewish readers especially in mind.
- **马可福音**：最短，节奏快，常用“立刻”。前半卷问“这是谁”，后半卷走向十字架。“刚开始信”路径里有四课逐段读马可福音。<br>
  **Mark**: The shortest and fastest-paced, often using “immediately.” The first half asks “who is this?” and the second half moves toward the cross. The New Believer path has four lessons that read Mark section by section.
- **路加福音**：写得最有次序，特别关心穷人、妇女、外邦人和被看不起的人。它和使徒行传是上下两卷。<br>
  **Luke**: The most orderly, with special concern for the poor, women, Gentiles, and the despised. It and Acts form two volumes of one work.
- **约翰福音**：和前三卷很不同。学者常把它的内容归纳为七个“神迹”和七个“我是”的说法，耶稣常有长篇的对话和讲论。<br>
  **John**: It is quite different from the other three. Scholars often summarise its content as seven “signs” and seven “I am” sayings, and Jesus often speaks in long conversations and discourses.

## 前三卷为什么那么相似 | Why the first three are so alike

马太、马可、路加三卷可以排在一起逐段对照，所以叫“符类福音”（Synoptic Gospels，意思是“可以一同看”）。它们有大量相同的内容，有时连字句都一样。这三卷书之间是什么关系，叫作“符类福音问题”。<br>
Matthew, Mark, and Luke can be set side by side and compared passage by passage, so they are called the Synoptic Gospels (“seen together”). They share much material, sometimes word for word. The question of how the three are related is called the Synoptic problem.

- **两源说**：现在多数学者认为马可福音最早，马太和路加都以马可福音为基础，另外还用了一份共同的耶稣语录来源，学者称为 Q（德文 Quelle，“来源”）。这份 Q 没有留下抄本，是从马太和路加的共同内容推出来的。<br>
  **The two-source hypothesis**: Most scholars today hold that Mark came first, that Matthew and Luke both built on Mark, and that they also used a shared source of Jesus' sayings, which scholars call Q (German Quelle, “source”). No manuscript of Q survives; it is inferred from what Matthew and Luke share.
- **其他看法**：奥古斯丁认为马太最早，马可是马太的缩写；这个看法在 19 世纪以前占主流。1789 年格利斯巴赫（Griesbach）主张马可用了马太和路加。现代也有学者接受马可最早，但认为不需要 Q，路加直接用了马太。<br>
  **Other views**: Augustine held that Matthew came first and that Mark abridged it; this view prevailed until the nineteenth century. In 1789 Griesbach argued that Mark used both Matthew and Luke. Some modern scholars accept that Mark came first but think Q is unnecessary, holding that Luke used Matthew directly.

不管哪一种看法，对一般读者的用处是一样的：同一件事在几卷书里写法不同，可以对照着读，看每位作者怎样安排材料，强调什么。<br>
Whichever view is taken, the use for ordinary readers is the same: where an event is told differently in several Gospels, read the accounts side by side and notice how each writer arranges the material and what he stresses.

## 几个读法 | Some ways to read

- **一卷一卷读，读完整卷。** 2 世纪的他提安（Tatian）把四卷合编成一卷《四福音合参》，合参对查找有帮助。但每卷书有自己的结构和重点，合在一起读就看不出来了。<br>
  **Read one Gospel at a time, all the way through.** In the second century Tatian combined the four into a single work, the Diatessaron. A harmony helps with looking things up. But each Gospel has its own structure and emphasis, and these disappear when the books are merged.
- **注意上下文的安排。** 福音书作者常把内容按主题编排。问：这段为什么放在这里？前后两段有什么关系？<br>
  **Notice how passages are arranged.** The Gospel writers often arrange material by theme. Ask: Why is this passage placed here? How does it relate to what comes before and after?
- **读比喻要找重点。** 比喻多半有一个或几个要点，不必给每个细节都找意思。也要看耶稣是对谁讲的，听的人有什么反应。<br>
  **In parables, look for the main point.** Most parables have one or a few points, and not every detail needs a meaning. Notice also whom Jesus was speaking to and how they responded.
- **把旧约连起来。** 复活的耶稣“从摩西和众先知起，凡经上所指着自己的话都给他们讲解明白了”（路 24:27）。福音书引用的旧约经文，值得回去读一读原来的上下文。<br>
  **Connect with the Old Testament.** The risen Jesus, “beginning from Moses and from all the prophets,” explained to the disciples “in all the Scriptures the things concerning himself” (Luke 24:27). When a Gospel quotes the Old Testament, it is worth going back to read the original context.
