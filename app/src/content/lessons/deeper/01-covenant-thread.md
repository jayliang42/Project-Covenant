## 什么是圣经里的“约”（Covenant）？ | What is a biblical covenant?

现代人说的“合同”（contract）通常是利益交换：你交钱、我交货，违约就散伙。但古代近东与圣经里的“盟约”（*berit* / *diatheke*）更深：它是**带着庄严誓言与记号、把双方绑定在立约关系里的承诺**。<br>
A modern contract is an exchange of goods or services; break a clause and walk away. A biblical covenant (*berit* in Hebrew, *diatheke* in Greek) goes deeper: it is **a solemn, oath-bound relationship commitment sealed with signs and promises**.

本项目的名字“Project Covenant（圣约计划）”正来自这条贯穿全书的主线。<br>
This is the thread from which Project Covenant takes its name.

## 不同的神学传统怎样读这条主线？ | How do theological traditions read this thread?

基督教正统传统都承认所有圣约最终在耶稣基督里成全（林后 1:20），但在细节结构上有所侧重：<br>
Across orthodox Christian traditions, all agree that God's promises find their "Yes" in Christ (2 Corinthians 1:20), while organizing the details with different emphases:

- **圣约神学（Covenant Theology，常见于改革宗/长老会等传统）**：强调堕落之后神只有一套恩典之约（Covenant of Grace），旧约各约与新约是同一恩典之约在不同历史阶段的施行。<br>
  **Covenant Theology (common in Reformed and Presbyterian traditions)**: Sees one overarching Covenant of Grace after the fall, administered in progressively clearer forms from Abraham through Moses and David to Christ.
- **渐进圣约主义（Progressive Covenantalism）与时代论传统（Dispensationalism）**：更强调各个历史圣约本身的阶段特征，以及各约如何一步步在基督里更新或区分以色列与教会的角色。<br>
  **Progressive Covenantalism and Dispensational traditions**: Place greater weight on the distinct shape of each historical covenant and how the covenants progress toward fulfillment in Christ (with differing views on the relationship between Israel and the church).

理解这些异同，能帮我们在读《加拉太书》《罗马书》和《希伯来书》时，看清使徒们究竟在论证什么。<br>
Knowing these frameworks helps you follow the arguments of Galatians, Romans, and Hebrews with much greater clarity.
