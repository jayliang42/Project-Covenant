## 圣经里的“约”是什么 | What a covenant is in the Bible

现代的“合同”（contract）通常是利益交换：你交钱，我交货，违约就各走各路。古代近东和圣经里的“盟约”（*berit* / *diatheke*）分量更重。它是**带着庄严誓言和记号的承诺，把立约双方绑在一个关系里**。<br>
A modern contract is usually an exchange: you pay, I deliver, and if either side breaks it we part ways. A covenant (*berit* in Hebrew, *diatheke* in Greek) in the ancient Near East and in the Bible carries more weight. It is **a promise made with a solemn oath and a sign, which binds the two parties in a relationship**.

本项目的名字“Project Covenant（圣约计划）”就取自这条贯穿全书的主线。<br>
This project's name, Project Covenant, is taken from this thread that runs through the whole Bible.

## 不同神学传统怎样读这条主线 | How theological traditions read this thread

正统的基督教传统都承认，所有圣约最终在耶稣基督里成全（林后 1:20）。在细节的结构上，各传统的侧重不同：<br>
All orthodox Christian traditions agree that every covenant is finally fulfilled in Jesus Christ (2 Corinthians 1:20). They differ in how they arrange the details:

- **圣约神学（Covenant Theology，常见于改革宗、长老会等传统）**：强调人堕落之后，神只有一个恩典之约（Covenant of Grace）。旧约各约和新约是这同一个恩典之约在不同历史阶段的施行。<br>
  **Covenant Theology (common in Reformed and Presbyterian traditions)**: Stresses that after the fall God has one Covenant of Grace. The Old Testament covenants and the New Covenant are the same Covenant of Grace administered in different periods of history.
- **渐进圣约主义（Progressive Covenantalism）与时代论传统（Dispensationalism）**：更强调每个历史圣约各自的阶段特征，以及各约怎样一步步在基督里成全。对于以色列和教会的关系，这些传统看法不一。<br>
  **Progressive Covenantalism and Dispensational traditions**: Put more weight on the distinct character of each historical covenant and on how the covenants move step by step toward fulfillment in Christ. They hold differing views on the relationship between Israel and the church.

了解这些异同，读《加拉太书》《罗马书》《希伯来书》时，更容易看清使徒在论证什么。<br>
Knowing these differences makes it easier to see what the apostles are arguing in Galatians, Romans, and Hebrews.
