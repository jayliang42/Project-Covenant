## 大家共同相信的 | What all hold in common

圣经说：“圣经都是神所默示的”（提后 3:16）。“默示”原文的意思是“神呼出的气”。彼得说，先知“被圣灵感动，说出神的话来”（彼后 1:21）。耶稣自己说：“经上的话是不能废的”（约 10:35）。<br>
Scripture says, “Every Scripture is God-breathed” (2 Timothy 3:16). Peter says that the prophets “spoke, being moved by the Holy Spirit” (2 Peter 1:21). Jesus himself says, “the Scripture can’t be broken” (John 10:35).

天主教、东正教和新教都相信，圣经是神借着人写成的：神是圣经的作者，写作的人也是圣经的作者，各有自己的文风和背景。各方也都接受新约的 27 卷书。分歧在下面三个问题上。<br>
Catholics, Orthodox, and Protestants all believe that Scripture was written by God through human beings: God is its author, and the human writers are its authors too, each with a style and setting of his own. All also accept the 27 books of the New Testament. The differences lie in the three questions below.

## 一、“无误”到什么程度 | 1. How far inerrancy extends

- **福音派的“无误”立场**：1978 年，三百多位福音派学者在芝加哥签署了《芝加哥圣经无误声明》。它说圣经原稿在它所肯定的一切事上都无误，并明确拒绝把无误限定在属灵和救赎的主题上，历史和科学方面的陈述也包括在内。声明也说，判断“无误”要考虑经文的文体和写作习惯，例如诗歌、比喻和概数。美南浸信会、美国长老会（PCA）等许多福音派教会持守这种立场。<br>
  **The evangelical inerrancy position**: In 1978 some three hundred evangelical scholars signed the Chicago Statement on Biblical Inerrancy in Chicago. It says that Scripture in its original manuscripts is without error in all that it affirms, and it expressly rejects limiting inerrancy to spiritual and redemptive themes, so statements on history and science are included. The statement also says that inerrancy must be judged with attention to literary form and the conventions of writing, such as poetry, figures of speech, and round numbers. The Southern Baptist Convention, the Presbyterian Church in America, and many other evangelical churches hold this position.
- **天主教**：梵二会议的《启示宪章》第 11 条说，圣经“坚定地、忠实地、无错误地”教导了神为了我们的救恩愿意写在圣经里的真理。天主教学者对这句话有不同的解读：有人认为无误限于和救恩有关的事；有人认为凡圣经作者所肯定的都无误，同时要区分作者所“教导”的和他只是顺带“假定”的事。<br>
  **Catholic**: Article 11 of Dei Verbum, from the Second Vatican Council, says that Scripture teaches “solidly, faithfully, and without error” the truth which God wanted put into the sacred writings for the sake of our salvation. Catholic scholars read this differently: some take inerrancy to be limited to matters of salvation, and others hold that all the biblical writers affirm is without error, while distinguishing what a writer teaches from what he merely assumes.
- **东正教**：东正教很少用“无误”这样的说法，强调圣经是在教会里、在圣灵的引导下被领受和解释的，圣经是神圣传统里最重要的部分。<br>
  **Orthodox**: The Orthodox seldom use the language of inerrancy. They stress that Scripture is received and interpreted in the church under the Spirit's guidance, and that it is the chief part of Holy Tradition.
- **一些主流新教教会**：认为圣经在信仰和生活上完全可靠，是神的话，但不一定在每个历史和科学细节上都无误。很多人用“无谬”（infallible，在信仰和生活上不会误导人）一词，以和“无误”（inerrant）相区别。<br>
  **Some mainline Protestant churches**: They hold that Scripture is wholly reliable for faith and life and is God's word, without necessarily being free of error in every historical and scientific detail. Many use “infallible” (never misleading in faith and life) in distinction from “inerrant.”

## 二、圣经和传统 | 2. Scripture and tradition

- **新教**：持守“唯独圣经”。圣经是信仰和生活最高的权威，教会的信经、会议和传统都很宝贵，但都要放在圣经下面检验。<br>
  **Protestant**: Protestants hold to Scripture alone. Scripture is the highest authority for faith and life. The church's creeds, councils, and traditions are valuable, and all are to be tested by Scripture.
- **天主教**：圣经和圣传是同一个启示的两种传递方式，由教会的训导权负责权威地解释。<br>
  **Catholic**: Scripture and Sacred Tradition are two modes of handing on one revelation, and the church's teaching office is charged with interpreting them authoritatively.
- **东正教**：圣经是在神圣传统之内的，传统还包括信经、大公会议、教父和礼仪。东正教不认为圣经和传统是两个并列的来源。<br>
  **Orthodox**: Scripture stands within Holy Tradition, which also includes the creeds, the ecumenical councils, the Fathers, and the liturgy. The Orthodox do not regard Scripture and tradition as two parallel sources.

## 三、旧约有哪些书 | 3. Which books are in the Old Testament

新约 27 卷，各传统一样。旧约就不同了：<br>
The 27 books of the New Testament are the same for all traditions. The Old Testament is another matter:

| 传统 Tradition | 旧约 Old Testament |
|---|---|
| 新教 Protestant | 39 卷，和希伯来文圣经的内容相同<br>39 books, the same content as the Hebrew Bible |
| 天主教 Catholic | 46 卷，多出多俾亚传、友弟德传、智慧篇、德训篇、巴路克、玛加伯上下，以斯帖记和但以理书也有增补<br>46 books, adding Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, and 1–2 Maccabees, with additions to Esther and Daniel |
| 东正教 Orthodox | 在天主教的基础上，还有以斯得拉一书、玛加伯三书、诗篇第 151 篇等，玛加伯四书和玛拿西祷词常列在附录<br>Beyond the Catholic list, also 1 Esdras, 3 Maccabees, Psalm 151, and others, with 4 Maccabees and the Prayer of Manasseh often in an appendix |

这些多出来的书大多来自希腊文的旧约译本“七十士译本”，早期教会普遍使用这个译本。4 世纪末的耶柔米主张旧约正典以希伯来文圣经为准，把这些书列为可以读、但不能作教义根据的书。宗教改革时，新教采用了耶柔米的看法；1546 年，特兰托会议正式确认这些书为正典。路德在他的德文圣经里保留了这些书，放在两约之间，题为“旁经”。东正教没有召开过大公会议来确定旧约的书目。<br>
Most of these additional books come from the Septuagint, the Greek translation of the Old Testament, which the early church widely used. In the late 4th century Jerome argued that the Old Testament canon should follow the Hebrew Bible, and he placed these books in a class to be read but not used to establish doctrine. At the Reformation Protestants followed Jerome's view; in 1546 the Council of Trent formally confirmed these books as canonical. Luther kept them in his German Bible, placed between the Testaments under the heading Apocrypha. The Orthodox Church has not held an ecumenical council to fix the list of Old Testament books.

这些书里有一些影响教义的经文，例如天主教用玛加伯下 12:43-46 为亡者祈祷作根据。所以这个问题和前面讲过的炼狱、为亡者祷告等分歧相关。<br>
Some passages in these books bear on doctrine. The Catholic Church, for example, cites 2 Maccabees 12:43-46 in support of praying for the dead. So this question is linked to differences discussed earlier, such as purgatory and prayer for the departed.

## 实际的建议 | A practical suggestion

和其他传统的信徒讨论圣经的时候，可以先确认双方说的是同一套书，再看彼此是在讨论经文的意思，还是在讨论经文的权威。很多争论其实卡在后一个问题上。<br>
When you discuss the Bible with believers from other traditions, first make sure you mean the same set of books, and then see whether you are discussing what a passage means or what authority it carries. Many arguments are actually stuck on the second question.
