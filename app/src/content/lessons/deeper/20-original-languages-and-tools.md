## 三种原文 | Three original languages

- **希伯来文**：旧约的大部分是用希伯来文写的。它从右往左写，有 22 个辅音字母。原来的抄本只写辅音，元音和诵读符号是马所拉学者大约在主后 7–10 世纪加上的。<br>
  **Hebrew**: Most of the Old Testament is in Hebrew. It is written right to left, with 22 consonant letters. The original manuscripts had only consonants; the vowel and reading marks were added by the Masoretes roughly between the 7th and 10th centuries AD.
- **亚兰文**：旧约有几段是亚兰文，主要在《但以理书》2–7 章和《以斯拉记》4–7 章的一部分。耶稣日常说的很可能是亚兰文，福音书保留了几句，例如“大利大，古米！”（可 5:41）。<br>
  **Aramaic**: A few sections of the Old Testament are in Aramaic, mainly parts of Daniel 2–7 and Ezra 4–7. Jesus probably spoke Aramaic day to day, and the Gospels keep a few of his words, such as “Talitha cumi!” (Mark 5:41).
- **希腊文**：新约是用当时通行的希腊文（Koine）写的。它有 24 个字母，动词变化很丰富，能表达细致的时态和语气。<br>
  **Greek**: The New Testament is written in the common Greek of the day (Koine). It has 24 letters and a rich verb system that can express fine distinctions of tense and mood.

## 原文里的几个例子 | A few examples from the original

- **“慈爱”（hesed）**：《诗篇》136 篇每一节都重复“他的慈爱永远长存”。这个希伯来词包含忠诚、守约的爱，很难用一个中文或英文词完全译出来，所以不同译本译作“慈爱”“信实的爱”“loving kindness”“steadfast love”等。<br>
  **Hesed**: Every verse of Psalm 136 repeats “his loving kindness endures forever.” The Hebrew word carries the sense of loyal, covenant-keeping love and is hard to capture in a single Chinese or English word, so translations render it as 慈爱, 信实的爱, “loving kindness,” “steadfast love,” and so on.
- **两个“爱”字**：《约翰福音》21 章里，耶稣前两次问彼得用的是 agapao，彼得回答用的是 phileo，第三次耶稣也用了 phileo。有人认为这两个词的差别很重要，也有不少学者认为约翰只是在变换用词。WEB 译本把 phileo 译作“affection”，可以看出译者的判断。<br>
  **Two words for love**: In John 21, Jesus' first two questions to Peter use agapao, Peter answers with phileo, and the third time Jesus also uses phileo. Some think the difference between the words is significant; many scholars think John is simply varying his vocabulary. The WEB renders phileo as “affection,” which shows its translators' judgment.

## 几种常用的工具 | Some common tools

1. **对照几个译本**：这是最简单也最有用的办法。直译的译本贴近原文的结构，意译的译本读起来更顺。几个译本不同的地方，往往就是原文有讲究的地方。<br>
   **Compare translations**: This is the simplest and most useful method. Literal translations stay close to the original structure; meaning-based translations read more smoothly. Where translations differ is often where the original has something worth noticing.
2. **经文汇编（concordance）**：可以查一个词在圣经里出现的所有地方。英文常用的有 19 世纪末出版的《斯特朗经文汇编》（Strong's），它给每个原文词编了号码。<br>
   **Concordance**: Lets you find every place a word appears in the Bible. A common English one is Strong's Concordance, published in the late 19th century, which gives each original-language word a number.
3. **原文对照本（interlinear）**：在原文下面逐字标出中文或英文。适合查某个词，不适合拿来当译本读，因为逐字对照常常不通顺。<br>
   **Interlinear Bible**: Puts a Chinese or English word under each original word. It is good for looking up a word, but not for reading as a translation, because word-for-word renderings are often awkward.
4. **研读本圣经和注释书**：研读本的注释会说明背景、难词和不同的解释。<br>
   **Study Bibles and commentaries**: Study notes explain background, difficult words, and different interpretations.

## 查原文时容易犯的错误 | Common mistakes with original words

- **词根谬误**：以为一个词的意思就是它词根的意思。词义要看它在当时怎样用。<br>
  **The root fallacy**: Assuming a word means whatever its root means. A word's meaning depends on how it was actually used.
- **时代错置**：用后来的词去解释原文，例如说希腊文的 dunamis（能力）就是“炸药”。炸药是 19 世纪才发明的。<br>
  **Anachronism**: Reading later words back into the original, such as saying the Greek dunamis (power) means “dynamite.” Dynamite was invented in the 19th century.
- **把所有意思都塞进去**：一个词在字典里有好几个意思，在一处经文里通常只用其中一个，要看上下文。<br>
  **Loading in every meaning**: A word may have several meanings in the dictionary, but in a given passage it usually carries one. The context decides.

这些错误在讲道和网上文章里都常见。学者卡森（D. A. Carson）在 *Exegetical Fallacies*（《解经谬误》）一书里专门讨论过这些问题。<br>
These mistakes are common in sermons and online articles. The scholar D. A. Carson discusses them in his book *Exegetical Fallacies*.

以斯拉时代的利未人“清清楚楚地念神的律法书，讲明意思，使百姓明白所念的”（尼 8:8）。查原文和用工具书，目的也是这样：明白经文本来的意思。<br>
In Ezra's time, the Levites “read in the book, in the law of God, distinctly; and they gave the sense, so that they understood the reading” (Nehemiah 8:8). Looking at the original languages and using study tools serves the same purpose: understanding what the text means.
