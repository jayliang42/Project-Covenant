## 原来的出埃及 | The original exodus

以色列人在埃及作奴隶。神对摩西说：“我要用伸出来的膀臂重重地刑罚埃及人，救赎你们脱离他们的重担……我要以你们为我的百姓，我也要作你们的神”（出 6:6-7）。<br>
Israel was enslaved in Egypt. God told Moses, “I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians... and I will redeem you with an outstretched arm, and with great judgments. I will take you to myself for a people. I will be your God” (Exodus 6:6-7).

出埃及的故事有几个要素：逾越节羔羊的血使长子得以存活；神带领百姓过红海；在旷野里，神用云柱火柱引导他们，降吗哪给他们吃；在西奈山，神和他们立约，颁布律法；神住在他们中间的会幕里；最后进入应许之地。<br>
The exodus story has several elements: the blood of the Passover lamb spares the firstborn; God leads the people through the Red Sea; in the wilderness he guides them by pillar of cloud and fire and feeds them with manna; at Sinai he makes a covenant with them and gives the law; he dwells among them in the tabernacle; and at last they enter the promised land.

次序很重要。十诫开头就说：“我是耶和华你的神，曾将你从埃及地为奴之家领出来”（出 20:2）。神先救赎，后颁布律法。顺服是对救恩的回应，不是得救的条件。<br>
The order matters. The Ten Commandments begin, “I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage” (Exodus 20:2). God redeems first, then gives the law. Obedience is a response to salvation, not a condition for it.

## 旧约里的记念 | Remembered in the Old Testament

以色列人每年守逾越节，记念出埃及（出 12 章）。诗篇一再述说神在埃及和红海行的奇事（例如诗 78、105、136 篇）。先知也常提醒百姓：神曾领他们出埃及。<br>
Each year Israel kept the Passover to remember the exodus (Exodus 12). The Psalms retell again and again God's wonders in Egypt and at the Red Sea (for example Psalms 78, 105, and 136). The prophets also reminded the people that God had brought them out of Egypt.

## 新的出埃及 | A new exodus

被掳到巴比伦以后，先知用出埃及的图画来描写神将来的拯救。以赛亚说：“耶和华在沧海中开道，在大水中开路……你们不要记念从前的事……看哪，我要做一件新事”（赛 43:16-19）。神要再一次带领百姓穿过旷野回家，而且这一次比第一次更大。<br>
After the exile to Babylon, the prophets used the picture of the exodus to describe God's coming salvation. Isaiah says, “Yahweh, who makes a way in the sea, and a path in the mighty waters... Don't remember the former things... Behold, I will do a new thing” (Isaiah 43:16-19). God would again lead his people home through the wilderness, and this time on a greater scale.

## 在耶稣身上 | In Jesus

新约用出埃及的语言来讲耶稣：<br>
The New Testament uses the language of the exodus to speak of Jesus:

- 婴孩耶稣从埃及回来，马太说这应验了“我从埃及召出我的儿子来”（太 2:15；何 11:1）。<br>
  The child Jesus returns from Egypt, and Matthew says this fulfils “Out of Egypt I called my son” (Matthew 2:15; Hosea 11:1).
- 耶稣在旷野受试探四十天，对应以色列在旷野四十年。他每次都引用申命记回答魔鬼。<br>
  Jesus is tempted forty days in the wilderness, echoing Israel's forty years there. Each time he answers the devil from Deuteronomy.
- 在登山变像时，摩西和以利亚同耶稣谈论他在耶路撒冷“将要成的”去世（路 9:31）；路加用的希腊文就是 exodos，“离去”或“出埃及”。<br>
  At the transfiguration, Moses and Elijah spoke with Jesus about “his departure, which he was about to accomplish at Jerusalem” (Luke 9:31); the Greek word Luke uses is exodos.
- 耶稣在逾越节的晚餐上设立圣餐。保罗说：“我们逾越节的羔羊基督已经被杀献祭了”（林前 5:7）。<br>
  Jesus instituted the Lord's Supper at a Passover meal. Paul says, “Christ, our Passover, has been sacrificed in our place” (1 Corinthians 5:7).
- 约翰福音 6 章，耶稣说自己是从天上降下来的“生命的粮”，比旷野的吗哪更大。<br>
  In John 6 Jesus calls himself the “bread of life” come down from heaven, greater than the manna in the wilderness.

## 在教会和末后 | In the church and at the end

保罗说，以色列人“都从海中经过，都在云里、海里受洗归了摩西”，又说“那磐石就是基督”（林前 10:1-4），用来提醒哥林多的信徒。歌罗西书说，神“救了我们脱离黑暗的权势，把我们迁到他爱子的国里”（西 1:13）。<br>
Paul says the Israelites “all passed through the sea; and were all baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,” and that “the rock was Christ” (1 Corinthians 10:1-4), using this as a warning to the Corinthians. Colossians says God “delivered us out of the power of darkness, and translated us into the Kingdom of the Son of his love” (Colossians 1:13).

启示录描写得胜的人站在玻璃海上，“唱神仆人摩西的歌和羔羊的歌”（启 15:3）。出埃及的故事，到最后成为全体得赎之民的歌。<br>
Revelation pictures the victorious standing by a sea of glass, singing “the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb” (Revelation 15:3). The exodus story becomes, at the end, the song of all the redeemed.
