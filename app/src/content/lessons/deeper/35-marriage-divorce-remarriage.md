## 共同的出发点 | The common starting point

法利赛人问耶稣，人可不可以随便休妻。耶稣回到创世记：“那起初造人的，是造男造女……因此，人要离开父母，与妻子连合，二人成为一体……所以，神配合的，人不可分开”（太 19:4-6）。他们又问，那摩西为什么准人休妻？耶稣说：“摩西因为你们的心硬，所以许你们休妻，但起初并不是这样”（太 19:8）。<br>
The Pharisees asked Jesus whether a man could divorce his wife for any reason. Jesus went back to Genesis: “he who made them from the beginning made them male and female... a man shall leave his father and mother, and shall be joined to his wife; and the two shall become one flesh... What therefore God has joined together, don’t let man tear apart” (Matthew 19:4-6). They asked why Moses then allowed divorce. Jesus said, “Moses, because of the hardness of your hearts, allowed you to divorce your wives, but from the beginning it has not been so” (Matthew 19:8).

各传统都同意：婚姻是一男一女在神面前立的终身盟约；夫妻要彼此相爱，“正如基督爱教会，为教会舍己”（弗 5:25）；离婚是一件令人痛心的事，不是神起初的心意。分歧在于两段经文怎样理解：<br>
All the traditions agree that marriage is a lifelong covenant between a man and a woman before God; that husband and wife are to love each other “even as Christ also loved the assembly, and gave himself up for it” (Ephesians 5:25); and that divorce is grievous and not what God intended from the beginning. They differ on how to read two passages:

- 马太福音 19:9：“凡休妻另娶的，若不是为淫乱的缘故，就是犯奸淫了。”马可福音 10:11 和路加福音 16:18 的平行经文没有“若不是为淫乱的缘故”这一句。<br>
  Matthew 19:9: “whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another, commits adultery.” The parallel passages in Mark 10:11 and Luke 16:18 lack the phrase “except for sexual immorality.”
- 哥林多前书 7:15：信主的一方如果被不信的配偶离弃，“就由他离去吧……遇着这样的事都不必拘束。”<br>
  1 Corinthians 7:15: if a believer is deserted by an unbelieving spouse, “let there be separation. The brother or the sister is not under bondage in such cases.”

## 各传统的教导 | What each tradition teaches

- **天主教**：婚姻是圣事，有效成立并圆房的婚姻不能被任何人间的权力解除。夫妻实在无法同住时可以分居，但在神面前仍是夫妻，不能另立婚姻（《天主教教理》1649）。离婚后在民法上再婚的人，如果第一次婚姻有效，新的结合不被承认，在这种情况持续期间一般不能领圣体（1650）。教会法庭可以审查第一次婚姻是否从一开始就因某些条件欠缺而无效；如果宣告“婚姻无效”，当事人可以在教会里结婚。2016 年教宗方济各发表《爱的喜乐》，呼吁牧者在个别情况下陪伴和分辨。阿根廷布宜诺斯艾利斯的主教们认为，在有限的情况下可以让这些人领圣事；另一些主教和神学家，例如当时的信理部长官米勒枢机，认为原来的规定没有改变。<br>
  **Catholic**: Marriage is a sacrament, and a valid, consummated marriage cannot be dissolved by any human power. When spouses are in fact unable to live together they may separate, yet before God they remain husband and wife and may not enter a new union (Catechism 1649). Those who divorce and remarry civilly, if the first marriage was valid, are in a union the church does not recognise, and while it continues they ordinarily may not receive communion (1650). Church tribunals can examine whether a first marriage was null from the start because something required was lacking. If it is declared null, the parties may marry in the church. In 2016 Pope Francis issued Amoris laetitia, asking pastors to accompany and discern in individual cases. The bishops of Buenos Aires held that in limited cases such persons may receive the sacraments; other bishops and theologians, such as Cardinal Gerhard Müller, then prefect of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, held that the existing discipline had not changed.
- **东正教**：同样认为婚姻应当终身，反对离婚。但教会出于对人灵魂的关怀，按“通融”（希腊文 oikonomia）的原则，准许离婚的人第二次、甚至第三次结婚。第二次和第三次的婚礼带有悔罪的性质，和第一次婚礼的仪式不同。<br>
  **Orthodox**: The Orthodox likewise hold that marriage should be lifelong and oppose divorce. Out of pastoral concern for souls, however, and by the principle of “economy” (Greek oikonomia), the church permits a divorced person to marry a second and even a third time. The rite for a second or third marriage has a penitential character and differs from the first.
- **改革宗和许多新教教会**：《威斯敏斯特信条》第 24 章说，只有两种情况能解除婚姻：配偶犯奸淫，以及教会或官府都无法挽回的故意遗弃；无辜的一方可以离婚，并且可以再婚。信条也要求按公开、有秩序的程序处理，不能由当事人自己决定。很多浸信会、长老会和福音派教会持类似的看法。<br>
  **Reformed and many Protestant churches**: Chapter 24 of the Westminster Confession says that only two things can dissolve a marriage: adultery, and wilful desertion that neither church nor civil authority can remedy. The innocent party may divorce and may remarry. The Confession also requires a public and orderly process, not one left to the couple's own decision. Many Baptist, Presbyterian, and evangelical churches hold a similar view.
- **更严格的新教看法**：有些福音派教会和牧者认为，马可福音和路加福音没有例外条款，所以离婚的人在前配偶还活着的时候都不应当再婚。<br>
  **A stricter Protestant view**: Some evangelical churches and pastors hold that since Mark and Luke contain no exception, divorced people should not remarry while a former spouse is living.
- **较宽的新教看法**：一些主流新教教会认为，除了奸淫和遗弃，长期的虐待等情况也会实际毁坏婚姻的盟约，在牧者辅导以后可以允许离婚和再婚。<br>
  **A broader Protestant view**: Some mainline Protestant churches hold that besides adultery and desertion, situations such as long-term abuse also effectively destroy the marriage covenant, and after pastoral counsel they may permit divorce and remarriage.

## 关于家庭暴力 | On domestic violence

无论属于哪个传统，各教会都同意：配偶的人身安全受到威胁时，可以离开，可以寻求帮助，也可以报警。天主教也明确允许在这种情况下分居。保持婚姻的教导，不是要人留在危险里。<br>
Whatever their tradition, churches agree that when a spouse's safety is threatened, that person may leave, seek help, and call the police. The Catholic Church also expressly permits separation in such cases. The teaching on keeping marriage intact does not ask anyone to remain in danger.

## 实际的建议 | Practical suggestions

- 如果你准备结婚，很多教会提供婚前辅导，可以认真参加。<br>
  If you are preparing to marry, many churches offer premarital counselling, and it is worth taking seriously.
- 如果你的婚姻遇到困难，尽早寻求牧者和专业婚姻辅导的帮助。<br>
  If your marriage is in difficulty, seek help early from a pastor and a professional marriage counsellor.
- 如果你经历过离婚，或者想知道自己能不能再婚，请向你所在教会的牧者请教，因为各教会的规定差别很大。<br>
  If you have been through divorce, or want to know whether you may remarry, ask the pastor of your church, because churches' rules differ greatly.
