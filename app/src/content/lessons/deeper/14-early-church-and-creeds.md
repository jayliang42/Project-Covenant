## 使徒以后的教会 | The church after the apostles

使徒们去世以后，教会在罗马帝国各地继续增长。在头三百年里，基督徒时常受到逼迫。罗马史学家塔西佗记载，主后 64 年罗马大火以后，尼禄皇帝把罪名加在基督徒身上，残酷地处死了许多人。3 世纪中期的德修皇帝和 4 世纪初的戴克里先皇帝，都发动过大规模的逼迫。<br>
After the apostles died, the church kept growing across the Roman Empire. For its first three centuries, Christians were often persecuted. The Roman historian Tacitus records that after the great fire of Rome in AD 64, the emperor Nero blamed the Christians and had many of them cruelly killed. The emperors Decius in the mid-3rd century and Diocletian in the early 4th century launched large-scale persecutions.

主后 313 年，君士坦丁和李锡尼颁布《米兰敕令》，准许基督徒公开敬拜。从这以后，教会可以公开开会，讨论信仰上的争议。<br>
In AD 313 the emperors Constantine and Licinius issued the Edict of Milan, allowing Christians to worship openly. From then on, the church could meet openly to deal with disputes about the faith.

## 几种不同的说法 | Competing ideas

头几百年里，出现了各种对耶稣的不同说法：<br>
In the first centuries, many different ideas about Jesus appeared:

- **诺斯底派**（2 世纪前后）：认为物质是坏的，得救靠一种秘密的知识。有些人因此说，耶稣没有真的血肉之体，只是看起来像人。<br>
  **Gnostic groups** (around the 2nd century): They held that matter is evil and that salvation comes through secret knowledge. Some therefore said Jesus had no real flesh and only seemed human.
- **马吉安**（2 世纪中期）：认为旧约的神和耶稣的父不是同一位，要把旧约和大部分新约书卷都去掉。<br>
  **Marcion** (mid-2nd century): He taught that the God of the Old Testament was not the Father of Jesus, and wanted to discard the Old Testament and most New Testament books.
- **亚流**（4 世纪初，亚历山大城的长老）：认为子是父所造的最高的受造者，“曾有一个时候没有子”。<br>
  **Arius** (early 4th century, a priest in Alexandria): He taught that the Son was the highest of the Father's creatures, and that “there was when he was not.”

## 几次大公会议 | The great councils

- **尼西亚会议（主后 325 年）**：回应亚流的主张，承认子与父“同质”（homoousios），子和父同样是神，不是受造的。亚他那修是当时为这个信仰辩护最有力的人之一。<br>
  **Nicaea (AD 325)**: Responding to Arius, it confessed that the Son is “of one substance” (homoousios) with the Father, God just as the Father is, and not created. Athanasius was one of the strongest defenders of this faith at the time.
- **君士坦丁堡会议（主后 381 年）**：扩充了尼西亚的信经，特别讲到圣灵是“主，是赐生命的”，与父、子同受敬拜。今天教会所说的《尼西亚信经》，通常就是这个扩充后的版本。<br>
  **Constantinople (AD 381)**: It expanded the Nicene creed, especially concerning the Holy Spirit, “the Lord, the giver of life,” who is worshipped together with the Father and the Son. What churches today call the Nicene Creed is usually this expanded version.
- **迦克墩会议（主后 451 年）**：讨论神性和人性在基督里的关系，定下“一个位格，两个本性”的说法：两性不相混、不改变、不分开、不分离。<br>
  **Chalcedon (AD 451)**: It addressed how the divine and human natures relate in Christ, and stated that he is one person in two natures, “without confusion, without change, without division, without separation.”

东正教、天主教和大多数新教教会都接受这几次会议的结论。也有一些古老的东方教会对迦克墩的说法有不同的表述。<br>
The Orthodox, Catholic, and most Protestant churches accept the conclusions of these councils. Some ancient Eastern churches express the teaching of Chalcedon differently.

## 信经和圣经的关系 | How the creeds relate to Scripture

信经的用词有些不在圣经里，例如“同质”“位格”。教会用这些词，是为了把圣经里的几种说法放在一起讲清楚：“道就是神”（约 1:1），他“本有神的形像”，又“成为人的样式”（腓 2:6-7）。<br>
Some words in the creeds are not in the Bible, such as “of one substance” and “person.” The church used them to hold together several things the Bible says: “the Word was God” (John 1:1), and he existed “in the form of God” yet was “made in the likeness of men” (Philippians 2:6–7).

这段时期，教会也逐渐确认了新约的书目。主后 367 年，亚他那修在一封复活节书信里列出的新约书卷，正好是今天的 27 卷。<br>
In this same period the church also came to a settled list of New Testament books. In AD 367 Athanasius, in an Easter letter, listed exactly the 27 New Testament books used today.

信经不能取代圣经，它们是对圣经要点的总结。《犹大书》说，要“为从前一次交付圣徒的真道竭力地争辩”（犹 1:3）。早期教会写信经，就是在守住这份交付下来的信仰。<br>
The creeds do not replace Scripture. They summarize its main points. Jude urges believers to “contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all delivered to the saints” (Jude 1:3). In writing the creeds, the early church was guarding the faith handed down to it.
