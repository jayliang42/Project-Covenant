## 结构和语言 | Structure and languages

但以理书分成两半：<br>
Daniel falls into two halves:

- **1–6 章：故事**。但以理和三个朋友被掳到巴比伦，在王的宫廷里服事。他们“立志不以王的膳和王所饮的酒玷污自己”（但 1:8）；三个朋友不拜金像，被扔进烈火的窑；但以理在禁令之下照常祷告，被扔进狮子坑。<br>
  **Chapters 1–6: stories.** Daniel and his three friends are taken to Babylon and serve at the king's court. Daniel “purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king's dainties” (Daniel 1:8); the three friends refuse to bow to the golden image and are thrown into the fiery furnace; Daniel keeps praying despite the ban and is thrown into the lions' den.
- **7–12 章：异象**。但以理看见四个巨兽代表的列国，看见“一位像人子的”得着永远的国度，也看见关于末后的异象。<br>
  **Chapters 7–12: visions.** Daniel sees nations represented by four great beasts, sees “one like a son of man” receive an everlasting kingdom, and sees visions of the end.

这卷书用两种语言写成：1:1–2:4 上半和 8–12 章是希伯来文，2:4 下半到 7:28 是亚兰文。语言的分界和故事、异象的分界并不一致。<br>
The book is written in two languages: 1:1–2:4a and chapters 8–12 in Hebrew, and 2:4b–7:28 in Aramaic. The language division does not match the division between stories and visions.

## 什么时候写的 | When it was written

这是旧约研究中争论很多的问题。<br>
This is one of the most debated questions in Old Testament study.

- **传统的看法**：作者是主前 6 世纪被掳到巴比伦的但以理，书写于主前 6 到 5 世纪。持这个看法的学者指出：书中的亚兰文和波斯借词符合较早的时代；死海古卷里有好几份保存良好的但以理书抄本，说明它的原稿要比马加比时代更早；耶稣称他为“先知但以理”（太 24:15）。<br>
  **Traditional view**: The author is Daniel, exiled to Babylon in the sixth century BC, and the book was written in the sixth or fifth century BC. Scholars holding this view point out that the book's Aramaic and Persian loanwords fit an earlier period; that several well-preserved copies of Daniel were found among the Dead Sea Scrolls, which suggests the original is earlier than the Maccabean period; and that Jesus refers to “Daniel the prophet” (Matthew 24:15).
- **批判学者的看法**：大英百科说，批判学者把它定在主前 167–164 年，安提阿古四世逼迫犹太人的时候。理由是：书中对希腊时期的历史描写到安提阿古统治时都很准确，之后就不准确了，看起来是从回顾历史转向预言；它在希伯来圣经里没有列在先知书中；它明确讲到复活（但 12:2）。按这个看法，异象是一种“启示文学”，借古人的名义写成，用来鼓励受逼迫的人。<br>
  **Critical view**: Britannica says critical scholars date it to 167–164 BC, during the persecution of the Jews under Antiochus IV. Their reasons: the book's account of the Greek period is accurate up to Antiochus's reign and then becomes inaccurate, suggesting a shift from history to prediction; it is not placed among the Prophets in the Hebrew Bible; and it clearly speaks of resurrection (Daniel 12:2). On this view the visions are apocalyptic literature written under the name of an ancient figure to encourage the persecuted.

最早提出后一种看法的，是 3 世纪的新柏拉图主义哲学家波菲利（Porphyry），他认为书中的预言其实是事后写成的历史。持守传统的学者认为，这个看法的前提是预言不可能发生。两种看法的人都同意，这卷书是写给在外邦势力下受苦的神的百姓的。<br>
The first to propose the later date was the third-century Neoplatonist philosopher Porphyry, who argued that the book's predictions were really history written after the fact. Scholars holding the traditional view argue that this assumes predictive prophecy is impossible. Both sides agree that the book was written for God's people suffering under foreign powers.

## 主题：在外邦人的国里忠于神 | Theme: faithful to God under foreign rule

但以理和朋友们没有拒绝为巴比伦王服事，他们学习巴比伦的语言和学问，担任官职；但在关键的事上，他们不肯妥协。三个朋友对王说：“我们所事奉的神能将我们从烈火的窑中救出来……即或不然，王啊，你当知道我们决不事奉你的神”（但 3:17-18）。<br>
Daniel and his friends did not refuse to serve the Babylonian king: they learned Babylon's language and learning and held office. But on the points that mattered most they would not compromise. The three friends told the king, “our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace... But if not, let it be known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods” (Daniel 3:17-18).

全书一再说明，地上的国会兴起也会倒下，只有神的国存到永远：“天上的神必另立一国，永不败坏”（但 2:44）。<br>
The book repeatedly shows that earthly kingdoms rise and fall, while God's kingdom stands forever: “the God of heaven will set up a kingdom which will never be destroyed” (Daniel 2:44).

## 人子 | The Son of Man

但以理在异象中看见“一位像人子的，驾着天云而来……得了权柄、荣耀、国度，使各方、各国、各族的人都事奉他”（但 7:13-14）。“人子”是耶稣最常用的自称。在大祭司面前，耶稣说：“你们必看见人子坐在那权能者的右边，驾着天上的云降临”（可 14:62）。大祭司听了，就说他说了僭妄的话。<br>
In a vision Daniel saw “one like a son of man” coming with the clouds, to whom was given “dominion... and glory, and a kingdom, that all the peoples, nations, and languages should serve him” (Daniel 7:13-14). “Son of Man” was Jesus' most common way of referring to himself. Before the high priest Jesus said, “You will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming with the clouds of the sky” (Mark 14:62). The high priest called this blasphemy.

## 读异象的提醒 | A caution on reading the visions

但以理书 7–12 章的异象，各派有很多不同的解释，尤其是七十个七（9 章）和最后几章。有人把它们主要看成已经在古代应验的事，有人认为一部分还要在末后应验。“基督再来和千禧年”一课介绍了几种末世观。读的时候，可以先抓住全书清楚的信息：神掌管历史，他的国必要来到。<br>
The visions of chapters 7–12 are interpreted in many ways, especially the seventy weeks (chapter 9) and the final chapters. Some see them mainly as fulfilled in ancient times; others believe part of them is still to be fulfilled at the end. The lesson on Christ's return and the millennium introduces several views of the end times. As you read, hold first to the book's clear message: God rules history, and his kingdom will come.
