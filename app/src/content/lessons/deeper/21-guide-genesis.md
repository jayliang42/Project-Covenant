## 结构 | Structure

《创世记》共 50 章，可以分成两大部分：<br>
Genesis has 50 chapters and falls into two large parts:

- **1–11 章：人类的开端**。创造、人的堕落、该隐和亚伯、洪水、巴别塔。<br>
  **Chapters 1–11: the beginnings of humanity.** Creation, the fall, Cain and Abel, the flood, and the tower of Babel.
- **12–50 章：以色列先祖的故事**。亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各和约瑟四代人。<br>
  **Chapters 12–50: the stories of Israel's ancestors.** Four generations: Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph.

书里反复出现一句话：“这是……的后代”（希伯来文 toledot），一共出现十次左右，把全书分成几个段落。读的时候可以留意它。<br>
A phrase recurs through the book: “This is the history of the generations of...” (Hebrew toledot). It appears about ten times and divides the book into sections. It is worth watching for as you read.

## 主线 | The main thread

**创造是好的**。神造了世界，每一天结束时都说“是好的”。人是神“照着自己的形像”造的（创 1:27），男人和女人都是。这是圣经讲人的尊严的根据。<br>
**Creation is good.** God makes the world and at the end of each day declares it good. Human beings are made “in God’s image” (Genesis 1:27), male and female alike. This is the Bible's ground for human dignity.

**罪进入世界，越来越重**。人不听神的话（3 章），接着是兄弟相杀（4 章），然后是“地上满了强暴”（6 章），最后人联合起来要“传扬我们的名”（11 章）。每一次神都审判，也每一次留下恩典：给亚当夏娃皮衣，给该隐记号，保全挪亚一家。<br>
**Sin enters the world and grows worse.** Humans disobey God (chapter 3), brother kills brother (chapter 4), the earth is “filled with violence” (chapter 6), and at last people unite to “make a name for ourselves” (chapter 11). Each time God judges, and each time he leaves grace: garments of skin for Adam and Eve, a mark for Cain, rescue for Noah's family.

**神的回应是一个应许**。第 12 章，神呼召亚伯拉罕：“地上的万族都要因你得福”（创 12:3）。从这里起，圣经的故事集中在一个家族身上，目的却是整个世界。<br>
**God's response is a promise.** In chapter 12 God calls Abraham: “All the families of the earth will be blessed through you” (Genesis 12:3). From here the Bible's story narrows to one family, and its aim remains the whole world.

**神借着软弱的人成就应许**。先祖们常常说谎、偏心、彼此欺骗。神仍然信守应许。全书结尾，约瑟对出卖他的哥哥们说：“从前你们的意思是要害我，但神的意思原是好的”（创 50:20）。<br>
**God keeps his promise through flawed people.** The ancestors often lie, play favourites, and deceive one another. God keeps his promise anyway. At the end of the book Joseph says to the brothers who sold him, “you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good” (Genesis 50:20).

## 谁写的 | Who wrote it

书里没有写作者的名字。犹太教和早期教会的传统认为，五经（创世记到申命记）出于摩西。<br>
The book does not name its author. Jewish and early Christian tradition attributed the five books of Moses, Genesis to Deuteronomy, to Moses.

19 世纪，德国学者威尔浩森把当时的研究整理成“底本假说”，认为五经是由四份不同时期的文献（简称 J、E、D、P）编在一起的，最早的在主前 9 世纪，最晚的在主前 5 世纪。这个理论在 20 世纪的学界影响很大。1970 年代以后，学者对它争论很多，有人修正它，有人提出别的模型，目前学界没有共识。<br>
In the 19th century the German scholar Julius Wellhausen drew earlier research together into the documentary hypothesis. It holds that the Pentateuch was compiled from four documents of different periods, labelled J, E, D, and P, the earliest from the 9th century BC and the latest from the 5th. The theory was highly influential in 20th-century scholarship. Since the 1970s it has been much debated: some scholars revise it and others propose different models, and there is now no consensus.

信徒的看法也不一样。很多福音派学者认为摩西是五经的主要作者，后人做过少量整理，例如申命记 34 章记载摩西的死。也有不少信徒学者接受五经经过较长时间编成的看法，同时认为它是神默示的。各方都同意，这卷书现在的样子就是神给教会的圣经。<br>
Believers differ as well. Many evangelical scholars hold that Moses was the principal author, with some later editing, such as the account of Moses' death in Deuteronomy 34. A good number of believing scholars accept that the Pentateuch was compiled over a longer period and still hold it to be inspired by God. All agree that the book in its present form is the Scripture God has given the church.

## 第 1 章的“六日” | The “six days” of chapter 1

创世记 1 章怎样和科学对宇宙年龄的估计放在一起，基督徒有几种理解。神学家 Vern Poythress 列出了十种，常见的有：<br>
Christians understand in several ways how Genesis 1 relates to scientific estimates of the age of the universe. The theologian Vern Poythress lists ten views. Common ones include:

- **年轻地球说**：六日是普通的日子，地球大约几千年。<br>
  **Young earth**: The six days are ordinary days and the earth is a few thousand years old.
- **日代说**：“日”指很长的时期，大致对应地质年代。<br>
  **Day-age**: The “days” are long periods, roughly matching geological ages.
- **框架说**：第 1 章是一个文学结构，前三天讲神造出空间，后三天讲神用万物充满这些空间，并不打算交代先后次序。<br>
  **Framework**: Chapter 1 is a literary structure, with the first three days forming realms and the last three filling them, and it does not intend to give a sequence.
- **类比日说**：“日”是按人一周工作和安息的样式来描述神的工作。<br>
  **Analogical days**: The “days” describe God's work by analogy with the human working week and Sabbath.

天主教会不反对宇宙和生命经过漫长时间形成的科学看法。1996 年，教宗约翰·保禄二世说进化论“不仅是一种假说”，同时坚持人的灵魂直接由神所造。福音派内部这几种看法都有人持守。各方都同意第 1 章的中心：只有一位神，他创造了万有，万有都是好的，人有神的形像。<br>
The Catholic Church does not oppose the scientific view that the universe and life developed over long ages. In 1996 Pope John Paul II said that evolution is “more than a hypothesis,” while holding that each human soul is created directly by God. Within evangelicalism each of these views has its holders. All sides agree on the centre of chapter 1: there is one God, he made all things, all things are good, and humans bear his image.
