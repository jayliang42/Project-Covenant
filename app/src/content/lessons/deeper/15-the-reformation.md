## 改革以前 | Before the Reformation

中世纪后期，西方教会出现了许多问题：一些神职人员腐败，平信徒大多读不到圣经，弥撒和圣经都用拉丁文，很多人听不懂。14 世纪英国的威克里夫推动把圣经译成英文，15 世纪初波希米亚的胡斯批评教会的腐败，1415 年被判为异端烧死。<br>
In the late Middle Ages the Western church had many problems: some clergy were corrupt, most lay people had no access to the Bible, and Mass and Scripture were in Latin, which many could not understand. In 14th-century England, John Wycliffe promoted translating the Bible into English. In early 15th-century Bohemia, Jan Hus criticized the church's corruption and was burned as a heretic in 1415.

15 世纪中期，古腾堡在德国用活字印刷术印出了圣经。印刷让书籍和小册子能很快传开，这对后来的改革影响很大。<br>
In the mid-15th century, Gutenberg printed the Bible in Germany using movable type. Printing let books and pamphlets spread quickly, which mattered greatly for the Reformation.

## 路德和《九十五条论纲》 | Luther and the Ninety-five Theses

马丁·路德是德国的修士和大学教授。他很长时间里为自己的罪感到痛苦，后来在研读《罗马书》时明白了“义人必因信得生”（罗 1:17）的意思：人在神面前被称为义，是神白白赐下的，人用信心领受。<br>
Martin Luther was a German monk and university professor. For a long time he was tormented by his sins. While studying Romans, he came to understand “the righteous shall live by faith” (Romans 1:17): being counted righteous before God is God's free gift, received by faith.

1517 年，他发表《九十五条论纲》，反对出售赎罪券。争论很快扩大。1521 年，他在沃木斯帝国议会上拒绝收回自己的著作，说除非用圣经和明白的道理说服他。他把新约译成德文，1522 年出版。<br>
In 1517 he published his Ninety-five Theses against the sale of indulgences. The debate spread quickly. In 1521, at the Diet of Worms, he refused to take back his writings unless he was convinced by Scripture and plain reason. He translated the New Testament into German and published it in 1522.

## 几个改教家关心的问题 | What the Reformers cared about

改教运动很快分成几支：瑞士苏黎世的慈运理、日内瓦的加尔文、主张信徒受洗的重洗派，还有英国的改革。他们彼此之间也有分歧，例如对圣餐的理解。后人常用几个“唯独”来概括他们共同的重点：<br>
The movement soon branched out: Zwingli in Zurich, Calvin in Geneva, the Anabaptists who practised believer's baptism, and the Reformation in England. They also disagreed among themselves, for example about the Lord's Supper. Later writers often sum up their shared emphases with several “alone” phrases:

- **唯独圣经**：圣经是信仰和生活最高的权威，教会的传统要放在圣经之下检验。<br>
  **Scripture alone**: The Bible is the highest authority for faith and life, and church tradition must be tested by it.
- **唯独恩典，唯独信心**：人得救是因为神的恩典，用信心领受，不靠人的功德。<br>
  **Grace alone, faith alone**: People are saved by God's grace, received through faith, not by human merit.
- **唯独基督**：基督是神和人之间唯一的中保（提前 2:5）。<br>
  **Christ alone**: Christ is the one mediator between God and people (1 Timothy 2:5).
- **信徒皆祭司**：每个信徒都可以借着基督直接来到神面前，日常的工作也可以是对神的事奉。<br>
  **The priesthood of all believers**: Every believer can come directly to God through Christ, and ordinary work can be service to God.

改教家还把圣经译成各国的语言，例如丁道尔 1526 年出版的英文新约。<br>
The Reformers also translated the Bible into the languages of the people, such as William Tyndale's English New Testament of 1526.

## 天主教的回应和今天的看法 | The Catholic response and views today

天主教会召开了特兰托会议（1545–1563），一方面整顿教会内部的问题，一方面明确拒绝新教关于称义的一些说法。天主教内部也有改革运动，例如 1540 年成立的耶稣会。<br>
The Catholic Church held the Council of Trent (1545–1563), which addressed problems within the church and also explicitly rejected some Protestant teaching on justification. There were reform movements within Catholicism as well, such as the Jesuits, founded in 1540.

几百年后，双方的关系有了一些变化。1999 年，信义宗世界联会和天主教会签署了《关于称义教义的联合声明》，表示在称义的基本真理上有共识，同时承认仍有分歧。<br>
Centuries later, relations changed somewhat. In 1999 the Lutheran World Federation and the Catholic Church signed the Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification, stating a consensus on basic truths of justification while acknowledging remaining differences.

今天的新教徒、天主教徒和东正教徒对这段历史有不同的评价。读这段历史，可以先弄清各方当时关心什么，再回到圣经本身去看。<br>
Protestants, Catholics, and Orthodox Christians today judge this history differently. When you read it, first work out what each side cared about at the time, and then go back to Scripture itself.
