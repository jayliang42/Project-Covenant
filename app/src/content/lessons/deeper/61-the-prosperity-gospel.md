## 什么是成功神学 | What the prosperity gospel is

按大英百科的介绍，成功神学是一种新教的教导，认为信心通过积极的思想、积极的言语和向教会奉献表现出来，就会给信徒带来健康、财富和快乐；疾病和贫穷被看作咒诅，可以靠着信靠耶稣打破。它也被称为“健康财富福音”。<br>
According to Britannica, the prosperity gospel is a Protestant teaching that faith, expressed through positive thinking, positive speech, and donations to the church, brings believers health, wealth, and happiness; illness and poverty are seen as curses that faith in Jesus can break. It is also called the “health and wealth gospel.”

## 来历 | Origins

大英百科说，这种教导吸收了 20 世纪初“新思想运动”关于积极思考的观念，后来在美国五旬节派中发展起来。肯扬（E. W. Kenyon）和哈金（Kenneth Hagin）推广“积极宣告”，就是用口说出想要的祝福；电视布道家奥若·罗伯茨（Oral Roberts）提倡“种子信心”，教导人把钱奉献给他的事工，会以七倍回到奉献者身上。这种教导后来通过电视布道和大型教会传到非洲、亚洲和拉丁美洲。<br>
Britannica says this teaching drew on early-twentieth-century New Thought ideas about positive thinking and developed among American Pentecostals. E. W. Kenyon and Kenneth Hagin promoted “positive confession,” speaking aloud the blessings one wants; the televangelist Oral Roberts preached “seed faith,” teaching that money given to his ministry would return to the donor sevenfold. Through televangelism and megachurches the teaching spread to Africa, Asia, and Latin America.

皮尤研究中心 2006 年对 10 个国家的调查发现，其中 9 个国家的多数五旬节派信徒同意“神会赐物质上的富足给所有信心够大的信徒”。<br>
A 2006 Pew Research Center survey of ten countries found that in nine of them most Pentecostals agreed that God will grant material prosperity to all believers who have enough faith.

## 常用的经文 | Favourite verses

支持者常引用：“我愿你凡事兴盛，身体健壮，正如你的灵魂兴盛一样”（约叁 2）；“以此试试我，是否为你们敞开天上的窗户，倾福与你们”（玛 3:10）；“少种的少收，多种的多收”（林后 9:6）。<br>
Supporters often cite: “I pray that you may prosper in all things and be healthy, even as your soul prospers” (3 John 2); “test me now in this... if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing” (Malachi 3:10); “he who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly” (2 Corinthians 9:6).

批评者指出：约翰三书 2 节是书信开头常见的问候；玛拉基书 3 章是对以色列不纳十分之一的责备，写给旧约下的以色列民；哥林多后书 9 章讲的是为耶路撒冷穷困的信徒捐献，保罗接着说神使人富足，是为了让人“多多施舍”（林后 9:11）。<br>
Critics point out that 3 John 2 is a customary greeting at the start of a letter; Malachi 3 rebukes Israel for withholding tithes and is addressed to Israel under the old covenant; and 2 Corinthians 9 concerns a collection for poor believers in Jerusalem, where Paul goes on to say that God enriches people so that they can be generous (2 Corinthians 9:11).

## 各方的批评 | Criticisms

大英百科说，一些新教徒和天主教徒批评成功神学扭曲了信仰：把神的恩宠和财富绑在一起，把人的不幸归咎于信心不足，还可能剥削贫穷和脆弱的人。<br>
Britannica says some Protestants and Catholics criticise the prosperity gospel for distorting the faith: tying God's favour to wealth, blaming people's misfortunes on a lack of faith, and potentially exploiting the poor and vulnerable.

2008 年和 2009 年，洛桑神学工作组的非洲分组在加纳的阿克罗蓬开会，发表关于成功神学的声明。声明肯定圣经所说的神的赐福包括物质上的福祉，也肯定勤劳和善用神所给的才能；但拒绝以下的说法：神的能力可以被人的话语、行为或仪式自动地启动；财富总是神赐福的记号；贫穷或疾病总是神的咒诅或信心不足的记号；用“奉献金钱”取代传统的悔改和信靠的呼召。<br>
In 2008 and 2009 the Africa chapter of the Lausanne Theology Working Group met in Akropong, Ghana, and issued a statement on prosperity teaching. It affirms that God's blessing in the Bible includes material welfare, and affirms hard work and the good use of God-given abilities. It rejects the ideas that God's power can be triggered automatically by human words, actions, or rituals; that wealth is always a sign of God's blessing; that poverty or illness is always a sign of God's curse or lack of faith; and that a call to give money can replace the call to repentance and faith.

## 圣经对金钱和受苦的整体教导 | The Bible's wider teaching

- 神确实赐福，也供应人的需要：“得货财的力量是他给你的”（申 8:18）。<br>
  God does bless and provide: “it is he who gives you power to get wealth” (Deuteronomy 8:18).
- 圣经也警告追求财富的危险：“敬虔加上知足的心便是大利了……那些想要发财的人，就陷在迷惑、落在网罗”（提前 6:6-9）；“人的生命不在乎家道丰富”（路 12:15）。<br>
  The Bible also warns of the danger of pursuing wealth: “godliness with contentment is great gain... those who are determined to be rich fall into a temptation, a snare” (1 Timothy 6:6-9); “a man's life doesn't consist of the abundance of the things which he possesses” (Luke 12:15).
- 保罗学会了在缺乏和丰富中都知足（腓 4:11-12）。希伯来书 11 章有很多有信心的人“受穷乏、患难、苦害”（来 11:37）。<br>
  Paul learned to be content in both want and plenty (Philippians 4:11-12). Hebrews 11 lists many people of faith who were “destitute, afflicted, ill-treated” (Hebrews 11:37).
- 福音的中心是基督“本来富足，却为你们成了贫穷，叫你们因他的贫穷，可以成为富足”（林后 8:9）。<br>
  At the centre of the gospel is Christ, who “though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor, that you through his poverty might become rich” (2 Corinthians 8:9).

“刚开始信”路径里“金钱和奉献”一课，讲了各传统对十一奉献的不同看法。<br>
The lesson on money and giving in the New Believer path introduces the traditions' different views on the tithe.
