## 背景 | Background

以赛亚在主前 8 世纪后半叶的耶路撒冷作先知，经历了乌西雅、约坦、亚哈斯、希西家四位犹大王（赛 1:1）。那时北方的亚述帝国正在扩张，北国以色列在主前 722 年被亚述灭亡，南国犹大也受到威胁。<br>
Isaiah prophesied in Jerusalem in the second half of the 8th century BC, under four kings of Judah: Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah (Isaiah 1:1). The Assyrian empire to the north was expanding. The northern kingdom of Israel fell to Assyria in 722 BC, and the southern kingdom of Judah was under threat.

## 三个部分 | Three parts

全书 66 章，通常分成三部分：<br>
The book's 66 chapters are usually divided into three parts:

- **1–39 章：审判和盼望**。责备犹大的罪，宣告列国的审判，其中夹着关于一位要来的君王的应许。36–39 章是历史记载：亚述围攻耶路撒冷，神拯救了这城；最后以赛亚预言犹大将来要被掳到巴比伦。<br>
  **Chapters 1–39: judgment and hope.** Rebukes of Judah's sin and oracles against the nations, with promises of a coming king woven in. Chapters 36–39 are narrative: Assyria besieges Jerusalem and God delivers the city, and at the end Isaiah foretells that Judah will be carried off to Babylon.
- **40–55 章：安慰**。开头是“你们要安慰，安慰我的百姓”（赛 40:1）。背景是被掳到巴比伦的人，神应许要领他们回家，并点名说波斯王古列（和合本译作“塞鲁士”）要下令重建耶路撒冷（赛 44:28）。这一段有四首“仆人之歌”，53 章是其中最后一首。<br>
  **Chapters 40–55: comfort.** It opens, “Comfort, comfort my people” (Isaiah 40:1). The setting is the exiles in Babylon. God promises to bring them home and names Cyrus, king of Persia, as the one who will order Jerusalem rebuilt (Isaiah 44:28). This section contains four “Servant Songs,” of which chapter 53 is the last.
- **56–66 章：新的百姓和新天新地**。讲回归以后的群体，神的救恩临到外邦人，最后应许“我造新天新地”（赛 65:17）。<br>
  **Chapters 56–66: a new people and new heavens and earth.** These chapters concern the community after the return, God's salvation reaching the nations, and finally the promise, “I create new heavens and a new earth” (Isaiah 65:17).

## 几段重要的经文 | Key passages

- **第 6 章，以赛亚蒙召**。他看见神坐在高高的宝座上，撒拉弗彼此呼喊：“圣哉！圣哉！圣哉！万军之耶和华”（赛 6:3）。“以色列的圣者”这个称呼在全书出现了二十多次。<br>
  **Chapter 6, Isaiah's call.** He sees the Lord on a high throne, and seraphim call to one another, “Holy, holy, holy, is Yahweh of Armies!” (Isaiah 6:3). The title “the Holy One of Israel” appears more than twenty times across the book.
- **7:14 和 9:6，一个孩子**。“必有童女怀孕生子，给他起名叫以马内利”，以及“因有一婴孩为我们而生……他名称为奇妙策士、全能的神”。马太福音用 7:14 讲耶稣的降生。<br>
  **7:14 and 9:6, a child.** “The virgin will conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel,” and “a child is born to us... His name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God.” Matthew applies 7:14 to the birth of Jesus.
- **第 53 章，受苦的仆人**。“他为我们的过犯受害，为我们的罪孽压伤……耶和华使我们众人的罪孽都归在他身上”（赛 53:5-6）。使徒行传 8 章里，腓利向埃提阿伯太监讲的就是这一段，他“从这经上起，对他传讲耶稣”。<br>
  **Chapter 53, the suffering Servant.** “He was pierced for our transgressions. He was crushed for our iniquities... Yahweh has laid on him the iniquity of us all” (Isaiah 53:5-6). In Acts 8 this is the passage Philip explains to the Ethiopian official, and “beginning from this Scripture, preached to him about Jesus.”

## “受苦的仆人”是谁 | Who is the suffering Servant?

中世纪以来的犹太教解释多数认为，这位仆人指以色列民族，他们为列国受苦。也有古代犹太解经家认为指弥赛亚。基督徒从新约开始就把这段经文读作关于耶稣的预言。<br>
Since the Middle Ages most Jewish interpretation has taken the Servant to be the people of Israel, suffering for the nations. Some ancient Jewish interpreters read it of the Messiah. Christians, from the New Testament on, have read the passage as a prophecy of Jesus.

以赛亚书自己也称以色列为“我的仆人”（赛 41:8）。很多基督徒学者认为两种读法有连接：以色列本应作神的仆人，却没有做到；耶稣作为以色列的代表，完成了这个使命。<br>
Isaiah itself calls Israel “my servant” (Isaiah 41:8). Many Christian scholars see a link between the readings: Israel was meant to be God's servant and failed, and Jesus, as Israel's representative, fulfilled the calling.

## 作者的问题 | The question of authorship

18 世纪后期开始，许多学者提出，40–55 章（常称“第二以赛亚”）出于被掳时期一位不知名的先知，56–66 章（“第三以赛亚”）出于回归以后的作者。理由包括：40 章以后的背景是巴比伦和波斯，直接提到古列的名字，文风也和前半部分不同。今天大多数学术注释书采用这种看法。<br>
From the late 18th century many scholars proposed that chapters 40–55 (often called “Second Isaiah”) come from an unnamed prophet of the exile, and chapters 56–66 (“Third Isaiah”) from writers after the return. Their reasons include the setting of Babylon and Persia from chapter 40 on, the naming of Cyrus, and a style different from the first half. Most academic commentaries today take this view.

另一些学者，特别是保守的福音派学者，认为全书出于以赛亚。理由包括：犹太传统和新约引用两部分时都称“以赛亚”，例如约翰福音 12:38-41 连着引用了 53 章和 6 章；“以色列的圣者”等用语贯穿全书；主前 125 年前后的以赛亚书大卷在 39 章和 40 章之间没有任何分隔。他们也认为，神能借着先知预先说出古列的名字。<br>
Other scholars, especially conservative evangelicals, hold that the whole book comes from Isaiah. Their reasons include that Jewish tradition and the New Testament name Isaiah when quoting both parts (John 12:38-41, for example, quotes chapter 53 and chapter 6 together), that terms such as “the Holy One of Israel” run through the whole book, and that the Great Isaiah Scroll of about 125 BC shows no break between chapters 39 and 40. They also hold that God could name Cyrus in advance through the prophet.

两种看法都把以赛亚书读作一部完整的书。最后编成的书卷有一个清楚的走向：从审判到安慰，再到新天新地。<br>
Both views read Isaiah as a single book. In its final form the book has a clear direction: from judgment, to comfort, to new heavens and a new earth.
