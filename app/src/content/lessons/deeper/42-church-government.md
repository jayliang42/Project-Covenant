## 三种基本的方式 | Three basic forms

新约里有长老、监督、执事等职分，但没有一份完整的“教会章程”。后来的教会按照对经文和历史的不同理解，形成了三种主要的治理方式。<br>
The New Testament has offices such as elder, overseer, and deacon, but it gives no complete church constitution. Later churches, reading Scripture and history differently, developed three main forms of government.

### 主教制 | Episcopal

权柄在主教的职分。主教管理一个地区的许多教会，按立神父或牧师。很多主教制的教会相信“使徒统绪”，就是主教的职分从使徒一代一代传下来。<br>
Authority rests in the office of bishop. A bishop oversees many churches in a region and ordains priests or ministers. Many episcopal churches hold to “apostolic succession,” the belief that the office of bishop has been handed down from the apostles generation by generation.

- 采用的教会：天主教、东正教、圣公会，循道宗的一部分，以及一些信义宗教会，例如美国福音信义会在 1988 年起设立主教。<br>
  Churches that use it: Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, part of the Methodist tradition, and some Lutheran churches; the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, for example, has had bishops since 1988.
- 常引用的根据：提摩太和提多受保罗差派，在各城设立长老（多 1:5），似乎有高于地方长老的职责；2 世纪初安提阿的伊格纳丢的书信里，已经有一位主教带领长老和执事的做法。<br>
  Grounds often cited: Timothy and Titus, sent by Paul, appointed elders in every city (Titus 1:5), which suggests a role above the local elders; and in the letters of Ignatius of Antioch in the early second century a single bishop already leads the presbyters and deacons.

### 长老制 | Presbyterian

权柄由长老共同承担。长老由会众选出，牧师（教导长老）和治理长老一同组成堂会；几个堂会的代表组成区会（presbytery），再往上有大会。上一级的会议有纪律的权柄，也可以受理上诉。<br>
Authority is shared by elders. Elders are elected by the congregation, and the minister (a teaching elder) and the ruling elders together form the session. Representatives of several sessions form a presbytery, and above that are higher assemblies. The higher bodies hold disciplinary authority and hear appeals.

- 采用的教会：苏格兰的长老会、北美各长老会，以及许多改革宗教会。<br>
  Churches that use it: the Presbyterian Church in Scotland, Presbyterian churches in North America, and many Reformed churches.
- 常引用的根据：保罗和巴拿巴“在各教会中选立了长老”（徒 14:23）；新约里“长老”和“监督”常指同一群人（徒 20:17、28）；耶路撒冷会议由“使徒和长老”一同商议，作出的决定各教会都接受（徒 15 章）。<br>
  Grounds often cited: Paul and Barnabas “appointed elders for them in every assembly” (Acts 14:23); in the New Testament “elder” and “overseer” often refer to the same people (Acts 20:17, 28); and at the Jerusalem council “the apostles and the elders” deliberated together and their decision was received by the churches (Acts 15).

### 会众制 | Congregational

每个地方教会自己决定教导、敬拜和事务，也自己执行教会纪律，不受上级机构管辖。各教会可以组成联会彼此合作，但联会没有管辖权。<br>
Each local church decides its own teaching, worship, and affairs, and carries out church discipline itself, with no higher body over it. Churches may form associations to work together, but these have no authority over them.

- 采用的教会：公理会（新英格兰是它的代表地区）、大多数浸信会、许多五旬节派和独立教会，以及贵格会。<br>
  Churches that use it: Congregationalists (New England is its classic home), most Baptists, many Pentecostal and independent churches, and the Society of Friends.
- 常引用的根据：耶稣说有人不听劝，就“告诉教会”（太 18:17）；耶路撒冷教会由会众选出七个人来管理饭食（徒 6:3）；保罗的书信多数写给全体会众。<br>
  Grounds often cited: Jesus said that if someone will not listen, “tell it to the assembly” (Matthew 18:17); the Jerusalem church chose seven men from among themselves to oversee the food distribution (Acts 6:3); and most of Paul's letters are addressed to the whole congregation.

## 实际上常常是混合的 | In practice, often mixed

很多教会是这几种方式的混合。例如不少浸信会和独立教会有一个长老团带领，重大的事由会员大会决定。你可以了解一下自己的教会是怎样安排的，哪些事由谁决定。<br>
Many churches mix these forms. Many Baptist and independent churches, for example, are led by a body of elders while major matters are decided by a members' meeting. It is worth finding out how your own church is arranged and who decides what.

不管哪一种制度，新约对领袖的要求是一样的：“务要牧养在你们中间神的群羊……不是辖制所托付你们的，乃是作群羊的榜样”（彼前 5:2-3）。<br>
Whatever the system, the New Testament asks the same of leaders: “Shepherd the flock of God which is among you... not as lording it over those entrusted to you, but making yourselves examples to the flock” (1 Peter 5:2-3).
