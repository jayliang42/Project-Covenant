## 城市里的教会 | Churches in cities

保罗的旅程大多沿着罗马的大路和海路，从一个城市到另一个城市。他建立的教会在腓立比、帖撒罗尼迦、哥林多、以弗所这样的城市里。这些城市有罗马官员、商人、工匠、奴隶，也有各种神庙和宗教活动。<br>
Paul's journeys mostly followed Roman roads and sea routes from one city to the next. The churches he planted were in cities such as Philippi, Thessalonica, Corinth, and Ephesus. These cities had Roman officials, merchants, craftsmen, and slaves, and many temples and religious activities.

## 三座城市 | Three cities

**腓立比**：路加说它是“马其顿这一方的头一个城，也是罗马的驻防城”（徒 16:12）。“驻防城”是罗马的殖民地，住着很多退伍军人，居民以拥有罗马公民身份为荣。保罗在这里说出自己是罗马人（徒 16:37）。后来他写信给腓立比教会说：“我们却是天上的国民”（腓 3:20）。<br>
**Philippi**: Luke calls it “a city of Macedonia, the foremost of the district, a Roman colony” (Acts 16:12). As a Roman colony it had many retired soldiers, and its people were proud of their Roman citizenship. Paul revealed here that he was a Roman citizen (Acts 16:37). Later he wrote to the church at Philippi, “Our citizenship is in heaven” (Philippians 3:20).

**哥林多**：主前 146 年被罗马摧毁，主前 44 年由凯撒重建为罗马殖民地。它控制两个港口，商业发达，人口来自各地。在德尔斐发现的一块铭文提到迦流（Gallio）任亚该亚方伯，学者据此推定他的任期约在主后 51–52 年。《使徒行传》18:12 说保罗就是在这时被带到迦流面前。<br>
**Corinth**: Destroyed by Rome in 146 BC, it was refounded as a Roman colony by Julius Caesar in 44 BC. It controlled two harbours, had busy trade, and drew people from everywhere. An inscription found at Delphi mentions Gallio as proconsul of Achaia, and scholars use it to date his term to about AD 51–52. Acts 18:12 says this is when Paul was brought before Gallio.

**以弗所**：亚细亚省的大城，有著名的亚底米（Artemis）神庙，是古代世界七大奇观之一。做亚底米神龛的银匠担心生意受损，煽动群众涌进剧场，喊着“大哉，以弗所人的亚底米啊！”（徒 19:28-29）。这座剧场的遗址今天还在。<br>
**Ephesus**: A great city of the province of Asia, home to the famous temple of Artemis, one of the seven wonders of the ancient world. Silversmiths who made shrines of Artemis feared for their business and stirred up a crowd that rushed into the theatre shouting, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” (Acts 19:28–29). The remains of that theatre can still be seen today.

## 社会处境怎样影响书信 | How the social setting shapes the letters

- **家庭聚会**：早期教会在信徒家里聚会（罗 16:5）。主人往往是比较有钱的人，奴隶和穷人也在同一个屋子里。<br>
  **House churches**: The early church met in believers' homes (Romans 16:5). The host was usually better off, while slaves and the poor were in the same room.
- **阶层的张力**：哥林多教会吃主的晚餐时，有钱的人先吃，穷人挨饿。保罗责备说：“还是藐视神的教会，叫那没有的羞愧呢？”（林前 11:22）。<br>
  **Class tensions**: When the Corinthian church ate the Lord's Supper, the wealthy ate first and the poor went hungry. Paul rebuked them: “Or do you despise God’s assembly and put them to shame who don’t have enough?” (1 Corinthians 11:22).
- **新的身份**：在一个重视身份、等级的社会里，保罗说：“并不分犹太人、希腊人，自主的、为奴的，或男或女，因为你们在基督耶稣里都成为一了”（加 3:28）。他请腓利门接纳奴隶阿尼西母（一般认为他是从腓利门家逃走的），“不再是奴仆，乃是高过奴仆，是亲爱的兄弟”（门 1:16）。<br>
  **A new identity**: In a society that cared a great deal about status and rank, Paul wrote, “There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free man, there is neither male nor female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus” (Galatians 3:28). He asked Philemon to receive the slave Onesimus (usually thought to have run away from Philemon's household) “no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, a beloved brother” (Philemon 1:16).
- **偶像和市场**：城市里卖的肉很多先献过偶像。哥林多信徒问能不能吃，保罗在《哥林多前书》8–10 章回答这个问题。<br>
  **Idols and the market**: Much of the meat sold in these cities had first been offered to idols. The Corinthians asked whether they could eat it, and Paul answers in 1 Corinthians 8–10.

了解这些背景，读书信时就更容易看出，保罗在对真实的人、真实的问题说话。<br>
With this background in mind, it is easier to see that Paul was speaking to real people about real problems.
