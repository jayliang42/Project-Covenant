## 旧约的安息日 | The Sabbath in the Old Testament

十诫的第四条（按路德和天主教的算法是第三条）说：“当记念安息日，守为圣日。六日要劳碌做你一切的工，但第七日是向耶和华你神当守的安息日”（出 20:8-10）。出埃及记的理由是创造：神六日造天地，第七日安息。申命记的理由是救赎：“你也要记念你在埃及地作过奴仆”（申 5:15）。安息日的休息包括儿女、仆婢、牲畜和寄居的人。<br>
The fourth commandment (the third in Lutheran and Catholic numbering) says, “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. You shall labor six days, and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to Yahweh your God” (Exodus 20:8-10). Exodus grounds it in creation: God made the world in six days and rested on the seventh. Deuteronomy grounds it in redemption: “You shall remember that you were a servant in the land of Egypt” (Deuteronomy 5:15). The Sabbath rest extended to children, servants, animals, and foreigners.

## 新约怎样讲 | What the New Testament says

耶稣常在安息日医病，和法利赛人发生争论。他说：“安息日是为人设立的，人不是为安息日设立的。所以，人子也是安息日的主”（可 2:27-28）。<br>
Jesus often healed on the Sabbath and clashed with the Pharisees over it. He said, “The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath. Therefore the Son of Man is lord even of the Sabbath” (Mark 2:27-28).

早期信徒在“七日的第一日”，就是耶稣复活的日子聚会（徒 20:7；林前 16:2），启示录称之为“主日”（启 1:10）。保罗说，在“节期、月朔、安息日”上不可让人论断你们，“这些原是后事的影儿；那形体却是基督”（西 2:16-17）。希伯来书说：“必另有一安息日的安息为神的子民存留”（来 4:9）。<br>
The early believers gathered on “the first day of the week,” the day Jesus rose (Acts 20:7; 1 Corinthians 16:2), which Revelation calls “the Lord's day” (Revelation 1:10). Paul says not to let anyone judge you “with respect to a feast day or a new moon or a Sabbath day, which are a shadow of the things to come; but the body is Christ's” (Colossians 2:16-17). Hebrews says, “There remains therefore a Sabbath rest for the people of God” (Hebrews 4:9).

这些经文怎样放在一起理解，各传统的看法不同。<br>
The traditions put these passages together in different ways.

## 几种看法 | Several views

- **改革宗和清教徒传统（“基督徒安息日”）**：《威斯敏斯德信条》第 21 章说，从基督复活起，安息日由一周的最后一日改为第一日，称为“主日”，一直到世界的末了。守主日要预先安排好日常的事，停下自己的工作、言语，以及关于工作和娱乐的思想，把整日用在公开和私下的敬拜上，以及必需和怜悯的事上。<br>
  **Reformed and Puritan (the “Christian Sabbath”)**: Chapter 21 of the Westminster Confession says that from Christ's resurrection the Sabbath was changed from the last day of the week to the first, called “the Lord's Day,” to continue to the end of the world. It is kept by arranging ordinary affairs beforehand, resting from one's own work, words, and thoughts about employment and recreation, and giving the whole day to public and private worship and to duties of necessity and mercy.
- **信义宗**：路德在《大教理问答》里说，守某一天、停止劳动的外在规定是“只给犹太人的”，基督徒在基督里得了自由。但这条诫命的目的仍然适用：劳动的人需要休息，人更需要聚集听神的话。路德说，使一天成为圣日的是神的话；基督徒其实每天都应当守这样的“圣日”，只是多数人做不到，所以需要定一天。<br>
  **Lutheran**: In the Large Catechism Luther says that the outward rule of keeping a particular day and resting from labour was “given to the Jews alone,” and Christians have been made free in Christ. Yet the purpose of the commandment still applies: workers need rest, and above all people need to gather to hear God's word. Luther says it is God's word that makes a day holy. Christians ought to keep such a holy day every day, but since most cannot, a set day is needed.
- **天主教**：主日是“主的日子”，信徒在主日和其他当守的庆节“有义务参与弥撒”；《天主教教理》第 2181 条说，故意不尽这个义务的人犯了重罪。教会也鼓励在主日休息，不做妨碍敬拜和休息的工作。<br>
  **Catholic**: Sunday is the Lord's Day. On Sundays and other holy days of obligation “the faithful are bound to participate in the Mass.” Paragraph 2181 of the Catechism says that “those who deliberately fail in this obligation commit a grave sin.” The Church also encourages rest on Sunday, avoiding work that hinders worship and rest.
- **基督复临安息日会和其他守第七日的教会**：认为第四条诫命仍然有效，安息日仍是一周的第七日，从星期五日落到星期六日落，是“创造的记念”和神与他子民之约的“永久记号”。<br>
  **Seventh-day Adventists and other seventh-day churches**: They hold that the fourth commandment remains in force and that the Sabbath is still the seventh day, from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday, a “memorial of Creation” and a “perpetual sign” of God's covenant with his people.
- **许多福音派和新约神学的学者**：认为安息日在基督里成全，信徒在基督里得到真安息（来 4 章）；主日是聚会敬拜的日子，但不必按旧约的方式停工。休息的原则仍然值得遵守。<br>
  **Many evangelicals and New Covenant Theology scholars**: They hold that the Sabbath is fulfilled in Christ, in whom believers find rest (Hebrews 4). Sunday is the day for gathering to worship, but it need not be kept with the Old Testament rules on ceasing work. The principle of rest is still worth keeping.

## 共同的地方 | Common ground

各传统都同意：基督徒应当定期聚会敬拜；人需要休息，休息是神的恩赐；安息日的本意是让人记得神是创造主和救赎主，人的价值不在于不停地工作。保罗也说，在守日子的事上，“各人心里要意见坚定”（罗 14:5），不要彼此论断。<br>
All traditions agree that Christians should gather regularly for worship, that people need rest and rest is God's gift, and that the Sabbath exists to remind us that God is Creator and Redeemer and that our worth does not lie in endless work. Paul also says that on observing days, “Let each man be fully assured in his own mind” (Romans 14:5), without judging one another.
