## 结构：先讲恩典，再讲生活 | Structure: grace first, then life

以弗所书可以分成两半：<br>
Ephesians falls into two halves:

- **1–3 章：神在基督里所做的**。信一开头是一段很长的颂赞：“愿颂赞归与我们主耶稣基督的父神！他在基督里曾赐给我们天上各样属灵的福气”（弗 1:3）。第 2 章说，人原本“死在过犯罪恶之中”，神却使人活过来：“你们得救是本乎恩，也因着信；这并不是出于自己，乃是神所赐的”（弗 2:8）。<br>
  **Chapters 1–3: what God has done in Christ.** The letter opens with a long blessing: “Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ” (Ephesians 1:3). Chapter 2 says people were “dead in transgressions and sins,” and God made them alive: “by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God” (Ephesians 2:8).
- **4–6 章：信徒怎样生活**。第 4 章以“所以”开头：“行事为人就当与蒙召的恩相称”（弗 4:1）。接下来讲教会的合一、旧人和新人、说话、夫妻、父母和儿女、主仆，最后讲属灵的争战：“要穿戴神所赐的全副军装”（弗 6:11）。<br>
  **Chapters 4–6: how believers live.** Chapter 4 begins with “therefore”: “walk worthily of the calling with which you were called” (Ephesians 4:1). It goes on to the unity of the church, the old self and the new, speech, husbands and wives, parents and children, masters and servants, and ends with spiritual warfare: “Put on the whole armor of God” (Ephesians 6:11).

2:8-10 把两半连在一起：人得救“不是出于行为”，但得救的人是神的工作，“为要叫我们行善”。<br>
Ephesians 2:8-10 holds the two halves together: salvation is “not of works,” but those who are saved are God's workmanship, “created in Christ Jesus for good works.”

## 一个新人，一个身体 | One new humanity, one body

以弗所书特别讲犹太人和外邦人的关系。基督“将两下合而为一，拆毁了中间隔断的墙”（弗 2:14），在他里面造成“一个新人”。所以信徒要竭力保守合一：“身体只有一个，圣灵只有一个……一主，一信，一洗，一神，就是众人的父”（弗 4:4-6）。<br>
Ephesians gives special attention to Jews and Gentiles. Christ “made both one, and broke down the middle wall of separation” (Ephesians 2:14), creating in himself “one new man.” So believers are to keep the unity of the Spirit: “There is one body and one Spirit... one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all” (Ephesians 4:4-6).

## 作者和收信人 | Author and recipients

- **作者**：信中自称是保罗写的，而且是在狱中写的（弗 3:1；4:1）。教会历来接受这一点，很多福音派学者今天也这样认为。大英百科介绍的批判学者认为，它更可能是保罗的门徒按保罗的思想写成的，因为它的用词和风格与几封公认的保罗书信有些不同，又大量借用其他保罗书信的内容。<br>
  **Author**: The letter presents itself as written by Paul from prison (Ephesians 3:1; 4:1). The church has long accepted this, and many evangelical scholars still do. Critical scholars summarised by Britannica think it more likely the work of a disciple writing in Paul's tradition, pointing to differences in vocabulary and style from the undisputed letters and its heavy reliance on Paul's other letters.
- **收信人**：1:1 的“在以弗所”几个字，最早的一些抄本和引文没有。保罗在以弗所住了大约三年（徒 20:31），信里却没有问候任何人。所以不少学者认为，这封信原本是写给小亚细亚几间教会传阅的。<br>
  **Recipients**: The words “in Ephesus” in 1:1 are missing from some of the earliest manuscripts and citations. Paul lived in Ephesus about three years (Acts 20:31), yet the letter greets no one by name. Many scholars therefore think it was originally circulated among several churches in Asia Minor.

## 常被讨论的经文 | Much-discussed passages

- **预定（1:4-5）**：“神从创立世界以前，在基督里拣选了我们。”各传统怎样理解拣选，见“神的主权和人的选择”一课。<br>
  **Predestination (1:4-5)**: “He chose us in him before the foundation of the world.” On how the traditions understand election, see the lesson on God's sovereignty and human choice.
- **夫妻（5:21-33）**：这一段以“存敬畏基督的心，彼此顺服”（5:21）开头，然后讲妻子顺服丈夫、丈夫爱妻子“正如基督爱教会”。互补派和平等派对这一段的理解不同，见“女性在教会中的服事”一课。<br>
  **Husbands and wives (5:21-33)**: The passage opens with “subjecting yourselves to one another in the fear of Christ” (5:21), then speaks of wives submitting to husbands and husbands loving their wives “even as Christ also loved the assembly.” Complementarians and egalitarians read it differently; see the lesson on women in ministry.
- **主仆（6:5-9）**：保罗劝仆人顺服，也命令主人不要威吓仆人，因为他们在天上有同一位主。这段经文在历史上曾被用来为奴隶制辩护，“教会做过很多错事”一课讲到这一点。<br>
  **Masters and servants (6:5-9)**: Paul urges servants to obey and commands masters not to threaten them, since they have the same Master in heaven. This passage was used in history to defend slavery; the lesson on wrongs done by the church discusses this.
