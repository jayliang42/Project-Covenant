## 耶稣为什么用比喻 | Why Jesus used parables

马可说：“耶稣用许多这样的比喻，照他们所能听的，对他们讲道……没有人的时候，就把一切的道讲给门徒听”（可 4:33-34）。比喻用日常生活里的事：撒种、找羊、婚宴、雇工、借钱，让人一听就记住，又要人想一想才明白。耶稣也说，比喻会让一些人“看是看见，却不晓得”（可 4:12）。比喻既打开，也考验听的人的心。<br>
Mark says, “With many such parables he spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear it... but privately to his own disciples he explained everything” (Mark 4:33-34). Parables draw on everyday life: sowing, searching for sheep, wedding feasts, hiring workers, lending money. They are easy to remember and require thought to understand. Jesus also said parables would leave some “seeing they may see, and not perceive” (Mark 4:12). Parables both open up truth and test the hearer's heart.

## 读法的历史 | A history of reading

- **寓意解经**：早期和中世纪的教会常把比喻的每个细节解释为象征。现存最早关于好撒马利亚人比喻的注释来自俄利根，他说“撒马利亚人就是基督”。奥古斯丁的寓意解释影响很大：落难的人是亚当，耶路撒冷是天上的城，客店是教会，等等。英国学者多德（C. H. Dodd）说，这种读法一直流行到 19 世纪。<br>
  **Allegorical reading**: The early and medieval church often treated each detail of a parable as a symbol. The earliest surviving comments on the Good Samaritan come from Origen, who said, “the Samaritan is Christ.” Augustine's allegory was very influential: the wounded man is Adam, Jerusalem the heavenly city, the inn the church, and so on. The British scholar C. H. Dodd said this approach prevailed into the nineteenth century.
- **“只有一个要点”**：19 世纪起，研究历史上的耶稣的学者把寓意的部分看作后人加上去的，认为每个比喻原本只说一个要点。多德在 1935 年出版的《天国的比喻》中认为，比喻一般不容许寓意的解释。<br>
  **“Only one point”**: From the nineteenth century, scholars studying the historical Jesus treated allegorical elements as later additions and held that each parable originally made a single point. Dodd, in The Parables of the Kingdom (1935), argued that parables in general do not admit allegory.
- **今天的看法**：很多学者认为“只有一个要点”太狭窄。耶稣自己就解释过撒种比喻里几样东西代表什么（可 4:13-20）。一个比喻可以有几个要点，常常和故事里的几个主要人物有关；但要从上下文出发，不必给每个细节都找意思。也有学者，例如帕森斯（Mikeal Parsons），认为古代教父的读法有时比近代的解释更注意福音书本身的上下文，值得重新认真对待。<br>
  **Today**: Many scholars find “only one point” too narrow. Jesus himself explained what several things in the parable of the sower stand for (Mark 4:13-20). A parable may make several points, often tied to its main characters, but interpretation should start from the context and need not find a meaning in every detail. Some scholars, such as Mikeal Parsons, argue that the church fathers' readings were sometimes more attentive to the Gospel's own context than modern interpretations, and deserve a fresh hearing.

## 几个实用的问题 | Some practical questions

1. **耶稣是对谁讲的？为什么讲？** 路加福音 15 章的三个比喻，是因为法利赛人和文士抱怨“这个人接待罪人，又同他们吃饭”（路 15:2）。所以浪子的比喻最后落在不肯进屋的大儿子身上。<br>
   **To whom did Jesus speak, and why?** The three parables of Luke 15 answer the complaint of the Pharisees and scribes: “This man welcomes sinners, and eats with them” (Luke 15:2). That is why the parable of the lost son ends with the elder brother who will not come in.
2. **有没有说明的话？** 有时福音书直接说出比喻的目的，例如“耶稣设一个比喻，是要人常常祷告，不可灰心”（路 18:1）。<br>
   **Is there an explanatory note?** Sometimes the Gospel states the purpose directly: “He also spoke a parable to them that they must always pray, and not give up” (Luke 18:1).
3. **当时的人会怎样反应？故事的转折在哪里？** 在好撒马利亚人的比喻里，祭司和利未人都走过去了，帮助人的竟是犹太人看不起的撒马利亚人。律法师问“谁是我的邻舍”（路 10:29），耶稣最后却问“哪一个是……的邻舍”，并说：“你去照样行吧”（路 10:36-37）。<br>
   **How would the first hearers react? Where is the twist?** In the Good Samaritan, the priest and the Levite pass by, and the one who helps is a Samaritan, whom Jews looked down on. The lawyer asked, “Who is my neighbor?” (Luke 10:29), but Jesus ends by asking which of the three “seemed to be a neighbor,” and says, “Go and do likewise” (Luke 10:36-37).
4. **它和“神的国”有什么关系？** 很多比喻以“天国好像……”开头，例如马太福音 13:44。问一问：这个比喻说明了神的国的什么特点？<br>
   **How does it relate to the kingdom of God?** Many parables begin, “The Kingdom of Heaven is like...” (for example Matthew 13:44). Ask what the parable shows about the kingdom.
5. **它要求我怎样回应？** 比喻常常把听的人放进故事里，要他作出选择。<br>
   **What response does it ask of me?** Parables often put the hearer into the story and call for a decision.
