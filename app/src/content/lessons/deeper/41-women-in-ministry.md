## 问题在哪里 | Where the question lies

各传统都同意，男女都是照神的形象造的，在基督里同样得救，同样领受圣灵和恩赐。分歧在于：教会里有没有某些职分，例如牧师、长老、主教或神父，是只给男性的？<br>
All traditions agree that men and women alike are made in God's image, saved in the same way in Christ, and receive the Spirit and his gifts. They differ on whether certain offices in the church, such as pastor, elder, bishop, or priest, are reserved for men.

## 天主教和东正教 | Catholic and Orthodox

天主教和东正教都只按立男性为神父和主教。1994 年 5 月 22 日，教宗若望保禄二世发表《司铎圣秩》（Ordinatio sacerdotalis），宣告“教会完全没有权柄授予女性司铎圣职”，并说这个判断要由全体信徒“确定地持守”。主要的理由是，基督自由地只拣选了男性作使徒。信函也说，马利亚没有领受使徒的职分，可见这并不表示女性的尊严比较低。<br>
The Catholic and Orthodox churches ordain only men as priests and bishops. On 22 May 1994 Pope John Paul II issued Ordinatio sacerdotalis, declaring that “the Church has no authority whatsoever to confer priestly ordination on women” and that “this judgment is to be definitively held by all the Church's faithful.” The main reason given is that Christ freely chose only men as his apostles. The letter adds that Mary did not receive the apostolic mission, which shows that this does not mean women have lesser dignity.

## 新教里的两种主要看法 | Two main Protestant views

**互补派**（complementarian）：男女同样尊贵，在家庭和教会里有不同而互补的角色。1987 年 12 月，一批福音派领袖在美国但弗斯（Danvers）起草宣言，1988 年 11 月由“圣经男女议会”（CBMW）发表。宣言说，“教会里有些治理和教导的职分只属于男性”。美南浸信会的《浸信会信仰与信息》（2000 年版）说：“牧师的职分按圣经的资格只限于男性。”<br>
**Complementarian**: Men and women are equal in dignity and have different, complementary roles in the home and the church. In December 1987 a group of evangelical leaders drafted a statement at Danvers, Massachusetts, published in November 1988 by the Council on Biblical Manhood and Womanhood (CBMW). It says that “some governing and teaching roles within the church are restricted to men.” The Southern Baptist Convention's Baptist Faith and Message (2000) says, “The office of pastor is limited to men as qualified by Scripture.”

**平等派**（egalitarian）：所有职分都向男女开放，按恩赐和呼召来分配。1988 年 1 月 2 日，“基督徒圣经平等协会”（CBE）成立，第一项主要工作是发表《男人、女人与圣经平等》，提出女性在教会和家庭中一同领导的圣经理由。<br>
**Egalitarian**: All offices are open to men and women and are assigned by gifting and calling. Christians for Biblical Equality (CBE) was founded on 2 January 1988. Its first major project was the statement “Men, Women, and Biblical Equality,” which set out the biblical case for women's shared leadership in church and family.

## 双方引用的经文 | The passages each side cites

互补派常引用：<br>
Complementarians often cite:

- “我不许女人讲道，也不许她辖管男人，只要沉静”（提前 2:12）。保罗接着以创造的次序为理由（提前 2:13）。互补派认为，这说明这条规定的根据在创造，适用于各时代的教会。<br>
  “I don't permit a woman to teach, nor to exercise authority over a man, but to be in quietness” (1 Timothy 2:12). Paul goes on to give the order of creation as a reason (1 Timothy 2:13). Complementarians argue that this grounds the rule in creation, so it applies to the church in every age.
- 作监督的要“只作一个妇人的丈夫”（提前 3:2）。<br>
  An overseer must be “the husband of one wife” (1 Timothy 3:2).
- 耶稣拣选的十二使徒都是男性。<br>
  The twelve apostles Jesus chose were all men.

平等派常引用：<br>
Egalitarians often cite:

- “并不分……或男或女，因为你们在基督耶稣里都成为一了”（加 3:28）。<br>
  “There is neither... male nor female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus” (Galatians 3:28).
- 女先知底波拉作以色列的士师（士 4:4）；五旬节时彼得引用约珥书，说“你们的儿女要说预言”（徒 2:17）；哥林多教会有女性在聚会中祷告和说预言（林前 11:5）。<br>
  The prophetess Deborah judged Israel (Judges 4:4); at Pentecost Peter quoted Joel, “Your sons and your daughters will prophesy” (Acts 2:17); women prayed and prophesied in the Corinthian church's meetings (1 Corinthians 11:5).
- 保罗举荐的非比是坚革哩教会的执事（罗 16:1，原文 diakonos）；亚波罗讲道以后，百基拉和丈夫亚居拉“就接他来，将神的道给他讲解更加详细”（徒 18:26）；犹尼亚“在使徒中是有名望的”（罗 16:7）。<br>
  Phoebe, whom Paul commends, was a servant (Greek diakonos) of the church at Cenchreae (Romans 16:1); after Apollos preached, Priscilla and her husband Aquila “took him aside, and explained to him the way of God more accurately” (Acts 18:26); Junia was “notable among the apostles” (Romans 16:7).
- 平等派认为，提前 2:12 和林前 14:34-35 针对的是当时具体教会的问题，例如没有受过教导的人传讲错误的教训。<br>
  Egalitarians argue that 1 Timothy 2:12 and 1 Corinthians 14:34-35 address specific problems in those churches, such as untaught people spreading false teaching.

双方对其中好几处经文的翻译和意思也有争论。例如罗马书 16:7 那句话，可以译成“在使徒中有名望”，也可以译成“被使徒们所看重”。和合本把非比译为“女执事”，有的英文译本译为“仆人”。<br>
The two sides also dispute the translation and meaning of several of these passages. Romans 16:7, for example, can be rendered “notable among the apostles” or “well known to the apostles.” The Chinese Union Version calls Phoebe a “deaconess,” while some English versions call her a “servant.”

## 各教会的做法 | What churches do

- 美南浸信会：2023 年 6 月的年会以大比数维持开除马鞍峰教会和费恩溪浸信会的决定，因为这两间教会有女牧师。<br>
  Southern Baptist Convention: At its annual meeting in June 2023, the convention voted overwhelmingly to uphold the removal of Saddleback Church and Fern Creek Baptist Church because they had women pastors.
- 英格兰教会：1994 年开始按立女性为牧师，2014 年起允许女性担任主教。2026 年 3 月 25 日，穆拉利（Sarah Mullally）就任第 106 任坎特伯雷大主教，是第一位担任这个职位的女性。<br>
  Church of England: It began ordaining women as priests in 1994 and has allowed women to be bishops since 2014. On 25 March 2026 Sarah Mullally was installed as the 106th Archbishop of Canterbury, the first woman to hold the office.
- 很多五旬节派、循道宗和其他教会按立女性；很多改革宗、浸信会和独立教会只让男性作牧师或长老，同时鼓励女性在教导妇女和儿童、探访、宣教等许多方面服事。<br>
  Many Pentecostal, Methodist, and other churches ordain women. Many Reformed, Baptist, and independent churches allow only men as pastors or elders, while encouraging women to serve in many areas, such as teaching women and children, visitation, and mission.

两种看法的人都说自己是在顺服圣经。了解对方引用的经文和理由，有助于在分歧中彼此尊重。<br>
People on both sides say they are submitting to Scripture. Understanding the passages and reasons the other side gives helps people respect one another amid disagreement.
