## 以马忤斯路上的那堂查经课 | The Bible study on the Emmaus road

复活节当天傍晚，两个门徒垂头丧气地往以马忤斯走。他们知道旧约，也爱耶稣，但他们想不通：如果耶稣真是弥赛亚，为什么会被钉死在十字架上？<br>
On the evening of the first Easter, two disciples walked toward Emmaus in grief. They knew the Hebrew Scriptures and loved Jesus, yet they could not make sense of how the Messiah could be crucified.

复活的耶稣走到他们身边，“从摩西和众先知起，凡经上所指着自己的话，都给他们讲解明白了”（路 24:27）。注意：耶稣不是抽出一两节孤立的预言，而是带他们看整部律法书、先知书和诗篇如何指向受苦又得荣耀的基督。<br>
The risen Jesus walked beside them and, "beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, explained to them what was said in all the Scriptures concerning himself" (Luke 24:27). He did not merely point to one or two isolated proof-texts; he showed how the Law, the Prophets, and the Psalms together point to a suffering and glorified Messiah.

## 既不把旧约变成道德鸡汤，也不搞牵强附会 | Avoiding both moralism and word-games

读旧约时常有两个极端：<br>
Readers of the Old Testament often slip into one of two extremes:

1. **只当道德故事读（Moralism）**：把亚伯拉罕、摩西、大卫完全当成“好榜样”，读完只得出“我也要更勇敢、更诚实”。但旧约明明诚实记录了这些人物的软弱与失败，说明连最好的领袖也需要被拯救。<br>
   **Mere moralism**: Treating Abraham, Moses, and David simply as self-help heroes ("be brave like David"). Yet the Old Testament candidly records their failures, showing that even the best human leaders needed a Savior.
2. **脱离上下文的牵强附会（Allegorizing）**：不管原文在讲什么，看见“木头”就说是十字架，看见“红色”就说是宝血，把读经变成猜谜。<br>
   **Context-free allegorizing**: Ignoring what the passage actually says and turning every piece of wood into the cross or every red thread into blood by free association.

## 三条健康的桥梁：应许、预表、空缺 | Three sound bridges: promise, pattern, and need

新约作者教我们用三条扎实的历史桥梁，从旧约走向基督：<br>
The New Testament writers model three grounded ways a passage leads to Christ:

- **应许与成全（Promise → Fulfillment）**：神对亚伯拉罕、大卫所立的圣约应许，以及先知关于受苦仆人（如以赛亚书 53 章）的盼望，在耶稣身上落到实处。<br>
  **Promise and fulfillment**: Covenant promises to Abraham and David, and prophetic hopes like the Suffering Servant of Isaiah 53, find their goal in Jesus.
- **历史预表（Typology / Pattern）**：逾越节的羔羊、出埃及的拯救、会幕与圣殿的同在、大祭司的中保职分——这些真实的历史制度本身是神设立的“教学模型”（来 8:5，10:1），为要预备人认识那位真正的羔羊与大祭司。<br>
  **Historical typology**: The Passover lamb, the Exodus deliverance, the tabernacle presence, and the high priesthood were real historical institutions designed by God as "shadows" (Hebrews 8:5; 10:1) preparing people to recognize the true Lamb and Great High Priest.
- **人的破产与渴望（Human failure → Need）**：当《士师记》或《列王纪》让我们看到“那时以色列中没有王，各人任意而行”，那种对公义君王的深切渴望，正是福音的序曲。<br>
  **Human failure and longing**: When Judges or Kings shows leader after leader failing, the ache for a truly righteous King prepares the heart for the Gospel.
