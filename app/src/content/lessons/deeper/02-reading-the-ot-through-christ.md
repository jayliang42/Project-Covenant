## 耶稣自己的读法 | Jesus' own reading

复活以后，耶稣在以马忤斯的路上，“从摩西和众先知起，凡经上所指着自己的话，都给他们讲解明白了”（路 24:27）。后来他又提到“摩西的律法、先知的书和诗篇”（路 24:44）。

After the resurrection, on the Emmaus road, Jesus, “beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, explained to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning himself” (Luke 24:27). Later he named “the law of Moses, the prophets, and the psalms” (Luke 24:44).

## 三步读法 | Three steps

1. **原意**：这段经文对最初的读者是什么意思？<br>**First meaning:** what did it mean to its first readers?
2. **故事线**：它在整本圣经的故事里处在什么位置，指向哪里？<br>**Storyline:** where does it sit in the whole story, and where does it point?
3. **在基督里成全**：新约怎样引用它，或者它的主题怎样在耶稣身上达到高峰？<br>**Fulfilment in Christ:** how does the New Testament use it, or how does its theme reach its peak in Jesus?

## 预表与寓意 | Typology and allegory

预表是在历史中真实的人物、事件或制度，和基督之间看出的对应，例如逾越节的羔羊。寓意则常常把细节当作密码，很容易越过经文本身。走完第一步，能防止第三步走偏。

Typology sees real historical people, events or institutions corresponding to Christ, such as the Passover lamb. Allegory often treats details as code and easily leaves the text behind. Doing step one keeps step three honest.
