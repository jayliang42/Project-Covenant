## 圣经里的酒 | Wine in the Bible

圣经对酒有正面的说法：神赐“酒能悦人心，得油能润人面，得粮能养人心”（诗 104:15）。耶稣在迦拿的婚宴上把水变成酒，管筵席的说“你倒把好酒留到如今”（约 2:10）。保罗劝提摩太“可以稍微用点酒”，因为他胃口不好（提前 5:23）。耶稣设立圣餐时说到“这葡萄汁”（太 26:29），按逾越节的习惯，杯里通常是葡萄酒。<br>
The Bible speaks of wine positively: God gives “wine that makes the heart of man glad, oil to make his face to shine, and bread that strengthens man's heart” (Psalm 104:15). At the wedding in Cana Jesus turned water into wine, and the master of the feast said, “You have kept the good wine until now” (John 2:10). Paul advises Timothy to “use a little wine” for his stomach (1 Timothy 5:23). When Jesus instituted the Lord's Supper he spoke of “this fruit of the vine” (Matthew 26:29); at Passover the cup normally held wine.

圣经也严厉警告：“酒能使人亵慢，浓酒使人喧嚷；凡因酒错误的，就无智慧”（箴 20:1）。箴言 23 章描写醉酒的人“谁有祸患？谁有忧愁？……就是那流连饮酒、常去寻找调和酒的人”（箴 23:29-30）。保罗说：“不要醉酒，酒能使人放荡；乃要被圣灵充满”（弗 5:18）。作监督的要“不因酒滋事”（提前 3:3）。<br>
The Bible also warns sternly: “Wine is a mocker and beer is a brawler. Whoever is led astray by them is not wise” (Proverbs 20:1). Proverbs 23 asks, “Who has woe? Who has sorrow?... Those who stay long at the wine” (Proverbs 23:29-30). Paul says, “Don't be drunken with wine, in which is dissipation, but be filled with the Spirit” (Ephesians 5:18). An overseer must be “not a drinker” (1 Timothy 3:3).

## 两种立场 | Two positions

**完全禁酒**：很多福音派教会提倡完全不喝酒，美南浸信会是其中之一。2006 年，美南浸信会的年会通过决议，表示“完全反对”制造、广告、销售和饮用含酒精的饮料，并且建议不选任饮酒的人担任宗派机构的董事或委员。支持者的理由包括：醉酒带来的家庭和社会伤害很大；他们认为今天的酒比古代的酒烈得多；不喝酒可以避免成为别人的绊脚石，“叫弟兄跌倒，一概不做才好”（罗 14:21）。<br>
**Total abstinence**: Many evangelical churches promote total abstinence; the Southern Baptist Convention is one of them. In 2006 the Southern Baptist Convention's annual meeting passed a resolution expressing “total opposition” to the manufacturing, advertising, distributing, and consuming of alcoholic beverages, and urging that users of alcohol not be elected as trustees or committee members of its entities. Supporters argue that drunkenness does great harm to families and society; that modern drinks are much stronger than ancient wine; and that abstaining avoids becoming a stumbling block: “It is good to not... drink wine, nor do anything by which your brother stumbles” (Romans 14:21).

**适量饮酒**：天主教、东正教、信义宗、圣公会和很多改革宗教会在圣餐中使用葡萄酒，一般不要求信徒完全禁酒，但强调节制。美南浸信会内部也有人反对 2006 年的决议，认为“神所默示的话禁止的是醉酒，不一定是喝酒本身”，并且指出“基督把水变成了酒”。<br>
**Moderate drinking**: Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Anglican, and many Reformed churches use wine in the Lord's Supper and generally do not require total abstinence, while stressing self-control. Within the Southern Baptist Convention, too, some opposed the 2006 resolution, arguing that “the authoritative, inspired word of God forbids drunkenness, not necessarily the drinking of an alcoholic beverage,” and pointing out that “Christ turned water into wine.”

## 各方都同意的原则 | Principles all agree on

- **不可醉酒。**<br>
  **Do not get drunk.**
- **不受辖制**：“凡事我都可行，但不都有益处……无论哪一件，我总不受它的辖制”（林前 6:12）。有酗酒问题或家族里有这方面问题的人，完全不喝是明智的；需要时应寻求专业的帮助。<br>
  **Do not be mastered**: “All things are lawful for me, but not all things are expedient... I will not be brought under the power of anything” (1 Corinthians 6:12). For anyone with a drinking problem, or with such problems in the family, abstaining completely is wise; seek professional help where needed.
- **为别人着想**：在可能叫别人跌倒的场合，宁可不喝（罗 14:21）。<br>
  **Consider others**: Where drinking might cause someone else to stumble, it is better not to (Romans 14:21).
- **遵守法律**：例如饮酒的法定年龄、不酒后驾车。<br>
  **Obey the law**: for example, the legal drinking age, and never driving after drinking.
- **不彼此论断**：在圣经没有明令的事上，“各人心里要意见坚定”（罗 14:5），喝的不要轻看不喝的，不喝的也不要论断喝的（参罗 14:3）。<br>
  **Do not judge one another**: In matters Scripture does not command, “Let each man be fully assured in his own mind” (Romans 14:5); those who drink should not despise those who don't, and those who don't should not judge those who do (compare Romans 14:3).
