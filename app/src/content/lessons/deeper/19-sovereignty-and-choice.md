## 圣经两方面的说法 | What the Bible says on both sides

圣经有不少经文讲神的主权和拣选：<br>
Many passages speak of God's sovereignty and his choosing:

- 神“从创立世界以前，在基督里拣选了我们”（弗 1:4）。<br>
  God “chose us in him before the foundation of the world” (Ephesians 1:4).
- “凡预定得永生的人都信了”（徒 13:48）。<br>
  “As many as were appointed to eternal life believed” (Acts 13:48).
- 耶稣说：“若不是差我来的父吸引人，就没有能到我这里来的”（约 6:44）。<br>
  Jesus said, “No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him” (John 6:44).

圣经也有不少经文讲人的选择和责任：<br>
Many other passages speak of human choice and responsibility:

- 约书亚对百姓说：“今日就可以选择所要事奉的……至于我和我家，我们必定事奉耶和华”（书 24:15）。<br>
  Joshua told the people, “Choose today whom you will serve ... but as for me and my house, we will serve Yahweh” (Joshua 24:15).
- 神“不愿有一人沉沦，乃愿人人都悔改”（彼后 3:9）。<br>
  God is “not wishing that anyone should perish, but that all should come to repentance” (2 Peter 3:9).
- 耶稣为耶路撒冷哀哭：“我多次愿意聚集你的儿女……只是你们不愿意”（太 23:37）。<br>
  Jesus wept over Jerusalem: “How often I would have gathered your children together ... and you would not!” (Matthew 23:37).

有时这两方面出现在同一段经文里。保罗说“当恐惧战兢做成你们得救的工夫”，又说“立志行事都是神在你们心里运行”（腓 2:12-13）。<br>
Sometimes both appear in the same passage. Paul says, “Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling,” and also, “it is God who works in you both to will and to work” (Philippians 2:12–13).

## 教会历史上的几种立场 | Positions in church history

- **奥古斯丁和伯拉纠（5 世纪初）**：伯拉纠认为人有能力靠自己行善、遵守神的命令。奥古斯丁认为人因罪而无力，得救全是神的恩典。教会否定了伯拉纠的主张。<br>
  **Augustine and Pelagius (early 5th century)**: Pelagius held that people are able to do good and keep God's commands by their own strength. Augustine held that sin leaves people powerless and that salvation is wholly God's grace. The church rejected Pelagius's view.
- **加尔文派（改革宗）**：强调神无条件的拣选。人得救的原因在于神的拣选，神的恩典会有效地把蒙拣选的人带到信心里。1618–1619 年的多特会议是这一立场的重要文件来源。<br>
  **Calvinist (Reformed)**: Stresses God's unconditional election. The reason a person is saved lies in God's choosing, and God's grace effectively brings the chosen to faith. The Synod of Dort (1618–1619) is a key source for this position.
- **亚米念派（卫理宗等）**：亚米念（1609 年去世）的追随者认为，神的恩典先临到所有人，使人能够回应，人也可以抗拒这份恩典；神的拣选和他预知谁会相信有关。18 世纪的卫斯理持这一类看法。<br>
  **Arminian (Methodist and others)**: The followers of Arminius (died 1609) held that God's grace goes ahead to all people, enabling them to respond, and that people can resist this grace; God's election is connected with his foreknowledge of who will believe. John Wesley in the 18th century held this kind of view.
- **其他看法**：路德宗、天主教和东正教都有各自的说法，这里不一一展开。<br>
  **Other views**: Lutheran, Catholic, and Orthodox traditions each have their own ways of putting it, which are not covered in detail here.

## 双方都同意的事 | What both sides agree on

在正统的范围里，各方都承认：神是主宰，他的旨意必定成就；人得救完全是靠神的恩典；人要为自己的选择负责；福音要传给所有的人。<br>
Within historic Christianity, all sides affirm that God is sovereign and his purposes will stand; that salvation is wholly by God's grace; that people are responsible for their choices; and that the gospel is to be preached to everyone.

分歧主要在于：神的拣选和人的回应，哪一个在先？神的恩典能不能被人抗拒？<br>
The disagreement is mainly about which comes first, God's choosing or the human response, and whether God's grace can be resisted.

## 讨论时的态度 | An attitude for discussion

这是一个基督徒讨论了一千多年的问题，真诚爱主的人站在不同的立场上。讨论的时候，可以先把对方最好的理由说清楚，再说自己的看法。<br>
Christians have discussed this question for well over a thousand years, and people who sincerely love the Lord stand on different sides. When you discuss it, first state the other side's best reasons clearly, and then give your own view.

保罗讲完神的拣选和怜悯，用赞美结束这一段：“深哉，神丰富的智慧和知识！他的判断何其难测！他的踪迹何其难寻！”（罗 11:33）。<br>
Paul ends his discussion of God's choosing and mercy with praise: “Oh the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and the knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments, and his ways past tracing out!” (Romans 11:33).
