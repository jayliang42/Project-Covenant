## 神是王 | God is King

“神的国”指的是神作王掌权。旧约一开始就把神写成全地的主。《诗篇》说：“你的国是永远的国！你执掌的权柄存到万代！”（诗 145:13）。<br>
The “kingdom of God” means God reigning as King. From the start the Old Testament presents God as Lord of all the earth. A psalm says, “Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom. Your dominion endures throughout all generations” (Psalm 145:13).

神造人的时候，吩咐人“治理这地，也要管理海里的鱼、空中的鸟，和地上各样行动的活物”（创 1:28）。人被托付代表神管理世界。人堕落以后，这份托付被扭曲了，人开始互相辖制。<br>
When God made people, he told them to “subdue it” and “have dominion over the fish of the sea, over the birds of the sky, and over every living thing” (Genesis 1:28). People were entrusted with ruling the world on God's behalf. After the fall this trust was twisted, and people began to dominate one another.

## 以色列的君王和盼望 | Israel's kings and hope

神在以色列中间立了王。理想的王要按神的律法治理（申 17:18-20）。神应许大卫，他的后裔要作王，国位“直到永远”（撒下 7:13）。可是以色列的王大多失败，国家最后亡了。<br>
God set up kings in Israel. The ideal king was to rule by God's law (Deuteronomy 17:18–20). God promised David that his descendant would reign and his throne would last “forever” (2 Samuel 7:13). But most of Israel's kings failed, and the nation finally fell.

被掳期间，先知但以理看见一连串帝国兴起又倒下，然后“天上的神必另立一国，永不败坏”（但 2:44）。到了耶稣的时代，很多犹太人盼望神的国来到，赶走罗马人，恢复大卫的王朝。<br>
During the exile the prophet Daniel saw a series of empires rise and fall, and then “the God of heaven will set up a kingdom which will never be destroyed” (Daniel 2:44). By Jesus' time, many Jews longed for God's kingdom to come, drive out the Romans, and restore David's dynasty.

## 耶稣宣告国度来到 | Jesus announces the kingdom

耶稣开始传道时说：“日期满了，神的国近了。你们当悔改，信福音！”（可 1:15）。他赶鬼、医病、赦罪、和罪人一起吃饭，用行动说明神作王是什么样子。他说：“我若靠着神的灵赶鬼，这就是神的国临到你们了”（太 12:28）。<br>
Jesus began his ministry saying, “The time is fulfilled, and God’s Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News” (Mark 1:15). He cast out demons, healed the sick, forgave sins, and ate with sinners, showing in action what God's reign looks like. He said, “If I by the Spirit of God cast out demons, then God’s Kingdom has come upon you” (Matthew 12:28).

耶稣的国度和很多人期待的不一样。他没有带兵起义。他说自己的国“不属这世界”（约 18:36），他作王的方式是服事人、舍命，最后被钉在十字架上，头上的牌子写着“犹太人的王”。<br>
Jesus' kingdom was not what many expected. He led no armed revolt. He said his kingdom was “not of this world” (John 18:36). He reigned by serving and giving his life, and he died on a cross under a sign that read “the King of the Jews.”

## 已经，还没有 | Already and not yet

新约把神的国描述成两个阶段。一方面，国度已经借着耶稣来到：他复活升天，“基督必要作王，等神把一切仇敌都放在他的脚下”（林前 15:25）。另一方面，国度还没有完全来到：世界上仍有罪恶、疾病和死亡，所以耶稣教门徒祷告“愿你的国降临；愿你的旨意行在地上，如同行在天上”（太 6:10）。<br>
The New Testament describes God's kingdom in two stages. On the one hand, it has already come through Jesus: he rose and ascended, and “he must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet” (1 Corinthians 15:25). On the other hand, it has not yet fully come: the world still has sin, sickness, and death, which is why Jesus taught his disciples to pray, “Let your Kingdom come. Let your will be done on earth as it is in heaven” (Matthew 6:10).

学者常把这叫作“已经，还没有”（already and not yet）。基督徒活在这两个阶段中间：一面跟随已经作王的基督，一面盼望他再来，使万物更新。<br>
Scholars often call this “already and not yet.” Christians live between the two stages, following Christ who already reigns and waiting for his return, when all things will be made new.
