## 照神的形象 | In God's image

“神就照着自己的形像造人，乃是照着他的形像造男造女”（创 1:27）。“神的形象”具体指什么，神学家有不同的说法：有人强调理性和道德，有人强调人和神、和别人的关系，有人强调人代表神管理世界的职分。各传统都同意：每一个人都有神的形象，所以每一个生命都有尊严。<br>
“God created man in his own image. In God's image he created him; male and female he created them” (Genesis 1:27). Theologians explain “God's image” in different ways: some stress reason and moral capacity, some stress relationship with God and others, and some stress humanity's calling to rule the world on God's behalf. All traditions agree that every person bears God's image, and so every human life has dignity.

人犯罪以后，神的形象并没有消失。挪亚洪水以后，神仍说不可流人的血，“因为神造人是照自己的形像造的”（创 9:6）。雅各书说，人用舌头“咒诅那照着神形像被造的人”是不对的（雅 3:9）。<br>
After humans sinned, God's image did not disappear. After the flood God still forbade shedding human blood, “for God made man in his own image” (Genesis 9:6). James says it is wrong to “curse men who are made in the image of God” (James 3:9).

## 一人的过犯 | One man's trespass

保罗在罗马书 5 章说：“罪是从一人入了世界，死又是从罪来的；于是死就临到众人，因为众人都犯了罪”（罗 5:12）。他把亚当和基督对比：“因一人的悖逆，众人成为罪人；照样，因一人的顺从，众人也成为义了”（罗 5:19）。<br>
In Romans 5 Paul says, “sin entered into the world through one man, and death through sin; so death passed to all men, because all sinned” (Romans 5:12). He contrasts Adam and Christ: “through the one man's disobedience many were made sinners, even so through the obedience of the one, many will be made righteous” (Romans 5:19).

亚当的罪怎样传到后代？后代是只承受了后果，还是也承担了罪责？这就是“原罪”的问题。<br>
How does Adam's sin pass to his descendants? Do they inherit only its consequences, or its guilt as well? This is the question of “original sin.”

## 奥古斯丁和伯拉纠 | Augustine and Pelagius

5 世纪初，修士伯拉纠（Pelagius）认为人的本性基本上是良善的，人可以自由地选择善恶。他的门徒色勒斯丢（Celestius）更否认原罪会传给后代。北非的主教奥古斯丁坚决反对，认为人靠自己不能成为义，完全需要神的恩典。<br>
In the early fifth century the monk Pelagius held that human nature is basically good and that people freely choose between good and evil. His disciple Celestius went further and denied that original sin passes to descendants. Augustine, a bishop in North Africa, strongly opposed them, arguing that people cannot become righteous by their own effort and depend wholly on God's grace.

主后 418 年的迦太基会议定罪了伯拉纠派，431 年的以弗所会议也定罪了他们。后来在南高卢流行的“半伯拉纠主义”，在 529 年的奥朗日会议被定罪。所以各大传统都不接受“人可以不靠恩典自己得救”。分歧在更细的地方。<br>
The Council of Carthage condemned the Pelagians in 418, and the Council of Ephesus condemned them again in 431. Semi-Pelagianism, which later spread in southern Gaul, was condemned at the Council of Orange in 529. So all the major traditions reject the idea that people can be saved by their own efforts without grace. They differ on finer points.

## 今天的几种说法 | Views today

- **天主教**：《天主教教理》第 405 条说，原罪在亚当的后代身上“不具有个人过错的性质”，是“原始圣德和原始正义的丧失”；人的本性“没有完全败坏”，但在本有的能力上“受了伤”。洗礼除去原罪，但留下倾向犯罪的软弱。<br>
  **Catholic**: Paragraph 405 of the Catechism says that original sin “does not have the character of a personal fault” in Adam's descendants. It is “a deprivation of original holiness and justice.” Human nature “has not been totally corrupted,” but it is “wounded in the natural powers proper to it.” Baptism removes original sin but leaves an inclination to sin.
- **东正教**：常称为“祖先的罪”。美国正教会的解答说，所有人都承受亚当犯罪的后果，“最主要的是死亡”，但只有亚当和夏娃对那次的罪有罪责。东正教不接受“继承罪责”的说法。<br>
  **Orthodox**: Often called “ancestral sin.” The Orthodox Church in America explains that all people bear the consequences of Adam's sin, “the foremost of which is death,” but only Adam and Eve are guilty of that sin. The Orthodox Church does not accept the idea of inherited guilt.
- **改革宗**：《威斯敏斯德信条》第 6 章说，亚当的罪责“归算”给他所有的后代，败坏的本性也“传给他们所有的后代”，使人对善“完全没有意愿”。这常被称为“全然败坏”，意思是罪影响到人的每一部分，并不是说人坏到不能再坏。<br>
  **Reformed**: Chapter 6 of the Westminster Confession says that the guilt of Adam's sin was “imputed” to all his descendants, and that the corrupted nature was “conveyed to all their posterity,” leaving people “utterly indisposed” to good. This is often called “total depravity,” meaning that sin affects every part of a person, not that people are as bad as they could possibly be.
- **信义宗和卫斯理宗**也接受原罪的教导。卫斯理宗强调神的“先在恩典”使每个人都能回应福音。关于人的意志在得救中的作用，请看“神的主权和人的选择”一课。<br>
  **Lutheran and Wesleyan** traditions also accept the doctrine of original sin. Wesleyans stress that God's “prevenient grace” enables every person to respond to the gospel. On the role of the human will in salvation, see the lesson on God's sovereignty and human choice.

## 为什么重要 | Why it matters

这个教导解释了为什么世上的罪这样普遍：“世人都犯了罪，亏缺了神的荣耀”（罗 3:23）。它也说明了为什么人需要一位新的亚当。罗马书 5 章的重点在后半句：在亚当里众人成为罪人，在基督里众人得称为义。<br>
This teaching explains why sin is so universal: “all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God” (Romans 3:23). It also shows why humanity needs a new Adam. The weight of Romans 5 falls on its second half: in Adam many were made sinners, and in Christ many are made righteous.
