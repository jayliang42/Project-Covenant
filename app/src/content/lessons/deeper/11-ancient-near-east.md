## 以色列的邻居 | Israel's neighbours

旧约的故事发生在今天叫作“古代近东”的地区：西边是埃及，东边是两河流域的亚述和巴比伦，北边有赫人和亚兰人，周围还有腓尼基、摩押、以东等小国。以色列夹在这些国家中间，常被大国争夺。<br>
The Old Testament story takes place in the region now called the ancient Near East: Egypt to the west, Assyria and Babylon in Mesopotamia to the east, the Hittites and Arameans to the north, and smaller nations such as Phoenicia, Moab, and Edom nearby. Israel sat between these powers and was often fought over.

考古学家在这些地区发现了大量文献：泥版上的楔形文字、埃及的象形文字、王室碑文、法律、条约、神话和祷文。这些材料让我们更了解旧约写作时的世界。<br>
Archaeologists have found a great many texts from these lands: cuneiform on clay tablets, Egyptian hieroglyphs, royal inscriptions, laws, treaties, myths, and prayers. These help us understand the world in which the Old Testament was written.

## 相似和不同 | Similarities and differences

- **创造**：巴比伦的创世史诗《埃努玛·埃利什》讲诸神争战，世界是从被杀的女神身体造出来的。《创世记》1 章里只有一位神，用话语造了万物。它把太阳和月亮称为“大光”“小光”（创 1:16），没有叫出它们的名字。许多学者认为，这是在提醒读者：周围民族拜的日月只是神造的东西。<br>
  **Creation**: The Babylonian creation epic Enuma Elish tells of war among the gods, with the world made from the body of a slain goddess. In Genesis 1 there is one God who makes everything by his word. It calls the sun and moon “the greater light” and “the lesser light” (Genesis 1:16) without naming them. Many scholars think this tells readers that the sun and moon their neighbours worshipped are only things God made.
- **洪水**：《吉尔伽美什史诗》等文献也有大洪水和方舟的故事，细节和《创世记》有相似之处。两者的差别在于诸神发洪水的原因，以及神和人的关系。<br>
  **The flood**: The Epic of Gilgamesh and other texts also tell of a great flood and a boat, with some details similar to Genesis. They differ in why the flood came and in how God or the gods relate to people.
- **法律**：巴比伦的《汉谟拉比法典》（约主前 18 世纪）和《出埃及记》21 章有一些相似的条文，例如关于牛触死人的规定。旧约的律法放在神与百姓立约的框架里，开头是神的拯救。<br>
  **Law**: The Babylonian Code of Hammurabi (around the 18th century BC) has some laws similar to Exodus 21, such as rules about an ox that gores someone. Old Testament law is set within God's covenant with his people and begins with God's rescue.
- **条约**：古代赫人的宗主条约常有序言、历史回顾、条款、见证和祝福咒诅。不少学者认为，西奈之约和《申命记》的结构和这类条约相似，学者对细节仍有讨论。<br>
  **Treaties**: Ancient Hittite suzerain treaties often have a preamble, a historical prologue, stipulations, witnesses, and blessings and curses. Many scholars see a similar structure in the Sinai covenant and Deuteronomy, though the details are still debated.

## 历史记录的交汇 | Where the records meet

有些外部记录提到圣经里的人物和事件。《列王纪下》18:13 说亚述王西拿基立攻打犹大。西拿基立自己的记录（通常称为“西拿基立棱柱”）也记载了这次出征，说他把希西家困在耶路撒冷，“像笼中鸟一样”。双方的记录角度不同，都没有说亚述攻下了耶路撒冷。<br>
Some outside records mention people and events from the Bible. 2 Kings 18:13 says the Assyrian king Sennacherib attacked Judah. Sennacherib's own account, usually called Sennacherib's Prism, also records the campaign and says he shut Hezekiah up in Jerusalem “like a bird in a cage.” The two records see things from different sides, and neither says Assyria took Jerusalem.

波斯的《居鲁士圆柱》记载了塞鲁士（居鲁士）准许被掳的民族回乡、恢复神庙的政策。它没有提到犹太人，但这种政策和《以斯拉记》1 章的记载相符。<br>
The Persian Cyrus Cylinder records Cyrus's policy of allowing deported peoples to return home and restore their temples. It does not mention the Jews. The policy it describes fits the account in Ezra 1.

## 比较时要注意什么 | What to keep in mind when comparing

1. **相似不等于抄袭**：生活在同一个文化环境里，用相似的形式说话是很自然的。<br>
   **Similarity does not mean copying**: People in the same cultural setting naturally use similar forms.
2. **不同的地方更值得看**：旧约常用当时人熟悉的形式，说出和周围民族不同的信息：只有一位神，他与人立约，看重孤儿寡妇和寄居的人。<br>
   **The differences deserve the most attention**: The Old Testament often uses forms its readers knew to say something different from its neighbours: there is one God, he makes a covenant with people, and he cares for orphans, widows, and foreigners.
3. **说清楚证据能支持到哪里**：考古材料能说明背景，也能和圣经的某些记载对照。它们不能直接证明信仰的内容，也不能直接否定。<br>
   **Be clear about how far the evidence goes**: Archaeological materials can illuminate the background and can be compared with some biblical accounts. They cannot directly prove the content of faith, and they cannot directly disprove it either.

摩西对以色列人说：“哪一大国的人有神与他们相近，像耶和华我们的神、在我们求告他的时候与我们相近呢？”（申 4:7）。了解古代近东，可以帮助我们看出，以色列的信仰在当时的世界里有多么特别。<br>
Moses told Israel, “For what great nation is there that has a god so near to them as Yahweh our God is whenever we call on him?” (Deuteronomy 4:7). Knowing the ancient Near East helps us see how distinctive Israel's faith was in its world.
