## 两个词，两件事 | Two words, two things

“称义”和“成圣”常常一起出现，意思却不一样：<br>
“Justification” and “sanctification” often appear together, but they mean different things:

- **称义**：神宣告一个人在他面前是义的。这是法庭的用语，好像法官宣判无罪。它是一次性的，靠的是基督为我们成就的事。“我们既因信称义，就借着我们的主耶稣基督得与神相和”（罗 5:1）。<br>
  **Justification**: God declares a person righteous before him. It is a law-court term, like a judge's verdict of not guilty. It happens once, and it rests on what Christ has done for us. “Being therefore justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ” (Romans 5:1).
- **成圣**：一个人在生活中渐渐变得像基督。这是一生的过程，有进有退。“我们众人……就变成主的形状，荣上加荣”（林后 3:18）。<br>
  **Sanctification**: A person gradually becomes more like Christ in daily life. It is a lifelong process, with progress and setbacks. “We all ... are transformed into the same image from glory to glory” (2 Corinthians 3:18).

新约有时也用“成圣”指信徒一开始就被分别出来归给神（林前 6:11）。本课主要讲成圣作为一生过程的这一面。<br>
The New Testament sometimes also uses “sanctified” for the way believers are set apart for God from the start (1 Corinthians 6:11). This lesson focuses on sanctification as a lifelong process.

## 两者怎样连在一起 | How they connect

称义和成圣不能分开，也不能混在一起。<br>
Justification and sanctification cannot be separated, and they should not be confused.

不能分开：神称一个人为义，也会开始改变这个人。保罗在讲完因信称义以后马上问：“我们可以仍在罪中、叫恩典显多吗？断乎不可！”（罗 6:1-2）。雅各也说，没有行为的信心是死的（雅 2:26）。<br>
They cannot be separated: when God declares someone righteous, he also begins to change that person. Right after explaining justification by faith, Paul asks, “Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? May it never be!” (Romans 6:1–2). James also says that faith without works is dead (James 2:26).

不能混在一起：成圣的进度不是称义的根据。一个人今天表现得好或不好，都不会改变他在神面前因基督而有的地位。<br>
They should not be confused: progress in sanctification is not the basis of justification. Whether someone does well or badly today does not change the standing he has before God because of Christ.

## 弄混了会怎样 | What goes wrong when they are confused

- **把成圣当作称义的条件**：人会一直不安，觉得自己做得不够，神随时可能不要他。也可能因为做得不错而骄傲，看不起别人。<br>
  **Treating sanctification as a condition of justification**: People live in constant anxiety, feeling they never do enough and that God might reject them at any moment. Or they grow proud when they do well and look down on others.
- **只要称义，不要成圣**：人会觉得“反正已经得救了，怎样生活都无所谓”。保罗在《罗马书》6 章正是在回应这种想法。<br>
  **Wanting justification without sanctification**: People think, “I'm saved anyway, so how I live doesn't matter.” Paul answers exactly this idea in Romans 6.

## 神做工，人也做工 | God works, and we work

保罗写道：“就当恐惧战兢做成你们得救的工夫。因为你们立志行事都是神在你们心里运行，为要成就他的美意”（腓 2:12-13）。这两句话放在一起：人要努力，而人能努力，是因为神先在人心里做工。<br>
Paul writes, “Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God who works in you both to will and to work, for his good pleasure” (Philippians 2:12–13). The two sentences belong together: we must make an effort, and we can make an effort because God is already at work in us.

成圣常用的方法很平常：读经、祷告、参加教会、领圣餐、彼此认罪代祷、在具体的事上顺服。改变常常很慢，几个月、几年以后回头看，才看得出来。<br>
The usual means of sanctification are ordinary: reading Scripture, prayer, church life, the Lord's Supper, confessing to and praying for one another, and obeying in specific matters. Change is often slow, and you may only see it when you look back after months or years.

## 不同传统的说法 | How traditions put it

天主教传统通常把称义理解为神使人内在成为义的过程，和成圣联系得更紧。新教传统强调称义是神的宣告，和成圣有分别。1999 年信义宗世界联会和天主教会的《关于称义教义的联合声明》，表示双方在基本真理上有共识，用词和侧重仍有不同。东正教传统常用“神化”（theosis）来描述人在基督里被更新、分享神的生命。<br>
Catholic tradition usually understands justification as God making a person inwardly righteous, closely linked with sanctification. Protestant traditions stress that justification is God's declaration, distinct from sanctification. In the 1999 Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification, the Lutheran World Federation and the Catholic Church stated a consensus on basic truths while their wording and emphases still differ. Eastern Orthodox tradition often uses “theosis” to describe being renewed in Christ and sharing in God's life.
