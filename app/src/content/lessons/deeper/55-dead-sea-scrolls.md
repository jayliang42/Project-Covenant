## 山洞里的古卷 | Scrolls in the caves

1947 年，一个牧童在死海西北岸昆兰（Qumran）附近的山洞里发现了几卷古代的经卷。此后十几年里，考古学家和当地人在昆兰附近一共 11 个山洞里找到了数百份抄本的残片。大英百科说，昆兰的古卷几乎都是主前 3 世纪到主前 1 世纪抄写的。<br>
In 1947 a shepherd boy found several ancient scrolls in a cave near Qumran, on the northwestern shore of the Dead Sea. Over the following years archaeologists and local people found fragments of hundreds of manuscripts in eleven caves near Qumran. According to Britannica, the Qumran scrolls were copied almost entirely between the third and first centuries BC.

## 古卷里有什么 | What the scrolls contain

- **圣经抄本**：除了以斯帖记，希伯来圣经的每一卷都有抄本或残片。最有名的是几乎完整的大以赛亚书卷。这些抄本比以前已知最早的希伯来文抄本早了约一千年，让学者可以比对文本的传承。“新旧约抄本是怎么保存下来的”一课讲过这一点。<br>
  **Biblical manuscripts**: Every book of the Hebrew Bible except Esther is represented by copies or fragments. The best known is the almost complete Great Isaiah Scroll. These manuscripts are about a thousand years older than the earliest Hebrew manuscripts previously known, allowing scholars to compare how the text was passed down. The lesson on how manuscripts were preserved covers this.
- **群体自己的著作**：例如规定入会、生活和纪律的“会规”，对哈巴谷书等先知书的“注释”（把经文解释为应验在他们自己的时代），以及祷文和诗歌。<br>
  **The community's own writings**: For example a “Community Rule” governing admission, life, and discipline; “commentaries” on prophets such as Habakkuk, which read the texts as fulfilled in their own day; and prayers and hymns.
- **其他宗教文献**：例如一些后来没有收进希伯来圣经的书。<br>
  **Other religious literature**: For example some books that were not later included in the Hebrew Bible.

## 他们是谁 | Who they were

大英百科说，多数学者认为昆兰是拥有这些古卷的群体居住的地方；有人认为这是艾赛尼派的图书馆，但这一点并没有定论。从他们的著作看，这个群体认为耶路撒冷圣殿的祭司已经败坏，于是离开城市，在旷野过严格的群体生活，等候神在末日施行审判和拯救。他们称自己的领袖为“公义的教师”。<br>
According to Britannica, most scholars think Qumran was home to the community that owned the scrolls; some believe it was an Essene library, though this is not settled. Their writings show a community that believed the Jerusalem temple priesthood had become corrupt, and so withdrew from the city to live a strict communal life in the wilderness, awaiting God's final judgment and salvation. They called their leader the “Teacher of Righteousness.”

## 和新约有什么关系 | How they relate to the New Testament

古卷里没有提到耶稣，也没有提到基督徒。但它们帮助我们了解耶稣时代以前和同时代犹太人的盼望和争论：<br>
The scrolls mention neither Jesus nor Christians. But they help us understand the hopes and debates of Jews before and during Jesus' time:

- **旷野和以赛亚书 40 章**：昆兰群体用以赛亚书 40:3“在旷野预备耶和华的路”来解释自己为什么住在旷野。福音书用同一节经文来介绍施洗约翰（太 3:3）。两者都在等候神的拯救，但福音书说，约翰预备的是耶稣的道路。<br>
  **The wilderness and Isaiah 40**: The Qumran community used Isaiah 40:3, “Prepare the way of Yahweh in the wilderness,” to explain why they lived in the wilderness. The Gospels use the same verse to introduce John the Baptist (Matthew 3:3). Both awaited God's salvation, and the Gospels say John was preparing the way for Jesus.
- **弥赛亚的盼望**：古卷里有对弥赛亚的期待，有的文献似乎盼望不止一位受膏者。这说明当时的犹太人对弥赛亚有多种想象，也说明为什么人们对耶稣是谁有那么多争论。<br>
  **Messianic hope**: The scrolls express expectation of a messiah; some texts seem to anticipate more than one anointed figure. This shows the variety of messianic hopes among Jews then, and helps explain why there was so much debate about who Jesus was.
- **“光明之子”和“黑暗之子”**：古卷常用光明和黑暗的对比，这和约翰福音、约翰书信的用语有相似的地方。<br>
  **“Sons of light” and “sons of darkness”**: The scrolls often contrast light and darkness, language with similarities to John's Gospel and letters.

也有人说施洗约翰或耶稣曾经是昆兰群体的成员。这种说法没有直接的证据，大多数学者也不接受。<br>
Some have claimed that John the Baptist or Jesus belonged to the Qumran community. There is no direct evidence for this, and most scholars do not accept it.

## 为什么重要 | Why it matters

以赛亚说：“草必枯干，花必凋残，惟有我们神的话必永远立定”（赛 40:8）。死海古卷一方面让我们看到圣经文本在千年里传承得相当稳定，另一方面让我们更清楚地看见耶稣和使徒所在的犹太世界。<br>
Isaiah says, “The grass withers, the flower fades; but the word of our God stands forever” (Isaiah 40:8). The Dead Sea Scrolls show us how stable the biblical text remained over a thousand years, and they give us a clearer view of the Jewish world in which Jesus and the apostles lived.
