## 写给谁，为什么写 | For whom, and why

这卷书更像一篇写成文字的讲道，作者自己称它为“劝勉的话”（来 13:22）。读者很可能是一群熟悉旧约的信徒，他们曾经为信仰受苦，“家业被人抢去”（来 10:34），现在有人想要退回去，不再聚会（来 10:25）。作者要劝他们不要放弃。<br>
The book reads more like a sermon set down in writing, and the author calls it “the word of exhortation” (Hebrews 13:22). The readers were probably believers well versed in the Old Testament. They had suffered for their faith, even “the plundering of your possessions” (Hebrews 10:34), and now some were drawing back and no longer meeting together (Hebrews 10:25). The author writes to urge them not to give up.

## 主题：耶稣更美 | Theme: Jesus is better

全书反复用一个词：“更美”。作者一项一项地比较：<br>
The book keeps using one word: “better.” The author compares point by point:

- **比众先知更美**：“神既在古时借着众先知多次多方地晓谕列祖，就在这末世借着他儿子晓谕我们”（来 1:1-2）。<br>
  **Better than the prophets**: “God, having in the past spoken to the fathers through the prophets at many times and in various ways, has at the end of these days spoken to us by his Son” (Hebrews 1:1-2).
- **比天使更美**（1–2 章）、**比摩西更美**（3 章）。<br>
  **Better than the angels** (chapters 1–2) and **better than Moses** (chapter 3).
- **比亚伦的祭司职分更美**：耶稣是“照着麦基洗德的等次”永远为祭司的（5–7 章）。他“也曾凡事受过试探，与我们一样，只是他没有犯罪”（来 4:15）。<br>
  **Better than Aaron's priesthood**: Jesus is a priest forever “after the order of Melchizedek” (chapters 5–7). He “has been in all points tempted like we are, yet without sin” (Hebrews 4:15).
- **更美的约和更美的祭物**（8–10 章）：旧约的祭物要年年献，基督“只一次献上他的身体”（来 10:10）。<br>
  **A better covenant and a better sacrifice** (chapters 8–10): Old covenant sacrifices had to be offered year after year; Christ made “the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all” (Hebrews 10:10).

后面三章转向劝勉。11 章是“信心的名人录”，从亚伯讲到众先知。12 章说：“我们既有这许多的见证人，如同云彩围着我们……仰望为我们信心创始成终的耶稣”（来 12:1-2）。<br>
The last three chapters turn to exhortation. Chapter 11 is the roll call of faith, from Abel to the prophets. Chapter 12 says, “seeing we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses... looking to Jesus, the author and perfecter of faith” (Hebrews 12:1-2).

## 谁写的 | Who wrote it

书里没有作者的名字。作者说，这救恩是“听见的人给我们证实了”（来 2:3），可见他自己不是亲眼见过耶稣的门徒。<br>
The book does not name its author. The author says this salvation “was confirmed to us by those who heard” (Hebrews 2:3), which suggests he was not himself an eyewitness of Jesus.

早期教会对此就有不同的看法。东方教会较早认为它出于保罗；2 世纪末的亚历山大的革利免认为是保罗用希伯来文写成、路加译成希腊文的。西方的特土良认为是巴拿巴写的。3 世纪的俄利根认为书中的思想是保罗的，但执笔的另有其人，他说：“究竟是谁写的，只有神知道。”西方教会到 4、5 世纪的耶柔米和奥古斯丁以后，才普遍接受它出于保罗。<br>
The early church already held different views. The Eastern church accepted Pauline authorship relatively early; Clement of Alexandria in the late 2nd century thought Paul wrote it in Hebrew and Luke translated it into Greek. In the West, Tertullian ascribed it to Barnabas. In the 3rd century Origen held that the thoughts were Paul's but that someone else wrote them down, and he said, “who wrote the epistle, truly only God knows.” In the West Pauline authorship became general only after Jerome and Augustine in the 4th and 5th centuries.

宗教改革时，路德提出作者可能是亚波罗。今天大多数学者，包括很多福音派学者，认为作者不是保罗，因为希腊文的文风和保罗书信很不一样。人选有巴拿巴、亚波罗、路加、百基拉等，没有定论。教会一直接受这卷书为正典，作者是谁并不影响这一点。<br>
At the Reformation Luther suggested Apollos as the author. Most scholars today, including many evangelicals, think the author was not Paul, because the Greek style differs greatly from Paul's letters. Candidates include Barnabas, Apollos, Luke, and Priscilla, and none is settled. The church has always received the book as Scripture, and the question of authorship does not affect that.

## 几段严厉的警告 | The severe warnings

希伯来书有几段很严厉的警告。最常被讨论的是 6:4-6：“论到那些已经蒙了光照、尝过天恩的滋味、又于圣灵有分……的人，若是离弃道理，就不能叫他们重新懊悔了。”<br>
Hebrews contains several severe warnings. The one most discussed is 6:4-6: “concerning those who were once enlightened and tasted of the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Spirit... and then fell away, it is impossible to renew them again to repentance.”

这段经文和“得救的确据”那一课讲的分歧直接相关，信徒有几种理解：<br>
This passage bears directly on the disagreement described in the lesson on assurance, and believers understand it in several ways:

- **真信徒可能离弃信仰而失落**：这是卫斯理宗和许多阿民念派信徒的理解。警告是真实的，所以信徒要持守。<br>
  **True believers can fall away and be lost**: This is the reading of Wesleyans and many Arminians. The warning is real, so believers must hold fast.
- **说的是从来没有真正重生的人**：他们接触过信仰、经历过教会生活，但离开以后显明他们本来就不是真信徒。很多改革宗信徒这样理解。<br>
  **It describes people who were never born again**: They had contact with the faith and the life of the church, and their falling away shows they were never true believers. Many Reformed believers read it this way.
- **警告本身是神保守信徒的方法**：作者真诚地警告，神正是借着这样的警告使真信徒坚持到底。作者接着就说：“亲爱的弟兄们，我们虽是这样说，却深信你们的行为强过这些，而且近乎得救”（来 6:9）。<br>
  **The warning is itself God's means of keeping believers**: The author warns sincerely, and God uses such warnings to keep true believers to the end. The author goes straight on: “But, beloved, we are persuaded of better things for you, and things that accompany salvation” (Hebrews 6:9).

各种理解都同意作者的用意：不要漂流，要持守，因为“耶稣基督昨日、今日、一直到永远，是一样的”（来 13:8）。<br>
All these readings agree on the author's intent: do not drift, hold fast, because “Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever” (Hebrews 13:8).
