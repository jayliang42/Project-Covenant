## 三个部分 | Three parts

《出埃及记》40 章，按地点可以分成三部分：<br>
Exodus has 40 chapters, which divide by setting into three parts:

- **1–18 章：在埃及和往西奈的路上**。以色列人在埃及受奴役，神呼召摩西，十灾，逾越节，过红海，旷野里的吗哪和磐石出水。<br>
  **Chapters 1–18: in Egypt and on the way to Sinai.** Israel's slavery, the call of Moses, the ten plagues, the Passover, the crossing of the sea, manna and water from the rock in the wilderness.
- **19–24 章：西奈山立约**。神颁布十诫和律例，以色列人答应遵行，用血立约。<br>
  **Chapters 19–24: the covenant at Sinai.** God gives the Ten Commandments and the laws, Israel agrees to keep them, and the covenant is sealed with blood.
- **25–40 章：会幕**。神吩咐建造会幕；中间以色列人造金牛犊，摩西代求；最后会幕建成，“耶和华的荣光就充满了帐幕”（出 40:34）。<br>
  **Chapters 25–40: the tabernacle.** God gives instructions for the tabernacle; in the middle Israel makes the golden calf and Moses intercedes; at the end the tabernacle is finished and “Yahweh’s glory filled the tabernacle” (Exodus 40:34).

## 主线 | The main thread

**神听见了**。书一开头，以色列人在埃及作苦工。神说：“我的百姓在埃及所受的困苦，我实在看见了”（出 3:7）。他在燃烧的荆棘中向摩西显现，自称“我是自有永有的”（出 3:14）。<br>
**God has heard.** At the start the Israelites are slaves in Egypt. God says, “I have surely seen the affliction of my people who are in Egypt” (Exodus 3:7). He appears to Moses in the burning bush and names himself “I AM WHO I AM” (Exodus 3:14).

**先拯救，后立约**。神在西奈山对百姓说：“你们若实在听从我的话，遵守我的约，就要在万民中作属我的子民……归我作祭司的国度，为圣洁的国民”（出 19:5-6）。十诫的第一句是“我是耶和华你的神，曾将你从埃及地为奴之家领出来”。律法是给已经得救的人的，告诉他们怎样作神的百姓。<br>
**Rescue first, then covenant.** At Sinai God tells the people, “if you will indeed obey my voice, and keep my covenant, then you shall be my own possession from among all peoples... a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation” (Exodus 19:5-6). The first line of the Ten Commandments is “I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.” The law is given to people already rescued, to show them how to live as God's people.

**神要住在百姓中间**。全书最后十几章讲会幕，很多读者觉得枯燥。但这些章节的意思很简单：神要和他的百姓同住。金牛犊事件以后，神向摩西宣告自己的名：“有怜悯有恩典的神，不轻易发怒，并有丰盛的慈爱和诚实”（出 34:6）。这句话后来在旧约里被引用了很多次。<br>
**God comes to live among his people.** The last dozen or so chapters describe the tabernacle, and many readers find them dull. Their point is simple: God means to dwell with his people. After the golden calf, God proclaims his name to Moses: “a merciful and gracious God, slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness and truth” (Exodus 34:6). This sentence is quoted many times later in the Old Testament.

## 新约怎样读出埃及记 | How the New Testament reads Exodus

新约常常用出埃及的故事来讲耶稣的工作。耶稣在逾越节被钉十字架，保罗称他为“我们逾越节的羔羊”（林前 5:7）。约翰说道成了肉身，“住在我们中间”，“住”字原文就是“支帐棚”（约 1:14）。保罗把过红海比作洗礼（林前 10:1-2）。<br>
The New Testament often uses the exodus to speak of Jesus' work. Jesus was crucified at Passover, and Paul calls him “our Passover” (1 Corinthians 5:7). John says the Word became flesh and “lived among us” (John 1:14), where the Greek verb means to pitch a tent. Paul compares the crossing of the sea to baptism (1 Corinthians 10:1-2).

## 出埃及发生在什么时候 | When the exodus took place

学者对出埃及的年代有几种看法：<br>
Scholars hold several views on the date of the exodus:

- **早期说**：列王纪上 6:1 说，所罗门建殿是在以色列人出埃及后第 480 年。按常用的年表推算，出埃及大约在主前 1446 年。批评者说，那个时期的考古资料和这个年代不大吻合。<br>
  **Early date**: 1 Kings 6:1 says Solomon began the temple in the 480th year after Israel left Egypt. By the usual chronology this puts the exodus at about 1446 BC. Critics say the archaeology of that period does not fit this date well.
- **晚期说**：出埃及记 1:11 说以色列人为法老建造兰塞城，这个名字和主前 13 世纪的法老拉美西斯二世相合。主前 1208 年前后的麦伦普塔石碑提到迦南已经有“以色列”。持这种看法的人认为“480 年”可能是象征的数字，例如 12 代乘 40 年。<br>
  **Late date**: Exodus 1:11 says the Israelites built the city of Rameses for Pharaoh, a name that fits Ramesses II of the 13th century BC. The Merneptah Stele of about 1208 BC mentions “Israel” already in Canaan. Those who hold this view suggest that “480 years” may be symbolic, for example twelve generations of forty years.
- **不定年代说**：有些学者认为，出埃及无法定在某一个年代。他们认为这段叙事是以色列人对埃及的历史记忆经过长期流传形成的。<br>
  **No fixed date**: Some scholars hold that the exodus is not a single datable event and that the account grew from Israel's long-handed-down memories of Egypt.

持守圣经权威的学者多数采用前两种看法之一。各方都承认，出埃及是以色列信仰的根基，旧约一再回到这件事上来。<br>
Scholars who hold to the authority of Scripture mostly take one of the first two views. All agree that the exodus is the foundation of Israel's faith, and the Old Testament returns to it again and again.
