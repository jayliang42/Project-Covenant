## 叙事：记事多，评论少 | Narrative: much reporting, little comment

从创世记到以斯帖记，旧约有大量叙事。叙事的作者很少停下来说“这是对的”或“这是错的”。亚伯拉罕两次说妻子是妹妹，雅各骗父亲的祝福，士师耶弗他许愿献女儿，经文都只是记下来。<br>
From Genesis to Esther, the Old Testament holds a great deal of narrative. Its writers seldom stop to say “this was right” or “this was wrong.” Abraham twice calls his wife his sister, Jacob tricks his father out of a blessing, and the judge Jephthah vows his daughter. The text simply records these events.

所以读叙事，不能把每件事都当作榜样。可以问几个问题：<br>
So in reading narrative, not every event can be taken as an example. Ask a few questions:

- **结果怎样？** 雅各骗了哥哥，后来在舅舅拉班家里也受了骗，在那里服事了二十年。作者常用后果来评价。<br>
  **What came of it?** Jacob deceived his brother. Later he was deceived in turn by his uncle Laban, and served there twenty years. Writers often judge through consequences.
- **有没有总结句？** 士师记四次说“那时以色列中没有王”，最后一句是“各人任意而行”（士 21:25）。这句话是整卷书的评语。<br>
  **Is there a summary line?** Judges says four times that “there was no king in Israel,” and its last line is “Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes” (Judges 21:25). That line is the verdict on the whole book.
- **神在做什么？** 叙事的主角是神。人有成有败，神的应许一直往前推进。<br>
  **What is God doing?** God is the main actor in the narrative. People succeed and fail, and God's promise keeps moving forward.
- **放在整本圣经里看。** 保罗说：“从前所写的圣经都是为教训我们写的”（罗 15:4），又说以色列人遭遇的事“都要作为鉴戒”（林前 10:11）。<br>
  **Read it within the whole Bible.** Paul says, “whatever things were written before were written for our learning” (Romans 15:4), and that what happened to Israel happened “by way of example” (1 Corinthians 10:11).

## 律法：写给谁，今天怎样用 | Law: to whom, and how to use it today

出埃及记后半部、利未记、民数记和申命记有大量律法。这些律法是神在西奈山和以色列人立约时颁布的，有十诫，也有关于献祭、洁净、节期、土地、奴仆和刑罚的条文。<br>
The second half of Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy contain much law. God gave these laws when he made his covenant with Israel at Sinai. They include the Ten Commandments and rules on sacrifice, purity, feasts, land, servants, and penalties.

耶稣说：“莫想我来要废掉律法和先知。我来不是要废掉，乃是要成全”（太 5:17）。保罗说：“爱人的就完全了律法”（罗 13:8）。可是新约也说，信徒不必守割礼和饮食的条例（徒 15 章；可 7:19）。哪些条文今天仍然适用，哪些已经在基督里成全？各传统的回答不同。<br>
Jesus said, “Don't think that I came to destroy the law or the prophets. I didn't come to destroy, but to fulfill” (Matthew 5:17). Paul said, “he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law” (Romans 13:8). Yet the New Testament also says believers need not keep circumcision or the food laws (Acts 15; Mark 7:19). Which provisions still apply today, and which have been fulfilled in Christ? The traditions answer differently.

## 几种看法 | Several views

- **三分法**：把律法分成道德律、礼仪律、民事律。这个分法早在俄利根、耶柔米那里就有，阿奎那把它发展成系统。《威斯敏斯德信条》第 19 章说，礼仪律在新约之下已经废止；民事律随以色列国家一同终止，只有其中的“普遍公义”（general equity）原则仍有约束力；道德律永远约束所有人。改革宗和许多福音派教会采用这个分法。<br>
  **The threefold division**: The law is divided into moral, ceremonial, and civil (judicial) law. The distinction goes back to Origen and Jerome, and Aquinas developed it into a system. Chapter 19 of the Westminster Confession says the ceremonial laws are abrogated under the New Testament; the judicial laws expired with the nation of Israel, except where their “general equity” applies; and the moral law binds everyone forever. Reformed and many evangelical churches use this division.
- **对三分法的批评**：D. A. Carson 等学者认为，这个分法是后人加在经文上的框架，保罗自己没有这样分。例如安息日在十诫里，通常算作道德律，新约却没有重申它，保罗还把守日子看作各人可以自己决定的事（罗 14:5；西 2:16-17）。他们主张先看新约实际怎样处理每一条，再归类。<br>
  **Criticism of the threefold division**: Scholars such as D. A. Carson argue that the division is a framework later readers placed on the text, and that Paul himself does not divide the law this way. The Sabbath, for example, is among the Ten Commandments and is usually classed as moral law, yet the New Testament does not restate it, and Paul treats the observance of days as a matter for each person to decide (Romans 14:5; Colossians 2:16-17). They propose first observing how the New Testament actually handles each law, and only then classifying it.
- **信义宗**：常用“律法和福音”的区分来读。律法显明人的罪，把人引到基督面前；福音宣告白白的赦免。律法在信徒生活中也有指引的作用。<br>
  **Lutheran**: Lutherans often read through the distinction between law and gospel. The law shows people their sin and drives them to Christ; the gospel announces free forgiveness. The law also guides believers in daily life.
- **新约神学（New Covenant Theology）和一些浸信会学者**：认为整套摩西律法作为一个约已经在基督里成全，基督徒现在活在“基督的律法”之下（林前 9:21；加 6:2）。旧约的诫命，凡在新约重申的，基督徒就遵守。<br>
  **New Covenant Theology and some Baptist scholars**: They hold that the law of Moses as a whole covenant has been fulfilled in Christ, and that Christians now live under “the law of Christ” (1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 6:2). Christians keep the Old Testament commands that the New Testament restates.

## 一个实际的读法 | A practical way to read

无论采取哪种看法，读一条律法时都可以问：这条规定在当时要保护什么，要表明神的什么性情？它怎样指向基督？在新约里，这个原则怎样表达出来？例如申命记 22:8 要人在房顶周围安栏杆，免得有人掉下去，这条规定保护的是邻舍的生命。今天很多基督徒不住平顶房，同样的爱心可以表现在其他安全措施上。<br>
Whatever view one takes, when reading a law one can ask: What was this rule meant to protect at the time, and what does it show of God's character? How does it point to Christ? How is the principle expressed in the New Testament? Deuteronomy 22:8, for example, requires a railing around a roof so that no one falls. The rule protects a neighbour's life. Many Christians today do not live in flat-roofed houses. The same love can show itself in other safety measures.
