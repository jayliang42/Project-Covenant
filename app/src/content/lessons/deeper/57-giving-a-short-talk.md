## 先自己读懂 | First understand it yourself

尼希米记记载，以斯拉和利未人“清清楚楚地念神的律法书，讲明意思，使百姓明白所念的”（尼 8:8）。保罗对提摩太说，要“按着正意分解真理的道”（提后 2:15）。分享的第一步，是自己先把经文读懂。<br>
Nehemiah records that Ezra and the Levites “read in the book, in the law of God, distinctly; and they gave the sense, so that they understood the reading” (Nehemiah 8:8). Paul tells Timothy to be “properly handling the Word of Truth” (2 Timothy 2:15). The first step in sharing is to understand the passage yourself.

- **选一段不太长的经文**，十几分钟的分享，几节到一章就够了。<br>
  **Choose a passage that is not too long.** For a talk of ten or fifteen minutes, a few verses to a chapter is enough.
- **多读几遍**，最好提前几天开始，用不同译本对照。<br>
  **Read it several times**, ideally starting a few days ahead, comparing different translations.
- **用“观察、解释、应用”三步**：经文说了什么？在原来的上下文里是什么意思？对今天的我们有什么要求？<br>
  **Use observation, interpretation, and application**: What does the passage say? What did it mean in its original context? What does it ask of us today?
- **再查参考资料**，例如注释书或研读本，看看自己的理解有没有偏差。<br>
  **Then check reference works**, such as a commentary or study Bible, to see whether your understanding is off track.

## 找出一个中心意思 | Find one main point

试着用一句话说出这段经文的中心意思。例如路加福音 15 章浪子的比喻，可以说：“天父欢喜迎接每一个回家的人，也邀请留在家里的人一起欢喜。”整个分享都围绕这一句话来安排。<br>
Try to state the passage's main point in one sentence. For the parable of the lost son in Luke 15, for example: “The Father joyfully welcomes everyone who comes home, and invites those who stayed home to rejoice too.” Arrange the whole talk around that one sentence.

## 安排内容 | Arrange the content

一个简单的结构：<br>
A simple structure:

1. **开头**：用一个问题或一件生活中的事，引出经文要回答的问题。一两分钟就够了。<br>
   **Opening**: Use a question or an everyday situation to raise the question the passage answers. A minute or two is enough.
2. **讲解经文**：按经文的次序讲，把背景和关键的词句讲清楚，让听的人自己能在经文里看到你说的意思。<br>
   **Explaining the passage**: Follow the order of the text, making the background and key words clear, so listeners can see in the passage what you are saying.
3. **应用**：具体地说，这段经文对我们这一周的生活有什么意义。可以只提一两点。<br>
   **Application**: Say concretely what the passage means for our lives this week. One or two points are enough.
4. **结束**：回到中心意思，用祷告结束，或者留时间让大家回应。<br>
   **Close**: Return to the main point, and end with prayer or leave time for people to respond.

## 几种常见的毛病 | Some common faults

- **经文只当引子**：读了一节经文，接下来讲的却是自己本来想讲的话。彼得说：“若有讲道的，要按着神的圣言讲”（彼前 4:11）。<br>
  **Using the passage as a springboard**: Reading a verse and then talking about whatever you already wanted to say. Peter says, “If anyone speaks, let it be as it were the very words of God” (1 Peter 4:11).
- **内容太多**：把查到的所有资料都讲出来。好的分享常常需要删去很多好东西。<br>
  **Too much content**: Passing on everything you found. A good talk often requires cutting many good things.
- **超时**：尊重别人的时间。预先读一遍，掌握长度。<br>
  **Running over time**: Respect people's time. Rehearse once to judge the length.
- **只讲别人，不讲自己**：先让经文对自己说话，再分享给别人。<br>
  **Applying it only to others**: Let the passage speak to you first, then share it with others.

## 最后 | Finally

讲完以后，可以请一位信得过的人给你一些具体的意见。分享的能力是可以慢慢练出来的。重要的是让人看见神的话，讲的人自己并不是重点。<br>
Afterwards, ask someone you trust for specific feedback. The ability to share grows with practice. What matters is that people see God's word; the speaker is not the point.
