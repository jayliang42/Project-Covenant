## 谁写的 | Who wrote it

信的开头说是“神和主耶稣基督的仆人雅各”写给“散住十二个支派之人”的（雅 1:1）。新约里有好几个雅各。教会传统认为作者是“主的兄弟雅各”（加 1:19），他后来是耶路撒冷教会的领袖，在耶路撒冷会议上发言（徒 15:13）。持这个看法的学者常把它定在 40 年代到 60 年代初，是新约最早的书信之一。<br>
The letter opens as from “James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ,” to “the twelve tribes which are in the Dispersion” (James 1:1). There are several men named James in the New Testament. Church tradition identifies the author as “James, the Lord's brother” (Galatians 1:19), who later led the church in Jerusalem and spoke at the Jerusalem council (Acts 15:13). Scholars who hold this view often date it from the 40s to the early 60s, making it one of the earliest New Testament letters.

大英百科介绍的另一种看法认为，它可能写于 1 世纪末，是后人借雅各的名字写的。学者对年代的看法差别很大。<br>
Another view, summarised by Britannica, holds that it may have been written near the end of the first century under James's name. Scholars differ widely on its date.

## 内容 | Content

雅各书像旧约的智慧书，也有很多地方和登山宝训相近。它不像保罗书信那样一步一步论证，而是一段一段地劝勉：<br>
James resembles the Old Testament wisdom books and has much in common with the Sermon on the Mount. It does not argue step by step like Paul's letters; it exhorts section by section:

- **试炼和智慧（1 章）**：遇见试炼要喜乐，缺少智慧要向神求。<br>
  **Trials and wisdom (chapter 1)**: Count trials as joy, and ask God for wisdom.
- **行道（1 章）**：“只是你们要行道，不要单单听道，自己欺哄自己”（雅 1:22）。“清洁没有玷污的虔诚，就是看顾在患难中的孤儿寡妇”（雅 1:27）。<br>
  **Doing the word (chapter 1)**: “Be doers of the word, and not only hearers, deluding your own selves” (James 1:22). “Pure religion and undefiled... is this: to visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction” (James 1:27).
- **不可偏待人（2 章）**：不要因为人穿得体面就尊重他，看不起穷人。<br>
  **No favouritism (chapter 2)**: Don't honour the well-dressed and despise the poor.
- **信心和行为（2 章）**：“信心若没有行为就是死的”（雅 2:17）。<br>
  **Faith and works (chapter 2)**: “Faith, if it has no works, is dead in itself” (James 2:17).
- **舌头（3 章）**：“最小的火能点着最大的树林”（雅 3:5）。<br>
  **The tongue (chapter 3)**: “See how a small fire can spread to a large forest!” (James 3:5).
- **骄傲、计划和财富（4–5 章）**：不要说“明天我们要往某城去”，要说“主若愿意”；警告欺压工人的富人。<br>
  **Pride, plans, and wealth (chapters 4–5)**: Don't say, “Tomorrow we will go into this city”; say, “If the Lord wills.” A warning to the rich who oppress their workers.
- **忍耐和祷告（5 章）**：病了要请长老来祷告，彼此认罪，互相代求。<br>
  **Patience and prayer (chapter 5)**: The sick should call the elders to pray; confess to one another and pray for one another.

## 路德和“稻草的书信” | Luther and the “epistle of straw”

1522 年，路德在德文新约的总序言里说，和约翰福音、保罗书信（特别是罗马书、加拉太书、以弗所书）、彼得前书相比，雅各书是“稻草的书信”。按 Themelios 一篇文章的分析，“稻草”借用的是林前 3 章的比喻，意思是它比较少直接讲福音，并不是说它没有价值。1537 年以后的德文圣经删去了这句话，但路德一直认为雅各书在称义的问题上和保罗不一致，把它排在新约的最后。他仍然在讲道中使用雅各书。<br>
In 1522, in the general preface to his German New Testament, Luther said that compared with John's Gospel, Paul's letters (especially Romans, Galatians, and Ephesians), and 1 Peter, James was “an epistle of straw.” According to an article in Themelios, “straw” borrows the image of 1 Corinthians 3 and means that James says less directly about the gospel, not that it is worthless. The phrase was dropped from German Bibles after 1537, but Luther continued to think James at odds with Paul on justification and placed it at the end of the New Testament. He still preached from James.

## 雅各和保罗冲突吗 | Do James and Paul conflict?

保罗说：“人称义是因着信，不在乎遵行律法”（罗 3:28）。雅各说：“人称义是因着行为，不是单因着信”（雅 2:24）。常见的解释有几层：<br>
Paul says, “a man is justified by faith apart from the works of the law” (Romans 3:28). James says, “by works, a man is justified, and not only by faith” (James 2:24). Common explanations work on several levels:

- **用词的意思不同**：改革宗常解释说，保罗讲的“称义”是神宣告人为义；雅各讲的是行为证明一个人的信心是真的。英国改教家丁道尔说：“信心使人在神面前称义，行为使人在世人面前称义。”<br>
  **Different senses of the word**: Reformed interpreters often explain that Paul uses “justify” for God declaring a person righteous, while James uses it for works showing that a person's faith is real. The English Reformer William Tyndale put it this way: “as faith only justifieth before God, so do deeds only justify before the world.”
- **反对的对象不同**：保罗反对的是靠守律法得救；雅各反对的是只在口头上说信、却没有行动的“信心”，连鬼魔也“信”（雅 2:19）。<br>
  **Different targets**: Paul opposes salvation by keeping the law; James opposes a “faith” that is only words without action, since even the demons “believe” (James 2:19).
- **其他传统**：天主教和东正教一般认为，信心和行为在得救的过程中一同起作用，雅各书正好说明这一点。“称义和成圣”一课介绍了各传统的看法。<br>
  **Other traditions**: Catholic and Orthodox Christians generally hold that faith and works operate together in salvation, and see James as making exactly this point. The lesson on justification and sanctification introduces the traditions' views.

保罗自己也说，有功效的是“使人生发仁爱的信心”（加 5:6）。在这一点上，两位使徒说的是同一件事的两面。<br>
Paul himself says what counts is “faith working through love” (Galatians 5:6). On this point the two apostles describe two sides of one thing.
