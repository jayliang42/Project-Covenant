## 伊甸园：神与人同在的地方 | Eden: where God and people were together

《创世记》说，耶和华神在园中行走（创 3:8）。不少学者注意到，后来会幕和圣殿的装饰常有基路伯、树木花果和金子，入口朝东，这些和伊甸园的描写相似。伊甸园可以看作第一个神与人同在的地方。<br>
Genesis describes Yahweh God walking in the garden (Genesis 3:8). Many scholars note that the later tabernacle and temple were decorated with cherubim, trees, fruit, and gold, and faced east, all of which echo the description of Eden. Eden can be seen as the first place where God and people were together.

人犯罪以后被赶出园子，基路伯守住了通往生命树的路（创 3:24）。从这里开始，圣经一直在问：人还能回到神面前吗？<br>
After they sinned, people were sent out of the garden, and cherubim guarded the way to the tree of life (Genesis 3:24). From here on, the Bible keeps asking: can people come back into God's presence?

## 会幕和圣殿 | The tabernacle and the temple

出埃及以后，神吩咐摩西：“又当为我造圣所，使我可以住在他们中间”（出 25:8）。会幕造好的时候，“耶和华的荣光就充满了帐幕”（出 40:34）。会幕是可以移动的帐篷，跟着以色列人在旷野行走。<br>
After the exodus, God told Moses, “Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8). When the tabernacle was finished, “Yahweh’s glory filled the tabernacle” (Exodus 40:34). The tabernacle was a portable tent that travelled with Israel through the wilderness.

所罗门在耶路撒冷建了圣殿。他在献殿时祷告：“神果真住在地上吗？看哪，天和天上的天尚且不足你居住的，何况我所建的这殿呢？”（王上 8:27）。神仍然应许垂听在这殿里的祷告。<br>
Solomon built the temple in Jerusalem. At its dedication he prayed, “Will God in very deed dwell on the earth? Behold, heaven and the heaven of heavens can’t contain you; how much less this house that I have built!” (1 Kings 8:27). God still promised to hear prayers made toward this house.

圣殿里最里面的至圣所，只有大祭司每年进去一次。以色列人不断背约以后，先知以西结看见“耶和华的荣耀从殿的门槛那里出去”（结 10:18）。不久，巴比伦烧毁了圣殿。被掳归回后重建的第二圣殿，规模远不如前，先知哈该却应许“这殿后来的荣耀必大过先前的荣耀”（该 2:9）。<br>
Only the high priest entered the innermost room, the most holy place, once a year. After Israel had broken the covenant again and again, the prophet Ezekiel saw “Yahweh’s glory went out from over the threshold of the house” (Ezekiel 10:18). Soon afterward Babylon burned the temple. The second temple, rebuilt after the exile, was far less grand, yet the prophet Haggai promised, “The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former” (Haggai 2:9).

## 耶稣：神住在人中间 | Jesus: God living among people

约翰写道：“道成了肉身，住在我们中间……我们也见过他的荣光”（约 1:14）。“住”的希腊文原意是“支搭帐幕”，让人想起会幕。耶稣还说：“你们拆毁这殿，我三日内要再建立起来。”约翰解释说：“耶稣这话是以他的身体为殿”（约 2:19-21）。<br>
John writes, “The Word became flesh, and lived among us. We saw his glory” (John 1:14). The Greek word for “lived” literally means “pitched his tent,” recalling the tabernacle. Jesus also said, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up,” and John explains, “He spoke of the temple of his body” (John 2:19–21).

耶稣死的时候，圣殿里的幔子裂开了（可 15:38）。从此人可以借着耶稣来到神面前。<br>
When Jesus died, the veil in the temple was torn (Mark 15:38). From then on, people could come to God through Jesus.

## 教会和新耶路撒冷 | The church and the New Jerusalem

五旬节圣灵降临以后，神住在信徒中间。保罗对哥林多教会说：“岂不知你们是神的殿，神的灵住在你们里头吗？”（林前 3:16）。<br>
After the Spirit came at Pentecost, God lived among believers. Paul told the church in Corinth, “Don’t you know that you are a temple of God, and that God’s Spirit lives in you?” (1 Corinthians 3:16).

到了圣经的结尾，有声音说：“神的帐幕在人间。他要与人同住”（启 21:3）。约翰说：“我未见城内有殿，因主神全能者和羔羊为城的殿”（启 21:22）。伊甸园里开始的事，在这里完成了：神与人同在，没有阻隔。<br>
At the end of the Bible a voice says, “God’s dwelling is with people, and he will dwell with them” (Revelation 21:3). John says, “I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God, the Almighty, and the Lamb, are its temple” (Revelation 21:22). What began in Eden is completed here: God and people together, with nothing in between.
