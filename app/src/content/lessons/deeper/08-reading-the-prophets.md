## 先知是什么人 | Who the prophets were

旧约的先知书包括大先知书（以赛亚书、耶利米书、以西结书、但以理书，还有耶利米哀歌）和十二卷小先知书。“大”“小”指的是篇幅长短。<br>
The prophetic books of the Old Testament include the Major Prophets (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and Daniel, along with Lamentations) and the twelve Minor Prophets. “Major” and “minor” refer to length.

先知是代神说话的人。神对耶利米说：“我已将当说的话传给你”（耶 1:9）。先知的话大部分是对当时的人说的，讲的是当时的罪和当时的选择。他们讲到将来的部分，也和这些有关。<br>
A prophet was someone who spoke for God. God told Jeremiah, “Behold, I have put my words in your mouth” (Jeremiah 1:9). Most of what the prophets said was addressed to their own generation, about the sins and choices of their own day. What they said about the future was connected with these too.

## 先知的话讲什么 | What the prophets said

先知的信息大多和神与以色列立的约有关，常有三部分：<br>
Most of the prophets' message is tied to God's covenant with Israel, and it usually has three parts:

1. **指出违约**：拜偶像、欺压穷人、只在形式上敬拜。阿摩司说：“惟愿公平如大水滚滚，使公义如江河滔滔”（摩 5:24）。<br>
   **Exposing covenant breaking**: idolatry, oppression of the poor, and empty worship. Amos said, “Let justice roll on like rivers, and righteousness like a mighty stream” (Amos 5:24).
2. **警告审判**：如果不回转，就会有灾祸，最后是亡国和被掳。<br>
   **Warning of judgment**: If they did not turn back, disaster would come, ending in the fall of the nation and exile.
3. **应许恢复**：审判以后神仍要施恩。“你们要安慰，安慰我的百姓”（赛 40:1）。以西结看见枯骨复活（结 37 章），耶利米听见新约的应许（耶 31 章）。<br>
   **Promising restoration**: After judgment God would still show mercy. “Comfort, comfort my people,” says your God (Isaiah 40:1). Ezekiel saw dry bones come to life (Ezekiel 37), and Jeremiah received the promise of a new covenant (Jeremiah 31).

## 警告常常带着条件 | Warnings often carry conditions

神借耶利米说，他若宣告要毁灭一国，“那一邦，若是转意离开他们的恶，我就必后悔，不将我想要施行的灾祸降与他们”（耶 18:8）。约拿宣告“再等四十日，尼尼微必倾覆了”，尼尼微人悔改，神就没有降灾（拿 3:4，10）。<br>
Through Jeremiah God said that if he announced the destruction of a nation, and “that nation ... turns from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do to them” (Jeremiah 18:8). Jonah announced, “In forty days, Nineveh will be overthrown!”, and when Nineveh repented, God did not bring the disaster (Jonah 3:4, 10).

所以读先知的警告时，要记住它的目的是叫人回转。<br>
So when you read the prophets' warnings, remember that their aim was to turn people back.

## 预言和应验 | Prophecy and fulfilment

新约常说某件事“应验”了先知的话。这里的“应验”有几种情况：<br>
The New Testament often says that something “fulfilled” what a prophet had said. Fulfilment works in more than one way:

- **直接的应许**：例如弥赛亚要从大卫的后裔中出来。<br>
  **Direct promises**: for example, that the Messiah would come from David's line.
- **重演一个模式**：马太引用“我从埃及召出我的儿子来”（太 2:15）。何西阿原来说的是以色列出埃及（何 11:1）。马太看到，耶稣在自己身上重演了以色列的历史。<br>
  **A pattern repeated**: Matthew quotes “Out of Egypt I called my son” (Matthew 2:15). Hosea was originally speaking of Israel's exodus (Hosea 11:1). Matthew saw that Jesus re-lived Israel's story in his own life.
- **分几步实现**：约珥说神要“将我的灵浇灌凡有血气的”（珥 2:28），彼得在五旬节说“这正是先知约珥所说的”（徒 2:16）。新约也盼望它在将来完全实现。<br>
  **Fulfilment in stages**: Joel said God would “pour out my Spirit on all flesh” (Joel 2:28), and at Pentecost Peter said, “This is what has been spoken through the prophet Joel” (Acts 2:16). The New Testament still looks forward to its full completion.

## 读先知书的几个建议 | Some suggestions for reading

- 先查这卷书的背景：先知在哪个王的时候、对哪个国家说话。<br>
  First find out the setting: under which king the prophet spoke, and to which nation.
- 一次读一整段，看它是责备、警告还是安慰。<br>
  Read a whole section at a time and ask whether it is rebuke, warning, or comfort.
- 留意先知用的图像，例如葡萄园、婚姻、牧人和羊。<br>
  Notice the images the prophets use, such as the vineyard, marriage, and the shepherd and his sheep.
- 不要用先知书去推算当代新闻和末日日期。<br>
  Do not use the prophets to decode today's headlines or calculate the date of the end.
