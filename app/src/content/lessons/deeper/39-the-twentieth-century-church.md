## 五旬节运动 | Pentecostalism

1900 年，传道人帕翰（Charles Fox Parham）在美国堪萨斯州的托皮卡开办了一所圣经学校。1901 年 1 月 1 日，学生奥兹曼（Agnes Ozman）在祷告中说起方言。帕翰教导说，说方言是受“圣灵的洗”的记号。<br>
In 1900 the preacher Charles Fox Parham opened a Bible school in Topeka, Kansas. On 1 January 1901 a student, Agnes Ozman, spoke in tongues while praying. Parham taught that speaking in tongues is the sign of “baptism in the Holy Spirit.”

帕翰的学生、黑人传道人西摩（William J. Seymour）把这个教导带到洛杉矶。从 1906 年 4 月 9 日起，他带领的聚会吸引了大批人，后来在阿苏萨街的使徒信心会持续了几年。不同种族的人在一起敬拜，这在当时的美国很少见。1914 年，一批五旬节派传道人成立了神召会（Assemblies of God），后来成为最大的五旬节宗派之一。<br>
Parham's student, the Black preacher William J. Seymour, took this teaching to Los Angeles. From 9 April 1906 the meetings he led drew large crowds and continued for several years at the Apostolic Faith Mission on Azusa Street. People of different races worshipped together, which was rare in America at the time. In 1914 a group of Pentecostal ministers formed the Assemblies of God, which became one of the largest Pentecostal denominations.

1960 年代起，五旬节派对圣灵的洗的教导进入圣公会、信义会和天主教等传统教会，叫作“灵恩运动”。皮尤研究中心 2011 年的报告引用的数字是，全世界约有 2.79 亿五旬节派信徒。如果把灵恩派也算进去，到 2020 年代中期的估计约为 6.44 亿，约占全世界基督徒的四分之一。<br>
From the 1960s the Pentecostal teaching on Spirit baptism entered older churches such as the Anglican, Lutheran, and Catholic churches. This became known as the charismatic movement. A 2011 Pew Research Center report cited a figure of about 279 million Pentecostals worldwide. Counting charismatics as well, an estimate for the mid-2020s is about 644 million, roughly a quarter of the world's Christians.

关于说方言、医病和预言这些恩赐今天是否继续，各传统看法很不相同，“各传统的不同教导”单元里“属灵恩赐今天还有吗”一课会介绍。<br>
The traditions differ widely on whether gifts such as tongues, healing, and prophecy continue today. The lesson on spiritual gifts today, in the unit on where the traditions differ, introduces the views.

## 合一运动 | The ecumenical movement

宗派林立让很多基督徒不安，尤其是在宣教工场上。1948 年 8 月 22 日，普世教会协会（World Council of Churches）在荷兰阿姆斯特丹成立，来自 44 个国家的 147 个教会团体派代表参加，大部分东正教会也在其中。天主教会受到邀请，但没有参加。<br>
Many Christians were troubled by the number of separate denominations, especially in mission fields. On 22 August 1948 the World Council of Churches was founded in Amsterdam, with representatives of 147 church bodies from 44 countries, including most of the Orthodox churches. The Catholic Church was invited but declined to take part.

1959 年 1 月 25 日，教宗若望二十三世宣布召开大公会议。第二次梵蒂冈大公会议在 1962 年 10 月 11 日开幕，1965 年闭幕。会议的决定包括允许用各地的语言举行弥撒，并开始和其他基督教会对话。<br>
On 25 January 1959 Pope John XXIII announced an ecumenical council. The Second Vatican Council opened on 11 October 1962 and closed in 1965. Its decisions included allowing the Mass to be celebrated in local languages and opening dialogue with other Christian churches.

对合一运动，各教会的看法不同。有的教会积极参与。也有不少教会没有加入普世教会协会，其中包括许多福音派教会；一些福音派人士担心，追求组织上的合一会淡化对福音和圣经权威的坚持。<br>
Churches view the ecumenical movement differently. Some take an active part. Many others, including many evangelical churches, have not joined the World Council of Churches; some evangelicals feared that seeking organisational unity would weaken commitment to the gospel and the authority of Scripture.

## 洛桑大会 | The Lausanne Congress

1974 年 7 月，在葛培理（Billy Graham）的推动下，约 2,700 名来自 150 多个国家的参加者和来宾在瑞士洛桑举行世界福音大会。会上发表的《洛桑信约》由斯托得（John Stott）主持起草。后来又有 1989 年的马尼拉大会（约 3,000 人，来自 170 个国家）和 2010 年的开普敦大会（约 4,000 人，来自 198 个国家）。<br>
In July 1974, at the initiative of Billy Graham, some 2,700 participants and guests from over 150 nations gathered in Lausanne, Switzerland, for the International Congress on World Evangelization. The Lausanne Covenant issued there was drafted by a committee chaired by John Stott. Later gatherings followed in Manila in 1989 (about 3,000 people from 170 countries) and Cape Town in 2010 (about 4,000 people from 198 countries).

## 基督徒住在哪里 | Where Christians live

20 世纪最大的变化也许是基督徒分布的改变。皮尤研究中心 2011 年的报告说，1910 年约三分之二的基督徒住在欧洲；到 2010 年，住在欧洲的基督徒只占约四分之一（26%），约 61% 住在南半球的发展中地区，也就是非洲、拉丁美洲和亚洲等地。全世界约有 21.8 亿基督徒，接近世界人口的三分之一。<br>
Perhaps the biggest change of the twentieth century was in where Christians live. A 2011 Pew Research Center report says that in 1910 about two-thirds of the world's Christians lived in Europe. By 2010 only about a quarter (26%) did, and about 61% lived in the Global South, in regions such as Africa, Latin America, and Asia. There were about 2.18 billion Christians, nearly a third of the world's population.

启示录描写的景象是“从各国、各族、各民、各方来的”人站在宝座前（启 7:9）。20 世纪的历史让很多基督徒更清楚地看到，教会不属于某一个地区或文化。<br>
Revelation pictures people “out of every nation and of all tribes, peoples, and languages” standing before the throne (Revelation 7:9). The history of the twentieth century helped many Christians see more clearly that the church does not belong to any single region or culture.
