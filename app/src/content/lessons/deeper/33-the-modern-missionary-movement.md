## 在克理以前 | Before Carey

福音向外传，从使徒时代就开始了：“在耶路撒冷、犹太全地，和撒马利亚，直到地极，作我的见证”（徒 1:8）。中世纪的修士把福音带到北欧和东欧；7 世纪景教传到唐朝的长安；16 世纪以后，天主教的耶稣会、方济各会等修会的传教士到了美洲、印度、日本和中国，利玛窦就是其中一位。<br>
The spread of the gospel began in the apostolic age: “You will be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the uttermost parts of the earth” (Acts 1:8). Medieval monks carried the gospel to northern and eastern Europe. In the 7th century the Church of the East reached Chang'an under the Tang. From the 16th century Catholic missionaries of the Jesuits, Franciscans, and other orders went to the Americas, India, Japan, and China; Matteo Ricci was one of them.

新教在宗教改革以后的头两百年，向海外传福音的工作很少。18 世纪德国的摩拉维亚弟兄会是一个早期的例外，他们派人到加勒比海和格陵兰等地。<br>
In the first two centuries after the Reformation, Protestants did little to spread the gospel overseas. An early exception was the Moravian Brethren in 18th-century Germany, who sent workers to the Caribbean, Greenland, and elsewhere.

## 克理威廉 | William Carey

克理是英格兰的一位鞋匠，后来成了浸信会的牧师。1792 年 5 月 12 日，他发表了《论基督徒使用方法使异教徒归主的责任》，主张耶稣“使万民作我的门徒”（太 28:19）的吩咐今天仍然约束所有基督徒。同年 10 月 2 日，浸信会差会在凯特琳成立。1793 年 11 月，克理带着妻子和四个孩子到了加尔各答。<br>
Carey was a shoemaker in England who became a Baptist pastor. On 12 May 1792 he published An Enquiry into the Obligations of Christians to Use Means for the Conversion of the Heathens, arguing that Jesus' command to “make disciples of all nations” (Matthew 28:19) still binds all Christians. On 2 October that year the Baptist Missionary Society was founded at Kettering. In November 1793 Carey arrived in Calcutta with his wife and four children.

克理在印度工作了四十年，没有回过英国。他和同工在塞兰坡把整本圣经译成了六种语言，部分经卷译成了二十多种语言。“圣经怎样来到中文世界”一课会讲到，最早的中文全本圣经之一也是在塞兰坡完成的。<br>
Carey worked in India for forty years without returning to England. With his colleagues at Serampore he translated the whole Bible into six languages and parts of it into more than twenty more. As the lesson on the Bible in Chinese explains, one of the first complete Chinese Bibles was also produced at Serampore.

克理以后，英美相继成立了许多差会。19 世纪被一些历史学家称为新教的“宣教大世纪”。<br>
After Carey, many mission societies were founded in Britain and America. Some historians call the 19th century the “great century” of Protestant mission.

## 戴德生和内地会 | Hudson Taylor and the China Inland Mission

英国人戴德生 1853 年第一次到中国。1865 年，他创立中国内地会，目标是把福音带到当时还没有宣教士的中国内地省份。内地会的宣教士穿中国人的衣服，不分宗派，不公开募捐。1875 年，戴德生呼吁派十八个人去九个没有宣教士的省份；1876 年到 1880 年之间，内地会的宣教士进入了这些省份。<br>
The Englishman Hudson Taylor first arrived in China in 1853. In 1865 he founded the China Inland Mission, aiming to take the gospel to the interior provinces of China that had no missionaries. CIM missionaries wore Chinese dress, were non-denominational, and made no public appeals for money. In 1875 Taylor appealed for eighteen people to go to nine provinces without missionaries, and between 1876 and 1880 CIM workers entered those provinces.

## 1910 年爱丁堡大会 | Edinburgh 1910

1910 年 6 月，世界宣教大会在苏格兰爱丁堡召开，有 1215 位代表参加。代表由各差会派出，所以亚洲代表只有十几位，没有来自非洲和拉丁美洲的代表。会上，来自中国的诚静怡发言，盼望早日建立一个不分宗派、合一的中国教会。这次大会通常被看作普世合一运动的开端。<br>
In June 1910 the World Missionary Conference met in Edinburgh, Scotland, with 1,215 delegates. Delegates were sent by the mission societies, so there were only about fifteen from Asia and none from Africa or Latin America. At the conference Cheng Jingyi from China spoke of his hope for a united, non-denominational Chinese church in the near future. The conference is usually regarded as the beginning of the ecumenical movement.

## 复杂的一面 | The complicated side

宣教运动和西方殖民扩张发生在同一个时代，两者有时交织在一起。在中国，19 世纪的几个不平等条约里有保护传教的条款，这使很多中国人把基督教看作“洋教”，和列强的势力连在一起。也有宣教士带着文化上的优越感，把西方的习俗当作信仰的一部分。<br>
The missionary movement took place in the same era as Western colonial expansion, and the two were sometimes intertwined. In China, several of the 19th-century unequal treaties included clauses protecting missionary work, which led many Chinese to see Christianity as a “foreign religion” linked to the power of the Western nations. Some missionaries also carried a sense of cultural superiority and treated Western customs as part of the faith.

同时也要看到，很多宣教士反对鸦片贸易，办学校和医院，翻译圣经时整理和推广了当地的文字，有的人为当地的百姓付出了生命。各国的信徒对这段历史有不同的评价，很多教会今天会公开反省其中的错误。<br>
It should also be seen that many missionaries opposed the opium trade, founded schools and hospitals, and in translating the Bible standardised and spread local scripts, and some gave their lives for the people they served. Believers in different countries judge this history differently, and many churches today openly reflect on its wrongs.

## 今天的世界教会 | The world church today

皮尤研究中心的统计显示，1910 年，全世界约 93% 的基督徒住在欧洲和美洲；到 2010 年，这个比例降到约 63%。今天，撒哈拉以南非洲、拉丁美洲和亚洲的信徒越来越多，很多国家的教会也开始差派宣教士到别的国家去。<br>
Pew Research Center figures show that in 1910 about 93 percent of the world's Christians lived in Europe and the Americas, and by 2010 the share had fallen to about 63 percent. Today believers in sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America, and Asia are increasing, and churches in many of these countries now send missionaries to other lands.

启示录描写的最后的景象是：“有许多的人，没有人能数过来，是从各国、各族、各民、各方来的，站在宝座和羔羊面前”（启 7:9）。<br>
The final scene Revelation describes is “a great multitude, which no man could count, out of every nation and of all tribes, peoples, and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb” (Revelation 7:9).
