## 圣经怎样看性 | How the Bible regards sex

圣经没有把性看作羞耻的事。创世记说，神造男造女，“人要离开父母，与妻子连合，二人成为一体”（创 2:24）。《雅歌》整卷书都在歌颂夫妻之间的爱。保罗嘱咐夫妻“不可彼此亏负”（林前 7:5）。<br>
The Bible does not treat sex as shameful. Genesis says that God made them male and female, and “a man will leave his father and his mother, and will join with his wife, and they will be one flesh” (Genesis 2:24). The whole Song of Songs celebrates love between husband and wife. Paul tells married couples, “Don’t deprive one another” (1 Corinthians 7:5).

圣经也把性放在婚姻的盟约里。“婚姻，人人都当尊重，床也不可污秽；因为苟合行淫的人，神必要审判”（来 13:4）。保罗说：“你们要逃避淫行……岂不知你们的身子就是圣灵的殿吗？……所以，要在你们的身子上荣耀神”（林前 6:18-20）。耶稣更进一步说，“凡看见妇女就动淫念的，这人心里已经与她犯奸淫了”（太 5:28），把标准从行为推到了心里。<br>
The Bible also places sex within the covenant of marriage. “Let marriage be held in honor among all, and let the bed be undefiled; but God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers” (Hebrews 13:4). Paul says, “Flee sexual immorality!... don’t you know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit...? Therefore glorify God in your body” (1 Corinthians 6:18-20). Jesus goes further: “everyone who gazes at a woman to lust after her has committed adultery with her already in his heart” (Matthew 5:28), moving the standard from conduct to the heart.

圣经也看重独身。耶稣说有人“为天国的缘故”不结婚（太 19:12），保罗认为独身是一种恩赐（林前 7:7）。<br>
The Bible also honours singleness. Jesus speaks of those who remain unmarried “for the Kingdom of Heaven’s sake” (Matthew 19:12), and Paul regards singleness as a gift (1 Corinthians 7:7).

## 各教会历来共同的教导 | What the churches have historically shared

两千年里，天主教、东正教和新教都教导：性关系只属于一男一女的婚姻；婚前的性关系、婚外情和色情都是罪。今天多数教会仍然这样教导。一些主流新教教会在婚前同居等问题上的牧养做法比较宽松，但在正式文件里很少改变这个基本立场。<br>
For two thousand years Catholics, Orthodox, and Protestants all taught that sexual relations belong only within the marriage of a man and a woman, and that sex before marriage, adultery, and pornography are sin. Most churches still teach this. Some mainline Protestant churches are more lenient in pastoral practice on matters such as cohabitation before marriage, though their formal documents seldom change this basic position.

圣经同样强调恩典。被带到耶稣面前的那个妇人，耶稣对她说：“我也不定你的罪。去吧，从此不要再犯罪了！”（约 8:11）保罗列举了各样的罪以后说：“你们中间也有人从前是这样；但如今……已经洗净，成圣，称义了”（林前 6:11）。<br>
Scripture equally stresses grace. To the woman brought before him, Jesus said, “Neither do I condemn you. Go your way. From now on, sin no more” (John 8:11). After listing various sins, Paul says, “Some of you were such, but you were washed. But you were sanctified. But you were justified” (1 Corinthians 6:11).

## 分歧一：避孕 | Disagreement 1: contraception

- **天主教**：1968 年教宗保禄六世发表《人类生命》通谕，重申反对人工避孕，理由是夫妻的结合和生育的意义不应当被人为地分开。天主教接受按女性生理周期安排的自然方法。<br>
  **Catholic**: In 1968 Pope Paul VI issued the encyclical Humanae vitae, reaffirming opposition to artificial contraception on the ground that the unitive and procreative meanings of marital union should not be deliberately separated. The Catholic Church accepts natural methods based on a woman's cycle.
- **东正教**：美国东正教会 1992 年的声明说，婚姻里只能使用不伤害已受孕胎儿的节育方法。<br>
  **Orthodox**: A 1992 statement of the Orthodox Church in America says that within marriage only means of controlling conception that do not harm a child already conceived are acceptable.
- **新教**：1930 年圣公会的兰柏会议有条件地接受了避孕，以后大多数新教教会都接受夫妻使用避孕方法。少数新教徒，例如一些改革宗和福音派家庭，出于对生育的看重而不使用避孕。<br>
  **Protestant**: The Anglican Lambeth Conference of 1930 accepted contraception under conditions, and since then most Protestant churches have accepted its use by married couples. A minority of Protestants, such as some Reformed and evangelical families, do not use contraception because of the value they place on children.

各教会都反对堕胎，认为人的生命从受孕起就应当受到保护。<br>
All of these churches oppose abortion, holding that human life is to be protected from conception.

## 分歧二：同性关系 | Disagreement 2: same-sex relationships

这是今天争议最大的问题，也在一些宗派里造成了分裂。下面先介绍两方的立场和理由，再介绍各教会的现状。<br>
This is the most contested question today, and it has split some denominations. Below are the positions and reasons on each side, followed by where churches now stand.

**传统的立场**认为，婚姻只能在一男一女之间，同性的性关系是罪。理由包括：创世记 1–2 章和耶稣在马太福音 19 章把婚姻建立在“造男造女”上；新约几处经文明确提到同性的性行为，例如罗马书 1:26-27 和哥林多前书 6:9；两千年来各传统一致这样理解。持这种立场的人同时强调，有同性倾向本身不是罪，教会要接纳、尊重和关怀这些人，就像对待每一个在试探中挣扎的信徒一样。<br>
**The traditional position** holds that marriage can only be between a man and a woman and that same-sex sexual relations are sin. Its reasons include that Genesis 1–2 and Jesus in Matthew 19 ground marriage in God's making them “male and female”; that several New Testament passages expressly mention same-sex sexual acts, such as Romans 1:26-27 and 1 Corinthians 6:9; and that all traditions have read Scripture this way for two thousand years. Those who hold this position also stress that same-sex attraction is not itself sin, and that the church is to welcome, respect, and care for such people, as it does every believer who struggles with temptation.

**认可同性伴侣的立场**认为，教会可以为委身、忠诚的同性伴侣证婚或祝福。理由包括：新约那几处经文针对的是当时常见的剥削性做法，例如主人和奴隶、成人和少年之间的关系，而不是今天彼此委身的关系；教会历史上曾经重新理解过关于奴隶和妇女的经文；耶稣看重爱人和接纳被排斥的人。批评者认为，这样解读改变了经文本来的意思，而且罗马书 1 章讲的是普遍的“顺性”和“逆性”，没有限定在剥削的关系上。<br>
**The affirming position** holds that the church may marry or bless committed, faithful same-sex couples. Its reasons include that the New Testament passages addressed exploitative practices common at the time, such as relations between master and slave or adult and youth, rather than today's committed relationships; that the church has in the past reread texts on slavery and on women; and that Jesus put weight on love and on welcoming the excluded. Critics reply that this reading changes what the texts meant, and that Romans 1 speaks generally of what is “natural” and “against nature” without limiting itself to exploitative relationships.

**各教会的现状**（截至本课写作时）：<br>
**Where the churches stand** (as of this writing):

- **天主教**：《天主教教理》第 2357–2359 条说，同性的性行为不能被认可；有同性倾向的人应当受到尊重、同情和体贴，避免对他们不公正的歧视；他们和所有人一样蒙召守贞洁。2023 年 12 月，教廷信理部发表《恳求的信赖》，准许神父在礼仪以外，给前来请求的“非正规”伴侣，包括同性伴侣，简短的、自发的祝福，同时明确说这不改变婚姻的教义，也不能和民事结合的仪式一起举行。<br>
  **Catholic**: Paragraphs 2357–2359 of the Catechism say that same-sex sexual acts cannot be approved; that people with such tendencies must be treated with respect, compassion, and sensitivity, avoiding unjust discrimination; and that they, like everyone, are called to chastity. In December 2023 the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith issued Fiducia supplicans, permitting priests to give brief, spontaneous, non-liturgical blessings to couples in “irregular situations,” including same-sex couples, who ask for them, while stating expressly that the doctrine of marriage does not change and that such blessings may not be given in connection with a civil union ceremony.
- **东正教**：坚持婚姻是一男一女之间的，不认可同性的性关系，同时教导要以怜悯对待有同性倾向的人。<br>
  **Orthodox**: The Orthodox hold that marriage is between a man and a woman and do not approve same-sex sexual relations, while teaching mercy toward those with same-sex attraction.
- **多数福音派和五旬节派**：例如美南浸信会、神召会、美国长老会（PCA），持传统立场。<br>
  **Most evangelical and Pentecostal churches**: For example the Southern Baptist Convention, the Assemblies of God, and the Presbyterian Church in America hold the traditional position.
- **一些主流新教教会**：美国圣公会（2015 年）、美国长老会（PCUSA，2015 年）、联合基督教会已经为同性伴侣证婚，通常允许持不同看法的牧师不主持这样的婚礼。美国信义会 2009 年通过的文件承认会内有不同的良心立场，由各堂会自行决定。联合卫理公会 2024 年的总议会取消了禁止按立同性恋者和禁止牧师主持同性婚礼的规定；此前约 7600 间持传统立场的美国堂会已经离开了这个宗派。<br>
  **Some mainline Protestant churches**: The Episcopal Church (2015), the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) (2015), and the United Church of Christ marry same-sex couples, usually allowing clergy who disagree not to officiate. The Evangelical Lutheran Church in America adopted a statement in 2009 recognising differing convictions of conscience within the church and leaving decisions to congregations. The 2024 General Conference of the United Methodist Church removed its bans on ordaining gay clergy and on clergy officiating at same-sex weddings; before that, about 7,600 traditionalist U.S. congregations had already left the denomination.
- **英国圣公会**：2023 年 11 月，总议会以很接近的票数通过试行为同性伴侣祈求祝福的特别礼仪，但教会的婚姻教义没有改变。全球圣公宗里，很多教省，特别是非洲的教省，坚持传统立场，对这些变化表示强烈反对。<br>
  **Church of England**: In November 2023 General Synod narrowly approved a trial of special services asking God's blessing for same-sex couples, while the church's doctrine of marriage was not changed. Across the Anglican Communion many provinces, especially in Africa, hold the traditional position and have strongly opposed these changes.

华人教会绝大多数持传统立场。<br>
The great majority of Chinese churches hold the traditional position.

## 怎样对待看法不同的人 | How to treat those who disagree

这些问题牵涉到具体的人，很多人为此深深痛苦。各方都同意，任何人都不应当因为自己的性倾向受到嘲笑、欺凌或暴力；教会要成为人可以坦白自己挣扎的地方。<br>
These questions concern real people, and many suffer greatly over them. All sides agree that no one should be mocked, bullied, or harmed because of sexual orientation, and that the church should be a place where people can speak openly about their struggles.

如果你自己在这方面有挣扎，或者想更深入地了解你所在教会的立场，请找一位你信任的牧者或成熟的信徒谈一谈。如果你感到长期的痛苦，也可以寻求专业辅导的帮助。<br>
If you yourself struggle in this area, or want to understand your own church's position better, talk with a pastor or mature believer you trust. If you feel prolonged distress, you can also seek help from a professional counsellor.
