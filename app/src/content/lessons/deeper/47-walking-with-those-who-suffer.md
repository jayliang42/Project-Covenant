## 先陪伴 | Be present first

约伯失去了儿女、财产和健康。他的三个朋友约好一同来安慰他，“他们远远地举目观看，认不出他来，就放声大哭……他们就同他七天七夜坐在地上，一个人也不向他说句话，因为他极其痛苦”（伯 2:12-13）。<br>
Job lost his children, his property, and his health. His three friends arranged to come together to comfort him. “When they lifted up their eyes from a distance, and didn't recognize him, they raised their voices, and wept... So they sat down with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and no one spoke a word to him, for they saw that his grief was very great” (Job 2:12-13).

这七天是他们做得最好的时候。后来他们开口，坚持说约伯受苦一定是因为犯了罪，结果神责备他们：“你们议论我不如我的仆人约伯说的是”（伯 42:7）。<br>
Those seven days were their best moment. Later they spoke, insisting that Job must be suffering because of sin, and in the end God rebuked them, saying, “you have not spoken of me the thing that is right, as my servant Job has” (Job 42:7).

耶稣在拉撒路的坟前也哭了（约 11:35），虽然他知道自己马上要叫拉撒路复活。保罗说：“与哀哭的人要同哭”（罗 12:15）。<br>
Jesus wept at Lazarus' tomb (John 11:35), even though he knew he was about to raise Lazarus. Paul says, “Weep with those who weep” (Romans 12:15).

## 探访的时候 | When you visit

- **先问方便的时间**，不要待太久。病人和刚失去亲人的人常常很累。<br>
  **Ask when is convenient**, and don't stay too long. People who are ill or newly bereaved are often exhausted.
- **多听少说**。可以问：“你现在最难的是什么？”让对方按自己的节奏说，也允许他不想说。<br>
  **Listen more than you speak.** You can ask, “What's hardest for you right now?” Let them speak at their own pace, and let them decline to talk.
- **允许对方难过、发问，甚至生神的气**。诗篇里有很多哀歌，例如“耶和华啊，你忘记我要到几时呢？”（诗 13:1）。圣经让受苦的人把真实的感受带到神面前。<br>
  **Let them grieve, ask questions, and even be angry with God.** The Psalms contain many laments, such as “How long, Yahweh? Will you forget me forever?” (Psalm 13:1). Scripture lets sufferers bring their real feelings to God.
- **问问对方愿不愿意你为他祷告**，祷告要简短。按雅各书 5:14，病人也可以请教会的长老来为他祷告。<br>
  **Ask whether they would like you to pray**, and keep the prayer short. Following James 5:14, the sick may also call for the church's elders to pray for them.
- **提供具体的帮助**。“有需要就告诉我”很难让人开口。可以说：“我星期四给你送晚饭，好吗？”接送、买菜、照顾孩子、帮忙处理文件，都是实际的爱。<br>
  **Offer specific help.** “Let me know if you need anything” is hard to take up. Try, “Can I bring dinner on Thursday?” Driving, shopping, childcare, and help with paperwork are all practical love.

## 最好不说的话 | Words better left unsaid

箴言说：“对伤心的人唱歌，就如冷天脱衣服”（箴 25:20）。下面这些话可能出于好意，但常常让人更难受：<br>
Proverbs says, “As one who takes away a garment in cold weather... so is one who sings songs to a heavy heart” (Proverbs 25:20). These words may be well meant but often make things worse:

- “一切都有神的美意。”这句话可能是真的，但刚失去亲人的人通常还听不进去。罗马书 8:28 是安慰，不是用来让人停止哭泣的。<br>
  “Everything happens for a reason.” This may be true, but someone newly bereaved usually cannot take it in yet. Romans 8:28 is a comfort, not a way to make someone stop crying.
- “你要有信心，就会好起来。”这会让病人觉得病不好是自己的错。<br>
  “If you have enough faith, you'll get better.” This makes the sick feel it is their fault if they don't recover.
- “我完全明白你的感受。”即使经历相似，每个人的痛苦也不一样。可以说：“我不能完全明白，但我愿意陪你。”<br>
  “I know exactly how you feel.” Even with similar experiences, each person's pain is different. Try instead, “I can't fully understand, but I want to be with you.”
- 追问原因或给对方讲道理。约伯的朋友就是在这里出了错。<br>
  Probing for causes or lecturing. This is where Job's friends went wrong.

## 持续的关怀 | Ongoing care

丧礼以后几周，来看望的人往往就少了，但难过的人这时常常最孤单。可以在日历上记下逝者的忌日、生日和第一个节日，那时发一条消息问候。<br>
A few weeks after a funeral, visitors tend to stop coming, yet this is often when the grieving person feels most alone. Note the anniversary of the death, the person's birthday, and the first holidays on your calendar, and send a message then.

保罗说，神“在一切患难中……安慰我们，叫我们能用神所赐的安慰去安慰那遭各样患难的人”（林后 1:4）。曾经受过苦的人，往往能更好地陪伴别人。<br>
Paul says God “comforts us in all our affliction, that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction” (2 Corinthians 1:4). People who have suffered are often well placed to walk with others.

## 知道自己的限度 | Know your limits

陪伴的人不需要解决所有问题，也不要一个人承担。下面这些情况要请牧师、教会负责的同工和专业人士一同帮助：<br>
A companion does not need to solve every problem and should not carry it alone. In the following situations, involve the pastor, responsible church leaders, and professionals:

- 长期失眠、吃不下饭、无法照顾自己，或者难过的状态持续很久没有减轻。<br>
  Ongoing sleeplessness, not eating, being unable to care for oneself, or grief that does not ease over a long time.
- 对方说起想伤害自己或别人，或者正在受虐待。这时先顾安全，及时联系当地的紧急服务。<br>
  The person speaks of wanting to harm themselves or others, or is being abused. Put safety first and contact local emergency services promptly.

请专业人士帮助，并不表示信心不够。教会的陪伴和专业的帮助可以同时进行。<br>
Seeking professional help is not a sign of weak faith. The church's companionship and professional help can go on together.
