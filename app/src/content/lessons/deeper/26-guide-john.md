## 和另外三卷不同 | Different from the other three

约翰福音里没有耶稣的比喻，没有赶鬼的记载，也没有设立圣餐的那段话。它有很多别的福音书没有的内容：迦拿的婚宴、尼哥底母、撒玛利亚妇人、拉撒路复活、最后晚餐时的长篇讲论（13–17 章）。对观福音的耶稣大多在加利利工作，约翰福音记了耶稣好几次上耶路撒冷过节，由此推算耶稣公开工作大约三年。<br>
John has no parables, no exorcisms, and no words instituting the Lord's Supper. It has much the other Gospels lack: the wedding at Cana, Nicodemus, the Samaritan woman, the raising of Lazarus, and the long discourse at the last supper (chapters 13–17). In the Synoptics Jesus works mostly in Galilee, while John records several visits to Jerusalem for the feasts, from which Jesus' public work is reckoned at about three years.

## 结构 | Structure

- **1:1-18 序言**：“太初有道，道与神同在，道就是神……道成了肉身，住在我们中间”（约 1:1，14）。<br>
  **1:1-18, the prologue**: “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... The Word became flesh, and lived among us” (John 1:1, 14).
- **1:19–12:50 神迹之书**：耶稣行了七个“神迹”，原文的意思是“记号”，指向他是谁。<br>
  **1:19–12:50, the Book of Signs**: Jesus performs seven “signs,” works that point to who he is.
- **13:1–20:31 荣耀之书**：最后的晚餐、受难和复活。约翰把十字架称为耶稣“得荣耀”的时候。<br>
  **13:1–20:31, the Book of Glory**: the last supper, the passion, and the resurrection. John speaks of the cross as the hour when Jesus is glorified.
- **21 章 结语**：复活的耶稣在加利利湖边向门徒显现，三次问彼得“你爱我吗”。<br>
  **Chapter 21, the epilogue**: The risen Jesus appears to the disciples by the Sea of Galilee and three times asks Peter, “Do you love me?”

## 七个神迹 | The seven signs

1. 迦拿婚宴上变水为酒（2:1-11）<br>Water turned into wine at Cana (2:1-11)
2. 医好大臣的儿子（4:46-54）<br>The official's son healed (4:46-54)
3. 毕士大池边医好瘫子（5:1-18）<br>The paralysed man healed at Bethesda (5:1-18)
4. 五饼二鱼喂饱五千人（6:1-15）<br>Five thousand fed (6:1-15)
5. 在海面上行走（6:16-21）<br>Walking on the water (6:16-21)
6. 医好生来瞎眼的人（9:1-41）<br>The man born blind healed (9:1-41)
7. 叫拉撒路复活（11:1-44）<br>Lazarus raised (11:1-44)

## 七个“我是” | The seven “I am” sayings

耶稣用“我是”加上一个比喻来说明自己：生命的粮（6:35）、世界的光（8:12）、羊的门（10:7）、好牧人（10:11）、复活和生命（11:25）、道路、真理、生命（14:6）、真葡萄树（15:1）。<br>
Jesus describes himself with “I am” and an image: the bread of life (6:35), the light of the world (8:12), the door of the sheep (10:7), the good shepherd (10:11), the resurrection and the life (11:25), the way, the truth, and the life (14:6), and the true vine (15:1).

约翰福音里还有几处没有比喻的“我是”，例如“还没有亚伯拉罕就有了我”（约 8:58）。听的人拿石头要打他，因为他们听出这是在用神在燃烧的荆棘中自称的名字。<br>
John also has several “I am” sayings without an image, such as “before Abraham came into existence, I AM” (John 8:58). His hearers picked up stones to throw at him, because they heard him using the name God gave himself at the burning bush.

## 写作的目的 | Purpose

约翰自己说明了为什么写这卷书：“记这些事要叫你们信耶稣是基督，是神的儿子，并且叫你们信了他，就可以因他的名得生命”（约 20:31）。“信”这个动词在约翰福音里出现了将近一百次。<br>
John states why he wrote: “these are written, that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name” (John 20:31). The verb “believe” appears nearly a hundred times in John.

“叫你们信”这个动词，古抄本有两种写法，一种可以理解为“开始信”，一种可以理解为“继续信”。所以学者对约翰的读者是不信的人还是信徒，看法不同。很多人认为两种读者都包括在内。<br>
The verb in “that you may believe” has two readings in the ancient manuscripts, one suggesting “come to believe” and the other “go on believing.” Scholars therefore differ on whether John wrote for unbelievers or for believers. Many think he had both in view.

## 谁写的 | Who wrote it

书里没有作者的名字，只说为这些事作见证并且记载的，是“耶稣所爱的那门徒”（约 21:20，24）。这位门徒在最后的晚餐时靠在耶稣怀里，在十字架下受托照顾耶稣的母亲。<br>
The book does not name its author. It says that the one who testifies to these things and wrote them is “the disciple whom Jesus loved” (John 21:20, 24). This disciple leaned against Jesus at the last supper and at the cross was entrusted with the care of Jesus' mother.

2 世纪的爱任纽说，这位门徒就是西庇太的儿子、使徒约翰，他晚年住在以弗所，在那里写了这卷福音书。这是教会的传统看法，很多学者至今仍持守。也有学者提出，作者可能是帕皮亚提到的“长老约翰”，或者是一位不在十二使徒之列的耶路撒冷门徒，还有人提出拉撒路。不少学者认为，书的最后定稿可能经过这位门徒的群体整理，21:24 的“我们也知道他的见证是真的”就是这群人的话。学者通常把成书时间定在主后 90 年代。<br>
In the 2nd century Irenaeus said that this disciple was John son of Zebedee, the apostle, who in old age lived at Ephesus and wrote the Gospel there. This is the church's traditional view, and many scholars still hold it. Others suggest that the author may have been “John the Elder,” whom Papias mentions, or a Jerusalem disciple outside the Twelve, and some have proposed Lazarus. A good number of scholars think the final form may have been completed by the disciple's community, and that “We know that his witness is true” in 21:24 is their voice. Scholars usually date the book to the AD 90s.
