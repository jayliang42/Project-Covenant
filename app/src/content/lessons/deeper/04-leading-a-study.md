## 两个要求：忠于经文，温和待人 | Two requirements: faithful to the text, gentle with people

带小组查经容易出两种问题。一种是带领者一个人讲四十分钟，其他人只是听，没有人自己读经文。另一种是经文念过一遍就放下，大家各谈各的感想，到最后没有人清楚这段经文在讲什么。<br>
Two things easily go wrong in a small-group Bible study. In one, the leader talks for forty minutes while everyone else only listens, and no one reads the passage for themselves. In the other, the passage is read once and set aside, people share whatever comes to mind, and by the end no one is sure what the passage is about.

《提摩太后书》2:15 和 2:24 提出两个要求：**“按着正意分解真理的道”**（忠于经文），以及**“温温和和地待众人，善于教导，存心忍耐”**（关心在场的人）。<br>
2 Timothy 2:15 and 2:24 give two requirements: **"properly handling the word of truth"** (faithfulness to the text) and being **"gentle toward all, able to teach, patient"** (care for the people in the room).

## 45–60 分钟查经的四个步骤 | Four steps for a 45–60 minute study

预备查经不需要写一篇讲章。你先自己把经文仔细读三遍，然后准备 **5–7 个由浅入深的问题**：<br>
You do not need to write a sermon to prepare. Read the passage carefully three times yourself, then prepare **five to seven questions that go from easy to harder**:

1. **破冰（5 分钟，1 个问题）**：问一个和这段经文主题有关的生活问题，没有神学背景的人也能回答。例如读《路加福音》15 章之前可以问：“你小时候有没有走丢过，或者丢过一件很贵重的东西？”<br>
   **Warm-up (5 min, 1 question)**: Ask an everyday question related to the passage's theme that anyone can answer without Bible background. Before reading Luke 15, for example: "Did you ever get lost as a child, or lose something very valuable?"
2. **观察：这里写了什么？（15 分钟，2 个问题）**：大家读完经文以后，先问看着经文就能回答的问题，例如：“这段里有哪几个人物？他们的态度有什么不同？”第一次来的朋友也答得上，就不那么紧张。<br>
   **Observation: what does the text say? (15 min, 2 questions)**: After the passage is read, start with questions that can be answered by looking at the page, such as "Who appears in this passage? How do their attitudes differ?" First-time visitors can answer these too, which helps them relax.
3. **解释：这段原本是什么意思？（15 分钟，2 个问题）**：带大家结合上下文来想：“作者为什么记下这件事？这件事显出神怎样的心意？主耶稣在这里怎样成全了旧约的盼望？”<br>
   **Interpretation: what did it mean in context? (15 min, 2 questions)**: Help the group think with the context in view: "Why did the author record this? What does it show about God's purposes? How does the Lord Jesus fulfill the hope of the Old Testament here?"
4. **应用与代祷（15 分钟，1–2 个问题）**：最后问：“这一周，这段经文会让我们在家庭、工作或忧虑的事上有哪一件具体的改变？”然后简短地彼此代祷。<br>
   **Application and prayer (15 min, 1–2 questions)**: Finally ask, "What one specific thing would this passage change this week in our family, work, or worries?" Then pray briefly for one another.

## 带领者的四个习惯 | Four habits for study leaders

- **请大家看第 X 节，不急着给答案**：有人提出疑问或者跑题的时候，可以说：“这个问题很好，我们一起看看第 X 节怎么说？”让大家从经文里找答案。<br>
  **Point to the verse before giving an answer**: When someone raises a question or the discussion drifts, you can say, "Good question. Let's look at verse X together. What does it say?" Let the group find the answer in the text.
- **分清经文明说的和推论出来的**：分清哪些是经文明说的主要真理，哪些是从历史背景得出的推论，哪些是不同神学传统理解不一的地方。遇到没把握的历史细节，可以直接说：“这一点我不确定，我回去查了资料再告诉大家。”这比勉强猜一个答案更让人信任。<br>
  **Separate what the text says from what is inferred**: Know which points the passage states plainly, which are inferences from historical background, and which are understood differently by different traditions. When you are unsure of a historical detail, say so: "I'm not sure about this. Let me look it up and get back to you." People trust that more than a guess.
- **给安静的人机会，也提醒健谈的人**：可以微笑着说：“刚才听了几位的分享，我也想听听还没开口的朋友怎么看第 X 节。”<br>
  **Make room for quiet people and gently limit talkative ones**: You might say with a smile, "We've heard from a few of you. I'd like to hear how those who haven't spoken yet see verse X."
- **保守隐私**：小组里分享的个人难处和代祷事项，没有本人同意不外传，也不写进公开的笔记。<br>
  **Keep confidences**: Personal struggles and prayer requests shared in the group are not passed on without the person's consent and are not written into public notes.
