## 三卷相似的福音书 | Three similar Gospels

马太、马可、路加三卷福音书常被称为“对观福音”，因为它们可以并排对照着读。马可福音大约九成的内容也出现在马太福音里，一半以上出现在路加福音里，很多地方连用词都相同。约翰福音的结构和内容和这三卷很不一样。<br>
Matthew, Mark, and Luke are often called the Synoptic Gospels because they can be set side by side and read together. About nine-tenths of Mark's content also appears in Matthew, and more than half in Luke, often in the same words. John differs a great deal from these three in structure and content.

三卷书这样相像，又各有不同，它们之间是什么关系？这个问题叫“对观福音问题”。<br>
Since the three are so alike and yet each different, how are they related? This question is called the Synoptic problem.

## 几种看法 | The main views

- **奥古斯丁的次序**：5 世纪的奥古斯丁认为马太最早，马可是马太的简写，路加在后。这种看法在教会里流行了一千多年。<br>
  **Augustine's order**: In the 5th century Augustine held that Matthew came first, Mark abridged Matthew, and Luke came after. This view prevailed in the church for more than a thousand years.
- **格里斯巴赫假说（1789 年）**：马太最早，路加用了马太，马可综合了马太和路加。今天仍有少数学者支持。<br>
  **The Griesbach hypothesis (1789)**: Matthew was first, Luke used Matthew, and Mark combined the two. A minority of scholars still support it.
- **两源说**：19 世纪的德国学者注意到，马太和路加在叙事次序上总是跟着马可走。由此提出：马可最早写成；马太和路加都用了马可，又各自用了一份已经失传的耶稣语录，学者称它为“Q”。大英百科称它是“普遍接受的解答”，今天大多数注释书以此为基础。<br>
  **The two-source hypothesis**: 19th-century German scholars noticed that Matthew and Luke follow Mark's order of events. They proposed that Mark was written first, and that Matthew and Luke each used Mark and also a lost collection of Jesus' sayings, which scholars call “Q.” Britannica calls it the generally accepted solution, and most commentaries today build on it.
- **法勒假说**：同意马可最早，但认为不需要假设 Q 存在，路加直接用了马太。近几十年也有一些学者支持这种看法。<br>
  **The Farrer hypothesis**: It agrees that Mark came first but holds that there is no need to posit Q, because Luke used Matthew directly. In recent decades some scholars have come to support it.

无论哪一种看法，大多数学者都同意三卷书之间有书面上的关系。他们认为，单凭各自记住的口传，写不出这么多相同的字句。持守圣经权威的学者也在这几种看法之间各有选择。这个问题讨论的是神怎样使用人写成福音书，不涉及福音书是否可靠。路加自己就说，他参考了“好些人”写下的记载（路 1:1-3）。<br>
Whichever view is taken, most scholars agree that the three are related in writing. They hold that oral tradition remembered separately could not produce so much identical wording. Scholars who hold to the authority of Scripture take different positions among these views. The question concerns how God used human writers to produce the Gospels and does not bear on whether the Gospels are reliable. Luke himself says he drew on accounts that “many” had written (Luke 1:1-3).

## 这对读经有什么用 | How this helps in reading

如果马太和路加用了马可，那么比较他们改动的地方，就能看出每位作者想强调什么。例如：<br>
If Matthew and Luke used Mark, then comparing where they changed it shows what each writer wanted to stress. For example:

- 马可记门徒在船上对耶稣说：“夫子！我们丧命，你不顾吗？”（可 4:38）马太记作“主啊，救我们，我们丧命啦！”（太 8:25）马可保留了门徒的埋怨，马太把它写成一句祷告。<br>
  In Mark the disciples in the boat say to Jesus, “Teacher, don’t you care that we are dying?” (Mark 4:38). Matthew has, “Save us, Lord! We are dying!” (Matthew 8:25). Mark keeps the disciples' complaint, and Matthew renders it as a prayer.
- 彼得认耶稣为基督，马可只记“你是基督”（可 8:29），马太记作“你是基督，是永生神的儿子”，接着是耶稣对彼得的称许（太 16:16-19）。<br>
  At Peter's confession, Mark records only “You are the Christ” (Mark 8:29). Matthew has “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God,” followed by Jesus' blessing of Peter (Matthew 16:16-19).

马可的写法常常更直接，门徒的软弱写得更明显。早期教会的传统说马可的内容来自彼得的讲道，有人认为，这也是马可不替门徒遮掩的原因之一。<br>
Mark's telling is often blunter, and the disciples' weakness shows more plainly. Early church tradition says Mark's material came from Peter's preaching, and some think this is one reason Mark does not cover for the disciples.

## 马可福音的重点 | Mark's emphases

前面四课已经按段落读过马可福音，这里只列出它的几个重点：开头就宣告耶稣是“神的儿子”（可 1:1）；前半部分不断问“这到底是谁”；8:29 彼得的认信是转折点；后半部分走向十字架，钥节是“人子来……要服事人，并且要舍命作多人的赎价”（可 10:45）；第一个亲口说出“这人真是神的儿子”的，是十字架下的罗马百夫长。<br>
The four earlier lessons read Mark section by section, so here are only its main emphases. It opens by announcing Jesus as “the Son of God” (Mark 1:1). Its first half keeps asking, “Who then is this?” Peter's confession at 8:29 is the turning point. Its second half moves toward the cross, with the key verse, “the Son of Man also came not to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many” (Mark 10:45). The first person to say aloud, “Truly this man was the Son of God,” is the Roman centurion at the cross.
