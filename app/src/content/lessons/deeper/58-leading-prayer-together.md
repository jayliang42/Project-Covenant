## 一起祷告 | Praying together

耶稣说：“若是你们中间有两个人在地上同心合意地求什么事，我在天上的父必为他们成全。因为无论在哪里，有两三个人奉我的名聚会，那里就有我在他们中间”（太 18:19-20）。初期教会遇到逼迫时，“同心合意地高声向神说：‘主啊！你是造天、地、海，和其中万物的’”（徒 4:24）。他们先称颂神，引用诗篇，然后才求神赐他们胆量传道（徒 4:24-30）。<br>
Jesus said, “if two of you will agree on earth concerning anything that they will ask, it will be done for them by my Father who is in heaven. For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there I am in the middle of them” (Matthew 18:19-20). When the early church faced persecution, “they lifted up their voice to God with one accord, and said, ‘O Lord, you are God, who made the heaven, the earth, the sea, and all that is in them’” (Acts 4:24). They first praised God and quoted a psalm, and only then asked for boldness to preach (Acts 4:24-30).

## 常见的问题 | Common problems

- **冷场**：大家不知道该说什么，或者不好意思先开口。<br>
  **Silence**: People don't know what to say, or are shy to go first.
- **代祷事项讲太久**：分享代祷事项用掉了大部分时间，真正祷告的时间很少。<br>
  **Long prayer requests**: Sharing requests takes most of the time, leaving little for prayer itself.
- **少数人包办**：几个人祷告得很长，其他人插不上。<br>
  **A few dominate**: A few people pray at length and others cannot get in.

## 几种带领的方法 | Some ways to lead

- **用经文引导**：读一篇诗篇，例如诗篇 23 篇或 103 篇，然后按经文的内容祷告：先赞美，再感谢，再求。<br>
  **Guide it with Scripture**: Read a psalm such as Psalm 23 or 103, then pray along its lines: praise first, then thanks, then requests.
- **按主题分段**：带领者说出一个主题，大家用短句祷告，然后进入下一个主题。例如：赞美神 → 认罪（可以默祷）→ 为组员的需要 → 为教会 → 为世界和城市。<br>
  **Divide it by theme**: The leader names a theme, people pray short sentences, then move to the next. For example: praising God → confession (silently) → members' needs → the church → the world and the city.
- **短句祷告**：鼓励每人每次只说一两句，可以多次开口。这样新来的人也容易参与，祷告也更像一场对话。<br>
  **Short sentences**: Encourage each person to pray only a sentence or two at a time, and as often as they like. This makes it easier for newcomers and more like a conversation.
- **分小组**：人多的时候，两三个人一组彼此代祷。<br>
  **Break into smaller groups**: When numbers are large, pray in twos or threes.
- **控制分享代祷事项的时间**：请大家用一两句话说出代祷事项，详细的情况可以会后再谈。<br>
  **Keep requests short**: Ask people to state their requests in a sentence or two, and talk about details afterwards.

## 让每个人自在 | Helping everyone feel at ease

- 说明可以只说一句话，也可以默祷，不点名要人开口。<br>
  Explain that a single sentence or silent prayer is fine, and don't call on people by name.
- 用平常的话祷告，不用华丽的词句。耶稣说，祷告不要“用许多重复话”，以为话多了必蒙垂听（太 6:7）。<br>
  Pray in ordinary words, not elaborate phrases. Jesus said not to “use vain repetitions,” as the Gentiles do, who “think that they will be heard for their much speaking” (Matthew 6:7).
- 祷告要让别人听得懂，能一同说“阿们”（林前 14:16）。<br>
  Pray so that others can understand and say “Amen” together (1 Corinthians 14:16).
- 对私人的代祷事项保密。<br>
  Keep personal prayer requests confidential.

保罗说：“你们要恒切祷告，在此警醒感恩”（西 4:2）。集体祷告重要的是一同来到神面前，说得好不好是次要的。<br>
Paul says, “Continue steadfastly in prayer, watching in it with thanksgiving” (Colossians 4:2). What matters in group prayer is coming before God together; eloquence is secondary.
