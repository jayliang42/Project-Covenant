## 两个越来越远的世界 | Two worlds drifting apart

罗马帝国在 4 世纪末分成东西两半。西方说拉丁文，以罗马为中心；东方说希腊文，以君士坦丁堡为中心。5 世纪西罗马帝国灭亡以后，罗马主教（教宗）在西方的地位越来越重要；东方的皇帝仍然在位，君士坦丁堡的牧首和皇帝关系密切。<br>
The Roman Empire split into eastern and western halves at the end of the 4th century. The West spoke Latin and centred on Rome; the East spoke Greek and centred on Constantinople. After the western empire fell in the 5th century, the bishop of Rome, the pope, grew ever more important in the West. In the East the emperor still reigned, and the patriarch of Constantinople worked closely with him.

几百年里，两边的语言、神学风格、礼仪和教会治理方式都渐渐不同。很多学者认为，1054 年的事件是长期疏远的结果，不是一夜之间发生的。<br>
Over several centuries the two halves came to differ in language, theological style, liturgy, and church government. Many scholars regard the events of 1054 as the outcome of long estrangement, not something that happened overnight.

## 争论的问题 | The issues in dispute

- **教宗的权柄**：西方认为罗马主教是彼得的继承人，对全教会有管辖权。东方承认罗马主教在众主教中居首位，是“同侪之首”，但不接受他有管辖其他教会的权柄。<br>
  **The pope's authority**: The West held that the bishop of Rome, as Peter's successor, has jurisdiction over the whole church. The East recognised the bishop of Rome as first among the bishops, “first among equals,” and did not accept that he had jurisdiction over other churches.
- **“和子”句**：381 年的《尼西亚信经》说，圣灵是“从父出来的”。西方教会后来在拉丁文的信经里加上了“和子”（filioque），成为“从父和子出来的”。东方反对两点：一是单方面改动大公会议的信经，二是这个说法本身。<br>
  **The filioque**: The Nicene Creed of 381 says the Holy Spirit “proceeds from the Father.” The Western church later added filioque, “and the Son,” to the Latin creed, making it “proceeds from the Father and the Son.” The East objected on two counts: the one-sided change to the creed of an ecumenical council, and the teaching itself.
- **圣餐的饼**：西方用无酵饼，东方用有酵饼。<br>
  **The bread of the Eucharist**: The West used unleavened bread, the East leavened bread.

## 1054 年 | The year 1054

1054 年，教宗利奥九世派枢机主教宏伯特到君士坦丁堡，和牧首米海尔·色路拉利乌斯谈判。谈判破裂。7 月 16 日，宏伯特把开除牧首教籍的诏书放在圣索菲亚大教堂的祭坛上；不久，牧首也开除了教宗使节的教籍。<br>
In 1054 Pope Leo IX sent Cardinal Humbert to Constantinople to negotiate with Patriarch Michael Cerularius. The talks broke down. On 16 July Humbert laid a bull excommunicating the patriarch on the altar of Hagia Sophia, and soon afterwards the patriarch excommunicated the papal legates.

当时很多人并不认为这就是永久的分裂。真正让双方关系难以挽回的是 1204 年：第四次十字军东征的军队攻破君士坦丁堡，大肆抢掠，并在那里设立了拉丁人的政权。东方信徒对这件事的记忆延续了好几百年。<br>
At the time many did not see this as a permanent split. What made the breach very hard to heal was 1204: the army of the Fourth Crusade took Constantinople, plundered it, and set up a Latin regime there. Eastern Christians remembered this for centuries.

后来双方几次尝试合一，例如 1439 年的佛罗伦萨会议，但东方的信徒和神职人员大多没有接受会议的决定。<br>
Later there were several attempts at reunion, such as the Council of Florence in 1439, but most of the clergy and faithful in the East did not accept its decisions.

## 今天的关系 | Relations today

1964 年，教宗保禄六世和君士坦丁堡普世牧首雅典纳哥拉一世在耶路撒冷会面。1965 年，双方在罗马和伊斯坦布尔同时举行仪式，撤销了 1054 年的互相绝罚。此后双方设立了神学对话委员会，历任教宗和普世牧首多次互访。<br>
In 1964 Pope Paul VI and Ecumenical Patriarch Athenagoras I of Constantinople met in Jerusalem. In 1965 the two held simultaneous ceremonies in Rome and Istanbul lifting the mutual excommunications of 1054. Since then the two sides have set up a joint theological commission, and successive popes and ecumenical patriarchs have visited each other many times.

两个教会至今仍没有恢复共融，教宗的权柄仍是最主要的分歧。双方在很多基本信仰上是一致的：三位一体、基督的神人二性、七次大公会议、七件圣事、使徒统绪、尊敬马利亚和圣徒。<br>
The two churches have still not restored communion, and the authority of the pope remains the chief difference. They agree on much that is basic: the Trinity, the two natures of Christ, the seven ecumenical councils, seven sacraments, apostolic succession, and the veneration of Mary and the saints.

新教是在 16 世纪从西方教会里分出来的，所以新教信徒对东正教通常了解得比较少。读东西教会分裂的历史，也能帮助新教信徒理解自己的传统是从哪里来的。<br>
Protestantism emerged from the Western church in the 16th century, so Protestants usually know relatively little about Orthodoxy. Reading the history of the East-West schism can also help Protestants understand where their own tradition came from.
