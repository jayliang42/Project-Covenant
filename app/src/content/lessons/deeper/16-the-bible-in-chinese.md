## 唐朝的景教 | The Church of the East in the Tang dynasty

最早有确切记载来到中国的基督教，是唐朝时的“景教”，属于东方教会。西安的“大秦景教流行中国碑”立于主后 781 年，记载主后 635 年，阿罗本等人带着经书和圣像来到长安，得到唐太宗的接待。这块碑在明朝末年（约 1620 年代）重新被发现。<br>
The earliest Christian presence in China with a firm record is the “Jingjiao” of the Tang dynasty, part of the Church of the East. The Nestorian Stele in Xi'an, erected in AD 781, records that in AD 635 Alopen and others came to Chang'an with scriptures and images and were received by Emperor Taizong. The stele was rediscovered in the late Ming dynasty, in the 1620s.

当时留下了一些中文的景教文献，但没有完整的中文圣经流传下来。唐朝后期景教在中原衰落。<br>
Some Chinese Church of the East texts from that time survive, but no complete Chinese Bible has come down to us. Later in the Tang dynasty the Church of the East declined in China's heartland.

## 19 世纪的译本 | Translations in the 19th century

明清时期，天主教传教士（例如利玛窦）在中国工作，有人译过部分经文，但很长时间里没有出版完整的中文圣经。<br>
During the Ming and Qing dynasties, Catholic missionaries such as Matteo Ricci worked in China, and some parts of Scripture were translated, but for a long time no complete Chinese Bible was published.

基督新教这边，19 世纪初有两部完整的中文圣经几乎同时完成：<br>
On the Protestant side, two complete Chinese Bibles were finished at almost the same time in the early 19th century:

- **马士曼和拉沙的译本**：在印度塞兰坡翻译，1822 年出版全本。<br>
  **Marshman and Lassar**: Translated at Serampore in India, with the whole Bible published in 1822.
- **马礼逊和米怜的译本**：马礼逊 1807 年来到广州，和米怜在 1819 年译完全本，1823 年在马六甲出版。<br>
  **Morrison and Milne**: Robert Morrison arrived in Guangzhou in 1807. He and William Milne finished the whole Bible in 1819, and it was published in Malacca in 1823.

1843 年，几个差会的传教士在香港开会，决定合作翻译文言文的“委办译本”。新约在 1852 年出版。旧约后来由麦都思等英国传教士译完，全本在 1850 年代中期出版。<br>
In 1843, missionaries from several mission societies met in Hong Kong and agreed to translate the Delegates' Version together, in classical Chinese. The New Testament was published in 1852. British missionaries led by Walter Medhurst later finished the Old Testament, and the whole Bible came out in the mid-1850s.

## 和合本 | The Union Version

1890 年，在上海召开的传教士大会决定合作翻译一部统一的中文圣经，分文言、浅文言和官话三种。官话的《和合本》在 1919 年出版，用的是接近口语的白话。它以英文《修订本》为基础，再用原文核对，参与工作的有西方传教士，也有中国的同工。<br>
In 1890 a missionary conference in Shanghai decided to translate a single shared Chinese Bible, in three forms: classical, simpler classical, and Mandarin. The Mandarin Union Version was published in 1919, in a written vernacular close to speech. It took the English Revised Version as its base and was checked against the original languages. Western missionaries and Chinese co-workers both took part.

翻译“God”这个词，传教士们争论了很久，这被称为“译名之争”。有人主张用“上帝”，有人主张用“神”。最后的办法是印两种版本，只在这个词上不同。所以今天的《和合本》有“神版”和“上帝版”。<br>
Missionaries argued for a long time over how to translate the word “God,” in what is called the Term Question. Some favoured 上帝 and others 神. The solution was to print two editions that differ only in this word. That is why the Union Version today has a 神 edition and a 上帝 edition.

和合本后来成为华人教会最常用的译本。1988 年前后出版了加上新式标点的版本，2010 年出版了《和合本修订版》。<br>
The Union Version became the most widely used translation in Chinese churches. A version with modern punctuation was published around 1988, and the Revised Chinese Union Version in 2010.

## 其他中文译本 | Other Chinese translations

20 世纪以后，又出现了许多直接从原文翻译的中文译本，例如吕振中译本、天主教的思高本（1968 年出版全本）、新译本等。不同译本各有长处，读经时对照两三个译本，常常能帮助理解。<br>
From the 20th century on, many more Chinese translations were made directly from the original languages, such as the Lü Zhenzhong Version, the Catholic Studium Biblicum Version (complete Bible published in 1968), and the Chinese New Version. Each has its strengths, and comparing two or three translations often helps understanding.

五旬节那天，各地来的人听见门徒“用我们的乡谈”讲说神的大作为（徒 2:11）。翻译圣经的人，做的就是同样的事：让人用自己的语言听见神的话。<br>
On the day of Pentecost, people from many lands heard the disciples speaking “in our languages the mighty works of God” (Acts 2:11). Bible translators are doing the same work: letting people hear God's word in their own language.
