## 圣经怎样描述神 | How the Bible describes God

- **永恒**：“从亘古到永远，你是神”（诗 90:2）。<br>
  **Eternal**: “From everlasting to everlasting, you are God” (Psalm 90:2).
- **不改变**：“我耶和华是不改变的”（玛 3:6）；“在他并没有改变，也没有转动的影儿”（雅 1:17）。<br>
  **Unchanging**: “I, Yahweh, don't change” (Malachi 3:6); “with whom can be no variation, nor turning shadow” (James 1:17).
- **无所不在**：“我往哪里去躲避你的灵？……我若升到天上，你在那里；我若在阴间下榻，你也在那里”（诗 139:7-8）。<br>
  **Everywhere present**: “Where could I go from your Spirit?... If I ascend up into heaven, you are there. If I make my bed in Sheol, behold, you are there!” (Psalm 139:7-8).
- **圣洁**：“圣哉！圣哉！圣哉！万军之耶和华”（赛 6:3）。<br>
  **Holy**: “Holy, holy, holy, is Yahweh of Armies!” (Isaiah 6:3).
- **爱**：“神就是爱”（约壹 4:8）。<br>
  **Love**: “God is love” (1 John 4:8).

《威斯敏斯德信条》第 2 章把这些总结为：神是“最纯全的灵，看不见，无身体、无肢体、无情欲，不改变”，又是无限的、永恒的、不可测度的、全能的、全智的、至圣的。<br>
Chapter 2 of the Westminster Confession summarises these: God is “a most pure spirit, invisible, without body, parts, or passions, immutable,” and also “immense, eternal, incomprehensible, almighty, most wise, most holy.”

## 两类属性 | Two kinds of attributes

神学上常把神的属性分成两类。一类只属于神，例如永恒、无所不在、全能，改革宗称为“不可传递的属性”。另一类是人可以在有限程度上有份的，例如爱、圣洁、智慧、公义，称为“可传递的属性”。神说：“你们要成为圣洁，因为我是圣洁的”（利 11:44）。<br>
Theology often divides God's attributes into two kinds. Some belong to God alone, such as eternity, omnipresence, and omnipotence; Reformed theology calls these “incommunicable attributes.” Others can be shared by people in a limited way, such as love, holiness, wisdom, and justice; these are called “communicable attributes.” God says, “be holy; for I am holy” (Leviticus 11:44).

## 圣经里的“后悔”和“忧伤” | God “relenting” and “grieving” in the Bible

圣经也说神“后悔造人在地上，心中忧伤”（创 6:6），又说神对以法莲“回心转意，我的怜爱大大发动”（何 11:8）。可是撒母耳记上 15:29 说：“以色列的大能者……决不后悔。”这些说法怎样放在一起？<br>
The Bible also says God “was sorry that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him in his heart” (Genesis 6:6), and says of Ephraim, “My heart is turned within me, my compassion is aroused” (Hosea 11:8). Yet 1 Samuel 15:29 says, “the Strength of Israel will not lie nor repent; for he is not a man, that he should repent.” How do these fit together?

传统的解释是：这些是神俯就人的语言，用人能明白的方式描述神对人的回应；神的本性和旨意并不改变。耶利米书 18:7-8 说，神宣告要毁灭一国，那国若转离恶行，神就“后悔”不降灾。这正是神不改变的性情在不同处境中的表现。<br>
The traditional explanation is that this is language by which God accommodates himself to human understanding, describing his response to people in terms we can grasp, while his nature and purpose do not change. Jeremiah 18:7-8 says that if God announces judgment on a nation and that nation turns from evil, God “relents.” This is his unchanging character expressed in different circumstances.

## 争论一：神会受苦吗？ | Debate 1: Can God suffer?

**古典的“不受苦性”**：早期教父认为，神自足、不改变，不会被外在的事物所左右。按 Themelios 一篇文章的介绍，这个教导并不表示神冷漠；它的意思是，神的爱是稳定的心意和作为，不会被所爱的对象改变。基督确实受了苦，但教父说那是在他的人性里受苦。亚历山大的区利罗说他“以不受苦的方式受苦”。<br>
**Classical impassibility**: The early church fathers held that God is self-sufficient and unchanging, not acted upon by anything outside himself. According to an article in Themelios, this does not mean God is cold; it means his love is a steady disposition and action that its object cannot change. Christ did suffer, and the fathers said he suffered in his human nature. Cyril of Alexandria said that he “suffered impassibly.”

**近代的“可受苦论”**：20 世纪，德国神学家潘霍华在狱中写道：“只有受苦的神能帮助人。”莫尔特曼在《被钉十字架的上帝》（英译本 1974 年）里主张，父和子都在十字架上受苦，神和受苦的人站在一起。很多近代神学家接受了某种形式的“神可受苦”。<br>
**Modern passibilism**: In the twentieth century, the German theologian Dietrich Bonhoeffer wrote from prison, “only the suffering God can help.” Jürgen Moltmann, in The Crucified God (English edition 1974), argued that both the Father and the Son suffer in the cross and that God stands with those who suffer. Many modern theologians accept some form of divine passibility.

持古典立场的人担心，如果神会被痛苦改变，他就不能成为受苦之人可靠的依靠；持可受苦论的人认为，古典的说法受希腊哲学影响太深，和圣经里有情感的神不符。<br>
Those holding the classical view worry that if God could be changed by suffering, he could not be a sure refuge for those who suffer. Passibilists argue that the classical view owes too much to Greek philosophy and does not fit the God of the Bible who has emotions.

## 争论二：神预知一切吗？ | Debate 2: Does God foreknow everything?

传统上，天主教、东正教和新教各派都认为，神完全知道将来，包括人将来的每一个选择：“我从起初指明末后的事，从古时言明未成的事”（赛 46:10）。各派的分歧在于神的预知和预定、人的自由之间是什么关系，“神的主权和人的选择”一课介绍了这一点。<br>
Traditionally Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant theologians alike have held that God knows the future completely, including every future human choice: “I declare the end from the beginning, and from ancient times things that are not yet done” (Isaiah 46:10). They differ on how God's foreknowledge and predestination relate to human freedom, as the lesson on God's sovereignty and human choice explains.

20 世纪后期，平诺克（Clark Pinnock）、桑德斯（John Sanders）等福音派学者提出“开放神论”：神知道将来一切可能的情况，但不是完全预先知道受造物会作出哪些自由的选择。他们认为这样才能说明圣经里神的“后悔”和祷告的意义。批评者认为，这个看法削弱了神的全知，也和预言的应验不合。2003 年，福音派神学会投票是否开除两人，平诺克获得的开除票只有约三分之一，桑德斯约 63%，都没有达到所需的三分之二。<br>
In the late twentieth century evangelical scholars such as Clark Pinnock and John Sanders proposed “open theism”: God knows every possible future but does not exhaustively foreknow which free choices his creatures will make. They argue this best explains God's “relenting” and the meaning of prayer in the Bible. Critics argue that it weakens God's omniscience and does not fit fulfilled prophecy. In 2003 the Evangelical Theological Society voted on expelling the two men; about a third voted to expel Pinnock and about 63% to expel Sanders, both short of the two-thirds required.

## 为什么重要 | Why it matters

我们怎样看神，会影响我们怎样祷告、怎样面对苦难、怎样信靠他。不管在这些争论上持什么立场，各传统都承认：神是信实的，“在他并没有改变”，我们可以把自己交托给他。<br>
How we see God shapes how we pray, how we face suffering, and how we trust him. Whatever position one takes in these debates, all traditions confess that God is faithful, that with him “can be no variation,” and that we can entrust ourselves to him.
