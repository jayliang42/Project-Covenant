## 新约用了好几种画面 | The New Testament uses several pictures

新约没有用一个定义来讲十字架，它用了好几种来自日常生活的画面：法庭（称义）、圣殿（献祭、挽回祭）、市场（赎价）、战场（得胜）、家庭（和好、儿子的名分）。教会历史上的神学家，常常抓住其中一种来深入讲解。<br>
The New Testament does not give one definition of the cross. It uses several pictures from everyday life: the law court (justification), the temple (sacrifice and atonement), the market (ransom), the battlefield (victory), and the family (reconciliation and adoption). Theologians through church history have often taken up one of these and explored it in depth.

## 几种主要的理解 | The main views

- **赎价与复原**（早期教父，如 2 世纪的爱任纽）：耶稣说他来是要“舍命作多人的赎价”（可 10:45）。爱任纽还讲到“同归于一”（recapitulation）：亚当悖逆了，基督作为新的人类元首，在亚当失败的地方完全顺服。<br>
  **Ransom and recapitulation** (early church fathers, such as Irenaeus in the 2nd century): Jesus said he came “to give his life as a ransom for many” (Mark 10:45). Irenaeus also spoke of recapitulation: where Adam disobeyed, Christ, as the new head of humanity, obeyed fully where Adam had failed.
- **补偿说**（11 世纪末，安瑟伦《神为何成为人》）：人的罪亏欠了神的荣耀，必须有补偿。人欠债却还不起，只有神能还，所以要由既是神又是人的那一位来还。<br>
  **Satisfaction** (late 11th century, Anselm, *Why God Became Man*): Human sin wrongs God's honour, and satisfaction must be made. Humans owe the debt but cannot pay it, and only God can, so it must be paid by one who is both God and man.
- **道德感化说**（12 世纪，亚伯拉德）：十字架显明了神极大的爱，这份爱打动人心，使人回转爱神。<br>
  **Moral influence** (12th century, Abelard): The cross shows how greatly God loves us, and that love moves people's hearts to turn and love God.
- **刑罚替代说**（宗教改革时期的加尔文等人加以发挥）：人的罪应当受审判，基督代替罪人承受了这审判。经文根据如“耶和华使我们众人的罪孽都归在他身上”（赛 53:6），以及神设立耶稣作挽回祭，“要显明神的义”（罗 3:25-26）。<br>
  **Penal substitution** (developed by Calvin and others in the Reformation): Human sin deserves judgment, and Christ bore that judgment in the place of sinners. Its scriptural basis includes “Yahweh has laid on him the iniquity of us all” (Isaiah 53:6), and God setting forth Jesus as an atoning sacrifice “to demonstrate his righteousness” (Romans 3:25–26).
- **基督得胜**（20 世纪 30 年代，奥伦《基督得胜》把它重新提出来，认为这是早期教会的“经典”理解）：基督借着死和复活，战胜了罪、死亡和魔鬼的权势（西 2:15；来 2:14-15）。<br>
  **Christus Victor** (revived in the 1930s by Gustaf Aulén's book *Christus Victor*, which argued it was the “classic” view of the early church): Through his death and resurrection, Christ defeated the powers of sin, death, and the devil (Colossians 2:15; Hebrews 2:14–15).

## 怎样把它们放在一起 | How they fit together

这几种理解各自抓住了新约的一部分画面。许多神学家认为它们可以互相补充：基督替我们承受了审判，因此也战胜了罪和死亡的权势；他的爱感动我们，他的顺服也成了我们的榜样（彼前 2:21）。<br>
Each view grasps part of the New Testament's pictures. Many theologians think they complement one another: Christ bore judgment in our place, and so he also defeated the power of sin and death; his love moves us, and his obedience becomes our example (1 Peter 2:21).

不同传统对哪一种最中心有不同看法。很多福音派和改革宗神学家认为刑罚替代是中心，其他画面都和它相连。东正教传统更强调基督战胜死亡、使人得着新生命。也有一些神学家批评刑罚替代说，认为它容易被讲成父和子对立。回应者指出，圣经说父差子来，是出于父的爱（约 3:16），子也是甘心舍命（约 10:18）。<br>
Traditions differ on which is most central. Many evangelical and Reformed theologians see penal substitution as central, with the other pictures connected to it. Orthodox tradition puts more stress on Christ defeating death and giving new life. Some theologians criticize penal substitution, saying it is easily presented as the Father set against the Son. Those who defend it reply that the Bible says the Father sent the Son out of love (John 3:16), and the Son laid down his life willingly (John 10:18).

读这几种理解的时候，可以回到经文本身，看每一段经文用的是哪一种画面，不要用一种画面去压掉别的画面。<br>
As you study these views, go back to the texts themselves, notice which picture each passage uses, and avoid letting one picture crowd out the others.
