## 第一层：普遍的共识 | Level one: broad agreement

大多数研究古代史的学者，不论有没有信仰，都承认拿撒勒人耶稣是历史人物，而且在罗马巡抚本丢·彼拉多手下被钉十字架。罗马史家塔西佗也提到了这件事。

Most scholars of ancient history, religious or not, accept that Jesus of Nazareth existed and was crucified under the Roman governor Pontius Pilate. The Roman historian Tacitus mentions it too.

## 第二层：最早的见证 | Level two: the earliest witness

保罗在哥林多前书 15:3-8 引用了一段他“领受”、又传给别人的信仰陈述：基督死了、埋葬了、第三天复活了，并且向彼得、十二使徒、五百多人和雅各显现。这封信一般认为写于主后 50 年代，而这段陈述本身被许多学者认为更早。

In 1 Corinthians 15:3–8, Paul passes on a statement he had “received”: Christ died, was buried, was raised on the third day, and appeared to Peter, the Twelve, more than five hundred people, and James. The letter is usually dated to the AD 50s, and many scholars think the statement itself is earlier still.

历史能确认的是：很早就有一群人宣称见过复活的耶稣，并且因此彻底改变了生活方向。

What history can establish is that very early on a group of people claimed to have seen the risen Jesus, and their lives changed course because of it.

## 第三层：你的判断 | Level three: your judgement

至于耶稣是不是真的从死里复活，已经超出单凭历史方法能决定的范围。人怎样衡量这些证据，跟他原本怎样看神、怎样看神迹密切相关。

Whether Jesus actually rose goes beyond what historical method alone can decide. How people weigh the evidence is closely tied to how they already see God and miracles.

这不等于“没有证据”，只是说证据需要你来衡量。分清这三层，能让讨论更诚实。

That doesn't mean “there is no evidence”. It means the evidence asks for your weighing. Keeping the three levels apart makes the conversation more honest.
