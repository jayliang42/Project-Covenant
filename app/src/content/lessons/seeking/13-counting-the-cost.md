## 耶稣请人先算代价 | Jesus asked people to count the cost

跟随耶稣的人越来越多的时候，耶稣对他们说：“你们哪一个要盖一座楼，不先坐下算计花费，能盖成不能呢？”（路 14:28）。他还说：“若有人要跟从我，就当舍己，天天背起他的十字架来跟从我”（路 9:23）。<br>
When large crowds were following him, Jesus said to them, “Which of you, desiring to build a tower, doesn’t first sit down and count the cost, to see if he has enough to complete it?” (Luke 14:28). He also said, “If anyone desires to come after me, let him deny himself, take up his cross, and follow me” (Luke 9:23).

跟随耶稣会带来一些改变：<br>
Following Jesus brings some changes:

- **谁作主**：以前是自己决定一切，现在要把耶稣当作主，在钱、时间、关系、工作上听他的话。<br>
  **Who is in charge**: Before, you decided everything yourself. Now you treat Jesus as Lord and listen to him about money, time, relationships, and work.
- **和别人的关系**：耶稣要人饶恕得罪自己的人，爱仇敌，和弟兄和好。<br>
  **How you treat others**: Jesus asks people to forgive those who wrong them, love their enemies, and make peace with their brothers and sisters.
- **可能的误解**：家人朋友有时不理解，也可能有人看不起你。<br>
  **Possible misunderstanding**: Family and friends sometimes do not understand, and some people may look down on you.

## 代价和喜乐 | Cost and joy

耶稣也讲了另一个比喻：“天国好像宝贝藏在地里，人遇见了就把它藏起来，欢欢喜喜地去变卖一切所有的，买这块地”（太 13:44）。这个人卖掉一切，心里是欢喜的，因为他知道自己得到的更好。<br>
Jesus told another parable too: “The Kingdom of Heaven is like treasure hidden in the field, which a man found, and hid. In his joy, he goes and sells all that he has, and buys that field” (Matthew 13:44). The man sells everything, and he is glad to do it, because he knows what he is gaining is better.

很多基督徒的经验是这样的：放下的东西是真的，得到的也是真的。彼得在许多人离开耶稣的时候说：“主啊，你有永生之道，我们还归从谁呢？”（约 6:68）。<br>
Many Christians describe it this way: what they gave up was real, and what they gained was real too. When many people were leaving Jesus, Peter said, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life” (John 6:68).

## 还没准备好怎么办 | If you are not ready yet

有一个富有的年轻人来问耶稣怎样得永生。耶稣“看着他，就爱他”，请他变卖产业、来跟从他。那人“忧忧愁愁地走了，因为他的产业很多”（可 10:21-22）。耶稣没有追上去降低条件，也没有责骂他。<br>
A rich young man came to ask Jesus how to inherit eternal life. Jesus, “looking at him loved him,” and asked him to sell what he had and follow him. The man “went away sorrowful, for he was one who had great possessions” (Mark 10:21–22). Jesus did not run after him to lower the terms, and he did not scold him either.

如果你现在还没准备好，这没有关系。下面几件事，你可以继续做：<br>
If you are not ready now, that is all right. Here are some things you can keep doing:

1. **读一本福音书**：从《马可福音》或《约翰福音》开始，自己看耶稣说了什么、做了什么。<br>
   **Read a Gospel**: Start with Mark or John and see for yourself what Jesus said and did.
2. **照实祷告**：即使不确定有没有神，也可以说：“神啊，如果你在，请让我认识你。”神借先知说：“你们寻求我，若专心寻求我，就必寻见”（耶 29:13）。<br>
   **Pray as you are**: Even if you are not sure God is there, you can say, “God, if you are there, help me know you.” Through the prophet God said, “You shall seek me, and find me, when you search for me with all your heart” (Jeremiah 29:13).
3. **找人聊**：和一位你信任的基督徒朋友谈谈你的问题，或者去一次查经小组，听听别人怎么想。<br>
   **Talk with someone**: Share your questions with a Christian friend you trust, or visit a Bible study group to hear what others think.
4. **把问题写下来**：哪些地方你还不明白，哪些地方让你犹豫，都可以写下来，一个一个去查。<br>
   **Write your questions down**: Note what you still do not understand and what makes you hesitate, and look into them one at a time.

如果你已经决定跟随耶稣，可以接着读“刚开始信”这条路径，它讲的是怎样开始读经、祷告，以及基督信仰的基本内容。<br>
If you have decided to follow Jesus, you can go on to the New believer path. It covers how to begin reading the Bible and praying, and the basics of the Christian faith.
