## 从一开始就有分歧 | Disagreement from the start

新约里的教会已经有分歧。哥林多的信徒分成几派，说“我是属保罗的”“我是属亚波罗的”，保罗劝他们“不可分党，只要一心一意，彼此相合”（林前 1:10-12）。保罗和巴拿巴因为要不要带马可同行，“起了争论，甚至彼此分开”（徒 15:39）。耶稣在被捕前为门徒祷告：“使他们都合而为一……叫世人可以信你差了我来”（约 17:21）。<br>
The church in the New Testament already had disagreements. The Corinthians split into factions saying, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos,” and Paul urged them that “there be no divisions among you, but that you be perfected together in the same mind” (1 Corinthians 1:10-12). Paul and Barnabas disagreed about taking Mark along, and “the contention grew so sharp that they separated from each other” (Acts 15:39). Before his arrest Jesus prayed for his disciples, “that they may all be one... that the world may believe that you sent me” (John 17:21).

## 宗派是怎样形成的 | How denominations formed

- **大的分裂**：1054 年东西方教会分裂，形成天主教和东正教；16 世纪宗教改革以后，形成新教的信义宗、改革宗（长老会）、圣公会和重洗派等传统。<br>
  **Major divisions**: The East-West schism of 1054 produced the Catholic and Orthodox churches; after the sixteenth-century Reformation came the Protestant traditions, such as Lutheran, Reformed (Presbyterian), Anglican, and Anabaptist.
- **复兴和宣教**：后来又有浸信会、循道宗（卫理公会）、五旬节派等，很多是从复兴运动中产生的。<br>
  **Revivals and mission**: Later came Baptists, Methodists, Pentecostals, and others, many arising from revival movements.
- **原因不只一种**：有的是因为对洗礼、圣餐、教会治理等问题理解不同；有的和语言、民族、地域有关；有的是因为人的骄傲和争竞。<br>
  **More than one cause**: Some divisions come from different understandings of baptism, the Lord's Supper, or church government; some relate to language, ethnicity, or region; some come from human pride and rivalry.

皮尤研究中心 2011 年的报告说，全世界基督徒中，天主教约占一半，新教约占 37%，东正教约占 12%。新教内部的宗派最多。<br>
A 2011 Pew Research Center report says that about half of the world's Christians are Catholic, about 37% Protestant, and about 12% Orthodox. Protestantism contains the most denominations.

## 共同相信的 | What they share

宗派虽然多，大多数宗派都承认早期信经所表达的基本信仰：一位神，圣父、圣子、圣灵；耶稣基督是神也是人，为人的罪死，第三天复活，将来要再来；圣经是神的话；人因神的恩典得救。宗派之间的分别，多数在这些基本信仰以外的地方。<br>
Though there are many denominations, most affirm the core faith expressed in the early creeds: one God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; Jesus Christ, God and man, who died for sins, rose on the third day, and will come again; Scripture as God's word; and salvation by God's grace. Most differences between denominations lie outside this core.

也要分清：有些团体虽然自称是基督教，却否认这些基本信仰，例如否认耶稣是神。各大传统一般不把这样的团体看作正统的基督教会。<br>
It also helps to distinguish: some groups call themselves Christian but deny this core, for example denying that Jesus is God. The major traditions generally do not regard such groups as orthodox Christian churches.

## 可以怎样看 | How to think about it

- **分裂是真实的问题**。教会的分裂和彼此攻击，是需要悔改的地方。保罗劝信徒“竭力保守圣灵所赐合而为一的心”（弗 4:3）。<br>
  **Division is a real problem.** Splits and mutual attacks among churches call for repentance. Paul urges believers to be “eager to keep the unity of the Spirit” (Ephesians 4:3).
- **不同不一定是敌对**。很多宗派之间有合作，例如一同翻译圣经、救灾、宣教。很多基督徒在不同宗派的教会之间转换，仍然认对方是弟兄姊妹。<br>
  **Difference is not necessarily hostility.** Many denominations cooperate, for example in Bible translation, disaster relief, and mission. Many Christians move between churches of different denominations and still regard one another as brothers and sisters.
- **先看耶稣是谁**。对慕道的朋友来说，宗派的问题可以先放一放。更重要的问题是：耶稣是谁？他真的复活了吗？这些问题，前面几课已经讨论过。<br>
  **Start with who Jesus is.** For a seeker, the question of denominations can wait. The more important questions are: Who is Jesus? Did he really rise? Earlier lessons in this path discuss these.
