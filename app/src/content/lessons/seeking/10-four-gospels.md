## 四个人写同一个人 | Four writers, one person

新约开头有四本书讲耶稣的一生：马太、马可、路加、约翰。教会从很早就同时使用这四本，没有把它们合并成一本。<br>
The New Testament opens with four books about Jesus' life: Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. From very early on the church used all four side by side and did not merge them into one.

关于写作年代，学者的看法不完全一样。比较常见的说法是：《马可福音》大约写于主后 60 年代后期，《马太福音》和《路加福音》在 70–80 年代，《约翰福音》在 90 年代。也有学者认为它们写得更早。即使按较晚的说法，离耶稣受难也只有几十年，当时还有亲眼见过耶稣的人活着。<br>
Scholars do not all agree on the dates. A common view is that Mark was written in the late 60s AD, Matthew and Luke in the 70s or 80s, and John in the 90s. Some scholars date them earlier. Even on the later dates, they were written within a few decades of Jesus' death, while people who had seen him were still alive.

路加在书的开头说，他“从起头都详细考察了”，要按次序写下来，使读者“知道所学之道都是确实的”（路 1:3-4）。<br>
Luke says at the start of his book that he “traced the course of all things accurately from the first” and wrote in order so that his reader might “know the certainty” of what he had been taught (Luke 1:3–4).

## 为什么有差别 | Why they differ

读的时候会发现，四本书的细节有时不一样。例如去坟墓的妇女，《马太福音》提到一位天使（太 28:2），《路加福音》说有两个人（路 24:4）。十字架上牌子的字，四本书的写法也不完全相同。<br>
As you read, you notice that the details sometimes differ. At the tomb, for example, Matthew mentions one angel (Matthew 28:2), while Luke says there were two men (Luke 24:4). The wording of the sign on the cross is not quite the same in any two of the Gospels.

有几个原因：<br>
There are several reasons:

- **每位作者有自己的读者和重点**：马太常引旧约，写给熟悉犹太传统的人。马可写得简短紧凑。路加注意穷人、妇女和外邦人。约翰挑了几个神迹，配上很长的讲论。<br>
  **Each author had his own readers and emphasis**: Matthew often quotes the Old Testament for readers who knew Jewish tradition. Mark is short and fast-moving. Luke pays attention to the poor, women, and Gentiles. John selects a few miracles and pairs them with long discourses.
- **古代传记的写法**：当时的传记常常按主题安排材料，会概括别人说的话，不一定逐字引用，也不一定严格按时间排列。<br>
  **How ancient biographies were written**: Biographies of the time often arranged material by topic, summarized speeches without quoting every word, and did not always keep strict chronological order.
- **作者必须选择**：约翰说，耶稣做的事若一一写出来，“所写的书就是世界也容不下了”（约 21:25）。<br>
  **The authors had to choose**: John says that if everything Jesus did were written down, “even the world itself wouldn’t have room for the books” (John 21:25).

## 这些差别说明什么 | What the differences show

几个证人讲同一件事，细节上有出入，主要内容却一致，法庭通常认为这样的证词比较可信。如果几份证词一字不差，反而会让人怀疑是事先串通好的。<br>
When several witnesses describe the same event and differ on details but agree on the main points, courts usually find that testimony more believable. If several statements matched word for word, people would suspect they had been coordinated.

在主要的事情上，四本书是一致的：耶稣在加利利传道、行神迹，挑选了十二个门徒，在耶路撒冷被钉十字架，第三天坟墓空了，他向门徒显现。<br>
On the main events the four agree: Jesus preached and did miracles in Galilee, chose twelve disciples, was crucified in Jerusalem, and on the third day the tomb was empty and he appeared to his disciples.

作者们也没有隐瞒自己的目的。约翰说：“但记这些事要叫你们信耶稣是基督，是神的儿子”（约 20:31）。读者可以知道作者想说服人，同时自己去查看他们写的内容是否站得住。<br>
The authors do not hide their purpose. John says, “These are written, that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God” (John 20:31). A reader can know that the author wants to persuade and still check for himself whether what is written holds up.
