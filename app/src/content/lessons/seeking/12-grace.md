## 一架天平 | A set of scales

很多人心里有一架天平：一边放好事，一边放坏事。只要好事多一点，神大概就会接纳我。<br>
Many people carry a set of scales in their heads, with good deeds on one side and bad deeds on the other. As long as the good outweighs the bad, God will probably accept me.

圣经不是这样说的。保罗写道：“你们得救是本乎恩，也因着信；这并不是出于自己，乃是神所赐的；也不是出于行为，免得有人自夸”（弗 2:8-9）。“恩典”的意思是白白给的好处，是人不配得、也挣不来的。<br>
The Bible says something else. Paul writes, “By grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, that no one would boast” (Ephesians 2:8–9). Grace means kindness given freely, which people do not deserve and cannot earn.

为什么不能靠自己挣？上一课说过，罪的问题在关系，在心里。一个人伤了朋友的心，靠多做几件好事抵消不了。还有，神的标准是尽心爱神、爱人如己（太 22:37-39），没有人能做到。<br>
Why can't it be earned? As the last lesson said, sin is a problem in our relationship with God and in our hearts. If you have broken a friend's heart, doing a few extra good deeds does not cancel it out. And God's standard is to love him with all your heart and to love your neighbour as yourself (Matthew 22:37–39). No one meets it.

## 耶稣讲的两个故事 | Two stories Jesus told

**浪子回头**（路 15:11-32）：小儿子要了家产，到远方挥霍光了。他想回家作个雇工。“相离还远，他父亲看见，就动了慈心，跑去抱着他的颈项，连连与他亲嘴”（路 15:20）。父亲给他穿上袍子、戴上戒指，摆宴席庆祝。学者常指出，在那个文化里，一个有身份的长者跑着去迎接儿子，是很少见的事。<br>
**The prodigal son** (Luke 15:11–32): A younger son took his share of the family's wealth and wasted it far away. He decided to go home and ask to be a hired hand. “While he was still far off, his father saw him, and was moved with compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him” (Luke 15:20). The father put a robe and a ring on him and threw a feast. Scholars often point out that in that culture it was unusual for a respected older man to run out to meet his son.

故事里还有一个大儿子。他一直留在家里做工，看到父亲这样待弟弟，生气不肯进去。父亲出来劝他。耶稣讲这个比喻时，听的人里有自认为守规矩的法利赛人（路 15:1-2）。<br>
The story also has an older son. He had stayed home and worked all along, and when he saw how his father treated his brother, he was angry and would not go in. The father came out to plead with him. Among those listening to this parable were Pharisees who thought of themselves as keeping the rules (Luke 15:1–2).

**葡萄园的工人**（太 20:1-16）：家主一早雇人做工，到下午五点还在雇人。发工钱时，每个人都拿到一钱银子。整天做工的人抱怨，家主说：“我给那后来的和给你一样，这是我愿意的”（太 20:14）。<br>
**The vineyard workers** (Matthew 20:1–16): An owner hired workers early in the morning and was still hiring at five in the afternoon. When the wages were paid, everyone received a denarius. Those who had worked all day complained, and the owner said, “It is my desire to give to this last just as much as to you” (Matthew 20:14).

## 恩典有代价吗？ | Does grace cost anything?

对接受的人来说，恩典是白白的。对给的人来说，恩典有代价。保罗说，神“因他爱我们的大爱，当我们死在过犯中的时候，便叫我们与基督一同活过来”（弗 2:4-5）。这份礼物的代价，是耶稣在十字架上付的。<br>
For the one who receives it, grace is free. For the one who gives it, grace is costly. Paul says that God, “for his great love with which he loved us, even when we were dead through our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ” (Ephesians 2:4–5). The cost of this gift was paid by Jesus on the cross.

蒙了恩典的人还要不要行善？要。保罗在同一段接着说，我们是神的工作，在基督里造成的，“为要叫我们行善”（弗 2:10）。行善是对恩典的回应，人不是靠行善去换恩典。<br>
Do people who receive grace still do good? Yes. In the same passage Paul goes on to say that we are God's workmanship, created in Christ “for good works” (Ephesians 2:10). Good works are a response to grace. People do not trade good works for grace.
