## 一个常见的看法 | A common view

很多人说：耶稣是一位伟大的道德老师，像孔子、苏格拉底一样，但他不是神；说他是神，是后来的门徒加上去的。这个问题可以从福音书本身来看：耶稣自己说了什么，做了什么，当时的人又怎样理解？<br>
Many people say that Jesus was a great moral teacher, like Confucius or Socrates, but not God, and that calling him God was added later by his followers. The question can be examined from the Gospels themselves: what did Jesus say and do, and how did people at the time understand it?

## 他做了只有神能做的事 | He did what only God can do

**赦罪**。有人把一个瘫子抬到耶稣面前，耶稣说：“小子，你的罪赦了。”在场的文士心里说：“这个人为什么这样说呢？他说僭妄的话了。除了神以外，谁能赦罪呢？”（可 2:5-7）人可以饶恕得罪自己的人，耶稣赦免的却是这个人一切的罪。<br>
**He forgave sins.** A paralysed man was brought to Jesus, and Jesus said, “Son, your sins are forgiven you.” Scribes who were there reasoned in their hearts, “Why does this man speak blasphemies like that? Who can forgive sins but God alone?” (Mark 2:5-7). A person can forgive wrongs done to himself; Jesus forgave this man's sins as a whole.

**接受敬拜**。犹太人只敬拜神。复活以后，多马对耶稣说：“我的主！我的神！”耶稣没有纠正他（约 20:28）。相比之下，使徒行传里有人要拜彼得和巴拿巴，他们立刻阻止（徒 10:25-26；14:14-15）。<br>
**He accepted worship.** Jews worshipped God alone. After the resurrection Thomas said to Jesus, “My Lord and my God!” and Jesus did not correct him (John 20:28). By contrast, when people in Acts tried to worship Peter and Barnabas, they stopped them at once (Acts 10:25-26; 14:14-15).

**说自己是安息日的主，比圣殿更大**。这些都是犹太人认为只属于神的东西（太 12:6-8）。<br>
**He called himself Lord of the Sabbath and greater than the temple.** Jews held both to belong to God alone (Matthew 12:6-8).

## 他说的话 | What he said

- “我与父原为一。”听的人拿起石头要打他，说：“你是个人，反将自己当作神”（约 10:30-33）。<br>
  “I and the Father are one.” His hearers took up stones and said, “you, being a man, make yourself God” (John 10:30-33).
- “还没有亚伯拉罕就有了我”（约 8:58）。“我是”是神在燃烧的荆棘中自称的名字。<br>
  “Before Abraham came into existence, I AM” (John 8:58). “I AM” is the name God gave himself at the burning bush.
- “人看见了我，就是看见了父”（约 14:9）。<br>
  “He who has seen me has seen the Father” (John 14:9).

有人说这些都出自约翰福音，是最晚写成的。不过最早的马可福音也有同样的内容：除了赦罪的事，还有耶稣受审的时候。大祭司问他：“你是那当称颂者的儿子基督不是？”耶稣说：“我是。你们必看见人子坐在那权能者的右边，驾着天上的云降临。”大祭司就撕开衣服，说他说了僭妄的话，公会定他该死（可 14:61-64）。<br>
Some say these sayings all come from John, the latest Gospel. But Mark, the earliest, has the same content: besides the forgiving of sins, there is Jesus' trial. The high priest asked him, “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?” Jesus said, “I am. You will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming with the clouds of the sky.” The high priest tore his clothes, called it blasphemy, and the council condemned him to death (Mark 14:61-64).

## 几种可能 | The possibilities

英国作家 C. S. 路易斯在《返璞归真》里提出一个有名的推理：一个说了这些话的人，不可能只是一位好老师。他要么是故意骗人，要么是精神不正常，要么他说的是真的。<br>
The British writer C. S. Lewis, in Mere Christianity, made a well-known argument: a man who said these things could not be merely a good teacher. Either he was deliberately deceiving people, or he was not in his right mind, or what he said was true.

这个推理也受到批评。最主要的批评是：还有第四种可能，就是福音书的记载不准确，这些话是后来的人加给耶稣的。如果要回应这个批评，就要回到前面几课讲的问题：福音书写得多早，作者是不是可靠的见证人，耶稣有没有复活。<br>
The argument has also been criticised. The main criticism is that there is a fourth possibility: the Gospel accounts are inaccurate, and these words were put in Jesus' mouth later. Answering this criticism means going back to the questions in earlier lessons of this unit: how early the Gospels were written, whether their writers were reliable witnesses, and whether Jesus rose.

持守传统信仰的学者有几点回应。第一，保罗的书信比福音书更早，信里已经把耶稣称为“主”，说信徒是“求告我主耶稣基督之名的人”（林前 1:2）。第二，学者赫塔多（Larry Hurtado）指出，敬拜耶稣的做法出现在最早的、只敬拜一位神的犹太信徒中间，出现得很快，他形容这“更像一次爆发，不像逐步演变”。第三，最早的马可福音也有这些说法。<br>
Scholars who hold the traditional faith make several replies. First, Paul's letters are earlier than the Gospels, and they already call Jesus “Lord” and describe believers as those “who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ” (1 Corinthians 1:2). Second, the scholar Larry Hurtado argues that devotion to Jesus appeared among the earliest Jewish believers, who worshipped one God, and that it appeared quickly, “more as an explosion than as an evolutionary development.” Third, Mark, the earliest Gospel, already contains these claims.

另一种看法以 20 世纪初的德国学者布塞特（Wilhelm Bousset）为代表。他认为，耶稣是在后来的外邦人教会里逐渐被当作神来敬拜的。赫塔多的研究就是在回应这种看法。<br>
Another view is represented by the early twentieth-century German scholar Wilhelm Bousset. He held that Jesus came to be worshipped as divine gradually, in the later Gentile churches. Hurtado's work answers this view.
