## 耶稣自己讲过审判 | Jesus himself spoke of judgment

有人以为审判和地狱是旧约的说法，耶稣只讲爱。实际上，福音书里关于地狱的话大部分是耶稣说的。他讲到“外面的黑暗”“不灭的火”，也讲到一条被“深渊限定”、无法越过的界线（路 16:26）。他说到最后的审判：“这些人要往永刑里去；那些义人要往永生里去”（太 25:46）。<br>
Some people assume that judgment and hell belong to the Old Testament and that Jesus spoke only of love. In fact most of what the Gospels say about hell was said by Jesus. He spoke of “outer darkness” and “unquenchable fire,” and of a line fixed by “a great gulf” that cannot be crossed (Luke 16:26). Of the last judgment he said, “These will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life” (Matthew 25:46).

讲这些话的，也是那位为罪人死的人。所以基督徒不能把审判从耶稣的教导里拿掉。<br>
The one who said these things is also the one who died for sinners. So Christians cannot remove judgment from the teaching of Jesus.

## 审判和爱怎样放在一起 | How judgment and love fit together

圣经把两件事都说得很清楚。<br>
The Bible states two things with equal clarity.

第一，神是公义的。亚伯拉罕问：“审判全地的主岂不行公义吗？”（创 18:25）很多人其实盼望有审判。遇到屠杀、虐待儿童这样的事，人会说“不能就这么算了”。如果神对恶无动于衷，他就不是良善的。<br>
First, God is just. Abraham asks, “Shouldn’t the Judge of all the earth do right?” (Genesis 18:25). Many people in fact long for judgment. Faced with massacre or the abuse of children, people say, “this cannot simply be let go.” A God who was indifferent to evil would not be good.

第二，神不愿意人灭亡。“主……宽容你们，不愿有一人沉沦，乃愿人人都悔改”（彼后 3:9）。“他愿意万人得救，明白真道”（提前 2:4）。按基督信仰，神为了让人不必受审判，自己在十字架上担当了刑罚。<br>
Second, God does not want people to perish. “The Lord... is patient with us, not wishing that anyone should perish, but that all should come to repentance” (2 Peter 3:9). He “desires all people to be saved and come to full knowledge of the truth” (1 Timothy 2:4). According to the Christian faith, God bore the penalty himself on the cross so that people need not face judgment.

很多基督徒这样理解地狱：它是人坚持不要神的最终结果。《天主教教理》把地狱定义为人自己选择、最终与神隔绝的状态，并且说神没有预定任何人下地狱。<br>
Many Christians understand hell as the final outcome of a person's settled refusal of God. The Catechism of the Catholic Church defines hell as a state of definitive self-exclusion from communion with God, and says that God predestines no one to go to hell.

## 对地狱的三种理解 | Three understandings of hell

教会里对最后审判的结果有三种理解。<br>
Three understandings of the outcome of the final judgment are found in the church.

- **永远的刑罚**：这是历史上多数教会的看法。天主教教导地狱是永远的，主要的痛苦是与神永远隔绝。改革宗、信义宗的信条和多数福音派教会也教导永远的、有知觉的刑罚。经文根据包括马太福音 25:46 和帖撒罗尼迦后书 1:9。东正教同样相信审判的结果是永远的，但常常这样解释：天堂和地狱是人对同一位神的同一种爱的不同经历，爱神的人以此为乐，拒绝神的人以此为苦。<br>
  **Everlasting punishment**: This has been the view of most churches through history. The Catholic Church teaches that hell is eternal and that its chief punishment is eternal separation from God. The Reformed and Lutheran confessions and most evangelical churches also teach everlasting, conscious punishment. Texts cited include Matthew 25:46 and 2 Thessalonians 1:9. The Orthodox likewise believe the outcome of judgment is eternal, and often explain it this way: heaven and hell are different experiences of the same love of the same God, a joy to those who love him and a torment to those who reject him.
- **最终消灭**（又叫“有条件的不朽”）：这种看法认为，只有神本来不死，永生是神赐给信的人的礼物，不信的人在审判后最终被消灭，不再存在。经文根据包括耶稣的话：“惟有能把身体和灵魂都灭在地狱里的，正要怕他”（太 10:28）。基督复临安息日会持这种看法。英国福音联盟 2000 年的报告《地狱的性质》指出，永远的刑罚仍是福音派里的主流看法，同时承认这种看法也是福音派可以持有的立场。<br>
  **Final destruction** (also called conditional immortality): On this view only God is immortal by nature, eternal life is his gift to those who believe, and unbelievers are finally destroyed after judgment and cease to exist. Texts cited include Jesus' words, “fear him who is able to destroy both soul and body in Gehenna” (Matthew 10:28). Seventh-day Adventists hold this view. The Evangelical Alliance UK report The Nature of Hell (2000) noted that everlasting punishment remains the dominant understanding among evangelicals and accepted that this view too is a position evangelicals may hold.
- **盼望人人最终得救**：少数神学家认为，神的爱最后会赢得所有的人。大多数教会不接受这种看法，认为它和耶稣讲的审判不符。英国福音联盟的那份报告也拒绝了它。也有一些人不敢断言，只说可以这样盼望和祷告。<br>
  **The hope that all will finally be saved**: A minority of theologians hold that God's love will in the end win every person. Most churches do not accept this, judging that it does not fit what Jesus said about judgment, and the Evangelical Alliance report also rejected it. Some others do not assert it and say only that one may hope and pray for it.

另外，天主教还教导“炼狱”：在神的恩典里死去、但还没有完全洁净的人，死后要经过炼净，然后进入天堂。东正教为离世的人祷告，但没有同样的炼狱教义。新教不接受炼狱。<br>
In addition, the Catholic Church teaches purgatory: those who die in God's grace but are not yet fully purified undergo purification after death and then enter heaven. The Orthodox pray for the departed but have no equivalent doctrine of purgatory. Protestants do not accept purgatory.

## 没有听过福音的人怎么办 | What about those who never heard?

这是另一个常见的问题，基督徒的回答也不一样。<br>
This is another common question, and Christians answer it differently.

- **有人认为**，新约说得救要靠信耶稣，所以教会要把福音传给万民。没有听过的人怎样受审判，要交托给公义的神。<br>
  **Some hold** that the New Testament ties salvation to faith in Jesus, which is why the church must take the gospel to all peoples, and that how those who never heard will be judged must be left to the just God.
- **也有人认为**，人只能靠基督得救，但神可以把基督的救恩施给那些没有机会听见、却按所得的光寻求他的人。《天主教教理》第 847 条说，那些并非出于自己的过错而不认识基督福音的人，如果诚心寻求神，按良心行事，也可能得到永远的救恩。不少新教徒也持类似的看法。<br>
  **Others hold** that people are saved only through Christ, and that God can apply Christ's salvation to those who had no chance to hear and yet sought him by the light they had. Paragraph 847 of the Catechism of the Catholic Church says that those who through no fault of their own do not know the gospel of Christ, and who sincerely seek God and follow their conscience, may also achieve eternal salvation. A good number of Protestants hold a similar view.

两种看法都同意一点，就是亚伯拉罕说的那句话：审判全地的主必行公义。没有人会受到不公平的对待。<br>
Both views agree on the point Abraham made: the Judge of all the earth will do right. No one will be treated unfairly.

对正在读这一课的人来说，更切身的问题是另一个：你已经听见了，你打算怎样回应？<br>
For the person reading this lesson, a nearer question is this one: you have heard, and how will you respond?
