## 先把问题说清楚 | First, be clear about the question

两段话说法不一样，不一定就是矛盾。矛盾是指两件事不可能同时为真。很多人说圣经有矛盾，其实没有具体读过相关的经文。这个问题值得认真对待，也值得具体地看。<br>
Two passages that say things differently do not necessarily contradict each other. A contradiction means two things cannot both be true. Many who say the Bible contradicts itself have not read the passages in question. The question deserves to be taken seriously, and to be looked at concretely.

## 几种常见的情况 | Some common cases

**1. 文体的问题**。箴言 26:4 说：“不要照愚昧人的愚妄话回答他，恐怕你与他一样。”下一节却说：“要照愚昧人的愚妄话回答他，免得他自以为有智慧。”编者显然是故意把这两句放在一起的：智慧在于分辨什么时候该回应，什么时候不该。<br>
**1. A matter of genre.** Proverbs 26:4 says, “Don't answer a fool according to his folly, lest you also be like him.” The next verse says, “Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own eyes.” The editor clearly placed these side by side on purpose: wisdom lies in knowing when to respond and when not to.

**2. 不同的角度和详略**。马可说耶稣出耶利哥时医好一个瞎子巴底买（可 10:46），马太说有两个瞎子（太 20:30）。马可只提了其中有名字的一位，并没有说只有一位。路加说是“将近耶利哥的时候”（路 18:35），有人认为当时有新旧两个耶利哥城，也有人认为路加按主题安排材料。几个证人讲同一件事，详略和角度不同，是常见的情况。<br>
**2. Different angles and detail.** Mark says Jesus healed a blind man, Bartimaeus, as he left Jericho (Mark 10:46); Matthew says there were two blind men (Matthew 20:30). Mark names one of them and does not say there was only one. Luke says it happened “as he came near Jericho” (Luke 18:35). Some suggest there were then an old and a new Jericho; others think Luke arranged the material by theme. Several witnesses telling the same event with different detail and angles is common.

**3. 两个说法可以并存，也可能需要解释**。马太说犹大“出去吊死了”（太 27:5），使徒行传说他“身子仆倒，肚腹崩裂”（徒 1:18）。从很早开始就有人认为两件事都发生了：他上吊以后尸体坠落。也有学者认为这是两个独立的传统，不必硬要合在一起。<br>
**3. Two accounts that may both stand, or may need explanation.** Matthew says Judas “went away and hanged himself” (Matthew 27:5); Acts says “falling headlong, his body burst open” (Acts 1:18). From early on some have held that both happened: after he hanged himself, his body fell. Other scholars regard them as two independent traditions that need not be forced together.

**4. 数字和抄写**。撒母耳记下 24:9 说以色列有拿刀的勇士八十万，犹大有五十万；历代志上 21:5 说以色列有一百一十万，犹大有四十七万。有人认为两书的统计范围不同，例如是否包括常备军；也有人认为数字在抄写中出了差错，因为在古代抄本里，数字是比较容易抄错的地方。学者对这一处没有一致的看法。<br>
**4. Numbers and copying.** 2 Samuel 24:9 counts 800,000 fighting men in Israel and 500,000 in Judah; 1 Chronicles 21:5 counts 1,100,000 in Israel and 470,000 in Judah. Some think the two books counted different groups, for example whether the standing army was included. Others think the numbers were miscopied, since numbers are among the places most easily miscopied in ancient manuscripts. Scholars do not agree on this passage.

## 各传统怎样理解 | How the traditions understand it

- **持守“圣经无误”的福音派**：1978 年的《芝加哥圣经无误声明》说，严格来说，默示只适用于原稿；抄本和译本在忠实于原稿的范围内是神的话。它也说，缺少现代的技术精确度、使用约数和夸张的说法、按主题编排材料、平行记载选取不同的材料、比较自由的引用，都不和无误冲突。<br>
  **Evangelicals who hold to inerrancy**: The Chicago Statement on Biblical Inerrancy (1978) says that, strictly speaking, inspiration applies only to the original text; copies and translations are God's word to the extent that they faithfully represent the original. It also says that a lack of modern technical precision, round numbers and hyperbole, topical arrangement, different selections of material in parallel accounts, and free citations do not count against inerrancy.
- **其他传统**：天主教和不少新教学者强调，圣经在为我们得救而记载的真理上没有错误，但对“无误”的范围有不同的理解。也有学者认为，圣经里有一些无法协调的地方，这并不影响它作为神的话的权威。“扎根成长”路径里“圣经的默示、权威和正典”一课把这些看法并排介绍。<br>
  **Other traditions**: Catholic and many Protestant scholars stress that Scripture teaches without error the truth recorded for our salvation, while understanding the scope of inerrancy differently. Some scholars hold that the Bible contains places that cannot be harmonised, and that this does not undermine its authority as God's word. The lesson on inspiration, authority, and canon in the Going Deeper path sets these views side by side.

## 读的时候可以怎样做 | What you can do as you read

- 读上下文，留意文体：诗歌、箴言、历史、比喻的读法不同。<br>
  Read the context and notice the genre: poetry, proverbs, history, and parables are read differently.
- 对照几个译本，查一本好的注释书。<br>
  Compare several translations and look at a good commentary.
- 对一时没有答案的问题，可以承认“我还不知道”，继续读下去。庇哩亚人“天天考查圣经，要晓得这道是与不是”（徒 17:11）。<br>
  When a question has no answer for now, you can say “I don't know yet” and keep reading. The Bereans examined “the Scriptures daily to see whether these things were so” (Acts 17:11).
