## 为什么会有这个印象 | Where the impression comes from

近代来中国的传教士大多来自欧洲和北美，教堂的样子、诗歌和很多做法也带着西方的色彩。所以很多中国人觉得基督教是“洋教”。这个印象有它的来历，但基督教的起源和早期历史，和这个印象很不一样。<br>
Most of the missionaries who came to China in modern times were from Europe and North America, and church buildings, hymns, and many practices had a Western flavour. So many Chinese people think of Christianity as a “foreign religion.” The impression has its reasons, but the origins and early history of Christianity look very different.

## 从亚洲西部开始 | It began in western Asia

耶稣是犹太人，生在伯利恒，在加利利和耶路撒冷生活和传道。这些地方都在亚洲的西部，就是今天的中东。圣经的作者几乎都是生活在这一带的犹太人。<br>
Jesus was a Jew, born in Bethlehem, who lived and taught in Galilee and Jerusalem. These places are in western Asia, in today's Middle East. Almost all the Bible's writers were Jews living in this region.

五旬节那天，听见门徒讲话的人来自帕提亚、米底亚、以拦、美索不达米亚、埃及、利比亚和阿拉伯等地（徒 2:9-11），这些地方大都在今天的伊朗、伊拉克、土耳其、埃及和利比亚。不久，腓利向一位埃塞俄比亚女王的官员传福音，那人受洗以后回到非洲（徒 8 章）。耶稣对门徒说，他们要“在耶路撒冷、犹太全地，和撒马利亚，直到地极”为他作见证（徒 1:8）。<br>
On the day of Pentecost, those who heard the disciples came from Parthia, Media, Elam, Mesopotamia, Egypt, Libya, Arabia, and elsewhere (Acts 2:9-11), places mostly in today's Iran, Iraq, Turkey, Egypt, and Libya. Soon after, Philip shared the gospel with an official of the Ethiopian queen, who was baptised and returned to Africa (Acts 8). Jesus told his disciples they would be his witnesses “in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the uttermost parts of the earth” (Acts 1:8).

## 早期向东、向南传 | Spreading east and south early on

- **非洲**：埃及的亚历山大城很早就是基督教的中心。在今天埃塞俄比亚一带的阿克苏姆王国，埃扎纳王大约在主后 333 年归信基督；EBSCO 的资料称阿克苏姆是“非洲最古老的基督教国家”。<br>
  **Africa**: Alexandria in Egypt was a centre of Christianity very early. In the kingdom of Aksum, in today's Ethiopia, King Ezana became a Christian around AD 333; an EBSCO reference calls Aksum “the oldest Christian state in Africa.”
- **印度**：印度西南部喀拉拉一带的“多马派基督徒”，按他们自己的传统，信仰来自使徒多马。大英百科称他们是“世界上最古老的基督教传统之一”。<br>
  **India**: The Thomas Christians of Kerala in southwestern India trace their faith, by their own tradition, to the apostle Thomas. Britannica describes them as “one of the oldest Christian traditions in the world.”
- **中国**：西安的“大秦景教流行中国碑”立于主后 781 年，记载东方教会的阿罗本在 635 年来到长安，受到唐太宗的接待。“扎根成长”路径里“圣经怎样来到中文世界”一课讲得更详细。<br>
  **China**: The Nestorian Stele in Xi'an, erected in AD 781, records that Alopen of the Church of the East reached Chang'an in 635 and was received by Emperor Taizong. The lesson on the Bible in the Chinese world, in the Going Deeper path, says more.

这些都比基督教传到北欧的一些地方还早。<br>
All of this came earlier than Christianity reached some parts of northern Europe.

## 今天的基督徒住在哪里 | Where Christians live today

皮尤研究中心 2011 年的报告说，1910 年约三分之二的基督徒住在欧洲；到 2010 年，住在欧洲的只占约四分之一，约 61% 住在南半球的发展中地区，比如非洲、拉丁美洲和亚洲。<br>
A 2011 Pew Research Center report says that in 1910 about two-thirds of the world's Christians lived in Europe; by 2010 only about a quarter did, and about 61% lived in the Global South, in places such as Africa, Latin America, and Asia.

## 福音和文化 | The gospel and culture

启示录描写的景象是：“从各国、各族、各民、各方来的”人一同站在宝座前（启 7:9）。圣经没有要求人放弃自己的语言和文化去成为另一个民族的人。使徒时代的教会讨论过外邦人要不要先守犹太人的规矩，结论是不必（徒 15 章）。<br>
Revelation pictures people “out of every nation and of all tribes, peoples, and languages” standing together before the throne (Revelation 7:9). The Bible does not require people to give up their language and culture to become members of another nation. The church in the apostles' time debated whether Gentiles must first keep Jewish customs, and concluded they need not (Acts 15).

当然，福音也会挑战每一种文化里和神的心意不合的地方，西方文化也不例外。基督徒在中国文化里怎样生活，例如怎样对待祭祖，各传统有不同的看法，需要认真思考。<br>
Of course, the gospel also challenges whatever in every culture is out of step with God's will, Western culture included. How Christians live within Chinese culture, for example regarding ancestral rites, is something the traditions see differently and that needs careful thought.
