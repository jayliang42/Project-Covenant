## 几部经典是怎样成书的 | How some classics came to be written

下面简单介绍几部影响很大的古代经典的成书经过。这里只讲成书方式，不评价它们的教导。<br>
Below is a short account of how several influential ancient classics came to be written. It covers only how they were written and does not judge their teaching.

- **《论语》**：记录孔子（主前 551–479 年）和弟子的言行，由他的弟子和再传弟子编成，大约成书于战国时期。1190 年，朱熹把它和另外三部书合编为“四书”。<br>
  **The Analects**: It records the words and deeds of Confucius (551–479 BC) and his disciples. It was compiled by his disciples and their students, probably during the Warring States period. In 1190 Zhu Xi published it with three other works as the Four Books.
- **巴利三藏**：南传佛教的经典，分律藏、经藏、论藏三部分。内容起初靠口传，大约在主前 1 世纪由斯里兰卡的僧团用巴利文写下来。<br>
  **The Pali canon**: The scriptures of Theravada Buddhism, in three parts: the Vinaya, the Sutta, and the Abhidhamma. Its content was first passed down orally and was written down in Pali by communities in Sri Lanka, probably in the 1st century BC.
- **《古兰经》**：按伊斯兰教的传统，是 7 世纪初分段降示给穆罕默德的。第三任哈里发奥斯曼（644–656 年在位）下令把一个定本抄送各地，并销毁其他抄本。<br>
  **The Qur'an**: According to Islamic tradition it was revealed to Muhammad in separate passages in the early 7th century. The third caliph, Uthman (reigned 644–656), had copies of one recension sent to the main cities and ordered other versions destroyed.

## 圣经成书的几个特点 | Some features of how the Bible was written

**许多作者，很长的时间**。圣经 66 卷，由大约四十位作者在大约一千五百年里写成，作者有君王、祭司、牧人、渔夫、税吏、医生。它是一部文集，有历史、律法、诗歌、先知书和书信。<br>
**Many writers over a long time.** The Bible's 66 books were written by about forty authors over about fifteen centuries. They include kings, priests, shepherds, fishermen, a tax collector, and a physician. It is a collection of history, law, poetry, prophecy, and letters.

**把事件放进可查的历史里**。路加开头说，他“从起头都详细考察了”，要让读者知道所学的是确实的（路 1:3-4）。他用当时的皇帝、巡抚、分封的王和大祭司来标明约翰开始传道的时间（路 3:1-2）。这些人名后来大多在圣经以外的史料或考古发现里得到了印证。<br>
**Events placed in checkable history.** Luke opens by saying he has “traced the course of all things accurately from the first,” so that his reader may know the certainty of what he was taught (Luke 1:3-4). He dates the start of John's preaching by the emperor, governor, tetrarchs, and high priests then in office (Luke 3:1-2). Most of these names have since been confirmed in sources or finds outside the Bible.

**信仰押在一件事上**。基督信仰的中心是一件历史事件：耶稣的死和复活。保罗写道，基督死了，埋葬了，第三天复活，显给彼得、十二使徒，又“一时显给五百多弟兄看，其中一大半到如今还在”（林前 15:6）。他等于在说：你们可以去问。他也承认，如果这件事是假的，信仰就是枉然（林前 15:14）。很多宗教最重要的是一套教导，教导本身不依赖某件事是否发生过。基督信仰却可以被历史证伪。<br>
**The faith staked on an event.** At the centre of the Christian faith is a historical event: the death and resurrection of Jesus. Paul writes that Christ died, was buried, rose on the third day, and appeared to Peter and the twelve, then “to over five hundred brothers at once, most of whom remain until now” (1 Corinthians 15:6). In effect he is saying, go and ask them. He also admits that if the event is false, faith is in vain (1 Corinthians 15:14). What matters most in many religions is a body of teaching that does not depend on whether a particular event took place. The Christian faith, by contrast, could be disproved by history.

**抄本多，年代早**。前面讲抄本的一课说过，新约的希腊文抄本有五千多份，最早的残片离原作只有几十年到一百多年。古代其他著作很少有这样多、这样早的抄本。<br>
**Many early copies.** As the lesson on manuscripts explained, there are more than five thousand Greek manuscripts of the New Testament, and the earliest fragments come within decades to a little over a century of the originals. Few other ancient works have copies so many or so early.

## 这些比较能说明什么 | What these comparisons show

这些特点说明了两件事：今天的圣经文本和最初写下的很接近；圣经的作者是把他们记的事当作真事来写的，并且写在可以查证的历史背景里。<br>
These features show two things: the Bible's text today is close to what was first written, and its writers recorded events as things that really happened, set in a history that can be checked.

它们不能代替每个人自己的判断。一本书抄得准、写得早，不等于它说的都对。基督徒相信圣经是神的话，这个信念最终建立在耶稣是谁、他是否复活这些问题上。前面几课讲过历史学者怎样看这些证据，你可以自己去衡量。<br>
They cannot replace each person's own judgment. A book that was copied accurately and written early is not thereby right in all it says. Christians believe the Bible is God's word, and that belief rests finally on who Jesus is and whether he rose. Earlier lessons showed how historians weigh this evidence, and you can weigh it yourself.

基督徒对圣经的权威也有不同的理解。多数福音派教会相信圣经在原稿上完全无误。天主教和东正教相信圣经是神默示的，同时看重教会传统对圣经的解释。一些主流新教教会认为圣经在信仰和生活上可靠，但不一定在每个历史和科学细节上无误。扎根成长路径里有一课专门讲这个问题。<br>
Christians also differ on how the Bible's authority should be understood. Most evangelical churches believe the Bible is without error in its original manuscripts. Catholics and Orthodox believe Scripture is inspired by God and also give weight to the church's tradition of interpreting it. Some mainline Protestant churches hold that the Bible is reliable for faith and life without necessarily being free of error in every historical and scientific detail. A lesson in the Going Deeper path takes up this question.
