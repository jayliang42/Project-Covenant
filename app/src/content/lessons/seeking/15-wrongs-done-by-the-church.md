## 这些事是真的 | These things happened

下面几件事常被人提起，它们都是历史事实：<br>
The following are often raised, and all of them are historical fact:

- **十字军**：11 世纪末到 13 世纪，西欧的基督徒多次出兵东征，途中有屠杀犹太人、穆斯林和东方基督徒的事。<br>
  **The Crusades**: From the late 11th to the 13th century, Christians from western Europe launched repeated military campaigns to the East, and along the way there were massacres of Jews, Muslims, and Eastern Christians.
- **宗教裁判所和宗教战争**：中世纪和近代早期，教会和信奉基督教的政府用监禁、酷刑和处死来对付被定为异端的人。宗教改革以后，天主教徒和新教徒也互相迫害。<br>
  **The Inquisition and the wars of religion**: In the medieval and early modern periods, churches and Christian governments used prison, torture, and execution against people judged to be heretics. After the Reformation, Catholics and Protestants also persecuted one another.
- **奴隶制**：很多基督徒参与了贩卖和奴役非洲人，还有人引用圣经为此辩护。<br>
  **Slavery**: Many Christians took part in buying, selling, and enslaving Africans, and some quoted the Bible in defence of it.
- **近年的丑闻**：一些神职人员侵害儿童，一些教会机构掩盖了这些事。这样的事在不同的宗派里都发生过。<br>
  **Recent scandals**: Some clergy abused children, and some church institutions covered it up. This has happened in more than one denomination.

基督徒没有理由否认或淡化这些事。<br>
Christians have no reason to deny these things or play them down.

## 圣经怎样看打着神的名义作恶 | How the Bible regards evil done in God's name

圣经对宗教上的虚伪批评得很重。耶稣对当时的宗教领袖说：“你们好像粉饰的坟墓，外面好看，里面却装满了死人的骨头和一切的污秽”（太 23:27）。旧约的先知也一再责备献祭却欺压穷人的人。<br>
The Bible is severe about religious hypocrisy. Jesus said to the religious leaders of his time, “you are like whitened tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness” (Matthew 23:27). The Old Testament prophets, too, repeatedly rebuked those who offered sacrifices while oppressing the poor.

耶稣还预先说过，会有人挂着他的名却不属于他：“凡称呼我主啊，主啊的人不能都进天国；惟独遵行我天父旨意的人才能进去”（太 7:21）。所以，有人奉基督的名作恶，这件事并没有推翻耶稣的话，耶稣自己早就说到了。<br>
Jesus also said beforehand that some would bear his name without belonging to him: “Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter into the Kingdom of Heaven; but he who does the will of my Father who is in heaven” (Matthew 7:21). So the fact that people have done evil in Christ's name does not overturn what Jesus said. He spoke of it himself.

评价这些事的标准，也正是耶稣自己的教导。他说要爱仇敌，要收刀入鞘，要作众人的仆人。十字军和宗教裁判所违背的就是这些话。<br>
The standard by which these things are judged is Jesus' own teaching. He said to love enemies, to put the sword back in its place, and to be the servant of all. The Crusades and the Inquisition went against exactly these words.

## 教会也认过错 | The church has also confessed

2000 年 3 月 12 日，教宗约翰·保禄二世在罗马主持了一次弥撒，为教会成员在历史上犯的罪公开求神赦免。祷文提到的过错有七类，包括为维护真理而使用不当的手段，破坏基督徒的合一，得罪以色列民，侵犯各民族的权利和文化，损害妇女的尊严。<br>
On 12 March 2000 Pope John Paul II presided at a Mass in Rome at which he publicly asked God's pardon for sins committed in history by members of the church. The prayers covered seven categories of wrong, including the use of wrongful methods in the service of truth, damage to Christian unity, sins against the people of Israel, violations of the rights and cultures of peoples, and offences against the dignity of women.

新教方面也有类似的例子。美南浸信会在 1995 年通过决议，为自己过去支持奴隶制和种族隔离道歉。<br>
There are similar examples among Protestants. In 1995 the Southern Baptist Convention adopted a resolution apologising for its past support of slavery and segregation.

## 同一段历史的另一面 | The other side of the same history

也应当看到另一面。推动废除奴隶贸易的人里，很多是基督徒。英国议员威伯福斯因为信仰投入这件事二十年，英国在 1807 年废除了奴隶贸易。早期的医院、孤儿院和许多大学，也是基督徒创办的。<br>
The other side should be seen as well. Many of those who pressed for the abolition of the slave trade were Christians. The British member of Parliament William Wilberforce gave twenty years to the cause because of his faith, and Britain abolished the slave trade in 1807. Early hospitals, orphanages, and many universities were also founded by Christians.

这些好事不能抵消前面的恶。把两方面放在一起，可以看到一个事实：自称基督徒的人，有的照耶稣的话做了，有的没有。<br>
These good things do not cancel the evils above. Set side by side, the two show one fact: of those who called themselves Christians, some did what Jesus said and some did not.

## 这对信仰说明了什么 | What this says about the faith

圣经从来没有说信徒是无罪的人。约翰说：“我们若说自己无罪，便是自欺”（约壹 1:8）。基督信仰的出发点是人有罪、需要拯救，教会里的人也包括在内。<br>
The Bible never claims that believers are sinless. John says, “If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves” (1 John 1:8). The Christian faith starts from the fact that people are sinful and need rescue, and people in the church are included.

所以，评价基督教是否可信，最后要看耶稣本人：他是谁，他说了什么，做了什么。信徒的失败是一个严肃的问题，但它是信徒的问题。<br>
So the final test of whether Christianity can be believed is Jesus himself: who he is, what he said, and what he did. The failure of believers is a serious matter, and it is a matter about believers.

如果你曾经被教会里的人伤害，你的愤怒是有道理的。耶稣对这样的事也发过怒。<br>
If you have been hurt by people in the church, your anger is justified. Jesus was angry at such things too.
