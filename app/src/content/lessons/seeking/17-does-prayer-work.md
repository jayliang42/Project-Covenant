## 一项研究 | One study

2006 年，《美国心脏杂志》发表了一项研究，简称 STEP，由哈佛医学院的赫伯特·本森主持。研究在六家医院进行，对象是 1802 位做冠状动脉搭桥手术的病人。病人分成三组：第一组有人代祷，但病人不确定；第二组没有人代祷，病人也不确定；第三组有人代祷，病人自己也知道。代祷的人是陌生人，按规定的内容祷告十四天。<br>
In 2006 the American Heart Journal published a study known as STEP, led by Herbert Benson of Harvard Medical School. It was carried out in six hospitals with 1,802 patients undergoing coronary bypass surgery. The patients were divided into three groups. The first was prayed for without being sure of it. The second was not prayed for and was likewise unsure. The third was prayed for and knew it. Those who prayed were strangers, who prayed in set words for fourteen days.

结果是：前两组的并发症比例没有明显差别。知道有人为自己代祷的那一组，并发症反而略多一些（59% 对 51%）。研究者说，他们对此没有明确的解释，也说明这项研究没有回答神是否垂听祷告。<br>
The result: the first two groups showed no significant difference in complications. The group that knew it was being prayed for had somewhat more complications (59 percent against 51 percent). The researchers said they had no clear explanation for this, and they stated that the study did not address whether God answers prayer.

这项研究值得诚实地面对。它说明，祷告并不像一种药，按剂量服用就产生可以统计的效果。<br>
The study deserves to be faced squarely. It shows that prayer does not behave like a drug that yields a measurable effect when taken in a set dose.

## 圣经讲的祷告是什么 | What the Bible means by prayer

圣经本来也没有把祷告讲成一种技术。耶稣说：“你们祷告，不可像外邦人，用许多重复话，他们以为话多了必蒙垂听……你们没有祈求以先，你们所需用的，你们的父早已知道了”（太 6:7-8）。<br>
The Bible never presents prayer as a technique. Jesus says, “In praying, don’t use vain repetitions, as the Gentiles do; for they think that they will be heard for their much speaking... your Father knows what things you need, before you ask him” (Matthew 6:7-8).

按耶稣的讲法，祷告是孩子对父亲说话。孩子可以求任何东西，父亲听见了，然后照他认为好的去做。所以祷告的结果不由祷告的人控制。一个人如果能用祷告操纵神，那位“神”就成了他的工具。<br>
On Jesus' account prayer is a child speaking to a father. The child may ask for anything. The father hears and then does what he judges good. So the outcome of prayer is not under the control of the one who prays. If a person could steer God by praying, that “god” would be the person's tool.

耶稣也鼓励人大胆地求：“你们祈求，就给你们；寻找，就寻见；叩门，就给你们开门”（太 7:7）。约翰补充了一个条件：“我们若照他的旨意求什么，他就听我们”（约壹 5:14）。<br>
Jesus also urges people to ask boldly: “Ask, and it will be given you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and it will be opened for you” (Matthew 7:7). John adds a condition: “if we ask anything according to his will, he listens to us” (1 John 5:14).

## 没有蒙应允的祷告 | Prayers that are not granted

圣经自己记下了没有照所求成就的祷告。耶稣在客西马尼园求父把苦杯撤去，然后说“不要照我的意思，只要照你的意思”，他还是上了十字架。保罗三次求主挪去他身上的“刺”，主没有挪去，只说“我的恩典够你用的”（林后 12:8-9）。<br>
The Bible itself records prayers that were not granted as asked. In Gethsemane Jesus asked the Father to take the cup away and then said, “not what I desire, but what you desire,” and he still went to the cross. Paul asked the Lord three times to remove the “thorn” in his flesh. The Lord did not remove it and said only, “My grace is sufficient for you” (2 Corinthians 12:8-9).

基督徒对此有几种理解，它们可以同时成立：神的回答有时是“可以”，有时是“不”，有时是“等一等”。神看到的比人多。祷告也会改变祷告的人。保罗说，把所要的告诉神，“神所赐、出人意外的平安必在基督耶稣里保守你们的心怀意念”（腓 4:6-7）。他应许的是平安，没有应许每件事都照人的意思成就。<br>
Christians understand this in several ways, which can all be true at once. God's answer is sometimes yes, sometimes no, and sometimes wait. God sees more than people do. Prayer also changes the person who prays. Paul says that when you make your requests known to God, “the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus” (Philippians 4:6-7). What he promises is peace. He does not promise that everything will turn out as we wish.

## 信的人和不信的人怎样看 | How believers and sceptics see it

不信的人会说：祷告成了就归功于神，不成就说是神的旨意，这样的说法没法检验。这个批评有它的道理。基督徒相信祷告，根据的主要是耶稣的教导和他的复活。祷告的“成功率”没法统计，也不是他们相信的理由。<br>
A sceptic will say that when prayer succeeds God gets the credit, and when it fails it is called God's will, so the claim cannot be tested. The criticism has some force. Christians trust prayer mainly on the ground of Jesus' teaching and his resurrection. The success rate of prayer cannot be counted, and it is not their reason for believing.

基督徒当中，对神今天多常用神迹回应祷告，也有不同的看法。五旬节派和灵恩派的教会期待神常常行神迹医治。另一些教会认为，神今天多半借着平常的途径回应，例如医生、药物和别人的帮助。两方都祷告，也都承认神可以照他的意思行事。<br>
Among Christians there are also different views on how often God now answers prayer by miracle. Pentecostal and charismatic churches expect God to heal miraculously often. Other churches think God now answers mostly through ordinary means, such as doctors, medicine, and the help of other people. Both pray, and both grant that God may act as he wills.

如果你还不确定有没有神，也可以试着祷告。可以这样说：“神啊，如果你在，请让我认识你。”这样的祷告，圣经说神喜欢听（耶 29:13）。<br>
If you are not yet sure there is a God, you can still try praying. You might say, “God, if you are there, let me come to know you.” The Bible says that God is glad to hear a prayer like this (Jeremiah 29:13).
