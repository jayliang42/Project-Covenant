## “罪”这个字 | The word “sin”

听到“罪”，很多人会想到犯法、坐牢。“我没杀人放火，怎么是罪人？”这是很自然的反应。<br>
When people hear the word “sin,” many think of crime and prison. “I haven't killed anyone or burned anything down. How am I a sinner?” That is a natural reaction.

圣经里最常译作“罪”的希伯来文和希腊文词，原意是“射不中目标”。人被造是要爱神、爱人，反映神的样子。罪就是没有达到这个目标。保罗说：“世人都犯了罪，亏缺了神的荣耀”（罗 3:23）。<br>
The Hebrew and Greek words most often translated “sin” in the Bible carry the sense of “missing the mark.” People were made to love God, love others, and reflect what God is like. Sin is falling short of that. Paul says, “All have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God” (Romans 3:23).

## 罪的几种样子 | What sin looks like

- **做了不该做的**：说谎、偷窃、伤害人、奸淫。这是大家最容易想到的。<br>
  **Doing what we should not**: lying, stealing, hurting others, adultery. These are what most people think of first.
- **没做该做的**：看见有需要的人，转身走开。雅各说：“人若知道行善，却不去行，这就是他的罪了”（雅 4:17）。<br>
  **Not doing what we should**: seeing someone in need and walking away. James writes, “To him therefore who knows to do good, and doesn’t do it, to him it is sin” (James 4:17).
- **心里的事**：耶稣说，恶念、凶杀、偷盗都是“从心里发出来的”（太 15:19）。他还说，向弟兄动怒的人，和杀人的一样要受审判（太 5:21-22）。<br>
  **What happens in the heart**: Jesus said evil thoughts, murders, and thefts come “out of the heart” (Matthew 15:19). He also said that anyone who is angry with his brother will face judgment, just as a murderer does (Matthew 5:21–22).
- **把别的东西放在神的位置上**：钱、成就、别人的认可、甚至家人，都可能成了人活着最看重的东西。圣经把这叫作拜偶像。<br>
  **Putting something else in God's place**: money, success, approval, even family can become what a person lives for most. The Bible calls this idolatry.

## 罪带来什么 | What sin leads to

先知以赛亚说：“你们的罪孽使你们与神隔绝”（赛 59:2）。罪最根本的后果，是人和神的关系断了。从这里又带出别的破裂：人和人互相伤害，人心里不安、羞愧。圣经还说：“罪的工价乃是死”（罗 6:23）。<br>
The prophet Isaiah says, “Your iniquities have separated you and your God” (Isaiah 59:2). The deepest result of sin is a broken relationship with God. Other breaks follow from it: people hurt one another, and people feel restless and ashamed inside. The Bible also says, “The wages of sin is death” (Romans 6:23).

这些话听起来很重。圣经讲罪，目的是带人回到神那里。约翰写道：“我们若认自己的罪，神是信实的，是公义的，必要赦免我们的罪，洗净我们一切的不义”（约壹 1:9）。耶稣说：“康健的人用不着医生，有病的人才用得着”（可 2:17）。<br>
These are heavy words. The Bible speaks about sin in order to bring people back to God. John writes, “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us the sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” (1 John 1:9). Jesus said, “Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick” (Mark 2:17).

医生说出病情，是为了治病。下一课讲的是圣经给的出路：恩典。<br>
A doctor names the illness in order to treat it. The next lesson is about the way out the Bible offers: grace.
