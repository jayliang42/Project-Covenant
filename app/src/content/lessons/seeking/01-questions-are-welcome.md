## 提问并不失礼 | Asking questions is not rude

有些人走进教会或翻开圣经时会担心：“我问太多问题，会不会显得不尊重？”<br>
Some people, coming to church or opening the Bible, worry: "If I ask too many questions, will I seem disrespectful?"

圣经里本来就有很多尖锐的问题。《诗篇》里有人问神“你为什么忘记我？”先知哈巴谷问神为什么容让恶人得势。四福音书里，不断有人当面向耶稣提问。<br>
The Bible itself contains many sharp questions. In the Psalms someone asks God, "Why have you forgotten me?" The prophet Habakkuk asks why God lets the wicked prosper. In the four Gospels people question Jesus to his face again and again.

## 拿但业和多马 | Nathanael and Thomas

《约翰福音》第 1 章里，拿但业听说弥赛亚来自小村镇拿撒勒，脱口就说：“拿撒勒还能出什么好的吗？”他的朋友腓力没有和他争，只说：**“你来看！”** 拿但业来到耶稣面前，耶稣称赞他是个“心里没有诡诈”的人。<br>
In John 1, Nathanael hears that the Messiah comes from the small town of Nazareth and says straight out, "Can anything good come out of Nazareth?" His friend Philip does not argue. He only says, **"Come and see!"** When Nathanael comes to Jesus, Jesus commends him as a man "in whom there is no deceit."

《约翰福音》第 20 章里，门徒多马没有亲眼见到复活的耶稣，就说：“我非看见他手上的钉痕……我总不信。”八天后耶稣再次显现。他没有赶走多马，对他说：“伸过你的指头来，摸我的手。”<br>
In John 20, the disciple Thomas had not seen the risen Jesus himself, and said that unless he saw the nail marks in his hands he would not believe. Eight days later Jesus appeared again. He did not send Thomas away. He said to him, "Put your finger here, and see my hands."

## 两种怀疑 | Two kinds of doubt

寻求信仰的过程中，分清两种态度会有帮助：<br>
As you explore faith, it helps to tell two attitudes apart:

- **找借口的怀疑**：并不想知道答案，提问只是为了把话题挡开。<br>
  **Doubt as an excuse**: Not wanting an answer, and asking questions only to keep the subject away.
- **求真的怀疑**：觉得这件事重要，不愿糊里糊涂地信，所以认真查考证据，也认真面对自己心里的结。<br>
  **Doubt that seeks the truth**: Thinking the matter important enough that you will not believe carelessly, so you examine the evidence and face what troubles you.

“认真思考信仰”这条路径是为第二种人准备的。带着你的问题往下读，就像腓力说的：“你来看。”<br>
The Seeking path is made for the second kind. Bring your questions and read on. As Philip said: "Come and see."
