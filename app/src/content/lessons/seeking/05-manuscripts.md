## 一个常见的疑问 | A common question

很多人读圣经之前会问：“圣经写成已经两三千年了，中间全靠手抄。会不会像传话游戏一样，传到今天已经完全走样？”这个问题问得合理。<br>
Before reading the Bible, many people ask: "The Bible was written two or three thousand years ago and copied by hand for centuries. Could it be like a game of telephone, with the message completely changed by now?" It is a fair question.

研究这个问题的学科叫**文本校勘学（Textual Criticism）**。下面看新旧约现存的抄本有哪些。<br>
The field that studies this is **textual criticism**. Here is what manuscripts of the Old and New Testaments survive.

## 抄本多，可以互相比对 | Many copies that can be checked against each other

传话游戏会走样，是因为只有一条线，前面的人说了什么也没有留下记录。圣经的流传情况不同：<br>
A game of telephone distorts the message because there is only one chain and no record of what earlier people said. The Bible was passed on differently:

- **旧约（希伯来圣经）**：有中世纪抄写严谨的**马所拉抄本**，如阿勒颇抄本和列宁格勒抄本。1947 年起，死海边的昆兰洞穴又出土了**死海古卷**，年代约在主前 250 年至主后 68 年之间。除《以斯帖记》外，旧约各卷都有抄本或残片，其中最著名的《大以赛亚书卷》几乎完整保存了全书 66 章。两批抄本相隔约一千年，比对下来，主体文本相当一致。<br>
  **The Old Testament (Hebrew Bible)**: There are the carefully copied medieval **Masoretic manuscripts**, such as the Aleppo and Leningrad Codices. From 1947 the **Dead Sea Scrolls** were found in the Qumran caves by the Dead Sea, dating from about 250 BC to AD 68. Every Old Testament book except Esther is represented by a copy or fragment, and the best known, the Great Isaiah Scroll, preserves almost all 66 chapters of the book. The two groups of manuscripts are about a thousand years apart, and when compared, the main text is largely the same.
- **新约（希腊文）**：现存**超过 5,800 份希腊文抄本**，在古代文献里很少见。其中有第二世纪的莎草纸残片 P52，有约主后 200 年前后的 P46、P66、P75，也有第四世纪完整的《西奈抄本》和《梵蒂冈抄本》。此外还有上万份拉丁文、叙利亚文、科普特文的古译本，以及早期教父的大量引文。<br>
  **The New Testament (Greek)**: **More than 5,800 Greek manuscripts** survive, which is rare for an ancient text. They include the second-century papyrus fragment P52, the papyri P46, P66, and P75 from around AD 200, and the complete fourth-century codices Sinaiticus and Vaticanus. There are also more than ten thousand manuscripts of ancient translations in Latin, Syriac, and Coptic, and many quotations in the early church fathers.

新约很早就传遍了地中海沿岸。抄本分散在亚历山大、安提阿、罗马等地，没有哪一个皇帝或主教能把它们全部收回来改掉。<br>
The New Testament spread around the Mediterranean very early. Copies were scattered in Alexandria, Antioch, Rome, and elsewhere, and no emperor or bishop could have gathered them all in to change them.

## 抄本之间的差异 | Differences between manuscripts

手抄一定会产生“异文”，也就是抄本之间字词上的差异。关于异文，有两点要同时知道：<br>
Hand copying always produces variants, that is, differences of wording between manuscripts. Two things should be known about them together:

1. **绝大多数异文是无心的抄写差错**，比如希腊文的拼写不同、词序调换。对比早期各系统的抄本，就能辨别出来。<br>
   **The great majority of variants are unintended copying slips**, such as differences in Greek spelling or changes in word order. They can be identified by comparing the early manuscript families.
2. **少数较长的异文，现代圣经都公开标明**。例如《马可福音》16:9-20 和《约翰福音》7:53–8:11（行淫时被拿的妇人），最早的希腊文抄本里没有。今天严谨的中文和英文圣经都会用括号或脚注写明：“最早的古卷没有这一段”。<br>
   **The few longer variants are marked openly in modern Bibles.** For example, Mark 16:9–20 and John 7:53–8:11 (the woman caught in adultery) are absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts. Careful Chinese and English Bibles today bracket or footnote them with a note such as "the earliest manuscripts do not include this passage."

结论是：**基督教的主要信仰没有哪一条建立在某一处有争议的异文上**。我们今天读到的新旧约，忠实地保存了先知和使徒要传达的信息。<br>
The conclusion: **no major Christian belief rests on a disputed variant.** The Old and New Testaments we read today faithfully preserve the message the prophets and apostles meant to pass on.
