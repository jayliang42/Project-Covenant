## 一个常见的顾虑：“传了这么久，早就被改了吧？” | A common worry: "Hasn't it been changed over the centuries?"

很多朋友在读圣经前会问一个非常合理的问题：“圣经写成已经两三千年了，中间全是手工抄写，会不会就像传话游戏一样，传到今天早就面目全非了？”<br>
Many thoughtful readers ask a fair question: "The Bible was written thousands of years ago and copied by hand for centuries. Could it have been altered like a game of telephone until the original message was lost?"

这个问题不需要靠盲目断言来回答，而是属于**文本校勘学（Textual Criticism）**的研究范围。我们可以把新旧约抄本的真实家底摊开来看。<br>
We do not need guesswork to answer this; it belongs to the historical discipline of **textual criticism**. Let us look at the actual manuscript evidence.

## 不是“单线传话”，而是“多地交叉比对” | Not a single-line whisper game, but multi-branch cross-checking

“传话游戏”之所以会失真，是因为只有一条单线、而且上一棒说的话没留下字据。但圣经文本的流传恰恰相反：<br>
A telephone game distorts a message because there is only one chain of whispers and earlier steps disappear. Biblical transmission worked very differently:

- **旧约（希伯来圣经）**：一方面有中世纪极度严谨的**马所拉抄本**（如阿勒颇抄本、列宁格勒抄本）；另一方面，1947 年起在死海昆兰洞穴出土了约主前 250 年至主后 68 年间的**死海古卷**（包含除《以斯帖记》外所有旧约书卷的抄本或残片，最著名的《大以赛亚书卷》几乎完整保存了全书 66 章）。跨越一千年两相比对，核心文本的吻合度令人惊叹。<br>
  **The Old Testament**: Alongside meticulous medieval **Masoretic manuscripts** (such as the Aleppo and Leningrad Codices), the **Dead Sea Scrolls** discovered from 1947 onward provided manuscripts and fragments from c. 250 BC – AD 68 (including the complete 66-chapter Great Isaiah Scroll). Comparing copies across a thousand-year gap revealed striking stability alongside helpful textual notes.
- **新约（希腊文圣经）**：新约在古代文献中拥有罕见的抄本密度——现存有**超过 5,800 份希腊文抄本**（从第二世纪的莎草纸残片如 P52、约主后 200 年前后的 P46/P66/P75，到第四世纪完整的《西奈抄本》《梵蒂冈抄本》），外加上万份拉丁文、叙利亚文、科普特文古译本以及早期教父的大量引文。<br>
  **The New Testament**: The New Testament is attested by **over 5,800 Greek manuscripts** — ranging from second- and third-century papyri (such as P52, P46, P66, and P75) and fourth-century complete codices (Sinaiticus and Vaticanus) to thousands of early translations and patristic quotations.

正因为新约很早就传遍地中海两岸，没有任何一个皇帝或主教能把分散在亚历山大、安提阿、罗马各地的抄本同时收回篡改。<br>
Because copies spread rapidly across the Mediterranean, no single ruler or bishop could ever round up all existing manuscripts to rewrite them secretly.

## 诚实面对“抄本异文”（Variants） | Facing manuscript variants with honesty

手抄时代当然会产生“异文”（抄本之间的字词差异）。保持诚实的证据边界，需要同时看清两件事：<br>
Hand copying inevitably introduced textual variants (differences between manuscripts). Intellectual honesty requires holding two facts together:

1. **绝大多数异文是无心之失**：比如希腊文拼写差异、同义词序调换，通过对比早期抄本家族就能清楚辨别。<br>
   **The vast majority of variants are minor**: Spelling differences, word order shifts, or accidental slips of the eye that can be spotted by comparing manuscript families.
2. **极少数较长异文在现代圣经里全部公开透明**：例如《马可福音》16:9-20 和《约翰福音》7:53–8:11（行淫时被拿的妇人），在最早的希腊文抄本中未见。今天你打开任何一本严谨的中文或英文圣经，都会看到括注或脚注清楚写明：“最早的古卷没有这一段”。<br>
   **The few longer variants are flagged openly in your Bible**: Passages such as Mark 16:9–20 and John 7:53–8:11 are absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts, and modern Chinese and English Bibles explicitly bracket and footnote them for every reader to see.

最关键的结论是：**没有任何一条核心基督教信仰，是建立在某一处有争议的孤立异文之上的**。我们今天读到的新旧约，忠实地保存了先知与使徒原本要传递的信息。<br>
The bottom line is straightforward: **no core Christian belief rests on a disputed manuscript variant**. The Bibles in our hands faithfully preserve the message the prophets and apostles wrote.
