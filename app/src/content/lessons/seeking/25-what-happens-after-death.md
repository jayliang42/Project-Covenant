## 圣经怎样讲死亡 | What the Bible says about death

圣经不回避死亡。它说死亡不是神起初造人的样子，是罪带进世界的（罗 5:12）。诗篇说：“求你指教我们怎样数算自己的日子，好叫我们得着智慧的心”（诗 90:12）。传道书说，神“将永生安置在世人心里”（传 3:11），人心里有一种超过今生的渴望。<br>
The Bible does not avoid death. It says death was not part of how God first made humanity; it came into the world through sin (Romans 5:12). A psalm prays, “teach us to count our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom” (Psalm 90:12). Ecclesiastes says God “has also set eternity in their hearts” (Ecclesiastes 3:11): people long for something beyond this life.

希伯来书说，耶稣成了血肉之体，“要借着死败坏那掌死权的……并要释放那些一生因怕死而为奴仆的人”（来 2:14-15）。基督教的盼望建立在一件事上：耶稣自己死了，又复活了。“复活在我，生命也在我。信我的人虽然死了，也必复活”（约 11:25）。<br>
Hebrews says Jesus shared our flesh and blood “that through death he might bring to nothing him who had the power of death... and might deliver all of them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage” (Hebrews 2:14-15). The Christian hope rests on one thing: Jesus himself died and rose again. “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies” (John 11:25).

## 死后到复活之前 | Between death and resurrection

新约讲到信徒死后的几句话：耶稣对十字架上的强盗说：“今日你要同我在乐园里了”（路 23:43）。保罗说：“情愿离世与基督同在，因为这是好得无比的”（腓 1:23），又说“更愿意离开身体与主同住”（林后 5:8）。新约也常把信徒的死称为“睡了”（帖前 4:13）。<br>
The New Testament says a few things about believers after death. Jesus told the thief on the cross, “today you will be with me in Paradise” (Luke 23:43). Paul says he desires “to depart and be with Christ, which is far better” (Philippians 1:23), and is “willing rather to be absent from the body and to be at home with the Lord” (2 Corinthians 5:8). The New Testament also often speaks of believers who have died as those who “have fallen asleep” (1 Thessalonians 4:13).

这段时间的细节，圣经说得不多，各传统的理解不同：<br>
Scripture says little about the details of this period, and the traditions understand it differently:

- **多数新教和东正教**：信徒死后，灵魂立刻与基督同在，等候身体复活。东正教会为离世的人祷告，但没有天主教那样的炼狱教义。<br>
  **Most Protestants and Orthodox**: At death believers' souls are immediately with Christ, awaiting the resurrection of the body. The Orthodox Church prays for the departed but has no doctrine of purgatory like the Catholic one.
- **天主教**：《天主教教理》第 1030 条说，在神的恩宠中死去、但还没有完全洁净的人，虽然已确定得救，死后还要经过炼净，才能进入天上的喜乐。天主教也为离世的人祈祷。新教不接受炼狱的教义。<br>
  **Catholic**: Paragraph 1030 of the Catechism says that those who die in God's grace but are not yet fully purified, though assured of eternal salvation, undergo purification after death in order to enter the joy of heaven. Catholics also pray for the dead. Protestants do not accept the doctrine of purgatory.
- **基督复临安息日会**：基本信仰第 26 条说，死亡对所有人都是“没有知觉的状态”，直到基督再来时义人复活。<br>
  **Seventh-day Adventists**: Fundamental Belief 26 says death is “an unconscious state for all people” until the righteous are raised when Christ returns.

## 最终的盼望 | The final hope

各传统都同意，基督徒最终的盼望是身体复活，和新天新地；灵魂永远离开身体并不是终点。保罗说，到那时“死被得胜吞灭”，又说：“死啊！你得胜的权势在哪里？死啊！你的毒钩在哪里？”（林前 15:54-55）。“刚开始信”路径里“我信身体复活，我信永生”一课讲得更详细。<br>
All traditions agree that the Christian's final hope is the resurrection of the body and a new heaven and earth; the soul's departure from the body is not the end of the story. Paul says that then “Death is swallowed up in victory,” and asks, “Death, where is your sting? Hades, where is your victory?” (1 Corinthians 15:54-55). The lesson on the resurrection of the body and the life everlasting, in the New Believer path, says more.

圣经也说，死后有审判（来 9:27）。“地狱和审判”一课讨论了这个难题。<br>
The Bible also says that judgment follows death (Hebrews 9:27). The lesson on hell and judgment discusses this hard question.

## 怎样面对死亡 | Facing death

基督徒也会为亲人的离世难过。耶稣在拉撒路的坟前也哭了。保罗说，信徒为睡了的人忧伤，但不“像那些没有指望的人一样”（帖前 4:13）。有盼望的哀伤，和没有盼望的哀伤，是不一样的。<br>
Christians also grieve when loved ones die. Jesus wept at Lazarus' tomb. Paul says believers grieve for those who have fallen asleep, but not “like the rest, who have no hope” (1 Thessalonians 4:13). Grief with hope is different from grief without it.
