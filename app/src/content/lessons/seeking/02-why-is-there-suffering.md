## 这是最诚实、也最沉重的问题 | The heaviest and most honest question

许多人不信神，不是因为傲慢，而是因为心痛：“如果真有一位全能又慈爱的神，为什么会有癌症、地震、战争和无辜孩子的眼泪？”<br>
For many people, the biggest barrier to faith is not arrogance but heartbreak: "If an all-powerful and loving God exists, why is the world full of cancer, earthquakes, war, and innocent tears?"

如果你带着这个问题来，首先要知道：**圣经从来没有回避这个问题，甚至比任何宗教经典都更密集地记录了受苦者的质问**。<br>
If you carry this question, know first of all that **the Bible never hides from it; in fact, it preserves the raw cries of sufferers more candidly than almost any ancient book**.

## 圣经首先拆毁了三种廉价的回答 | Three cheap answers the Bible rejects

在《约伯记》和四福音书里，圣经首先帮我们排除了三种不诚实的回答：<br>
Across Job, the Psalms, and the Gospels, Scripture rules out three false comforts:

1. **它不否认痛苦的真实**：圣经没有说“痛苦只是幻觉”。死亡在圣经里被称为“仇敌”（林前 15:26），耶稣在朋友拉撒路的坟墓前亲自流泪（约 11:35）。<br>
   **It never calls pain an illusion**: In Scripture, death is an enemy (1 Corinthians 15:26), and Jesus himself wept at his friend Lazarus's grave (John 11:35).
2. **它拒绝冷酷的“因果报应公式”**：当约伯无故失去一切时，他的三个朋友一口咬定“你受苦一定是因为暗中犯了罪”。但在全书结尾，神严厉责备那三个朋友没有说实话（伯 42:7）；耶稣也明确否定“人生来瞎眼是因为他或父母犯了罪”（约 9:1-3）。<br>
   **It rejects mechanical karma**: When Job lost everything, his three friends insisted he must have sinned secretly. At the end of the book, God rebuked the three friends (Job 42:7); Jesus likewise rejected the idea that a man born blind was being punished for his or his parents' sins (John 9:1–3).
3. **它不要求人强颜欢笑**：《诗篇》里有超过三分之一是“哀歌”（Lament）——圣经教人把“我的神，你为什么远离我”直接带到神面前倾诉。<br>
   **It never demands fake smiles**: Over a third of the Psalms are laments — prayers that bring "Why, Lord?" directly to God.

## 圣经给出的三重回应 | Three layers of the biblical response

我们必须诚实承认：当具体的苦难临到一个人身上时，没有任何一套简短的理论能立刻消除眼泪。但圣经给出了三个层面的亮光：<br>
No tidy formula erases grief when tragedy strikes. Yet Scripture offers three anchors that hold together:

- **过去：世界已经偏离了原本的设计（创 3；罗 8:20-22）**：人类的自私与叛逆不仅带来人与人之间的伤害（道德之恶），也使整个受造界落在败坏与叹息之中（自然之苦）。这世界现在的样子，不是神起初的心意。<br>
  **The past — a world out of joint (Genesis 3; Romans 8:20–22)**: Human rebellion introduced both moral cruelty between people and a groaning disorder across creation. The world as it is now is not the world as God designed it to be.
- **现在：一位亲自走进苦难的神（赛 53；路 23）**：基督教最震撼的地方在于——神没有躲在天上当旁观者。在耶稣基督里，神亲自来到人间，经历贫穷、背叛、冤屈与十字架的剧痛。当你受苦时，你不需要向一位不懂疼痛的神解释眼泪。<br>
  **The present — a God with scars (Isaiah 53; Luke 23)**: God did not remain a distant spectator. In Jesus Christ, God stepped into human flesh, tasted betrayal, injustice, and crucifixion. When you suffer, you speak to a God who knows pain from the inside.
- **未来：眼泪不是故事的最后一页（启 21:4）**：复活显明死亡和邪恶没有最后的发言权。神应许在万物更新的日子，要亲自擦去人一切的眼泪——苦难有终点，而爱没有终点。<br>
  **The future — tears are not the last page (Revelation 21:4)**: The resurrection of Jesus means evil and death do not get the final word. God has promised a renewed creation where every tear is wiped away.
