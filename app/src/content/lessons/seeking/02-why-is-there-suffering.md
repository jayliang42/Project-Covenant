## 一个沉重的问题 | A heavy question

许多人不信神，原因是心痛：“如果真有一位全能又慈爱的神，为什么有癌症、地震、战争，为什么无辜的孩子要流泪？”<br>
Many people do not believe in God because of heartbreak: "If there is an all-powerful and loving God, why are there cancer, earthquakes, and war, and why do innocent children weep?"

如果你带着这个问题来，先要知道：**圣经里记录了大量受苦者对神的质问**。<br>
If you come with this question, know first that **the Bible records many sufferers questioning God**.

## 圣经否定的三种回答 | Three answers the Bible rejects

在《约伯记》《诗篇》和四福音书里，圣经先排除了三种说法：<br>
In Job, the Psalms, and the four Gospels, Scripture first rules out three answers:

1. **“痛苦只是幻觉”**：圣经承认痛苦是真实的。死亡在圣经里被称为“仇敌”（林前 15:26），耶稣在朋友拉撒路的坟墓前流了泪（约 11:35）。<br>
   **"Pain is only an illusion"**: Scripture treats pain as real. It calls death an "enemy" (1 Corinthians 15:26), and Jesus wept at the grave of his friend Lazarus (John 11:35).
2. **“受苦一定是因为犯了罪”**：约伯无故失去一切，三个朋友却认定他暗中犯了罪。全书结尾，神责备这三个朋友说得不对（伯 42:7）。耶稣也否定了一种说法：这人生来瞎眼，是因为他自己或父母犯了罪（约 9:1-3）。<br>
   **"Suffering always means you sinned"**: When Job lost everything without cause, his three friends insisted he had sinned in secret. At the end of the book God rebukes the three friends for not speaking rightly (Job 42:7). Jesus also rejected the idea that a man was born blind because he or his parents had sinned (John 9:1–3).
3. **“信徒应该强颜欢笑”**：《诗篇》里超过三分之一是“哀歌”（lament）。圣经教人把“我的神，你为什么远离我”这样的话直接带到神面前。<br>
   **"Believers should put on a smile"**: Over a third of the Psalms are laments. Scripture teaches people to bring words like "My God, why are you so far from me?" straight to God.

## 圣经的三方面回应 | Three parts of the Bible's response

要承认，苦难临到一个具体的人时，没有哪套简短的理论能马上止住眼泪。圣经从三个方面回应：<br>
We should admit that when suffering comes to a particular person, no short theory stops the tears at once. Scripture responds in three ways:

- **过去：世界偏离了起初的样子**（创 3；罗 8:20-22）。人的自私和悖逆带来人与人之间的伤害（道德之恶），也使整个受造界落在败坏和叹息之中（自然之苦）。世界现在的样子，和神起初的心意不同。<br>
  **The past: the world has departed from how it began** (Genesis 3; Romans 8:20–22). Human selfishness and rebellion brought harm between people (moral evil) and left all creation in decay and groaning (natural suffering). The world as it is now differs from what God first intended.
- **现在：神亲自进入苦难**（赛 53；路 23）。在耶稣基督里，神来到人间，经历贫穷、背叛、冤屈和十字架的剧痛。你受苦的时候，面对的是一位懂得疼痛的神。<br>
  **The present: God himself entered suffering** (Isaiah 53; Luke 23). In Jesus Christ, God came among us and went through poverty, betrayal, injustice, and the agony of the cross. When you suffer, you are speaking to a God who knows pain.
- **未来：眼泪会被擦去**（启 21:4）。耶稣的复活表明，死亡和邪恶不会胜到最后。神应许，在万物更新的日子，他要亲自擦去人一切的眼泪。<br>
  **The future: tears will be wiped away** (Revelation 21:4). Jesus' resurrection shows that death and evil will not win in the end. God has promised that when all things are made new, he himself will wipe away every tear.
