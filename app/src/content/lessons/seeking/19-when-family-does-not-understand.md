## 圣经要人孝敬父母 | The Bible commands honour for parents

有人以为信了耶稣就是“不要祖宗”“不孝”。圣经的教导正相反。十诫的第五条是：“当孝敬父母”（出 20:12）。耶稣责备过那些借口奉献给神而不供养父母的人（可 7:9-13）。保罗说：“人若不看顾亲属，就是背了真道，比不信的人还不好”（提前 5:8）。<br>
Some people assume that believing in Jesus means abandoning one's ancestors and failing in filial duty. The Bible teaches the opposite. The fifth of the Ten Commandments is, “Honor your father and your mother” (Exodus 20:12). Jesus rebuked people who used gifts to God as an excuse for not supporting their parents (Mark 7:9-13). Paul says, “if anyone doesn’t provide for his own, and especially his own household, he has denied the faith, and is worse than an unbeliever” (1 Timothy 5:8).

所以信了耶稣的人，应当比以前更尊重父母，更关心家人。很多父母起初反对，后来态度改变，是因为看到孩子信了以后变得更体贴、更负责。<br>
So a person who comes to believe in Jesus should respect parents and care for family more than before. Many parents who objected at first changed their minds because they saw their child become more considerate and more responsible.

## 耶稣也说过难听的话 | Jesus also said hard things

耶稣同时说：“爱父母过于爱我的，不配作我的门徒”（太 10:37）。这句话的意思是，神在人心里的位置要在一切之上，包括最亲的人。大多数时候，爱神和爱父母并不冲突。只有当父母要求孩子否认信仰、或者做违背神的事的时候，才需要做选择。<br>
Jesus also says, “He who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me” (Matthew 10:37). The saying means that God's place in a person's heart comes above everything, including those closest to us. Most of the time, loving God and loving parents do not conflict. A choice has to be made only when parents require their child to deny the faith or to act against God.

即使在这种时候，态度也很重要。彼得说，回答别人的时候要“以温柔、敬畏的心”（彼前 3:15）。不同意父母的要求，仍然可以说话恭敬，照常尽儿女的责任。<br>
Even then, manner matters. Peter says to answer others “with humility and fear” (1 Peter 3:15). A person can decline what parents ask and still speak respectfully and carry out a child's duties as before.

## 祭祖怎么办：各教会的做法 | Ancestor rites: what churches do

这是华人信徒问得最多的问题之一。各教会都同意两点：第一，孝敬父母、纪念先人是应当的。第二，敬拜和祈求只能归给神，十诫的第一、第二条禁止拜别的神和偶像。分歧在于：上香、献供、跪拜这些动作，算是表达敬意，还是算宗教上的敬拜。<br>
This is one of the questions Chinese believers ask most. All churches agree on two points. First, honouring parents and remembering the dead is right. Second, worship and petition belong to God alone, and the first and second commandments forbid worshipping other gods and idols. The disagreement is whether acts such as offering incense, presenting food, and kneeling express respect or amount to religious worship.

- **天主教**：这个问题在历史上争论了很久，称为“中国礼仪之争”。明末清初的耶稣会士认为，祭祖祭孔是表达敬意的礼俗，可以容许。道明会和方济各会的会士不同意。教宗克勉十一世在 1704 年和 1715 年下令禁止，1742 年本笃十四世重申禁令。1939 年 12 月 8 日，教廷发布法令，准许信徒参加敬孔和敬祖的礼仪。今天华人天主教堂在春节常有敬祖的仪式。<br>
  **Catholic**: The matter was disputed for a long time in what is called the Chinese Rites Controversy. The Jesuits of the late Ming and early Qing held that rites for ancestors and for Confucius were customs expressing respect and could be tolerated. Dominicans and Franciscans disagreed. Pope Clement XI banned the rites in 1704 and 1715, and Benedict XIV confirmed the ban in 1742. On 8 December 1939 the Holy See issued a decree permitting the faithful to take part in ceremonies honouring Confucius and their ancestors. Chinese Catholic parishes today often hold a rite honouring ancestors at the Lunar New Year.
- **多数华人新教教会**：不上香，不献供，不向牌位跪拜，认为这些动作在民间信仰里带有敬拜和祈求先人保佑的意思。他们用别的方式纪念先人，例如扫墓、献花、默哀、整理家谱、举行追思礼拜。<br>
  **Most Chinese Protestant churches**: They do not offer incense or food and do not kneel before ancestral tablets, holding that in folk religion these acts carry the sense of worship and of asking the dead for protection. They remember the dead in other ways, such as visiting graves, laying flowers, standing in silence, keeping the family genealogy, and holding memorial services.
- **新教内部也有不同**：20 世纪 80 年代，亚洲的福音派学者专门开会讨论过祭祖问题。与会者一致认为敬拜祖先违背圣经，但对界线划在哪里看法不同：有人主张找出可以替代的纪念方式，有人主张连扫墓和修家谱也应当避免。鞠躬是否可以，各教会的意见也不一样。<br>
  **Protestants also differ among themselves**: In the 1980s evangelical scholars in Asia held a consultation on ancestor practices. The participants agreed that worshipping ancestors is contrary to Scripture and differed on where the line falls. Some proposed finding substitute ways of remembrance, and others held that even grave-sweeping and keeping genealogies should be avoided. Churches also differ on whether bowing is acceptable.

如果你将来信了主，遇到这个问题，可以请教你所在教会的牧者，也可以提前和家人商量你能做什么。很多信徒的做法是：主动承担扫墓、准备饭菜、照顾长辈这些事，让家人看到自己并没有忘本。<br>
If you come to faith and meet this question, ask the pastor of your church, and talk with your family beforehand about what you are able to do. Many believers take the lead in visiting the graves, preparing the family meal, and caring for the elders, so that their family can see they have not forgotten their roots.

## 几个实际的建议 | Some practical suggestions

- **不急着争论**。先让家人看到你的改变，再找合适的时候说明你信的是什么。<br>
  **Do not hurry into argument.** Let your family first see the change in you, and then find a good moment to explain what you believe.
- **说清楚你没有不要这个家**。很多父母担心的是失去孩子。<br>
  **Make clear that you are not leaving the family.** What many parents fear is losing their child.
- **听他们的担心**。有些担心是具体的，例如怕你被骗、怕你耽误学业。这些可以用事实来回应。<br>
  **Listen to their worries.** Some are concrete, such as fear that you are being deceived or that your studies will suffer. These can be answered with facts.
- **为家人祷告，也要有耐心**。家人的态度改变可能需要很多年。<br>
  **Pray for your family, and be patient.** A change in their attitude may take many years.
