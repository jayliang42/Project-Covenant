## 相近的地方 | Where religions are alike

很多宗教都教人诚实、孝顺、不偷盗、帮助穷人。不少宗教都有祈祷、经典和节期，也都在回答人生的大问题：我从哪里来？活着为了什么？死后会怎样？<br>
Many religions teach honesty, respect for parents, not stealing, and helping the poor. Many have prayer, scriptures, and festivals, and all try to answer life's big questions: Where did I come from? What am I here for? What happens after death?

圣经也承认人有寻求神的心。保罗在雅典对一群哲学家说，神造了万族的人，“要叫他们寻求神，或者可以揣摩而得，其实他离我们各人不远”（徒 17:27）。<br>
The Bible also recognizes that people seek God. In Athens Paul told a group of philosophers that God made every nation “that they should seek the Lord, if perhaps they might reach out for him and find him, though he is not far from each one of us” (Acts 17:27).

## 回答不同的地方 | Where the answers differ

把几个大宗教放在一起比较，会发现它们对几个基本问题的回答很不一样：<br>
When you set the major religions side by side, you find that they answer some basic questions very differently:

- **神是谁？** 有的宗教相信一位创造主，有的相信很多神，有的不以神为中心。<br>
  **Who is God?** Some religions believe in one Creator, some in many gods, and some do not centre on a god at all.
- **人出了什么问题？** 有的说是无知，有的说是欲望，基督教说是人和神的关系破裂了，就是圣经所说的“罪”。<br>
  **What is wrong with people?** Some say ignorance, some say desire. Christianity says the relationship between people and God is broken, which the Bible calls sin.
- **出路在哪里？** 许多宗教要人靠修行、行善或遵守规条，一步步往上走。基督教说，神亲自来到人中间，人得救靠的是神的恩典。<br>
  **What is the way out?** Many religions ask people to climb step by step through spiritual discipline, good deeds, or keeping rules. Christianity says God himself came down to us, and people are saved by his grace.

这些回答不能同时都对。说“所有宗教都一样”听起来很宽容。可是这样说，就没有认真听每个宗教自己说了什么。<br>
These answers cannot all be right at the same time. Saying “all religions are the same” sounds generous. Yet it ignores what each religion says about itself.

## 耶稣的说法 | What Jesus claimed

耶稣说：“我就是道路、真理、生命；若不借着我，没有人能到父那里去”（约 14:6）。这句话让人很难对他保持中立。如果他说的是错的，他就是一个自视过高的老师。如果他说的是真的，人就需要认真面对他。<br>
Jesus said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me” (John 14:6). This makes it hard to stay neutral about him. If he was wrong, he was a teacher who thought far too much of himself. If he was right, people need to deal with him seriously.

基督徒相信这句话，也要尊重别人。保罗在雅典先认真看了他们的宗教，再清楚说出自己相信什么（徒 17:22-31）。今天的基督徒也可以这样做：尊重信其他宗教的朋友，听他们怎么说，也坦白说出自己为什么相信耶稣。<br>
Christians who believe this are still called to respect others. In Athens Paul first paid close attention to their religion and then said clearly what he believed (Acts 17:22–31). Christians today can do the same: respect friends of other faiths, listen to them, and say plainly why they trust Jesus.

如果你还在比较和思考，这很正常。一个比较好的起点，是直接去读耶稣在福音书里说了什么、做了什么，再自己判断。<br>
If you are still comparing and thinking it over, that is normal. A good place to start is to read for yourself what Jesus said and did in the Gospels, and then judge for yourself.
