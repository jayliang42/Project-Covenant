## 先承认这个问题很难 | First, the problem is real

《申命记》和《约书亚记》记载，神吩咐以色列人进入迦南地，把一些城里的居民全部除灭。很多人读到这里很难受，信徒也一样。这个问题不该被轻轻带过。<br>
Deuteronomy and Joshua record that God commanded Israel to enter Canaan and to destroy all the inhabitants of certain cities. Many people find this hard to read, believers included. The question should not be brushed aside.

下面先看经文自己提供了哪些说明，再看基督徒的几种理解。<br>
Below we look first at what the text itself supplies, and then at the ways Christians understand it.

## 经文自己说了什么 | What the text itself says

- **这是审判，原因是罪**。《利未记》18 章列出迦南人的一些做法，包括把儿女献给神明作祭物，然后说：“连地也玷污了，所以我追讨那地的罪孽”（利 18:25）。<br>
  **It was a judgment, and the cause was sin.** Leviticus 18 lists some Canaanite practices, including the sacrifice of children to a god, and then says, “The land was defiled. Therefore I punished its iniquity” (Leviticus 18:25).
- **神等了几百年**。神对亚伯拉罕说，他的后裔要过四代才回到那地，“因为亚摩利人的罪孽还没有满盈”（创 15:16）。<br>
  **God waited for centuries.** God told Abraham that his descendants would return to the land only after four generations, “for the iniquity of the Amorite is not yet full” (Genesis 15:16).
- **原因不在以色列人比别人好**。“你进去得他们的地，并不是因你的义，也不是因你心里正直，乃是因这些国民的恶”（申 9:5）。后来以色列人犯了同样的罪，神也让他们亡国被掳。<br>
  **The reason was not that Israel was better.** “Not for your righteousness or for the uprightness of your heart do you go in to possess their land; but for the wickedness of these nations” (Deuteronomy 9:5). Later Israel committed the same sins, and God let their kingdom fall and sent them into exile.
- **愿意归向神的人得了保全**。耶利哥城的喇合承认以色列的神是“上天下地的神”，她和全家都活了下来（书 2:11；6:25）。她的名字后来出现在耶稣的家谱里（太 1:5）。<br>
  **Those who turned to God were spared.** Rahab of Jericho confessed that Israel's God is “God in heaven above, and on earth beneath,” and she and her whole family lived (Joshua 2:11; 6:25). Her name later appears in the genealogy of Jesus (Matthew 1:5).
- **这个命令只限于那个时间和地点**。律法对迦南地以外的城另有规定，要先向对方宣告和平（申 20:10）。<br>
  **The command was limited to that time and place.** For cities outside Canaan the law set a different rule, requiring that peace first be offered (Deuteronomy 20:10).
- **神不喜悦恶人死亡**。“我断不喜悦恶人死亡，惟喜悦恶人转离所行的道而活”（结 33:11）。<br>
  **God takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked.** “I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked; but that the wicked turn from his way and live” (Ezekiel 33:11).

## 基督徒的几种理解 | How Christians understand it

对这些经文，认真的基督徒学者有不同的看法。Biola 大学的旧约学者 Charlie Trimm 把常见的回应归纳成几类：<br>
Serious Christian scholars read these passages differently. Charlie Trimm, an Old Testament scholar at Biola University, groups the common responses as follows:

- **接受经文的记载，把它看作神在特定时刻的审判**。这种看法认为，神是生命的主，也是审判者。他在那个时候借以色列人审判迦南人，这是独特的事件，预示着末后的审判。很多福音派和改革宗的学者持这种看法。<br>
  **Accept the account as God's judgment at a particular moment.** On this view God is the Lord of life and the judge. At that time he judged the Canaanites through Israel, in a unique event that points ahead to the final judgment. Many evangelical and Reformed scholars hold this view.
- **认为经文用的是当时战争记录惯用的夸张说法**。这种看法指出，古代近东的战争记录常说“全部除灭”，实际指的是决定性的胜利。《约书亚记》说某些地方的人被除尽了，《士师记》里这些人却仍然住在那里。持这种看法的学者认为，战事的规模比字面上小，主要针对军事据点。<br>
  **Read the language as the conventional exaggeration of ancient war records.** This view notes that ancient Near Eastern war accounts often say “utterly destroyed” when they mean a decisive victory. Joshua says the people of certain places were wiped out, and in Judges the same peoples are still living there. Scholars who hold this view think the campaigns were smaller than the wording suggests and were aimed mainly at military strongholds.
- **认为这些经文反映了古人对神有限的认识，要用耶稣来衡量**。这种看法认为，以色列人按当时的观念理解并记录了神的旨意，而神完全的启示在耶稣身上，耶稣教导人“要爱你们的仇敌”（太 5:44）。持这种看法的人多数不接受“圣经无误”的说法。批评者认为，这样会让读者自己决定哪些经文算数。<br>
  **Hold that these texts reflect a limited ancient understanding of God, to be measured by Jesus.** On this view Israel understood and recorded God's will in the terms of its day, while God's full revelation is in Jesus, who taught, “love your enemies” (Matthew 5:44). Most who hold this view do not accept the inerrancy of Scripture. Critics say it leaves readers to decide for themselves which texts count.

也有人因为这些经文而拒绝圣经的神。这是不信的人常提的理由之一。<br>
There are also people who reject the God of the Bible because of these texts. It is one of the reasons unbelievers most often give.

## 几点可以确定的事 | A few things that are clear

各种理解在几点上是一致的。第一，教会历来都不认为这些命令今天还适用。历史上有人引用这些经文为征服和屠杀辩护，这是对圣经的滥用。第二，新约没有给教会任何用武力传教的吩咐。第三，圣经从头到尾都说神会审判恶。如果世上的恶永远没有人追究，那并不是一个更好的世界。<br>
The different understandings agree on several points. First, the church has never held that these commands apply today. People in history have quoted these texts to defend conquest and massacre, and that was an abuse of the Bible. Second, the New Testament gives the church no instruction to spread the faith by force. Third, the Bible says from beginning to end that God will judge evil. A world in which evil is never called to account would not be a better world.

基督徒还会指出一件事：圣经里审判落得最重的地方是十字架。按基督信仰，神的儿子在那里亲自担当了罪的刑罚。这没有回答关于迦南的全部问题，但它说明了施行审判的是怎样的一位神。<br>
Christians point to one more thing. The place in the Bible where judgment falls most heavily is the cross. According to the Christian faith, God's own Son bore the penalty of sin there. This does not answer every question about Canaan. It does show what kind of God it is who judges.

你不需要现在就接受其中任何一种理解。可以带着这个问题继续读，也可以找人一起讨论。<br>
You do not need to accept any of these understandings now. You can keep reading with the question open, and you can talk it through with others.
