## 为什么“有神吗”从来不是一个过时的问题？ | Why "Does God exist?" never goes out of date

在人类历史上，无论是古人仰望浩瀚星空，还是现代人透过望远镜观察星系、透过显微镜观察细胞里的遗传密码，都会忍不住问同一个问题：**这一切是从哪里来的？背后有一位设计者与赋予意义的主宰吗？**<br>
Across history — whether ancient shepherds looking up at the night sky or modern scientists peering into galaxies and genetic code — people keep asking the same question: **Where did all of this come from, and is there a Mind and purpose behind it?**

圣经并没有把“相信有神”建立在盲目迷信上，而是邀请人留意身边三个每天都能接触到的路标（神学上常称为“普遍启示”与“特殊启示”）。<br>
The Bible does not ask for blind superstition; it invites us to pay attention to three signposts we encounter every day.

## 路标一：宇宙的存在与可理解的秩序（诗篇 19） | Signpost 1: Cosmic existence and intelligible order (Psalm 19)

为什么“有万物存在，而不是一无所有”？为什么宇宙遵循着高度精密、可以用数学公式优美描述的物理定律，使生命得以存在？<br>
Why is there something rather than nothing? And why does the universe follow deep, mathematically elegant laws capable of sustaining life?

这里要守住诚实的边界：这些观察并不是实验室里把神“装进试管”的物理证明；但它们像一幅杰作上的签名，合理地指向一位有智慧的创造主（诗 19:1）。而且，**自然科学与信仰并不处在彼此抢地盘的冲突中**：科学回答水壶里的水“怎样”因分子运动而沸腾，而信仰与目的回答“为什么”有人要烧这壶水——读懂自然界的机制，并不取消立法者的存在。<br>
To be clear about boundaries: these observations are not a laboratory experiment that puts God in a test tube; rather, like signature marks on a canvas, they point reasonably toward a wise Creator (Psalm 19:1). Nor do **science and faith compete for the same explanatory slot**: physics explains *how* the water in a kettle boils, while personal agency explains *why* someone put the kettle on to make tea.

## 路标二：人心深处的道德直觉与渴望（罗马书 2:14-15） | Signpost 2: Moral conscience and human longing (Romans 2:14–15)

几乎所有人——无论在什么文化里——在看到欺凌弱小、背叛与虐待时，心里都会涌起一种强烈的直觉：“这是不对的！”我们不只是觉得它“不合群”或“不划算”，而是相信存在着一条高于强权、连掌权者也必须受审的**客观公义与良善标准**。<br>
Almost everyone, across every culture, feels a deep conviction when witnessing cruelty against the helpless: "That is truly wrong!" We do not merely feel it is socially inconvenient; we sense an **objective standard of justice and goodness** that stands above human power.

《罗马书》2:15 说，这种是非之心（良知）像刻在人心里的律法痕迹，指向一位公义良善的立法者。<br>
Romans 2:15 describes conscience as the trace of God's law written on the human heart, pointing to a righteous Moral Giver.

## 路标三：如果神存在，他为什么不说话？（约翰福音 1:14, 18） | Signpost 3: If God is there, has he spoken? (John 1:14, 18)

单靠观察星空和良心，我们能感到背后似乎有一位造物主，却无法确知他的心意。基督教信仰最核心的宣告是：**这位创造主没有保持沉默**。他在历史中借着众先知说话，最终在拿撒勒人耶稣身上亲自走进人类历史——“从来没有人看见神，只有在父怀里的独生子将他表明出来”（约 1:18）。<br>
Looking at the stars and conscience can suggest a Creator, yet leave us wondering who he is personally. The central claim of Christianity is that **the Creator did not stay silent**. He spoke through the prophets and finally stepped into history in Jesus of Nazareth: "No one has seen God at any time. The one and only Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, he has declared him" (John 1:18).

因此，探索“有没有神”最好的下一步，就是直接去读一读福音书里的耶稣，看他是否配得你认识与信靠。<br>
That is why the best next step in asking "Does God exist?" is to look directly at Jesus in the Gospels and see what kind of God he reveals.
