## 一个一直有人问的问题 | A question people keep asking

古人仰望星空，现代人用望远镜看星系、用显微镜看细胞里的遗传密码，都会问同一个问题：**这一切从哪里来？背后有没有一位设计者，一位赋予意义的主宰？**<br>
Ancient people looked up at the night sky. Modern people study galaxies through telescopes and the genetic code through microscopes. Both ask the same question: **where did all this come from, and is there a designer behind it who gives it meaning?**

圣经没有要人盲目相信。它请人留意三个每天都能接触到的路标。神学上把前两个称为“普遍启示”，第三个称为“特殊启示”。<br>
The Bible does not ask for blind belief. It asks us to notice three signposts we meet every day. Theology calls the first two general revelation and the third special revelation.

## 路标一：宇宙存在，而且有秩序（诗篇 19） | Signpost 1: The universe exists and is ordered (Psalm 19)

为什么有万物存在，而不是什么都没有？为什么宇宙遵循精密的物理定律，这些定律能用数学描述，又使生命得以存在？<br>
Why is there something and not nothing? Why does the universe follow precise physical laws that can be described mathematically and that allow life to exist?

这些观察算不上实验室里的物理证明。但它们合理地指向一位有智慧的创造主（诗 19:1）**。自然科学和信仰也并不冲突**。科学能解释水壶里的水怎样因分子运动而沸腾。“有人想泡茶”能解释这壶水为什么被烧开。两种解释可以同时成立。同样，弄懂了自然界的机制，并不能因此排除创造主的存在。<br>
These observations are not a laboratory proof. But they point reasonably toward a wise Creator (Psalm 19:1). **Natural science and faith do not conflict either.** Science can explain how the water in a kettle boils through the motion of molecules. "Someone wanted tea" explains why the kettle was put on. Both explanations can be true at once. In the same way, understanding the mechanisms of nature does not rule out a Creator.

## 路标二：人心里的道德直觉（罗马书 2:14-15） | Signpost 2: The moral sense within us (Romans 2:14–15)

几乎所有人，无论在什么文化里，看到欺凌弱小、背叛和虐待，心里都会强烈地觉得“这是不对的”。这种感觉比“不合群”“不划算”强得多。我们相信有一个**客观的公义和良善的标准**，高于强权，连掌权的人也要按它受审。<br>
Almost everyone, in every culture, feels strongly that bullying the weak, betrayal, and abuse are wrong. The feeling is much stronger than finding them antisocial or costly. We believe there is an **objective standard of justice and goodness** above power, by which even rulers are judged.

《罗马书》2:15 说，这种是非之心（良知）是律法的功用刻在人心里。它指向一位公义良善的立法者。<br>
Romans 2:15 describes conscience as the work of the law written on human hearts. It points to a just and good lawgiver.

## 路标三：神有没有说话（约翰福音 1:14, 18） | Signpost 3: Has God spoken? (John 1:14, 18)

只看星空和良心，我们能感到背后似乎有一位造物主，却无法确知他的心意。基督教信仰的中心宣告是：**这位创造主说话了**。他在历史中借着众先知说话，最后在拿撒勒人耶稣身上亲自来到人间：“从来没有人看见神，只有在父怀里的独生子将他表明出来”（约 1:18）。<br>
From the stars and conscience alone, we may sense a Creator behind them, but we cannot know his mind. The central claim of the Christian faith is that **this Creator has spoken**. He spoke in history through the prophets, and finally came among us in person in Jesus of Nazareth: "No one has seen God at any time. The one and only Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, has declared him" (John 1:18).

所以，想弄清有没有神，下一步可以直接读福音书里的耶稣，看他值不值得你认识和信靠。<br>
So a good next step in asking whether God exists is to read about Jesus in the Gospels and see whether he is worth knowing and trusting.
