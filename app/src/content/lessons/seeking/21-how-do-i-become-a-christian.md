## 新约里的人怎样回应 | How people responded in the New Testament

新约记载了很多人信主的经过，情形各不相同。<br>
The New Testament records many people coming to faith, each in different circumstances.

- 五旬节那天，众人听了彼得讲道，“觉得扎心”，问：“我们当怎样行？”彼得说：“你们各人要悔改，奉耶稣基督的名受洗，叫你们的罪得赦”（徒 2:37-38）。<br>
  On the day of Pentecost the crowd heard Peter preach, “were cut to the heart,” and asked, “what shall we do?” Peter said, “Repent, and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of sins” (Acts 2:37-38).
- 腓立比的狱卒在地震之后问保罗：“我当怎样行才可以得救？”保罗说：“当信主耶稣，你和你一家都必得救”（徒 16:30-31）。当夜他和全家就受了洗。<br>
  After an earthquake the jailer at Philippi asked Paul, “what must I do to be saved?” Paul said, “Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved, you and your household” (Acts 16:30-31). That night he and his household were baptized.
- 和耶稣同钉十字架的一个犯人说：“耶稣啊，你得国降临的时候，求你记念我！”耶稣说：“今日你要同我在乐园里了”（路 23:42-43）。他没有机会受洗，也没有机会做任何事。<br>
  One of the criminals crucified with Jesus said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your Kingdom.” Jesus said, “today you will be with me in Paradise” (Luke 23:42-43). He had no chance to be baptized or to do anything at all.

这些记载有共同的内容：人认识到自己需要神，转向耶稣（悔改），信靠他是主、是救主（信）。保罗把它概括成一句话：“你若口里认耶稣为主，心里信神叫他从死里复活，就必得救”（罗 10:9）。约翰说：“凡接待他的，就是信他名的人，他就赐他们权柄作神的儿女”（约 1:12）。<br>
These accounts share a common core. A person sees the need for God, turns to Jesus (repentance), and trusts him as Lord and Saviour (faith). Paul sums it up in one sentence: “if you will confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved” (Romans 10:9). John says, “as many as received him, to them he gave the right to become God’s children, to those who believe in his name” (John 1:12).

## 各教会的做法不同 | Churches do this differently

人怎样“正式”成为基督徒，各教会的做法和理解不一样。<br>
Churches differ in practice and understanding on how a person “formally” becomes a Christian.

- **福音派和很多华人教会**：常常邀请人做“决志祷告”，用自己的话向神认罪，接受耶稣作救主和生命的主，然后在教会里学习，再受洗。这种祷告的固定说法圣经里没有，它是 19 世纪宣教运动中逐渐形成的，20 世纪的布道会使它广为流传。一些福音派牧者批评说，有人以为念过一次祷告就“保险”了，却没有真正悔改。2012 年美南浸信会通过决议，肯定这种祷告可以是悔改和信心在圣经上的表达。<br>
  **Evangelical and many Chinese churches**: They often invite a person to pray a “prayer of commitment,” confessing sin to God in their own words and receiving Jesus as Saviour and Lord, then to learn in the church and be baptized. No fixed form of this prayer appears in the Bible. It took shape in the 19th-century missionary movement and spread widely through 20th-century evangelistic campaigns. Some evangelical pastors criticise it, saying that some people think one recited prayer has made them safe without real repentance. In 2012 the Southern Baptist Convention adopted a resolution affirming such a prayer as a biblical expression of repentance and faith.
- **浸信会和多数独立教会**：信的人受浸，作为公开见证。洗礼是顺服的行动，人在受洗以前已经因信得救。<br>
  **Baptist and most independent churches**: Believers are baptized by immersion as a public testimony. Baptism is an act of obedience, and a person is already saved by faith before being baptized.
- **天主教**：成人通过“成人入门圣事礼”加入教会，先慕道学习，一般在复活节前夕同时领受洗礼、坚振和圣体。天主教教导神借着洗礼赦免罪、赐下新生命。<br>
  **Catholic**: Adults enter the church through the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults. They first study as catechumens and usually receive baptism, confirmation, and the Eucharist together at the Easter Vigil. The Catholic Church teaches that God forgives sin and gives new life through baptism.
- **东正教**：慕道学习以后，三次浸入水中受洗，随即领受傅圣油（坚振）和圣餐。同样相信洗礼是神施恩的圣事。<br>
  **Orthodox**: After a period of instruction, a person is baptized by threefold immersion and then receives chrismation and Holy Communion. The Orthodox likewise hold that baptism is a sacrament through which God gives grace.
- **信义宗、圣公会和改革宗**：给成人和婴孩施洗，认为洗礼是神的应许的记号和印证。信义宗特别强调神在洗礼里实际赐下赦罪的恩典。<br>
  **Lutheran, Anglican, and Reformed**: These churches baptize adults and infants and regard baptism as a sign and seal of God's promise. Lutherans stress that in baptism God really gives the grace of forgiveness.
- **基督教会（Churches of Christ）**：源于 19 世纪美国的“恢复运动”，认为信的人必须受浸，罪才得赦（徒 2:38；22:16）。<br>
  **Churches of Christ**: Arising from the 19th-century American Restoration Movement, these churches hold that believers must be immersed for their sins to be forgiven (Acts 2:38; 22:16).

各传统都同意：人得救是因为神的恩典，借着信基督，信的人要受洗，并且在教会里继续跟随基督。<br>
All traditions agree that a person is saved by God's grace through faith in Christ, that believers are to be baptized, and that they go on following Christ in the church.

## 说不清是哪一天 | When you cannot name the day

很多人说不清自己是哪一天信的。有人是在一次聚会里，有人是读经很久以后慢慢明白的，有人是在一个家里从小就听。日期并不重要，重要的是你现在是否信靠耶稣。<br>
Many people cannot say on what day they came to believe. For some it happened at a single meeting, for others slowly after long reading, and for others it came from growing up in a believing home. The date matters less than whether you trust Jesus now.

## 如果你想信 | If you want to believe

你可以现在就用自己的话向神祷告。例如：“神啊，我承认我需要你。我相信耶稣为我的罪死了，又从死里复活。我愿意信靠他，跟随他。求你帮助我。”这几句话本身没有特别的力量，重要的是心里的转向。<br>
You can pray to God now in your own words. For example: “God, I admit that I need you. I believe that Jesus died for my sins and rose from the dead. I want to trust him and follow him. Please help me.” The words themselves have no special power. What matters is the turning of the heart.

然后去找一间教会，告诉牧者或一位信徒你的决定。他们可以陪你继续学习，也会和你谈受洗的事。这个 App 的“刚开始信”路径也是为这个时候准备的。<br>
Then find a church and tell a pastor or a believer what you have decided. They can keep learning with you and will talk with you about baptism. The New Believer path in this app was written for this time too.
