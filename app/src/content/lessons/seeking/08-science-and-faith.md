## 一个常见的印象 | A common impression

很多人有这样的印象：科学越发达，信仰就越站不住；科学家大多不信神。这个说法在 19 世纪后期流行起来。今天研究科学史的学者大多认为，这种“冲突论”把历史讲得太简单了。<br>
Many people have the impression that the more science advances, the less faith can stand, and that scientists mostly do not believe in God. This view became popular in the late 19th century. Most historians of science today think this “conflict thesis” makes the history far too simple.

近代科学的许多开创者是有信仰的人，例如开普勒、波义耳、牛顿。他们相信世界出于一位有秩序的创造主，所以值得用理性去研究。到了现代也有这样的例子：提出宇宙有开端的勒梅特是天主教神父，领导人类基因组计划的柯林斯（Francis Collins）是公开的基督徒。<br>
Many founders of modern science were people of faith, such as Kepler, Boyle, and Newton. They believed the world came from an orderly Creator and was therefore worth studying with reason. There are modern examples too. Lemaître, who proposed that the universe had a beginning, was a Catholic priest, and Francis Collins, who led the Human Genome Project, is an open Christian.

伽利略和教会的冲突确实发生过，可是那件事牵涉到当时的学术权威、政治和个人关系，用“科学对宗教”一句话来概括并不准确。<br>
The conflict between Galileo and the church did happen. But it involved the academic authorities of the day, politics, and personal relationships, and “science versus religion” is not an accurate summary of it.

## 怎样读《创世记》1 章 | How to read Genesis 1

基督徒对《创世记》1 章的读法不完全一样。常见的有三种：<br>
Christians do not all read Genesis 1 the same way. Three views are common:

- **年轻地球创造论**：把六日理解为六个普通的日子，认为地球的年龄大约几千年到一万年左右。<br>
  **Young-earth creation**: Takes the six days as ordinary days and holds that the earth is roughly several thousand to ten thousand years old.
- **古老地球创造论**：接受地球和宇宙有几十亿年的历史，认为“日”可以指较长的时期，或者认为第 1 章是一种有文学结构的叙述。<br>
  **Old-earth creation**: Accepts that the earth and universe are billions of years old, and takes the “days” as longer periods or reads chapter 1 as a carefully structured literary account.
- **进化创造论**：接受生物进化的科学结论，认为进化是神创造生命的方式。<br>
  **Evolutionary creation**: Accepts the scientific account of biological evolution and sees evolution as the way God created life.

这三种看法都承认同一件事：神是创造主，人按神的形像被造，有特别的尊严和责任。<br>
All three views agree on the same points: God is the Creator, and people are made in God's image, with a special dignity and responsibility.

## 科学能回答什么，不能回答什么 | What science can and cannot answer

科学研究的是自然界怎样运作，用观察和实验来检验。它很擅长回答“怎样”的问题：星星怎样形成，疾病怎样传播。<br>
Science studies how the natural world works and tests its ideas by observation and experiment. It is very good at “how” questions: how stars form, how diseases spread.

有一些问题，实验室里测不出来：宇宙为什么存在，而不是什么都没有？人生有没有意义？为什么我们应该善待弱者？这些问题需要哲学、伦理和信仰一起来思考。<br>
Some questions cannot be measured in a laboratory. Why does the universe exist at all? Does life have meaning? Why should we treat the weak with kindness? These questions call for philosophy, ethics, and faith to think about them together.

基督徒也要小心一种做法：把科学暂时解释不了的地方都说成“这是神做的”。等科学解释了，神好像就被挤出去了。圣经说的神，是整个世界的创造主和托住者，“万有也靠他而立”（西 1:17）。科学能解释的部分，也在神的手里。<br>
Christians should also be careful of one habit: pointing to whatever science cannot yet explain and saying “God did that.” When science later explains it, God seems to be pushed out. The God of the Bible is the Creator and sustainer of the whole world, in whom “all things are held together” (Colossians 1:17). The parts science can explain are in his hands too.
