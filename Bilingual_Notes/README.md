## Language and Source Status | 语言与来源状态

Completed dated notes use bilingual titles and paired English/Chinese summaries or sections. The chronological index records the teaching type and language status. A source transcript is not presented as a polished bilingual note until the recoverable content, uncertainty, and date are clear.

已完成的日期笔记使用双语标题，并提供成对的英文／中文总结或章节。时间索引会记录教导类型与语言状态。只有在可恢复内容、不确定处与实际日期都已说明后，来源逐字稿才会被整理为双语精修笔记。

A note may summarize a sermon rather than reproduce it. Direct quotations, inferred details, personal reflections, and supplemental research must be visibly distinguished from one another.

笔记可以总结讲道，但不复制整篇逐字稿。直接引语、谨慎推断、个人反思与补充研究必须彼此清楚区分。

## Add or Update a Note | 新增或更新笔记

1. Preserve the actual teaching date and teaching type.
   保留实际教导日期与类型。
2. Use a descriptive filename and a bilingual H1 title in the form `English | 中文`.
   使用描述性文件名，并以 `English | 中文` 形式写双语一级标题。
3. Pair substantive English and Chinese content; do not treat a translated title alone as a complete bilingual note.
   实质内容须中英成对；不能只翻译标题就标为完整双语。
4. Link the note from [INDEX_Chronological.md](./INDEX_Chronological.md) and add a series route here when several notes form a coherent sequence.
   把笔记加入 [INDEX_Chronological.md](./INDEX_Chronological.md)；若多篇构成清楚系列，也在本页补充系列入口。
5. Run `python3 scripts/audit_markdown.py` from the repository root.
   在仓库根目录运行 `python3 scripts/audit_markdown.py`。
