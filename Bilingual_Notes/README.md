## Reading Status | 阅读状态

Notes use bilingual titles and paired English/Chinese summaries or sections. The index records the source date and language coverage. Language completion does not establish human review: the Draft label at the top of each major page records that review is still pending.<br>
笔记使用双语标题，并提供成对的英文／中文总结或章节。索引记录来源日期与语言覆盖。语言整理完成不代表已经人工审阅；主要页面开头的 Draft 标注说明人工审阅仍待完成。

## Privacy Boundary | 隐私边界

Public notes retain Scripture references, teaching claims, and general applications. The source church is described generically, without its name, website, or meeting details. A date identifies the source session, not the maintainer's attendance. Personal stories, medical or mental-health histories, private pastoral-care details, addresses, roles, and contact information must be removed or generalized before publication.<br>
公开笔记保留经文、教学论点和一般应用；来源教会只以泛称描述，不写名称、网址或聚会细节。日期标识来源聚会，不表示维护者出席。个人经历、医疗或心理健康经历、私人牧养关怀细节、地址、职分和联系方式，必须在发布前删除或泛化处理。

## Contribution Rule | 整理规则

Keep the source teaching date, use a descriptive bilingual filename when creating a new polished note, and link it from the chronological index. Preserve necessary direct quotations, distinguish them from editorial summaries, and do not publish personal reflections or identifiable anecdotes.<br>
新建精修笔记时，保留来源讲道日期，使用描述性的双语文件名，并加入时间索引。保留必要的直接引语并与编辑总结清楚区分；不得发布个人反思或可识别的私人轶事。
