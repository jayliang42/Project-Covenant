# People of the Bible | 圣经人物

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> This page introduces, in story order, thirty-three entries (including two married couples) for people who carry the Bible’s story forward. Each entry tells you where to read about the person and what part they play. It summarizes the biblical text; it does not settle historical or dating questions.<br>
> 本篇按故事顺序介绍三十三组推动圣经故事的人物（其中两组是夫妇）。每一条都写明该读哪几章，以及此人在故事中的角色。这里概括的是圣经文本，不处理历史考证或定年问题。

> **Language status | 语言状态:** Fully bilingual, paragraph by paragraph.
>
> **语言状态 | Language status：** 全文按段落提供中英双语。

When a passage names someone you do not know, look the person up here, read the chapters listed, and then return to your passage. The Bible does not present these people as heroes to copy in everything. Many of them fail badly, and the story keeps pointing past them to God’s faithfulness.<br>
读经时遇到不认识的人名，可以先在这里查，读一读所列的章节，再回到原来的经文。圣经并没有把这些人写成处处值得效法的英雄；许多人跌倒得很重，故事一再越过他们，指向神的信实。

<a id="beginnings"></a>
## Beginnings | 起初

<a id="adam-and-eve"></a>
### Adam and Eve | 亚当和夏娃

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 1–3 / 创世记 1–3 章

The first man and woman are made in God’s image and placed in a good garden. They distrust God’s word, disobey, and are sent out of Eden, yet God already speaks of a future victory over evil.<br>
第一对男女按神的形象被造，被安置在美好的园子里。他们不信神的话、违背神，被赶出伊甸园；但神当时已经说到将来要胜过邪恶。

<a id="noah"></a>
### Noah | 挪亚

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 6–9 / 创世记 6–9 章

In a world full of violence, Noah walks with God and builds the ark at God’s word. After the flood, God makes a covenant with all living things and gives the rainbow as its sign.<br>
在充满强暴的世界里，挪亚与神同行，照神的话造方舟。洪水之后，神与一切活物立约，并以彩虹为记号。

<a id="family-of-promise"></a>
## The Family of Promise | 应许之家

<a id="abraham"></a>
### Abraham | 亚伯拉罕

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 12–25 / 创世记 12–25 章

God calls Abraham to leave his homeland and promises him offspring, land, and blessing for all the families of the earth. He trusts God, though not always well, and becomes the father of the promised family.<br>
神呼召亚伯拉罕离开本地，应许赐他后裔、土地，并要地上万族因他得福。他信靠神，虽然并非每次都信得好，却成了应许之家的先祖。

<a id="sarah"></a>
### Sarah | 撒拉

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 12–23 / 创世记 12–23 章

Abraham’s wife waits many years without a child and once laughs at the promise. In her old age she gives birth to Isaac, a sign that the promise depends on God, not on human strength.<br>
亚伯拉罕的妻子多年无子，也曾对应许发笑。她年老时生下以撒，表明应许的成就在于神，而不在于人的力量。

<a id="isaac"></a>
### Isaac | 以撒

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 21–27 / 创世记 21–27 章

The long-awaited son of promise is nearly offered on Mount Moriah, but God provides a ram in his place. Later he passes the family blessing on, though not to the son he intended.<br>
等候多年才得的应许之子，在摩利亚山上几乎被献上，神却预备了一只公羊代替他。后来他把家族的祝福传下去，却不是按他原本的心意。

<a id="jacob"></a>
### Jacob | 雅各

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 25–35 and 46–49 / 创世记 25–35 章、46–49 章

A grasping younger twin, Jacob deceives his brother and father, then is himself deceived. God meets him again and again, renames him Israel, and his twelve sons become the heads of Israel’s tribes.<br>
雅各是个争抢的双胞胎弟弟，骗了哥哥和父亲，后来自己也被人欺骗。神一次又一次遇见他，给他改名为以色列；他的十二个儿子成了以色列各支派的先祖。

<a id="joseph"></a>
### Joseph | 约瑟

**Read | 阅读：** Genesis 37–50 / 创世记 37–50 章

Sold by his brothers into slavery, Joseph rises to rule in Egypt and saves many lives in a famine. He forgives his brothers and sees God’s good purpose in what they meant for harm.<br>
约瑟被哥哥们卖为奴隶，后来在埃及掌权，在饥荒中救了许多人的性命。他饶恕了哥哥们，看见他们的恶意中仍有神美好的旨意。

<a id="rescue-and-covenant"></a>
## Rescue and Covenant | 拯救与立约

<a id="moses"></a>
### Moses | 摩西

**Read | 阅读：** Exodus 1–20, then Numbers and Deuteronomy / 先读出埃及记 1–20 章，再读民数记和申命记

Saved as a baby and later called at the burning bush, Moses leads Israel out of slavery in Egypt. At Sinai he receives God’s law and stands between God and the people through forty years in the wilderness.<br>
摩西婴孩时蒙拯救，后来在燃烧的荆棘中蒙召，带领以色列人走出埃及的奴役。他在西奈山领受神的律法，又在旷野四十年间站在神与百姓之间。

<a id="joshua"></a>
### Joshua | 约书亚

**Read | 阅读：** Numbers 13–14; Joshua 1–6 and 23–24 / 民数记 13–14 章；约书亚记 1–6 章、23–24 章

Moses’ assistant becomes Israel’s leader and brings the people into the promised land. Near the end of his life he calls them to choose to serve the Lord alone.<br>
摩西的助手后来成了以色列的领袖，带百姓进入应许之地。他晚年呼召百姓选择单单事奉耶和华。

<a id="judges-and-kings"></a>
## Judges and Kings | 士师与君王

<a id="deborah"></a>
### Deborah | 底波拉

**Read | 阅读：** Judges 4–5 / 士师记 4–5 章

A prophet who judges Israel, Deborah summons a commander to fight an oppressor and then sings of the victory God gives. Her story shows God raising leaders in a disordered time.<br>
底波拉是一位作士师的女先知，她召来将领迎战欺压者，事后歌颂神所赐的胜利。她的故事显明神在混乱的时代兴起带领者。

<a id="ruth"></a>
### Ruth | 路得

**Read | 阅读：** Ruth 1–4 / 路得记 1–4 章

A Moabite widow stays loyal to her mother-in-law Naomi and finds protection through Boaz, a kinsman. She becomes the great-grandmother of King David, an outsider woven into Israel’s royal line.<br>
摩押寡妇路得忠心跟随婆婆拿俄米，并借着亲族波阿斯得着保护。她成了大卫王的曾祖母——一位外邦女子被编进以色列的王族家谱。

<a id="samuel"></a>
### Samuel | 撒母耳

**Read | 阅读：** 1 Samuel 1–16 / 撒母耳记上 1–16 章

Born in answer to his mother Hannah’s prayer, Samuel serves as prophet, priest, and the last great judge. He anoints Israel’s first two kings, Saul and David.<br>
撒母耳是母亲哈拿祷告蒙应允所生的孩子，作先知、祭司，也是最后一位重要的士师。他膏立了以色列最初的两位君王扫罗和大卫。

<a id="saul"></a>
### Saul | 扫罗

**Read | 阅读：** 1 Samuel 9–31 / 撒母耳记上 9–31 章

Israel’s first king begins with promise but repeatedly disobeys God and grows jealous of David. His tragic end shows that a king the people wanted is not the same as a king after God’s heart.<br>
以色列第一位君王起初看似大有可为，却一再违背神，并嫉妒大卫。他悲剧性的结局说明：百姓想要的王，不等于合神心意的王。

<a id="david"></a>
### David | 大卫

**Read | 阅读：** 1 Samuel 16 to 1 Kings 2; Psalms 23 and 51 / 撒母耳记上 16 章至列王纪上 2 章；诗篇 23 篇、51 篇

A shepherd boy becomes Israel’s greatest king and a writer of psalms. He also sins gravely and repents. God promises that his royal line will last forever, a promise the New Testament sees fulfilled in Jesus.<br>
牧童大卫成了以色列最重要的君王，也写下许多诗篇。他也犯了大罪，又真心悔改。神应许他的王位要永远延续；新约认为这应许在耶稣身上得以成全。

<a id="solomon"></a>
### Solomon | 所罗门

**Read | 阅读：** 1 Kings 1–11 / 列王纪上 1–11 章

David’s son asks God for wisdom, builds the temple in Jerusalem, and rules in great wealth. In later life his heart turns to other gods, and after his death the kingdom splits in two.<br>
大卫的儿子所罗门向神求智慧，在耶路撒冷建造圣殿，国势富强。他晚年心偏向别神；他死后，王国分裂为二。

<a id="prophets-exile-return"></a>
## Prophets, Exile, and Return | 先知、被掳与归回

<a id="elijah"></a>
### Elijah | 以利亚

**Read | 阅读：** 1 Kings 17–19 and 2 Kings 2 / 列王纪上 17–19 章、列王纪下 2 章

In the northern kingdom, Elijah confronts King Ahab and the worship of Baal. On Mount Carmel he calls Israel to decide whom they will follow, and later God meets him in a quiet voice when he is worn out.<br>
以利亚在北国对抗亚哈王和巴力崇拜。他在迦密山上呼召以色列人决定要跟从谁；后来他精疲力竭时，神以微小的声音与他相遇。

<a id="isaiah"></a>
### Isaiah | 以赛亚

**Read | 阅读：** Isaiah 6, 9, 40, and 53 / 以赛亚书 6、9、40、53 章

Isaiah sees the Lord high and lifted up, then warns Judah of judgment and comforts God’s people with hope. His words about a coming king and a suffering servant are often quoted in the New Testament.<br>
以赛亚看见主坐在高高的宝座上，随后警告犹大将受审判，也用盼望安慰神的子民。他关于将来之王和受苦仆人的话，在新约中常被引用。

<a id="jeremiah"></a>
### Jeremiah | 耶利米

**Read | 阅读：** Jeremiah 1, 29, and 31 / 耶利米书 1、29、31 章

Called while young, Jeremiah warns Jerusalem for decades before it falls to Babylon. He weeps over his people and announces a new covenant in which God will write his law on their hearts.<br>
耶利米年轻时蒙召，几十年间警告耶路撒冷，直到城被巴比伦攻陷。他为百姓哭泣，并宣告一个新约：神要把律法写在他们心上。

<a id="daniel"></a>
### Daniel | 但以理

**Read | 阅读：** Daniel 1–6 / 但以理书 1–6 章

Taken to Babylon as a young man, Daniel serves foreign kings while staying faithful to God, even when it puts his life at risk. His visions in later chapters speak of God’s kingdom outlasting every empire.<br>
但以理年轻时被掳到巴比伦，在外邦君王手下供职，却始终忠于神，甚至冒着生命危险。书中后半部分的异象宣告神的国要比一切帝国长久。

<a id="esther"></a>
### Esther | 以斯帖

**Read | 阅读：** Esther 1–10 / 以斯帖记 1–10 章

A Jewish woman in the Persian court becomes queen. When her people face destruction, she risks her life to plead for them. God is never named in the book, yet the story is full of reversals.<br>
一位犹太女子在波斯宫廷中成为王后。她的民族面临灭绝时，她冒死为同胞求情。全书没有直接提到神的名字，故事却充满出人意料的逆转。

<a id="nehemiah"></a>
### Nehemiah | 尼希米

**Read | 阅读：** Nehemiah 1–8 / 尼希米记 1–8 章

A Jewish official in the Persian court prays over Jerusalem’s broken walls and returns to rebuild them. Together with Ezra, he leads the returned community back to God’s law.<br>
尼希米是波斯宫廷中的犹太官员，他为耶路撒冷倒塌的城墙祷告，回去带领重建。他和以斯拉一同带领归回的群体重新遵行神的律法。

<a id="jesus-and-followers"></a>
## Jesus and His First Followers | 耶稣与最早的跟随者

<a id="mary-mother-of-jesus"></a>
### Mary, the mother of Jesus | 耶稣的母亲马利亚

**Read | 阅读：** Luke 1–2; John 2:1–11 and 19:25–27 / 路加福音 1–2 章；约翰福音 2:1–11、19:25–27

A young woman in Nazareth receives the angel’s message and answers, “I am the Lord’s servant.” She stays near her son from his birth to his death on the cross.<br>
拿撒勒的一位年轻女子领受天使的信息，回答说：“我是主的使女。”从耶稣出生到祂死在十字架上，她都在儿子身边。

<a id="john-the-baptist"></a>
### John the Baptist | 施洗约翰

**Read | 阅读：** Luke 1 and 3; John 1:19–34 / 路加福音 1、3 章；约翰福音 1:19–34

A prophet in the wilderness, John calls people to repent and be baptized. He points beyond himself to Jesus, calling him “the Lamb of God.”<br>
施洗约翰是旷野中的先知，呼召人悔改受洗。他不把人引向自己，而是指向耶稣，称祂为“神的羔羊”。

<a id="jesus"></a>
### Jesus | 耶稣

**Read | 阅读：** Mark 1–16, then Matthew, Luke, and John / 先读马可福音 1–16 章，再读马太、路加、约翰福音

Jesus of Nazareth announces the kingdom of God, teaches, heals, welcomes outsiders, and gathers disciples. The Gospels present his death on the cross and his resurrection as the center of the whole biblical story.<br>
拿撒勒人耶稣宣告神的国，教导人、医治人、接纳被排斥的人，并招聚门徒。四福音把祂在十字架上的死和复活，呈现为整本圣经故事的中心。

<a id="peter"></a>
### Peter | 彼得

**Read | 阅读：** Mark 8:27–33 and 14:66–72; John 21; Acts 2 / 马可福音 8:27–33、14:66–72；约翰福音 21 章；使徒行传 2 章

A Galilean fisherman, Peter confesses Jesus as the Messiah, then denies him three times. Restored by the risen Jesus, he preaches boldly at Pentecost and leads the early church in Jerusalem.<br>
加利利渔夫彼得承认耶稣是弥赛亚，后来却三次不认主。复活的耶稣恢复了他；他在五旬节放胆讲道，带领耶路撒冷的初期教会。

<a id="mary-magdalene"></a>
### Mary Magdalene | 抹大拉的马利亚

**Read | 阅读：** Luke 8:1–3; John 20:1–18 / 路加福音 8:1–3；约翰福音 20:1–18

Healed by Jesus, Mary Magdalene follows him and supports his ministry. She stays at the cross, comes to the tomb, and in John’s Gospel is the first to meet the risen Jesus and tell the disciples.<br>
抹大拉的马利亚蒙耶稣医治后跟随祂，并供给祂的事工。她留在十字架旁，又来到坟墓前；在约翰福音中，她是第一个遇见复活的耶稣、并去告诉门徒的人。

<a id="church-goes-out"></a>
## The Church Goes Out | 教会走向万民

<a id="stephen"></a>
### Stephen | 司提反

**Read | 阅读：** Acts 6–7 / 使徒行传 6–7 章

Chosen to help care for widows in the Jerusalem church, Stephen speaks boldly about Israel’s story and Jesus. He becomes the first Christian martyr, praying for those who stone him.<br>
司提反被选出来协助照顾耶路撒冷教会中的寡妇，他放胆讲述以色列的历史和耶稣，成为第一位殉道的基督徒，临死时还为用石头打他的人祷告。

<a id="paul"></a>
### Paul | 保罗

**Read | 阅读：** Acts 9 and 13–28; Romans 1–8 / 使徒行传 9 章、13–28 章；罗马书 1–8 章

Once a persecutor of the church, Paul meets the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus. He travels the Roman world planting churches among Jews and Gentiles, and his letters make up much of the New Testament.<br>
保罗原本逼迫教会，在往大马士革的路上遇见复活的耶稣。他走遍罗马世界，在犹太人和外邦人中建立教会；他的书信占了新约很大一部分。

<a id="barnabas"></a>
### Barnabas | 巴拿巴

**Read | 阅读：** Acts 4:36–37, 9:26–27, and 11–15 / 使徒行传 4:36–37、9:26–27、11–15 章

His name means “son of encouragement.” Barnabas vouches for Paul when others are afraid of him and travels with him on their first journey.<br>
巴拿巴的意思是“劝慰子”。当别人都怕保罗时，是他为保罗作保，后来又与保罗一起走第一次宣教旅程。

<a id="lydia"></a>
### Lydia | 吕底亚

**Read | 阅读：** Acts 16:11–15 and 40 / 使徒行传 16:11–15、40

A dealer in purple cloth from Thyatira, Lydia hears Paul by a river in Philippi and believes. She opens her home, which becomes a meeting place for the first believers in that city.<br>
吕底亚是推雅推喇城卖紫色布的商人，在腓立比的河边听保罗讲道而信主。她打开自己的家，这家成了当地最早信徒聚会的地方。

<a id="priscilla-and-aquila"></a>
### Priscilla and Aquila | 百基拉和亚居拉

**Read | 阅读：** Acts 18; Romans 16:3–5 / 使徒行传 18 章；罗马书 16:3–5

A married couple who make tents, as Paul does, they work and travel with him. They explain the way of God more accurately to a gifted teacher, and churches meet in their home.<br>
这对夫妇和保罗一样以织帐棚为业，与保罗同工同行。他们把神的道更准确地讲解给一位有恩赐的教师，也在自己家中接待教会聚会。

<a id="timothy"></a>
### Timothy | 提摩太

**Read | 阅读：** Acts 16:1–5; Philippians 2:19–22; 1 Timothy 1:2; 2 Timothy 1 / 使徒行传 16:1–5；腓立比书 2:19–22；提摩太前书 1:2；提摩太后书 1 章

Raised in faith by his mother and grandmother, Timothy becomes Paul’s younger co-worker. Paul sends him to strengthen churches and writes to him as “my true son in the faith.”<br>
提摩太从小受母亲和外祖母的信仰影响，后来成为保罗年轻的同工。保罗差派他去坚固各地教会，并在信中称他为“因信主作我真儿子的”。

<a id="john-of-revelation"></a>
### John, who sees the visions of Revelation | 领受启示录异象的约翰

**Read | 阅读：** Revelation 1, 4–5, and 21–22 / 启示录 1、4–5、21–22 章

On the island of Patmos, a servant of Jesus named John receives visions for seven churches. The book ends with God making all things new and dwelling with his people. Christians have long debated whether this John is the apostle.<br>
在拔摩岛上，一位名叫约翰的耶稣仆人为七个教会领受异象。全书以神使万物更新、与祂的子民同住作结。这位约翰是否就是使徒约翰，历来有不同看法。
