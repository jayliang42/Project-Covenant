<a id="big-story"></a>

## The Big Story In One Line | 一句话总览

The Bible tells how God creates a good world, humanity falls into sin, God forms a covenant people, sends the promised [Messiah](../Glossary/README.md#messiah), and through Jesus begins the restoration that will be completed in the new creation.<br>
圣经讲的是：神创造了美好的世界，人类陷入罪中，神拣选并塑造一个立约的子民，差来[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)中的[弥赛亚](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)，并借着耶稣开启最终将在新天新地完成的恢复工作。

<a id="quick-map"></a>

## Quick Map | 快速地图

If you feel that “the Bible is made of many books but I cannot see how they fit together,” this is the shortest big-picture map:<br>
如果你常觉得“圣经有很多卷书，但我看不出它们怎么连起来”，最短的大图可以这样抓：

1. Genesis: God creates, humanity falls, and God begins to carry his promise through one family.
   创世记：神创造世界，人类堕落，神开始让应许借着一个家族延续下去。
2. [Exodus](../Glossary/README.md#exodus) to Deuteronomy: God rescues [Israel](../Glossary/README.md#israel) and gives covenant law.
   出埃及记到申命记：神拯救[以色列](../Glossary/README.md#israel)，并赐下立约的律法。
3. Joshua to Esther: Israel enters the land, becomes a kingdom, falls, is exiled, and partially returns.
   约书亚记到以斯帖记：以色列进入应许之地、建立王国、逐渐败坏、被掳，后来部分人归回。
4. Job to Song of Songs: [wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom), worship, suffering, love, and life before God.
   约伯记到雅歌：关于[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)、敬拜、苦难、爱情和人在神面前的生活。
5. Isaiah to Malachi: [prophets](../Glossary/README.md#prophet) interpret Israel's history and announce judgment plus future hope.
   以赛亚书到玛拉基书：[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)解读以色列的历史，宣告审判，也宣告将来的盼望。
6. Gospels: Jesus is the promised Messiah and the center of the whole story.
   [四福音](../Glossary/README.md#gospels)：耶稣就是应许中的弥赛亚，也是整本故事的中心。
7. Acts to Jude: the risen [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) forms and sends the church.
   使徒行传到犹大书：[复活](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection)的[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)建立教会，并差遣教会出去。
8. Revelation: God completes judgment, redemption, and new creation.
   启示录：神完成最终的审判、救赎与新创造。

<a id="story-arc"></a>

## Story Arc | 故事弧线

- Creation: the world begins good.
  创造：世界起初是美善的。
- Fall: sin, death, and exile enter.
  堕落：罪、死亡与流离从此进入人类历史。
- Covenant: God binds himself to a people through a promise.
  立约：神与一群子民立约，用应许把自己和他们连在一起。
- Kingdom: Israel receives land, king, [temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple), and law.
  国度：以色列得着土地、君王、圣殿和律法。
- Exile: covenant breaking brings judgment.
  被掳：背约带来审判。
- Promise: prophets speak of return, a new covenant, the gift of the Spirit, and the coming Messiah.
  应许：先知宣告归回、新的约、[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)的浇灌和弥赛亚的来临。
- Fulfillment: Jesus fulfills Israel's story and opens [salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation) to the nations.
  成全：耶稣成全以色列的故事，并把[救恩](../Glossary/README.md#salvation)向万国打开。
- Church: the [gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospel) goes outward by the Spirit.
  教会：福音借着圣灵向外扩展。
- New Creation: God's dwelling with his people is fully restored.
  新创造：神与祂的子民同住，这关系被完全恢复。

## Timeline | 时间线

<a id="stage-01"></a>
### 1. Creation and Fall | 创造与堕落

![The sun rising over sea and land, with birds, a leaping fish, and trees / 太阳从海与陆地上升起，有飞鸟、跃鱼和树木](../assets/art/creation-dawn.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Beginning of history
  大致阶段：历史起初
- Main books: Genesis 1-3
  主要书卷：创世记 1-3
- Key events: Creation, Adam and Eve, fall, curse, promise of future victory
  关键事件：创造、亚当夏娃、堕落、咒诅、对将来得胜的应许

#### Connection | 连接意义

The Bible begins with God as Creator and humanity as image-bearers. Sin enters early, so every later covenant and act of salvation addresses a problem introduced here.<br>
圣经一开始就确立了神是创造主，人是按祂形象被造的。罪很早就进入世界，所以后面所有的立约与拯救，都在回应这里出现的问题。

<a id="stage-02"></a>
### 2. Flood and Noah | 洪水与挪亚

![An ark resting on a hillside under a rainbow as the water recedes / 洪水退去，方舟停在山坡上，天空有彩虹](../assets/art/ark-rainbow.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Early world history; no date is assigned here
  大致阶段：远古历史；本页不给出年代
- Main books: Genesis 6-9
  主要书卷：创世记 6-9
- Key events: Human wickedness increases, flood judgment, Noah preserved, covenant sign of the rainbow
  关键事件：人的邪恶加增、洪水审判、挪亚一家蒙保守、彩虹之约

#### Connection | 连接意义

Judgment and mercy appear together. God judges sin but preserves a line through which his purposes will continue.<br>
审判与怜悯同时出现。神审判罪，却保守了挪亚一家，使祂的计划得以延续。

<a id="stage-03"></a>
### 3. Abraham and the Patriarchs | 亚伯拉罕与列祖

![An old man outside his tent at night, looking up at countless stars / 夜里，一位老人站在帐棚外仰望无数星星](../assets/art/abraham-stars.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Around the early second millennium BC
  大致阶段：约主前第二个千年早期
- Main books: Genesis 12-50
  主要书卷：创世记 12-50
- Key events: Abraham called, covenant [promises](../Glossary/README.md#promise), Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, family moves to Egypt
  关键事件：亚伯拉罕蒙召、立约应许、以撒、雅各、约瑟、全家下埃及

#### Connection | 连接意义

God promises land, offspring, and [blessing](../Glossary/README.md#blessing), including blessing for all nations. This covenant becomes a major backbone of the rest of Scripture.<br>
神应许土地、后裔与赐福，而且这[祝福](../Glossary/README.md#blessing)要临到万国。这个亚伯拉罕之约成为整本圣经后续发展的重要骨架。

<a id="stage-04"></a>
### 4. Exodus and Sinai | 出埃及与西奈

![Travelers walking on dry ground between two walls of water, led by a pillar of light / 一行人在两道水墙之间的干地上行走，前面有光柱引路](../assets/art/sea-crossing.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Often placed around the second millennium BC; exact dating is debated
  大致阶段：通常放在主前第二个千年；确切年代有不同看法
- Main books: Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy
  主要书卷：出埃及记、利未记、民数记、申命记
- Key events: Deliverance from Egypt, [Passover](../Glossary/README.md#passover), Red Sea, law at Sinai, [tabernacle](../Glossary/README.md#tabernacle), wilderness wandering
  关键事件：[出埃及](../Glossary/README.md#exodus)、[逾越节](../Glossary/README.md#passover)、过红海、西奈颁律法、[会幕](../Glossary/README.md#tabernacle)、旷野漂流

#### Connection | 连接意义

Here God forms Israel into a covenant nation. Exodus becomes the central [Old Testament](../Glossary/README.md#old-testament) picture of redemption, later echoed in the gospel.<br>
在这里，神把以色列塑造成一个立约民族。出埃及也成为旧约中最核心的救赎图像，后来在福音中一再被呼应。

<a id="stage-05"></a>
### 5. Conquest and Judges | 进迦南与士师

![People carrying horns walking around a walled city / 一行人拿着角号绕着有城墙的城行走](../assets/art/jericho-walls.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: After the exodus and before the monarchy; the dates depend on how the exodus is dated
  大致阶段：出埃及之后、王国建立之前；具体年代取决于出埃及怎样定年
- Main books: Joshua, Judges, Ruth
  主要书卷：约书亚记、士师记、路得记
- Key events: Entering the land, partial conquest, repeated cycles of sin, oppression, crying out, and deliverance
  关键事件：进入应许之地、未完全征服、反复出现犯罪-受压-呼求-拯救的循环

#### Connection | 连接意义

This era shows that external deliverance is not enough. God's people need inward renewal and faithful leadership.<br>
这个时期显明，外在得拯救还不够；神的子民需要内在更新，也需要忠心的带领者。

<a id="stage-06"></a>
### 6. United Kingdom | 统一王国

![A lyre, a sling, five stones, and a small crown on a rock above a pasture / 草场上方岩石上的竖琴、机弦、五块石子和小王冠](../assets/art/david-harp.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Around 1050-930 BC
  大致阶段：约主前 1050-930 年
- Main books: 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings 1-11, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles 1-9, many [Psalms](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)
  主要书卷：撒母耳记上、撒母耳记下、列王纪上 1-11、历代志上、历代志下 1-9、许多[诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)
- Key figures: Saul, David, Solomon
  关键人物：扫罗、大卫、所罗门

#### Connection | 连接意义

David is especially important because God promises a lasting royal line. This becomes the foundation for messianic hope fulfilled in Jesus.<br>
大卫特别关键，因为神应许他的王位后裔要延续下去。这成为后来弥赛亚盼望的重要基础，并最终在耶稣里实现。

<a id="stage-07"></a>
### 7. Divided Kingdom | 分裂王国

![Fire falling on an altar of twelve stones on a mountaintop / 火从天降在山顶十二块石头筑成的坛上](../assets/art/carmel-altar.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Around 930-722 BC, from the split to the fall of the northern kingdom; Judah continues until 586 BC
  大致阶段：约主前 930-722 年，从王国分裂到北国灭亡；南国犹大延续到主前 586 年
- Main books: 1 Kings 12-22, 2 Kings 1-17, 2 Chronicles 10-28, prophetic books
  主要书卷：列王纪上 12-22、列王纪下 1-17、历代志下 10-28、先知书
- Key events: Kingdom splits into Israel and [Judah](../Glossary/README.md#judah), idolatry increases, prophets call for [repentance](../Glossary/README.md#repentance)
  关键事件：王国分裂为以色列与犹大、偶像崇拜加深、先知不断呼召[悔改](../Glossary/README.md#repentance)

#### Connection | 连接意义

The prophets interpret history theologically. Political collapse is not random; it is tied to covenant unfaithfulness.<br>
先知从神学角度解释历史。政治崩塌不是偶然事件，而是与背约直接相关。

<a id="stage-08"></a>
### 8. Exile | 被掳

![A long line of people carrying bundles along a dusty road away from home / 一长列背着包袱的人沿着尘土飞扬的路离开家乡](../assets/art/exile-road.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: 722 BC for Israel; 586 BC for Judah and Jerusalem
  大致阶段：北国以色列于主前 722 年；南国[犹大](../Glossary/README.md#judah)和耶路撒冷于主前 586 年
- Main books: 2 Kings, 2 Chronicles, Ezekiel, Daniel, parts of Jeremiah and Lamentations
  主要书卷：列王纪、历代志、以西结书、但以理书、耶利米书部分内容、耶利米哀歌
- Key events: Assyrian conquest of Israel, Babylonian destruction of Jerusalem, exile
  关键事件：亚述灭北国、巴比伦毁耶路撒冷、百姓被掳

#### Connection | 连接意义

Exile is one of the great turning points of the Bible. It looks like covenant failure and national death, yet the prophets also speak of return, new covenant, new heart, and future restoration.<br>
被掳是圣经中的重大转折点之一。它看起来像立约失败和国家性的死亡，但先知同时也宣告归回、新约、新心和将来的恢复。

<a id="stage-09"></a>
### 9. Return and Second Temple Period | 归回与第二圣殿时期

![Workers rebuilding a city wall and gate with scaffolding and baskets / 工人借着木架和筐子重建城墙和城门](../assets/art/rebuilding.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: From 538 BC onward
  大致阶段：从主前 538 年开始
- Main books: Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi
  主要书卷：以斯拉记、尼希米记、以斯帖记、哈该书、撒迦利亚书、玛拉基书
- Key events: [Return from exile](../Glossary/README.md#return), temple rebuilt, walls rebuilt, ongoing weakness and expectation
  关键事件：从被掳中归回、[圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)重建、城墙重建、百姓仍在软弱中等待

#### Connection | 连接意义

The people return physically, but the larger promises still await fulfillment. This creates expectation for a greater salvation still to come.<br>
百姓虽然身体上回到了土地，但更大的应许仍未完全实现，因此整段历史都带着对更大拯救的等待。

Between the late Persian period and the Gospels, Judea passed through Hellenistic kingdoms, the Maccabean revolt, the Hasmonean dynasty, Roman takeover, and Herodian rule. See [Between the Testaments to the Gospels: Second Temple, Hellenistic, and Roman World](./两约之间至四福音_第二圣殿与罗马背景.md).<br>
从波斯末期到四福音之间，犹太地先后经历希腊化王国、马加比反抗、哈斯摩尼王朝、罗马接管和希律统治；详见[《两约之间至四福音：第二圣殿、希腊化与罗马世界》](./两约之间至四福音_第二圣殿与罗马背景.md)。

<a id="stage-10"></a>
### 10. Jesus the Messiah | 弥赛亚耶稣

![A large round stone rolled away from an open tomb at sunrise / 日出时，大圆石已从敞开的坟墓门口滚开](../assets/art/empty-tomb.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: Early first century AD
  大致阶段：主后一世纪初
- Main books: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John
  主要书卷：马太福音、马可福音、路加福音、约翰福音
- Key events: [Incarnation](../Glossary/README.md#incarnation), kingdom proclamation, miracles, cross, [resurrection](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection), ascension
  关键事件：[道成肉身](../Glossary/README.md#incarnation)、宣讲[天国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)、神迹、十字架、复活、升天

#### Connection | 连接意义

Jesus is the center of the Bible's storyline. He fulfills the law, the prophets, the sacrificial system, the Davidic kingship, and the hope of redemption for Israel and the nations.<br>
耶稣是整本圣经故事线的中心。祂成全律法、先知、[献祭](../Glossary/README.md#sacrifice)体系、大卫之王权，以及以色列和万国得救赎的盼望。

For the earliest resurrection tradition, the [manuscript](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript) endings of Mark, burial archaeology, and the limits of Roman evidence, see [Resurrection And Early Witnesses: Textual History And Historical Boundaries](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md).<br>
林前 15 的早期复活传统、马可结尾[抄本](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript)、墓葬考古和罗马材料的证据边界，见[《复活与早期见证：文本史、墓葬背景与历史研究边界》](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md)。

<a id="stage-11"></a>
### 11. Church and Mission | 教会与使命

![A diverse group gathered in a room, with small flames above them and wind sweeping through / 不同背景的人聚在屋里，头上有火焰，有风吹过](../assets/art/pentecost.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: From Acts through the New Testament letters
  大致阶段：从使徒行传到新约书信时期
- Main books: Acts, [New Testament](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament) letters
  主要书卷：使徒行传、新约书信
- Key events: [Pentecost](../Glossary/README.md#pentecost), spread of the gospel, formation of churches, mission to Jews and [Gentiles](../Glossary/README.md#gentile)
  关键事件：[五旬节](../Glossary/README.md#pentecost)、福音广传、教会建立、向犹太人与[外邦人](../Glossary/README.md#gentile)宣教

#### Connection | 连接意义

The church is not a side note. It is the community formed by the risen Christ and empowered by the Spirit to witness to the nations.<br>
教会不是附属章节，而是复活的基督借着圣灵所建立的群体，承担向万民作见证的使命。

### 12. New Creation | 新创造

![A bright river flowing from a city gate between fruit trees / 明亮的河从城门流出，两岸有果树](../assets/art/river-of-life.svg)

*Original illustration for this library; not historical evidence. / 本资料库原创插画，不作为历史证据。* [About the illustrations | 插画说明](../CREDITS.md#original-illustrations)

- Approximate setting: The promised future that completes the story
  大致阶段：完成整个故事的应许中的将来
- Main books: Revelation 21-22, with roots throughout Scripture
  主要书卷：启示录 21-22，同时整本圣经早有铺垫
- Key events: Final judgment, evil removed, new heavens and new earth, God dwelling with his people
  关键事件：最终审判、邪恶被除去、新天新地、神与祂子民永远同住

#### Connection | 连接意义

The Bible ends not with escape from creation but with the renewal of creation. The story moves from Eden to new creation, from lost fellowship to perfected fellowship with God.<br>
圣经的结局不是“逃离受造界”，而是“受造界被更新”。整个故事从伊甸园走向新创造，从失落的相交走向与神完全的同住。

## Where The Books Fit | 各卷书大致放在哪里

- Genesis: creation, fall, flood, Babel, patriarchs.
  创世记：创造、堕落、洪水、巴别、列祖。
- Exodus to Deuteronomy: Moses, exodus, wilderness, covenant.
  出埃及记到申命记：摩西、出埃及、旷野、立约。
- Joshua: conquest and land.
  约书亚记：征服与得地。
- Judges and Ruth: unstable era before kingship.
  士师记、路得记：王国建立前不稳定的时期。
- 1-2 Samuel: Samuel, Saul, David.
  撒母耳记上、下：撒母耳、扫罗、大卫。
- 1 Kings 1-11 and 1 Chronicles; parts of Psalms and Proverbs: Solomon and united kingdom.
  列王纪上 1-11、历代志上，以及部分诗篇、箴言：所罗门与联合王国。
- 1 Kings 12 to 2 Kings 25; 2 Chronicles: divided kingdom to exile.
  列王纪上 12 到列王纪下 25、历代志下：分裂王国直到被掳。
- Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther: return and post-exilic period.
  以斯拉记、尼希米记、以斯帖记：归回与被掳后时期。
- Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs: wisdom and worship literature placed across different periods.
  约伯记、诗篇、箴言、传道书、雅歌：跨不同时期的智慧与敬拜文学。
- Major and Minor Prophets: mostly speak during the divided kingdom, exile, and return.
  大小先知书：大多在分裂王国、被掳和归回时期发声。
- Gospels: life, death, and resurrection of Jesus.
  四福音：耶稣的生、死与复活。
- Acts: spread of the gospel after Jesus' ascension.
  使徒行传：耶稣升天后福音扩展。
- Letters: theological instruction to churches and leaders in the apostolic age.
  书信：使徒时代对教会和领袖的教导。
- Revelation: apocalyptic vision of final judgment and renewal.
  启示录：关于最终审判与更新的启示性异象。

## Prophet Placement | 先知书大致位置

- Before exile, mostly to the northern kingdom: Jonah, Amos, Hosea.
  被掳前，主要对北国说话：约拿书、阿摩司书、何西阿书。
- Before exile, mostly to Judah: Isaiah, Micah, Zephaniah, Habakkuk, Joel, Jeremiah.
  被掳前，主要对南国说话：以赛亚书、弥迦书、西番雅书、哈巴谷书、约珥书、耶利米书。
- During exile: Ezekiel, Daniel, parts of Jeremiah, Obadiah.
  被掳期间：以西结书、但以理书、耶利米书部分内容、俄巴底亚书。
- After return: Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi.
  归回之后：哈该书、撒迦利亚书、玛拉基书。

The dates of Joel and Obadiah are debated, so their placement here is only a common reading.<br>
约珥书和俄巴底亚书的年代有争议，这里的位置只是常见的一种排法。

## Main People Thread | 主要人物主线

1. Adam and Eve: humanity's beginning and fall.
   亚当和夏娃：人类起头与堕落。
2. Noah: preserved through judgment.
   挪亚：在审判中蒙保守。
3. Abraham: God's promise is carried through one family.
   亚伯拉罕：神的应许聚焦到一个家族身上。
4. Isaac and Jacob: promise continues through the family line.
   以撒和雅各：应许沿着家族血脉继续。
5. Joseph: the family is preserved and moved into Egypt.
   约瑟：这个家被保全，并迁入埃及。
6. Moses: deliverer, covenant mediator, lawgiver.
   摩西：拯救者、立约中保、律法颁布者。
7. Joshua: entry into the land.
   约书亚：带领百姓进地。
8. Judges, Ruth, Samuel: transition from tribal instability toward kingship.
   士师、路得、撒母耳：从部落混乱走向王国建立的过渡人物。
9. Saul, David, Solomon: rise of monarchy.
   扫罗、大卫、所罗门：君王制度兴起。
10. Elijah and Elisha; later prophets: covenant confrontation and warning.
    以利亚、以利沙，以及后来的先知：对约的提醒、责备与警告。
11. Ezra and Nehemiah: post-exilic rebuilding.
    以斯拉、尼希米：被掳后重建。
12. John the Baptist: forerunner of the Messiah.
    施洗约翰：弥赛亚的先锋。
13. Jesus: the center and fulfillment.
    耶稣：中心与成全者。
14. Peter, Paul, and the [apostles](../Glossary/README.md#apostle): gospel witness to the church and the nations.
    彼得、保罗和众使徒：把福音见证带给教会与万国。

## How The Bible Connects | 圣经是怎样连起来的

- Creation explains human dignity and the goodness of the world.
  创造解释了人的尊严，以及世界原初的美善。
- The fall explains sin, death, and the need for redemption.
  堕落解释了罪、死亡和救赎的必要。
- Covenants provide the structural backbone of the storyline.
  各个“约”构成整条圣经故事线的重要骨架。
- Israel's story prepares for the Messiah.
  以色列的历史是在为弥赛亚作预备。
- Jesus fulfills the whole Bible and holds it together around himself.
  耶稣使整本圣经连成一个中心明确的整体。
- The church lives between Christ's first coming and his return.
  教会活在基督第一次来与再来之间。
- New creation completes what creation began and sin damaged.
  新创造完成了创造起初所展开、却因罪而受损的计划。

## Promise To Fulfillment | 从应许到成全

- Genesis 3 introduces the hope that evil will not win forever.
  创世记 3 章已经埋下“邪恶不会永远得胜”的盼望。
- Abraham receives the promise of offspring, land, and blessing to the nations.
  亚伯拉罕得着后裔、土地和万国得福的应许。
- Passover and exodus become the Bible's pattern for redemption.
  逾越节与出埃及成为圣经中救赎的模式。
- David receives the promise of an enduring throne.
  大卫得着王位延续的应许。
- The prophets speak of a coming king, servant, shepherd, new covenant, and Spirit.
  先知不断宣告将来的王、仆人、牧者、新约和圣灵。
- Jesus fulfills these threads in a deeper and larger way than many expected.
  耶稣以比多数人预期更深、更大的方式成全这些线索。
- The church lives in an “already, not yet” tension: Christ has come, but the story is not yet complete.
  教会活在“已然未然”的张力里：基督已经来了，但故事尚未完全结束。

## Common Confusions | 常见容易混乱的地方

- `1-2 Chronicles` and `1-2 Kings` overlap in period, but they write history from different angles.
  `历代志上下` 和 `列王纪上下` 在年代上有大量重叠，只是书写角度不同。
- `Prophets` are not all placed after the historical books because they happened later; many belong inside the history of Kings and Chronicles.
  `先知书` 不是因为“发生在历史书之后”才排在后面；很多先知其实就是活在列王纪、历代志所写的历史里面。
- `Psalms` were written across different periods, so they are not from one single moment.
  `诗篇` 横跨不同时代，不是同一时间写成的一本“单时段作品”。
- `The Gospels` overlap in time because they tell the same Jesus story from different emphases.
  `四福音` 在时间上大幅重叠，因为它们是从不同重点讲同一位耶稣。
- `Acts` comes after the Gospels, not because Jesus is gone from the story, but because the risen Christ continues his work through the Spirit and the church.
  `使徒行传` 接在福音书后面，不是因为耶稣已经退出故事，而是因为复活的基督继续借着圣灵和教会工作。

## Main Covenant Thread | 主要“约”的主线

1. Creation covenant themes: life, blessing, rule, presence.
   创造之约主题：生命、祝福、治理、同在。
2. Noahic covenant: preservation of the world.
   挪亚之约：世界被保守。
3. Abrahamic covenant: people, land, blessing to the nations.
   亚伯拉罕之约：子民、土地、万国得福。
4. Mosaic covenant: Israel's life under God's law.
   摩西之约：以色列在神律法下的生活。
5. Davidic covenant: the promised king.
   大卫之约：应许中的王。
6. New covenant: [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness), new heart, Spirit, final restoration in Christ.
   新约：赦罪、新心、圣灵，以及在基督里展开的最终恢复。

## Notes | 注释

- Some dates in Old Testament chronology are approximate and debated.
  旧约部分有些年代是大致性的，学界也存在不同看法。
- The timeline is designed for big-picture understanding, not fine-grained historical argument.
  这份时间线主要服务于“整体理解”，不是细密历史争论用表。
- To study deeper, read the Bible both chronologically and canonically. Each approach helps in a different way.
  若要进一步学习，建议同时用“按时间顺序”和“按正典顺序”两种方式读经，两者会带来不同帮助。
