<a id="where"></a>
## Where the Prayer Comes From | 这篇祷文从哪里来

The prayer appears twice. In Matthew 6:9–13, Jesus teaches it in the Sermon on the Mount, right after warning against praying to be seen or heaping up empty words. In Luke 11:1–4, a disciple asks, “Lord, teach us to pray,” and Jesus gives a shorter form. The two versions say the same things in slightly different words, which suggests Jesus taught it as a pattern as well as a set of words.<br>
这篇祷文出现了两次。在马太福音 6:9–13，耶稣在登山宝训中教导这篇祷文，就在祂提醒人不要为了让人看见而祷告、也不要重复空话之后。在路加福音 11:1–4，一个门徒说：“求主教导我们祷告”，耶稣就给了一个较短的版本。两个版本的意思相同，用词略有不同，这说明耶稣既把它当作可以照着念的祷词，也把它当作祷告的样式。

The prayer has two halves. The first three requests are about God: his name, his [kingdom](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom), and his will. The last three are about our needs: bread, [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness), and protection.<br>
这篇祷文分为两半。前三个祈求是关于神的：祂的名、祂的[国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)、祂的旨意。后三个祈求是关于我们的需要：饮食、[赦免](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness)和保护。

<a id="lines"></a>
## Line by Line | 逐句解读

1. **“Our Father in heaven” | “我们在天上的父”** — Jesus invites his followers to speak to God as a child speaks to a loving father, and to say “our,” praying as one family. “In heaven” reminds us that this Father is also the great and holy God.
   耶稣邀请跟随祂的人像孩子对慈爱的父亲说话那样向神说话，并且说“我们的”，作为一家人一同祷告。“在天上”提醒我们，这位父也是伟大而圣洁的神。
2. **“Hallowed be your name” | “愿人都尊你的名为圣”** — “Hallowed” means treated as holy. This asks that God be honored for who he truly is, in our own lives and in the whole world (compare Ezekiel 36:23).
   “尊为圣”的意思是当作圣洁来尊敬。这一句是求神按着祂真实的样子受尊崇，在我们自己的生命中，也在全世界（参以西结书 36:23）。
3. **“Your kingdom come” | “愿你的国降临”** — The kingdom of God is God’s good rule setting the world right. Jesus announced that it had come near (Mark 1:15); this line asks for it to come fully.
   神的国是神美好的掌权，要使世界归正。耶稣宣告神的国近了（马可福音 1:15）；这一句是求神的国完全来到。
4. **“Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven” | “愿你的旨意行在地上，如同行在天上”** — This asks that God’s will be done here as fully as it is in heaven, and it includes being willing to do it ourselves. Jesus prayed in the same spirit in Gethsemane: “not my will, but yours” (Luke 22:42).
   这一句是求神的旨意在地上完全实现，像在天上一样，其中也包括我们自己愿意遵行。耶稣在客西马尼园也是这样祷告：“不要成就我的意思，只要成就你的意思”（路加福音 22:42）。
5. **“Give us today our daily bread” | “我们日用的饮食，今日赐给我们”** — This asks for what we need for today, not for riches. It recalls the manna God gave Israel one day at a time in the wilderness (Exodus 16). The Greek word translated “daily” is rare, and some scholars think it may mean “bread for the coming day.”
   这一句是求今天所需要的，而不是求财富。它让人想起神在旷野中每天赐给以色列人的吗哪（出埃及记 16 章）。译作“日用”的希腊文词很少见，有些学者认为它的意思可能是“明天的饮食”。
6. **“Forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors” | “免我们的债，如同我们免了人的债”** — Sin is pictured as a debt we cannot repay. Luke says “forgive us our sins” (Luke 11:4). Right after the prayer, Jesus stresses that people who have been forgiven must also forgive others (Matthew 6:14–15).
   这里把罪比作我们还不起的债。路加福音写作“赦免我们的罪”（路加福音 11:4）。紧接着这篇祷文，耶稣强调蒙了赦免的人也要饶恕别人（马太福音 6:14–15）。
7. **“And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one” | “不叫我们遇见试探，救我们脱离凶恶”** — This asks God to keep us from being overcome when we are tested, and to rescue us from evil. Many translations read “the evil one,” meaning the devil; others read simply “evil.” James 1:13 makes clear that God does not tempt anyone to sin.
   这一句是求神在我们受试探时保守我们不被胜过，并救我们脱离凶恶。许多译本译作“那恶者”，指魔鬼；也有译本只译作“恶”。雅各书 1:13 清楚说明，神不试探人去犯罪。

<a id="ending"></a>
## The Ending | 结尾的颂赞

Many churches end the prayer with “For yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever. [Amen](../Glossary/README.md#amen).” This ending is not in the oldest manuscripts of Matthew, so many modern Bibles print it in a footnote. It appears in an early Christian handbook called the Didache, and it echoes David’s praise in 1 Chronicles 29:11. The Chinese Union Version includes it in the text.<br>
许多教会在这篇祷文的结尾说：“因为国度、权柄、荣耀，全是你的，直到永远。[阿们](../Glossary/README.md#amen)！”这段结尾不见于马太福音最古老的抄本，所以许多现代译本把它放在脚注中。它出现在一本早期基督徒的手册《十二使徒遗训》里，也呼应大卫在历代志上 29:11 的颂赞。和合本把它放在正文中。

<a id="praying"></a>
## Praying It Today | 今天怎样用它祷告

1. **Pray it slowly.** Say one line, pause, and think about what you are asking.
   **慢慢地念。**念一句，停一停，想一想自己在求什么。
2. **Use it as an outline.** After each line, add your own words: what in your life needs God’s rule, what you need today, whom you need to forgive.
   **把它当作提纲。**每一句之后，加上自己的话：生活中哪些地方需要神的掌权，今天需要什么，需要饶恕谁。
3. **Pray it with others.** The prayer says “our” and “us” throughout; it was given to a community.
   **和别人一起祷告。**这篇祷文从头到尾都说“我们”；它是赐给一个群体的。
