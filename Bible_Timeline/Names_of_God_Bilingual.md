<a id="the-name"></a>
## The Name Revealed to Moses | 向摩西启示的名

At the burning bush, Moses asks God for his name. God answers, “I AM WHO I AM,” and then gives the personal name written with four Hebrew letters, YHWH (Exodus 3:13–15). Out of reverence, Jewish readers came to say Adonai, “Lord,” instead of pronouncing it. Many English Bibles follow this by printing the name as “LORD” in small capitals. Scholars commonly reconstruct the pronunciation as “Yahweh”; the older form “Jehovah” came from combining the four letters with the vowels of Adonai, and the Chinese 耶和华 follows that older form.<br>
在燃烧的荆棘那里，摩西求问神的名字。神回答说“我是自有永有的”，接着赐下用四个希伯来字母写成的名字 YHWH（出埃及记 3:13–15）。出于敬畏，犹太读者渐渐改读 Adonai（“主”），而不直接念出这个名字。许多英文圣经照这个传统，把这个名字印成小号大写的“LORD”。学者通常把它的读音重建为“雅威”（Yahweh）；较早的读法“Jehovah”是把这四个字母与 Adonai 的元音结合而来，中文的“耶和华”就沿用了这种较早的读法。

Chinese Bibles also differ in how they translate the general word for God: some editions print 神 and others 上帝. Both refer to the same God; see the [Bible Reading Toolkit](./Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#faq-shen) for more.<br>
中文圣经翻译“神”这个普通名词时也有不同：有的版本印作“神”，有的印作“上帝”。两者指的是同一位神；详见[读经工具箱](./Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#faq-shen)。

<a id="god"></a>
## Names and Titles of God | 神的名字与称号

1. **God — Elohim | 神（Elohim）** — Genesis 1:1. The usual Hebrew word for God, used from the first verse of the Bible; it is grammatically plural but used with singular verbs for the one God of Israel.
   创世记 1:1。希伯来文中对神最常用的称呼，从圣经第一节就出现；它在文法上是复数形式，用来指以色列独一的神时却配单数动词。
2. **The LORD — YHWH | 耶和华（YHWH）** — Exodus 3:13–15. God’s personal name, connected with “I AM,” used thousands of times in the Old Testament.
   出埃及记 3:13–15。神自己的名字，与“我是”相连，在旧约中出现数千次。
3. **Lord — Adonai | 主（Adonai）** — Psalm 8:1. A title meaning “master” or “lord,” often joined with the personal name.
   诗篇 8:1。意思是“主人”或“主”的称号，常与神的名字连用。
4. **God Almighty — El Shaddai | 全能的神** — Genesis 17:1. The name God uses when he renews his promise to Abraham.
   创世记 17:1。神向亚伯拉罕重申应许时所用的名号。
5. **God Most High — El Elyon | 至高的神** — Genesis 14:18–20. The priest-king Melchizedek blesses Abram in the name of God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth.
   创世记 14:18–20。祭司王麦基洗德奉天地的主、至高神的名为亚伯兰祝福。
6. **The LORD will provide | 耶和华以勒** — Genesis 22:14. Abraham names the mountain after God provides a ram in place of Isaac.
   创世记 22:14。神预备了一只公羊代替以撒，亚伯拉罕就给那地方起了这个名字。
7. **The LORD of hosts | 万军之耶和华** — Isaiah 6:3. “Hosts” means armies, heavenly and earthly; the title presents God as the King over every power.
   以赛亚书 6:3。“万军”指天上和地上的军队；这个称号表明神是掌管一切权势的君王。
8. **Shepherd | 牧者** — Psalm 23:1. God leads, feeds, and protects his people as a shepherd cares for sheep.
   诗篇 23:1。神引导、喂养、保护祂的子民，如同牧人照顾羊群。
9. **Rock | 磐石** — Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalm 18:2. A picture of God as steady, strong, and safe to shelter in.
   申命记 32:4；诗篇 18:2。这个图像表明神稳固、有力，是可以投靠的避难所。
10. **Father | 父** — Isaiah 63:16; Matthew 6:9. The Old Testament occasionally calls God Father; Jesus teaches his followers to pray “Our Father.”
    以赛亚书 63:16；马太福音 6:9。旧约偶尔称神为父；耶稣教导门徒祷告说“我们在天上的父”。
11. **Abba | 阿爸** — Mark 14:36; Romans 8:15. An Aramaic word for “father” that Jesus used in prayer, and that Paul says the Spirit helps believers cry out.
    马可福音 14:36；罗马书 8:15。亚兰文的“父亲”，耶稣祷告时使用；保罗说圣灵使信徒也能呼叫“阿爸，父”。

<a id="jesus"></a>
## Names and Titles of Jesus | 耶稣的名字与称号

12. **Jesus | 耶稣** — Matthew 1:21. The Greek form of the Hebrew name Joshua, meaning “the LORD saves.”
    马太福音 1:21。希伯来文名字“约书亚”的希腊文形式，意思是“耶和华拯救”。
13. **Christ, or Messiah | 基督（弥赛亚）** — Mark 8:29. Both mean “anointed one,” the promised king. See [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ).
    马可福音 8:29。两个词都是“受膏者”的意思，指应许中的君王。见[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)。
14. **Immanuel | 以马内利** — Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23. “God with us.”
    以赛亚书 7:14；马太福音 1:23。意思是“神与我们同在”。
15. **Son of Man | 人子** — Daniel 7:13–14; Mark 2:10. Jesus’ favorite way of speaking about himself, echoing Daniel’s vision of one given everlasting authority.
    但以理书 7:13–14；马可福音 2:10。耶稣最常用来称呼自己的方式，呼应但以理异象中那位得着永远权柄的人子。
16. **Lamb of God | 神的羔羊** — John 1:29. John the Baptist’s title for Jesus, the one who takes away the sin of the world.
    约翰福音 1:29。施洗约翰对耶稣的称呼：除去世人罪孽的那一位。
17. **“I am” sayings | “我是”的宣告** — John 6:35; 8:12; 10:11; 11:25; 14:6. Jesus calls himself the bread of life, the light of the world, the good shepherd, the resurrection and the life, and the way, the truth, and the life.
    约翰福音 6:35；8:12；10:11；11:25；14:6。耶稣称自己是生命的粮、世界的光、好牧人、复活和生命，以及道路、真理、生命。
18. **Lord | 主** — Romans 10:9; Philippians 2:11. The earliest Christians confessed “Jesus is Lord,” using a title that Greek translations of the Old Testament had used for God.
    罗马书 10:9；腓立比书 2:11。最早的基督徒宣认“耶稣是主”；这个称号在旧约的希腊文译本中用来称呼神。

<a id="spirit"></a>
## The Holy Spirit | 圣灵

19. **Advocate, or Helper | 保惠师** — John 14:16, 26. Jesus promises “another” who will be with his followers, teach them, and remind them of what he said.
    约翰福音 14:16、26。耶稣应许“另外一位”要与门徒同在，教导他们，并叫他们想起祂所说的话。
20. **Spirit of truth | 真理的圣灵** — John 16:13. The Spirit guides Jesus’ followers into all truth.
    约翰福音 16:13。圣灵引导耶稣的跟随者进入一切的真理。
