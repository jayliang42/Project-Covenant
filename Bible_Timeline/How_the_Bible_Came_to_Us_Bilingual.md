<a id="written"></a>
## Written over Many Centuries | 在许多世纪中写成

The Old Testament was written mostly in Hebrew, with a few chapters in Aramaic, such as parts of Daniel and Ezra. The New Testament was written in Greek, the common language of the eastern Roman world, mostly in the first century AD. Many authors, poets, prophets, and editors were involved, and scholars debate when and how several books reached their final form. The [Bible Timeline](./Bible_Timeline_Bilingual.md) shows where each part fits in the story.<br>
旧约主要用希伯来文写成，少数章节用亚兰文，例如但以理书和以斯拉记的部分内容。新约用希腊文写成，那是罗马帝国东部通用的语言，大多写于主后一世纪。参与其中的有许多作者、诗人、先知和编辑者；学者对几卷书在何时、怎样成为最终形式仍有讨论。各部分在故事中的位置，见[圣经时间线](./Bible_Timeline_Bilingual.md)。

<a id="copied"></a>
## Copied by Hand | 靠手抄流传

Before printing, every Bible was copied by hand. No original manuscript of any biblical book survives; what we have are copies. That is normal for ancient writings, and the number and age of the biblical copies are unusually large.<br>
在印刷术出现以前，每一本圣经都是手抄的。圣经任何一卷书的原稿都没有留存下来，我们所有的都是抄本。古代文献大多如此；而圣经抄本的数量与年代之早，都相当突出。

- **The Dead Sea Scrolls**, found near Qumran from 1947 onward, include copies or fragments of almost every Old Testament book, many from before the time of Jesus.
  **死海古卷**自 1947 年起在昆兰附近陆续发现，包含几乎每一卷旧约书卷的抄本或残片，其中许多早于耶稣的时代。
- **The Masoretic Text** is the carefully preserved Hebrew text copied by Jewish scribes called the Masoretes, who added vowel signs and notes. The Leningrad Codex, from about AD 1008, is the oldest complete Hebrew Bible.
  **马所拉文本**是由称为“马所拉学者”的犹太抄写者精心保存的希伯来文本，他们加上了元音符号和注记。约主后 1008 年的列宁格勒抄本，是现存最古老的完整希伯来文圣经。
- **The Septuagint** is the ancient Greek translation of the Jewish scriptures, begun in the third century BC; the New Testament often quotes it. See [Septuagint](../Glossary/README.md#septuagint).
  **七十士译本**是犹太经典的古希腊文译本，始于主前三世纪；新约常常引用它。见[七十士译本](../Glossary/README.md#septuagint)。
- **New Testament manuscripts** survive in thousands of Greek copies, from small second- and third-century papyrus fragments to large fourth-century volumes such as Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Vaticanus.
  **新约抄本**留存下来的希腊文抄本有数千份，从二、三世纪的小块蒲草纸残片，到四世纪的大型抄本，如西奈抄本和梵蒂冈抄本。

Copies differ in small ways, as all hand copies do. Scholars compare them carefully to recover the earliest text, and modern Bibles note the places where an important difference remains, such as the ending of Mark. See [Manuscript](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript) and [Resurrection and Early Witnesses](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md#resurrection-mark-s-ending-honest-manuscript-reading-and-a-firmer-basis-for-faith).<br>
和一切手抄本一样，各抄本之间有些细小差异。学者仔细比较这些抄本，以求恢复最早的文本；现代圣经会在仍有重要差异的地方加注，例如马可福音的结尾。见[抄本](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript)和[《复活与早期见证》](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md#resurrection-mark-s-ending-honest-manuscript-reading-and-a-firmer-basis-for-faith)。

<a id="canon-section"></a>
## Which Books Belong | 哪些书属于圣经

The canon is the list of books a community receives as Scripture. The Jewish Bible counts its books as twenty-four; Protestant Bibles divide the same books into thirty-nine. Catholic and Orthodox Bibles also include further books, such as Tobit and 1 Maccabees, and Orthodox churches include some that Catholics do not. For the New Testament, the same twenty-seven books were widely recognized by the late fourth century; a letter of Athanasius in AD 367 lists exactly these twenty-seven.<br>
正典是一个群体接纳为圣经的书卷名单。犹太人的圣经把书卷算作二十四卷；新教圣经把同样的内容分成三十九卷。天主教和东正教的圣经还包括另外一些书，例如多俾亚传和马加比一书，而东正教又包括一些天主教没有收入的书。新约方面，同样的二十七卷书在四世纪后期已得到广泛承认；亚他那修在主后 367 年的一封书信，列出的正是这二十七卷。

For the ancient Old Testament book lists and how 22, 24, and 39 relate, see [Ancient Old Testament Book Lists](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md).<br>
关于古代的旧约书目，以及 22 卷、24 卷与 39 卷之间的关系，见[《古代旧约书目》](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md)。

<a id="chapters-verses"></a>
## Chapters and Verses | 章与节

The biblical authors did not write in numbered chapters and verses. The chapter divisions used today were added in the early thirteenth century, and verse numbers for the whole Bible were printed in the mid-sixteenth century. They make passages easy to find, but they sometimes break a thought in the middle, so it helps to read across chapter breaks. The [Bible Reading Toolkit](./Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#reference) explains how to read a reference.<br>
圣经作者写作时并没有编号的章和节。今天使用的分章是在十三世纪初加上的，整本圣经的节数则在十六世纪中叶印行。章节使经文容易查找，但有时会把一个意思从中间切开，所以读经时不妨跨过章的分界继续读。怎样看懂经文出处，见[读经工具箱](./Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#reference)。

<a id="translated"></a>
## Translated into Many Languages | 翻译成许多语言

Translation began early. Besides the Greek Septuagint, there were ancient translations into Aramaic, Syriac, Latin, and other languages; Jerome’s Latin translation, later called the Vulgate, was made in the late fourth century. In English, the Bible was translated in the fourteenth century under John Wycliffe, by William Tyndale in the sixteenth century, and in the King James Version of 1611, followed by many modern translations.<br>
翻译工作很早就开始了。除了希腊文的七十士译本，古代还有亚兰文、叙利亚文、拉丁文等译本；耶柔米的拉丁文译本（后来称为“武加大译本”）完成于四世纪后期。英文方面，十四世纪有威克里夫主持的译本，十六世纪有丁道尔的译本，1611 年有英王钦定本，此后又有许多现代译本。

In Chinese, Robert Morrison and William Milne completed a full Bible translation published in 1823. The Chinese Union Version (和合本), published in 1919 after decades of work by a committee, became the most widely used Chinese Bible for the next century, and many newer Chinese translations have followed. See [Bible Translations](../Bible_Translations/README.md) for the translations this library uses.<br>
中文方面，马礼逊与米怜完成了整本圣经的翻译，于 1823 年出版。和合本由一个委员会历时数十年译成，1919 年出版，此后一百年间成为使用最广的中文圣经；之后又陆续出现许多新的中文译本。本资料库使用的译本，见[圣经译本](../Bible_Translations/README.md)。

<a id="for-readers"></a>
## What This Means for Readers | 这对读者意味着什么

1. **Read a good translation with confidence, and compare a second one** when a word seems important or unclear.
   **可以放心读一个好的译本；** 遇到重要或难懂的词，再对照另一个译本。
2. **Pay attention to footnotes.** They show alternative translations and the places where manuscripts differ.
   **留意脚注。** 脚注会列出其他译法，以及抄本有差异的地方。
3. **Remember that chapters and verses came later,** and read the surrounding passage, not only a single verse.
   **记得章节是后来加上的，** 要读上下文，而不只是读单独的一节。
4. **Notice that Christians use slightly different canons,** and ask which Bible a guide or book is using.
   **留意基督徒使用的正典略有不同，** 读导读或书籍时，可以问它用的是哪一种圣经。
