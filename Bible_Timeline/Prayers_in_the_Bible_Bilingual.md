# Prayers in the Bible | 圣经中的祷告

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> The Bible does not only tell people to [pray](../Glossary/README.md#prayer); it shows real people praying in joy, fear, guilt, and grief. This page gathers twenty-one of those prayers in four groups, each with where to read and what it shows about praying. Read the prayer itself in your own Bible and notice how honest it is.<br>
> 圣经不只是吩咐人[祷告](../Glossary/README.md#prayer)，也记下真实的人在喜乐、恐惧、愧疚和哀伤中怎样祷告。本篇分四组收集其中二十一篇祷告，写明出处，以及它让我们看见祷告的哪一面。请在自己的圣经里读祷告原文，留意它有多么坦诚。

<a id="learning"></a>
## Learning from the Bible’s Prayers | 从圣经的祷告中学习

As you read each prayer, ask three simple questions:<br>
读每一篇祷告时，可以问三个简单的问题：

1. **What does this person know about God?** Many prayers begin by remembering who God is or what he has promised.
   **这个人对神有什么认识？** 许多祷告一开始就记念神是谁，或祂应许过什么。
2. **What is honestly said?** Biblical prayers include complaint, confusion, and confession as well as thanks.
   **这里坦诚地说了什么？** 圣经中的祷告除了感谢，也有抱怨、困惑和认罪。
3. **What does the person ask for, and why?** Notice how often a request is tied to God’s name, his people, or his purpose.
   **这个人求什么，为什么这样求？** 留意有多少祈求是与神的名、祂的子民或祂的心意连在一起的。

<a id="intercession"></a>
## Standing in for Others | 为别人代求

1. **Abraham pleads for Sodom | 亚伯拉罕为所多玛求情** — Genesis 18:22–33. Abraham asks God again and again to spare the city for the sake of the righteous, appealing to God’s own justice.
   创世记 18:22–33。亚伯拉罕一次又一次求神为义人的缘故饶恕那城，所凭的是神自己的公义。
2. **Moses after the golden calf | 金牛犊事件后摩西的代求** — Exodus 32:11–14. When Israel turns to an idol, Moses asks God to remember his promises to Abraham, Isaac, and Israel.
   出埃及记 32:11–14。以色列人拜了偶像，摩西求神记念祂向亚伯拉罕、以撒和以色列所应许的。
3. **Solomon dedicates the temple | 所罗门献殿的祷告** — 1 Kings 8:22–53. Solomon admits that heaven cannot contain God and asks him to hear every prayer made toward this house, including the prayers of foreigners.
   列王纪上 8:22–53。所罗门承认天也不足以容纳神，求神垂听一切向这殿所发的祷告，连外邦人的祷告也在内。
4. **Nehemiah for Jerusalem | 尼希米为耶路撒冷祷告** — Nehemiah 1:4–11. Hearing that Jerusalem’s walls are broken, Nehemiah weeps, confesses his people’s sins, and asks God for favor before the king.
   尼希米记 1:4–11。听说耶路撒冷城墙倒塌，尼希米哭泣，为本族的罪认罪，并求神使他在王面前蒙恩。
5. **Daniel confesses for his people | 但以理为百姓认罪** — Daniel 9:3–19. Daniel says “we have sinned,” not “they,” and asks for mercy not because of their righteousness but because of God’s great compassion.
   但以理书 9:3–19。但以理说“我们犯了罪”，而不是“他们”；他求怜悯，不是因自己的义，而是因神的大怜悯。

<a id="lament"></a>
## Lament and Confession | 哀诉与认罪

6. **Hannah’s silent prayer | 哈拿无声的祷告** — 1 Samuel 1:9–18. In deep distress, Hannah pours out her soul so quietly that the priest thinks she is drunk.
   撒母耳记上 1:9–18。哈拿心里愁苦，在神面前倾心吐意，声音轻得连祭司都以为她喝醉了。
7. **“How long, LORD?” | “耶和华啊，要到几时呢？”** — Psalm 13. The psalm moves from feeling forgotten by God to choosing to trust his unfailing love.
   诗篇 13 篇。诗人从觉得被神忘记，走到决定信靠神不变的慈爱。
8. **David’s confession | 大卫的认罪** — Psalm 51. After his sin with Bathsheba, David asks not only for forgiveness but for a clean heart and a renewed spirit.
   诗篇 51 篇。大卫在与拔示巴的事上犯罪之后，不只求赦免，也求神为他造清洁的心、使他里面重新有正直的灵。
9. **Jonah from inside the fish | 约拿在鱼腹中的祷告** — Jonah 2. From the depths, a reluctant prophet remembers God and declares, “Salvation comes from the LORD.”
   约拿书 2 章。一位不情愿的先知在深处想起神，宣告“救恩出于耶和华”。
10. **Habakkuk’s hard question | 哈巴谷的难题** — Habakkuk 1:2–4 and 3:17–19. Habakkuk asks why God allows injustice, and ends by rejoicing in God even if the crops fail.
    哈巴谷书 1:2–4、3:17–19。哈巴谷质问神为什么容许不公，最后却说即使田里没有出产，仍要因神欢欣。

<a id="praise"></a>
## Praise and Trust | 赞美与信靠

11. **Hannah’s song | 哈拿的颂歌** — 1 Samuel 2:1–10. After Samuel’s birth, Hannah praises God who lifts the poor and humbles the proud.
    撒母耳记上 2:1–10。撒母耳出生后，哈拿赞美那提拔贫穷人、降卑骄傲人的神。
12. **David’s response to God’s promise | 大卫回应神的应许** — 2 Samuel 7:18–29. Overwhelmed that God has promised him a lasting house, David asks, “Who am I?” and prays that God will do as he has said.
    撒母耳记下 7:18–29。神应许大卫的家必永远坚立，大卫受宠若惊地问“我是谁？”，并求神照祂所说的成就。
13. **Elijah on Mount Carmel | 以利亚在迦密山上的祷告** — 1 Kings 18:36–37. A short, simple prayer that the people will know the LORD is God and turn their hearts back.
    列王纪上 18:36–37。一篇简短朴素的祷告，求神使百姓知道耶和华是神，使他们的心回转。
14. **Hezekiah spreads out the letter | 希西家把信展开在神面前** — 2 Kings 19:14–19. Threatened by Assyria, King Hezekiah lays the enemy’s letter before God and asks him to act so all kingdoms will know he alone is God.
    列王纪下 19:14–19。希西家王受亚述威胁，把敌人的信展开在神面前，求神出手，使天下万国都知道唯有祂是神。
15. **A prayer of Moses | 摩西的祷告** — Psalm 90. Life is short before the eternal God, so “teach us to number our days” and establish the work of our hands.
    诗篇 90 篇。人生在永恒的神面前十分短暂，所以求神“指教我们怎样数算自己的日子”，并坚立我们手所做的工。
16. **Mary’s song | 马利亚的颂歌** — Luke 1:46–55. Mary praises God for remembering the humble and keeping his promise to Abraham.
    路加福音 1:46–55。马利亚赞美神顾念卑微的人，并持守祂向亚伯拉罕所发的应许。

<a id="jesus-and-church"></a>
## Jesus and the Church at Prayer | 耶稣与教会的祷告

17. **The Lord’s Prayer | 主祷文** — Matthew 6:9–13. Jesus teaches his followers to pray first for God’s name, kingdom, and will, then for bread, forgiveness, and deliverance. See [The Lord’s Prayer, Line by Line](./Lords_Prayer_Bilingual.md).
    马太福音 6:9–13。耶稣教导门徒先为神的名、神的国、神的旨意祷告，然后才为饮食、赦免和救脱祷告。见[主祷文逐句读](./Lords_Prayer_Bilingual.md)。
18. **Jesus in Gethsemane | 耶稣在客西马尼园的祷告** — Mark 14:32–42. In anguish before the cross, Jesus asks the Father to take the cup away, yet prays, “not what I will, but what you will.”
    马可福音 14:32–42。耶稣在十字架前极其难过，求父把这杯撤去，却仍祷告说：“不要从我的意思，只要从你的意思。”
19. **Jesus prays for his followers | 耶稣为跟随者祷告** — John 17. Before his arrest, Jesus prays that his followers will be kept, made holy, and united, including those who will believe later.
    约翰福音 17 章。耶稣被捕之前，祷告求父保守、圣化跟随祂的人，使他们合而为一，连后来信祂的人也包括在内。
20. **The church prays for courage | 初代教会求胆量** — Acts 4:23–31. Threatened by the authorities, the believers do not ask to be safe but to speak God’s word boldly.
    使徒行传 4:23–31。信徒受到当权者的恐吓，没有求平安无事，而是求神使他们放胆讲论神的道。
21. **Paul prays for the Ephesians | 保罗为以弗所信徒祷告** — Ephesians 3:14–21. Paul asks that believers grasp how wide, long, high, and deep Christ’s love is.
    以弗所书 3:14–21。保罗求神使信徒能明白基督的爱是何等长阔高深。
