# Miracles of Jesus | 耶稣的神迹

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> The Gospels report many miracles of Jesus: healings, freeing people from evil spirits, power over nature, and raising the dead. This page introduces twenty of them in four groups, with where to read and what each shows in the Gospel story, and then explains the seven “signs” in John. It describes what the Gospels say; it does not try to prove or disprove the events by historical method.<br>
> 福音书记载了耶稣的许多神迹：医治、赶出污鬼、掌管自然，以及叫死人复活。本篇分四组介绍其中二十个神迹，写明出处，以及每个神迹在福音书故事中显明了什么，然后说明约翰福音中的七个“神迹”（记号）。本篇描述福音书怎样记载，而不试图用历史方法证明或否定这些事件。

In the Gospels, Jesus’ miracles are not magic tricks or proofs on demand. They are signs that the [kingdom of God](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom) is arriving, acts of compassion toward people in need, and invitations to trust him. They did not force anyone to believe: in his hometown, Mark says, Jesus could do few miracles there and was amazed at their unbelief. For questions about history and evidence, see [Resurrection and Early Witnesses](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md#key-points).<br>
在福音书中，耶稣的神迹不是魔术，也不是应要求而作的证明。它们是[神的国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)正在临到的记号，是向有需要之人显出的怜悯，也是邀请人信靠祂。神迹并不强迫人相信：马可福音记载，耶稣在家乡没有行多少异能，并且诧异他们不信。关于历史与证据的问题，见[《复活与早期见证》](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md#key-points)。

<a id="healing"></a>
## Healing | 医治

1. **Peter’s mother-in-law | 彼得的岳母** — Mark 1:29–31. Jesus takes her by the hand, the fever leaves, and she gets up to serve them.
   马可福音 1:29–31。耶稣拉着她的手，热病就退了，她便起来服事他们。
2. **A man with leprosy | 长大麻风的人** — Mark 1:40–45. Jesus touches a man others would avoid, and he is made clean.
   马可福音 1:40–45。耶稣摸了一个别人都会躲开的人，那人就洁净了。
3. **A paralyzed man lowered through a roof | 从房顶缒下来的瘫子** — Mark 2:1–12. Jesus first says “your sins are forgiven,” then heals him to show he has authority to forgive.
   马可福音 2:1–12。耶稣先说“你的罪赦了”，然后医好他，表明自己有赦罪的权柄。
4. **The woman who touched his cloak | 摸耶稣衣裳的女人** — Mark 5:25–34. After twelve years of bleeding, she is healed, and Jesus calls her “daughter” in front of the crowd.
   马可福音 5:25–34。她患血漏十二年，得了医治；耶稣在众人面前称她为“女儿”。
5. **A centurion’s servant | 百夫长的仆人** — Luke 7:1–10. A Roman officer trusts that Jesus only needs to say the word, and Jesus marvels at his faith.
   路加福音 7:1–10。一位罗马军官相信耶稣只要说一句话就够了，耶稣对他的信心感到惊奇。
6. **Ten men with leprosy | 十个长大麻风的人** — Luke 17:11–19. All ten are healed, but only one, a Samaritan, comes back to give thanks.
   路加福音 17:11–19。十个人都得了洁净，却只有一个撒玛利亚人回来感谢。
7. **Blind Bartimaeus | 瞎子巴底买** — Mark 10:46–52. A blind beggar keeps calling out despite the crowd; Jesus stops, heals him, and he follows Jesus on the road.
   马可福音 10:46–52。一个瞎眼的乞丐不顾众人拦阻，一直呼喊；耶稣停下来医好他，他就在路上跟随耶稣。

<a id="power-over-evil"></a>
## Power over Evil | 胜过邪恶

8. **In the Capernaum synagogue | 在迦百农的会堂里** — Mark 1:21–28. Jesus commands an unclean spirit to come out, and the people are amazed at his authority.
   马可福音 1:21–28。耶稣吩咐污鬼出来，众人都惊讶祂的权柄。
9. **The man among the tombs | 住在坟茔里的人** — Mark 5:1–20. A man no chain could hold is set free and sent home to tell what the Lord has done for him.
   马可福音 5:1–20。一个连铁链都捆不住的人得了释放，耶稣打发他回家，去传扬主为他所做的事。
10. **A mother’s persistent faith | 一位母亲坚持的信心** — Mark 7:24–30. A Gentile woman answers Jesus’ hard saying with humble persistence, and her daughter is freed.
    马可福音 7:24–30。一位外邦妇人以谦卑而坚持的话回应耶稣难懂的话，她的女儿就得了释放。

<a id="nature"></a>
## Power over Nature and Provision | 掌管自然与供应

11. **Calming the storm | 平静风浪** — Mark 4:35–41. Jesus rebukes the wind and waves, and the frightened disciples ask, “Who is this?”
    马可福音 4:35–41。耶稣斥责风和海，惊惧的门徒彼此说：“这到底是谁？”
12. **Feeding the five thousand | 喂饱五千人** — Mark 6:30–44. The only miracle before Easter told in all four Gospels: five loaves and two fish feed a crowd, with twelve baskets left over.
    马可福音 6:30–44。这是复活之前唯一记载在全部四卷福音书中的神迹：五饼二鱼喂饱一大群人，还剩下十二篮。
13. **Walking on the water | 在水面上行走** — Mark 6:45–52. Jesus comes to his struggling disciples on the lake and says, “Take courage! It is I. Don’t be afraid.”
    马可福音 6:45–52。门徒在湖上苦苦摇橹，耶稣在水面上走到他们那里，说：“你们放心！是我，不要怕！”
14. **A great catch of fish | 网满了鱼** — Luke 5:1–11. After a night of catching nothing, Simon lets down the nets at Jesus’ word, and then leaves everything to follow him.
    路加福音 5:1–11。西门整夜没有打着什么，照耶稣的话下网，网满了鱼；后来他撇下一切跟从了耶稣。

<a id="raising"></a>
## Raising the Dead | 叫死人复活

15. **Jairus’s daughter | 睚鲁的女儿** — Mark 5:21–24, 35–43. Told his daughter has died, Jairus hears Jesus say, “Don’t be afraid; just believe,” and she rises.
    马可福音 5:21–24、35–43。睚鲁听说女儿已经死了，耶稣对他说：“不要怕，只要信！”女孩就起来了。
16. **The widow’s son at Nain | 拿因城寡妇的儿子** — Luke 7:11–17. Moved with compassion for a grieving widow, Jesus stops the funeral and gives her son back to her.
    路加福音 7:11–17。耶稣怜悯一位哀哭的寡妇，叫住出殡的行列，把她的儿子交还给她。
17. **Lazarus | 拉撒路** — John 11:1–44. Jesus weeps at his friend’s tomb, then calls Lazarus out four days after his death.
    约翰福音 11:1–44。耶稣在朋友的坟前哭了，然后在拉撒路死了四天之后呼叫他出来。

<a id="seven-signs"></a>
## The Seven Signs in John | 约翰福音的七个神迹

John’s Gospel calls Jesus’ miracles “signs,” because they point beyond themselves to who Jesus is. Many readers count seven in the first half of the book. John says these were written so that readers may believe that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and have life in his name (John 20:30–31).<br>
约翰福音把耶稣的神迹称为“神迹”或“记号”，因为它们指向神迹本身以外的事——耶稣是谁。许多读者在这卷书的前半部分数出七个。约翰说，记这些事是要叫读者信耶稣是基督，是神的儿子，并且因信祂的名得生命（约翰福音 20:30–31）。

18. **Water into wine | 水变为酒** — John 2:1–11. At a wedding in Cana, the first sign reveals his glory.
    约翰福音 2:1–11。在迦拿的婚宴上，这头一个神迹显出了祂的荣耀。
19. **The official’s son | 大臣的儿子** — John 4:46–54. Jesus heals a boy from a distance, and the father believes his word.
    约翰福音 4:46–54。耶稣远距离医好一个孩子，孩子的父亲信了耶稣的话。
20. **The man born blind | 生来瞎眼的人** — John 9. Jesus, “the light of the world,” gives sight to a man who then defends him before the authorities.
    约翰福音 9 章。“世界的光”耶稣使一个生来瞎眼的人看见，这人随后在当权者面前为耶稣辩护。

The other signs usually counted are the healing at the pool of Bethesda (John 5:1–15), the feeding of the five thousand (John 6:1–15), walking on the water (John 6:16–21), and the raising of Lazarus (John 11). Some readers count the list differently or treat the resurrection as the greatest sign.<br>
通常算在其中的另外几个神迹是：在毕士大池边的医治（约翰福音 5:1–15）、喂饱五千人（约翰福音 6:1–15）、在水面上行走（约翰福音 6:16–21），以及叫拉撒路复活（约翰福音 11 章）。也有读者的计算方式不同，或把复活视为最大的神迹。
