# The Sermon on the Mount at a Glance | 登山宝训概览

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> Matthew 5–7 is the longest block of Jesus’ teaching in the Gospels, describing what life looks like for people who belong to the [kingdom](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom) of heaven. This page maps the sermon section by section, so you can see how it moves from blessing, through a deeper reading of the [law](../Glossary/README.md#law), to trust in the Father and a final call to build on Jesus’ words. Each section links to the passage and, where available, to a teaching note in this library.<br>
> 马太福音 5–7 章是福音书中耶稣最长的一段教导，描述属于天[国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)的人过的是怎样的生活。本篇逐段整理这篇讲道，让你看到它怎样从祝福开始，经过对[律法](../Glossary/README.md#law)更深的理解，走向对天父的信靠，最后呼召人把生命建造在耶稣的话上。每一段都附上经文出处，有对应的讲道笔记时也一并链接。

<a id="setting"></a>
## The Setting | 讲道的场景

Jesus has begun preaching “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has come near,” healing many people in Galilee, and calling his first [disciples](../Glossary/README.md#disciple) (Matthew 4:17–25). Seeing the crowds, he goes up on a mountainside, sits down to teach as rabbis did, and speaks to his disciples while the crowds listen (Matthew 5:1–2). At the end, the crowds are amazed, because he teaches “as one who had authority” (Matthew 7:28–29). Luke 6:17–49 records a shorter, similar sermon given on “a level place.”<br>
耶稣已经开始传讲“天国近了，你们应当悔改”，在加利利医治了许多人，也呼召了最早的[门徒](../Glossary/README.md#disciple)（马太福音 4:17–25）。祂看见许多人，就上了山，像当时的拉比那样坐下来教导；祂对门徒讲话，众人也在旁边听（马太福音 5:1–2）。讲完之后，众人都很希奇，因为祂教训他们“正像有权柄的人”（马太福音 7:28–29）。路加福音 6:17–49 记载了一篇较短、内容相近的讲道，地点是“一块平地”。

<a id="map"></a>
## Map of the Sermon | 讲道的结构

1. **The Beatitudes | 八福** — Matthew 5:1–12. Jesus calls blessed the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the meek, the hungry for righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, the peacemakers, and the persecuted. Notes: [Poor in Spirit, Mourning, and Meekness](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-03-08_Sunday_Poor_in_Spirit_Mourning_and_Meekness.md); [Hunger, Mercy, and Purity](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-03-15_Sunday_Hunger_Mercy_and_Purity.md).
   马太福音 5:1–12。耶稣说虚心的人、哀恸的人、温柔的人、饥渴慕义的人、怜恤人的人、清心的人、使人和睦的人、为义受逼迫的人有福了。笔记：[虚心、哀恸与温柔](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-03-08_Sunday_Poor_in_Spirit_Mourning_and_Meekness.md)；[饥渴、怜悯与清心](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-03-15_Sunday_Hunger_Mercy_and_Purity.md)。
2. **Salt and light | 盐和光** — Matthew 5:13–16. Jesus’ followers are to keep their distinctive flavor and let their good works shine so that people praise the Father. Note: [Salt, Light, and a City on a Hill](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-03-29_Sunday_Salt_Light_and_City_on_a_Hill.md).
   马太福音 5:13–16。跟随耶稣的人要保持自己独特的“咸味”，让好行为照在人前，使人荣耀天父。笔记：[盐、光与山上之城](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-03-29_Sunday_Salt_Light_and_City_on_a_Hill.md)。
3. **Jesus fulfills the Law | 耶稣成全律法** — Matthew 5:17–20. Jesus did not come to abolish the Law and the Prophets but to fulfill them, and he calls for a [righteousness](../Glossary/README.md#righteousness) deeper than outward rule-keeping.
   马太福音 5:17–20。耶稣来不是要废掉律法和先知，而是要成全；祂呼召人有比外表守规条更深的[义](../Glossary/README.md#righteousness)。
4. **“You have heard… but I tell you” | “你们听见有话说……只是我告诉你们”** — Matthew 5:21–48. Six examples take the Law to the heart: anger, lust, divorce, oaths, revenge, and love for enemies. Note: [Anger and Reconciliation](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-04-12_Sunday_Anger_and_Reconciliation.md).
   马太福音 5:21–48。六个例子把律法带到内心：怒气、淫念、休妻、起誓、报复，以及爱仇敌。笔记：[愤怒与和好](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-04-12_Sunday_Anger_and_Reconciliation.md)。
5. **Giving, praying, and fasting in secret | 暗中施舍、祷告和禁食** — Matthew 6:1–18. Do these for the Father who sees in secret, not to be seen by others. The Lord’s Prayer sits at the center. Notes: [Spiritual Practices and the Father](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-10_Sunday_Spiritual_Practices_and_the_Father.md); [The Lord’s Prayer, Line by Line](./Lords_Prayer_Bilingual.md).
   马太福音 6:1–18。这些事要做给那在暗中察看的父看，而不是为了让人看见。主祷文就在这一段的中心。笔记：[属灵操练与天父](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-10_Sunday_Spiritual_Practices_and_the_Father.md)；[主祷文逐句读](./Lords_Prayer_Bilingual.md)。
6. **Treasure and worry | 财宝与忧虑** — Matthew 6:19–34. Store up treasure in heaven, do not try to serve both God and money, and stop worrying, because the Father feeds the birds and clothes the lilies. Seek first his kingdom. Notes: [Treasuring Jesus](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-24_Sunday_Treasuring_Jesus.md); [Overcoming Worry](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-31_Sunday_Overcoming_Worry.md).
   马太福音 6:19–34。要积攒财宝在天上，不能又事奉神、又事奉玛门；也不要忧虑，因为天父养活飞鸟，装扮百合花。要先求祂的国。笔记：[以耶稣为至宝](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-24_Sunday_Treasuring_Jesus.md)；[胜过忧虑](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-31_Sunday_Overcoming_Worry.md)。
7. **Judging, asking, and the Golden Rule | 论断、祈求与金律** — Matthew 7:1–12. Look at your own faults before judging others; ask, seek, and knock, trusting the Father’s goodness; and treat others as you want them to treat you.
   马太福音 7:1–12。论断人之前先看自己的过错；要祈求、寻找、叩门，信靠天父的美善；你们愿意人怎样待你们，你们也要怎样待人。
8. **Two ways, two trees, two builders | 两条路、两种树、两种盖房子的人** — Matthew 7:13–27. Choose the narrow gate, judge teachers by their fruit, and build your life on Jesus’ words like a house on rock.
   马太福音 7:13–27。要进窄门；凭着果子认出教师的真假；要把生命建造在耶稣的话上，像把房子盖在磐石上。

<a id="notice"></a>
## Three Things to Notice | 值得留意的三件事

1. **Blessing comes before command.** The sermon opens with “Blessed are…,” not with rules. Jesus first describes the people God welcomes, then shows how they live.
   **祝福在命令之前。**这篇讲道以“……的人有福了”开头，而不是以规条开头。耶稣先描述神所接纳的人，再说明他们怎样生活。
2. **It is about the heart.** Again and again, Jesus moves from the outward act to what is inside: anger before murder, desire before adultery, motive behind prayer and giving.
   **它关乎人心。**耶稣一再从外在的行为转向内心：杀人之前的怒气，奸淫之前的欲念，祷告和施舍背后的动机。
3. **It is centered on the Father.** The sermon mentions “your Father” many times. The life it describes rests on trusting a Father who sees, knows, and provides.
   **它以天父为中心。**这篇讲道多次提到“你们的父”。它所描述的生活，建立在信靠一位察看、知道、供应的天父之上。
