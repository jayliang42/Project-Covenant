# Key Passages to Know | 值得熟悉的经文

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> This page lists thirty-six short passages that Christians often read, quote, or memorize, in six groups. Each entry gives the reference and one sentence on why the passage matters. Read the passage itself in your own [Bible](../Glossary/README.md#bible); the summaries here do not replace it.<br>
> 本篇分六组列出三十六段基督徒常读、常引用或背诵的经文。每一条写明出处，并用一句话说明这段经文为什么重要。请在自己的[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)里读经文本身；这里的简介不能代替经文。

> **Language status | 语言状态:** Fully bilingual, paragraph by paragraph.
>
> **语言状态 | Language status：** 全文按段落提供中英双语。

You do not need to read these in order. Pick one a day, read the whole passage slowly, and ask what it shows about God and how it fits into the larger story. If a reference is new to you, the [Bible Reading Toolkit](./Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#reference) explains how to find it.<br>
不必按顺序读。可以每天选一段，慢慢读完整段经文，问问它显明了神的什么、在整个故事中处于什么位置。如果不熟悉经文出处的查法，[读经工具箱](./Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#reference)有说明。

<a id="beginnings"></a>
## Beginnings and Covenant | 起初与立约

1. **Genesis 1:1–2:3 | 创世记 1:1–2:3** — God creates an ordered, good world and makes humanity in his image.
   神创造一个有秩序、美好的世界，并按自己的形象造人。
2. **Genesis 3 | 创世记 3 章** — Humanity distrusts God and disobeys, and everything after this chapter deals with the damage.
   人不信靠神、违背神；这一章之后的整本圣经都在处理由此带来的破坏。
3. **Genesis 12:1–3 | 创世记 12:1–3** — God calls Abram and promises that through his family all peoples on earth will be blessed.
   神呼召亚伯兰，应许地上万族都要因他的家族得福。
4. **Exodus 3:1–15 | 出埃及记 3:1–15** — At the burning bush, God reveals his name to Moses and promises to rescue his people.
   神在燃烧的荆棘中向摩西启示自己的名，并应许要拯救祂的百姓。
5. **Exodus 20:1–17 | 出埃及记 20:1–17** — The Ten Commandments begin with God’s rescue, then describe life with God and neighbor. See [The Ten Commandments](./Ten_Commandments_Bilingual.md).
   十诫先讲神的拯救，再描述人怎样与神、与邻舍相处。见[十诫](./Ten_Commandments_Bilingual.md)。
6. **Deuteronomy 6:4–9 | 申命记 6:4–9** — Israel’s central confession: the LORD is one, so love him with all your heart and teach this at home.
   以色列信仰的核心宣认：耶和华是独一的主，要尽心爱祂，并在家中把这话教导儿女。

<a id="psalms-and-wisdom"></a>
## Psalms and Wisdom | 诗篇与智慧

7. **Psalm 1 | 诗篇 1 篇** — Two ways of life: the person rooted in God’s word like a tree by water, and the way that blows away.
   两条路：扎根于神话语的人像栽在溪水旁的树，另一条路却如糠秕被风吹散。
8. **Psalm 23 | 诗篇 23 篇** — The LORD as shepherd and host, present even in the darkest valley.
   耶和华是牧者，也是摆设筵席的主人；即使在最幽暗的山谷中也与人同在。
9. **Psalm 51 | 诗篇 51 篇** — A prayer of confession that asks God for a clean heart after serious sin.
   一篇认罪的祷告，在犯了重罪之后求神造一颗清洁的心。
10. **Psalm 139 | 诗篇 139 篇** — God knows a person completely and is present everywhere, which becomes comfort rather than threat.
    神完全知道一个人，也无处不在；这对诗人成了安慰，而不是威胁。
11. **Proverbs 3:5–6 | 箴言 3:5–6** — Trust the LORD with your whole heart rather than leaning only on your own understanding.
    要专心仰赖耶和华，不可只倚靠自己的聪明。
12. **Ecclesiastes 3:1–14 | 传道书 3:1–14** — There is a time for everything, and God has set eternity in the human heart.
    凡事都有定时；神又将永生安置在世人心里。

<a id="prophets"></a>
## The Prophets’ Hope | 先知的盼望

13. **Isaiah 6:1–8 | 以赛亚书 6:1–8** — Isaiah sees God’s holiness, is cleansed, and answers, “Here am I. Send me!”
    以赛亚看见神的圣洁，蒙洁净后回应说：“我在这里，请差遣我！”
14. **Isaiah 40:28–31 | 以赛亚书 40:28–31** — The everlasting God gives strength to the weary who wait for him.
    永在的神赐力量给疲乏、等候祂的人。
15. **Isaiah 52:13–53:12 | 以赛亚书 52:13–53:12** — The servant who suffers for others’ sins; the New Testament reads this passage in light of Jesus.
    为别人的罪受苦的仆人；新约圣经从耶稣的角度来读这段经文。
16. **Jeremiah 31:31–34 | 耶利米书 31:31–34** — The promise of a new covenant with the law written on the heart.
    神应许要立新约，把律法写在人心上。
17. **Ezekiel 37:1–14 | 以西结书 37:1–14** — In a valley of dry bones, God promises to breathe new life into his people.
    在满是枯骨的山谷中，神应许要把生气吹进祂的百姓里面，使他们复活。
18. **Micah 6:8 | 弥迦书 6:8** — What God asks: to act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with him.
    神所要的，是行公义、好怜悯，存谦卑的心与神同行。

<a id="jesus-teaching"></a>
## Jesus’ Teaching | 耶稣的教导

19. **Matthew 5:1–12 | 马太福音 5:1–12** — The Beatitudes: Jesus calls the poor in spirit, the mourners, and the merciful blessed.
    八福：耶稣称虚心的、哀恸的、怜恤人的人是有福的。
20. **Matthew 6:9–13 | 马太福音 6:9–13** — The Lord’s Prayer, a pattern for praying to God as Father.
    主祷文，教导人怎样向天父祷告。
21. **Matthew 22:34–40 | 马太福音 22:34–40** — The greatest commandments: love God and love your neighbor as yourself.
    最大的诫命：爱神，并爱人如己。
22. **Luke 15 | 路加福音 15 章** — Three stories of the lost being found, and the joy of God over one who returns.
    三个失而复得的故事，以及神为一个回转的人所发的喜乐。
23. **John 15:1–17 | 约翰福音 15:1–17** — Jesus is the vine; his followers bear fruit by remaining in him and loving one another.
    耶稣是葡萄树；跟随祂的人常在祂里面、彼此相爱，就能结出果子。
24. **Matthew 28:18–20 | 马太福音 28:18–20** — The risen Jesus sends his disciples to make disciples of all nations and promises to be with them.
    复活的耶稣差遣门徒使万民作祂的门徒，并应许常与他们同在。

<a id="jesus-identity"></a>
## Jesus’ Identity, Death, and Resurrection | 耶稣的身份、受死与复活

25. **John 1:1–18 | 约翰福音 1:1–18** — The Word who was with God and was God became flesh and lived among us.
    太初与神同在、本身就是神的道，成了肉身住在我们中间。
26. **John 3:16–17 | 约翰福音 3:16–17** — God so loved the world that he gave his Son, not to condemn the world but to save it.
    神爱世人，甚至将祂的儿子赐给他们；不是要定世人的罪，而是要拯救世人。
27. **Philippians 2:5–11 | 腓立比书 2:5–11** — Christ humbled himself to death on a cross, and God highly exalted him.
    基督自己卑微，以至于死在十字架上；神就将祂升为至高。
28. **Mark 15:33–39 | 马可福音 15:33–39** — Jesus dies on the cross, the temple curtain is torn, and a Roman soldier calls him the Son of God.
    耶稣死在十字架上，圣殿的幔子裂开，一位罗马百夫长称祂为神的儿子。
29. **1 Corinthians 15:3–8 | 哥林多前书 15:3–8** — An early summary of the good news: Christ died, was buried, was raised, and appeared to many.
    早期福音的摘要：基督死了，被埋葬，复活了，并向许多人显现。
30. **Romans 8:31–39 | 罗马书 8:31–39** — Nothing in all creation can separate God’s people from his love in Christ.
    在一切受造之物中，没有什么能使神的子民与祂在基督里的爱隔绝。

<a id="life-in-christ"></a>
## Life in Christ and the Hope Ahead | 在基督里的生活与将来的盼望

31. **Romans 12:1–2 | 罗马书 12:1–2** — In view of God’s mercy, offer your whole life to him and be transformed by a renewed mind.
    因着神的慈悲，把整个人献给神，心意更新而变化。
32. **1 Corinthians 13 | 哥林多前书 13 章** — Love is patient and kind, and without love even great gifts are empty.
    爱是恒久忍耐，又有恩慈；没有爱，再大的恩赐也算不得什么。
33. **Galatians 5:22–23 | 加拉太书 5:22–23** — The fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, and six more qualities that the Spirit grows.
    圣灵所结的果子：仁爱、喜乐、和平，以及圣灵培养的另外六种品格。
34. **Ephesians 2:8–10 | 以弗所书 2:8–10** — Saved by grace through faith, not by works, and created for good works.
    得救是本乎恩、也因着信，不是出于行为；我们却是为行善而被造的。
35. **Hebrews 11:1–12:3 | 希伯来书 11:1–12:3** — A long line of people who lived by faith, ending with Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith.
    一长串凭信心生活的人，最后指向耶稣——为我们信心创始成终的那一位。
36. **Revelation 21:1–5 | 启示录 21:1–5** — A new heaven and a new earth where God dwells with his people and makes all things new.
    新天新地：神与祂的子民同住，并将一切都更新了。
