## Evidence Method | 证据方法

The research separates **material type** from **judgment strength**. A manuscript can be excellent evidence for textual transmission without proving an event narrated inside the book; a city or inscription can illuminate a historical setting without proving a miracle or theological conclusion.

本研究把**材料类型**与**判断强度**分开。一份抄本可以是文本流传的强证据，却不能因此证明卷内叙事事件；一座城市或一方铭文可以照明历史处境，却不能因此证明神迹或神学结论。

### Material Types | 材料类型

| Code | Meaning | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EV** | Event or person｜事件或人物 | Names or directly contextualizes a person, power, place, or event｜提及或直接照明人物、政权、地点或事件 |
| **BG** | Background or institution｜背景或制度 | Explains language, law, worship, economy, geography, or social setting｜解释语言、法律、敬拜、经济、地理或社会处境 |
| **MS** | Manuscript or textual transmission｜抄本或文本流传 | Establishes a surviving textual witness, variant, or transmission history｜建立现存文本见证、异文或流传史 |
| **RX** | Reception and interpretation｜接收与解释 | Shows later catalogue, use, commentary, liturgy, or translation｜显示后期书目、使用、注释、礼仪或译本接收 |
| **UT** | Untestable theological boundary｜不可直接检验的神学边界 | Marks where archaeology or history cannot decide the theological claim｜标明考古或历史方法无法裁定之处 |

### Strength Grades | 强度等级

| Grade | Meaning | Reading discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — direct and strong｜直接且强** | Context, dating, and reading are comparatively secure; the source directly bears on the claim.｜语境、年代与释读较稳，材料直接涉及相关判断。 | Use as a firm historical or textual anchor, while retaining genre and scope.｜可作稳固锚点，但仍保留文类与范围。 |
| **B — strong but indirect｜有力但间接** | The source closely fits the period, institution, or setting but does not uniquely prove the narrative.｜材料高度吻合时代、制度或处境，却不能唯一证明叙事。 | Use to illuminate plausibility and background, not as automatic proof.｜用于照明背景与可理解性，不当作自动证明。 |
| **C — materially disputed｜有实质争议** | Dating, identification, reading, or interpretation remains contested.｜年代、认定、释读或解释仍有主要分歧。 | Present the major positions and avoid absolute wording.｜并列主要观点，避免绝对表述。 |
| **D — not core evidence｜不作核心证据** | Provenance is unknown, context is lost, forgery is suspected, or only promotional retellings remain.｜来源不明、语境丢失、疑似伪造或只见宣传转述。 | Discuss only as a dispute, warning, or misinformation case.｜只作为争议、警示或误传案例。 |

## Responsible Reading Workflow | 负责任的阅读步骤

1. **Read the biblical passage first.｜先读经文。** Record people, places, literary movement, and explicit claims before consulting background material.
   先记录人物、地点、文脉推进与经文直接宣告，再查背景资料。
2. **Identify the material type.｜辨认材料类型。** Ask whether the source is EV, BG, MS, RX, or a UT boundary.
   判断资料属于 EV、BG、MS、RX，还是 UT 边界。
3. **Evaluate strength separately.｜另行判断强度。** A–D describes confidence, not category.
   A–D 描述判断把握，不代表材料类别。
4. **State support and limits together.｜同时写出支持与边界。** Do not turn correlation into proof or absence into disproof.
   不把对应关系扩大为证明，也不把尚未发现材料自动写成反证。
5. **Return to the storyline.｜回到故事线。** Ask how the passage advances covenant, kingdom, presence, redemption, mission, and new creation.
   追问经文怎样推进圣约、国度、同在、救赎、使命与新创造。
