<a id="three-parts"></a>
## Three Parts | 三个部分

The creed has three parts, one for each person of the [Trinity](../Glossary/README.md#trinity): God the Father, Jesus Christ the Son, and the [Holy Spirit](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit). The middle part is the longest, because it tells the story of Jesus. Wording differs slightly between churches and translations; the phrases below follow the common modern form.<br>
信经分为三个部分，分别对应[三一神](../Glossary/README.md#trinity)的三个位格：圣父、圣子耶稣基督和[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)。中间的部分最长，因为它讲述耶稣的故事。不同教会和译本的措辞略有差别；下面的句子采用常见的现代说法。

<a id="father"></a>
## God the Father | 圣父

1. **“I believe in God, the Father almighty, creator of heaven and earth.” | “我信上帝，全能的父，创造天地的主。”** — “I believe” means more than agreeing with an idea; it means trusting God. Everything that exists was made by him (Genesis 1:1), and Jesus taught his followers to call him Father (Matthew 6:9).
   “我信”不只是同意某个观点，而是信靠神。一切存在的都是祂所造的（创世记 1:1），耶稣也教导门徒称祂为父（马太福音 6:9）。

<a id="son"></a>
## Jesus Christ | 耶稣基督

2. **“I believe in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord.” | “我信我主耶稣基督，上帝的独生子。”** — Jesus is the Son of God in a unique way (John 3:16), and Christians confess him as Lord (Philippians 2:11). See [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) and [Lord](../Glossary/README.md#lord).
   耶稣以独特的方式是神的儿子（约翰福音 3:16），基督徒宣认祂为主（腓立比书 2:11）。见[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)与[主](../Glossary/README.md#lord)。
3. **“He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary.” | “因着圣灵成孕，由童贞女马利亚所生。”** — Jesus’ birth came about by God’s own action, and he was born as a real human child (Matthew 1:18–25; Luke 1:26–38).
   耶稣的降生出于神自己的作为，祂也是作为一个真实的婴孩出生（马太福音 1:18–25；路加福音 1:26–38）。
4. **“He suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried.” | “在本丢彼拉多手下受难，被钉于十字架，死了，埋葬了。”** — Naming Pilate, the Roman governor, ties Jesus’ death to a real time and place in history (Mark 15; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4).
   提到罗马巡抚本丢彼拉多的名字，就把耶稣的死与历史上真实的时间和地点连在一起（马可福音 15 章；哥林多前书 15:3–4）。
5. **“He descended to the dead.” | “降在阴间。”** — This phrase says Jesus truly died and shared the condition of the dead; some read it in light of 1 Peter 3:18–20. It was added to the creed later than most phrases, and some Chinese versions leave it out.
   这一句表明耶稣真的死了，进入死人的境况；也有人参照彼得前书 3:18–20 来理解。这一句比大部分句子更晚才加入信经，有些中文版本没有这一句。
6. **“On the third day he rose again.” | “第三天从死人中复活。”** — The [resurrection](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection) is at the heart of the gospel Paul received and passed on (1 Corinthians 15:3–8).
   [复活](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection)是保罗所领受、又传给别人的福音的核心（哥林多前书 15:3–8）。
7. **“He ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Father.” | “升天，坐在全能父上帝的右边。”** — After forty days Jesus was taken up (Acts 1:9–11). Sitting at God’s right hand is a picture of sharing God’s rule (Hebrews 1:3).
   四十天之后，耶稣被接升天（使徒行传 1:9–11）。坐在神的右边，表示与神一同掌权（希伯来书 1:3）。
8. **“He will come to judge the living and the dead.” | “将来必从那里降临，审判活人死人。”** — Christians wait for Jesus’ return, when he will set all things right (Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:1).
   基督徒等候耶稣再来，那时祂要使万事归正（使徒行传 10:42；提摩太后书 4:1）。

<a id="spirit"></a>
## The Holy Spirit and the Church | 圣灵与教会

9. **“I believe in the Holy Spirit.” | “我信圣灵。”** — The Spirit is God present with his people, whom Jesus promised and who came at Pentecost (John 14:16–17; Acts 2).
   圣灵是与祂子民同在的神；耶稣应许了圣灵，圣灵在五旬节降临（约翰福音 14:16–17；使徒行传 2 章）。
10. **“The holy catholic church.” | “我信圣而公之教会。”** — “Catholic” here means universal: the one [church](../Glossary/README.md#church) of all believers in every place and time, not one denomination (Ephesians 4:4–6). Chinese versions usually say 圣而公之教会; older ones say 圣公会, which here means the same thing, not the Anglican Church.
    这里的“公”是“普世”的意思：指各时各地所有信徒组成的同一个[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)，而不是某一个宗派（以弗所书 4:4–6）。所以中文版本通常译作“圣而公之教会”；较早的版本作“圣公会”，在这里也是同样的意思，并不是指圣公宗教会。
11. **“The communion of saints.” | “我信圣徒相通。”** — In the New Testament, “saints” means all God’s people (Romans 1:7). They share one life in Christ and belong to one another (Acts 2:42).
    在新约中，“圣徒”指神所有的子民（罗马书 1:7）。他们在基督里同享一个生命，彼此相属（使徒行传 2:42）。
12. **“The forgiveness of sins.” | “我信罪得赦免。”** — God forgives everyone who turns to him through Jesus (Acts 2:38; 1 John 1:9). See [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness).
    凡借着耶稣归向神的人，神都赦免他们的罪（使徒行传 2:38；约翰一书 1:9）。见[赦免](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness)。
13. **“The resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Amen.” | “我信身体复活，我信永生。阿们。”** — Christian hope is not escape from the body but a renewed, raised body (1 Corinthians 15:42–44) and life with God forever in a new creation (Revelation 21–22).
    基督徒的盼望不是脱离身体，而是得着更新、复活的身体（哥林多前书 15:42–44），并在新天新地中与神永远同在（启示录 21–22 章）。

<a id="origins"></a>
## Where the Creed Came From | 信经从哪里来

Despite its name, the creed was not written by the twelve apostles. It grew out of the questions put to new believers at baptism in the early church and out of short summaries of faith that writers such as Irenaeus and Tertullian called the “rule of faith” in the late second century. The earliest complete text of its ancestor, the Old Roman Creed, survives in a letter from about AD 340. A few phrases, such as “he descended to the dead” and “the communion of saints,” were added later, and the wording used today was settled in the West by about the eighth century. Its name reflects the belief that it faithfully summarizes the apostles’ teaching.<br>
虽然名叫“使徒信经”，它并不是十二使徒写的。它源于早期教会在洗礼时向初信者提出的问题，以及二世纪后期爱任纽、特土良等作者所说的“信仰准则”这类简短的信仰纲要。它的前身“古罗马信经”最早的完整文本，保存在约主后 340 年的一封书信中。“降在阴间”“圣徒相通”等少数句子是后来加入的；今天所用的措辞，大约到八世纪才在西方教会定型。它之所以叫“使徒信经”，是因为人们相信它忠实地概括了使徒的教导。

For the sources behind these dates, see [Irenaeus](./史料与考古旁证索引.md#evidence-141) and [Tertullian](./史料与考古旁证索引.md#evidence-142) on the rule of faith, and [Marcellus’ letter with the Old Roman Creed](./史料与考古旁证索引.md#evidence-144) in the evidence index.<br>
这些年代背后的来源，见旁证索引中[爱任纽](./史料与考古旁证索引.md#evidence-141)和[特土良](./史料与考古旁证索引.md#evidence-142)论信仰准则的条目，以及[马尔凯鲁斯书信中的古罗马信经](./史料与考古旁证索引.md#evidence-144)。
