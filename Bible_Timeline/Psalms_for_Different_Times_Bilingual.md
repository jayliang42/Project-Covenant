# Psalms for Different Times | 不同处境中的诗篇

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> The [Psalms](../Glossary/README.md#psalm) give words for almost every part of life: fear, grief, guilt, thanks, waiting, and wonder. This page suggests two or three psalms for ten common situations, with one line on why each fits. Read the whole psalm slowly, and let it become your own prayer.<br>
> [诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)几乎为人生的每个处境都提供了言语：恐惧、哀伤、愧疚、感恩、等候与惊叹。本篇为十种常见处境各推荐两三篇诗篇，并用一句话说明为什么合适。请慢慢读完整篇诗，让它成为你自己的祷告。

Many psalms do not stay in one mood. A psalm that begins in complaint often ends in trust, and a few, like Psalm 88, stay in the dark to the end. That honesty is part of their gift: you do not have to feel better before you pray.<br>
许多诗篇不会停留在同一种情绪里。以抱怨开头的诗篇常以信靠结束；也有少数诗篇，比如诗篇 88 篇，直到最后仍在黑暗中。这种坦诚正是诗篇的恩赐之一：你不必先感觉好起来，才可以祷告。

This page is a reading guide, not a replacement for care. If you or someone near you is in crisis or in danger, contact a trusted person or local emergency services right away.<br>

<a id="afraid"></a>
## When You Are Afraid | 害怕的时候

- **Psalm 27 | 诗篇 27 篇** — “The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?” A psalm that holds courage and longing together.
  “耶和华是我的亮光，是我的拯救，我还怕谁呢？”这篇诗把勇气与渴慕放在一起。
- **Psalm 46 | 诗篇 46 篇** — God is a refuge even when the earth shakes; the psalm ends with a call to be still and know that he is God.
  即使大地震动，神仍是避难所；诗的结尾呼召人休息，知道祂是神。
- **Psalm 56 | 诗篇 56 篇** — A short prayer for frightened people: when I am afraid, I will trust in you.
  一篇写给惧怕之人的短诗：我惧怕的时候，要倚靠你。

<a id="grieving"></a>
## When You Are Grieving or in Pain | 哀伤或痛苦的时候

- **Psalm 13 | 诗篇 13 篇** — Four times the psalmist asks “How long?”, and still ends by choosing to trust God’s unfailing love.
  诗人四次问“要到几时呢？”，最后仍决定倚靠神不变的慈爱。
- **Psalms 42–43 | 诗篇 42–43 篇** — A thirsty, downcast soul talks to itself and keeps pointing itself back to hope in God.
  一个干渴、忧闷的心灵对自己说话，一再把自己引回到对神的盼望。
- **Psalm 88 | 诗篇 88 篇** — The darkest psalm, which ends without relief; it shows that even unresolved pain can be spoken to God.
  最黑暗的一篇诗，到结尾仍没有得着舒解；它说明即使没有答案的痛苦，也可以向神诉说。

<a id="done-wrong"></a>
## When You Have Done Wrong | 犯了错的时候

- **Psalm 32 | 诗篇 32 篇** — Hiding sin weighs a person down; confessing it brings the relief of being forgiven.
  隐瞒罪会压垮一个人；承认罪却带来蒙赦免的释放。
- **Psalm 51 | 诗篇 51 篇** — A prayer for mercy and a clean heart, not only for the consequences to go away.
  求神怜悯、为自己造清洁的心，而不只是求后果消失。
- **Psalm 130 | 诗篇 130 篇** — “Out of the depths I cry to you”: forgiveness is with God, so his people wait for him.
  “我从深处向你求告”：赦免在于神，所以祂的子民等候祂。

<a id="thankful"></a>
## When You Are Thankful | 感恩的时候

- **Psalm 100 | 诗篇 100 篇** — A short call for all the earth to enter God’s presence with thanksgiving.
  一篇短诗，呼召全地带着感谢进入神的面前。
- **Psalm 103 | 诗篇 103 篇** — Do not forget all his benefits: forgiveness, healing, and a love as high as the heavens.
  不可忘记祂的一切恩惠：赦免、医治，以及高如天的慈爱。
- **Psalm 136 | 诗篇 136 篇** — Every line repeats that God’s love endures forever, through creation and the whole story of Israel.
  每一节都重复说神的慈爱永远长存，从创造一直讲到以色列的整个历史。

<a id="cannot-sleep"></a>
## When You Cannot Sleep | 夜里难以入睡的时候

- **Psalm 4 | 诗篇 4 篇** — An evening psalm that ends, “In peace I will lie down and sleep.”
  一篇晚祷诗，结尾说：“我必安然躺下睡觉。”
- **Psalm 121 | 诗篇 121 篇** — The one who keeps you will neither slumber nor sleep.
  保护你的，必不打盹，也不睡觉。
- **Psalm 131 | 诗篇 131 篇** — Three verses about a quieted heart, like a child resting with its mother.
  只有三节，描写一颗平静安稳的心，好像孩子安息在母亲身边。

<a id="waiting"></a>
## When You Are Waiting | 在等候的时候

- **Psalm 40 | 诗篇 40 篇** — “I waited patiently for the LORD,” and he lifted me out of the pit and put a new song in my mouth.
  “我曾耐性等候耶和华”，祂把我从祸坑里拉上来，使我口唱新歌。
- **Psalm 62 | 诗篇 62 篇** — The soul waits in silence for God alone, its rock and its fortress.
  我的心默默无声，专等候神；祂是我的磐石，我的高台。

<a id="injustice"></a>
## When You Are Angry at Injustice | 为不公而愤怒的时候

- **Psalm 10 | 诗篇 10 篇** — Why does God seem to stand far off while the wicked prey on the weak? The psalm brings that question straight to him.
  为什么恶人欺压软弱的人时，神好像站在远处？这篇诗把这个问题直接带到神面前。
- **Psalm 73 | 诗篇 73 篇** — The psalmist nearly gives up, envying the wicked, until he enters God’s sanctuary and sees things differently.
  诗人几乎失脚，嫉妒恶人的亨通，直到进了神的圣所，才看明白。

<a id="creation"></a>
## When You Wonder at Creation | 为创造而惊叹的时候

- **Psalm 8 | 诗篇 8 篇** — Under the night sky, who are human beings that God should care for them?
  在夜空之下：人算什么，神竟眷顾他？
- **Psalm 19 | 诗篇 19 篇** — The heavens declare God’s glory, and his word gives light and joy.
  诸天述说神的荣耀，祂的话语使人明亮、喜乐。
- **Psalm 104 | 诗篇 104 篇** — A long song of praise for land, sea, animals, and seasons, all depending on God.
  一首长长的赞美诗，歌颂陆地、海洋、动物和季节，万物都仰赖神。

<a id="far-from-god"></a>
## When You Feel Far from God | 觉得神很遥远的时候

- **Psalm 22 | 诗篇 22 篇** — “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” Jesus prayed these words on the cross, and the psalm turns toward praise.
  “我的神，我的神，为什么离弃我？”耶稣在十字架上说了这句话；这篇诗后来转向赞美。
- **Psalm 77 | 诗篇 77 篇** — In a sleepless night of doubt, the psalmist decides to remember what God has done.
  在充满疑惑、无法入眠的夜里，诗人决定回想神从前的作为。

<a id="guidance"></a>
## When You Need Guidance | 需要引导的时候

- **Psalm 25 | 诗篇 25 篇** — A prayer to be shown God’s ways and taught his paths, by someone who knows they need forgiveness too.
  求神把祂的道路指示我、把祂的路径教训我；祷告的人也知道自己需要赦免。
- **Psalm 143 | 诗篇 143 篇** — Let me hear of your unfailing love in the morning, and show me the way I should go.
  求你使我清晨得听你慈爱之言，求你使我知道当行的路。
