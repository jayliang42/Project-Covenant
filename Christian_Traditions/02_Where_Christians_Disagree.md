<a id="shared-center"></a>
## Shared Center Before Disagreement | 先看共同中心，再看分歧

**English:** Roman Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, and most historic Protestant churches share the Nicene confession of one God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—and confess Jesus Christ as truly divine and truly human, crucified and risen. They read the Old and New Testaments as Scripture, practice baptism and the Lord’s Supper or Eucharist, pray, worship, and understand the church as called to witness and serve.<br>
**中文：** 罗马天主教、东正教与多数历史性新教共同认信尼西亚信经所宣告的独一神——圣父、圣子、圣灵——并承认耶稣基督真神真人、被钉十架并复活。他们都把新旧约视为圣经，实行洗礼与圣餐，祷告、敬拜，并认为教会蒙召作见证与服事。

**English:** This shared center is substantial, but it does not erase real divisions. Some groups using the word “Christian” reject Nicene Trinitarian doctrine. Nicene churches also differ over recognition of ministry and sacraments and are not necessarily in full communion with one another. The page therefore records both common confession and institutional boundaries.<br>
**中文：** 这个共同中心相当重要，却不会消除真实分裂。有些使用“基督教”名称的群体不接受尼西亚三一论。接受尼西亚信经的教会对圣职与圣礼的相互承认也有差异，并且不一定彼此处于完整共融。因此本页同时记录共同信仰与制度边界。

<a id="conflict-axes"></a>
## Nine Conflict Axes | 九条冲突轴

| Axis / 议题轴 | The question underneath / 背后的问题 | Representative range, not exhaustive / 代表性范围，非穷尽 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scripture, Tradition, and interpretation / 圣经、传统与释经 | Where does binding teaching authority reside, and who interprets faithfully? / 具有约束力的教导权在哪里，谁有权作忠实解释？ | Catholic teaching joins Scripture, apostolic Tradition, and the Magisterium; Orthodoxy reads Scripture within Holy Tradition and liturgical life; classical Protestants make Scripture the supreme norm while differing over confessions, historical criticism, inerrancy, and interpretive community. / 天主教把圣经、使徒传统与训导职相连；东正教在圣传统与礼仪生命中阅读圣经；古典新教以圣经为最高规范，但在信条、历史批判、无误论与释经群体上仍有分歧。 |
| Salvation and atonement / 救恩与赎罪 | How does Christ save, how is grace received, and how are justification and transformation related? / 基督怎样拯救、恩典怎样领受，称义与生命改变是什么关系？ | Traditions share the priority of divine grace but dispute justification by faith alone, baptism and sacramental participation, monergism and synergism, election and free response, perseverance, the meaning of the cross, and the final scope of salvation. / 各传统共同承认神恩典的首要性，却在唯独因信称义、洗礼与圣礼参与、神恩单作与神人合作、拣选与自由回应、圣徒坚忍、十字架意义及救恩最终范围上有争论。 |
| Church and ministry / 教会与圣职 | What makes the church visible and who may exercise teaching or sacramental authority? / 什么构成有形教会，谁可以行使教导或圣礼权柄？ | Papal and episcopal communion, Orthodox conciliar episcopacy, Anglican episcopacy, Presbyterian rule by elders, Methodist connectional structures, and Baptist congregational autonomy embody different accounts of continuity and authority. / 教宗及主教共融、东正教议会式主教制、圣公宗主教制、长老治理、卫理连接制与浸信会会众自治，体现了不同的延续性与权威观。 |
| Baptism / 洗礼 | Who should be baptized, what does baptism do, and how is it related to faith and church membership? / 谁应受洗、洗礼产生什么功效，它与信心及会籍有什么关系？ | Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, and Methodist churches normally baptize infants as well as converts, though they explain grace and covenant differently. Baptist, Anabaptist, and many Pentecostal churches normally baptize professing believers; immersion is especially common or required in many Baptist and Pentecostal bodies, while Anabaptist practice varies. / 天主教、东正教、路德宗、改革宗、圣公宗与卫理宗通常为婴儿及归信者施洗，但对恩典与圣约的解释不同。浸信会、再洗礼派与许多五旬节教会通常为公开认信者施洗；许多浸信会与五旬节组织特别常见或要求浸礼，而再洗礼派的施洗方式并不统一。 |
| Eucharist or Lord’s Supper / 圣餐 | How is Christ present, what happens in the meal, and who may receive it? / 基督怎样临在、圣餐中发生什么、谁可以领受？ | Representative emphases include Catholic transubstantiation, Orthodox confession of the Eucharist as a holy mystery and true communion in Christ, Lutheran confession of Christ’s body and blood as truly present, Reformed accounts of spiritual feeding on Christ, and memorial emphases in some Baptist churches. These are not exhaustive or internally uniform; admission may also be closed, guarded, or open. / 代表性重点包括天主教的变质说、东正教把圣餐认信为圣奥秘及在基督里真实相交、路德宗认信基督身体与血的真实临在、改革宗对属灵领受基督的说明，以及部分浸信会的纪念重点。这些并非穷尽或内部完全统一的分类；领餐也可能采取封闭、受监督或开放方式。 |
| Holy Spirit and gifts / 圣灵与恩赐 | Are miraculous gifts expected today, and is Spirit baptism distinct from conversion? / 今日是否应期待神迹性恩赐，圣灵洗是否与归信有别？ | Classical Pentecostals often teach a distinct empowering experience and continuing gifts; charismatics practice gifts inside many older churches; cessationists hold that some sign gifts were tied especially to the apostolic era; many Christians fit neither pole neatly. / 古典五旬节派常教导归信之后另有能力性的经历，并接受恩赐持续；灵恩者在许多传统教会内实践恩赐；终止论者认为部分神迹性恩赐特别属于使徒时期；许多基督徒并不完全落在任一端。 |
| Gender, sexuality, and family / 性别、性伦理与家庭 | How should creation, embodiment, holiness, covenant, equality, and pastoral care be interpreted together? / 怎样把创造、身体、圣洁、圣约、平等与牧养关怀一同解释？ | Churches disagree over women’s ordination, the offices open to women, same-sex marriage, LGBTQ ordination, celibacy, divorce and remarriage, and responses to gender transition. These decisions often expose deeper differences about Scripture and authority. / 各教会在女性按立、女性可担任的职分、同性婚姻、LGBTQ 圣职、独身、离婚再婚与性别转换回应上有分歧；这些决定常显出更深的圣经观与权威观差异。 |
| Creation, science, and last things / 创造、科学与末世 | Which readings are doctrinally required and which are models open to revision? / 哪些解释是教义要求，哪些模型可以修订？ | Young-earth, old-earth, evolutionary-creation, and nonliteral literary readings coexist across Christianity. Premillennial, amillennial, and postmillennial readings differ, as do dispensational and covenantal systems. / 年轻地球、古老地球、演化创造与非字面文学解释并存于基督教内部；前千禧、无千禧、后千禧，以及时代论与圣约体系也彼此不同。 |
| Church, state, and social order / 教会、国家与社会秩序 | Should public witness prioritize conversion, moral law, religious liberty, institutional reform, liberation, or separation from coercive power? / 公共见证应优先个人归信、道德律、宗教自由、制度改革、解放，还是远离强制权力？ | Several overlapping levels must be separated: institutional arrangements such as establishment or church-state separation; church-specific teaching traditions such as Catholic social teaching; cross-denominational theologies and movements such as the Social Gospel, Black public theology, and liberation traditions; political ideologies such as Christian nationalism; and ethical traditions or practices such as Anabaptist nonviolence. Any of these may combine with party politics in more than one way. / 必须区分几个彼此重叠的层级：政教建制或政教分离等制度安排；天主教社会训导等特定教会传统的教导；社会福音、黑人公共神学与解放传统等跨宗派神学和运动；基督教民族主义等政治意识形态；以及再洗礼派非暴力等伦理传统或实践。任何一层都可能以不止一种方式与政党政治结合。 |

**English:** No row is a two-team contest. For example, a conservative Catholic and a conservative Baptist may agree on abortion yet disagree deeply on authority, baptism, Eucharist, and church structure; two Presbyterians may share sacraments and polity yet vote differently and disagree on marriage.<br>
**中文：** 每一行都不是两队对决。例如，保守天主教徒与保守浸信会信徒可能在堕胎问题上接近，却在权威、洗礼、圣餐与教会结构上有深刻分歧；两位长老会信徒也可能共享圣礼与治理传统，却投不同的票，并对婚姻持不同看法。

**English:** The nine rows are an orientation map, not an exhaustive doctrinal chart. The sources below sample primary texts, current official positions, and research definitions for several representative contrasts; they do not document every phrase in the table, establish that every named family is uniform, or show every possible position.<br>
**中文：** 这九行是定位地图，不是穷尽教义的总表。下方来源抽样提供若干代表性对照所用的第一手文本、现行官方立场与研究定义；它们并不为表中每个短语逐一举证，也不能证明每个教会家族内部完全一致，更不表示本页列尽了所有可能立场。

<a id="conflict-levels"></a>
## How Serious Is a Disagreement? | 分歧有多严重？

| Level / 层级 | What is at stake / 涉及什么 | Example of the question / 问题示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creedal boundary / 信经边界 | Whether a teaching fits Nicene Christianity / 某教导是否属于尼西亚基督教 | Is God confessed as Trinity and Christ as truly divine and human? / 是否认信三一神与基督真神真人？ |
| Full-communion boundary / 完整共融边界 | Whether churches recognize one another’s ministry and sacraments / 教会是否彼此承认圣职与圣礼 | May clergy preside and members receive across the two bodies? / 双方圣职能否互相主持、会友能否彼此领受？ |
| Denominational identity / 宗派身份边界 | A durable confessional or governance difference / 持久的信条或治理差异 | Infant or believer’s baptism; episcopal or congregational polity? / 婴儿洗或信徒洗；主教制或会众制？ |
| Internal contested teaching / 宗派内部争议 | A body permits, debates, or disciplines more than one position / 一个宗派容许、争论或规训多种立场 | Women’s ordination, sexuality, creation models, gifts? / 女性按立、性伦理、创造模型、恩赐？ |
| Prudential judgment / 实践判断 | Shared moral goals but different policy means / 共同道德目标下不同政策手段 | Which tax, health, education, migration, or policing policy best loves one’s neighbor? / 哪种税收、医疗、教育、移民或治安政策最能实践爱邻舍？ |

**English:** The same issue may sit at different levels in different churches. A policy treated as confessional discipline in one body may be left to pastoral judgment in another. Always name the specific church body and date.<br>
**中文：** 同一议题在不同教会中可能处于不同层级。某宗派把一项政策视为信条纪律，另一个宗派却可能交给牧养判断；因此必须写明具体教会组织与日期。

<a id="worked-examples"></a>
## Three Worked Examples | 三个比较示例

### Example 1: Baptism | 示例一：洗礼

**English:** Do not summarize the conflict as “tradition versus Bible.” Both infant-baptizing and believer-baptizing churches appeal to Scripture, but they connect household passages, covenant continuity, personal profession, regeneration, and church membership differently. A fair comparison asks what each side thinks baptism signifies and effects before asking which verses it cites.<br>
**中文：** 不要把这场分歧简化成“传统对圣经”。施行婴儿洗与只施信徒洗的教会都诉诸圣经，只是对家庭受洗经文、圣约延续、个人认信、重生与会籍的连接方式不同。公平比较应先问双方认为洗礼象征并成就什么，再看各自引用哪些经文。

### Example 2: Women and LGBTQ Questions | 示例二：女性与 LGBTQ 议题

**English:** “Affirming” and “traditional” are conclusions, not complete arguments. Readers should compare each body’s interpretation of creation, disputed Pauline texts, Jesus’ teaching, holiness, justice, pastoral harm, ordination, and marriage. A political position cannot substitute for that theological map.<br>
**中文：** “接纳派”与“传统派”只是结论，不是完整论证。读者应比较各宗派怎样解释创造、保罗争议经文、耶稣教导、圣洁、公义、牧养伤害、按立与婚姻；政治立场不能代替这张神学论证图。

### Example 3: Faith in Public Life | 示例三：公共生活中的信仰

**English:** Christians may agree that faith has public consequences yet disagree over establishment, coercion, pluralism, protest, civil disobedience, economic structures, and party alliances. Criticizing Christian nationalism is not the same as demanding politically silent Christianity; supporting public Christian reasoning is not by itself Christian nationalism.<br>
**中文：** 基督徒可能同意信仰具有公共后果，却在政教建制、强制、多元共存、抗议、公民不服从、经济结构与政党联盟上有分歧。批评基督教民族主义不等于要求基督教在政治上沉默；支持基督徒公开说明信仰理由，也不自动等于基督教民族主义。

<a id="comparison-checklist"></a>
## Fair-Comparison Checklist | 公平比较清单

1. **Name the body and date / 写明组织与日期：** “PC(USA), 2025–2027 Book of Order” is stronger than “Presbyterians believe.” / “PC(USA)《教会法规》2025–2027”比“长老会都认为”更准确。
2. **Use its own standard first / 先读其自身规范：** Begin with the current confession, catechism, constitution, or position paper. / 先查现行信条、教理、章程或立场文件。
3. **Distinguish doctrine from practice / 区分教义与实践：** An official rule may be unevenly taught or followed. / 正式规则在实际教导与遵行中可能不一致。
4. **State the strongest rationale / 陈述最有力的理由：** Do not define one side only by its opponents’ criticism. / 不要只用对手的批评定义一方。
5. **Mark internal diversity / 标出内部差异：** A denomination, congregation, clergy member, and layperson may not speak with one voice. / 宗派、堂会、圣职人员与平信徒未必同声同调。
6. **Do not infer salvation or character / 不推断得救与品格：** A comparison page can locate a doctrine; it cannot read a person’s heart. / 比较页可以定位教义，却不能读取人的内心。

- [The Lutheran *Augsburg Confession*](https://bookofconcord.org/augsburg-confession/) and [Church of England, *Articles of Religion*](https://www.churchofengland.org/prayer-and-worship/worship-texts-and-resources/book-common-prayer/articles-religion) — two historical primary standards on grace, church, baptism, and the Lord's Supper; the Book of Concord link is a privately maintained text archive, while the Church of England link is official. / 两份关于恩典、教会、洗礼与圣餐的历史第一手规范；Book of Concord 链接来自私人维护的文本档案，英国圣公会链接则是官方来源。
- [Southern Baptist Convention, *Baptist Faith and Message 2000*](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/) and [PC(USA), *Our Challenging Way: Faithfulness, Sex, Ordination and Marriage*](https://pcusa.org/resource/our-challenging-way-faithfulness-sex-ordination-and-marriage) — an official conservative Baptist standard and a PC(USA)-hosted 2014 interpretive paper explaining the denomination's decisions on ordination and marriage. These different source types illustrate contrasting approaches but do not represent every Baptist or Presbyterian. / 一份保守浸信会官方规范，以及一篇由 PC(USA) 官网收录、解释该宗派按立与婚姻决定的 2014 年阐释性论文。这两种不同类型的来源显示对照路径，却不能代表所有浸信会或长老会。
- [Assemblies of God, *Statement of Fundamental Truths*](https://ag.org/Beliefs/Statement-of-Fundamental-Truths) and [Orthodox Presbyterian Church, “What about Prophecy and Tongues Today?”](https://www.opc.org/feature.html?feature_id=62) — an official Pentecostal standard and an OPC-hosted essay by an Orthodox Presbyterian minister, offered as examples of continuing-gifts and cessationist reasoning rather than equivalent binding standards for two complete camps. / 一份五旬节派官方规范，以及一篇由 OPC 官网收录、出自一位正统长老会牧者的文章；两者作为恩赐持续论与终止论论证的例子，并不是约束两个完整阵营的同等正式规范。
- [Christian Reformed Church, Creation and Science](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/position-statements/creation-and-science) — one denomination's official example of holding creation, Scripture, confessions, and scientific inquiry together without canonizing one scientific hypothesis. / 一个宗派如何同时处理创造、圣经、信条与科学研究，并不把某个科学假说奉为教义的官方例子。
- [Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.), Constitution](https://pcusa.org/about-pcusa/agencies-entities/life-witness/ministry-areas/constitutional-interpretation/constitution), [Confessions](https://pcusa.org/confessions), and [Sacraments](https://pcusa.org/about-pcusa/agencies-entities/life-witness/ministry-areas/theology-worship/worship/sacraments) — current examples from one mainline Reformed body. / 一个主流改革宗组织的现行例子。
- [Mennonite Church USA, “Peace, Justice, and Nonresistance”](https://www.mennoniteusa.org/who-are-mennonites/what-we-believe/confession-of-faith/peace-justice-and-nonresistance/) and [United Methodist Church, Social Principles: The Political Community](https://www.umc.org/content/social-principles-the-political-community) — official examples of two different forms of Christian public ethics, neither reducible to a political party. / 两种不同基督徒公共伦理的官方例子；双方都不能被简化为某个政党立场。
- [Oxford Academic, *Taking America Back for God*](https://academic.oup.com/book/33661/chapter-abstract/288202584) and [PRRI, 2025 American Values Atlas report](https://prri.org/research/mapping-christian-nationalism-across-the-50-states-insights-from-prris-2025-american-values-atlas/) — an academic account and one survey operationalization of Christian nationalism, not a church confession or a definition binding every researcher. / 基督教民族主义的一项学术说明与一种调查测量方法；它们不是教会信条，也不是约束所有研究者的唯一定义。
- [United Methodist Church, *Baptism: God's Gift to Us All*](https://www.umc.org/en/content/baptism-gods-gift-to-us-all) — an official example showing why infant-baptizing traditions can vary in mode. / 一个官方例子，用来说明施行婴儿洗的传统在施水方式上也可以不同。
