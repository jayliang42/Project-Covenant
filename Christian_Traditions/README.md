> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> When you read Christian books or meet Christians from other churches, you will hear names such as Catholic, Orthodox, Baptist, evangelical, or charismatic. These words do not all mean the same kind of thing: some name a church family, some a denomination, and some a movement that crosses many churches. This section helps you tell them apart, so you can understand a book or a conversation without jumping to conclusions about people.<br>
> 读基督教书籍，或认识其他教会的基督徒时，你会听到天主教、东正教、浸信会、福音派、灵恩派等名称。这些词指的并不是同一类东西：有的指教会大家族，有的指宗派，有的指跨越许多教会的运动。本区帮助你分清它们，使你能读懂一本书或一段对话，而不急于给人下结论。

## One Trunk, Many Branches | 同一树干，许多枝子

Most Christians around the world share a common center: one God who is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; Jesus Christ who died and rose again; and the Bible as Scripture. Over two thousand years, churches have grown apart in worship, church government, and some teachings, much like the branches of one tree. Knowing the main branches makes it easier to follow what a writer or speaker means.<br>
世界各地的大多数基督徒有一个共同的中心：独一的神是圣父、圣子、圣灵；耶稣基督受死并复活；圣经是神的话语。两千年来，各教会在敬拜方式、教会治理和部分教导上渐渐分开，就像同一棵树分出的枝子。了解主要的枝子，就更容易明白作者或讲员所说的意思。

## A Simple Habit When Reading | 阅读时的一个简单习惯

When a book or person uses a church label, ask three things: What kind of label is it? Who is using it, and how does that group describe itself? What does the source actually show? A church’s name alone does not settle every teaching, and it never describes every member.<br>
当一本书或一个人使用某个教会标签时，可以问三件事：这是哪一类标签？是谁在使用它，那个群体又怎样描述自己？资料实际说明了什么？一个教会的名称不能定下它的全部教导，也不能代表每一位成员。
