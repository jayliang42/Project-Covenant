# Christian Traditions and Public Theology | 基督教传统与公共神学

> This column explains Christian communions, denominations, cross-denominational movements, theological approaches, and their public-life disagreements. It also separates measured demographic patterns from racial stereotypes.
> 本专栏解释基督教的教会共融体、宗派、跨宗派运动、神学取向及其公共议题分歧，并把可以量化的人口趋势与族裔刻板印象分开。

<a id="column-scope"></a>
## Scope and Reading Boundary | 范围与阅读边界

**English:** The demographic pages use United States surveys because percentages only make sense after fixing a place, year, sample, and denominator. A separate page discusses Chinese and Chinese American churches without treating those populations as interchangeable.<br>
**中文：** 人口比例必须先限定地区、年份、样本与分母，因此统计页以美国调查为主；另设华人教会页，并且不会把中国境内、美国华裔与全球华人混为一谈。

**English:** This is a comparison guide, not a ranking of who is a “real Christian.” It reports how traditions describe themselves, where they overlap, and where their official teachings or common practices diverge.<br>
**中文：** 这是比较导读，不是判定谁才是“真基督徒”的排行榜。它会说明各传统如何自我描述、有哪些共同点，以及正式教导或常见实践在哪里分歧。

<a id="three-corrections"></a>
## Three Corrections Before Comparing | 比较前先校正三个概念

| Common shorthand / 常见简称 | More accurate description / 更准确的说法 |
| --- | --- |
| “Chinese Christians are fundamentalists.” / “华人基督徒多是基要真理派。” | “Fundamentalist” is not a single denomination, and Chinese church usage of “holding to the fundamentals” does not always equal the historical English label. Survey evidence must be stated by country and sample. / “基要派”不是单一宗派，华人教会所说的“持守基要真理”也不一定等于英语历史语境中的 Fundamentalism；调查结论必须注明国家与样本。 |
| “White Christians are evangelicals.” / “白人基督徒多是福音派。” | Evangelicalism crosses denominations and races. A large share of U.S. evangelicals are White, but that does not mean most White adults are evangelical. The two denominators answer different questions. / 福音派跨越宗派与族裔。“美国福音派成员中许多是白人”不等于“多数美国白人都是福音派”；两种分母回答的是不同问题。 |
| “James Talarico belongs to the progressive denomination.” / “James Talarico 属于进步神学宗派。” | Progressive Christianity is a broad theological and public-life orientation, not one denomination. Talarico is a Presbyterian case study, and he has publicly resisted reducing his faith to either a progressive or conservative label. / 进步基督教是一种宽泛的神学与公共生活取向，不是一个宗派。Talarico 是长老会背景的个案，而且他公开反对把自己的信仰只归为“进步”或“保守”。 |

<a id="reading-method"></a>
## A Four-Question Reading Method | 四问阅读法

1. **What level is this label? / 这个标签属于哪一层？** Is it a worldwide communion, denomination, movement, theological method, worship style, or political identity? / 它是全球教会共融体、具体宗派、运动、神学方法、敬拜风格，还是政治身份？
2. **Who is speaking? / 谁在发言？** Distinguish an official confession, a denominational decision, a local congregation, a scholar, and one public figure. / 区分正式信条、宗派决议、地方堂会、学者与单个公众人物。
3. **What is the denominator? / 分母是什么？** “Share of evangelicals who are White” and “share of White adults who are evangelical” are not interchangeable. / “福音派成员中白人占比”与“白人成年人中福音派占比”不能互换。
4. **What does the evidence stop short of? / 证据不能推出什么？** A survey describes a population; it does not determine an individual’s doctrine, politics, character, or spiritual life. / 调查只能描述群体，不能决定个人的教义、政治、品格或灵命。
