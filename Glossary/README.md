## Bible and the Story | 圣经与故事

<a id="bible"></a>
### Bible | 圣经

A collection of books that Christians read as Scripture, with different kinds of writing.
基督徒视为圣经的多卷书合集，包含故事、诗歌、书信等不同文体。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#first-steps-overview)

<a id="old-testament"></a>
### Old Testament | 旧约

The part of the Christian Bible that tells of creation and Israel before Jesus. Christian traditions differ in which books they include.
基督教圣经中叙述创造与耶稣以前以色列故事的部分；不同传统的书目不完全相同。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-creation)

<a id="new-testament"></a>
### New Testament | 新约

The Christian writings about Jesus and the early church; here the word names a collection of books.
关于耶稣与早期教会的基督教文献；这里指一组书卷。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="creation"></a>
### Creation | 创造

God brings the world into being and gives it order and purpose. The word can also mean the world God made.
神使世界存在，并赋予秩序和目的；这个词也可指神所造的世界。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-creation)

<a id="fall"></a>
### Fall | 堕落

A short name for the human turn away from trust in God in Genesis 3 and the broken life that follows.
对创世记第三章中人离开对神的信任，以及其后生命破裂的简短说法。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-fall)

<a id="sin"></a>
### Sin | 罪

Turning from God in ways that harm people, relationships, and the world. It includes what people do and the broken condition they share.
人背离神，伤害自己、别人和受造世界；既指具体过错，也指共同面对的破裂处境。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-fall)

<a id="covenant"></a>
### Covenant | 约（圣约）

A committed relationship with promises and responsibilities. In the Bible, God establishes covenants with people.
带有应许和责任的郑重关系；圣经叙述神与人立约。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-covenant)

<a id="promise"></a>
### Promise | 应许

What God commits to do; readers follow how the story carries that commitment forward.
神承诺要做的事；阅读时可以追踪这份承诺怎样延续。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-covenant)

<a id="blessing"></a>
### Blessing | 祝福

God gives life and good gifts so that people can live well and share good with others. It is more than wealth or comfort.
神赐下生命与美善，使人得以生活并惠及别人；不等于财富或舒适。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-covenant)

<a id="faith"></a>
### Faith | 信心

Trusting God and responding to him, even without having every question answered.
信任神并作出回应，不是先把所有问题解决。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-covenant)

<a id="terms-10"></a>
## Jesus and Response | 耶稣与回应

<a id="grace"></a>
### Grace | 恩典

God freely gives favor and help; people do not buy it or earn it by being impressive.
神白白施恩和帮助，人不能靠交易或表现换取。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="repentance"></a>
### Repentance | 悔改

Turning from a wrong direction toward God, with a changed way of living. It involves more than feeling bad.
离开错误方向，转向神并改变生活，不只是难过。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="forgiveness"></a>
### Forgiveness | 赦免

God deals with wrongdoing and opens a way for restored relationship. It does not call the harm good.
神处理人的过错，使关系能够恢复；这不把伤害说成好事。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="salvation"></a>
### Salvation | 救恩

God rescues people from sin and death and brings them into life with him.
神拯救人脱离罪与死亡，使人与祂一同生活。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="redemption"></a>
### Redemption | 救赎

Rescue described with the picture of release from slavery or debt. The surrounding passage explains the picture.
用脱离奴役或债务来说明拯救；具体含义要看经文。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="messiah"></a>
### Messiah | 弥赛亚

The Hebrew title meaning anointed one. Christians use it for Jesus as the promised king and rescuer.
希伯来语中意为受膏者的称号；基督徒用它称耶稣为所应许的君王与拯救者。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="christ"></a>
### Christ | 基督

The Greek equivalent of Messiah, used as a title for Jesus rather than a family name.
弥赛亚的希腊语对应称号，用于耶稣，不是姓氏。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="gospel"></a>
### Gospel | 福音

The good news about Jesus and what God does through him. It can also name one of the four books about Jesus.
关于耶稣及神借祂所作之事的好消息；也可指四卷福音书中的一卷。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="gospels"></a>
### Gospels | 福音书

The four New Testament books Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. They tell of Jesus’ life, teaching, death, and resurrection from different perspectives.
指《马太福音》《马可福音》《路加福音》《约翰福音》四卷书；从不同侧面记述耶稣的生活、教导、受死与复活。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-between)

<a id="kingdom"></a>
### Kingdom of God | 国度（神的国）

God putting things right through his rule. The phrase is not simply the name of a territory.
神掌权并使生命归正；这个词不只是一个地域名称。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-kingdom)

<a id="holy-spirit"></a>
### Holy Spirit | 圣灵

God present and active with his people, giving life and helping them follow Jesus. Christians speak of the Spirit personally, not as an impersonal force.
神与子民同在、赐生命并帮助人跟随耶稣；基督徒所说的圣灵不是一种无人格的力量。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="terms-20"></a>
## People and Worship | 群体与敬拜

<a id="disciple"></a>
### Disciple | 门徒

A learner who follows Jesus and practices what he teaches.
跟随耶稣、学习并实践祂教导的人。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="apostle"></a>
### Apostle | 使徒

A person sent with a task; in the New Testament it often refers to witnesses commissioned by Jesus.
受差遣承担使命的人；新约常用来指受耶稣委托的见证人。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="church"></a>
### Church | 教会

The people gathered around Jesus, whether in a local community or spoken of together. The word does not mainly mean a building.
围绕耶稣聚集的群体，可指一处群体，也可统称众信徒；主要不是建筑物。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="gentile"></a>
### Gentile | 外邦人

In many biblical passages, a person who is not part of the Jewish people. Read the term in its ancient context.
许多经文中对非犹太人的称呼，应放在古代语境中理解。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="israel"></a>
### Israel | 以色列

The name can refer to Jacob, the people descended from him, or the northern kingdom after the division. Context tells you which meaning is intended.
可指雅各、其后裔群体，或王国分裂后的北国；要按上下文区分。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="judah"></a>
### Judah | 犹大

A son of Jacob, a tribe, or the southern kingdom whose capital was Jerusalem. These uses are related but not identical.
可指雅各的儿子、一个支派，或以耶路撒冷为都城的南国；这些用法有关，但不能混同。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-kingdom)

<a id="prophet"></a>
### Prophet | 先知

A person who speaks a message from God, often calling people back to faithfulness. Prediction is only one part of this work.
传达神信息的人，常呼吁人回到忠信；预告未来只是其中一部分。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-kingdom)

<a id="priest"></a>
### Priest | 祭司

A person who serves in worship and helps the community approach God according to its covenant practices.
在敬拜中服事，并按立约群体的礼仪帮助人亲近神的人。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="law"></a>
### Law or Torah | 律法

God’s instruction, often referring to the teaching associated with Moses. Ask which passage and covenant setting are being discussed.
神的教导，常指与摩西有关的训诲；阅读时要辨明具体经文与立约背景。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="tabernacle"></a>
### Tabernacle | 会幕

The movable worship tent in Israel’s wilderness story, a sign of God dwelling among the people.
以色列旷野故事中的可移动敬拜帐幕，表达神住在子民中间。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="temple"></a>
### Temple | 圣殿

The worship center in Jerusalem in the biblical story. Different temples and building periods must not be treated as one unchanged structure.
圣经故事中耶路撒冷的敬拜中心；不同圣殿及建造时期不能视为从未改变的同一建筑。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-kingdom)

<a id="sacrifice"></a>
### Sacrifice | 献祭

An offering in worship, used in different passages for thanksgiving, fellowship, or dealing with wrongdoing.
敬拜中的奉献；在不同经文中用于感恩、相交或处理过错。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="passover"></a>
### Passover | 逾越节

The festival that remembers Israel’s rescue from Egypt. The New Testament also uses this background when speaking of Jesus.
记念以色列离开埃及、蒙拯救的节期；新约谈论耶稣时也使用这一背景。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="terms-33"></a>
## Journey and Hope | 旅程与盼望

<a id="exodus"></a>
### Exodus | 出埃及

Israel’s departure from slavery in Egypt in the biblical story; it is also the English name of the second book of the Bible.
圣经故事中以色列脱离埃及奴役的经历，也是一卷圣经书的名称。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exodus)

<a id="exile"></a>
### Exile | 被掳

Forced removal from one’s homeland, especially the deportations of Israel and Judah in the biblical story.
被迫离开故土；圣经故事中常指以色列与犹大遭强制迁徙。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exile)

<a id="return"></a>
### Return | 归回

The return of some exiles and the rebuilding of community and worship. It did not immediately resolve every problem.
部分被掳者归乡，并重建群体和敬拜；并不是所有问题立刻结束。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-exile)

<a id="resurrection"></a>
### Resurrection | 复活

Being raised from death into life. In Christian teaching, Jesus’ resurrection grounds the hope of a final resurrection.
从死亡中被兴起进入生命；基督教以耶稣复活作为最终复活盼望的根基。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="parable"></a>
### Parable | 比喻

A story or comparison that invites listeners to see something differently. Its setting and main point matter.
借故事或比较帮助听者换个角度理解事情；要留意语境与重点。

See [Parables of Jesus](../Bible_Timeline/Parables_of_Jesus_Bilingual.md) for nineteen examples and how to read them.<br>
十九个例子及读法，见[耶稣的比喻](../Bible_Timeline/Parables_of_Jesus_Bilingual.md)。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="psalm"></a>
### Psalm | 诗篇

A song or prayer, often expressing praise, grief, trust, or complaint before God.
向神表达赞美、悲伤、信任或申诉的诗歌与祷告。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-kingdom)

<a id="wisdom"></a>
### Wisdom | 智慧

Learning to live well before God through discernment, experience, and instruction. Wisdom writing is not always a promise that life will be easy.
通过辨别、经验和教导学习在神面前好好生活；智慧文学并不总是保证人生顺利。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-kingdom)

<a id="canon"></a>
### Canon | 正典

The collection of books a faith community receives as Scripture. Christian traditions differ over some Old Testament books.
信仰群体接纳为圣经的书卷范围；基督教传统对部分旧约书卷的认定不同。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-between)

<a id="pentecost"></a>
### Pentecost | 五旬节

A Jewish festival that forms the setting of Acts 2, where the Spirit equips Jesus’ followers to bear witness.
使徒行传第二章的犹太节期背景；经文叙述圣灵帮助耶稣的跟随者作见证。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="new-creation"></a>
### New creation | 新创造

God’s final renewal, with evil and death overcome and God dwelling with his people. The hope is not a date-setting exercise.
神最终更新万有，胜过罪恶与死亡，与子民同住；这份盼望不是让人推算日期。

[First use in First Steps | 入门路线首次使用](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-new-creation)


<a id="terms-43"></a>
## Prayer and Church Life | 祷告与教会生活

<a id="prayer"></a>
### Prayer | 祷告

Speaking with God: praising, thanking, confessing, asking, and listening. Jesus taught his followers a model prayer, often called the Lord’s Prayer (Matthew 6:9–13).<br>
与神说话，包括赞美、感谢、认罪、祈求和聆听。耶稣教门徒一篇范例祷告，常称为“主祷文”（马太福音 6:9–13）。

See [Prayers in the Bible](../Bible_Timeline/Prayers_in_the_Bible_Bilingual.md) for twenty-one prayers to read.<br>
二十一篇可以阅读的祷告，见[圣经中的祷告](../Bible_Timeline/Prayers_in_the_Bible_Bilingual.md)。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-05-17_Sunday_Growth_Through_Prayer.md)

<a id="worship"></a>
### Worship | 敬拜

Honoring God as God. It includes gathered praise, prayer, and hearing Scripture, and it also includes everyday trust and obedience.<br>
尊荣神为神。它包括聚会中的赞美、祷告和聆听圣经，也包括日常生活中的信靠与顺服。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bilingual_Notes/2025-11-02_Sunday_Worship_and_Sacrifice.md)

<a id="baptism"></a>
### Baptism | 洗礼

A washing with water that marks a person’s entry into the community of Jesus’ followers. Churches differ on whether infants are baptized and on how water is used.<br>
用水施行的礼仪，标志一个人加入跟随耶稣的群体。各教会在是否为婴儿施洗，以及用水的方式上看法不同。

[Related stop in First Steps | 入门路线相关站点](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="lords-supper"></a>
### Lord’s Supper | 圣餐

A shared meal of bread and cup that remembers Jesus’ death until he comes (1 Corinthians 11:23–26). It is also called Communion or the Eucharist, and traditions explain Christ’s presence in it differently.<br>
以饼和杯共同领受的记念，纪念耶稣的死，直等到祂来（哥林多前书 11:23–26）。它也称为“圣餐礼”或“感恩祭”；各传统对基督在其中如何同在的理解不同。

[Related stop in First Steps | 入门路线相关站点](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-church)

<a id="cross"></a>
### Cross | 十字架

A Roman method of execution, used for Jesus’ death. Christians see the cross as the place where God’s love meets human sin; see also [atonement](#atonement).<br>
罗马人的一种处决刑具，耶稣就死在十字架上。基督徒视十字架为神的爱与人的罪相遇之处；另见[赎罪](#atonement)。

[Related stop in First Steps | 入门路线相关站点](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="holiness"></a>
### Holiness | 圣洁

Being set apart for God. It describes God’s own purity and difference from everything else, and his people are called to reflect it in how they live.<br>
被分别出来归给神。这个词描述神自己的纯洁与超越，神的子民也蒙召在生活中反映这种圣洁。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#genre-law)

<a id="righteousness"></a>
### Righteousness | 义

Being in right relationship with God and acting rightly toward others, as God defines what is right. Paul also uses the word for God’s faithful action to put people right; see [justification](#justification).<br>
按神所定义的“正确”，与神有正确的关系，并以正直对待他人。保罗也用这个词表达神使人与祂和好的信实作为；另见[称义](#justification)。

[Related stop in First Steps | 入门路线相关站点](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="mercy"></a>
### Mercy | 怜悯

Compassion shown to people who are suffering or in the wrong. [Grace](#grace) stresses a free gift; mercy stresses pity that relieves misery.<br>
向受苦或犯错的人显出的同情。[恩典](#grace)强调白白的赐予；怜悯强调体恤并解除苦难。

[Related stop in First Steps | 入门路线相关站点](../Bible_Timeline/First_Steps_Bilingual.md#stop-jesus)

<a id="sabbath"></a>
### Sabbath | 安息日

The seventh day of rest in Israel’s law (Exodus 20:8–11). Most Christians gather on Sunday, the Lord’s Day, to remember the resurrection; some keep Saturday.<br>
以色列律法中第七日的休息（出埃及记 20:8–11）。多数基督徒在星期日、即“主日”聚会，记念复活；也有些基督徒守星期六。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bilingual_Notes/2026-02-15_Sunday_Rest_and_Peace_in_Christ.md)

<a id="lord"></a>
### Lord | 主

A title meaning master. Early Christians confessed “Jesus is Lord” (Romans 10:9). In many English Bibles, LORD in capitals stands for God’s personal name.<br>
意为“主人”的称号。早期基督徒宣认“耶稣是主”（罗马书 10:9）。在许多英文圣经中，大写的 LORD 代表神的名字。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#faq-lord)

<a id="amen"></a>
### Amen | 阿们

A Hebrew word meaning “it is true” or “so be it,” said to agree with a prayer or a truth.<br>
希伯来词，意思是“诚然如此”或“愿是这样”，用来认同一个祷告或一项真理。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#four-questions)

<a id="hallelujah"></a>
### Hallelujah | 哈利路亚

A Hebrew call meaning “praise the LORD.” It appears often in the last psalms (Psalms 146–150).<br>
希伯来语的呼召，意思是“你们要赞美耶和华”，常见于最后几篇诗篇（诗篇 146–150 篇）。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#genre-poetry)

<a id="pharisee"></a>
### Pharisee | 法利赛人

A member of a Jewish group in Jesus’ day devoted to careful keeping of the law. The Gospels record sharp debates with Jesus, but Pharisees were not all alike, and Paul had been one (Philippians 3:5).<br>
耶稣时代的犹太群体成员，热心谨守律法。福音书记载了他们与耶稣的激烈辩论，但法利赛人并非千人一面，保罗从前也是法利赛人（腓立比书 3:5）。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/两约之间至四福音_第二圣殿与罗马背景.md)

<a id="epistle"></a>
### Epistle | 书信

A formal word for a letter. Twenty-one New Testament books, Romans through Jude, are letters to churches or individuals.<br>
“书信”的正式说法。新约中从罗马书到犹大书共 21 卷，都是写给教会或个人的信。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#genre-letters)

<a id="apocalyptic"></a>
### Apocalyptic | 启示文学

A kind of writing that uses visions and symbols to reveal God’s rule over history, as in parts of Daniel and the book of Revelation.<br>
借异象和象征揭示神掌管历史的文体，见于但以理书的部分章节和启示录。

[Read more | 延伸阅读](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#genre-apocalyptic)

<a id="trinity"></a>
### Trinity | 三一／三位一体

Christians confess one God as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. The three are distinct persons, not three gods or one person wearing different masks.<br>
基督徒承认一位神，就是父、子、圣灵。三个位格彼此有别，不是三位神，也不是一个位格轮流换角色。

<a id="incarnation"></a>
### Incarnation | 道成肉身

The Son of God becomes human in Jesus without ceasing to be God. Christians use this word when reading about Jesus’ birth and life.<br>
神的儿子在耶稣里成为人，仍然是神。基督徒用这个词谈耶稣的降生与生命。

神借着基督接纳人，宣告人与祂的关系归正，人凭信领受。本库笔记把这份接纳，与随后生命的改变分开说明。

<a id="manuscript"></a>
### Manuscript | 抄本

A handwritten copy of a text, complete or fragmentary. The date of a surviving copy is not automatically the date of the original writing or the events it describes.<br>
用手抄写的文本，可以是整卷，也可以只剩残片。现存副本的年代，不自动等于原作或所叙事件的年代。

<a id="reception-history"></a>
### Reception history | 接收史

The study of how later readers use, explain, collect, translate, or publicly read a work. Such evidence shows later reading practices without by itself proving the events in the work.<br>
研究后来的读者怎样使用、解释、收集、翻译或公开宣读一部作品。这些材料显示后来的阅读实践，本身不能证明作品中的事件。

### Septuagint | 七十士译本

A name for ancient Greek translations of Jewish scriptures. Different books were translated and revised at different times.<br>
对犹太经典古希腊文译本的通称。不同书卷的翻译和修订发生在不同时间。
