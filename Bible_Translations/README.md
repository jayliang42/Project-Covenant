<a id="two-ways"></a>
## Two Ways to Translate | 两种翻译方式

Some translations stay close to the original words and sentence order. They are good for careful study, but can sound less natural. Others translate the meaning of each phrase into everyday language. They are easier to read, but the translators make more interpretive choices for the reader. Many translations sit somewhere between these two.<br>
有些译本紧贴原文的字词和语序，适合仔细研读，但读起来可能不太顺口。另一些译本把每个短语的意思译成日常语言，读起来容易，但译者替读者作的解释性选择也更多。许多译本介于两者之间。

Neither approach is simply right or wrong. A close translation shows you more of the original’s shape; a meaning-based one helps you follow the flow of a story or argument.<br>
这两种方式没有简单的对错。贴近原文的译本让你更多看到原文的样貌；意译的译本帮助你跟上故事或论证的脉络。

<a id="chinese"></a>
## Common Chinese Translations | 常见中文译本

- **Chinese Union Version (和合本)** — Published in 1919 and still the Bible most Chinese churches read. Its wording is dignified and close to the original, though some words now feel old-fashioned. It is printed in a 神 edition and a 上帝 edition; see the [Bible Reading Toolkit](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#faq-shen).
  **和合本** —— 1919 年出版，至今仍是大多数华人教会使用的圣经。文字庄重，贴近原文，但有些词语今天读来略显古旧。它有“神版”和“上帝版”两种印本，见[读经工具箱](../Bible_Timeline/Bible_Reading_Toolkit_Bilingual.md#faq-shen)。
- **New Punctuation Union Version (新标点和合本)** — The same text with modern punctuation and some updated characters, often used in churches today.
  **新标点和合本** —— 文字与和合本相同，改用现代标点，并更新了部分用字；今天许多教会使用的就是这一版。
- **Revised Chinese Union Version (和合本修订版)** — A careful revision published in 2010 that updates wording while keeping the Union Version’s style.
  **和合本修订版** —— 2010 年出版的审慎修订本，在保留和合本风格的同时更新了用词。
- **Newer translations in modern Chinese** — For example 新译本 and 中文标准译本. They can help when an old Union Version phrase is hard to understand.
  **用现代中文翻译的新译本** —— 例如新译本和中文标准译本。和合本的某个旧说法难以理解时，可以拿来对照。

<a id="english"></a>
## Common English Translations | 常见英文译本

- **Closer to the original wording:** English Standard Version (ESV), New American Standard Bible (NASB), and the historic King James Version (KJV) of 1611.
  **较贴近原文字句：** 英文标准译本（ESV）、新美国标准译本（NASB），以及 1611 年的英王钦定本（KJV）。
- **A balance of accuracy and readability:** New International Version (NIV) and Christian Standard Bible (CSB).
  **兼顾准确与易读：** 新国际译本（NIV）和基督教标准译本（CSB）。
- **Meaning-based and easy to read:** New Living Translation (NLT).
  **意译、易读：** New Living Translation（NLT）。

For readers learning English, a balanced or meaning-based translation is usually easier to start with. This library also has a guide to the [International Standard Version (ISV)](./ISV_International_Standard_Version_Access_and_ESL_Guide.md), a modern translation with a free online edition.<br>
对正在学英语的读者来说，从兼顾型或意译型译本开始通常比较容易。本资料库也有一篇关于[国际标准译本（ISV）](./ISV_International_Standard_Version_Access_and_ESL_Guide.md)的指南；这是一个提供免费在线版的现代译本。

<a id="two-translations"></a>
## Reading with Two Translations | 对照两个译本来读

1. **Choose one main translation** and read it regularly, so the wording becomes familiar.
   **选一个主要译本**固定阅读，让经文的措辞变得熟悉。
2. **Keep a second translation nearby,** ideally of the other type: for example the Union Version with the NIV or NLT.
   **手边放第二个译本，**最好是另一种类型的，例如和合本配 NIV 或 NLT。
3. **Compare when a verse matters or seems unclear.** If the two differ a lot, check the footnotes; the original may allow more than one reading.
   **遇到重要或难懂的经文时再对照。** 如果两个译本差别很大，看看脚注；原文可能允许不止一种理解。
4. **Read the surrounding passage.** A single word in one translation should not decide a teaching on its own.
   **读上下文。** 不要单凭某个译本里的一个词就定下一项教导。
5. **Respect copyright.** Most modern translations limit how much text may be copied or shared; check the publisher’s terms before sharing long passages.
   **尊重版权。** 大多数现代译本都限制可复制或分享的经文数量；分享较长的经文之前，先查看出版方的条款。

For how the Bible was copied and translated over the centuries, see [How the Bible Came to Us](../Bible_Timeline/How_the_Bible_Came_to_Us_Bilingual.md#translated).<br>
圣经在历代怎样被抄传与翻译，见[圣经是怎样传到我们手中的](../Bible_Timeline/How_the_Bible_Came_to_Us_Bilingual.md#translated)。
