## 中文

### 这个项目是什么

`Project Covenant` 是一个个人灵性成长资料库，用来长期整理：

- 主日讲道与平时 fellowship / study 的中英双语笔记
- 圣经时间线和整本圣经的大故事
- 重要主题、经文、人物、圣约和神学脉络
- 后续可以反复复习、祷告和应用的材料

这个项目的重点不是保存原始记录，而是把学习内容整理成清楚、可靠、容易复习的笔记。

### 内容结构

- [`Bilingual_Notes/`](./Bilingual_Notes/)
  中英双语讲道、查经和 fellowship 笔记。
- [`Bible_Timeline/`](./Bible_Timeline/)
  圣经时间线、按书卷梳理、一页速查表、旧约和新约分拆版。

### 推荐从哪里开始

- [讲道笔记索引](./Bilingual_Notes/INDEX_Chronological.md)
  按日期排列，并标注 `Sunday Teaching`、`Sunday Fellowship`、`Wednesday Fellowship`。
- [全部 teaching 总结](./Bilingual_Notes/All_Notes_Summary_Chronological.md)
  快速浏览目前已经整理过的全部内容。
- [圣经时间线总览](./Bible_Timeline/Bible_Timeline_Bilingual.md)
  从创造、堕落、立约、国度、被掳、耶稣、教会到新创造。
- [圣经一页速查表](./Bible_Timeline/Bible_Timeline_Cheat_Sheet.md)
  适合快速复习整本圣经大图。

### 笔记原则

- 忠实呈现 teaching 的主旨，而不是逐字稿。
- 保留足够细节，方便之后复习、默想和祷告。
- 对不确定的内容保持谨慎，不把推断写成确定事实。
- 尽量把讲员主旨、经文脉络、神学注释和个人应用区分开。

## English

### What This Project Is

`Project Covenant` is a personal spiritual-growth knowledge base for organizing:

- bilingual sermon, fellowship, and study notes
- the Bible timeline and the big story of Scripture
- key themes, passages, people, covenants, and theological threads
- reviewable materials for prayer, reflection, and application

The goal is not to preserve raw source material. The goal is to turn learning into clear, reliable, reusable notes.

### Project Structure

- [`Bilingual_Notes/`](./Bilingual_Notes/)
  Bilingual notes for sermons, Bible studies, and fellowship sessions.
- [`Bible_Timeline/`](./Bible_Timeline/)
  Bible timeline resources, book-by-book summaries, cheat sheets, and Old/New Testament timeline splits.

### Where To Start

- [Teaching Notes Index](./Bilingual_Notes/INDEX_Chronological.md)
  Chronological index with labels such as `Sunday Teaching`, `Sunday Fellowship`, and `Wednesday Fellowship`.
- [All Teaching Notes Summary](./Bilingual_Notes/All_Notes_Summary_Chronological.md)
  A quick overview of all currently summarized teachings.
- [Bible Timeline Overview](./Bible_Timeline/Bible_Timeline_Bilingual.md)
  The story from creation, fall, covenant, kingdom, exile, Jesus, church, and new creation.
- [Bible Timeline Cheat Sheet](./Bible_Timeline/Bible_Timeline_Cheat_Sheet.md)
  A short review sheet for the whole Bible storyline.

### Note Principles

- Summarize the main teaching faithfully rather than preserving a transcript.
- Keep enough detail for review, meditation, and prayer.
- Mark uncertainty carefully instead of presenting inference as fact.
- Separate the speaker's main point, biblical context, theological notes, and personal application where possible.
