## What This Library Does | 这个资料库在做什么

Project Covenant reads Scripture as one connected story: creation, fall, covenant, kingdom, exile, promise, Christ, church, and new creation. It combines biblical theology, historical context, manuscripts, archaeology, and reception history while distinguishing what each kind of evidence can—and cannot—show.

Project Covenant 把圣经读成一条连贯的故事线：创造、堕落、圣约、国度、被掳、应许、基督、教会与新创造。资料库同时使用圣经神学、历史语境、抄本、考古与接收史，并清楚区分每类材料能支持什么、不能支持什么。

The project is a study library, not a substitute for Scripture, a local church, the original books being discussed, or professional historical and scientific research. Guides summarize and organize sources without reproducing copyrighted works.

本项目是研读资料库，不取代圣经、地方教会、所讨论的原书，也不取代专业历史或科学研究。导读负责总结、整理和核查来源，不复制受版权保护的作品。

## Language Status | 语言状态

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Full bilingual｜完整双语** | Substantive content is available in both English and Chinese.｜实质正文同时提供英文与中文。 |
| **Chinese full + English orientation｜中文全文＋英文导读** | The complete research body is Chinese; English readers receive an accurate title, scope, and route.｜完整研究正文为中文；英文读者可获得准确标题、范围与使用路线。 |
| **English full + Chinese orientation｜英文全文＋中文导读** | The complete guide is English; Chinese readers receive a reliable orientation or summary.｜完整正文为英文；中文读者可获得可靠导读或摘要。 |
| **Bilingual navigation｜双语导航** | An index or hub is bilingual even when linked long-form files have different translation status.｜索引或入口本身为双语；所链接长文的翻译状态可能不同。 |

A mixed-language heading alone does not make a document fully bilingual. Translation status is stated explicitly so readers know whether they are opening a complete parallel guide, a navigation layer, or a one-language research dossier with an orientation in the other language.

只有中英混合标题并不等于“完整双语”。本项目明确标注翻译状态，让读者在打开文件前知道它是完整对照导读、双语导航，还是带另一语言简介的单语研究专题。

## Research Boundaries | 研究边界

Historical and archaeological materials can illuminate people, places, institutions, texts, and historical settings. They do not automatically prove every narrative detail, traditional author, miracle, or theological conclusion. Each research route therefore asks both **what a source supports** and **where interpretation must stop**.

史料与考古可以照明人物、地点、制度、文本与历史处境，却不会自动证明每一项叙事细节、传统作者、神迹或神学结论。因此每条研究路线都同时追问：**材料能支持什么**，以及**解释必须停在哪里**。
